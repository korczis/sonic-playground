Roman Flügel’s remix of “Idle Eyes” takes C.A.R.'s original track and strips it down to its most delicate essence. The title, "Idle Eyes," suggests a detached gaze, perfectly mirrored by Flügel's minimal approach, which lets the music float weightlessly in space. The remix becomes a meditative experience, where subtle textures and layers of sound build a dreamlike atmosphere.

Original Version

With its delicate, almost haunting quality, this remix amplifies the mysterious and introspective vibe of the original, creating a minimalist journey where every sound feels deliberate and meticulously crafted. Roman Flügel, a master of refined electronic music, provides a remix that is as spacious as it is hypnotic, guiding listeners through a soundscape that feels both distant and intimately close.

Genre and Mood

C.A.R.'s "Idle Eyes," remixed by Roman Flügel, falls into the realm of minimal electronic with a heavy emphasis on atmosphere. The track radiates a hypnotic and ethereal vibe, perfect for moments of deep thought or late-night listening sessions where every sound resonates in the silence.

About the Artists

C.A.R. (Chloé Raunet) is an electronic artist known for her ability to blend cold wave, post-punk, and minimal synth into a distinct, introspective sound. Her vocals often carry a raw, vulnerable tone, exploring themes of solitude and self-reflection.

Roman Flügel, a legend in the electronic music world, is celebrated for his ability to create lush, experimental soundscapes that push the boundaries of electronic music. His remixes, like his original work, are meticulous, often leaning towards minimalism and deep atmospheric exploration.

Song Summary

Roman Flügel’s remix of “Idle Eyes” opens with a subtle, almost imperceptible pulse, drawing listeners in with its minimalist approach. The track unfolds slowly, layering delicate synth lines and pulsating beats that give the remix its hypnotic and atmospheric feel. Each sound is deliberate, creating a meditative, dreamlike space for the listener to get lost in.
