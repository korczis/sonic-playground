Artist Background

Mélissa Laveaux is a Canadian singer-songwriter and guitarist recognized for her distinctive blend of folk, indie rock, and Haitian-influenced sounds. Her music delves into themes of identity, heritage, and personal experiences, showcasing her unique vocal style and innovative guitar work.

Original Song Context

"Needle in the Hay," originally written and performed by Elliott Smith in 1995, is known for its stark, minimalist arrangement. The song explores deeply personal themes, focusing on struggles with addiction and feelings of isolation.

Genre and Mood

Laveaux's rendition of "Needle in the Hay" firmly resides within the indie folk and alternative genres, drawing from her diverse musical influences. The mood resonates with melancholy and introspection, honoring the emotional depth of the original while infusing it with Laveaux's distinctive artistic perspective.
