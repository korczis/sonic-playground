Luca Malina brings the energy with his track FCKRZ. This electrifying electronic piece infuses deep house rhythms with vibrant melodies, encapsulating the essence of nightlife and the thrill of the dance floor.

"FCKRZ" showcases Luca Malina's talent for creating infectious beats and captivating soundscapes. The track is designed to immerse listeners in the euphoric atmosphere of club culture, inviting them to lose themselves in the music and dance.
