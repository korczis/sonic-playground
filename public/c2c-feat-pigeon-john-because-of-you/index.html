Genre and Mood

C2C collaborates with Pigeon John to create an uplifting fusion of hip-hop, electronic, and soul in Because of You. Known for their genre-blending style, C2C delivers a feel-good anthem, while Pigeon John's engaging vocals bring warmth and sincerity to the track. The song’s joyful energy and catchy rhythm make it a celebration of gratitude and positivity.

Song Summary

Because of You explores themes of appreciation and positive influence. The lyrics highlight the power of connection and the impact people can have on each other’s lives. Through vibrant production and heartfelt lyrics, C2C and Pigeon John craft a track that resonates with anyone who has felt inspired or supported by others.

Lyrics Breakdown

Intro: Setting the Vibe

The song opens with an infectious groove that combines rhythmic beats and soulful instrumentation. This upbeat intro sets a welcoming tone, inviting listeners to feel the energy and embrace the song’s joyful message.

Verse 1: Recognizing the Impact of Others

In the first verse, Pigeon John’s smooth delivery emphasizes gratitude and the importance of personal connections. His laid-back yet expressive vocals bring warmth, reflecting on how the people around us can shape who we are.

Chorus: The Heartfelt Declaration

The chorus is a celebration of appreciation, as Pigeon John sings “because of you” with conviction. The catchy hook and C2C’s skillful production create a euphoric feel, reinforcing the song’s message of gratitude and joy.

Verse 2: Embracing the Journey

The second verse dives deeper into the journey of self-discovery and growth through meaningful relationships. Pigeon John’s lyrics capture moments of reflection, while the upbeat rhythm maintains a positive, forward-moving energy.

Bridge: A Moment of Reflection

In the bridge, the music slows slightly, creating a space for contemplation. This pause allows listeners to absorb the message, reflecting on the people who have made a difference in their lives. The soulful instrumentation adds an emotional layer to this reflective moment.

Final Chorus: A Joyful Celebration

The final chorus returns with renewed energy, celebrating connection and gratitude with full intensity. The track’s spirited finish leaves listeners feeling uplifted, as the message of gratitude and joy resonates long after the song ends.
