# The owner's answer of 2026-11-16

Asked by Claude Code in session `7e1d03a3-52cc-40aa-a61a-4f9713c27f2a` on 2026-11-16, after
the owner sent again their request for "visualizace ala winamp v pozadi" when the full-screen
visualiser and the hero picture (2d755364) were already live. The question and answer are quoted
as given.

## Where the visualiser runs

- Question: "The visualiser is live (?viz=tunnel; on the landing page behind the hero, 1024 px
  and wider). What is still missing for you?"
- Options offered:
  - "Behind every page while playing";
  - "I can't see it";
  - "Stronger in the hero";
  - "It's fine as it is".
- Answer: "Behind every page while playing". The option's description read: "While music plays,
  a subtle visualiser runs in the background of the whole site (not just the hero), off by
  default or on, unobtrusive."
- Consequence: a background layer behind every page's content draws a calm mode while the
  player plays and fades out when it stops.
  - It never covers the video, the cards or the controls.
  - A switch in the visualiser's settings turns it off.
  - It stays off for readers who ask for reduced motion.
