## Backlog songs no source describes, and the sound-alike release

- The open question, put to the owner by the history lane: should the backlog songs that no
  source describes be skipped, or written as pages that say no source describes their sound?
  The same question covered "The Hanging Tree - The Hanging Tree" (yearbook 2017 #100, playlist
  2023-06-13 #191), a sound-alike release on Blue Charts Records rather than the film recording.
- Answer: "write all missing pages, prodcution quality".
- Consequence: every pending backlog song gets one canonical page, the sound-alike included, as
  its own recording. Production quality does not license invention:
  - every fact, facet and description rests on a cited source;
  - genre comes from the release's own classification;
  - feelings come from attributed descriptions of the album, label copy or the lyrics' theme;
  - the page says plainly what no source describes;
  - `edu_exempt` is set where no concept or listening guide can be grounded.
