## Genre and Mood

**Dapayk & Padberg** with **Caro** made **Island** for an album that Resident Advisor described as a blend of experimental pop and minimal grooves, by a couple it called minimal-pop. Discogs files the record under minimal and glitch. That is the space Island lives in: the sparse, patient frame of German minimal techno with a song inside it, a combination that invites a hypnotic, dreamy and intimate kind of listening.

## Song Summary

Island is the eighth of ten tracks on Black Beauty and runs five minutes and forty-seven seconds. The writing is credited to Niklas Worgt and Eva Padberg, the two halves of the duo, together with Randy Jones, the producer behind the alias Caro, who appears as featured artist. On this album, according to Resident Advisor, Worgt and Padberg take turns at the microphone themselves, so the guest credit marks Island out from the tracks around it. The title, an island, suggests something small and self-contained, which suits the minimal frame.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Album Version

The version embedded here is the album recording, provided to YouTube by the label's distributor: the original of 2007, before any remix.

### The Remixes

In 2008 Nôze remixed Island for Black Beauty Rmxs, Part 2, and that version went on to DJ compilations. In 2010 a remix by Florian Meindl appeared on Dapayk's retrospective Decade One, which Textura described as a thoroughly gyroscopic treatment.

## Concepts in Focus

The credit "feat. Caro" marks a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: a guest invited onto someone else's record. Caro is an alias of Randy Jones, a co-founder of the label Orac, and the album credits him as a co-writer of Island. A featured credit tells the listener that the track is a meeting, not only the duo's own work.

Island is also a good case for the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. Nôze's version took on a life of its own: Will Saul mixed it into his Balance 015 compilation in 2009, and it reappeared on collections such as Late Night Sessions 2012. A remix gives an album track a second career in clubs, often a longer one than the original. Compare how Will Saul's own remix work in {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-mnek-will-saul-ready-for-your-love-close-remix.md" /> }} stretches a song into a long club journey.

## Artistic Context

Dapayk is the artist name of Niklas Worgt, born in 1978 in Bad Frankenhausen, a producer counted among the protagonists of German minimal techno, who calls his own sound "Frickel" and founded the label Mo's Ferry Productions in 2000. Dapayk & Padberg is his project with the model Eva Padberg, whom he married in 2006; their debut album Close Up came out in 2005. Black Beauty, their second, was released on Mo's Ferry on 24 September 2007 on triple vinyl and CD.

## Impact and Reception

Between the original and its remixes, Island has had a long life in clubs and on compilations: a shorter version of the original reappeared on the compilation City Lounge 10 in 2013, and Nôze's remix on mixes from Balance 015 to Late Night Sessions 2012.
