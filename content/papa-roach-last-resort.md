## Genre and Mood

**Papa Roach**'s **Last Resort** is the song that, in Screen Rant's account, helped carry nu metal to a wider audience in 2000. It is intense and raw, a three-minute outburst, and for many listeners it has also been cathartic: a song about the worst moment of a person's life that crowds now shout back together.

## Song Summary

The song is about suicidal thoughts. Jacoby Shaddix wrote it about one of his best friends, a roommate who attempted suicide around 1997 or 1998, was hospitalised and later changed his life. Shaddix wrote it in the first person, and many listeners assumed it was about him. He has since said that years later the song came to describe his own struggle as well. Musically it began with bassist Tobin Esperance, who wrote its central riff on piano before the band moved it to guitar.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Shaddix wanted this song, unlike the band's others, to open with a vocal rather than a riff, so the voice comes before the guitars.

### Main Section

Esperance described what the band were after as a funky hip-hop groove with a punk rock chorus, and named Wu-Tang Clan, the Fugees and East Coast hip-hop as influences. The verses carry the groove: Jerry Horton on guitar, Esperance on bass, Dave Buckner on drums.

### Chorus

The chorus is the punk rock half of that plan, the part Louder calls anthemic.

### Outro

No source describes how the recording ends; the album version runs about 3:20.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} shows how much an instrument changes an idea. On piano, Shaddix said, it sounded like a classical piece; on guitar, with a beat, it became the hook of a rock hit. Some people heard it as close to the bridge of Iron Maiden's Genghis Khan; Shaddix has answered that he was not an Iron Maiden fan as a kid.

The song is also a clear case of {{ <concept_link name="verse-chorus form" /> }} built on contrast: a groove borrowed from hip-hop in the verses, a shouted punk chorus that releases it.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Marcos Siega, shows the band playing on a floor surrounded by about 500 fans, cut with fans alone in their rooms, a move from lonely despair to a shared live show.

## Artistic Context

Papa Roach formed in 1993 and released three EPs and an album before a 1999 demo helped them sign to DreamWorks. They recorded Last Resort at NRG Studios in Hollywood with producer Jay Baumgardner. It came out on 7 March 2000 as their debut single, on the Ready to Rumble soundtrack and then on Infest in April.

## Impact and Reception

Last Resort topped Billboard's Modern Rock chart for seven weeks, reached number three in the UK and is certified six times platinum in the US. Shaddix has said thousands of people have told him it saved their life. If its subject is close to you, you do not have to carry it alone: talk to someone you trust or a local crisis line. Then listen to it as Shaddix has described it, a cry for help.
