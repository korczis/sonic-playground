## Genre and Mood

**The Supermen Lovers** meet **Jay Lamar** and **Jesse Oliver** on **Moments (Jay Lamar & Jesse Oliver Remix)**, a rework that pulls a French house song towards deep house with a disco glow. The mood is nostalgic, dreamy and quietly groovy: music for the hour when the party has thinned out and the people still dancing are the ones who mean it.

## Song Summary

Moments, as its title suggests, is about holding on to fleeting time, and the Helsinki duo's remix stretches that idea across almost six minutes. Instead of punching up the original, they slow the pulse of attention: warm chords linger, the bass rounds off every corner and the melodic hooks return like memories rather than announcements. It is a remix that values atmosphere over impact, and it is better for it.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix follows a patient deep house arc, introducing its elements one at a time and letting each settle before the next arrives.

### Intro

A steady four-on-the-floor kick and crisp hats set the tempo, with a soft pad hovering in the background like light through a curtain.

### Build-up

The bassline enters, round and supple, and the groove gains a gentle swing. Small percussion details add movement without raising the temperature.

### Main Section

The heart of the remix brings in the song's melodic material, warm chords and the familiar hook drifting over the groove, recast in softer, hazier colours.

### Bridge

A breakdown strips the beat away and lets the chords bloom on their own, a brief, wistful moment of stillness that suits the song's theme.

### Climax

The kick returns under the full arrangement, the chords and bass locking together for the track's most euphoric passage, still unhurried but fully alive.

### Outro

The layers thin out gradually, leaving the rhythm for DJs to mix out of, the melody echoing just a little longer.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} that changes genre without changing the song. Guillaume Atlan's original belongs to French disco-funk; Jay Lamar and Jesse Oliver keep its melodic material and rebuild everything around it as deep house. Play it straight after {{ <track_link source="the-supermen-lovers-moments.md" /> }} and listen for what moved: the tempo of attention, the softness of the chords, the length.

The new frame is a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}: a kick on every beat, hi-hats between them and a bassline that bounces against the kick. Deep house plays this pattern softly and warmly, so it rocks rather than pushes, which is exactly the mood the song's title asks for.

The emotional centre is the {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }}. When the beat drops away, only the chords remain, and a track about fleeting moments briefly stops moving. The return of the kick then feels like time starting again.

## Artistic Context

The Supermen Lovers is the project of the Paris-born French producer Guillaume Atlan, who founded the label Lafessé Records in 1999 and is best known for the 2001 French house hit Starlight, with Mani Hoffman, which reached number 2 in both France and the UK. Moments was a 2013 single, released on 29 June 2013 on Lafessé Records and La Tebwa, with Atlan credited as producer, composer and lyricist and a package of remixes that included this version by Jay Lamar and Jesse Oliver, a DJ and production duo from Helsinki. Their take connects Atlan's filter-house heritage to the warmer, deeper house sound that dominated clubs at the time.

## Impact and Reception

The remix found its audience in DJ sets and deep house playlists rather than on the charts, and it has aged gracefully because it never chased a trend too hard. It is on this playground for its sense of warmth and time slowing down. Put it on as the evening turns late and let it carry you somewhere gentle.
