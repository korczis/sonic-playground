## Genre and Mood

**Lilja Bloom** sings Mother, and **Parov Stelar** remixes it here as **Mother (Parov Stelars Cry and Dance Remix)**. Discogs files the remix release under jazzdance, broken beat, progressive house and future jazz, which this page sums up as nu-jazz and house. The feelings, bittersweet and groovy, are taken from the remix's own title, crying and dancing at once, not from any review: no critic is known to have written about it.

## Song Summary

Mother was a 2010 single on Etage Noir Recordings, the label Parov Stelar founded in 2003. The original runs about three and a half minutes, and Discogs files it under downtempo. Later that year Etage Noir released Mother (The Parov Stelar Remixes): her original, his Piano Dub of about three minutes, and this Cry and Dance Remix, at 4:47 the longest of the three. The YouTube release credits the production to Marcus Füreder, Parov Stelar's real name. No lyric source, interview or review describes what the song is about, so this page does not guess.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts, usually in another producer's hands. Here the producer is the best-known name on the label, and he made two remixes for one release, each pointing somewhere else: one named after a piano, one named after crying and dancing.

The other remix's title, Piano Dub, carries a word with a long history. {{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }} began in Jamaican studios, where engineers stripped records to drums and bass and played with echo and drop-outs; dance music later borrowed the word for its stripped-back versions. No source says what the Piano Dub contains, but hearing it beside the Cry and Dance Remix shows how far two reworkings of one song can drift apart.

## Artistic Context

Parov Stelar, born Marcus Füreder in 1974 and raised in Linz, is counted among the pioneers of electro swing; his two-part album Coco of 2009 was his breakthrough. Lilja Bloom is the stage name of Barbara Lichtenauer. Wikipedia notes that she and Stelar were married and co-founded the design label Stelarbloom. Discogs credits her voice on his records from Shine in 2007 through Coco to Step Two in 2017, and lists More And More, her own 2014 single on Etage Noir. The YouTube release also names Marcus and Barbara Füreder alongside the two artists, without giving their roles.

## Impact and Reception

No chart entry or review of the remix has been found. It has had a life on compilations: Discogs lists it on Style Up Your Life! in 2012, and Etage Noir issued the release again digitally in 2016 under the plain title Mother. Play the original, then the Piano Dub, then this remix, and listen to one song travel from downtempo towards the dance floor.
