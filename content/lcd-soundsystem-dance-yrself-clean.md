## Genre and Mood

**LCD Soundsystem**'s **Dance Yrself Clean** is dance-punk at its most patient: an indie dance and electronic track that spends its first minutes barely there and then lets go. It is cathartic, euphoric and intense, but it earns those feelings slowly; the whole design is the distance between a murmur and a release.

## Song Summary

The track runs 8:56 and opens This Is Happening. James Murphy wrote and produced it, and the album credits him alone on it, for vocals, guitar and drums. The title is the idea: to sweat something out on the floor. Rolling Stone's Jody Rosen turned the phrase around, arguing that in Murphy's songs your crises follow you onto the dance floor however hard you try. Paste's Ross Bonaime noted that it opens so softly you have to turn the volume up to hear it.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Reviewers heard a small, almost naive vocal melody over unhurried synth-bass, tinny percussion and 8-bit synth curlicues. The NZ Herald's Jacqueline Smith called it "a nice little serenade"; The Quietus thought it suggested a rather twee new direction.

### Main Section

In the NZ Herald's words, "at the three-minute mark it drops into electro-rock so thick it makes your platelets change shape". SPIN heard a speaker-lacerating modular synth blast; The Quietus a colossal bass-synth bounce, strained falsetto vocals and a slow-motion disco undertow.

### Outro

No source describes how the final minutes unfold, so this review does not either. Paste wrote that the track builds and then abruptly pulls the volume back down, and Stereogum remembers crowds losing themselves in its cathartic breaks.

## Concepts in Focus

Dance Yrself Clean takes the {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} to an extreme. The quiet opening is a section in its own right, about three minutes long, so the arrival of the full band feels like a door blown open. Stereogum called the song "a masterful exercise in building and releasing tension".

The burst rests on a {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }}, a drum pattern built from broken, syncopated rhythms rather than a steady kick on every beat. Chris Power, reviewing the album for the BBC, heard the song turn into a gigantic block-rocker driven by one.

Over it Murphy reaches into a strained {{ <concept_link name="falsetto" /> }}, as The Quietus heard it. Compare the half-spoken delivery critics heard in {{ <track_link source="freeland-under-control.md" /> }}, a 2009 single measured against Murphy's style.

## Artistic Context

LCD Soundsystem formed in Brooklyn in 2002 around James Murphy and have been signed to his label, DFA Records, since the start. This Is Happening was recorded from April 2009 to February 2010 at the Mansion in Los Angeles and at DFA in New York, and released on 17 May 2010. It became the band's first US top-ten album. In 2011 they disbanded with a farewell show at Madison Square Garden; they reunited in 2015.

## Impact and Reception

Kid Cudi sampled the track in "All Talk" soon after release. Critics named it among the best songs of 2010 and of the decade: Paste ranked it number one of the 2010s, and Billboard's staff placed it eighth among the decade's dance songs. Start it quietly, as Paste advises, and let the third minute surprise you.
