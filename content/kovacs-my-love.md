## Genre and Mood

**Kovacs** made **My Love** the calling card of a voice. It sits where the Dutch critic Ruud de Zwart placed her whole debut album: a penetrating mix of soul, jazz, trip-hop and pop noir, songs that seem to come straight out of films. The mood is dark and soulful, and the lyric of a singer turning away a lover who is too late gives it its defiance.

## Song Summary

My Love was Sharon Kovacs's first single, released in 2014 as the title track of her first EP, which she recorded with the producer Oscar Holleman and, according to English Wikipedia, partly in Cuba. The writing credits name both of them; Holleman produced and mixed it and Kovacs co-produced. Dutch Wikipedia adds the detail that makes the song a lesson: it contains parts of El Arreglito, the habanera by the Spanish composer Sebastián Iradier that Bizet adapted for Carmen. The next year the song took second place on Shades of Black, her debut album.

The album's credits list Holleman on drums, percussion and bass, Ruud de Groot on guitar and Wouter Hardy on piano.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="interpolation" /> }} means re-performing a borrowed tune rather than sampling a recording. El Arreglito was published in 1863; Bizet took it for Carmen's Habanera believing it was a folk song, and only added a note on its source when told its composer had died ten years before. My Love borrows from the same song a century and a half later.

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} can also be a player. Holleman, known for his work with Within Temptation and Krezip, recorded and mixed the album at the RS29 studio and played this song's rhythm section himself. Kovacs had won him over with a link to her version of a Grace Jones song.

{{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} shapes the mood: in Festivalinfo's hearing the strings give the singles their atmosphere, with piano and guitar around the voice. Compare {{ <track_link source="amy-winehouse-back-to-black.md" /> }}: both Dutch sources compare her voice with Winehouse's.

## Artistic Context

Sharon Kovacs was born in 1990 in Baarlo and studied at the Rock City Institute in Eindhoven, graduating in 2013. She names Betty Davis, Etta James and Tina Turner as influences. Shades of Black came out on 3 April 2015 through Wolf Recordings, licensed to Warner Music outside the Benelux, and two more albums followed, Cheap Smell in 2018 and Child of Sin in 2023.

## Impact and Reception

My Love reached number 5 on the Dutch Tipparade and number 32 in the Single Top 100, became a 3FM Megahit and, Maxazine reported, topped iTunes in Greece. In 2014 Kovacs was named a 3FM Serious Talent. Shades of Black entered the Dutch album chart at number one, stayed 56 weeks and won an Edison for best newcomer.
