## Genre and Mood

**Mando Diao**'s **Dance With Somebody (The Salazar Brothers Remix)** is the dance-floor version of a song that already wanted to be one. Discogs files the 2009 promo that carried it, alongside remixes by Tocadisco and Azzido Da Bass, under electronic music and house, and it files two of the compilations that picked the remix up under house as well. No published review describes the remix itself, so its feelings here, energetic and longing, rest on those placements and on the song's own plea for someone to dance with, not on a listener's account of this mix.

## Song Summary

The remix runs 4:57, almost a minute longer than the 4:01 radio version and a little shorter than the 5:19 album version. Its credits keep the whole band: Björn Dixgård and Gustaf Norén on vocals and guitar, Carl-Johan Fogelklou on bass guitar, Mats Björke on keyboards, Samuel Giers on drums, Kerstin Thörn on violin and Pauline Kamusewu on backing vocals. The Salazar Brothers are credited as remixers, mixers and sound engineers. No source describes what they kept, cut or added, and this page does not guess.

## Concepts in Focus

Most {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} credits go to an outsider who takes a finished song somewhere new. This one is unusual: the Salazar Brothers had already produced the original. Wikipedia names them as Salla Salazar of the Swedish hip-hop group The Latin Kings and his brother Masse, the producers of the whole album Give Me Fire!, recorded in 2008 at Studio De la Santa and Redline Studio in Stockholm. Here the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} returns to the same song in a second role.

The remix then lived largely on the {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}. MusicBrainz lists it on Clubland Volume 3 in Germany in 2009, and Discogs on Taneční Liga 114 in the Czech Republic that year and on The Disco Boys Volume 10 in Germany in 2010.

## Artistic Context

Dance with Somebody was released on 9 January 2009 as the lead single from Give Me Fire!, an album Wikipedia describes as danceable, with a strong focus on drums, bass and percussion. It became the band's most successful single, reaching number one in Austria and number two in Germany. The radio version has its own page: {{ <track_link source="mando-diao-dance-with-somebody-radio-version.md" /> }}.

No chart run or review of the remix is documented. Its reach is the record of where it was released and compiled, from a Swedish promo disc to German and Czech dance compilations. Put it next to the original and decide which one you would rather dance to.
