## Genre and Mood

**Trauma**, credited as Traum:a on the original single, gives **Sunny - Extended Mix** the deep house treatment, in the version made for the dance floor rather than for radio. On the surface the mood is relaxed and carefree, a summer record. Underneath runs the nostalgia that comes with any new reading of a song this old and this well known.

## Song Summary

Sunny is a soul-jazz standard that Bobby Hebb wrote in 1963 and released as a single in June 1966. It is one of the most performed popular songs, with hundreds of versions. Hebb wrote it after his older brother was stabbed to death outside a Nashville nightclub, though he later said the song simply chose a sunny disposition over a lousy one. Traum:a keeps the song and changes its setting: a steady house pulse, a summery glow, and a saxophone that listeners on austriancharts.at singled out, some fondly and some less so. The extended mix runs 5:26, against 3:15 for the radio edit.

## Musical Breakdown

The official video carries the shorter radio edit; the extended mix plays the same song with more room around it.

### Intro

A house version is built from the beat up, and Hebb's melody sits on top of that pulse.

### Main Section

The familiar tune does most of the work: it is recognisable within a few bars, so the arrangement can stay light.

### Outro

The extended mix gives the ending time to run on, the space a DJ needs to bring in the next record.

## Concepts in Focus

Sunny is a clear lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }}. A cover can be faithful or it can move a song into another genre, and this one moves it to the dance floor. What survives is the melody; what changes is the pulse, the instruments and the setting. Compare how {{ <track_link source="faul-wad-pnau-changes.md" /> }} built a saxophone-lit house groove around another artist's song in the same years.

The {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} exists for clubs. The single offered six versions: radio edit, extended mix and two remixes each by Mario Fiebiger and Arts & Leni, each in a short and a long form. The extended mix runs about two minutes longer than the radio edit. That length lets a DJ mix in and out without cutting into the song.

## Artistic Context

Traum:a's Sunny came out on 14 March 2014 on Planet Punk Music, with Sony Music Entertainment Germany holding the recording copyright. Discogs files the release under the deep house, house and tech house styles, and also lists the act as Trauma. The song travelled on compilations: The Dome Vol. 69, Club Sounds Best Of 2014, #afterhour Volume 1 and KuschelLounge 3, among others.

## Impact and Reception

It did not chart in Austria, but it reached number 71 in the Netherlands, where it stayed four weeks, and number 187 in France. The listeners who rated it on austriancharts.at were split, and two of the five comments shown there are about the saxophone. It is here as a summer record that does not try too hard. Play the extended mix outdoors and let Hebb's old tune do the rest.
