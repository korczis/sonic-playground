## Genre and Mood

**Dead or Alive**'s **You Spin Me Round (Like a Record)** in its **Murder Mix** is the same Hi-NRG anthem, synth-pop and disco driven by machines, stretched to about eight minutes for the dancefloor. It is energetic, bold and theatrical, and here it has room to keep going.

## Song Summary

The You Spin Me Round Murder Mix is not a remix by an outside producer but the extended version made inside the hit factory itself. Mike Stock's own list of the song's versions gives the 7-inch at 3:19 and the Murder Mix at 8:02, both mixed by Phil Harding, who also engineered the track for Stock Aitken Waterman. It was the A-side of the original UK 12-inch, Epic TX4861, backed with an extended Misty Circles, and it closes the 2010 compilation That's the Way I Like It.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is a longer version made for clubs and DJs rather than radio. In 1984 its home was the 12-inch single, and according to the Irish Daily Star's 2020 profile of Harding, seventy percent of this single's sales were on the 12-inch, a reminder that for many buyers this was the record.

The craft behind it is {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }}. Harding, who often worked in partnership with Ian Curnow on PWL's remixes, described a huge difference between a 7-inch radio edit and the approach taken with the dancefloor version, and the same article calls the Murder Mix a masterpiece that has stood the test of time.

Behind both stands {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Harding recalls that Pete Waterman wanted records made for the clubs as much as for pop radio, because records could be broken in the clubs and the mix had to be right for DJs.

## Artistic Context

The single came out on Epic in November 1984 and took seventeen weeks to reach number one in the UK in March 1985, Stock Aitken Waterman's first. The Murder Mix kept circulating: Epic's later 12-inch issues of 1985, 1989 and 1997 all lead with it, the last one paired with Brand New Lover. A later Performance Mix of about seven and a half minutes went onto a separate remix 12-inch.

## Impact and Reception

The Murder Mix is the version the 12-inch buyers took home, and the work the Irish Daily Star's profile picks out as Harding's craft. Give it the full eight minutes, ideally loud, and hear how much longer a song can spin.
