## Genre and Mood

**Ryan Blyth** delivers an energising house track with **Once Twice**, featuring **Rachel K Collier**. It is bright and uplifting, a sung house record that is short, direct and built to make a floor move.

## Song Summary

The track pairs Blyth's beat with Collier's voice. It was premiered in June 2016 on Love & Other, a channel for deep, bass and electronic music, and the video version runs under three minutes, so it gets to the point quickly: groove, voice and a repeated hook.

## Lyrics Breakdown

### Intro: Setting the Tone

The song opens with bright beats that set the tempo, and Collier's voice soon introduces the melody.

### Verse 1: The Power of Connection

In the first verse the vocal and the rhythm settle into each other, the topline riding the groove.

### Chorus: The Uplifting Anthem

The chorus brings the title phrase as the hook, the moment the track is built around.

### Verse 2: Embracing the Moment

The second verse returns to the groove with the same light touch.

### Bridge: A Reflective Interlude

A softer passage shifts the focus to melody before the final chorus.

### Final Chorus: Celebrating Life

The last chorus returns with the full beat and ends the track at its brightest.

## Concepts in Focus

Once Twice is a typical {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} record. The producer, Ryan Blyth, owns the track; the singer, Rachel K Collier, brings the topline, the melody and words that sit on top of the beat. Ableton's profile of Collier notes that she worked as a topline writer and featured artist early in her career, including on Mat Zo's work that brought her a UK top 20 single and a Grammy nomination, before becoming known as a producer and live performer in her own right.

Underneath is a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}: a steady kick on every beat and percussion on the off-beats, a pulse designed to carry a voice without competing with it.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the title phrase, repeated in the chorus so that it sticks after one listen. In a track this short, the hook arrives early and returns often; compare the way a featured singer's line carries {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-mnek-will-saul-ready-for-your-love-close-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Rachel K Collier is an electronic music producer, vocalist and performer from Swansea in Wales. She has worked in the commercial music industry since 2013, uses Ableton's Push 2 as the heart of her live setup, teaches as an Ableton Certified Trainer, and has performed at SXSW and the BBC's Biggest Weekend.
