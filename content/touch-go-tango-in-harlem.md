## Genre and Mood

**Touch & Go**'s **Tango In Harlem** sits in the lounge-jazz world of the London project's debut album, a record Discogs files under big beat, Latin and jazzdance. Its mood here is playful and humorous, and both words rest on the lyric rather than on any review: a frightening story that ends on a joke.

## Song Summary

Tango In Harlem is a track from I Find You Very Attractive, released by V2 in 1999; Wikipedia also lists it as a single of 2001. The YouTube art track credits David Lowe and Julia Fischer as writers, and Musixmatch gives them in full as David Hodson Lowe and Julia Lara Fischer. Discogs credits Julia Lara with the vocal. The lyric, as Musixmatch transcribes it, is an anecdote: walking home one snowy evening to Riverside Drive in Harlem, a young woman who dreamed of dancing finds a knife at her throat at her own door, with fifty dollars to survive on. It closes on a deadpan turn, praising the neighbourhood's muffins and bagels.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes Tango In Harlem on its own, and none describes it part by part. What follows rests on the transcription, the credits and the label's description of the album.

### The Story

The words tell one incident in order, from the walk home to the knife, with the name Harlem returning again and again.

### The Voice

The transcription is full of false starts and fillers, which suggests a told story rather than a sung melody; how Julia Lara delivers it, no source says.

### The Arrangement

Discogs credits Steve Mellor on saxophone and Sovra Wilson-Dixon on violin for this track, and David Lowe on keyboards, drums and bass across the album.

### The Ending

The story stops on the bagels: the joke is the last thing the transcription records.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="spoken word" /> }} vocal is talked rather than sung. A transcription can only hint at it: here the stammered openings and the asides read like someone remembering aloud. Listen and decide whether Tango In Harlem is spoken, sung, or something between, and compare it with Louie Austen's told tour of the world in {{ <track_link source="louie-austen-one-night-in-rio.md" /> }}.

Touch & Go is the work of a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} more than of a band. Acidjazz.ru calls it a project conceived and masterminded by David Lowe, who writes the material, produces, arranges and plays drums, keyboards and most of the bass, while singers and players who are not officially members fill in the rest. The credits of this track show the same pattern: Lowe's production, a guest vocalist who co-wrote it, and a saxophonist and violinist brought in for it.

## Artistic Context

**Touch & Go** came out of London in 1998; Wikipedia names Vanessa Lancaster, James Lynch, David Lowe and Gordon Nelki and calls the music jazz pop. Acidjazz.ru describes I Find You Very Attractive as retro-lounge jazz, swing and Latin-Caribbean sounds combined with modern dance beats and samples, its songs often built on come-on lines. Lowe's background was writing music for television and commercials.

## Impact and Reception

The project's hit was its first single, Would You...?, a number three in the UK in 1998; no source records a chart placing for Tango In Harlem. Acidjazz.ru notes that much of the album was later licensed to films, television and advertising. Listen for the story first, then for the band around it.
