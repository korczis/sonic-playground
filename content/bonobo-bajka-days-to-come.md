## Genre and Mood

**Bonobo** and **Bajka** give the album **Days To Come** its title track and its first voice. Wikipedia files the album under electronica, nu jazz, trip hop and ambient, and Bonobo's own Bandcamp tags it downtempo and chill out. Basic Soul heard in it a much deeper jazz flavour than before and named the title track an outstanding example. The feelings here follow the album's own description, big, brooding, emotional and uplifting, and the soul critics heard in Bajka's voice.

## Song Summary

Days To Come is the second track of the album, after a short intro, and runs 3:50. Simon Green, who records as Bonobo, composed and arranged it and produced it; Bajka wrote the lyric and sings lead, the first of her four songs on the record. MusicBrainz credits Green on bass guitar, keyboards, percussion and xylophone, with Ben Cooke as a guest on saxophone. The limited edition added a second disc with an instrumental version of the track.

## Musical Breakdown

No review walks through the title track part by part, so this page draws no map of it and claims no tempo or key. What the reviews describe is the album around it: The Skinny heard clarinets, oboes, horns, flutes and a multitude of drums and keys, few samples and full beats with traces of hip-hop; Exclaim heard electronic rhythms fused with rich bass, horns and loose snares. The Italian site Debaser called the title track so powerful that you risk hitting repeat instead of going on.

## Concepts in Focus

Bajka is more than a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} added to a finished beat: MusicBrainz credits her as the lyricist, and Basic Soul heard her voice on this track as an outstanding example of the album's deeper jazz. Hear her again on {{ <track_link source="radio-citizen-bajka-summer-days.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="reverb" /> }} is the quality The Skinny singled out: Green lets the instruments breathe, with natural echo and reverb around them, and his stated aim was an album that sounded as if it had been recorded in one room, in one day.

The instrumental is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} that lets you hear the arrangement without the voice and judge how much the song rests on her.

## Artistic Context

Days To Come, Bonobo's third studio album, came out on Ninja Tune on 2 October 2006. Bandcamp's note says Green wrote, played and manipulated nearly every note himself. Bajka, the German artist born Bajka Pluwatsch in India in 1978, raised in Portugal and South Africa and based in Berlin, is a poet as well as a singer; The Skinny praised the clear-eyed positivism of her lyrics.

## Impact and Reception

The Skinny called the vocals stunning, Exclaim said her smoky voice lent its raspy soul to four tunes with positive results, and Prefix Magazine gave the album nine out of ten. In 2012 it was certified silver by IMPALA for 20,000 copies sold across Europe. Start with the title track and see whether you can move on.
