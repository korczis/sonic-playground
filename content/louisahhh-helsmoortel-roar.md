## Genre and Mood

**Louisahhh** and **Helsmoortel** made **ROAR** for clubs, and their label Artoffact tagged its upload techno, new beat and EBM; industrial follows from Louisahhh's own work, which Chain D.L.K. called industrial rave poetry. No review describes ROAR on its own, so its feelings, defiant, intense and dark, are this page's reading of the title and of what Louisahhh said about the record it belongs to: club-ready on the surface, darker underneath.

## Song Summary

ROAR is the third of six tracks on Sugar & Violence, released by the Toronto label Artoffact on 12 September 2025; it runs 4:57, and a dub version of it, 4:52, closes the EP. Artoffact posted ROAR on YouTube in August, a month before the EP, linking to a single release of the track. The credits name both artists, Willem Helsmoortel and Louisa Pillot, as producers. The Bandcamp page carries the words; this page does not quote them.

## Concepts in Focus

## Artistic Context

Louisahhh, Louisa Pillot, a New York native now based in France, has worked for two decades as a producer, performer and DJ, from New York basements in the early 2000s to festivals such as Sónar; she co-founded the label RAAR and is a member of the five-piece Pain Magazine. Helsmoortel's biography says Belgium's new beat scene shaped him, along with boom bap, rave, jungle and drum and bass, and that he likes hard-hitting drums and distorted sounds under melodies that take hold of the floor.
