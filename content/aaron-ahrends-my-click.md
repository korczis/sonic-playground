## Genre and Mood

**Aaron Ahrends** made **My Click** for the club, at a length of nearly seven minutes that DJs can work with. Discogs files the compilation that first carried it under deep house, progressive house and techno, and Apple Music files the later EP under dance. No published review describes the track, so its two feelings, hypnotic and groovy, rest on those classifications and its length rather than on a listener's account. Read them as context, not as a description.

## Song Summary

My Click runs 6:57. It first appeared in October 2017 as the opening track of Dantze, Baby III, a ten-track compilation from the German digital label Dantze, which Discogs lists as run by the producers Philip Bader and Niconé. In March 2018 Ahrends posted an official video for it on his own channel, and in 2023 Doubt Records issued a My Click EP that carries the date and three tracks of his 2016 Focus EP, Focus, I Want You Back and Rain, with My Click added. I found no published lyrics or description of its sound, so this review does not describe whether it has a voice.

Mastering is the last step before release: the finished mix is balanced for loudness and tone so that it holds up on a club system as well as on headphones, and so that the tracks of one release sit together.

## Artistic Context

Discogs describes Aaron Ahrends as a trained sound engineer and music producer who releases electronic music under his own name and fronts the Berlin synth-electro-pop band Say Yes Dog as songwriter, bandleader and singer. The trio met while studying in The Hague; Ahrends grew up in Berlin. His solo releases began with Brief Embrace on the label &nd in 2014, and his Focus EP on Dantze in December 2016 included a remix by Niconé. His voice in the band can be heard on {{ <track_link source="claptone-say-yes-dog-before-i-lose-my-mind.md" /> }}.
