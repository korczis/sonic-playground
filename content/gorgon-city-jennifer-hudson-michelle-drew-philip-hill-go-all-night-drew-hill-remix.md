## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City**, **Jennifer Hudson** and the duo **Drew Hill** meet in **Go All Night - Drew Hill Remix**, a remix of a house single that put Hudson in the role of the classic club diva. No review describes this version, so house and electronic are the frame the song and its remix package give it, not a verdict on the track. Soulful is Hudson's part of the song; dreamy is this page's reading of how Mixmag described Drew Hill's work that year, melodic and crooning, with a delightfully cosmic remix behind them.

## Song Summary

The original Go All Night came from Gorgon City's 2014 debut album Sirens, written by the duo's Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott with Kiesza and sung by Hudson; Complex called it one of the album's biggest surprises, a soulful throwback. For the single, five remixers made versions: Wilkinson, Booka Shade, Illyus & Barrientos, Drew Hill and Aquilo. Drew Hill's runs 5:31. It came out on the remix release in November 2014, and in April 2015 it returned as track eight of Sirens (Remixes), two places after Booka Shade's version of the same song.

## Musical Breakdown

No premiere, review or interview walks through the Drew Hill remix, and this page will not invent a map of it: no tempo, no key, no account of where the vocal enters or drops out. What can be said is its frame. At 5:31 it is longer than the 3:20 single and shorter than Booka Shade's seven minutes, and the credit on the official video keeps Jennifer Hudson's name beside Gorgon City's. ## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is an answer to an original. The Go All Night package set five of them side by side, from Wilkinson's version to Booka Shade's long deep house cut, which Complex heard keep the bassline and only well-timed pieces of the vocal. Put Drew Hill's beside {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-jennifer-hudson-booka-shade-go-all-night-booka-shade-remix.md" /> }} and hear two remixers make different choices from the same parts.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} travels with the song. Every remix in the package is still credited to Gorgon City featuring Jennifer Hudson, with the remixer's name added in brackets, so the guest voice stays on the label of every version.

## Artistic Context

Drew Hill were Michelle Drew and Philipp Hill, from Denmark and Cologne; Discogs dates the duo to 2013-2017, making originals and remixes. Mixmag said their remix of Maribou State's Tongue in 2013 set the tone of things to come, and by 2014 they had released the Solitude EP on Born Electric. Gorgon City, the London duo of Gibbon and Robson-Scott, released Sirens on Virgin EMI.

## Impact and Reception

No source reviews the Drew Hill remix. The single it serves reached number 14 in the UK and number one on Billboard's Dance Club Songs chart, and the remix has lived on as part of Sirens (Remixes). Play it after the original and listen for what changed.
