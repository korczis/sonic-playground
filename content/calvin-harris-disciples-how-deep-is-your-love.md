## Genre and Mood

**Calvin Harris** and **Disciples** made **How Deep Is Your Love** a deep house song for summer radio: unhurried, sultry, hypnotic in its repetition and groovy from the first bar. Written in E minor at 122 beats per minute, it is built on restraint.

## Song Summary

The lyric is a short, sensual question asked again and again: how deep is your love, is it like the ocean. The verses ask to be breathed in and let in on every secret; the refrain answers with nothing but the question. Around it the production stays lean. Your EDM, quoted by Wikipedia, heard "bass-y plucks and piano chords", and Jezebel's Jia Tolentino called it a minimalist "full-on heater" with "a contagious playfulness in its three main hooks", crediting Disciples for its subtlety.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

No timed analysis maps the song, so this page follows the critics. In the verses the voice carries the melody over the plucked bass and piano; Will Adams of The Singles Jukebox singled out the "tubular bass" underneath.

### Chorus

The hook is where the voices multiply. Adams heard "modulated vocal samples" around the lead, and Micha Cavaseno described the vocoder part as mutated. The question is sung plainly, then bent and chopped by machines.

### Build-up

Patrick St. Michel named the shape plainly: "the build-up-to-big-release formula". Crystal Leww heard in it the groove and energy of Duke Dumont's best.

## Concepts in Focus

The song's {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }} gives it a cool, slightly shadowed colour that suits the lyric's longing, while the groove at 122 BPM never hurries.

The {{ <concept_link name="vocoder" /> }} effect lets one singer play two roles: the natural lead, which David Sheffieck said has a quaver that sells the song, and a processed echo that turns her words into texture. Listen for the difference between them in the chorus.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Emil Nava and filmed in Malibu with Gigi Hadid, follows her from party to pool to a swarm of motorcycles; it won Best Electronic Video at the 2016 MTV Video Music Awards. The player here holds the audio upload, the exact single.

## Artistic Context

Disciples, the South London trio of Nathan Duvall, Gavin Koolmon and Luke McDermott, wrote the song with the Norwegian songwriter Ina Wroldsen in early 2014, and her one-take demo vocal stayed on the record although she was not credited. The demo reached Harris through Fly Eye, his publishing company; he told KISS-FM he wanted "something a bit different" after his album Motion. Like {{ <track_link source="calvin-harris-rag-n-bone-man-giant.md" /> }}, it later joined his compilation 96 Months.

## Impact and Reception

Released on 17 July 2015, it peaked at number two in the UK, became Harris's first number one in Australia, topped the UK Dance chart and reached number 27 on the Billboard Hot 100. Not every critic was won over: Scott Mildenhall called it "pure glossy homogeny". Put it on and decide how deep it goes.
