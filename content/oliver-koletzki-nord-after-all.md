## Genre and Mood

**Oliver Koletzki** and **NÖRD**'s **After All** is electronic music built around a singer. In the label's account, the emotional voice of NÖRD's Leo Eisenach floats over heavy electronica beats, synths, creaking bass and nostalgic piano keys: indie electronic in the plain sense, a song rather than a club tool, emotional and nostalgic in the label's own words.

## Song Summary

After All is the second of thirteen tracks on I am OK, Koletzki's fifth album, which came out on 16 May 2014. The label introduces it right after the opener, Gravity: "Next up, NÖRD's Leo Eisenach delivers emotional vocals on After All", over Koletzki's "tight arrangement". Apple Music credits Koletzki as composer and producer, Eisenach, MiMi and Oded Kaydar as co-writers, and Jochen Schmalbach with the mix. No source we found discusses the lyric, so this page leaves it to you.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found walks through After All part by part, so this breakdown sticks to the elements the label names.

### Main Section

The ground is rhythmic: heavy beats and a creaking bass, with synths around them. Over that the voice is left to float, and the piano adds the nostalgic colour the label hears in it. Listen for the layering rather than for one big moment.

### Other Versions

The single added club remixes by Claptone and Kellerkind, both over six minutes long. Before it, Koletzki and Eisenach had played the song with the hr-Sinfonieorchester in Frankfurt.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} usually brings a voice to someone else's track. Here the credit goes to the band, NÖRD, while the voice belongs to its lead singer, Leo Eisenach, who also co-wrote the song. I am OK has guests on nine of its thirteen tracks, and Eisenach is the only one heard twice, on After All and on Too Soon.

An {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} decides which instruments play what, and the label calls Koletzki's tight. The song also exists in a second one: at the Music Discovery Project 2014, on 31 January and 1 February in the Jahrhunderthalle Frankfurt, the hr-Sinfonieorchester under John Axelrod played it with Koletzki and Eisenach, in arrangements by Gunter Papperitz.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a track for another use. The label's single, SVT130X, dated 4 April 2014, paired the song with versions by Claptone, heard on {{ <track_link source="claptone-clap-your-hands-say-yeah-ghost.md" /> }}, and Kellerkind.

## Artistic Context

Koletzki, born in Braunschweig in 1974, founded his label Stil vor Talent in 2005. I am OK came out there under licence to Universal Music. NÖRD is led by the siblings Paul and Leo Eisenach; Musicboard Berlin, which funded them, calls their music hyperactive pop. They finished their debut album in 2014 and in 2015 scored the Dutch film NENA, shown at the Berlinale.

## Impact and Reception

I am OK reached number 25 in Germany and Switzerland and number 53 in Austria. We found no chart record or review for After All itself; what it has is a music video, two remixes and an orchestral life. Play the album version, then the Frankfurt performance, and listen for what each arrangement does with the same voice.
