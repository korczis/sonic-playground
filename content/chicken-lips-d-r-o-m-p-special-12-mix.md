## Genre and Mood

**Chicken Lips**' **D.R.O.M.P (Special 12" Mix)** is house with disco and electro in its make-up, the styles Discogs files it under. It is groovy and playful, and the title sets the mood: the letters stand for Don't Rain On My Parade, a celebratory refusal to let anything spoil the night.

## Song Summary

D.R.O.M.P. was Chicken Lips' 2012 single for Southern Fried Records, written by Andrew Meecham, Dean Meredith and Charmaine Baines, produced by Meecham and Meredith, recorded at Lipstudio 2 and mixed at Rogue Cat Sound. The Special 12" Mix is the long version, just over eight minutes, and opens the seven-track remix release, ahead of five remixes and a radio edit of 3:35.

An eight-minute version is made for DJs: a long stretch to mix in and out of, where the radio edit has to get to the point.

### Main Section: A Funk Workout

Rocksucker heard the core of the single as a fun funk workout, a groove built for dancing rather than a song structure, and began with a question: Grace Jones if she lived in east London?

### Build-up: Clatter and Carnival

In the same review the workout flowers into a clattery percussive thing for those keeping the carnival fever going: the track grows by adding rhythm rather than melody.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is a club format: the same production stretched for a DJ, with longer intros and outros that let a groove settle. Here the radio edit runs less than half the length of this mix.

The record is also a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} package, with versions by The Emperor Machine, Andy Meecham's own side project, The Rhythm Odyssey, Robi Insinna with Headman, Amine Edge & Dance and Sona Vabos. Curiously, Discogs lists the Special 12" Mix not on the 12-inch vinyl, which carries the Emperor Machine version, the radio edit and two remixes, but first on the label's promo CD.

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the point of it all: a funk-derived pattern that keeps a dance floor moving, pushed along here, by the review's account, by percussion.

## Artistic Context

Chicken Lips are a dance music band and production team from Stafford, England. Meecham and Meredith had been members of Bizarre Inc, whose Playing with Knives reached number four, before they began as Chicken Lips in 1999, making left-field house also called disco-dub and a stream of 12-inch singles on Kingsize; Steve Kotey of Bear Funk later made them a trio. Southern Fried is the London independent founded in 1994 by Norman Cook, the producer behind {{ <track_link source="fatboy-slim-bird-of-pray.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Little has been written about D.R.O.M.P. beyond record-shop listings and one review: Rocksucker gave the single three quails out of five and found the acronym fun to say. Put the eight minutes on loud and see whether the parade stays dry.
