## Genre and Mood

**Gravagerz**'s **Chihiro** takes a Billie Eilish song onto the house floor. His agency describes his signature sound as euphoric house, blending house with UK garage and dubstep; the words he kept are full of longing, a voice asking someone to open the door and wondering what to say on coming back.

## Song Summary

Chihiro was written by Billie Eilish and her brother Finneas O'Connell for her third album, Hit Me Hard and Soft, released on 17 May 2024; Wikipedia describes the original as mid-tempo dance-pop, funk and R&B with touches of tech house, and its lyric as being about feelings of being lost and found, reminiscent of the Spirited Away character Chihiro. Gravagerz's version is a single released on 22 June 2024 that runs about two minutes. Deezer credits the composition to Eilish, Finneas and Alessandro Bruno and lists Gravagerz himself as the label.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes this recording passage by passage, so this breakdown stays with what the release and the artist's own pages say.

### The Vocal

The official release is a cover rather than a remix: according to his Insomniac and agency biographies, Gravagerz replaced the vocals of his earlier remix before putting it out as his own version. No source names the new singer.

### The Refrain

The lyric printed with the official video centres on a repeated line asking someone to open up the door, sung over and over before a short passage about waiting in the garden.

### The Length

At about two minutes and nine seconds, it is well under half the length of Eilish's original, which runs over five minutes.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks the original recording, usually keeping the original singer's voice. His biographies say it was his remix, first posted on SoundCloud, that went viral; on Bandcamp, an extended mix of it carries the album's own release date, 17 May 2024.

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} records the song anew. By replacing the vocals, he turned his remix into a cover, a new recording of Eilish and Finneas's song under his own name.

A {{ <concept_link name="self-release" /> }} needs no label in between, and Deezer gives the label as Gravagerz. Compare the original writers' earlier work in {{ <track_link source="billie-eilish-copycat.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Insomniac's artist page gives his origin as Italy. At 17, in 2020, he signed with the manager Hajriz Bajrami of Leaf Music Management; early releases included Look Back at It on B1 Recordings. After Chihiro came an official remix of Quavo and Lana Del Rey's Tough and an official cover of Disclosure and London Grammar's Help Me Lose My Mind.

## Impact and Reception

His biographies, written for promotion, say the remix went viral on TikTok and Instagram, with over 200 million combined streams and 1.2 million creators using the track. Eilish's own Chihiro was released to Italian radio as a single only in January 2025. Play the two side by side.
