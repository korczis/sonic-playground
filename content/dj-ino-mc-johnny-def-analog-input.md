## Genre and Mood

**DJ Ino** and **MC Johnny Def** made **Analog Input** for the deep-house floor, and the genres here are theirs: the track sits on a compilation called 25 Years of Deep House Revolution. Groovy and hypnotic are what that music aims for. No review of this track could be found, so this page describes its setting more than its sound and leaves the listening to you.

## Song Summary

Analog Input is an original mix of a little over six minutes. DJ Ino's own upload credits the writing and production to Ino Planes and Joao Nathis, for the label Be Adult Music. MusicBrainz lists the track on Nassau Beach Club Ibiza 2017, an April 2017 compilation, and in July 2020 it became one of 25 tracks on 25 Years of Deep House Revolution; a C Rock remix followed on a remix album that September. The title points to analog equipment, but no source says how the track was made.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady, rolling pulse that lets a dance floor move for hours. Deep house keeps that pulse but softens what surrounds it; DJ Ino's NTS profile traces his style to jazz, soul and funk. Test that by ear here.

{{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} made both men. Ino opened House Café in 1997, described as the first all-night house club in northern Spain, and in 2000 opened Telefunken in A Coruña with DJ Grobas. Johnny Def started in 1994 as resident warm-up DJ, light jockey and master of ceremonies at Rock's Club in Vila Nova de Gaia, then was master of ceremonies at Lisbon's Paradise Garage after-hours from 2002 to 2004. An MC is the voice of the night.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} can be a self-portrait. In August 2020 Ino told Music Is 4 Lovers he had given exactly 25 years to deep house; the compilation marks that span.

## Artistic Context

DJ Ino was born in the early 1970s in north-western Spain, played guitar in local bands, began collecting house records in 1992 and founded the label House Café Music. MC Johnny Def is described as one of the underground founding fathers of electronic dance music in Portugal; he also performs live as the Acid Driver, mixing acid, deep, classic and Chicago house. The pair worked together often: Def sings on the Deep Site album We Were Better In The Future, and their Love Is In The Air was licensed through Be Adult Music.
