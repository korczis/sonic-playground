## Genre and Mood

**Crookers** and **Róisín Murphy** meet in **Royal T** somewhere between house and the heavy low end of dubstep, and two critics in 2010 heard it from opposite sides. Resident Advisor heard a dubstep feel that pointed to the sub-genre's unlikely pop potential; The FADER heard Murphy deliver a traditionally euphoric house-diva vocal that Crookers' hyperactive production made sound, at first, like pop. That pull gives the track its mood: euphoric, energetic and a little edgy.

## Song Summary

Royal T is one of two songs Murphy sang on Tons of Friends, the debut album of the Italian duo, released on 8 March 2010 on Southern Fried Records. It came out as a single on 23 May 2010, and in October 2012 it returned as one of 47 tracks on From Then Until Now, a Southern Fried retrospective of the project's originals and remixes. Resident Advisor noted that Murphy's songs for the album keep her eccentric, edgy style rather than reaching for summer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} brings a voice and an identity to someone else's record. Tons of Friends was built that way: nearly every track names a guest, from Kelis and will.i.am to Yelle and Kid Cudi, and Murphy sings on two of them.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can reveal what a song was all along. Through the summer of 2010, MusicBrainz lists remixes by Riva Starr, Foamo, Panton, The House Moguls, House of House, Kashii and Danton Eeprom. The FADER argued that the House of House version, with piano and chopped-up vocal snippets, brought out the house character of Murphy's singing.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} gives a single a second life. From Then Until Now gathers Crookers' own tracks next to their remixes for others, among them the Wow remix of {{ <track_link source="chemical_brothers_the_salmon_dance.md" /> }}, and closes with a continuous mix.

## Artistic Context

Crookers began as Phra (Francesco Barbaglia) and Bot (Andrea Fratangelo), who worked together from 2003 to 2012; Bot left in October 2012, the month the retrospective appeared, and Phra continued alone. Their earlier {{ <track_link source="crookers-mad-kidz.md" /> }} came out on the same label. Murphy, an Irish singer, songwriter and producer, had been one half of Moloko and had released the solo albums Ruby Blue and Overpowered, from which comes {{ <track_link source="roisin-murphy-primitive.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

According to Wikipedia, Royal T was featured on the seventh season of So You Think You Can Dance, and it later appeared on a German catwalk-hits compilation. Resident Advisor found the album thrilling, albeit disjointed; The FADER's verdict on the remix was simpler: "So, so good." Play the original first, then the House of House remix.
