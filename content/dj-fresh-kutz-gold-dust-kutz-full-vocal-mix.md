## Genre and Mood

**DJ Fresh** wrote **Gold Dust** as drum and bass, and **Kutz** remade it as **Gold Dust (Kutz Full Vocal Mix)**, one of the remixes on the 2012 single. This page files it under remix and electronic music rather than a narrower style, because no source describes how Kutz's version sounds. The feelings, energetic and carefree, are the song's: a ragga-tinged dance record whose video is all skipping ropes, skateboards and free running.

## Song Summary

Gold Dust has had more lives than most singles. It began in 2008 as an instrumental 12-inch on Breakbeat Kaos, the label DJ Fresh co-founded with Adam F. In August 2010 it returned with a vocal by Ce'cile, a combination Tropical Bass described as drum and bass meeting ragga-jungle. In 2012 a version with Ms. Dynamite went onto the album Nextlevelism, and in December Ministry of Sound released Shy FX's re-edit as a single, with remixes by TC, WestFunk & Steve Smart, Riddim Commission, Max Polyphonic and Kutz.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

This is where the page has to be honest. No review, timed analysis or interview describes the Kutz Full Vocal Mix; its upload on DJ Fresh's channel says only that it is "the Kutz Full Vocal remix". What the release tells you is its length, 5:26, against roughly three minutes for the radio versions, and its title, which promises the vocal in full rather than chopped into fragments. Wikipedia lists the 2012 single under the song's Ms. Dynamite version, but no source names the voice on this mix, so this page does not either. Listen for the structure yourself.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to another producer. On the 2012 single, six producers each took Gold Dust somewhere different; Dancing Astronaut heard Shy FX's lead version turn it into "drum and bass, jungle, and even reggae". Compare Kutz's mix with that one and hear how far a remix can travel from the same parts.

Gold Dust is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: instrumental, Ce'cile vocal, Ms. Dynamite vocal, re-edit, remixes. The song is less one recording than a family of them.

## Artistic Context

DJ Fresh is Dan Stein from Worthing, a member of the drum and bass group Bad Company. By the time of this single he had two UK number ones behind him: Louder, the first dubstep number one, and Hot Right Now, the first drum and bass number one, both from Nextlevelism, released by Ministry of Sound in October 2012.

## Impact and Reception

The Shy FX re-edit reached number 22 in the UK and 39 in Ireland, and in 2024 the song's versions together were certified triple platinum in the UK. For its tenth anniversary, in 2022, a new set of remixes followed, among them one by Bou & Used ({{ <track_link source="bou-all-u-gotta-do.md" /> }}). Press play on the Kutz mix and decide where it belongs in the family.
