## Genre and Mood

**Lenka**'s **Everything at Once** is pop at its lightest, close to indie pop in how little it needs. Diffuser called it a bouncy tune and the Oklahoma Gazette a nice pared-down one: playful and upbeat, over before it has a chance to outstay its welcome.

## Song Summary

The song is the fifth track of Two, Lenka's second solo album, and runs two minutes and thirty-seven seconds. She is its only credited writer, and David Kosten produced it. Its title is its promise: Diffuser suspected that the final line was part of why Microsoft chose the song to say that Windows 8 offered everything at once. It came out first in 2011 as a promotional single through iTunes and became a hit only after the advertisement.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found describes the song part by part, and no source gives times, so this guide stays with what the critics heard in it as a whole.

### Main Section

A pared-down arrangement leaves the voice in front, and Stephen Carradini heard a relatively aggressive vocal performance there, forceful for Lenka, though less fierce than Regina Spektor.

### Outro

The song ends on the line Diffuser thought fit Microsoft's message so well.

## Concepts in Focus

Everything at Once is a clear case of {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. A track already released on an album was licensed to Microsoft for its 2012 Windows 8 campaign; Diffuser described a British spot built on the system's bright colours and phone-like interface, and Wikipedia says the song then became Lenka's second most successful single after The Show.

The ad did not even need new pictures. It reused footage from the {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, in which Lenka, in a black-and-white striped dress, dances with two others while the background changes to match their dresses, until she falls backwards into their arms.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} matters to the sound. Two was split between Eg White, Guy Sigsworth and David Kosten, whom Diffuser introduced as the producer of Bat for Lashes' albums. Carradini's word for this song, pared-down, describes a producer's choice: what to leave out.

## Artistic Context

Lenka Kripac was born in Bega, New South Wales, in 1978, to a Czech jazz trumpeter and an Australian schoolteacher. She was an actress in Australian television and film and a member of the electronic rock band Decoder Ring before her solo debut in 2008, whose single The Show made her name. Two, which Wikipedia says was inspired by her engagement, came out in 2011 on Epic.

## Impact and Reception

Diffuser noted in November 2012 that the song was a hit in Canada and throughout Europe but not in Australia. It topped the airplay charts in the Czech Republic and Germany, reached number eight in both halves of Belgium and went gold in Germany. Two and a half minutes: give it all of them.
