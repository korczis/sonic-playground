## Genre and Mood

**Pischinger & Dermota** and **Anne Eck** made **Can't Breathe** for an album that Discogs files under deep house, tech house and techno; the single was filed under deep house alone. The duo describe their own sound as impulsive, atmospheric and diversified, located somewhere between house, tech house and techno, and handmade. Atmospheric and dreamy are the feelings named here, taken from that self-description and the title rather than from a review, since none describes the track in detail.

## Song Summary

Can't Breathe is the fifth of twelve tracks on Reisedrang, the duo's debut album, released by 35 Grad Records in June 2015, and in its original mix it runs six minutes and five seconds. The credits divide the work clearly: the music was produced by Julian Dermota and Michael Pischinger, and the vocals and lyrics are Anne Eck's. In September the label made it the album's single, with a radio edit, two remixes and a video.

## Concepts in Focus

Anne Eck is the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, but she is more than a borrowed voice: she wrote the lyrics, and she also managed the video's production. A Viennese singer-songwriter who writes her songs at the piano, she brings a songwriter's song into a club track.

The single shows what a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is for. The original mix runs 6:05; the radio edit on the single runs 4:00. The minutes a radio station does not want are typically the long introduction and ending a DJ needs to mix. The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed and filmed by Martin Domkar, was shot in August 2015 at the Wienerwaldbad baths in Purkersdorf, near Vienna, with Eck and both producers in the cast. It runs under five minutes, shorter than the album mix embedded here.

## Artistic Context

Pischinger & Dermota are Michael Pischinger and Julian Dermota from Vienna, friends who, according to their profile, have produced and DJed together for as long as they have known each other, playing four-on-the-floor in clubs around Europe while also making pop. Reisedrang features one other guest, Kaspar, on its title track.

## Impact and Reception

The duo's profile calls Can't Breathe a highly clicked single. Remixes by Joseph Cochon and Oliver Gruen gave it more versions. Start with the album mix, then try the edit.
