## Genre and Mood

**Muff Deep**'s **Enter the Muff** is house in the old sense, built for a club and in no hurry. Its release notes, as the distributor Forced Exposure printed them, call it "warm, bumping and decidedly sticky", and that is the range of feeling here: groovy and warm, but intense enough that the same notes promise the dancefloor "an elegant slap upside the head". Deep house tags on the release and the tech house style on its Discogs entry place it in the long lineage of house built on bass and repetition.

## Song Summary

Enter the Muff is the A-side of TART018, a twelve-inch released by the Copenhagen label Tartelet in late summer 2011, with Steady Muffin, a longer piano track, on the flip. At just over nine minutes it is a record for DJs rather than for radio. The release notes list its ingredients: heavy sub-bass, organ licks, "cooing soul vox" and rides galore, adding up to "intense, classic house".

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady, repeating pattern that a house record is built on, meant to carry dancers for minutes rather than to tell a story. Enter the Muff's length is the clearest sign of that purpose.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} here is described as heavy sub-bass, the frequencies at the bottom of the range that a club system makes physical. On headphones or small speakers part of it is felt rather than heard, which is why a track like this changes so much from a laptop to a dance floor.

Both ideas belong to {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Tartelet itself grew out of a string of Copenhagen parties in 2007, and Muff Deep grew out of the same scene: two friends who built their rapport at parties and in back-to-back DJ sets.

## Artistic Context

Muff Deep are Emil Margetli, Tartelet's label manager, and the Copenhagen DJ Mathias Mesteño, who, in the release notes' words, share a mutual interest in vintage electronic music; the twelve-inch credits the writing and production to Matthias Toksvaerd and Emil Margetli Nyholm. Tartelet was founded by Tomas Barfod and Frederik Bille Brahe, and The Vinyl Factory called it an ambassador of "off-kilter house and hazy soul". Muff Deep followed with the Shawarma House EP in 2012 and Pizza Trance in 2018.
