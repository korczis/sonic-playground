## Genre and Mood

**Guts** made **What Is Love** for an album that its label filed under electronic, hip-hop and instrumental hip-hop, and it sits where those meet: beat-driven, unhurried, closer to a downtempo afternoon than to a club. The mood is warm and relaxed, in keeping with an album a French DJ described as full of sunshine, soft and smooth.

## Song Summary

What Is Love is the sixth of eleven tracks on Paradise for All, Guts' third album, released on 23 March 2012 by Heavenly Sweetness with his own label Pura Vida and mastered by John Dent. Its roots are older. On Retrospective 1992 - 2012, an overview of his career released on Pura Vida that November, Guts dated the track to 2002 and listed what it was made with: an MPC 4000, a double bass and a Rhodes. MusicBrainz also lists it, as What Is Love?, on an earlier digital edition of the album from October 2011.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes this track part by part, and none gives timings, so this walk-through stays with the three sources of sound Guts named and leaves their order to your ear.

### The Beat

The MPC 4000 is the machine that holds the track: a sampler and sequencer that plays back sounds and programmed patterns.

### The Bass

A double bass, listed by Guts himself, puts an acoustic instrument in the low end.

### The Keys

A Rhodes electric piano is the third element, the one keyboard Guts named.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} is the beatmaker's basic craft: recording pieces of sound and replaying them as material for something new. Rush Hour wrote that Guts' newer tracks were more structured and crammed with subliminal samples, hidden rather than displayed.

The {{ <concept_link name="sequencer" /> }} is the other half. The Akai MPC, of which the MPC 4000 is one model, combines a sampler with a sequencer, so a producer can trigger sounds from pads and program them into repeating patterns. Guts' retrospective names, for almost every track, the machine it was made on, a reminder that for a beatmaker the instrument is the box itself. Compare {{ <track_link source="guts-patrice-the-studio-school-voices-nyc-want-it-back.md" /> }}, where his beats carry guest singers.

## Artistic Context

Guts is Fabrice Henri, born in 1971 in Boulogne-Billancourt, who founded and produced the French rap group Alliance Ethnik in 1990. After moving to Ibiza in 2007 he made his first solo album, Le Bienheureux, and founded the label Pura Vida; Heavenly Sweetness introduced him in 2012 as a beatmaker based in the Balearic Islands.

## Impact and Reception

The album's single was Laissez Lucie faire, which Radio Nova added to its playlist. MusicBrainz lists What Is Love on later compilations, among them French Touch, Volume 01: Electronic Music Made in France (2014). Listen for the double bass under the machine.
