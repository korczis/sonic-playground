## Artistic Context

The Polish Ambassador is the stage name of David Sugalski, a musician, producer and DJ from Oakland, California, who began producing around 2002. His thrift-store jumpsuit became his signature look and named his label, Jumpsuit Records. He has often sold albums on a pay-what-you-want basis, joined touring to environmental work with action days for ecological projects, and also records as Ample Mammal and with Ayla Nereo as Wildlight.
