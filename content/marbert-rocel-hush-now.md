## Genre and Mood

**Marbert Rocel** keep things quiet on **Hush Now**, a track that combines downtempo electronics with a soulful voice. Soft melodies and gentle rhythms create a tranquil atmosphere, made for winding down rather than dancing.

## Song Summary

Hush Now is a song about calm and stillness, and the music is built to match: lush, layered sounds, an unhurried pace and a vocal that soothes rather than performs. At 5 minutes 34 seconds it is the twelfth of thirteen tracks on the trio's second album Catch A Bird, a moment of rest near the end of the record.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro: Gentle Beginnings

The song opens with soft synth pads and delicate instrumental elements that set a calm tone and let the listener ease in.

### Verse 1: Soulful Vocals

Antje Seifarth's voice enters gentle and heartfelt, complementing the laid-back instrumentation.

### Chorus: Embracing Stillness

The chorus repeats its phrases over a soothing melody, and the repetition becomes hypnotic.

### Bridge: Textural Layers

Midway through, richer textures and subtle electronic details deepen the sound.

### Verse 2 and Outro: Sustaining the Mood

The second verse keeps the serene energy, and the song fades gently, leaving calm behind.

## Concepts in Focus

Hush Now is built on {{ <concept_link name="ambient texture" /> }}: sustained pads and slowly changing layers that create atmosphere rather than a hook or a beat to dance to. Listen to how the sound seems to surround the voice rather than accompany it. The texture is the mood.

Like much German downtempo and house of its time, the track grows from a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}. A few bars repeat while elements are added and removed, so change comes slowly and the ear never feels pushed. That approach comes from club music, where Marbert Rocel's producers Marcel Aue and Robert Krause also work, and here it is simply slowed down. For the club side of the same Thuringian scene, listen to {{ <track_link source="marek-hemmann-gemini.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Marbert Rocel are a German trio of two producers and a singer: Marcel Aue (DJ Malik), Robert Krause (Panthera Krause) and the vocalist Antje Seifarth (Spunk), with members from Erfurt and Weimar and a home base in Leipzig; Martin Kohlstedt joined them between 2009 and 2015. Their records came out on the Munich label Compost Records: Speed Emotions in 2007, Catch A Bird on 23 October 2009, Small Hours in 2012 and In the Beginning in 2015. They have also remixed artists from Lana Del Rey to Marek Hemmann and Clueso, and in 2010 formed Karocel with Mathias Kaden and Michael Nagler, whose album Plaited came out on Freude am Tanzen in 2013.

## Impact and Reception

Juno's review of Catch A Bird described the trio's second album as soulful techno with prominent guitar and clarinet layers. Hush Now was not a single, but it shows the side of Marbert Rocel that sits between soul and electronica, at its quietest.
