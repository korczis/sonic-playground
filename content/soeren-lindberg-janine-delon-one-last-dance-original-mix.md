## Genre and Mood

**Soeren Lindberg** and **Janine Delon**'s **One Last Dance** sits between house and deep house in its own listings: Apple Music files the single under dance and most of the compilations that carry it under house, and several of those compilations call themselves deep house in their titles. Groovy and hypnotic are the two feelings this playground pairs most often with house and deep house, and that classification is all they rest on.

## Song Summary

One Last Dance came out on 11 April 2014 on Fadersport, ℗ 2014 Fadersport Records, as a two-track single credited to Soeren Lindberg featuring Janine Delon. This page is about the first track, the 4:48 Original Mix; the second is the Original Radio Mix of 3:16. The credits of the art tracks list lyricists, so the song has words, but we found no published lyrics and no source that says what they are about or who sings them. Its title is the one hint of a theme, and we leave it at that.

## Concepts in Focus

Two ideas are on the release itself. A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited beside the main artist: here Janine Delon, named on the single as "feat. Janine Delon", while Soeren Lindberg is the artist the single belongs to. A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is a shorter cut for radio and playlists, and the single carries one beside the full version: the Original Mix runs 4:48, the Original Radio Mix a minute and a half less, at 3:16. Compilations chose between them, the radio mix going to collections such as Tracks to Travel, Vol. 1, the long mix to Fader Sounds (Smashing Club Tracks) and Deep Down Lake.

## Artistic Context

Fadersport is a record label based in Hamburg, whose catalogue on Discogs runs from the late 2000s into the 2020s. Its releases of 2014 and 2015 spread One Last Dance widely: Apple Music lists the track on more than a dozen compilations between 2014 and 2017, five of them under Fadersport's own ℗ line (Tracks to Travel, Vol. 1, Fadersport, Vol. 4, Fader Sounds, Deep Down Lake and Selected Sea Sounds), the rest from compilers such as EDM Tools and ECH Compilations. Even the songwriting credits differ between them: the art tracks provided to YouTube by Kontor New Media name Olaf Kruegel and Vitali Zestovskih as composers and lyricists, those provided by EDM Tools name Hendrik Riemer and Frank Lattig. We found no biography of Soeren Lindberg or Janine Delon.
