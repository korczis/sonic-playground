## Genre and Mood

**Discolight (Acid Pauli Remix)** is **Acid Pauli**'s reading of a song by **Pilocka Krach**, and it belongs to the club side of her catalogue: the 12-inch it closes is filed under techno, tech house and acid, and five years later a label named after a Berlin club compiled it. Hypnotic and groovy are the feelings that setting promises; no review describes this remix, so take them as a starting point.

## Song Summary

Discolight is Pilocka Krach's own song: she is credited as its writer, lyricist and producer, and her version runs three minutes thirteen on the Oh Yeah Voll Krass / Discolight release and just under four minutes on her 2015 Best Of. In April 2012 her label Greatest Hits International gave it to four producers for a 45 rpm 12-inch. Hrdvsion's NSD Remix and Hannah Holland's remix fill the A-side; Patric Catani's version and Acid Pauli's remix, five minutes seventeen long, fill the B-side. No source describes the remix's arrangement or sounds, so this page keeps to what is documented.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} package like this one is a conversation between producers. One song, four readings and no original: Hrdvsion's opens the record, Acid Pauli's closes it, and a DJ picks the one that fits the night. Compare the A-side in {{ <track_link source="hrdvision-nsd-discolight.md" /> }} and listen for what the two producers chose differently from the same material.

Five years later the remix found a second life on a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}. Bar 25 Music placed it ninth of 23 tracks on Bar 25 - Zeitgeist, released on 4 August 2017 as the continuation of the soundtrack to the film Bar25 - Timeless Days, beside a track Acid Pauli made with Nico Stojan, Acid Revisited.

That compilation is a document of {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Its label describes music for endless nights and timeless days that defined the style of an entire party generation, and its label manager Danny Faber added a DJ-mix of it. The remix is heard there as one moment of a long night.

## Artistic Context

Acid Pauli is Martin Gretschmann, born in 1973 in Schongau, Bavaria, a member of The Notwist from 1996 and its programmer, and the man behind the electronic project Console. Wikipedia tells how the alias began: at a small illegal party in Munich a friend shouted "Acciiiiidd Pauliiii" while he played a laptop running Rebirth, and, he said, everything made with that software sounded like acid house. He calls his music somewhere between ambient and techno, and in 2005 he co-founded the Munich club Rote Sonne.

## Impact and Reception

No review of the Discolight 12-inch or of this remix has been found; the mail-order house Forced Exposure announced it in one line, put your hands in the air. Its documented life is the compilation: in 2017 a club label chose it to stand for a party generation. Start it in the middle of a long set and give it the full five minutes.
