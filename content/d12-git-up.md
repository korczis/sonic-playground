## Genre and Mood

**D12**'s **Git Up** is hip-hop that opens an album with its fists up. Hot Press heard it as a straight-left/right cross combination, and the mood follows: aggressive, defiant, and irreverent in the way the whole of D12 World is.

## Song Summary

Git Up is the first track of D12 World and runs four minutes and three seconds. Eminem produced it, with Luis Resto as additional producer, and the writing credits name Marshall Mathers (Eminem), Ondre Moore (Swifty McVay), Von Carlisle (Kuniva), Resto and the guitarist Steve King. Wikipedia lists a sample of Shirley Ellis's The Name Game, the 1964 hit built on a rhyming game with people's names. It was not one of the album's singles, which were My Band and How Come.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the track section by section or gives times; this guide keeps to what the reviews and the credits say.

### Intro

The album starts here, on what Hot Press called a whipcrack skip-rope beat, the sound of a boxer's training before the fight.

### Main Section

The verses pass among the group. The Chicago Maroon singled out Eminem's turn, an 80-second ode to guns in its words, delivered with a rhythmic, singsong flow. Under the voices, MusicBrainz credits Resto on keyboards and King on guitar.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} credit points to The Name Game, a 1964 pop hit inside a 2004 rap opener. No review we found says where the sample sits or how it is used: listen for it yourself, and notice how much a credit can tell you before you hear anything.

Git Up also shows the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} as a member of the group. Eminem produced the track and, with King, mixed it, so the rapper who takes the most talked-about verse also decided how the whole track sounds.

The reviews separate {{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} as delivery from rapping as content. The Chicago Maroon credited Eminem's rhythm and singsong delivery but thought the lyrics added nothing, and it heard the other members, on Git Up and My Band, mimicking his style rather than finding their own.

## Artistic Context

D12 formed in Detroit in 1996 around Proof; Eminem joined after Bugz was killed in 1999. D12 World, released by Shady and Interscope on 27 April 2004, was the group's second album and its last with Proof, who was killed in 2006. Its beats came from Dr. Dre, Kanye West, Hi-Tek and others, and Eminem and Resto made several of them, among them {{ <track_link source="d12-6-in-the-morning.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The album entered at number one in the United States, Britain and Australia, while Metacritic averaged its reviews at 58. Git Up drew both sides of that split: a hard opening punch for Hot Press, a showcase of flow over empty words for the Chicago Maroon. Hear the punch for yourself.
