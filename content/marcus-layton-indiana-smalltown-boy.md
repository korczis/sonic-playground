## Song Summary

Smalltown Boy was Bronski Beat's debut single, released in 1984 and written by Steve Bronski, Jimmy Somerville and Larry Steinbachek. Its narrator is a young man who is bullied and decides to leave home; it became a gay anthem, carried by Somerville's falsetto and an electro-pop pulse. Layton's version came out on 25 March 2022 on the label Selected. as a one-track single of 2:24. Qobuz credits the three original writers and Cohen Yusha as mixing and mastering engineer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song first released by someone else. Smalltown Boy has had many: Kate Ryan, Orville Peck in 2020 and the Knocks with Perfume Genius in 2024 are among those its encyclopedia entry names. The writers' credit stays with the original three, which is how a cover differs from a song written anew.

The credit line also shows a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: the single is Marcus Layton's, with Indiana featured. A producer-led dance record often works this way, the producer building the track and a guest singer carrying the song.

## Artistic Context

Layton's profile places him in Berlin, with a studio in Kreuzberg, and describes him as a producer, remixer and DJ. Indiana is Lauren Henson from Loughborough, whose Solo Dancing reached number 14 in Britain in 2014 and whose debut album No Romeo followed in 2015; she describes her music as emotional electronica with 1980s-inspired synths.
