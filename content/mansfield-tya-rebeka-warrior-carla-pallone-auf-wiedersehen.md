## Genre and Mood

**Mansfield.TYA** built **Auf Wiedersehen**, the first single from their fifth album, on a contradiction that critics kept returning to. It sits between new wave, electronic music and the radical end of French chanson, and its mood is dark, hypnotic and bittersweet at once: a farewell sung over music that almost asks you to dance. Turtlenek heard a mesmerising new wave sound; Addict-Culture called it a devilishly formidable disco prayer.

## Song Summary

The title is German, the lyric French. Its lines refuse to look back and repeat that what is left behind is no longer the singer's problem, and they close on the words "Amen et Auf Wiedersehen", a blessing and a goodbye in one breath. Indiepoprock found the song chilling and destabilising for exactly that reason: words without varnish or ambiguity, sung in an assured voice and without pathos, over a base that is at moments almost danceable. The duo wrote and composed it themselves; a marimba part is credited to J. Quentin.

### Main Section

The verses and the refrain carry the song's central tension: a steady, near-dance pulse under a lyric of separation. Indiepoprock describes the album as a whole as built on a more synthetic base that adopts some codes of new wave, with Carla Pallone's organic violin lines set against its cold sounds.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is where the song's contradiction lives. Cold synthetic sound meets acoustic colour: the marimba credited on the track, and across the album Pallone's violin. The words are stark and the setting moves, and neither cancels the other.

The {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} was done by David Chalmin and the mastering by Alexis Bardinet at Globe Audio Mastering, according to the duo's own upload. Mixing balances every recorded part into one stereo track; mastering prepares that track for release.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Nicolas Medy and published in December 2020, runs almost eight minutes, more than twice the song's length. Addict-Culture's interviewer described it as more than a clip: a short film, cinema.

## Artistic Context

Mansfield.TYA are Rebeka Warrior (Julia Lanoë) and Carla Pallone, who met in Nantes in 2002 and named the duo after June Mansfield, the wife of Henry Miller. Lanoë also sings in Sexy Sushi and Kompromat. Monument ordinaire, released on Warriorecords in February 2021, more than five years after Corpo Inferno, has twelve songs, with guests from Bérurier Noir and Odezenne. Its own description promises happy melancholy and songs that make you dance even while crying; reviewers read it as written after the loss of someone close.

## Impact and Reception

Auf Wiedersehen announced the album in December 2020. Addict-Culture called it hyper-efficient, proof that the duo know how to make hits, and Les Oreilles Curieuses gave the album 7.5 out of 10. Listen for the moment the farewell turns into something you can move to.
