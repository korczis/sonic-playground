## Genre and Mood

Wikipedia files **Kollektiv Turmstrasse** under minimal techno, and **Schwindelig** shows another side of the album Rebellion der Träumer: slow, electronic and close to downtempo, which is how Qobuz describes most of the record. The title is German for dizzy. Resident Advisor heard elegiac strings in it, and AllMusic's reviewer called it the album's clearest example of swooning elegance; melancholic and dreamy is this page's reading of those two descriptions.

## Song Summary

Schwindelig is the thirteenth of nineteen tracks and runs four minutes and fourteen seconds. The YouTube upload credits it to the duo's two members of the time, Christian Hilscher and Nico Plagemann, as composers. Resident Advisor's Tal Rosenberg summed it up in one line: a textbook hip-hop drumbeat, woodwind-sounding keys and elegiac strings, with a short, chopped vocal on top. No review says who sings or where the vocal comes from.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

On the album the track arrives out of Dazwischen 6, one of the short ambient interludes; no source describes its own first bars.

### Main Section

The hip-hop drumbeat carries the track, slow and plain, with the keys and strings above it and the vocal fragment laid over the drums. No review maps how these layers enter or change, so listen for which of them stays constant and which come and go.

### Outro

No source describes the ending. On the album, Dazwischen 7 follows directly.

## Concepts in Focus

Rebellion der Träumer is built around the {{ <concept_link name="interlude" /> }}. Resident Advisor counted only ten full songs among its nineteen tracks: every song is separated from the next by an ambient piece called Dazwischen, German for in between, nine of them in all, most around a minute long. Schwindelig sits between the sixth and the seventh, so on the album it is heard as a song framed by pauses rather than as a single.

The track also had a second life in the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. Rebellion der Träumer Remixes Part 3, released by Connaisseur in 2012, carries versions by Wanderlust, Ian O'Donovan, Birdcage and LectroNatic that run from just over seven to more than nine and a half minutes, up to twice the length of the album track. Compare the duo's own remix work for the same label, {{ <track_link source="kollektiv-turmstrasse_on-the-streets-let-freedom.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Kollektiv Turmstrasse began in 1998 as a duo of Nico Plagemann and Christian Hilscher and is based in Hamburg; the project announced Hilscher's departure in 2023. Connaisseur Recordings released Rebellion der Träumer on 19 November 2010. The music publisher Freibank calls it the duo's debut album, although Wikipedia lists an earlier one, Verrückte Welt, from 2006.

## Impact and Reception

Resident Advisor was mixed: it heard the album's sounds as enjoyable but a little plain and at times imitative, and praised Affekt and Deine Distanz. Freibank called the album timeless, and Connaisseur marked its tenth anniversary in 2020 with a new series of remixes. Listen to Schwindelig with the interludes around it, the way the album presents it.
