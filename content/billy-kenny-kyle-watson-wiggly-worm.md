## Genre and Mood

**Billy Kenny** and **Kyle Watson** made **Wiggly Worm** for the club, and it runs 5:12. No store or database classification of the track itself was found; the genres here, tech house and house, are those Wikipedia gives for Billy Kenny, and Kyle Watson is filed under house, deep house and techno. The feelings rest on the one published description of the track, which called it one of the most fun tracks its writer had heard in a while: playful and, once it gets going, energetic.

## Song Summary

Wiggly Worm closes the Billy Kenny and Friends EP, six collaborations that the Leeds producer released on This Ain't Bristol, the label he co-runs, in April 2017. Mixmag reported that the EP came out on Beatport on 14 April; Spotify lists 28 April. Each track pairs Kenny with a friend: Walker & Royce on The Lonely Robot, Kry Wolf on Rave Cave, GotSome on The Pharaoh, Low Steppa on Clap Clap, Josh Brown on Liftoff and Kyle Watson on Wiggly Worm. Kenny told Mixmag it was the most fun and diverse EP he had worked on so far.

## Artistic Context

Billy Kenny is a producer from Leeds who has released on Relief, Dirtybird and his own This Ain't Bristol, which Wikipedia places in Hanover. Kyle Watson is a DJ and producer from Johannesburg, active since 2007, who was nominated for Best Producer and Best Male DJ at the Dance Music Awards South Africa in 2017 and began touring the United States in 2018. This Ain't Bristol appears among the labels that have released his music.
