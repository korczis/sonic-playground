## Genre and Mood

**Sunset** is deep house with a broken heart. **Compuphonic**, the Belgian producer Maxime Firket, built the track, and **Marques Toliver**, the American soul singer and violinist, gave it a voice. Get Physical, the Berlin label that released it, describes pained male vocals over deep, heartbroken house beats: melancholic and romantic at once, music for the end of a day rather than its peak.

## Song Summary

Firket is credited as composer and lyricist, and the label's own summary names the three things that build the track: the vocal, the beat and neon chords that flesh it out, so that the whole thing drips with a sombre sense of romanticism. The original runs 6:12. When it came out, Harder Blogger Faster called it probably Compuphonic's deepest track yet and heard an R&B tinge in the guest vocal that did not sound too manufactured.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit matters here. Compuphonic is the artist, Toliver the guest, yet it is his voice that most listeners remember. A house producer often needs exactly that: a singer who brings a story the beat alone cannot tell.

The original release, GPM186 in June 2012, also carried a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} of 3:22, about half the length of the original. The official video, posted on Compuphonic's channel in March 2013, runs 3:22. It replaced an upload with just a simple logo, and XLR8R reported that it set the music to an edited version of Much Better Now, a short film by the studio Salon Alpin about a bookmark that comes to life and surfs the pages of a book.

The track then lived on as a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} project. DJ T.'s remix was on the first release; a 2013 package added Aeroplane, Waze & Odyssey, Aashton & Swift and others, and in 2018 Get Physical commissioned new versions from Andre Lodemann & Fabian Dikof, Noema and Tim Engelhardt.

## Artistic Context

Firket was born in Liège in 1983, trained as a cellist, and turned to dance music through Kraftwerk, Depeche Mode, Front 242 and the music of Detroit. He started on the Belgian label Dirty Dancing and runs his own, Union Match. Amsterdam Dance Event calls Sunset a piece of hedonistic house and quotes his aim of a positive melancholy. Compare his later remix of {{ <track_link source="him-self-her-kieran-fowkes-compuphonic-gone-too-long-compuphonic-remix.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Sunset spread by word of mouth: by early 2013 Extra Music counted it among 2012's biggest underground hits, and the logo upload had drawn about three million YouTube views by the time the official video arrived. Get Physical still called it an enduring hit with house lovers in 2018. Let it play as the light goes.
