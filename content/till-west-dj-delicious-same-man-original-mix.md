## Genre and Mood

**Till West** and **DJ Delicious** built **Same Man** as house with an electro edge: Discogs files the record under house and electro, and its UK release under electro house. The feelings are energetic and playful, a party call borrowed from an older record and set on a club beat.

## Song Summary

Same Man was the first single of two Cologne childhood friends, released in early 2006 on their own label, Phunkwerk. It is built on a sample of For The Same Man, a 1983 record by the B Beat Girls, and its writing credits combine the two producers with that record's writers, Libby McLaren, Matt Noble and Rebecca Rifkin. The lyric printed with the official video starts with a voice telling a girl they have a party to go to tonight, ready and out the door, and returns again and again to being hungry for the same man. This page is about the original mix, the long version on side A of the Phunkwerk twelve-inch.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the original mix section by section, so this breakdown stays with the lyric and the release data.

### Intro

The spoken party call opens the lyric, a voice talking rather than singing.

### Main Section

The refrain line about the same man returns between the spoken lines; at 6:20 on the Phunkwerk record, the original mix gives it far more room than the 2:37 radio edit.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" text="Sampling" /> }} is the whole idea here. Defected describes For The Same Man as sparse, raw electro beats with disco riffs and an other-worldly new-wave vocal by Rebecca Rifkin, a record that was still sought after and sampled decades later.

Its {{ <concept_link name="spoken word" /> }} intro is the famous part: according to Defected, it was sampled many times after appearing on a late-1980s acapella compilation, and Josh Wink's I Am Ready uses its phrase about being ready as title and main hook. Compare the preached voice of {{ <track_link source="disclosure-when-a-fire-starts-to-burn.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is what most people saw: the official video, which Ministry of Sound reissued as an HD remaster, uses a short edit, while the original mix is made for DJs.

## Artistic Context

Till West played house sets in Cologne clubs such as Tiefenrausch, Apollo and Teatro, according to his Amsterdam Dance Event profile, which also says DJs including Pete Tong, Erick Morillo and Armand van Helden played the track before its release. The Phunkwerk twelve-inch, PHW 003, came out in March 2006 with a remix by Sharam Jey on side B. Other labels released it the same year: Data in the UK, Kontor in Germany, Spinnin' in the Netherlands, Refune in Sweden, Vendetta in Spain and Ultra in the US.

## Impact and Reception

The ADE profile calls Same Man a worldwide success. In the UK, the Data single reached number 36 in July 2006 and spent eight weeks in the top 100. Start with the spoken lines and let the long mix carry you.
