## Genre and Mood

**Tricky** made **Armor** with **Terra Lopez** of Rituals of Mine on the microphone, and it is one of the shortest, most direct songs on his 2017 album ununiform. Reviewers placed it between trip-hop's darkness and pop: RapReviews counted it with "Dark Days" as the album's electro-pop, Abysmal Hymns heard the pace pick up for a somewhat poppy song, and Northern Transmissions heard evil-sounding beats and an ominous space. The mood is dark, and the voice in the middle of it is bold.

## Song Summary

Armor runs two minutes and 25 seconds, the ninth of thirteen tracks on ununiform. The album credits Terra Lopez with the vocals on it. No source discusses its lyric, so this page does not interpret it beyond its title. What the reviews agree on is the singer: Northern Transmissions called Lopez a commanding voice of power who controls the track, and Abysmal Hymns thought hers might be the album's best vocal.

## Concepts in Focus

ununiform is built on guests, and Armor shows what a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} can bring to someone else's record. Tricky worked on the album with the singers Francesca Belmonte, Mina Rose, Asia Argento, Avalon Lurks, Martina Topley-Bird and Terra Lopez and with the rapper Scriptonite; each changes the colour of his music. It is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} as author. Tricky is credited with the album's production alongside his co-producers, and Northern Transmissions wrote that tracks like Armor, built around another vocalist, show how strong he has become as a producer.

## Artistic Context

Tricky, born and raised in Bristol and a pioneer of trip-hop since his 1995 solo debut Maxinquaye, moved to Berlin in 2015, and ununiform, his thirteenth studio album, was recorded there and released by False Idols on 22 September 2017. RapReviews called it the first Tricky album in a while that he made for the love of making music. Rituals of Mine, from Sacramento, began in 2010 as Sister Crayon, formed by Terra Lopez and Dani Fernandez, and took its new name in 2016; its label describes it as a hybrid R&B-electronic project and lists Tricky among the artists it has shared a stage with.

## Impact and Reception

ununiform divided critics: Metacritic averages 71 from twelve reviews, while Pitchfork and Exclaim! were cool on it. Armor, though, was singled out more than once as a highlight. Play it twice; at two and a half minutes it asks for that.
