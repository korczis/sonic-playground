## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** and **The Lost Amigos** made **Ay Caramba** for a party: electronic music that a record-shop listing of its first release files under house and that its label described in electro terms. The feelings are playful and energetic, the mood of an artist her publisher calls an electric rock star of the techno world.

## Song Summary

Few sources describe the track itself. When the Berlin label Katermukke released it in 2017, it presented Berlin's wild electro rock star crushing the party with bleeping wild sounds and a catchy hook, a single word stretched out: amazing. In October 2020 Pilocka Krach released it again, as a two-track single on her own label Greatest Hits International: Ay Caramba and Ay Caramba 2020, both 4:14 long. Bandcamp credits the writing to Fatima Njai, Pilocka Krach, Lupe Carranza, Nick Bloom-Scaglione and Anton Cerda, and the production to Pilocka Krach alone. No source sets out the lyric, so this page does not paraphrase it.

### Main Section

The bleeping sounds and the shouted hook are the two elements on record. Listen for when the hook arrives and how often it returns; everything else is yours to describe.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} does not need a melody. Here, by the label's account, it is one word, drawn out until it becomes a sound in its own right: the kind of hook a crowd can shout back without knowing any other line.

The 2020 single is also a small study in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. It carries two tracks of the same song at the same length, and three years earlier the song had already come out on Katermukke. No source says what differs between them, which is a good reason to listen side by side.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} on her channel, published on 1 November 2020, is tagged as satire and was funded by Musicboard Berlin, according to its description.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick and grew up in Berlin-Mitte; she runs her own label, Greatest Hits International. In 2016 a Musicboard Berlin grant took her to Detroit, where she connected with Underground Resistance. The Lost Amigos first appeared in the title of her 2017 double album Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, whose Lost Amigos tracks she made in Tulum, Guadalajara, Detroit and Berlin; her label's text says she is Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos herself, her band and all at once. Compare {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach_la_luna-y-el-sol.md" /> }} from that album.

## Impact and Reception

No review of Ay Caramba has been found. Its 2017 release was a clear vinyl twelve-inch limited to 300 copies, the fourth part of Katermukke 150, and the 2020 reissue put the song back under her own name and label. Put it on loud and wait for the hook.
