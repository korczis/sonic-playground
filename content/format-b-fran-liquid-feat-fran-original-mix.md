## Genre and Mood

**Format:B** are known for club-tailored tech house, and **Liquid** is the exception on their second album: the one track where MusicBrainz credits a singer beside them. It belongs to tech house and house, and its feelings here, groovy, alluring and hypnotic, are this page's reading rather than a critic's verdict, because no published review we could verify describes the track in detail.

## Song Summary

Liquid is track two of Restless, released on Formatik Records in October 2011, and the album's credited collaboration: MusicBrainz lists it as Format:B and Fran, and Formatik's own upload names her in the title. Fran is the German singer of the duo Oliver Koletzki & Fran, whose single Hypnotized came out in 2009. The Original Mix is the album version, 6:43 long, the full-length cut a DJ would play.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit marks a guest on someone else's record. In tech house, where most tracks are instrumental, a featured singer changes the balance: the producers' groove becomes the setting for a voice. Format:B chose Fran, already known for the songs she made with Oliver Koletzki, whose own music is in {{ <track_link source="oliver-koletzki-through-the-darkness.md" /> }}.

The words Original Mix only make sense next to a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. Restless was followed in 2012 by a series of remix 12-inches on Formatik; on the first, Super Flu combined Liquid with the album's opener Desire into a single track. The original is the producers' own version, the one the remixes answer.

The track also belongs to {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Format:B met at the SAE Institute in Berlin, became resident DJs at the club Watergate and built a label as a platform for themselves and friends; Restless was made to be played by DJs like them.

## Artistic Context

Format:B are Franciskus Sell, from Erfurt, and Jakob Hildenbrand, from Berlin. They released 12-inches on Highgrade from 2005, then on Stil vor Talent and Opossum, and founded Formatik Records in late 2009. Restless was their second album, after Steam Circuit in 2008, and Formatik's first full-length, containing only unreleased productions and issued digitally, on CD and as a double LP.
