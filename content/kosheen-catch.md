## Genre and Mood

**Kosheen**'s **Catch** is drum and bass in a three-minute single, close to trip-hop and breakbeat in its mix. Armada Music, writing for its 25th anniversary, called it a showcase of the group's fusion of trip-hop, drum and bass and breakbeat; Hot Press called it a dance stomper. The feelings it carries here are intense, defiant and dark; Hot Press wrote in 2002 that the band's ethos remained distinctly punk.

## Song Summary

Catch is a three-minute single written by the group's three members of the time, Sian Evans, Mark Morrison and Darren Beale, and produced by Decoder & Substance at Ledge One in Bristol. The version on this page is the 2021 remaster of the band's debut album Resist, where Catch is the third track; MusicBrainz lists it at 3:24. The memorable detail of its story is its second chance: a song that stalled on its first release and became a hit a year later.

## Concepts in Focus

Catch rests on the {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }}, the broken, syncopated drum pattern that drum and bass speeds up and chops apart. Armada's description puts it at the centre of the band's sound.

The version here is a {{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }}. Moksha reissued Resist on 15 July 2021, "remastered for 2021". A remaster reworks the finished mix for a new release, its level and tone, without changing the performance; no source says who remastered Resist or what changed.

Catch also lived through the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. The 2000 single came with mixes by Way Out West, Rennie Pilgrem and Decoder & Substance among others, the 2001 one with a remix by Ferry Corsten, and 2025 brought new reworks for the song's 25th anniversary.

## Artistic Context

Kosheen are a Bristol group formed in 1999 by Evans, Markee Ledge and Darren Beale. Hot Press reported that their diverse backgrounds were joined by the late-1980s rave scene. Resist, released in September 2001 on Moksha, reached number 8 in the UK. The band's later turn is heard in {{ <track_link source="kosheen-addict.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Released on 6 November 2000 as a double A-side with Demonstrate, Catch stopped at 86 in the UK but reached number 5 in Flanders and 16 on the Dutch Top 40. Re-released on its own on 10 December 2001, it reached number 15 in the UK, number 3 on the UK Dance chart and the top 20 in Germany and Finland. Put it on loud.
