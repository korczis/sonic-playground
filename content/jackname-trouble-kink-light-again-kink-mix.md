## Genre and Mood

**Jackname Trouble** and **KiNK**'s **Light Again - KiNK Mix** is the Bulgarian producer's remix of the title track of a Polish producer's 2010 EP. Resident Advisor named no genre for it, but heard it as the EP's offer to DJs who do not play slow tempos; this page files it under house and electronic. Groovy and playful are this page's reading of a review that found it marvelously fresh and immediately familiar at the same time.

## Song Summary

Pets Recordings released the Light Again EP under the catalogue number PETS003, and Resident Advisor reviewed it in May 2010. The EP holds four short originals and, last, this remix, which runs about 5:48. The review's Terrence Fuller heard the originals as closer to Los Angeles wonky hip-hop than to anything expected from a label run by Catz 'n Dogz, and the title track as faster than the rest yet still more Daedelus than Deetron. In the reviewer's words, it fell to KiNK to offer fodder for DJs unaccustomed to such tempos.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can carry a track into another world. Three of the EP's originals, Resident Advisor wrote, gurgle and bounce at 100 BPM, and none reaches four minutes; the remix is nearly six minutes long and was heard as the record's offer to DJs who play other tempos.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is where KiNK changes his habits. The review heard a producer who had seemingly never met a high, rotating melody he did not like choose the low end instead, to great effect.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} supplies the rest: a string sample with a scratchy grain, and sampled percussion chopped up in a plainly digital way. Resident Advisor called this fearless mashing of organic and digital sounds distinctive, adding that few were doing it this way, or as well.

## Artistic Context

KiNK is Strahil Velchev, a producer, DJ and live performer from Sofia, known on stage for improvising and playing with the crowd; Resident Advisor's readers voted him best live act in 2015 and 2016. In 2010 the review could still call him a rising Eastern European star. For the label's own founders at work as remixers, hear {{ <track_link source="dop-close-up.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The remix travelled beyond its EP: MusicBrainz lists it again on the Pets compilations Friends Will Carry You Home, Part 3 in 2011 and My Favourite PETS vol. 5 in 2018. Start with the bassline and let the cut-up percussion pull you in.
