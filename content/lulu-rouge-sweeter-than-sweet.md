## About the Artists

**Lulu Rouge** is a Danish electronic music duo consisting of DJ T.O.M. and Buda, 
both well-known figures in the electronic scene. Their music is characterized by 
dark, immersive soundscapes, often blending minimal beats with rich, emotive layers. 
Alice Carreri, with her powerful and velvety voice, adds depth and soul, making this 
collaboration particularly memorable.
