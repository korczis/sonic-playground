## Genre and Mood

**Minuit** introduced themselves in 2015 with **Flash**, and French writers reached for three words at once: rock, funk and disco. Charts in France heard an energetic, offbeat rock; Time Out Paris said the song brought a disco-funk colour to the new French scene; a Fnac writer called the band's sound a funk rock close to the Rita Mitsouko, and fairly vintage. The feelings named here are energetic and mysterious, the second taken from the singer herself.

## Song Summary

Simone Ringer told Time Out that when the band wrote Flash she pictured something abstract and mysterious, and the band's own description of the song keeps it that way. It speaks of colours exploding that no longer need words, of emotions first and pop poetry that prefers the boldness of the unknown to explaining its lyric. What it does describe is the build: a guitar enters like an alarm, then a bass, keyboards and drums, where the organic meets the synthetic. And the voice, which the same text calls rallying and snarling, sensual and proud.

A guitar comes in alone, like an alarm going off. It is the first thing the description mentions and the sound the song is built on.

### Main Section

The bass, keyboards and drums arrive and the song becomes a band, half played, half synthetic. Ringer's vocal sits on top with the mix of warmth and bite the description promises.

## Concepts in Focus

Flash is a small lesson in {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }}: the decision about which instrument plays when. Here the band lets the guitar announce the song alone and adds the other parts one by one, so the listener hears the groove assembled rather than delivered whole.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Arnaud Giacomini, is shot in black and white, minimalist and with kitsch costumes; Charts in France noted the contrast with the song's energy. Ringer sings alongside a boxing match, with the boxers Michael Paz and Soké Hiezé, that turns into a rout. Compare the French funk of {{ <track_link source="general-elektriks-whisper-to-me.md" /> }}, which also leans on keyboards.

## Artistic Context

Minuit is a Paris band of five: Simone Ringer on vocals, her brother Raoul Chichin and Joseph Delmas on guitars, Clément "Klem" Aubert on bass and Tanguy Truhé on drums. Simone and Raoul are children of Catherine Ringer and Fred Chichin of the Rita Mitsouko; Raoul had led Raoul and the Crazy Dog, and Simone had been heading for a career as a graphic designer. Flash was the band's first single and opens their five-track debut EP, released by Because Music in September 2015.

## Impact and Reception

The band made an impression at the Printemps de Bourges in April 2015, and in September won the jury prize of the Inrocks Lab contest. In 2017 they played Flash at the Victoires de la Musique, the French music awards. Their first album, Vertigo, followed later. Put the song on and wait for the guitar that sounds the alarm.
