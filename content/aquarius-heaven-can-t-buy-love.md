## Genre and Mood

**Aquarius Heaven** made **Can't Buy Love** the title track of his 2011 EP on Wolf + Lamb, and Discogs files the record under house and dub. The mood is dark, eerie and provocative at once: Resident Advisor's Stephen Worthy heard popping drum patterns and eerie, funereal synths, carrying a lyric he called a potty-mouthed treatise on male/female interaction.

## Song Summary

The song sets a blunt subject, the gap between love and what money can get, against music that sounds like a wake. The full version runs 5:45 on the label's own upload, the length of the EP's original mix. Discogs credits the writing and production to Aquarius Heaven and lists the American singer Dani Siciliano as featured; Wolf + Lamb released the EP on 12-inch vinyl in September 2011 as WLM16, with Keep On, Summer Love and Stop beside the title track.

### Rhythm

The drums pop rather than thud, in Worthy's word, giving the track movement while everything above them stays low and slow.

### Synths

The synths set the tone, eerie and funereal, the sound of the song's darkness before a word is sung.

### Vocals

The words are the provocation. Which lines belong to Aquarius Heaven and which to Dani Siciliano no source says, and this review does not guess.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit names a guest on someone else's track. Dani Siciliano's career was already long: she sang on Matthew Herbert's records from Around the House in 1998 and released her own albums on !K7. Her name on Can't Buy Love links a Berlin house producer to Herbert's electronica.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} came out at the end of September 2011, the EP's month, in a shorter edit of about three and a half minutes, directed and filmed by Annikki Heinemann and Anna Piltz with art direction and concept by Kieran Behan, a Crystalmafia production with costumes, styling and six models.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} gave the track its widest audience. In December 2011 the crew Visionquest put it into Fabric 61, their mix for the London club's series, between Green Velvet and Tale Of Us.

## Artistic Context

Aquarius Heaven is Brian Brewster, a former reggae, dancehall and hip-hop singer. Resident Advisor tells how he fell under the wing of the French trio {{ <track_link source="dop-close-up.md" /> }} during a stint in Paris before settling in Berlin, and how his earlier EP 7 Days introduced his quirky vocal style. Wolf + Lamb, the New York label of {{ <track_link source="nicolas-jaar-time-for-us.md" /> }}, was praised in the same review for electronica that subverts while keeping one foot on the dance floor.

## Impact and Reception

Can't Buy Love made no chart, but within three months of release it was on a Fabric mix CD, and Resident Advisor reviewed its EP. Turn it up, listen to the drums against the synths, and decide whether the words or the music hurt more.
