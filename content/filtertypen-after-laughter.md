## Genre and Mood

**Filtertypen**'s **After Laughter** is tech house from Hannover, a long club track that its own label calls floor-filling. Groovy and hypnotic are the words this playground files it under; they are our reading of a genre built on repetition, because no critic we found has described the track at all.

## Song Summary

After Laughter is the track that turned a club night brand into a record label. Filtertypen, a DJ duo from Hannover, had run Yippiee since 2006 as the name for their event management. In the summer of 2010, the label's own history says, they decided to release After Laughter themselves, together with Benny Ferkel, who had worked with them for years. It appeared on the Laughing Bird EP, Yippiee's first release, which MusicBrainz dates to January 2011, running 7:30. The composer credit goes to Steffen Esser. In August 2013 Voltaire Music collected it on the compilation Re:face Session Six.

Yippiee began as the brand of Filtertypen's events, and its fifth release gathered twelve artists who had played the Treibgut events in Hannover over five years. The record label grew out of the dance floor, not the other way round, much like {{ <track_link source="dirty-doering-casino-aquatique.md" /> }} opened a Berlin DJ's label in the same year.

## Artistic Context

Filtertypen started as a DJ duo in Hannover a few years before 2006, and Yippiee followed them from parties to records. Their label's account says the Laughing Bird EP stayed in the sales charts for months, and that by its fifth release Yippiee was pressing three vinyl records of Treibgut regulars.

## Impact and Reception

There is no chart record and no press review to quote. The duo's SoundCloud upload had passed half a million plays when we looked, a sign that the track kept finding listeners long after 2011.
