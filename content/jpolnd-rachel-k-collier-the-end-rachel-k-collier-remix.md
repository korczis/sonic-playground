## Genre and Mood

**JPOLND** wrote **The End** as dark pop, the vein Tunefind says her side project channels, and **Rachel K Collier** reworked it as a remix that Apple Music files under electronic. No review describes how the remix sounds, so this page does not guess its tempo or instruments. Dark and sultry describe the song it starts from: a lyric about doomed love, heard first over a steamy television scene.

## Song Summary

The original came out on 25 December 2020, the day Bridgerton arrived on Netflix, and plays at the end of the first season's sixth episode, as the newly married Simon and Daphne walk through his family home by lantern light. Jessica Vaughn, who records as JPOLND, told Tunefind she wrote it with her friend Jeff Plate in New Jersey and sent it to Charlie Lowell of Jars of Clay to produce. The remix followed on 7 May 2021 on Head Bitch Music, the company Vaughn founded, and runs 3:14, with Collier credited as its producer and remixer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version built from a recording's parts, usually by another producer. Here the producer is also credited as an artist, so the remix carries Collier's name next to JPOLND's.

The song's fortune is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. By 7 January 2021, Tunefind reported, it had been Shazamed more than 100,000 times and streamed on Spotify more than 200,000 times; Exclaim!, citing BuzzFeed News, counted over a million Spotify streams in less than a month.

The remix sits among several versions of one song: Stripped with Strings, credited to JPOLND with Chess Theory and Pan dö Baré, came out in April 2021 and a TAIINA remix in September. A new arrangement by the song's own artist is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}; a remix hands the recording to another producer.

## Artistic Context

Tunefind traces Vaughn's career from Charlotte Sometimes at eighteen to the second season of The Voice and the alt-pop project LACES. Collier is a producer and performer from Swansea who worked with Ray Foxx on the UK top 20 single Boom Boom (Heartbeat) and teaches as an Ableton Certified Trainer. Head Bitch Music posted an interview of Collier by JPOLND three days before release. Hear Collier as a singer on {{ <track_link source="rose-rachel-k-collier-craving.md" /> }}.
