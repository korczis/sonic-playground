## Genre and Mood

**Pigeon John** goes full speed with **The Bomb**, a hip-hop single with the bounce of pop and the push of rock. The result is a bold, high-energy track that radiates confidence and good humour, the sound of a rapper enjoying himself.

## Song Summary

The Bomb opens Dragon Slayer, the album Pigeon John released on 12 October 2010 on Quannum Projects, the Bay Area label he had joined after Lyrics Born brought him in. He produced the whole record with Hervé Salters of the French band General Elektriks, and for the first time he sampled original instrumentation rather than flipping samples from other people's records. The song is short, about three and a half minutes, and built for energy: a driving groove, a title made for shouting and a rapper with a light, quick delivery.

## Lyrics Breakdown

### Intro: Building Anticipation

The track gets moving at once, as an album opener should, setting up its groove and its mood before the first verse.

### Verse 1: Embracing the Struggle

Pigeon John enters with a quick, conversational flow, riding the beat with an easy confidence rather than aggression.

### Chorus: The Anthem of Strength

The title phrase arrives as the hook, simple and repeated, the part of the song everyone remembers.

### Verse 2: Unyielding Confidence

The second verse keeps the energy up and the tone light, the groove never letting go.

### Bridge: The Calm Before the Storm

A short change of texture gives a breath before the final chorus.

### Final Chorus: Explosive Finale

The hook comes back for a last round and the song ends at full energy.

## Concepts in Focus

The Bomb is a lesson in a different kind of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Hip-hop producers traditionally build beats by flipping fragments of old records; on Dragon Slayer Pigeon John and Hervé Salters recorded original instrumentation and then sampled that, cutting and arranging their own playing as if it were found sound. The result keeps hip-hop's chopped, looped construction but avoids clearing anyone else's recordings.

The song lives on its {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}: two words, "the bomb", slang for something excellent, repeated until they stick. A hook like this is designed to be understood and sung on the first listen, which is exactly what made the song useful to advertisers and television: in late 2011 Cartoon Network used it to promote the premiere of its live-action TV film Level Up.

## Artistic Context

Pigeon John is the Los Angeles rapper John Dust, born in 1972, a former member of the collective L.A. Symphony. He began in the early 1990s in the duo Brainwash Projects, released his first solo album in 2001 and moved to Quannum Projects after touring with Lyrics Born. The Bomb travelled well in Europe: in 2011 it was issued as a CD single in Germany, Austria and Switzerland through Universal's Urban label, and the official video embedded here is published by the French arm of [PIAS]. His voice also leads the French turntablists C2C's {{ <track_link source="c2c-feat-pigeon-john-because-of-you.md" /> }}.
