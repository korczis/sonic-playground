## Genre and Mood

**IN PARALLEL**'s **NOW IT'S GONE (ELY OAKS REMIX)** is a remix of a UK garage single by the Austrian DJ and producer Ely Oaks. It is filed under dance and electronic music, and as energetic and hypnotic, from what Songfacts says of the original: looped, chopped vocal snippets over a club beat. No review describes this remix itself, and this page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

NOW IT'S GONE is the debut single of IN PARALLEL, a UK-based duo of George Denver and Harrison Baker, according to Songfacts; the label's metadata credits the composition to George Stevenson and Harrison Baker. Songfacts describes the original as a heady mix of deep basslines, melodic hooks and beats, linked to UK garage and bassline, with vocals made from sampled snippets that are looped and processed with pitch modulation and rhythmic chopping. The original appeared on Method 808 on 17 October 2024; Ely Oaks's remix followed on 22 November, with IN PARALLEL credited as producers and Maxwell Dowling as mixing and mastering engineer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a track from its parts. Released five weeks after the original, this one gave a new single a second version before it had charted at all.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} is how the song is built: by Songfacts' account, the voice is not a performance sung for the track but snippets taken and looped.

{{ <concept_link name="pitch shifting" /> }} is among the processing Songfacts names: raised or lowered, a voice can become less a singer and more an instrument.

## Artistic Context

Ely Oaks is Elias Grauß, an Austrian DJ and producer based in Berlin. A few months after this remix, his own Running Around became his first hit; hear it in another producer's remix, {{ <track_link source="ely-oaks-running-around-niall-t-remix.md" /> }}. IN PARALLEL, Songfacts says, grew up on festival dance tents and Ministry of Sound and Hedkandi compilations.

## Impact and Reception

The song had an unusual chart life. On the Official Singles Chart it peaked at number 76 and spent 23 weeks in the top 100 between February and July 2025; Songfacts reports that by late June it had spent 20 consecutive weeks there without reaching the top 75, a record for that part of the chart. It reached number 17 on the Official Dance Singles Chart, with 36 weeks there. Play it next to {{ <track_link source="bovski-low-again.md" /> }}.
