## Genre and Mood

**Blood Orchid**'s **Melatonin** sits where alternative rock meets electronic pop. Marmoset, the music-licensing agency that carries it, files it under rock, pop and electronic, and Apple Music lists the single as dance. The feelings it is tagged with, gritty, rebellious and mysterious among them, suit a song about refusing the outside world while admitting that the room you hide in is no cure.

## Song Summary

The lyric, printed under the official video, portrays someone not in the mood to talk, always alone in their room, staring at a phone with nothing to do, and with no melatonin left: the sleep hormone, in the song's words, has been removed. The chorus answers every question in advance. The narrator is fine, does not like to go outside, and repeats being addicted to this life three times, before conceding to never being satisfied. Musically, Marmoset lists electric guitar, drum machine and synthesizer under a male lead vocal, with a medium energy and a steady arc.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review walks through the track, so this follows the lyric's own structure, as printed with the video, and the library's description of the sound.

### Verse 1

Four short lines set the scene. Each verse ends on the same line about melatonin, which works as a small refrain inside the verse.

### Chorus

The chorus is built on repetition. Its central line comes three times in a row, then the turn arrives: never satisfied, so please don't kill my vibe, and the repetition starts again.

### Verse 2

The second verse moves from the room to the mind: a state of déjà vu, a residue the narrator tries to remove, a point of view nobody else will see. It ends on the melatonin line again before the chorus returns.

### Music Video

The official video, published on 27 January 2020, was directed by Digital Boy and Alyssa Wood. It runs 2:56, against the 3:16 of the single embedded here.

## Artistic Context

Blood Orchid is an American alternative rock and indie pop project formed in Miami, Florida, in 2019 by the multi-instrumentalist Niko Javan, according to Marmoset. Melatonin came out as a single on 1 January 2020, with a 2019 phonographic copyright held by Chrome Lotus. The same day, the project posted the full-length audio on its own YouTube channel.

## Impact and Reception

Melatonin has no chart history or press reviews that we could find. Marmoset's staff marked it as a staff pick, which puts it in front of the filmmakers and advertisers who license music there. Put it on late at night, when the phone is still lit.
