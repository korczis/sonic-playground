## Genre and Mood

**Lukas Graham** made **Drunk in the Morning** in the pop-soul mix the band is known for. It is playful, catchy and upbeat: a song about a bad decision at five in the morning, set to piano chords and horns and written with a wink.

## Song Summary

The lyric is a phone call. The narrator has been out with his friends, it is late or very early, he is not sure who he is calling, and he hopes she is still awake and lonely enough to let him come by. Kim Taylor Bennett of Noisey called it a playful request for a late-night booty call and an obvious radio hit, thanks to its perky piano chords and catchy hooks. The version heard here is the one on the band's 2016 international album, credited to the producers Future Animals, Pilo and Brody Brown.

## Musical Breakdown

No timed analysis is published, so this follows the lyric and the credits.

### Verse

The first lines set the scene in plain words: the hour, the uncertainty, the time since they last spoke, over the piano.

### Chorus

The title phrase arrives as the answer to the verse, sung over and over until it is the part you remember.

### Arrangement

The credits name the players around Lukas Forchhammer's voice: Morten Ristorp on piano, Fender Rhodes and saxophone, Brody Brown on bass guitar, Morten Jessen on trombone, Tobias Wiklund on trumpet and Mark Falgren on drum programming.

## Concepts in Focus

The chorus is a textbook {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}: a short phrase, the song's own title, repeated until it sticks. The Arts Desk's Thomas H. Green, otherwise cool on the album, called the song astutely calibrated pop, and the chorus is where the calibration shows.

This page is about an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. The song first appeared in 2012 at 3:30, produced by Backbone. On the 2016 international edition of the band's second album it runs 3:23 with new producer credits, and MusicBrainz lists it as a new version. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="lukas-graham-7-years.md" /> }}, the hit that opened that album.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} came in two stages. Noisey reports that Forchhammer first posted a homemade video, his friends drinking beer in the kitchen, against his label's advice; it drew two and a half million views, and the official video of 2012 drew twice that. An official video directed by Marc Klasfeld is on the band's channel.

## Artistic Context

Lukas Graham formed in 2011 in Copenhagen, around Forchhammer, who was born in 1988 in Christiania, the self-governing community there. Drunk in the Morning came out on 13 February 2012 on Copenhagen Records as the second single from the band's debut album, written by Forchhammer, Sebastian Fogh, Stefan Forrest, Morten Ristorp, Magnus Larsson and Falgren.

## Impact and Reception

The single went to number one in Denmark and was certified four times platinum there; it also reached number 30 in Germany, 42 in Austria and 127 in the UK. Play it at a sensible hour.
