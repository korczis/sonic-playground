## Genre and Mood

**Benin City** wrote the song; **Hold Them Close (Rachel K Collier Remix)** is the version **Rachel K Collier** made of it, one of two the band commissioned in spring 2020. The song is indie electronic music; the remix rebuilds it from its parts. Heartfelt is the song's message; energetic follows the band's own word for what came back, a banger. No published review describes how this remix sounds, so this page sets out what is known around it and leaves the listening to you.

## Song Summary

Glasse Factory's review of the original single says the band wrote and rewrote it after a string of world events, Brexit and Hurricane Maria among them, and that its message is simple: hold the people you love close while there is still time. The critic heard The xx and LCD Soundsystem in it, called it a pulsing track, and praised how Joshua Idehen's and Shanaz Dorsett's voices blend. The remix came out on 26 June 2020 on Hold Them Close (The Remixes): Collier's version, 3:34, first, and a remix by Jo Goes Hunting, 3:16, second.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version made by reworking a finished recording's parts. This one followed a spring spent indoors. The band had planned a March tour with a London headline show featuring Jo Goes Hunting and Rachel K Collier, two of what it called its artist-crushes. Then, in the band's words, 2020 had to 2020, and it asked the two to remix the song instead.

The Bandcamp credits are unusually complete. Collier produced the remix and also did its {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }}, balancing every part into one stereo track, and its {{ <concept_link name="mastering" /> }}, the final pass over the whole sound before release. On the second track the work is split: Jo Goes Hunting produced and mixed it, and Joshua Eustis mastered it.

## Artistic Context

Benin City formed in 2008, took its name from the Nigerian city Idehen's parents came from, and mixes spoken word poetry with electronica and brass. The trio released Fires in the Park on Audio Doughnuts in 2013 and Last Night on Moshi Moshi in April 2018, an ode to London's shrinking nightlife. Collier is a Welsh electronic producer and performer from Mumbles in Swansea. She featured on Ray Foxx's Boom Boom (Heartbeat), number 12 in the UK in 2013, and teaches Ableton Live as a certified trainer. Hear her as a featured singer on {{ <track_link source="ryan-blyth_once-twice-feat-rachel-k-collier.md" /> }} and beside JPOLND on {{ <track_link source="jpolnd-rachel-k-collier-toxic.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Glasse Factory called the original a very good single. No source records how the remixes were received; they remain a small document of a spring when a tour turned into a remix swap. Start with the band's original, then put on Collier's version and hear the same plea carried by one producer from production to master.
