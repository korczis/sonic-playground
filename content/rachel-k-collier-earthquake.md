## Genre and Mood

**Rachel K Collier**'s **Earthquake** is compact electronic dance-pop: a track of under three minutes with two drops, a bassline, chopped vocals and sung verses. The title promises force, and the shape of the track, built towards a first drop and a final one, is energetic and bold by design.

## Song Summary

Earthquake was made for an episode of Andrew Huang's YouTube series 4 Producers, this time 4 Producers vs Arturia, published on 10 November 2022: four producers, SoWylie, L.Dre, Collier and Huang, each made a track, and the instrument maker Arturia sponsored the show. Collier released hers as a single two days earlier, on 8 November 2022. No review of the track turned up; what we know of its sound comes from the producer's own breakdown video and its chapter titles.

## Musical Breakdown

Collier's breakdown walks through the track in this order: the PolyBrute, the first drop, the bassline, vocal chops, the second verse, vocals, the final drop, synth work, an ad-lib vocal freeze and the outro. The parts below follow it; how each one sounds, no written source says, so listen for them yourself.

### Intro

The breakdown starts with Arturia's PolyBrute synthesizer; which of the opening sounds it plays, the chapter titles do not say.

### Drop

The first drop is the first arrival, followed in the breakdown by the bassline and the vocal chops.

### Verse 2

The second verse and the vocals come next, the song inside the dance track.

### Climax

The final drop is the peak, with more synth work and the ad-lib vocal freeze, a vocal effect she gives its own chapter, before the outro closes the track.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} is the moment an electronic track has been building towards, when the full beat and bass arrive. Earthquake has two, named in Collier's breakdown as the first and the final drop, so even in under three minutes the track has a shape: build, arrive, return, arrive again.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is close to the brief here. The episode was sponsored by Arturia, and Collier's breakdown opens with the company's PolyBrute before anything else: it is the first instrument she presents.

Between the drops sits the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}, which gets its own chapter right after the first drop. Compare how {{ <track_link source="rachel-k-collier-don-t-know-what.md" /> }}, made for the previous challenge, found its groove only once drums were added to a bassline she found too plain.

## Artistic Context

Collier is a Welsh producer, singer and songwriter from Swansea. She came up as a featured vocalist, most successfully on Ray Foxx's Boom Boom (Heartbeat), a UK number 12 in 2013, and is now known for multi-instrument live performances of her own songs and for teaching production as an Ableton Certified Trainer. Earthquake opens her album Songs in the Key of Internet, released on 29 September 2023, which also collects Don't Know What.

## Impact and Reception

No chart or press review of Earthquake turned up. Like its predecessor, it was published together with the challenge episode and Collier's own breakdown, a track and its lesson side by side. Play it loud enough to feel the final drop.
