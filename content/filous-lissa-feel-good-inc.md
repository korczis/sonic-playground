## Genre and Mood

**filous** and **LissA** take **Feel Good Inc.**, one of the most recognisable songs of the 2000s, and slow it into a chillout daydream. It is electronic and it is a cover, but it is above all a mood: dreamy, relaxed and quietly nostalgic, the sound of a famous tune remembered on a lazy afternoon.

## Song Summary

The Gorillaz original, the 2005 lead single from Demon Days, is twitchy and sardonic, a song about numbness and manufactured happiness with a cackling laugh and a De La Soul rap at its centre. This version drops the rap and the menace. What remains is the melody, carried by LissA's soft, airy voice over a delicate xylophone, plucked strings and an easy, padded beat. Dancing Astronaut called it "subtler than the original, more subdued", full of "glassy chords and crystalline, kinetic melodies". The irony of the lyric survives, but it now sounds wistful rather than bitter.

## Musical Breakdown

The arrangement grows slowly from a single melodic line to a gentle electronic groove and back again.

### Intro

A delicate xylophone plays the familiar melody before the beat appears, so the song creeps up on you instead of announcing itself.

### Verse 1

LissA sings the verse close and calm, while soft kicks and light percussion settle in beneath her.

### Chorus

The famous hook arrives without any of its original swagger. It floats, cushioned by glassy chords, and the "feel good" line suddenly sounds sincere.

### Main Section

filous lets the instrumental breathe between vocal passages, layering plucked, mandolin-like strings and gentle electronic swells into a sun-hazed groove.

### Outro

The beat slips away and the melodic figures are left alone again, closing the circle the intro opened.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} that keeps very little: the melody and the words. The rap, the bassline's bite, the laugh and the band's attitude are all gone, and the instruments are new. That makes it a good test of what a song is. If you still recognise Feel Good Inc. from a xylophone and a soft voice, the identity of the song lives in its melody, not in its production. Compare the remix approach of {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-19-2000-soulchild-remix.md" /> }}, which reworks Gorillaz's own recording rather than replaying the song.

What replaces the original's grit is {{ <concept_link name="ambient texture" /> }}: sustained, softly glowing chords and small, chiming melodic figures that create atmosphere rather than drive. Cover Me noted how LissA's light voice, set against the deep voices of the original, gives the song a fairytale quality. The texture is the reason the lyric's irony now feels gentle; the music no longer sneers along with the words.

## Artistic Context

filous is an Austrian producer who built his following on remixes and covers that pair acoustic sounds with soft electronic production, and EARMILK introduced him in 2016 as an 18-year-old. He released this cover with the singer LissA in February 2016 to mark the start of his From Dusk Till Dawn tour of Europe, following his debut EP Dawn. The source is a bold choice: the Gorillaz single reached number 2 in the UK and number 14 in the US, and few songs of that decade are as instantly identifiable.

## Impact and Reception

The cover found its audience fast on streaming and blogs; EARMILK welcomed its chill spin on the classic, and in 2020 Cover Me picked it among its five favourite covers of the song. It belongs here as proof that a great melody can wear a completely different mood. Play it after the original and hear how much one song can hold.
