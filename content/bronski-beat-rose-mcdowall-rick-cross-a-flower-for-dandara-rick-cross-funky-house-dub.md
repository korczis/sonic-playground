## Genre and Mood

**Bronski Beat**'s **A Flower for Dandara**, with **Rose McDowall**, is heard here in **Rick Cross**'s Funky House Dub, one of eight club remixes of the song released in July 2017. The genre is in the title: house, by way of funk, in the long dub form DJs use. The mood is set by the song's purpose as much as by its beat. It is a memorial made for dancing, groovy by design and defiant in what it remembers.

## Song Summary

The song is a tribute to Dandara dos Santos, a Brazilian travesti beaten and shot to death in Fortaleza on 15 February 2017. Her killing became public sixteen days later, when videos of the attack spread on social media, and it led to rallies against LGBTphobia and to a day against transphobia in the state of Ceará. Bronski Beat, whose first single had been about a gay teenager forced to leave home, answered with a song carrying her name. No official upload of this dub or of the single was found, so the page has no video.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Song

A Flower for Dandara closes the main disc of The Age of Reason, sung by the reformed band's vocalist Stephen Granville with Rose McDowall, once of Strawberry Switchblade, and written by Steve Bronski, Ian Donaldson and Granville.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks the parts of a recording into a new version, often by another producer. The Remixed single gathers eight of them, and their titles promise different rooms: a Mainroom Remix, a Club Anthem, a Late Night Mood, a Funky House Dub.

The word {{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }} in a remix title goes back to Jamaican studio practice: strip a record towards drums and bass and treat the rest with echo and drop-outs. In club music a dub mix traditionally pares the vocal back and leaves DJs a longer groove; how far this one goes, no source says.

Rose McDowall's credit as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} joins two acts of 1980s pop, Strawberry Switchblade and Bronski Beat, on one record.

## Artistic Context

Bronski Beat formed in London in 1983 around Jimmy Somerville, Steve Bronski and Larry Steinbachek. In 2016 Bronski revived the name with Ian Donaldson and the singer Stephen Granville, and on 28 July 2017 Strike Force Entertainment, a Cherry Red imprint, released The Age of Reason, a reworking of the debut album The Age of Consent, with A Flower for Dandara as one of its new songs. The Remixed single came a week earlier, on 21 July. Steve Bronski died in 2021.

No chart or review records this dub. Its value lies in what it carries: Dandara's name, from a Brazilian street to the dance floor. Read about her first, then listen.
