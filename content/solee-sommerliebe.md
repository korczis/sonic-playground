## Genre and Mood

**Solee** released **Sommerliebe** on an EP that Discogs files under tech house, deep house and experimental. The feelings on this page, dreamy and warm, are our reading of a long instrumental club track whose title means summer love; no review describes how it sounds, so take them as a starting point rather than a verdict.

## Song Summary

Sommerliebe runs eight minutes 27 seconds and shares the EP Sommerliebe / Traumschiff with a second original, Traumschiff, or dream ship. Parquet Recordings released it digitally in August 2013 under the catalogue number PARQUET064: Discogs gives 16 August, MusicBrainz 30 August. Two remixes of Sommerliebe complete the package, by Eelke Kleijn and by Roland M. Dill and Koook, along with a Normandie remix of Solee's earlier track Blown. A month before the release, on 12 July 2013, Solee posted a video edit of four minutes 19 on his own channel. The track returned on his album The Seven Year Itch that December and, in 2026, on the retrospective XX, whose upload this page plays.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} cuts a track down for listeners who are not on a dance floor. Solee's video edit does that job for YouTube: half the length of the original mix, it is the version made to be watched rather than mixed.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} sends the same track in other directions. Eelke Kleijn's version was the one that travelled: it appeared on the label's compilation Parquet Goodies 2013 and on Milk & Sugar's mixed compilation House Nation, and Finnebassen played it at Sankeys Ibiza for DanceTrippin. That is {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} at work, with DJs choosing what a crowd hears and carrying a record far beyond its label.
