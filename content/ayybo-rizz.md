## Genre and Mood

**AYYBO**'s **RIZZ** is a tech house single of three minutes and twenty seconds, made for the floor and sold on its groove. It sits squarely inside house and electronic dance music: the only descriptions available, a promotion channel's note and the shape of its release, put the weight on the bass and the percussion. Groovy and energetic is the brief.

## Song Summary

No critic has written about RIZZ, so this page stays close to what is documented. The single came out on 15 November 2023 on GDB Records, the label AYYBO runs himself, with an extended mix released the same day. Its title is the internet slang word for charm or attractiveness, popularised by the streamer Kai Cenat and named word of the year for 2023 by Oxford University Press a few weeks after the release. AYYBO directed the official video with Nolan Rugg, edited it himself and dedicated it to Elijah Ruiz, who handled its lighting and grip.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the low, repeated figure that carries a house track's movement, and in tech house it often matters more than any melody. Gray Area heard a bassline-heavy, almost ominous tech house sound in his earlier tracks GDB and Do This, and his single {{ <track_link source="ayybo-bass-glide.md" /> }} named itself after the bass.

The {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is the version for DJs: longer, with room to blend into and out of the next record. RIZZ came out in both shapes on the same day, one for streaming and one for the booth.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is where those booth versions end up. In February 2024 LP Giobbi included RIZZ on her fabric presents Continuum: Life and Death mix, between Alan Dixon and Gab Rhome, which shows how a self-released single can travel into the mix series of the London club fabric.

## Artistic Context

AYYBO is Aaron Bonnema, a house producer and DJ from Anaheim, California, who focused on cinematography and the visual arts before music, which shows in a video he directed and edited himself. He releases his singles on his own small label, GDB Records, and uses TikTok as a sounding board, posting snippets of unreleased music and asking his followers for feedback before he releases a track.

## Impact and Reception

The clearest sign of the track's reach is the fabric mix: a few months after release, another DJ chose it for a compilation built to be heard from start to finish. Play the single, then the extended mix, and listen for what the extra two minutes are for.
