## Genre and Mood

**Luca Malina**'s **Trust Me When I Lie** is the title track of her debut EP, and her own site gives the coordinates: a mix of her own between alternative pop, R&B and electronic sounds, "mal zart, mal punchy, aber immer frech", sometimes tender, sometimes punchy, always cheeky. Pop, R&B and electronic, then, and the feelings this page reads in the title and that description are irreverent and emotional.

## Song Summary

The song came out on 28 July 2023 with the EP of the same name on Matches Music. The YouTube credits list Luca Malina as singer, her lyrics written with Christina Kerschner, the music composed by the two of them with Luca Pivetz and Robert Wallner, and Pivetz and Wallner as producers, with Nikodem Milewski as engineer. It runs 3:11. Her site says her lyrics explore relationships between people and dive deep into intense emotional worlds; no source we found discusses this lyric, so the title, a request that contradicts itself, is all this page quotes.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or interview we found describes Trust Me When I Lie, so this page describes less than usual and offers a way to listen instead.

### Main Section

Start with the voice: her site says it switches effortlessly between soft and powerful, and a three-minute song is enough to hear whether this one does. Then listen to what the two producers have built around it, and to how the title's contradiction is carried by the music as well as the words.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes the sound of a recording; here the two producers, Luca Pivetz and Robert Wallner, are also credited as composers, so the line between writing the song and making the record is blurred. Pivetz had produced her 2021 single {{ <track_link source="luca-malina-fckrz.md" /> }} as well, so this EP grows out of a working partnership.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} balances the finished parts into one recording. Nikodem Milewski, credited as engineer on this song, also mixed and mastered FCKRZ; on the EP as a whole, her site credits three people with mixing and three with mastering, a sign of how many hands a short independent release can pass through.

## Artistic Context

Luca Malina was born in Vienna in 2000 into a family of artists, Falter writes, with dance, acting and music at home. Her site dates her first release to 2020, calls her an art student as well as a musician, and notes her work as an actress, in films such as Lovecut. The EP, catalogue number LMEP001, was supported by the SKE Fonds and the Österreichischer Musikfonds, Austria's music fund.

## Impact and Reception

Her site says the EP took the charts, without naming which; we found no chart record and no review of the song. It led to her debut album, I don't wanna be so slow, in October 2025. Read the credits, then play the song and decide what it means to trust someone when they lie.
