## Genre and Mood

**"High Enough - RAC Remix"** hands **K.Flay**'s alternative hit to **RAC**, the Portland producer whose name began as the Remix Artist Collective. Qobuz files it under alternative and indie; RAC's own records are indie electronic. The feelings are the song's own: uplifting and reflective, a love song that asks whether it needs anything stronger than what it already feels.

## Song Summary

K.Flay has said High Enough began as a reply to the many songs about getting high: what if I am already high enough? She wrote it with Mike Elizondo, who produced the original. The remix single, released on 4 August 2017 by Interscope and Night Street Records, credits RAC as remixer, Elizondo as producer and Adam Hawkins as mixer. It runs 3:28, shorter than the original's 3:51. We found no review that describes what RAC changed, so this page says what the credits establish and leaves the listening to you.

### The Song

What any remix of it starts from: K.Flay's flow, halfway between rap and singing, and a chorus that turns the title into the hook.

### The Remix

RAC is credited as remixer, while the writers, the producer and the mixer stay named; the words and melody are K.Flay's, the frame is his.

### The Length

At 3:28, it is a radio-length version, twenty-three seconds shorter than the original.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version built from a recording's parts, usually keeping the vocal and rebuilding the music around it. RAC had just won the Grammy for Best Remixed Recording with {{ <track_link source="bob-moses-tearing-me-up.md" /> }}, so this song went to a remixer fresh from the top award of his field.

The credits also show that a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} and a remixer are different roles. Elizondo, credited as producer, shaped the original recording; RAC reshaped it afterwards. Adam Hawkins is credited with {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }}, the stage where the separate parts are balanced into the finished track.

## Artistic Context

RAC is André Allen Anjos, born in Portugal in 1985. He started the Remix Artist Collective in January 2007 as a student at Greenville College in Illinois, aiming to move beyond the club-mix style, and later released albums of his own, Strangers in 2014 and Ego in 2017. K.Flay, born Kristine Flaherty, released the original {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }} on 10 March 2017 as the third single from her album Every Where Is Some Where.

## Impact and Reception

The original reached number 4 on Billboard's Alternative Airplay chart and was certified platinum in the US. Wikipedia lists the RAC remix among the song's digital releases, but we found no review or chart entry for it. Play it after the original and decide what RAC brought to it.
