## Genre and Mood

**Chicks On Speed** recorded **Kaltes Klares Wasser** in 1999 and put it on their debut album, Chicks On Speed Will Save Us All!, which Wikipedia files under electroclash and Discogs under electro, synth-pop and house. It is a cover, and an energetic, catchy one: a 2002 review praised its immediate, out-and-out appeal against the darker mood of the song it came from.

## Song Summary

The song belongs to Malaria!, the West Berlin band of Bettina Köster and Gudrun Gut, and first appeared in 1982 on the 12-inch Weisses Wasser: White Water. Its title means cold, clear water. Chicks On Speed kept the German words, credited to Köster, added lyrics of their own and produced the track with Tobi Neumann at Apollo Studios in 1999. The album version runs 4:37.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the cover section by section, so this breakdown compares versions, as its first reviewers did.

### The Original

The gullbuy review heard Malaria!'s recording as early-1980s dark new wave, given its edge by the singer's deeper voice and built on a grand, sweeping scale.

### The Chicks Version

The same reviewer heard Chicks On Speed turn it into something entirely different and yet just as special, the version with the immediate appeal.

### The Other Side

Barbara Morgenstern's version on the B-side of the same 10-inch stayed much closer to the original in music and structure, calmer and less immediate.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} can be faithful or can transform its source, and the 2000 10-inch on Monika Enterprise and Go Records put both kinds on one record: the Chicks version on one side, Morgenstern's on the other. Hear how differently the group treat Cracker in {{ <track_link source="chicks-on-speed-euro-trash-girl.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }} was the scene Chicks On Speed helped start, and covering Malaria! drew a line from early-1980s German electronic new wave to their own late-1990s electro.

The song then lived on as a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} record: in 2001 Superstar Recordings issued it as Malaria! vs. Chicks On Speed, with versions by Elektrochemie LK, DJ Koze and Thomas Schumacher.

## Artistic Context

Chicks On Speed formed in Munich in 1997 at the Academy of Fine Arts, a feminist art collective as much as a band; the album's writing credits name Alex Murray-Leslie, Melissa Logan and Kiki Moorse. Malaria!, active from 1981 to 1993, grew out of the band Mania D and is linked with Neue Deutsche Welle and no wave. The official video was directed by Deborah Schamoni.

## Impact and Reception

Released as a single after the album, the cover took Chicks On Speed onto the German version of Top of the Pops, as Drowned in Sound later noted, though not onto British television. Critics of the album praised its reinterpretations of 1980s songs, and the song itself later reached a Hollywood film, X-Men: Apocalypse, according to Wikipedia's article on Malaria!. Play the two versions back to back.
