## Genre and Mood

**Yeah Yeah Yeahs**' **Heads Will Roll** is dance-punk made by an alternative rock band, energetic, dark and theatrical at once. It is usually described as dance-rock, a shift from the garage rock and art punk the trio was known for, and Grammy.com heard in it the glittering 1970s disco and 1980s glam-punk of It's Blitz!'s louder tracks.

## Song Summary

The song is about an ill-fated night on the dancefloor, and Karen O's lyric was inspired by horror stories. Credited to the whole band and produced by Nick Launay and David Andrew Sitek, it was released in June 2009 as the second single from It's Blitz!. Sheet music published by Universal Music Publishing Group puts it in 4/4 and G minor at 132 beats per minute.

## Musical Breakdown

No source divides the song into timed sections; what the sources do describe is how it was built.

### The Riff

A vintage-sounding string riff drives the song. Nick Zinner played it, and it came from a Mellotron sample on a sound card.

### The Beat

The rhythm uses pre-recorded samples of Brian Chase's drums, a departure from the band's earlier sessions. The Austin Chronicle heard a Donna Summer and Cher backbeat, and noted that Zinner's jagged guitar now takes a back seat to a synthesizer.

### The Voice

Karen O wrote the lyric and sings it; the sources describe its subject, not the delivery.

## Concepts in Focus

The album was built from the {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} up. Launay described a typical session: Chase played many drumbeats, the producers chopped them into a groove loop, Zinner jammed over it, and Karen O found a vocal melody on top. Heads Will Roll came out of one of those sessions, in one of the five studios the band used.

The {{ <concept_link name="analog synthesizer" /> }} at its centre is Zinner's vintage ARP. Classic Rock reported that he packed it for the sessions in Tornillo, west Texas, as downtime entertainment, and it became the defining noise of the album.

The {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by the Canadian DJ A-Trak, released in November 2009, ran 6:23 and was cut to a radio edit; Rolling Stone ranked it 124th of the 200 greatest dance songs.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Richard Ayoade, sets the band in an underground club where a dancing werewolf attacks the crowd; red glitter and confetti stand in for blood, and Karen O keeps singing after being decapitated. It was nominated for an MTV Video Music Award for Breakthrough Video.

## Artistic Context

Karen O, Zinner and Chase came up in the New York garage rock and post-punk revival of the 2000s with Fever to Tell (2003). It's Blitz!, their third album, was released in March 2009 on Interscope and nominated for the Grammy for Best Alternative Music Album.

## Impact and Reception

Heads Will Roll became the band's best-selling single, reached the top ten of three Billboard charts and was certified double platinum. Scarlett Johansson sang it in Sing 2, Nia Archives sampled the A-Trak remix in 2023, and the BBC used it as its theme for the 2025 Women's Euro. Start with the original, then the remix.
