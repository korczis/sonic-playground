## Genre and Mood

**Icare Sandilas** makes electronic music that leans toward the alternative, and **Chaos** is its title track: atmospheric and introspective, if the words are any guide. No review of the song turned up in our research, so this page leans on what the artist has published, the lyric, the video and the description of the project on his profiles, and says less about the sound than usual.

## Song Summary

Chaos came out as a single on 28 May 2021 and a month later, on 25 June, as the second of seven tracks on the release of the same name, between Brothers and Over, a collaboration with ORI. Both appeared on Rouge Neon Records; the track runs 3:21. The lyric, posted with the video, begins by waking up in chaos in a shivering town, addressing someone who floats around with a demon inside, and turns toward wanting something to pull everyone together. It ends on a paradox: all alone, but together.

## Concepts in Focus

Chaos is a small lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }}. Icare Sandilas is not a band but a name: MusicBrainz identifies it as Adrien Casalis, and the video's description says the song was originally released under VIMALA, his earlier project name. His profile on Gotobeat presents the change as a new turn for the same composer and producer, continuing the trajectory of his sound. A project name lets an artist mark a new phase without changing the person behind it.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was shot and directed by Adrien Casalis himself, with mastering credited to Studio RTM. When the producer also holds the camera, image and sound come from one hand, which suits a project built on atmosphere.

## Artistic Context

Gotobeat describes the project as blending electronic minimalism, mystical pop and experimental textures, with deep, cinematic soundscapes where organic and electronic sounds meet, and names its inspirations as Nicolas Jaar, DARKSIDE, Labrinth and Oneohtrix Point Never; compare {{ <track_link source="nicolas-jaar-time-for-us.md" /> }} and {{ <track_link source="darkside-paper-trails.md" /> }}. On SoundCloud he calls himself a music producer and film composer. His releases on Rouge Neon Records go back to First EP in 2017; Hope followed Chaos in 2022, and the twelve-track Forever and Ever in 2026.
