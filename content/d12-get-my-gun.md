## Genre and Mood

**D12**'s **Get My Gun** is hip-hop and rap at its most cartoonish: aggressive, irreverent and dark, a round of threats so exaggerated that they turn into jokes. HotNewHipHop called it gleefully twisted, a showcase for the group's taste for gallows humour and over-the-top violence.

## Song Summary

The track sits eleventh on D12 World, the Detroit group's second album, released on 27 April 2004. The premise is in the title: each member describes a quarrel that ends with him going off to fetch a weapon, and each tries to top the one before. Eminem produced it, with Luis Resto on additional production and keyboards. Cleveland Scene, otherwise unimpressed by Eminem's beats on the record, made one exception for its fuzz-bass groove.

## Musical Breakdown

No review walks through the track in order; the parts below follow the Genius transcription.

### Intro

Eminem and Kon Artis share the opening lines.

### Verse 1

Swifty McVay sets the track off. HotNewHipHop called him the arguable standout, riding a cartoonishly sinister instrumental with a sharpened flow.

### Chorus

Eminem takes the hook, each of his lines answered by the title shouted back like a slogan.

### The Middle Verses

Kuniva and Proof follow; then Eminem plays a skit over the chorus and takes a verse in which the joke turns on himself: too drunk to aim, he hits the wrong man.

### Outro

Kon Artis takes the fifth verse, and Bizarre closes the track with a petty errand that escalates absurdly.

## Concepts in Focus

Get My Gun is a {{ <concept_link name="posse cut" /> }}: one beat, many rappers. Every member lends a verse, and the order matters, from Swifty's sharp opening to Bizarre's comic exit.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the shouted title. Returning after almost every verse, it gives six voices one frame.

The beat shows what a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} brings to a group record. Cleveland Scene praised this groove, and musicCRITIC compared the track with G-Unit's gun song My Buddy and judged D12's version far better executed. Compare the slower crunk of {{ <track_link source="d12-40-oz.md" /> }} on the same album.

## Artistic Context

D12 World came out on Shady Records and Interscope, executive-produced by Eminem, with other beats from Kanye West, Dr. Dre and Hi-Tek. It debuted at number one in the US and the UK and went double platinum in America. It was also the last D12 album with Proof, who died in 2006, and the last with Eminem as a member. Its title track, {{ <track_link source="d12-d-12-world.md" /> }}, shows the crew over an outside producer's beat.

## Impact and Reception

The album drew mixed reviews, an average of 58 on Metacritic. Plugged In listed a line from Get My Gun among the raps it said glamorise thug life, while HotNewHipHop revisited it in 2021 as one of the album's highlights. The track was later an unlockable song in the 2006 video game Scarface: The World Is Yours. Listen for the turn of each new voice.
