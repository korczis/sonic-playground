## Genre and Mood

**Miike Snow** blend indie pop with house music in **"Paddling Out,"** a piano-driven electropop song on a four-on-the-floor beat, with touches of disco, Italo house and acid jazz. It sounds upbeat and ready for a dance floor, but the mood underneath is reflective and wistful.

## Song Summary

"Paddling Out" pairs a bright arrangement with a melancholic lyric, a contrast one critic summed up as sing-along melancholy. Glitchy piano chords and a steady house kick drive it, synthesizers and the odd siren-like sound fill it out, and Andrew Wyatt's cool, almost androgynous vocal floats above. The memorable detail is that tension between the body, which wants to dance, and the words, which look back.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro: Inviting Vibes

The track opens with glitchy piano chords that set an upbeat tone before the beat arrives.

### Build-up: Rhythmic Energy

The four-on-the-floor kick and the drums come in, and synths and percussion are layered on into a danceable groove.

### Main Section: Catchy Melodies

The chorus stands out, its hook catchy and singable, Wyatt's voice cool against the warmth of the piano.

### Climax: Emotional Release

The arrangement is at its fullest as the elements come together for the song's peak.

### Outro: Reflective Closure

The song draws to a close and the sound recedes, leaving the wistful mood behind.

## Concepts in Focus

The engine is {{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" /> }}, a kick drum on every beat of the bar, the pulse of disco and house. Put under piano chords and an indie vocal, it changes the song's category: what could have been a mid-tempo ballad becomes something a DJ can play. The live drums of Thomas Hedlund, a touring member of Phoenix, give it a human edge.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} sits in the chorus and is built for singing along, which is exactly what makes the melancholy of the lyric so effective: you find yourself singing a sad line happily. The same trick drives {{ <track_link source="foster-the-people-pumped-up-kicks.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Andreas Nilsson, follows a man abducted by childlike aliens in Victorian dress and transformed into a "Perfect Human". It is the first part of a trilogy continued in the videos for "The Wave" and "Pretender", so the album's singles also work as chapters of one story.

## Artistic Context

Miike Snow is a Stockholm-based trio formed in 2007 by Christian Karlsson and Pontus Winnberg, the production duo Bloodshy & Avant, who won a Grammy for Britney Spears's "Toxic", and the American singer Andrew Wyatt. "Paddling Out" was recorded in 2011 in a studio previously used by ABBA and released on 23 January 2012 as the lead single from their second album, *Happy to You*, which the band produced themselves and which came out in March 2012 on Columbia in the UK and Downtown and Universal Republic in the US. Remixes came from Wolfgang Gartner, Carli and Jacques Lu Cont.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews were generally positive: PopMatters called it the best track on *Happy to You*, and The Guardian praised its catchiness. It reached number 9 on the Flemish Ultratip chart, 32 on the Dutch Top 40 and 17 on the UK dance chart, and it found a wider audience through the soundtracks of FIFA 13 and Forza Horizon and a UK Budweiser advert. Put it on and see whether you hear the joy or the sadness first.
