## Genre and Mood

**MEUTE** and **pølaroit** made **Verden** from the meeting of two ways of playing electronic music: an acoustic marching band that arranges techno and deep house, and a producer duo with synthesizers and e-drums. No review of the single has been found, so the feelings on this page, hypnotic and atmospheric, are its own reading of the track rather than a critic's.

## Song Summary

Verden came out on 14 June 2023 as a single on the label TUMULT, five minutes and forty-four seconds long. It is not one of the band's arrangements of another producer's club track but a new piece written together: the credits name Marius Lesemann and Jonas Gewald of pølaroit and MEUTE's sousaphonist Philipp Westermann as writers, and Westermann as arranger. It was produced by the two members of pølaroit with Daniel Bongard and MEUTE's founder Thomas Burhorn. No source explains the title or describes the music itself.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} sets a piece for the instruments that play it. MEUTE's whole idea depends on it: the band, in its own words, wants to detach electronic music from the DJ desk, so every synth line and drum pattern has to be rewritten for horns, reeds, marimba and drums. Here Westermann arranged a track he also co-wrote.

{{ <concept_link name="electronic drums" text="Electronic drums" /> }} are pads struck like drums that trigger electronic sounds. Lesemann plays them, so even the electronic side of the track is performed rather than only programmed.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, filmed by Steffi Retti, shows the musicians in Hanover playing the track before a show; its credits add a lighting and a monitor engineer. It runs a few seconds longer than the single, and no source says whether its sound is the same recording.

## Artistic Context

MEUTE is an eleven-piece techno marching band founded in Hamburg in 2015 by Thomas Burhorn, known for brass versions of club tracks such as Âme's Rej. pølaroit, Lesemann and Gewald, grew up in the countryside near Hanover; Gewald has a classical background and Lesemann came from electronic production, and they have released on Deutsche Grammophon, Stil vor Talent and Ki Records.
