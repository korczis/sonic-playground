## Genre and Mood

**Meg Myers** opens her first EP with **Curbstomp**, alternative rock that is dark and intense by design. Interview magazine heard in her music the lovelorn intimacy of Sinéad O'Connor or Fiona Apple and, under it, a tumult that goes back to her teenage love of grunge; this song leans towards the tumult.

## Song Summary

Myers explained the song in July 2012: Curbstomp is about death and dying, about not being afraid to die and just letting go. The lyric is a chain of first-person confessions, ending each time in a promise to let go, and one of its lines, about being a daughter in the choir, named the whole EP. She wrote it with Andy Rosen, who produced it as Doctor Rosen Rosen; she said the two of them recorded everything themselves.

## Musical Breakdown

No review walks through Curbstomp section by section, so this breakdown keeps to what Myers and the reviewers said.

### Verses

The confessions arrive one after another, in the voice The Music Ninja called powerhouse and passionate.

### Chorus

The turn Myers described: when the choruses kick in, she said, it is like you are dead, like what death feels like. Whatever the instruments do there, no source describes; the feeling is hers to name.

### Outro

The song ends the way the lyric argues, on letting go; how the recording itself closes, the sources do not say.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="verse-chorus form" /> }} is usually about catchiness. Here Myers gave it a meaning: the verses hold the confessions, and the choruses are the release she compared to dying.

Trish Sie's {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, published on 9 October 2012 from a concept by Elizabeth Laime, stages a tea party that turns nightmarish when the stuffed animals beat her up. Hypebeast saw the video as like Myers herself, cute and cuddly until she sings. Myers, asked about the blood and bruises, said the video was complete humour to her.

The {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} placement came quickly: according to Myers' official upload, the song played in the autopsy scene of The Lady in the Lake, an episode of CSI: NY's ninth season.

## Artistic Context

Born in Nashville in 1986, Myers grew up in the Smoky Mountains, Ohio and Florida, where she began writing songs, and moved to Los Angeles just before turning twenty. She learned bass on Nirvana's Smells Like Teen Spirit, taught by her brother, a song you can hear transformed in {{ <track_link source="patti-smith-smells-like-teen-spirit.md" /> }}. Daughter in the Choir came out in March 2012 as a free download from her website; after she signed with Atlantic later that year, the label reissued it in April 2013.

## Impact and Reception

The Music Ninja wrote in March 2012 that her songwriting was up there with the best and the arrangement superb, and by July Myers said Curbstomp was the song that had got the most love online. Within months Atlantic had signed her. Turn it up for the chorus.
