## Genre and Mood

**Lecomte De Brégeot** and **Elle Valenci** share the credit on **Paris 1985**, a single from a French producer whose music Indie Hoy describes as a subtle alloy of techno, new wave and EBM, with nostalgic, melancholy harmonies that give it an extra soul. ## Song Summary

Paris 1985 came out on 19 November 2019 on LDB Records as a single of 4:56, and ten days later it reappeared on the EP Fragment. Apple Music and MusicBrainz credit it to Lecomte De Brégeot and Elle Valenci together; the official video, published the same day, credits her with additional vocals. Beyond those facts, no source describes its arrangement, its lyric or how its voice and synths are balanced, so this page describes less than usual rather than guess.

## Artistic Context

Lecomte de Brégeot started with the raves of the late 1990s and early 2000s as a teenager, became a DJ and organised parties, then turned to writing songs with synthesizers. He played the Techno Parade in Paris in 2015 and released his first EP, There Was A Day, in 2016, followed by Pretty Ashes and Fragment. He has worked with Violet Chachki and Allie X, and has partnered with Universal Music Publishing; the video names GUM as this song's publisher.
