## Genre and Mood

**GusGus** filled Arabian Horse, their 2011 album for Kompakt, mostly with vocal songs, and **Magnified Love** is electronic dance-pop with a synth-pop chorus, sung by one voice. The mood is sultry and full of longing, in the reading of Textura, which heard Daníel Ágúst's pleading vocal give the track a sexy vibe reminiscent of Tainted Love.

## Song Summary

Magnified Love is the seventh of ten tracks on Arabian Horse, 4:54 long, with Daníel Ágúst alone on lead vocals. Its title is also a phrase from another song on the album: musicOMH quoted "you're my magnified love" from Over, the duet three tracks earlier. What makes the song memorable depends on who is listening, because the two most detailed reviews disagree about it completely.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part of a song you carry away, and Magnified Love splits critics on exactly that point. The Quietus called it the album's only real misstep, by the numbers, with the hooks that fill the rest of the record sadly missing. Textura heard the opposite in the chorus: a hook strong enough to recall Tainted Love. A hook works or fails in the listener's memory, which is why two careful critics can disagree.

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} came first. On 18 April 2011, five weeks before the album, Kompakt released Magnified Love / Within You Dubs as a digital single: a President Bongo Instrumental Mix of Magnified Love running 10:15 and Biggi Veira's Veiran Dubdrop of Within You. President Bongo is Stephan Stephensen of GusGus, so this is the band rebuilding its own song for the club, without the voice. For the duet Textura praised most, hear {{ <track_link source="gusgus-over.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Arabian Horse, GusGus's eighth studio album, came out on Kompakt on 23 May 2011. The core was Stephan Stephensen, Biggi Veira and Daníel Ágúst, with Urður Hákonardóttir back and Högni Egilsson of Hjaltalín and Davíð Þór Jónsson as guests. The album credits GusGus with writing, producing and mixing every track.

## Impact and Reception

Arabian Horse topped the Icelandic album chart and went gold there. Its reviews were warm: musicOMH praised its majestic production and gave it four stars, and Textura heard in its best songs some of the strongest electronic vocal pop of the year. Magnified Love is where the critics split. Listen and pick a side.
