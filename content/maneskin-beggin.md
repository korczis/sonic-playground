## Genre and Mood

**Måneskin** recorded **Beggin'** as a young rock band showing what it could do with someone else's song. Wikipedia files it under funk rock and glam rock: a soul-era tune played by guitar, bass and drums, sung with swagger, energetic, bold and, in its producer's account of the session, raw.

## Song Summary

Beggin' was written by Bob Gaudio and Peggy Farina and released by the Four Seasons in 1967, when it reached number 16 in the US. In 2007 Madcon reworked it with tweaked lyrics and rap verses and took it to number one in several European countries. Måneskin's cover largely follows the Madcon version's structure, rap verse included, so it is as much a cover of that cover as of the original. The band first played it on the eleventh season of X Factor Italia, coached by Manuel Agnelli, and then recorded it for their debut EP, Chosen.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Structure

The song keeps Madcon's shape: sung sections from the Four Seasons' song with a rapped verse among them. No source gives a section-by-section account of Måneskin's recording.

### The Vocal

Damiano David leads, and his delivery is the part critics described. Stereogum's Chris DeVille heard his growl as ostentatious and the whole performance as a jittery, feral scat attack, and did not mean it kindly.

### The Band

Victoria De Angelis plays bass, Thomas Raggi guitar and Ethan Torchio drums. The sources name the players but do not describe their parts.

## Artistic Context

Chosen came out on 8 December 2017 on Sony and RCA, with Beggin' as its fourth track. Lucio Fabbri produced it at his Metropolis studio in Milan in sessions that ran mostly at night, while the band was still competing on the show, and lasted only a few days. He called it a low-budget session and said the song was almost recorded live, which in his view kept a human component that much processed modern music loses.

## Impact and Reception

The recording waited four years for its moment. After Måneskin won Eurovision in May 2021, their catalogue began charting across Europe and Beggin' went viral on TikTok; on 3 July 2021 it became the number one song on Spotify's global chart. It peaked at number 13 on the Billboard Hot 100, reached the UK top ten and topped the charts in countries including Austria, the Czech Republic, Germany, the Netherlands and Switzerland. It won Top Rock Song at the 2022 Billboard Music Awards. Not every critic was won over: DeVille called it truly the pits. Play it next to the Four Seasons and Madcon and decide whose song it has become.
