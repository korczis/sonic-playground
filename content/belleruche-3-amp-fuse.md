## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** made **3 Amp Fuse** for their third album, 270 Stories, and it belongs to the record's rougher side. The trio's music sits between trip-hop and soul, built from scratched beats, bluesy guitar and a soulful voice; on this album The Couch Sessions heard it warped into funk-rock-soul. The feelings here are gritty and energetic: a song that reviewers heard come at them rather than drift.

## Song Summary

3 Amp Fuse is the fourth of eleven tracks on 270 Stories, released by Tru Thoughts on 11 October 2010 and, like the whole album, written, performed, engineered and produced by the band themselves; the Bandcamp notes say it was all made "in the cave". On 4 April 2011 it became the album's third single, an EP with a radio edit, an instrumental and three remixes of other Belleruche songs. No review describes the song part by part, so this page keeps to what reviewers said of it and of the album around it.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

Two reviewers of 2010 picked this song out. The Couch Sessions named it with {{ <track_link source="belleruche-clockwatching.md" /> }} as songs that come hard and in your face, proof the group was not stuck on one lo-fi note; Ear to the Sound put it beside Fuzz Face as a far cry from the band's earlier Minor Swing. Around it, the album has harder-edged beats from DJ Modest, Ricky Fabulous on guitar and often on bass, and Kathrin deBoer looping and layering her own voice. Which of these you hear in this song, and where, is yours to find out.

## Concepts in Focus

Belleruche had no drummer: their rhythm came from DJ Modest's decks, and {{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is the band's signature. On 270 Stories a reviewer praised his improved knack to mix and scratch at all tempos.

The single carried an {{ <concept_link name="instrumental" /> }} of 3 Amp Fuse beside its radio edit, the same track without deBoer's voice, for DJs and anyone curious how the beat and guitar stand alone.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Ivan Mule, was made with projectors, a singer in Australia and a band in Hackney, very loosely based on an idea by Jamie Roberts, with a camera operator in the southern hemisphere.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005: deBoer on vocals, Fabulous on guitar, Modest on turntables. After three limited 7-inch singles on their own Hippoflex label they signed to Brighton's Tru Thoughts in 2007; 270 Stories followed Turntable Soul Music and The Express, and Rollerchain came in 2012. The band announced its end in October 2013 and new recordings in June 2025.

## Impact and Reception

Reviewers heard 270 Stories as a change. Ear to the Sound wrote that the band had run sandpaper across their polished sound; In The Pocket called it tougher, more aggressive and more introspective, and advised a couple of listens. 3 Amp Fuse is one of the songs that change is made of. Play it after Minor Swing and hear the distance.
