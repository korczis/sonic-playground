## Genre and Mood

**John Newman** sings it and **Gemini** remade it: **Love Me Again (Gemini Remix)** is the version of Newman's 2013 debut single by a young English producer from the dubstep scene. Electronic and remix are this page's honest genre words, with soul for the voice at its centre; longing belongs to the lyric, and emotional is the word Gemini himself chose for his music.

## Song Summary

The original Love Me Again, written by Newman with Steve Booker, is a plea for a second chance that went to number one in the UK. Gemini's remix was one of four on the single's digital download, the third track between Kove's and Ejeca's, and it runs 4:02 against the original's 4:00. The UKF channel put it on YouTube on 25 June 2013, announcing its release for 30 June, the day the single came out in the UK.

## Musical Breakdown

No review of this remix could be found, so this guide claims no tempo, structure or sound beyond its credits and its length.

### Main Section

Newman's voice carries the remix, as the credits say. Where Ejeca's remix on the same release stretches the song to almost seven minutes, this one keeps almost exactly the single's length. What Gemini put under the voice no source describes, so listen and decide for yourself.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts, and a single is often released with several of them so that it can reach different dance floors. Here the same download carried four, from house to drum and bass: compare {{ <track_link source="john-newman-ejeca-love-me-again-ejeca-remix.md" /> }} and {{ <track_link source="john-newman-kove-love-me-again-kove-remix.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} balances the parts of a recording into a finished stereo track. The credits of the official upload name Mike Spencer, a co-producer of the original single, as mixer.

{{ <concept_link name="mastering" /> }} is the last step, preparing the mix for release so that it sounds consistent on every system and beside other tracks. The same credits name Lewis Hopkin as mastering engineer.

## Artistic Context

Gemini is Tom Slinger, born and raised in Leicester. He released his first EP, Blue, in February 2011 while studying at Leeds University, co-owns the label Inspected, and was played on BBC Radio 1's night-time shows. Dancing Astronaut described him in 2012 as known for dark dubstep and heard his Mercury EP move toward synths, electro rhythms and house beats. In 2011 he told Skiddle that he usually starts a track at a piano.

## Impact and Reception

The original single was nominated for British Single of the Year at the 2014 BRIT Awards. Gemini's version travelled on its own: MusicBrainz lists it inside several Ministry of Sound DJ mixes released in 2013, among them Ibiza Annual '13. Play it after the original and hear what a remixer does with four minutes.
