## Genre and Mood

**Daso**'s **Meine** is deep house that climbs. Ghostly International, whose Spectral Sound label released it, called it a euphoric eight-minute ascent, and Resident Advisor called it unbelievably positive: euphoric and uplifting are the feelings, carried over nearly eight minutes of house.

## Song Summary

Meine is the A-side of the Meine Idee EP, a three-track 12-inch released on 31 October 2007 with Idee and Deine Schuhe. Ghostly described it as an ascent that delighted dancefloors worldwide and later became something of a classic. Its strength, in Ghostly's words, is Daso's unique gift for graceful melody, which the EP captures at a moment when it shone brilliantly. This recording runs 7:55.

## Artistic Context

Daso Franke was born in Pune, India, in 1981, grew up in Hannover and moved to Cologne in 2003 to study design. He made his name on MBF, a sublabel of Traum, and from 2005 played clubs in Germany and abroad, among them Tresor, Maria am Ostbahnhof and Arenaclub in Berlin. German Wikipedia traces his sound to 1980s pop, Neue Deutsche Welle and new wave, mixed with the scenes of Hannover and Cologne. He worked with Pawas and Juli Holz, among others. He was 26 and based in Cologne when Spectral Sound released this EP.

## Impact and Reception

In 2018, Ghostly wrote, Resident Advisor called Meine one of the best songs of the century so far. Daso died of cancer in Berlin on 2 April 2018; in the final moments of his life, his friends played Meine from a stereo in the corner of his hospital room. With his family's support, Ghostly made the EP available again in his memory. Give it the full eight minutes and let it rise.
