## Genre and Mood

**Peggy Gou**'s **"Starry Night"** is house with a disco memory, uplifting, nostalgic and groovy, and this is its **Edit**: the same song in under four minutes. Resident Advisor called it an acid-tinged piano house track for daytime sets, and Pitchfork heard in it house music at its balmiest.

## Song Summary

"Starry Night" came out on 22 March 2019, on the day Gou announced her own label, Gudu Records, and in April it led Moment, the label's first release, a two-track EP she produced. The original runs about six and a half minutes; MusicBrainz lists this edit, at 3:53, on the Starry Night single. In her native Korean, wrote Resident Advisor's Marissa Cetin, Gou sings about how quickly popularity and tastes can change, and twice she switches to English. Pitchfork's Philip Sherburne heard the throwback sensibility of her earlier singles: gliding hi-hats, machine claps, a staccato bassline with the faintest hint of freestyle, and bold, pumping piano chords holding it together.

## Musical Breakdown

No source we found describes what the edit cuts from the original, or walks through either version section by section, so this breakdown stays with the layers critics heard.

### The Piano

The piano is the glue, in Pitchfork's word. The Guardian heard fun piano stabs and retro drum claps, and Resident Advisor heard the warm piano return later in the track.

### The Rhythm and the Bass

Hi-hats glide and claps are machine-made. Exclaim! heard a bass-heavy mix under house beats and keys, the foundation for Gou's voice.

### The Voice and the Details

Resident Advisor heard a light-as-air xylophone and dreamy oohs around Gou's Korean vocal.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shortens a track for radio and streaming. Club tracks are long so that DJs can mix in and out of them; an edit usually keeps the song and trims the room left for mixing.

A {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady pulse of house music, and Pitchfork's list of hi-hats, claps and piano chords names its classic parts. The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} here is staccato, short notes rather than a held line, which is where Sherburne heard a hint of freestyle, the Latin-inflected dance-pop of 1980s New York. Compare {{ <track_link source="dj-s-k-t-must-be-a-reason-radio-edit.md" /> }}, another sunny house track cut short.

## Artistic Context

**Peggy Gou** is Kim Min-ji, born in Incheon in 1991 and based in Berlin, a DJ, producer and singer who first sang on a record with her 2018 EP Once. She founded Gudu Records to give artists starting out a better deal than she received.

## Impact and Reception

Wikipedia calls Starry Night her hit of 2019. Its video, directed by Jonas Lindstroem and shot in South Korea with the actor Yoo Ah-in, drew on Ganggangsullae, a traditional Korean dance; it appeared first on Apple Music, then on YouTube in September. The song was nominated at the International Dance Music Awards and the Korean Music Awards. Put the edit on when you have four minutes of daylight.
