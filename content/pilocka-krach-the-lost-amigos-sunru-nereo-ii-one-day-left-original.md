## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**, **The Lost Amigos**, **Sunru** and **Nereo II** share the credit of **One Day Left**, here in its original version: electronic music that its Bandcamp page tags as electro, house, techno and punk at once. It feels groovy and playful, though the only word the sources give its sound is disco.

## Song Summary

One Day Left first appeared in 2017 as One Day Left (I Would Not Dress), a seven-minute track on the Lost Amigos half of Pilocka Krach's double album Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos. In September 2021 she made it the title of a release of its own: five remixes, two instrumentals and this original, which runs 7:00 against the album's 7:02. The lyric is by the two singers, Sun Ru Carter and Nereo II; the music is by Pilocka Krach, who wrote and produced every Lost Amigos track. No source publishes the words, so this page does not paraphrase them.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach is a Berlin DJ and producer who runs her own label, Greatest Hits International; her publisher calls her the electric rock star from Berlin. A Musicboard Berlin grant took her to Detroit in 2016, where she connected with the techno collective Underground Resistance. By the album's credits, the Lost Amigos tracks were written and produced in Tulum on the Caribbean, at 9corners in Guadalajara, at Submerge in Detroit and in Berlin-Marienfelde in 2015 and 2016, and her label says she is The Lost Amigos herself, her band and all at once.
