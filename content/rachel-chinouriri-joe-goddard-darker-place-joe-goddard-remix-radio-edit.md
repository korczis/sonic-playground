## Genre and Mood

**Rachel Chinouriri**'s **Darker Place - Joe Goddard Remix, Radio Edit** is the short cut of a remix by **Joe Goddard**, and it joins an indie-pop singer from Croydon with an electronic producer from Hot Chip. Gigwise heard the original as a song that switches between dark and bubblegum pop with a sinister through-line; Beats Per Minute found its lyric welcomingly eerie. Those words describe the original. No review describes the remix, so dark and haunting are the song's feelings as the critics heard them, and the page does not claim to know how Goddard's version treats them.

## Song Summary

Chinouriri says Darker Place is about the battle between light and darkness, and that she wrote it about a person who follows someone around, torn between acting on impulse and holding back. The original single came out in January 2021 with a video by Milo Blake. Parlophone released Goddard's remix on 12 February 2021, in a full version of 4:12 and this radio edit of 2:48, which also closes the deluxe edition of her EP Four° In Winter.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts. The original was produced by HYLNU, Jamie Lloyd Taylor and Oli Bayston, with drums by Liam Hutton; Goddard received that work and made something new around the vocal. What exactly he kept, beyond the voice, no source records, which is the best reason to compare the two yourself.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} usually exists to make a track shorter. Here it brings a four-minute remix back to the length of the song it came from, so the edit is less about time than about shape: a quicker start, fewer repeats, a version for radio and playlists.

## Artistic Context

Chinouriri, born in 1998 in Kingston upon Thames and raised in Croydon, writes and produces her own songs and records for Parlophone. Four° In Winter came out in 2021; a deluxe edition, reported by Dork in October that year, added If Only, an interlude, a demo and two remixes, this edit last of all. Her debut album What a Devastating Turn of Events followed in 2024 and reached number 17 in the UK. Goddard, born in London in 1979, co-founded Hot Chip with Alexis Taylor, is a member of The 2 Bears and co-founded the label Greco-Roman; his own songs include {{ <track_link source="joe-goddard-taking-over.md" /> }}.
