## Genre and Mood

**Ben Khan**'s **warriors rose** ends an album that 5 Finger Review heard as pop, funk and R&B in futuristic, electronics-soaked grooves, and that The Skinny found more about atmosphere than about beats. Those words describe the record. No review found for this page describes the song itself, so what follows keeps to what is known and leaves the rest to your ears.

## Song Summary

"warriors rose" is the fifteenth and last track on Ben Khan, released by Dirty Hit on 10 August 2018, and runs 3:38. The writing is credited to Benedict Khan alone. The title, lower-case like every title on the record, is not explained anywhere this page could find.

### What Comes Before

It follows waterfall, which the Guardian counted among the album's strong slower moments and called an excellent instrumental. Before that, the second half of the record is broken up by short sketches such as the 23-second dntwnturluv... and the 55-second ruby1strecording.

### The Sound It Shares

The Guardian heard sinister, John Carpenter-like synths emerging like neon beams through dust, and a production as humid as the air before a thunderstorm. The Skinny heard fluorescent synths, bubbling electronics and pathos hiding inside shimmering instrumentals.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="deep cut" /> }} is an album track that was never a single and that mostly people who play the whole record know. A closing track often is one: by the fifteenth track, only the patient listener is still there.

The album is also a study in {{ <concept_link name="lo-fi" /> }} as a choice. The Guardian heard an appealingly raw, bedroom-recorded quality, even though the album was produced with Flood, mixed by Spike Stent and mastered by Dave Cooley, with Khan playing every instrument himself, according to 5 Finger Review.

Finally, the running order mixes songs with fragments, some under a minute, which work as an {{ <concept_link name="interlude" /> }} would; DIY wrote that these swirling interludes give the album the feel of a trip. "warriors rose" is a full song that ends that trip.

## Artistic Context

Khan is a London singer and producer who emerged with two EPs in 2014 and 2015 and then went quiet before returning with this debut. Hear its opening track, {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-2000-angels.md" /> }}, for the sound the reviews describe.

## Impact and Reception

Critics divided over the album: DIY found it inventive but muddled, and God Is In The TV saw little point in discussing it track by track. Its closer drew no comment of its own. Play the record through to the end and judge how it lands.
