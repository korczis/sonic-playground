## Genre and Mood

**Daniele Di Martino** made **Wasted** with **TYLR** credited as featured artist, and this is its **Radio Edit**. Armada Music, which released it, files it under deep house and calls the EP it comes from atmospheric, "filled with the sweetest vibes" and ready to groove. Atmospheric and groovy are the two feelings this page can stand behind; anything more specific would be a guess.

## Song Summary

Wasted is one of two tracks on the No Time Wasted EP, released by Armada on 15 October 2015; the other is Time, and the EP's title joins the two. Both credit TYLR. The label's own note says the pair embraces "all that Deep House music stands for", which is a promise of mood rather than a description of parts. No review, interview or analysis of Wasted itself has turned up, so this page says what can be checked and leaves the rest to your ears.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

The radio edit runs 3:17 in the label's upload, against 4:54 for the original mix released the same day. That difference is the one structural fact on record: the edit trims about a minute and a half, which in a deep house track usually means a shorter run-in and fewer repeats. Where exactly the cuts fall, and how the arrangement develops, no source describes, and this page does not invent a timeline.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is the version made for radio and streaming. Here you can hear the idea at its plainest by playing the edit and the original mix one after the other: the material is the same, the time it takes is not.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit names a guest on someone else's record. On both tracks of this EP the guest is TYLR, and the credit itself is all the sources give: who wrote what, and what TYLR performs, is not documented. ## Artistic Context

Di Martino told the blog Do You Like That Song in 2013 that he was half German and half Italian, born in Germany, living in London and about to move to Berlin to set up his own studio; by 2015 Armada introduced him as Berlin-based. Indie Shuffle, writing about him in March 2015, called him one of the better underground DJs of the moment, said his work ranges from an intense club sound to more melodic, vocal house, and compared it with Lane 8 and Kidnap Kid. Armada presented the EP as its deep house side; for the same German moment on another label, hear {{ <track_link source="tube-berger-ghost-loft-set-free-original-mix.md" /> }}.
