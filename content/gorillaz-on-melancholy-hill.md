## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz**' **On Melancholy Hill** is synth-pop with a new wave outline, electronic in its making and dreamy and bittersweet in its feeling. Wikipedia adds bubblegum pop to the list, and the title already holds the contradiction: a sweet tune on a melancholy hill.

## Song Summary

It is the tenth of Plastic Beach's sixteen tracks, 3:53 on the album, and was released as the third single on 12 June 2010 on Parlophone. Damon Albarn wrote it while making the album of The Good, the Bad & the Queen, and recorded it at Studio 13 in London, credited with vocals, synthesizers, acoustic guitar and drum programming. In character, the band's cartoon bassist Murdoc Niccals said it was nice to break up the album with something a little lighter.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song section by section or gives times, so this breakdown stays with what reviewers heard in it.

### The Melody

Darren Lee of The Quietus called it maybe one of the sweetest melodies Albarn has ever composed, a sublimely dreamy electro-ballad recalling Yuko & Hiro from Blur's The Great Escape.

### The Voice

Erik Thompson of The Line of Best Fit heard Albarn pulling the curtain back on the animated facade, and that sincerity made the song genuine and triumphant. For Alexander E. Traub of The Harvard Crimson, the soft sound of Albarn's warble matters most, even where his words grow hard to make out.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} carries the song: Albarn is credited with the synths, and a PAN M 360 review heard in them some of his best orchestrations, a dreamlike sound it also found in {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-rhinestone-eyes.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} trims a song for airplay. The promo CD carried one of 3:28 against the album's 3:53. The upload embedded here is the official {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, which runs 4:23 and is not the album recording. Its story has an ocean liner attacked by two fighter planes, Noodle escaping in a lifeboat as a giant Russel rises beneath it, a submarine fleet crewed by the album's guests from Lou Reed to Snoop Dogg, and Plastic Beach appearing as the fog clears. It was teased on 9 June 2010 and released on 14 June, first on iTunes.

## Artistic Context

Plastic Beach, the third Gorillaz album, came out on 3 March 2010, on Virgin in the United States, recorded from June 2008 to November 2009. It debuted at number two in the UK and on the Billboard 200. On Melancholy Hill leads straight into {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-broken.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Ryan Leas of Stereogum called it one of Albarn's finest compositions this century, no matter the vehicle; Traub called it arguably the album's best song. It peaked at number 78 on the UK Singles Chart but has since been certified platinum in the UK. It came 42nd in Triple J's Hottest 100 of 2010, and has been played at almost every Gorillaz concert since the Escape to Plastic Beach tour. It later turned up in Life Is Strange 2 and in Tesco's 2024 Christmas adverts. Listen for the melody first; the rest follows it.
