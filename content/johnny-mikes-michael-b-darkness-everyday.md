## Genre and Mood

**Johnny Mikes** and **Michael B**'s **Darkness Everyday** is deep house in the literal sense: dark, melancholic and haunting, a long club track whose label described it as a deep, dark and bass-heavy outing. Its sadness comes from an unexpected place, a soul classic of 1971.

## Song Summary

Lower East, the label, introduced it in 2013 as a new cut from the London-based DJ and producer Johnny Mikes. Over the bass, Michael B sings what the release notes call a haunting take on Bill Withers' Ain't No Sunshine, and it is that vocal which supplies the shades of melancholy. The credits name both Mikes and Withers as composers. Beatport lists the original mix at 7:07, at 118 beats per minute in E-flat minor.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the track section by section, so this sticks to what the label and the release data say.

### The Bass

The label heard the track as true to Lower East's trademark sound: deep, dark and heavy at the bottom end.

### The Vocal

Michael B's voice carries the borrowed song. Withers' original, from his 1971 debut Just As I Am, is famous for a bridge that repeats "I know" 26 times; here its words and mood are set inside a seven-minute house track.

### The Long Form

At just over seven minutes, the original mix takes its time; the release paired it with two remixes of similar length.

## Concepts in Focus

Darkness Everyday works by {{ <concept_link name="interpolation" /> }}: rather than sampling Withers' record, it has Michael B sing his song again, and the credits add Withers as a composer, which is how borrowed melodies are usually acknowledged. Compare a deep-house track that draws on an older song in {{ <track_link source="sascha-braemer-no-home.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }} colours the mood: Beatport lists the original in E-flat minor, and that darker colour fits the melancholy the label heard in it.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can move a track to another floor. The 2013 release added versions by Timo Maas, listed as tech house at 146 beats per minute, and by Alexis Raphael, listed as indie dance. Hear Maas in his own work in {{ <track_link source="timo-maas-shifter.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Darkness Everyday came out on Lower East on 24 June 2013, catalogue LOW030, as Johnny Mikes' fifth release for the label. The recording embedded here is the one on the compilation 7 Years of Lower East, released in 2017, which still carries the 2013 copyright of the original single.

## Impact and Reception

Before release, Pete Tong gave it a world premiere on his BBC Radio 1 show; Johnny Mikes' own upload of that broadcast dates it to 10 May 2013. Let it run the full seven minutes.
