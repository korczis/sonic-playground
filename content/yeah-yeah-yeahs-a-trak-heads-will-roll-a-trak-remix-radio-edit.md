## Genre and Mood

**Heads Will Roll (A-Trak Remix Radio Edit)** is the short form of **A-Trak**'s remix of a **Yeah Yeah Yeahs** single, a New York dance-rock song taken onto the dance floor: Wikipedia files the remix as dance and house, and this edit packs it into three minutes twenty-three. The feelings are energetic and euphoric, the mood of a track CBC Music calls a party anthem.

## Song Summary

The original, the band's best-selling single, came from It's Blitz! in 2009, written by Karen O, Nick Zinner and Brian Chase and produced by Nick Launay and David Andrew Sitek. Its string riff came from a Mellotron sample that Zinner played, over pre-recorded samples of Chase's drums and Zinner's ARP synthesizer. A-Trak rebuilt it. CBC Music's Kelsey Adams lists the changes: he speeds up the tempo, ices out the synths, flattens the drums, adds percussion to the chorus and loops the vocal into an emotional swell, staying true to the source while pushing the envelope. The full remix runs six minutes twenty-three; this edit keeps about half.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the passages of the radio edit, so the breakdown follows the changes the critics name rather than a clock.

### Main Section

The flattened drums and the faster pulse carry the track; the synths sit colder than in the original. Listen for the stab riff Rolling Stone singles out and for the chorus, where the added percussion thickens the beat.

### Climax

The looped vocal is the release: Karen O's voice repeated until, in Adams's words, it becomes an emotional swell.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} usually starts with a request from the artist. This one went the other way. A-Trak told The Ringer he saw the band at Coachella in 2009 and wanted to flip the song, got the stems, and found the original so bare bones that even the drums were simple. A remixer, he said, wants to beat the original.

This page is the {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}, three minutes twenty-three against six twenty-three for the club version. The 2009 release of the remix that MusicBrainz lists carries a club mix, a dub mix and this edit.

The {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} of Project X made it famous. CBC Music writes that the remix became a party anthem after the 2012 film and has passed 250 million Spotify streams.

## Artistic Context

A-Trak is Alain Macklovitch, born in Montreal in 1982. At 15 he became the youngest and first Canadian winner of the DMC World DJ Championship; he was Kanye West's tour DJ, co-founded the label Fool's Gold in 2007 and formed Duck Sauce with Armand Van Helden. His brother Dave 1 is half of Chromeo. Dancing Astronaut reports that he worked on the remix for months while touring and that Interscope at first turned it down; it circulated until the label asked to release it.

## Impact and Reception

Rolling Stone placed the remix 124th among the 200 greatest dance songs of all time in 2022 and quoted A-Trak a decade on: I still hear DJs play it. He put it on his 2016 retrospective In the Loop: A Decade of Remixes, and in 2023 Nia Archives sampled it for Off Wiv Ya Headz. Play it loud and wait for the vocal loop.
