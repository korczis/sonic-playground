## Genre and Mood

**Stimming** gave **The Song** more than eight and a half minutes and almost nothing else: a beat, a voice and space. It belongs to deep house and tech house by its label and its pulse, but it is closer to a spoken piece over a groove than to a club tool. The feeling is intimate and hypnotic, and because the voice talks about why people write songs at all, reflective too.

## Song Summary

The Song is one of three tracks on Stimming's Window Shopping EP, released by Diynamic Music in March 2012 (catalogue DIYNAMIC056) and placed on Bandcamp between the title track and November Morning. Resident Advisor's Stephen Worthy heard it as the EP's counterpoint to an intricate opener: "not much more than a crunchy, cut-up handclap accompanying a soft-shoe shuffle and metronomic kick drum". What gives it humanity, he wrote, is The Lazarusman's narration, a Mark Murphy-esque voice proselytising about the healing benefit of writing songs. Resident Advisor's tracklist credits him as featured; Bandcamp and MusicBrainz list Stimming alone.

### Sound Breakdown

Listen for how little changes, and how that steadiness makes room for the voice.

### The Voice

The Lazarusman speaks rather than sings, and he speaks about the subject in the title: what writing a song can heal. Resident Advisor compares his delivery to the jazz singer Mark Murphy. No source quotes the text, so neither does this page.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="spoken word" /> }} over a beat puts the meaning of the words in front of the melody. In The Song the narration is the lead part, and the beat stays out of its way.

The {{ <concept_link name="kick drum" /> }} is the other half of the track. Worthy calls it metronomic: an even, unchanging pulse that turns the eight and a half minutes into a steady walk rather than a build.

The Lazarusman is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} in the old sense of the term, a voice with its own name on a producer's record. Stimming's early work with the South African vocalist, Wikipedia notes, built him a loyal following in South Africa.

## Artistic Context

Martin Stimming, born in Giessen in 1983 and based in Hamburg, trained classically on violin, piano and drums before studying electronic music production, and he releases on Diynamic, the Hamburg label founded by Solomun and Adriano in 2006. He is known for handmade sounds and field recordings of everyday objects. Resident Advisor heard Window Shopping as grown-up Stimming, indulging his lust for orchestration and organic grooves, with strings, a doleful woodwind and Rhodes piano elsewhere on the EP. The EP's third track has its own page here: {{ <track_link source="stimming-david-christensen-bernd-ruf-november-morning.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

By Resident Advisor's account The Song is the sparest piece on an EP it heard as a tapas of moods, tempos and melodies. Give it its full length, and let the voice do the work.
