## Song Summary

Horizons is the second of five tracks on the Horizons EP, released by Diez Music on 19 May 2017, and at 2:41 it is the shortest of them. Qobuz credits the composition to Victor Belin and Raphaël Aucler; the live session's notes say it was written and composed by Arigato Massaï and interpreted by Anna Majidson, whom MusicBrainz spells Anna Magidson. The official audio went up on the project's YouTube channel the day the EP came out. No source gives the lyrics or says what the song is about.

## Artistic Context

Arigato Massaï is a French electronic project on Diez Music, which had already released its Waifu Material EP in November 2015. Two tracks of the new EP came out first as singles, Later on 18 April and A Dollar a Sin on 28 April 2017. Later releases include the 2019 single Hours of Glass, again with Tessa B., singles in 2023 and 2025 and the album Set Me Free in January 2026.
