## Genre and Mood

**Il Est Vilaine**'s **Cris en theme** is a short track from a debut album that its shops file under electronic, electro and French electro pop, and that the British site The Playground heard as something akin to synth-pop, ready to rebel from any genre at any moment. No review describes this track on its own. Quirky and playful are this page's reading of a duo that, as two sources put it, refuses to take itself seriously.

## Song Summary

Cris en theme is the seventh of twelve tracks on Les Mystères de Lorient, which Dialect Recordings released on 21 May 2021, and it runs 2:27. The credits say Il Est Vilaine, Lionel Boutang and Hervé Bécart, wrote, composed, produced and performed it, as they did every track but Agitation, written by Christophe and Alain Kan. Four tracks credit guest singers; this one names none. Read aloud, its title sounds like chrysanthème, the French word for chrysanthemum; no source says whether the pun is meant.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }} ties its songs to one idea. The Playground called Les Mystères de Lorient an audio concept album, and Oye Records described its theme as a road trip through Brittany: titles name Lorient and Carnac, and the duo's own name sounds like Ille-et-Vilaine, a Breton département. A track of two and a half minutes may make most sense heard in that order.

Cris en theme is also a {{ <concept_link name="deep cut" /> }}: an album track that was never a single and that no critic singled out. That leaves the listener without a guide, which is a good reason to listen closely.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the album's main colour for The Playground, which heard the whole record as synth-washed; whether it leads here is for your ears to judge.

## Artistic Context

Il Est Vilaine are a Paris electronic duo. Oye Records wrote that, before this long-awaited debut, they had released EPs on labels such as Lumière Noire, Kill The DJ and Bahnsteig 23, and that despite the pun in their name they are Parisians. The album was recorded at Coloritmo, mixed by Jerome Caron and mastered at Optimum. Chloé Raunet, known as C.A.R., sings on its second track; hear her own work remixed in {{ <track_link source="car-idle-eyes.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The Playground greeted the album on its release day as the work of yet another electronic act to watch from the French scene, and ended with "Consider us hooked." Cris en theme itself has no reviews; play the album from Agitation to Night in Carnac and decide for yourself what this short piece is doing between them.
