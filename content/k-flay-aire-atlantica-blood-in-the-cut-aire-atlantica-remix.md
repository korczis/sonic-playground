## Genre and Mood

**Blood In The Cut - Aire Atlantica Remix**, as the streaming services title it, takes **K.Flay**'s song, nominated for Best Rock Song at the 60th Grammy Awards, into electronic dance music; Discogs files the remix release as dance-pop. The feelings stay the song's: defiant, cathartic and intense, a breakup anthem in a new body.

## Song Summary

K.Flay wrote Blood in the Cut with JT Daly and Lena Kwan Simon, and Daly produced it. The lyric describes a breakup and its violent effects. She wrote the lyrics and the riff in twenty minutes, she has said, in her parents' basement at Christmas and in a dark emotional place, and writing it filled her with a vigour she had been missing; producing it meant embracing a spirit of release in the chorus. Aire Atlantica, the alias of the producer Joseph Pincus, made this version, which runs 3:19 against the original's 3:08.

What any remix starts from: K.Flay's vocal, the riff she wrote with the lyric, and a chorus built as a release.

### The Remix

The credits name Aire Atlantica as remixer and keep the original writers, so the words and melody are K.Flay's while the frame is his.

### The Length

At 3:19, the remix stays close to the length of the original instead of stretching into a long club version.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording's parts into a new version. Interscope did not commission one but three: Blood in the Cut (Remixed), released on 3 March 2017, opens with Aire Atlantica's mix and follows it with versions by Ojivolta and Awoltalk. K.Flay's channel had posted this one as out now in October 2016. Three producers handling one vocal show what a remixer decides.

The song had already proved it could travel. It appears on the {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} of the film XXX: Return of Xander Cage and in the BoJack Horseman episode "Stupid Piece of Sh*t". A placement on screen and a dance remix do similar work, carrying a song to listeners beyond alternative rock radio.

## Artistic Context

K.Flay, born Kristine Flaherty in Wilmette, Illinois, in 1985, began making music at Stanford University and signed to RCA in 2012, leaving a year later. Blood in the Cut, released on 19 August 2016 through Interscope and Night Street Records, first opened her Crush Me EP and then became the lead single of her second album, Every Where Is Some Where. Aire Atlantica's own releases came out on labels such as Dim Mak and Brooklyn Fire. Hear the album's lighter side in {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The original earned K.Flay her Grammy nomination, and the album was nominated for Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical.
