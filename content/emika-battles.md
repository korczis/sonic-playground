## Genre and Mood

**Emika** opens her album DREI with **Battles**, electronic music that sits between electro-pop and the experimental. The feelings named here follow the critics: dark and haunting, because the Electricity Club heard an unsettling creepiness over her sub-bass and Ibiza Spotlight a dark and brooding world; defiant, because Spectrum Culture heard an artist who wants more battles and is ready to seize the opportunity.

## Song Summary

DREI is, in Emika's own words, ultimately about freedom: coping with it, limitations against it, the madness of being alone and the perils of a limitless creative mind. Ibiza Spotlight heard that dualism of limits and freedom set out in the opening lines of Battles. The other half of her statement is about sound: a desire to produce fat hard beats without needing anyone. She wrote the album, reportedly in a single two-week period, and produced and mixed it herself.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through Battles section by section; the reviews describe its elements, so the breakdown follows them and leaves the rest to your ears.

### Intro

The album starts here, with the words Ibiza Spotlight read as its theme. Listen to how much room the first lines are given before the track fills in.

### Main Section

The Electricity Club heard attention to detail above all in the voice: Emika's vocal is pitch-shifted, and sibilant cut-ups, the hiss of sung syllables, are processed until they act as percussion with a haunting effect. Underneath sits what the same review calls her trademark sub-dub bass.

### Outro

No source describes how Battles ends. Listen for whether the voice or the bass is the last to leave, and for how the next track picks up.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} is the stage where every recorded part is balanced into one finished track, and it is often left to a specialist. On DREI Emika did it herself, so the placement of each cut-up and the weight of the bass are her decisions as much as the melody is.

Battles also shows two ways a song can multiply. Emika's own {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" text="alternate versions" /> }} include an instrumental and an a cappella, released in 2017, and Battles Pt. II with Sebastian, which her Bandcamp edition of DREI lists as its last track. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is someone else's reading: on DREI Remixes, Cyclic Defrost heard The Black Dog stay close to the album's brooding, stripped-back landscapes, while Kamikaze Space Programme put the emphasis on clattering drum programming and handclaps.

## Artistic Context

Emika is Ema Jolly, born in Milton Keynes in 1986 to a Czech mother from Příbram and based in Berlin since 2006. After two albums on Ninja Tune she launched Emika Records in 2014; DREI came out on it on 4 May 2015, the same year as her piano album Klavirni. The song that follows Battles on the album is {{ <track_link source="emika-my-heart-bleeds-melody.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Spectrum Culture heard DREI route back towards her signature electronic form, and the Electricity Club, looking back a year later, cited Battles as proof of her care as a producer. Put it on with headphones and follow the hissing syllables rather than the words.
