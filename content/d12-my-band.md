## Genre and Mood

**D12**'s **My Band** is hip-hop as comedy sketch, rap that is humorous and satirical about the group itself. Its premise is that Eminem is the lead singer of a "band", which leaves the rest of D12 jealous and disrespected; the Detroit News called it a self-effacing boy band send-up.

## Song Summary

The song was the first single from D12 World, released in March 2004. Eminem produced it with Luis Resto as additional producer; the writers are the six members with Resto and Steve King, who also played bass and guitar on it. In the chorus Eminem boasts that girls fall for the group because of him, though they "don't even know the name of" his band. Each verse then hands the microphone to someone who resents that.

### Intro and Chorus

A brief introduction states the joke, and Eminem's chorus frames everything after it.

### The Verses

Eminem opens with his popularity and the trouble it causes inside the group. Swift takes the microphone to list the indignities: no Pro Tools, a van instead of the tour bus, names mixed up, a dressing room "smaller than a decimal". Kon Artis and Kuniva share the third verse, unable to confront Eminem. Proof answers a fan who loves the band: they "ain't a band", they "don't play instruments". Bizarre claims to be the real star and threatens to quit.

### Boy-Band Hook and Ending

Eminem sings a short boy-band hook with the others backing him, Bizarre tries the chorus to laughter, and the song closes on a mock-Spanish plug for a next single, "My Salsa", before stopping dead.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="posse cut" /> }} usually shows off its MCs; this one makes their unequal standing the subject. HipHopDX wrote that, apart from Proof and Bizarre, the other three might as well be the same guy, "that's what My Band is all about".

{{ <concept_link name="parody" text="Parody" /> }} is the method. Eminem's "My Salsa" mocks Kelis's Milkshake, his boy-band vamp borrows from the Backstreet Boys, and Bizarre's workout mimics 50 Cent's In Da Club video.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Philip G. Atwell and co-directed by Eminem, adds an *NSYNC-style routine and a re-enactment of Janet Jackson's "wardrobe malfunction". It was nominated for Video of the Year, Best Group Video and Best Rap Video at the 2004 MTV Video Music Awards.

## Artistic Context

D12 World came out on Shady and Interscope in April 2004 and entered the Billboard 200 at number one. Exclaim! wrote that My Band tackles the uneven dichotomy between Marshall Mathers and his hometown crew. Compare the title track, {{ <track_link source="d12-d-12-world.md" /> }}, where the group raps without him.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number two in the UK and number six on the Billboard Hot 100, and topped the charts in Australia, New Zealand and Norway. AllMusic called it the group's greatest moment and the tamest track on the album; the Indiana Daily Student thought it hardly stands on its own without the visuals.
