## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** tagged **Bird Mess** on their own Bandcamp under blues, electronic, soul, abstract and turntable; Tru Thoughts introduced the trio in the band's own words, handmade hip-hop blues soul. No critic seems to have written about this song on its own, so soulful and nostalgic are this page's reading of what reviewers heard across the album, smoky blues vocals over hip-hop beats, not a description of the track.

## Song Summary

Bird Mess is the sixth of eleven tracks on Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts on 16 July 2007, and runs 4:04. The album credits all three members, Ricky Fabulous, Kathrin deBoer and DJ Modest, with the writing, production, engineering and recording, done at a place they called Flat 2. The song is two years older than the album: it was the B-side of the first single on the band's own Hippoflex label. Neither the band nor any reviewer explains the title, and no source prints the lyric, so what the song is about is left to the listener.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} is the second song of a single, once literally the back of the vinyl. In 2005 Belleruche pressed Reflection with Bird Mess on the reverse as a 7-inch on their own Hippoflex label, catalogue number HF001, in an edition Discogs gives as 200 copies. Both songs returned on the album.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} gave the song its first CD release. In March 2006 the label Wah Wah 45s put Bird Mess on Underground Hits And Exclusive Bits!, a collection Discogs files under broken beat, jazz-funk and downtempo, marking the song as first time on CD.

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is in the album's title and the band's tags for the song. XLR8R heard a dusting of it over jazzy guitars and handcrafted beats. Compare the band's other early single on {{ <track_link source="belleruche-13-6-35.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005. Their first records were limited 7-inch singles, among them Reflection/Bird Mess and The Itch/13:6:35 on Hippoflex, followed by the 4 Songs EP in 2006, before Tru Thoughts, the Brighton label, signed them in 2007. By then the band had worked with DJ Vadim and with Bonobo's live band.

## Impact and Reception

Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut album in Tru Thoughts' history. XLR8R compared deBoer to Róisín Murphy and a latter-day Sarah Vaughan; Basic Soul found it consistent but lacking one standout moment. Bird Mess is not on the band's 2013 Best Of. Start with the single it came from, then let the album play on.
