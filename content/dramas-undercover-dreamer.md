## Genre and Mood

**DRAMAS** released **Undercover Dreamer** in May 2020 as the first glimpse of their second album. The Viennese duo are filed under art pop and electro-pop, and Austrian Music Export hears new wave in this one; the mood those labels suggest, melancholic and dreamy, matches a band their own release notes say wraps heavy, oppressive content in reduced, deeply moving music.

## Song Summary

DRAMAS are two people: Viktoria Winter, who sings and writes most of the lyrics, and Mario Wienerroither, who produces. The single's Bandcamp page credits the song as written, performed and produced by DRAMAS and mastered by Arnold Kasar at Calyx in Berlin; on the album, Wienerroither is credited as producer and the music was recorded and mixed at digitalofen audiobakery. No source explains the lyric or the title, and this page does not invent a meaning.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes this song section by section, so what follows stays with what the sources say about its sound.

### The Sound

New wave is the reference point Austrian Music Export gives for this single, and it fits Wienerroither's own account of the album: he said he could fully live out his love for the 1980s and their ballads.

### The Voice and the Production

Winter's voice sits at the centre; around it, Wienerroither blends cinematic soundscapes, electronic beats and percussion, the combination the band's profile names as their sound.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes how a song sounds as much as the writer shapes what it says. In DRAMAS the split is unusually clear: Winter brings the words and the voice, Wienerroither the music and its production, and on this album, he said, he often came up with the ideas.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the instrument that links them to new wave. Their press biography describes cinematic synthesizer, drum and piano textures merged with Winter's voice, the palette of 1980s pop reworked by a 2020s electronic duo.

Their songs can begin as a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}. Winter described the two of them jamming with a loop station, trying out funny melodies and beatboxing, and improvising nonsense syllables over a loop to find the vowels that work before any lyric is written: the music comes first, then the text.

## Artistic Context

DRAMAS formed in Vienna in 2016 and released their debut album Nothing Is Permanent on Fabrique Records in 2018. Undercover Dreamer followed a collaboration with the Swedish producers Radiotapes in early 2020 and preceded the self-titled album Dramas of April 2021, where it is the eighth of ten tracks. The band said every song on that album deals with some kind of fight; a later single from it was {{ <track_link source="dramas-bloodbath.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Fabrique counts Undercover Dreamer among the songs that established DRAMAS beyond Austria, with airplay on over a hundred radio stations across Europe. Listen with the 1980s in mind, and hear what a Viennese duo keeps from them.
