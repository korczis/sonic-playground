## Genre and Mood

**Gone Too Long - Compuphonic Remix** is the Belgian producer's take on **Him_Self_Her** and **Kieran Fowkes**' song about absence. It stays deep house, but the label's notes promise more bounce than the original, so the longing comes with a groovier step.

## Song Summary

The song is the same one: someone has been away too long, and Fowkes sings the ache of waiting for them. What changes is the production around his voice. The Crosstown Rebels notes describe the original as house with feeling and a massive bassline, and say the Compuphonic remix injects more bounce, bleeps and emotive melody. It runs a little over seven minutes, almost exactly as long as the original mix.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} takes the parts of a finished record and builds a new version from them. Here the part that stays is the voice: the remix still tells the story of the vocal, but the music under it is Compuphonic's. Crosstown Rebels put it on the same 12-inch as the original and Yousef's Circus Rework, which the notes describe as rolling drums and dubby textures, so a DJ could choose the version that fits the hour.

The credit Him_Self_Her featuring Kieran Fowkes shows the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} at work: the duo produce, Fowkes sings, and his voice is what lets three versions remain one song.

Under both versions runs a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}, the steady four-on-the-floor pulse of house. The notes say the remix makes it bounce more; listen for how that shift changes the mood of the same words.

## Artistic Context

Him_Self_Her are Claire and Leon from Leicester, whom Damian Lazarus signed to Crosstown Rebels. Gone Too Long first came out on the Mexican label Cream Couture before Crosstown Rebels reissued it on 30 September 2013 as CRM 116, with this remix and Yousef's rework. Compuphonic is Maxime Firket, born in Liège in 1983, who started on the Belgian label Dirty Dancing, released Sunset with Marques Toliver on Get Physical and remixed Crystal Fighters, Martin Solveig and dEUS. Amsterdam Dance Event describes his music as hedonistic house and quotes his own word for it: positive melancholy.

## Impact and Reception

The notes for the reissue mention support from Pete Tong, who made the track an Essential New Tune, and from Jamie Jones. The remix earns its own place here because it shows how one vocal can carry two moods. Play it after the original and hear what bounce does to longing.
