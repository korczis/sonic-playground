## Genre and Mood

**Roderic** made **Choses qui se passent** with **matt.i** for his 2018 album It All Depends; in **Choses qui se passent - Pilocka Krach Remix**, the Berlin DJ and producer **Pilocka Krach** rebuilds it for the label's remix release. It sits in electronic music and house, the category its vinyl release was sold under. No review describes the remix, so the feelings here are a reading, not a quotation: warm, after Electronic Groove's line that the album brings the summer sun, and hypnotic, as six minutes of steady dance music tend to be.

## Song Summary

The title is French for "things that happen". Roderic told Electronic Groove that matt.i wrote and sang the track's French lyrics; the distributor's credits name Rodrigo Emiliano Ortiz Otero as composer. The original, 5:59 long, came out on Katermukke on 22 June 2018. Pilocka Krach's remix, 6:00, followed on 13 December 2019 as part of It All Depends (Remixes). What she kept and what she replaced is not written down anywhere; this page gives you the questions to bring.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} need not be longer than its original; this one is a second longer. What changes is the hand behind it. Roderic named Pilocka Krach among his favourite artists in a 2018 interview; a year later she remixed him. Compare her remix of {{ <track_link source="prada-meinhoff-pilocka-krach-express-pilocka-krach-s-night-train-express-remix.md" /> }}, which turned a band single into a six-minute ride.

matt.i is the track's {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, credited after Roderic, and more than a guest: according to Electronic Groove he was also the album's executive producer. The track borrows his voice and, with it, a language.

## Artistic Context

Electronic Groove introduced Roderic as a rising Mexican producer who blends jazz, blues, Balkan, Latin, African, psychedelic, rock and trance music, aiming at organic electronic dance music. It All Depends, his second album after a 2016 debut, was recorded in a studio built at a friend's family house in the Dominican Republic and released by Berlin's Katermukke, which he reached through Raz Ohara. In 2018 he was touring with Acid Pauli, Moonbootica and Nicola Cruz, heard on {{ <track_link source="nicola-cruz-cumbia-del-olvido.md" /> }}. Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick, runs her own label, Greatest Hits International, and in 2016 travelled to Detroit on a Berlin Musicboard grant, where she connected with Underground Resistance.

## Impact and Reception

Electronic Groove heard It All Depends as a more dance-floor oriented Roderic and an album about nature and philosophy; nobody reviewed the remix. Play the 2018 original, then this, and decide what happened to the things that happen.
