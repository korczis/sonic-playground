## Genre and Mood

**Jan Blomqvist**'s **Time Again - Jan Blomqvist Club Mix** is the deep house version of a song that already lived between electronic music and songwriting. It keeps the melancholy of the original and its hypnotic pulse, then leans into the groove: the same voice and the same story, given more room and more patience for a club.

## Song Summary

The 2014 Time Again release offered the song in several shapes: a radio edit, the original mix, this club mix by Blomqvist himself and remixes by Peer Kusiv, the lyricist Ryan Mathiesen and Animal Trainer. On the 12-inch issued by Rent A Record Company, the original runs 4:48 and the club mix 7:08. The credits are the original's: music and vocals by Blomqvist, words by Mathiesen, guitars by Blomqvist and Maurice Catastrof, piano and Rhodes by Felix Lehmann, production by Blomqvist and Lehmann.

## Musical Breakdown

### Breakdowns

Flux BPM Online, reviewing the release in 2014, wrote that in the club mix Blomqvist dives deeper and lets the track breathe, with more focused breakdowns and a whole lot of measured restraint.

### Main Section

The guitar, the shiny hook of the original, is held back until just the right moment, and even then the review hears only enough of it to keep the ear wanting more. A catchy Rhodes riff is added, and the vocals are recut.

### Outro

No source describes how the club mix ends; listen for whether it strips back for the next record, as a DJ-friendly mix often does.

## Concepts in Focus

This is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} rather than a remix: the same artist reworking his own recording, with the same players and the same vocal, recut. The difference lies in arrangement, and Flux BPM Online heard it as recasting the song as a proper party-starting dancefloor bomb.

Its tool is the {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }}. Where the original opens on a massive chorus, the club mix works with passages where the beat thins out and lets the track breathe, so that the return of the full groove feels earned.

At seven minutes it also does what an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} does: it gives a DJ time to mix in and out. Hear the original mix first in {{ <track_link source="jan-blomqvist-time-again.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Blomqvist is a Berlin singer, producer and bandleader whom Beatportal credits with helping bring what is loosely called concert techno to main stages, as one of the few electronic artists who brought a drum set and a piano into Berlin clubs. Flux BPM Online placed Time Again in a guitar-led electronic pop club sound fusing acoustic, folk, soul and pop with a warm dance pulse. The release came out in July 2014 on RAR, marketed by Motor Entertainment.

## Impact and Reception

The same review listed early support for the release from DJs including Tensnake, Animal Trainer, Solomun, Joyce Muniz and Kaiserdisco. Play the original, then this, and listen for where the patience goes.
