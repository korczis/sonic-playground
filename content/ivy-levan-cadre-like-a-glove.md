## Genre and Mood

**Ivy Levan** brings **Cadre** along for **Like A Glove**, a sleek blend of pop, R&B and electro-pop with a dark, theatrical streak. The mood is confident and alluring, often provocative, the sound of someone who knows exactly how good the fit is and wants you to notice.

## Song Summary

The title says it all: this is a song about chemistry so tight it feels tailor-made. Levan's smoky, slightly vintage voice slinks over a bass-heavy, minimal beat, and the production leaves plenty of negative space so every purr and syllable lands. The memorable detail is her delivery, playful and dangerous in the same breath, part torch singer and part pop provocateur.

## Musical Breakdown

At just over three minutes, the track is built for maximum swagger with minimum clutter.

### Intro

A low, throbbing synth bass and sparse percussion set a slinky, nocturnal mood straight away.

### Verse 1

Levan comes in close to the microphone, half-whispered and teasing, the beat leaving her voice almost bare.

### Build-up

Layers of harmony and a tightening rhythm push the tension up, a slow strut towards the hook.

### Chorus

The hook opens with her full, brassy voice over a heavier beat, the title line delivered with a knowing grin.

### Bridge

The feature section shifts the texture, Cadre's contribution adding contrast before Levan takes the spotlight back.

### Climax

The final chorus piles on vocal layers and bass weight, the song at its most commanding.

### Outro

The beat cuts out and leaves a last, breathy echo, a confident exit rather than a fade.

## Artistic Context

Ivy Levan is an American singer whose debut studio album No Good came out on 7 August 2015 through Cherrytree/Interscope Records. The album's official singles were Biscuit and Killing You, the latter featuring Sting. Like A Glove sits fifth on the eleven-track record, and its credits name Levan alongside her producers Nissley and Banker as writers. The video linked here is a performance version filmed at the DTS Magic Speaker.

## Impact and Reception

Like A Glove was not a single, and reviews were mixed: some critics found it unremarkable for Levan, while others felt the feature diluted her distinctive style. Heard on its own terms, though, it is a stylish, slow-burning showcase of her voice and attitude. That is why it earns a place here. Dim the lights and listen to how much she does with so little.
