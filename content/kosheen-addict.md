## Genre and Mood

**Kosheen** return to their darker roots on **Addict**, the opening track of their fourth album. It is trip-hop with an electronic backbone and a downtempo drag, and its mood is dark, intense and haunting, a song about a pull you cannot resist and probably should.

## Song Summary

The title says it all: the lyric turns dependency, on a person or a feeling, into something both seductive and frightening. The production rests on deep, heavy bass and insistent drums, with brooding textures swirling around Sian Evans' voice, which is by turns fragile and fierce. The memorable detail is the contrast between the slow, oppressive groove and the urgency in her singing.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Low, ominous tones and a slow beat establish the atmosphere straight away, foreboding and thick, like fog rolling into a city at night. It is the Bristol sound in miniature: slow, heavy and full of shadow.

### Verse 1

Sian Evans enters quietly, almost confessional, her voice floating above the dark bass. The drums stay steady and patient, tightening the grip.

### Build-up

The layers thicken, percussion grows sharper and the tension rises as the lyric moves from temptation to need.

### Chorus

Her voice opens up to full power over the heavy groove, the hook delivered with the ache of someone who knows exactly how bad this is for her.

### Bridge

A brief, sparser section strips things back to atmosphere, a moment of lucidity before the pull returns.

### Outro

The bass lingers and the voice fades into echoes, leaving a sense of unease that stays after the music stops.

## Artistic Context

Kosheen are a trio formed in Bristol by singer Sian Evans with producers Markee Substance and Darren Decoder, who broke through in the early 2000s with "Hide U" and "Catch" from their debut *Resist*. *Independence* was released in 2012 on BreakBeat Culture, after a long delay tied to a change of label, and reviewers heard it as a return to the trip-hop and dark electronic sound of their beginnings. "Addict" was released as a single from it.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of *Independence* repeatedly singled out "Addict" as a strong opener, describing it as dark, foreboding but utterly insistent, with deep bass and drums. It is on this playground because it shows a band rediscovering what made them special. After a decade of shifting styles, this is Kosheen sounding confident, heavy and completely themselves again. Listen in the dark and feel the pull.
