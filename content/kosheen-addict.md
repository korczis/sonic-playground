## Genre and Mood

**Kosheen** return to their darker roots on **Addict**, the opening track of their fourth album. It is trip-hop with an electronic backbone and a downtempo drag, and its mood is dark, intense and haunting, a song about a pull you cannot resist and probably should.

## Song Summary

The title says it all: the lyric turns dependency, on a person or a feeling, into something both seductive and frightening. The production rests on deep, heavy bass and insistent drums, with brooding textures swirling around Sian Evans' voice, which is by turns fragile and fierce. The memorable detail is the contrast between the slow, oppressive groove and the urgency in her singing; a Dutch review praised exactly that, the dark, deep bass and drums building tension under a commanding vocal.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Low, ominous tones and a slow beat establish the atmosphere straight away, foreboding and thick, like fog rolling into a city at night. It is the Bristol sound in miniature: slow, heavy and full of shadow.

### Verse 1

Sian Evans enters quietly, almost confessional, her voice floating above the dark bass. The drums stay steady and patient, tightening the grip.

### Build-up

The layers thicken, percussion grows sharper and the tension rises as the lyric moves from temptation to need.

### Chorus

Her voice opens up to full power over the heavy groove, the hook delivered with the ache of someone who knows exactly how bad this is for her.

### Bridge

A brief, sparser section strips things back to atmosphere, a moment of lucidity before the pull returns.

### Outro

The bass lingers and the voice fades into echoes, leaving a sense of unease that stays after the music stops.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the song's engine. Reviewers heard the album opening on dark, deep bass and drums, and the low end does more than support: it sets the mood, carries the groove and gives the voice something to fight against. Listen to how little the bass moves, and how that stillness becomes oppressive.

The arrangement is a long {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} without a release in the dance-music sense. Layers thicken and the voice grows from a murmur to full power, but the tempo stays slow and the beat stays heavy, so the tension is never fully let go, which is exactly how an addiction feels.

## Artistic Context

Kosheen formed in Bristol around singer-songwriter Sian Evans and the producers Markee Ledge and Darren Beale, who also worked as Markee Substance and Darren Decoder, and broke through with their 2001 debut *Resist*, which reached number eight in the UK and leaned on drum and bass. *Independence*, their fourth album, was first announced for May 2012 and finally released in autumn 2012; it followed Evans' success on DJ Fresh's UK number one "Louder" and opens with "Addict", credited to all three founding members.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of *Independence* singled out "Addict" as a strong opener: one called it dark, foreboding but insistent, and the Dutch site Festivalinfo praised how its bass and drums build tension under Evans' voice. It is on this playground because it shows a band rediscovering what made them special. After a decade of shifting styles, this is Kosheen sounding confident, heavy and completely themselves again. Listen in the dark and feel the pull.
