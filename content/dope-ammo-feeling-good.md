## Genre and Mood

**Dope Ammo** and Jasmine Knight's **Feeling Good** is jungle and drum and bass from a producer who describes his own sound as a fusion of reggae, jump-up and jungle. Its first home was a Jungle Cakes compilation called Welcome to the Jungle, which says where it belongs. The feelings chosen here, energetic and groovy, are the qualities Dope Ammo says his label looks for in a record: energy and groove on the dance floor. No review of this particular song turned up, so this page says what is documented and leaves the rest to your ears.

## Song Summary

This is not the Nina Simone standard of the same name. The credits name Anil Shah, Dope Ammo's real name, as composer and Jasmine Knight as lyricist, so this is their own song: a producer's track carrying a singer's own words. It first appeared as the Original Mix on Benny Page, Dope Ammo & DJ Hybrid presents Welcome to the Jungle, released on 13 March 2020. Dope Ammo Records released the song as a single on 4 August 2023, the upload embedded here, and its 2024 compilation Drum N Bass, Vol.2 carries it at 3:39, a few seconds longer than the Original Mix.

### The Breaks

Jungle's engine is rapid, heavily syncopated breakbeats, often sampled from funk and jazz records.

### The Bass

Underneath runs the genre's other half, deep basslines taken from dub, reggae and dancehall, the same roots Dope Ammo claims for his sound.

### The Voice

Over both sits Jasmine Knight's vocal. A Loopmasters profile credits her with a big vocal range; listen for how the producer leaves room for it.

## Concepts in Focus

Jasmine Knight is the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, but not a guest who sings someone else's words: she is credited with the lyric, so the song is a meeting of a producer's track and a songwriter's text.

Feeling Good also shows how a dance song multiplies. Beside the 2020 Original Mix and the 2023 single, YouTube's official channel for Dope Ammo carries three {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} versions, by Halflight, T-Phonic and Zapya; MusicBrainz lists the Zapya Remix on Drum N Bass, Vol 1.

That makes the {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} its natural home: the song has lived on a label sampler and a label's own collections rather than on an artist album.

## Artistic Context

Dope Ammo is Anil Shah, known as Coco, a DJ and producer based in Banbury, Oxfordshire, who also records as The Drunken Masters; he set up Dope Ammo Records around 1998 as an outlet for his own music. Jasmine Knight is a singer-songwriter from South London. Jungle itself grew up in the UK in the early 1990s from breakbeat hardcore, rave and Jamaican sound-system culture, and was the direct precursor of drum and bass.
