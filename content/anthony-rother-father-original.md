## Genre and Mood

**Anthony Rother** made his name with electro, the machine-driven style Wikipedia credits him with pioneering in Europe, but **Father** is the moment that sound turned into pop. Faze Magazine heard synth-pop influence and emotional depth in it; Resident Advisor called it a pop song so stirring, uplifting and melodic that it was simply perfect. The feelings on this page, uplifting, euphoric and emotional, follow those two readings.

## Song Summary

Father is the second track of Popkiller, the album Rother released on his label Datapunk in 2004, right after a short opening piece called Day; the album original runs about six and a half minutes, the version Spotify lists as Father - Original. Before release, Rother drove a demo to his distributor, Intergroove, to play them how special he thought the song was, and he told Faze Magazine that the distribution people could not quite grasp it at that early stage. He described how he felt back then as synchronicity.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is where Father found its audience. MusicBrainz lists it in mixed albums by Sven Väth in 2004 and by Tiga at the Robert Johnson club in Offenbach: a long album track gives a DJ room to blend in and out.

The {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} took it further. According to Resident Advisor, Erick Morillo wanted the track and had Harry "Choo Choo" Romero remix it, which the reviewer called butchery; MusicBrainz also lists an Erick Morillo vocal mix and dub. A remix can carry a song into a scene the original never aimed at.

## Artistic Context

Popkiller set motifs Rother returned to: Popkiller 2 in 2010 opened with Night where the first opened with Day, and answered Father with Mother. Compare the harder, chanted electro of {{ <track_link source="anthony-rother-brainshaker.md" /> }} from two years later.

## Impact and Reception

Faze Magazine wrote that many fans consider Father one of his most iconic tracks. Its success was also a weight: Rother ended Datapunk in 2015 because he did not want to spend the rest of his life playing Father, and returned to it only in 2026 with a retrospective. Hear what he could not escape.
