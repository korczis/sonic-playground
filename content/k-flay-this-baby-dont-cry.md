## Genre and Mood

**K.Flay**'s **This Baby Don't Cry** is alternative rock with a hip-hop flow and a pop-rock bounce, defiant and playful at once. Bong Mines Entertainment heard drum-driven instrumentation flavoured with alternative rock and hip-hop; RIFF Magazine called it bouncy.

## Song Summary

The lyric looks back at someone who used to worry about how she walked and dressed, and who has stopped caring what the haters think. The refrain is the title, a refusal to cry. K.Flay told Nylon that her favourite lyric on the album is here: the line that turns an old wish to say an early goodbye into a disco queen who keeps "staying alive". She said she had survived dark moments of her own, and that a sense of humour about dark things is very important.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track section by section or gives times; this guide keeps to how K.Flay described its making and to what reviewers heard.

### The Bass Riff

The song began with Imagine Dragons' Dan Reynolds telling her the record was missing a riff-driven, kind of punky song, and playing a version of the riff on bass. Every later version, she said, felt strongest with it.

### The Verses

K.Flay started freestyling over the riff, and the verses grew from there: short, conversational lines about old insecurities.

### The Chorus

When she took the sketch to producer Tommy English it had no chorus. It came after a day she had spent crying, a feeling she wanted to reframe; a version with a different chorus was set aside.

### The Handclaps

The handclap pattern is, in her words, all Tommy: the two got on the mic and clapped until it worked. Dork heard cheer squad handclaps, Entertainment Voice a handclap-heavy backbeat.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} is a short figure repeated until a song stands on it. Here it came before any words: a bass line, played to fill a gap in the album, that the verses then ride.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shaped more than the sound. Tommy English, credited as a writer under his own name, Thomas Schleiter, heard an unfinished idea, worked on it at once and supplied the claps. K.Flay said she likes a song richened over time, because it forces you to revise.

{{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }} took the song further: in 2019 it was placed in the third season of the series 13 Reasons Why.

## Artistic Context

K.Flay is the American artist Kristine Flaherty. Solutions, her third studio album, came out on 12 July 2019 on Interscope and its imprint Night Street, after {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }} had made her name two years earlier. This Baby Don't Cry, its third track, was released on 29 April 2019, after the first single, Bad Vibes. She and Reynolds, she told RIFF, had never written together before it; RIFF heard Solutions as her first record with a positive worldview.

## Impact and Reception

Dork gave Solutions five stars and mentioned these handclaps. K.Flay told Nylon the song is really fun to play live, with an attitude meant for a stage. Play it loud, and clap along where Tommy English did.
