## Genre and Mood

**Mat Zo**'s **Only For You**, sung by **Rachel K Collier**, is heard here in the remix by **Croquet Club**, the French producer whose own records have come out on Anjunadeep and its imprints. It sits in deep house: the reviewers of 2014 heard it as the deeper, gloomier of the single's three remixes, melancholic rather than euphoric, and dark in the sense of lights dimmed rather than menace.

## Song Summary

The original Only For You is the second track of Damage Control, Mat Zo's 2013 debut album, which Dancing Astronaut called serene. Rachel K Collier co-wrote it and sings it. When Anjunabeats released it as a single on 18 February 2014, it came with three remixes, and Croquet Club's was the quiet one: in Complex's words, it gave listeners the emotion without the bright lights. The remix runs 4:34.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Remix

No review or timed analysis walks through this remix, so this page does not map its sections. What the sources give is its direction: deeper and gloomier than the song it reworks, and calmer than the remixes released beside it. Listen for where Collier's voice enters and how much space is left around it.

### The Versions Around It

Maor Levi's remix, a melodic crossover of electro and big room, kicked the song up a notch; Estiva's was a groovy progressive mix. Croquet Club's was heard as sitting between them. Playing the three side by side is the clearest way to hear what each producer changed.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording into a new version, and a remix package like this one shows how far a single song can be pulled. The same voice and melody went to three producers and came back as a big-room track, a progressive groove and this darker, more restrained take.

Collier's credit as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} undersells her part: she is a co-writer of Only For You as well as its singer, and a producer herself, trained in creative music technology at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama.

The remix later became a {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }}. On 6 November 2023 Anjunabeats issued Damage Control (10 Year Anniversary Edition), adding a previously unreleased demo, a club mix and three remixes, this one among them, after the original album. A version first released as a single's extra had become part of the album it came from.

## Artistic Context

Mat Zo is Matan Zohar, a British-Israeli DJ and producer born in London in 1990 and long associated with Anjunabeats. Damage Control, released on Anjunabeats and Astralwerks, was nominated for the Grammy for Best Dance/Electronic Album. Croquet Club is Jeremy Villecourt, who grew up in south-west France; his music has been described as ambient house and delicate electronica.

## Impact and Reception

No chart records the remix. It travelled the way club records do, through the single, Anjunabeats' Chilled Summer compilation later in 2014 and, a decade on, the anniversary edition of the album. Start with the original, then play this one, and listen for what was turned down.
