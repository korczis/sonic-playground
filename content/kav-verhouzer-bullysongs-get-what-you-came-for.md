## Genre and Mood

**Kav Verhouzer** and **BullySongs** meet on **Get What You Came For**, a slice of melodic deep house with a pop singer-songwriter's heart. Dance music and electronic polish carry a mood that is uplifting, feel-good and quietly confident: the sound of a summer evening just as the terrace starts to fill.

## Song Summary

The song is an invitation rather than a demand, a voice telling someone to stop hesitating and take what they want from the night. Verhouzer builds the track from warm, rounded chords, a gently thumping four-on-the-floor beat and airy percussion, leaving plenty of room for BullySongs' husky, soulful vocal. The detail that sticks is the contrast between that slightly rough voice and the smooth, sunlit production around it.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Soft chords and a light groove open the track, relaxed and bright, setting a tempo that invites swaying more than jumping.

### Verse 1

BullySongs enters close and intimate, his grainy tone giving the lyric a lived-in warmth. The beat stays understated so every word lands.

### Build-up

Percussion and bass add momentum, and the harmony lifts, pulling the song toward its hook with a gentle swell rather than a hard rise.

### Chorus

The title hook arrives with a sing-along ease, the vocal chopped and echoed through the production, the kind of melody you find yourself humming hours later.

### Bridge

A short breakdown lets the chords breathe alone for a moment, a summery pause before the groove returns.

### Outro

The final chorus repeats and the arrangement thins out gracefully, ending on the same warm glow it began with.

## Artistic Context

Kav Verhouzer is a Dutch DJ and producer known for melodic, radio-friendly deep house. BullySongs is the project of the British singer and songwriter Andrew Bullimore, whose voice has also appeared on tracks with bigger names in dance music. "Get What You Came For" was released by Armada Music in 2016, written by Bullimore, Josh Record and Verhouzer, and Armada later gave it an official music video.

## Impact and Reception

The track found its audience on playlists and in sunny DJ sets rather than at the top of the charts, which suits its unhurried charm. It is on this playground as a perfect warm-up record, the song you put on when the evening is still young and everyone is in a good mood. Pour something cold, open the window and let it do its work.
