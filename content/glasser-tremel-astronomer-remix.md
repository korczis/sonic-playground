## Genre and Mood

**Glasser**'s **Tremel (Astronomer Remix)** takes an art-pop song into electronic dance territory and keeps it there for nine minutes. Discogs tags the single with styles from ethereal to UK garage. Hypnotic suits a remix three times the original's length, ethereal a voice that, as The FADER heard it, floats over everything put beneath it.

## Song Summary

This is the fourth and longest version on the Tremel single Young Turks released on 29 March 2010, nine minutes beside the original's three; on the 12-inch it is the second track of side B. Cameron Mesirow wrote and sings the song; Astronomer, whom Discogs identifies as the producer Alex Egan, remade it. Reviewing the remix on 1 March 2010, The FADER's Sam Hockley-Smith heard it beef up the original with blurpy, laser-like synth stabs while her voice floated effortlessly over the top, and called it another inch forward for a singer who could sound natural over anything.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the remix section by section or gives times, so this guide stays with what The FADER heard and leaves the structure to your ears.

### The Weight

The original single, as DIY heard Tremel, already had thick bass and tribal percussion. The remix, The FADER wrote, beefs that up.

### The Stabs

Astronomer's signature addition is synthetic: short stabs the post called blurpy and laser-like, a remixer living up to his name.

### The Voice

Mesirow's vocal stays on top of the new production. How much of it the remix keeps over nine minutes no review says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a finished recording: here the voice stays and the arrangement under it is rebuilt and stretched. Hear the three-minute starting point in {{ <track_link source="glasser-tremel.md" /> }}, then this.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the remixer's main tool here. A stab is a short, accented synth chord or hit, and The FADER's blurpy laser stabs are the clearest thing a listener can follow.

The {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} gave the remix its room. On the 12-inch, the original and the Romantic Three remix fill side A, Jamie xx and Astronomer side B, so the single works for listeners and DJs at once.

## Artistic Context

Mesirow, born in Boston and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area, released Tremel on Young Turks six months before her debut album Ring. Discogs credits Astronomer with remixes from 2009 and 2010 for Micachu, Metronomy, Joakim, Foals and The xx, whose Basic Space he reworked in 2009; their {{ <track_link source="the-xx-crystalised.md" /> }} had come out on Young Turks too.

## Impact and Reception

The FADER shared the remix weeks before the single's release, and in December 2010 the Irish blog Nialler9 put it on its list of the year's best remixes, beside mixes by Four Tet, Caribou and Gui Boratto. Play the original first, then let these nine minutes show you how far a voice can travel.
