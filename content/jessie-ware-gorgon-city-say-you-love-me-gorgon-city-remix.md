## Genre and Mood

**Jessie Ware** and **Gorgon City** meet on **Say You Love Me (Gorgon City Remix)**, the London duo's club version of Ware's 2014 ballad. SoulBounce heard a hybrid of UK garage and deep house. The mood stays soulful, because Ware's performance is kept, but it turns groovy and energetic: where the original might crush your heart, Stereogum wrote, the remix might level your whole body.

## Song Summary

The original Say You Love Me is a pop and soul ballad, written by Ware with Ed Sheeran, Benny Blanco and Ben Ash and produced by BenZel, the second single from her album Tough Love. Gorgon City keep her vocal and rebuild what is under it for the dance floor, stretching the song past five minutes. Theirs was the last of three remixes for the single, with Shura's and Alex Adair's, released on 28 September 2014. The detail to remember is the one SoulBounce picked out: her chorus gliding over the wide valleys of a steeply dropping bass.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The remix begins with a drum-laden, percussion-heavy introduction before the voice arrives.

### Main Section

Ware's breathy vocal coasts over the garage and deep house beat. Echoing vocal loops and hand claps fill the space around her, and the chorus rides on the deep, dropping bass.

### Breakdown

The track then settles into what Dancing Astronaut called a soft plateau, with lush synths laid under the original vocals.

### Outro

No source describes the ending; listen for whether it strips back to the beat, as club remixes often do.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can change a song's purpose while keeping its voice. The words and the singer are the ballad's, but the bass, the percussion and the length are new. Gorgon City are the remixers this time; on {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-katy-menditta-weiss-imagination-weiss-remix.md" /> }} their own song is the one rebuilt.

The {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} is the calmer section after the busy opening, the soft plateau of synths and Ware's voice: a moment for dancers to breathe.

A {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} is a sound repeated seamlessly. The echoing vocal loops here take small pieces of Ware's voice and repeat them, so her singing becomes part of the rhythm as well as the melody.

## Artistic Context

Jessie Ware, born in London in 1984, sang backing vocals for Jack Peñate and recorded with SBTRKT and Joker before her debut album Devotion was shortlisted for the Mercury Prize in 2012. Tough Love followed in October 2014 and reached number nine in the UK. Gorgon City, the London production duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott, released their debut album Sirens the same month; this remix and one of Kiesza's Hideaway were later included on their Sirens (Remixes), released in April 2015.

## Impact and Reception

The original single reached number 22 in the UK and went platinum. Stereogum called the remix a club anthem, and Dancing Astronaut shared it as a free download. Start with the ballad, then play this, and listen for how far one vocal can travel.
