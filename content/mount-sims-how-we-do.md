## Genre and Mood

**Mount Sims** delivers a dark and pulsing electronic track with **How We Do**, from his 2002 debut album *UltraSex*. Gritty machine beats and haunting, minor-key melodies create a raw, rebellious energy: electroclash, the early-2000s fusion of 1980s electro and synth-pop with punk attitude.

## Song Summary

The song pulls the listener into the club underground of its moment, cold synths and programmed drums carrying a defiant vocal. *UltraSex* was conceived as an album about technology and sexuality, and How We Do, which opens the US edition, sets out that world: machine-made, stylish and confrontational.

## Lyrics Breakdown

### Intro: Entering the Underground

The track begins with a dark, atmospheric intro, and the pulsing beat sets the tone.

### Verse 1: Embracing Rebellion

The first verse brings in Mount Sims's vocal, cool and assertive, the defiant stance that runs through the track.

### Chorus: The Anthem of Nonconformity

In the chorus the energy intensifies and the title becomes a statement of identity, a claim to doing things one's own way.

### Verse 2: A Call to Action

The second verse keeps up the pressure, the vocal delivered with fierce determination over the machine groove.

### Bridge: A Moment of Reflection

A quieter moment lets the haunting melodies come forward, a contrast to the high-energy sections.

### Final Chorus: Liberation and Energy

The final chorus returns with renewed force for the track's peak.

## Concepts in Focus

How We Do is a clear example of {{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }}, the scene that grew around labels such as Munich's International DeeJay Gigolo, which released *UltraSex* in Europe. Its ingredients are all here: the drum-machine pulse of 1980s electro, synth-pop melody, deadpan cool and a punk refusal to be polite.

The darkness comes from the {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }}. Rolling Stone's review singled out the album's dark, minor-key melodies; their lowered third gives the tunes a shadowed, melancholic colour even when the beat is built for dancing, the same trick much dance music uses.

The instrumentation is the {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} and its programmed partners rather than a band: Mount Sims wrote, performed, produced and arranged the album himself, with Mickey Petralia mixing. Compare the Gigolo label's other emblematic voice in {{ <track_link source="miss-kittin-frank-sinatra.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Mount Sims is Matthew Sims, born in Milwaukee and later based in Berlin, a DJ, performance artist and producer who had earlier played in the American alternative rock band Citizen King. *UltraSex* was released on 3 September 2002 by International DeeJay Gigolo Records in Europe and Emperor Norton Records in the US; "How We Do" is track 5 of the European edition and the opening track of the American one. His darker second album, *Wild Light*, followed on Gigolo in 2005, and he later worked with The Knife and Planningtorock on the opera *Tomorrow, In a Year*.
