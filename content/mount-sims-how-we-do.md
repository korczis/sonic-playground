## Genre and Mood

**Mount Sims** delivers a dark and pulsing electronic experience with 
his track **How We Do.** The gritty beats combined with haunting 
melodies create a raw energy that invites listeners to break free 
from societal norms and embrace a rebellious spirit.

## Song Summary

The song immerses listeners in a world of underground sounds, 
capturing the essence of defiance against conformity. Its 
striking production and evocative lyrics resonate deeply with 
those seeking authenticity and individuality in a conformist 
society.

## Lyrics Breakdown

### Intro: Entering the Underground

The track begins with a dark, atmospheric intro, drawing listeners 
into a realm where conventional sounds are left behind. The pulsing 
beat sets the tone for the journey ahead.

### Verse 1: Embracing Rebellion

As the first verse unfolds, Mount Sims introduces themes of 
defiance and individuality. The lyrics convey a sense of 
freedom, urging listeners to reject societal expectations 
and embrace their true selves.

### Chorus: The Anthem of Nonconformity

In the chorus, the energy intensifies, embodying the spirit of 
rebellion. The infectious rhythm serves as an anthem for those 
who dare to be different, encouraging listeners to dance 
to the beat of their own drum.

### Verse 2: A Call to Action

The second verse reinforces the message of empowerment, 
challenging the status quo. Mount Sims' vocals resonate with 
a fierce determination, motivating listeners to take charge 
of their lives and reject conformity.

### Bridge: A Moment of Reflection

The bridge introduces a quieter moment, allowing listeners to 
reflect on the journey. The haunting melodies evoke a sense of 
longing and introspection, contrasting with the previous 
high-energy sections.

### Final Chorus: Liberation and Energy

The final chorus returns with renewed vigor, celebrating the 
liberation found in self-expression. The track culminates in 
a euphoric finish, leaving listeners inspired to embrace their 
uniqueness and dance to their own rhythm.
