## Genre and Mood

**Satori**, **Miou Amadee** and **Crussen** share the credit of **Days Without You (Crussen Remix)**, a remix Discogs files under deep house, of a song from an album it files under downtempo and house. The feelings on this page, relaxed and carefree, follow the label's own introduction: a pretty downbeat mix, pure fun for the morning hours.

## Song Summary

Days Without You is the ninth of ten tracks on In Between Worlds, the album the Dutch producer Djordje Petrovic released as Satori on Underyourskin Records in 2015, with Miou Amadee as the featured singer; Petrovic is credited as its writer and producer. The album came back in a series of remix EPs. The first, in June 2015, already held a Micronaut remix of Days Without You. The third, UYSR029, which the label called the closing chapter, opens with this version by Crussen, released digitally on 11 March 2016. It runs ten minutes 44 seconds; the original on Satori's channel lasts six minutes 42.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} series lets a label return to one album again and again. Underyourskin spread In Between Worlds across several remix EPs and gave Days Without You to two remixers, The Micronaut and Crussen, so the same song came out in different shapes within a year; compare the remix package of {{ <track_link source="solee-unique-repeat-phoenix-unique-repeat-remix.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} gives a producer's track a voice and a second name. Here the credit survives the remix: Miou Amadee is named on Crussen's version as on Satori's.

## Artistic Context

Satori is based in Amsterdam and, according to MusicBrainz, began in Nijmegen. Crussen, Robbin van der Crussen, describes himself as a Swedish producer, harpist and DJ based in the forest in Norway, who plays his electronic music with live harmonica. The first remix EP of the series also held Acid Pauli's version of The Wishing Spell; hear him in {{ <track_link source="bonaparte-acid-pauli-not-today.md" /> }}.
