## Genre and Mood

**Federleicht** released **Herr B** as the flip side of a 10-inch that Discogs files under tech house, and one of its pressings also under minimal. The feelings on this page, hypnotic and introspective, are our reading, guided by the duo's Discogs profile: full of smart understatement, built on transparent rhythms, each song creating an ambient feeling away from the usual restrictive club style.

## Song Summary

Herr B, listed as Herr B. on the release, runs eight minutes 13 seconds and is the B-side of On The Streets Xtra 10", catalogue number cns026x-6, which Connaisseur Recordings released on 9 February 2009; the digital listing dates it to October 2008. Side A holds Kollektiv Turmstrasse's Let Freedom Ring mix of Federleicht's On The Streets, so Herr B is the only original Federleicht track on the record. Florian Schirmacher and Marc Bammann are credited as its writers and producers, and the record was mastered at True Busyness. In 2010 it appeared again, as Herr B (Original), on Livingroom Techno, another Connaisseur release.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="ambient texture" /> }} is sound that builds atmosphere rather than melody or beat. Federleicht's profile names it as their aim: an ambient feeling in every song, even on records pressed for clubs. Herr B is a test of that promise, a tech house track by a duo whose profile ends: they bridge the gap.

The record around it shows the power of a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. Groove later counted Kollektiv Turmstrasse's version of On The Streets, heard in {{ <track_link source="kollektiv-turmstrasse_on-the-streets-let-freedom.md" /> }}, among Connaisseur's most memorable club hits. Herr B is what the original artists put on the other side.

## Artistic Context

Federleicht, German for light as a feather, is Florian Schirmacher and Marc Bammann. Schirmacher, a singer and composer, is also known from Glowing Glisses, Here Today, Hatikvah and Wareika, and is credited on Wareika's remix of {{ <track_link source="console-a-homeless-ghost.md" /> }}. The profile counts Orbital and Aphex Twin among the influences on Bammann, the producer of the pair.
