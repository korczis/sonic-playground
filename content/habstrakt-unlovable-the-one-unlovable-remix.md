## Genre and Mood

**Habstrakt**'s original The One is bass house, true to what We Rave You called his electro-infused house sound; **The One (Unlovable Remix)** moves the same song toward breakbeat. We Rave You described it as a more melodic and breakbeat-infused take on the track, a version to put on whatever the situation. It stays energetic, and the lyric keeps its longing: a singer wondering whether someone could be the one while she feels herself breaking down.

## Song Summary

Monstercat released the remix on 26 August 2019, the same day as Habstrakt's original and its music video, his first. The vocal is the thread between the two. Dancing Astronaut heard it on the original as a heavily pitched-up cut by Pauline Herr, Habstrakt's close friend and longtime collaborator, and Habstrakt told We Rave You that the moment he heard her vocals, he knew something huge was going to happen. The release credits the writing to Adam Jouneau, Habstrakt's own name, and the remix runs 3:20.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the remix section by section, so this breakdown follows what the reviews contrast with the original.

### The Original

We Rave You heard the original as a slowly burning bassline under distorted female vocals, with kick-drum-driven toplines building toward an intense drop led by mechanical synths.

### The Remix

The remix keeps the song and, by We Rave You's account, changes two things: breakbeat drums and more melody. Where its breaks enter and how its ending is built, no source says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is usually another producer's reworking of a finished track. This one is less simple. The Monstercat fan wiki, citing a Monstercat label showcase on Tomorrowland's One World Radio, reports that Unlovable is a side project of Habstrakt's and that the Unlovable version is the earlier form of the song, with Habstrakt's single the newer one. The same wiki lists Herr alongside Habstrakt as Unlovable. If so, the remix is really an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} by the same hands.

{{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} names the broken, syncopated drum patterns that grew from sampled funk breaks, as opposed to the straight kick of house. Hear the difference by playing this after {{ <track_link source="timo-maas-shifter.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Habstrakt is Adam Jouneau, a French bass-music producer whom Dancing Astronaut placed in Paris. He debuted on the label Monstercat with Infinite in June 2019; The One was his third single there, and the original went into the soundtrack of the driving game Rocket League. In October 2019 the label collected remixes by NGHTMRE, Basstrick, Asdek, Effin, Nitepunk and Unlovable on The One (The Remixes).

## Impact and Reception

No review we found treats the remix on its own; the coverage at release went mostly to the video and the original. Yet the label kept it, placing it on the remix EP two months later. Put it on after the original and listen to the same voice over a different beat.
