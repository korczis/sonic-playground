## Genre and Mood

**The Weeknd**'s "Blinding Lights" is synth-pop, electro-pop and new wave rebuilt for the 2020s: nostalgic in every sound it chooses, euphoric in its rush, and full of longing in what it says.

## Song Summary

The lyric is about the importance of a partner and the need to see them at night. The Weeknd told Esquire that it is about driving to someone while intoxicated, blinded by streetlights, because you are so lonely, and called that its dark undertone. He wrote it with Max Martin, Oscar Holter, Belly and DaHeala, and produced it with Martin and Holter. It came out on 29 November 2019 on XO and Republic Records, the second single from his fourth album, After Hours.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The song starts on a programmed beat that Billboard's Frank DiGiacomo called a DeLorean back to Michael Jackson's "Beat It", with a synth line he compared to Rod Stewart's "Young Turks" and A Flock of Seagulls.

### Verses

Slate's Chris Molanphy describes "scale-laddering verses", melodies that climb step by step, as part of the "melodic math" Max Martin is known for. The pre-chorus names Las Vegas by its nickname, "Sin City", cold and empty.

### Chorus

The chorus is built on tension, with a crystalline synth hook, as Variety put it, and the "Hey, hey, hey" calls that DiGiacomo pictured listeners punching the air to.

### The Video's Slowdown

The official video has a passage of its own: the tempo drops for three and a half measures and the song moves into D Dorian, a change the album recording does not have.

## Concepts in Focus

Almost every sound here is a {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} or a {{ <concept_link name="drum machine" /> }} chosen to evoke the 1980s; Rolling Stone's Kory Grow heard "fuzzy synths and hopscotching drum-machine line" and the best new wave song this side of Duran Duran.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Anton Tammi and filmed over four nights in Las Vegas and Los Angeles, shows the Weeknd bloodied and speeding through an empty city. It also contains a {{ <concept_link name="tempo change" /> }}, the slowdown described above, so the version you watch is not quite the version you stream.

## Artistic Context

The Weeknd has said that 1980s acts such as Siouxsie and the Banshees and the Cocteau Twins have shaped his sound since his first mixtape. Blinding Lights was first heard in a Mercedes-Benz commercial before its release.

## Impact and Reception

Critics received it with near-universal acclaim. It topped the charts in more than 40 countries, spent 90 weeks on the Billboard Hot 100, was named the chart's best-performing song of all time in 2021, and became the most streamed song in Spotify's history. Play it loud, at night, and you will hear why.
