## Genre and Mood

**Chase & Status** made **Step Away** as drum and bass with a singer at its centre: **MC Singing Fats**, one of the scene's best-loved voices. The track is energetic, as the genre demands, and soulful in the way its guest always was, a voice that came to jungle from soul, rare groove and reggae.

## Song Summary

Step Away is the fourth track of Tribe, the duo's fourth studio album, released on 18 August 2017. It runs 4:07, and its writing is credited to Saul Milton and Will Kennard, the two members of Chase & Status, together with Wildale Spencer, the man behind the name MC Fats. The duo produced, programmed and mixed it themselves; Rob Macfarlane engineered it and Stuart Hawkes mastered it. Before any of that was public, it was a dubplate: UKF counted Step Away and Tribes as two of the duo's most heavily hyped dubs in years.

## Concepts in Focus

As a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Fats is not decoration. Credited as a writer and as the vocalist, he is the voice of the track, and UKF's tribute remembered an MC who could toast, host and sing over any kind of drum and bass.

A {{ <concept_link name="dubplate" /> }} is a track a DJ plays before anyone else can. UKF wrote that Step Away had been an integral part of Chase & Status's and Andy C's sets for what felt like forever before it came out on the album.

That route from DJ sets to a record is {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} at work. Fats himself belonged to it for three decades, a long-time resident MC at London's fabric.

## Artistic Context

Tribe came out under exclusive licence to Mercury. Clash's James Kilpin heard an album drawing together everything the duo had explored on three earlier albums, with nods to their roots beside songs made for festival main stages, and wrote that guests such as Mr Vegas, Shy FX and MC Singing Fats paid homage to the roots of their sound. Fats, born Wildale Spencer, had been part of jungle since its early days and worked with Calibre, Dillinja and DJ Hype.

## Impact and Reception

Tribe reached number seven on the UK Albums Chart and number two on the UK Dance Albums Chart. UKF called Step Away one of the key highlights of the record. Fats died in March 2023, after years of ill health. Put on Step Away and listen to the voice first.
