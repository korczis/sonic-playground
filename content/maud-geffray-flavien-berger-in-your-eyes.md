## Genre and Mood

**Maud Geffray**'s **In Your Eyes**, sung with **Flavien Berger**, is electro-pop and synth-pop from a French electronic producer's solo album. Its label calls it a perfect pop duo, and the title and the two voices make it the most romantic moment the sources point to on a record the same label calls a mixture of fire and ice.

## Song Summary

In Your Eyes is the sixth of twelve tracks on Polaar, released by Pan European Recording on 12 May 2017. The album grew from a musical film Geffray made for the Louvre, shot in Lapland by her friend Jamie Harley, where they stayed one winter; she then turned that music into an album. Working alone in her studio, she told TITEL kulturmagazin, she wanted more of herself in the tracks: she put effects on her voice and little pieces of it in every track. For one song she wanted a man's voice, and she asked Berger.

## Concepts in Focus

Berger is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, credited on the album as "feat. Flavien Berger". Geffray knew him through their shared label but says that was not the reason: she went to one of his concerts in Paris, was struck by his voice and proposed the song to him. A feature like this brings a second voice and a second audience: Berger had just had great success in France with his debut album Léviathan.

Polaar also ends with an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: In Your Eyes (Alternate Mix), the twelfth track, about a minute shorter. Two versions of one song on one album invite comparison, showing how much of a song's mood comes from production rather than melody and words.

The official upload on Geffray's channel is a 360-degree {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, in which the viewer turns the frame; no source we found describes what it shows.

## Artistic Context

Geffray is one half of Scratch Massive, the French duo she formed with Sébastien Chenut in the early 2000s; after Chenut moved to Los Angeles, the two had to learn to work separately, which Geffray told TITEL was finally great. Berger, born in Paris in 1986, studied sound design at ENSCI - Les Ateliers and is described as blending electronic music with psychedelia; In Your Eyes is listed among his guest appearances.
