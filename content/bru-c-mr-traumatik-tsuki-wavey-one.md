## Genre and Mood

**Bru-C** and **Mr Traumatik** share the microphone on **Wavey One**, a track from Bru-C's debut album that Apple Music files under jungle and drum and bass, and that the credits hand to the producer Tsuki. It is MC music first: two voices over a fast beat, in a scene where wit and energy count as much as melody. Playful and energetic are the words for the company it keeps rather than for anything a critic has written about it, because no published review describes this recording.

## Song Summary

The streaming credits name Josh Bruce, Bru-C's real name, as composer and lyricist, with Mr Traumatik and Tsuki as featured artists; the music video's title calls Tsuki the producer. The album version runs four and a half minutes. No source explains the lyric, so this page does not. The one clue is the word itself: in an interview with UKF, Bru-C described being "a bit wavey" in the club with a friend, trading jokes until one line sticks and starts a track.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

How Wavey One unfolds, which MC goes first and what the beat does under them, is not described anywhere we could find, so this breakdown stays with what the sources establish. Bru-C brings what UKF called his distinctive Midlands twang and quick-fire bars. Mr Traumatik, born Marcus Coakley in Cardiff, is described by Skiddle as an idiosyncratic emcee who made his name through controversial and provocative lyrics. Listen for how those two voices sit against each other.

### Music Video

Ben Welch directed the video, released on Bru-C's channel on 10 November 2019, twelve days before the album. It runs longer than the album version.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" text="Rapping" /> }} in UK dance music grew from the MC, who hosts and hypes over a DJ's records. Bru-C told UKF that he came up as a host of bassline nights after years in grime and rap battles, and that background is why the voice leads here.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit shows how fluid billing can be. Mr Traumatik is a second lead, not a guest chorus, and Tsuki appears as both a featured artist and, in the video, the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}: in electronic music the person who builds the beat is often billed like a performer.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} came first, before the album, to put the track in front of fans. Compare another MC-led drum and bass track, {{ <track_link source="bou-inja-cous-cous.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Bru-C, from Long Eaton in Derbyshire and based in Nottingham, released Original Sounds on CruCast on 22 November 2019. Asked about Wavey One, he said getting Mr Traumatik on the album "was a must", and that their talks run to food, books and places rather than small talk. He later signed to Def Jam in 2021.

## Impact and Reception

UKF reported that Original Sounds passed almost 14 million streams in its first two months and praised it for flowing like an album rather than a collection of tracks. Wavey One was not singled out by critics. Play it and judge the interviewer's question for yourself: did these two strong characters work together?
