## Genre and Mood

**Ben Khan**'s **ruby** is the slow, glowing centre of an album otherwise full of warped electro-pop and funk. It keeps the soul in his sound and lets the rest drift: Noisey heard it as a dreamlike, abstract place where you float, calm, under arcade-coloured lights. Dreamy fits that description; longing fits a lyric that looks back on a relationship from the other side.

## Song Summary

Khan told DIY that ruby is sung from the other person's perspective in an old relationship of his, perhaps him trying to understand and empathise with a messy situation. He shared it on 22 June 2018, the day he announced his self-titled debut, as track four of fifteen; Noisey counted it as the fourth track taken from the record. Dirty Hit released the album on 10 August. At 5:24, ruby is the longest track on it.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through ruby section by section, so these parts follow the layers the reviews heard rather than the song's order.

### The Beat

The Line of Best Fit heard a muffled kick pulsating under the song. Everything else floats on it.

### The Guitars

The National Student described acoustic chords tinted by electronics; The Line of Best Fit called the guitars swoony and beguiling. Listen for both.

### The Voices

The National Student heard the harmonies and the lyric step forward here, and the track catching its breath in time with the words.

### The Background

Epigram picked out echoes of helicopter blades and faint dhrupad vocals; dhrupad is a style of Hindustani, North Indian, classical singing.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="kick drum" /> }} usually pushes a dance track forward. In ruby it is muffled, which makes it feel less like a command and more like a pulse under the skin, and the song's slow glow depends on it.

{{ <concept_link name="vocal harmony" /> }} is how Khan widens a song he made alone: he plays every instrument on the album, and the harmonies give ruby more than one voice, a fitting device for a lyric sung as somebody else. The next track, {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-a-t-w-against-the-wall.md" /> }}, drew the same Jai Paul comparisons.

For the {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, Khan was his own director. Promonews described a smorgasbord of stylish visuals, his second video with the producer Dom Thomas.

## Artistic Context

Khan is a London singer-producer whose first EPs, 1992 and 1000, came out in 2014 and 2015; The Skinny wrote that he rode the Jai Paul wave with them, then took his time over an album. That debut was produced with Flood, known for work with PJ Harvey, Depeche Mode and Nine Inch Nails, mixed by Spike Stent and mastered by Dave Cooley. The track list also carries a 55-second piece called ruby1strecording, which no review describes.

## Impact and Reception

Critics split on ruby. The Line of Best Fit called it one of the most gorgeous things you would hear all year, glowing with a red passion. The Guardian heard Jai Paul's Jasmine heavily scenting it. The Music, more sceptical, found the ambient track went on far too long. Give it the full five minutes and decide for yourself.
