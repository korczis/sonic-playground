## Genre and Mood

**Whilk & Misky** bring their blend of acoustic warmth and electronic pulse to **Get What We Want**, a track that moves between electronic production, indie pop and soul. Its mood is uplifting, groovy and romantic, a song that feels hopeful without ever becoming saccharine. It is a gentle, sunny song that rewards repeat listens.

## Song Summary

The song builds on a simple idea, the wish to finally get what we want, and gives it a soulful, rolling groove. A warm voice carries the melody over a steady beat, with organic textures such as guitar and live-sounding percussion woven into the electronic production. The track is less about big drops than about a constant, gentle momentum, the kind that makes you nod along before you notice you are doing it. The production feels handcrafted, with acoustic textures sitting naturally inside the electronic beat, and the result is music that works as well on a quiet afternoon as it does in a relaxed club set. Its simple wish feels universal, which is part of its charm.

## Artistic Context

Whilk & Misky are a British and Iranian duo who first drew attention with tracks such as "Darklands", building a sound that mixes acoustic songwriting with electronic and house influences. "Get What We Want" appears on *My Round*, a release that also spawned a remix package with reworks from producers such as Seb Wildblood. Their music sits in a space between singer-songwriter intimacy and house rhythm, a combination they have kept refining across their releases.

## Impact and Reception

The duo have built a loyal following through their warm, genre-blurring approach, and *My Round* showed them refining that sound into compact, soulful songs. Their earlier tracks had already shown a talent for marrying acoustic instruments with a dance-floor pulse, and this song continues that thread with a warmer, more optimistic tone. It is on this playground as a companion to "Darklands", another side of the same duo. Put it on for a slow, sunny moment.
