## Genre and Mood

**Emika**'s **My Heart Bleeds Melody** is electronic music with roots in electroclash and electro-pop. The feelings named here come from the critics: melancholic, after Spectrum Culture's "depressed post-industrial"; hypnotic, after the Electricity Club's intricate pulsing layers; dark, like the brooding world Ibiza Spotlight heard across the whole album.

## Song Summary

The title promises heartbreak, and the delivery refuses to dramatise it: the Electricity Club heard solemn detachment over the layers, a combination it found captivating and growing with each listen. The music and lyrics are Emika's, and as on the rest of DREI she produced and mixed the track herself; the video credits its mastering to Hue Jah Fink. It was the album's promo single in April 2015, ahead of DREI's release on 4 May.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }} was a scene of the late 1990s and early 2000s that crossed 1980s electro and synth-pop with punk attitude and deadpan vocals. Emika, who moved to Berlin in 2006, released this track in 2015, and critics heard the scene in it: the detached voice, the bass, the beats. Compare the cool double vocal of {{ <track_link source="ladytron_seventeen.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was uploaded by Emika Records in April 2015, with montage and video effects credited to Monsieur Zupika and further effects and colour correction to Chris Glazewski and Kee Zoo. It is a video edit, shorter than the album version.

Emika has also released {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" text="alternate versions" /> }} of her own: an instrumental and an a cappella of My Heart Bleeds Melody. Hearing the voice alone shows how much of the detachment lives in the singing, and the instrumental shows how much lives in the layers.

## Artistic Context

Emika, born Ema Jolly in Milton Keynes in 1986, with a Czech mother from Příbram, made two albums for Ninja Tune before starting Emika Records in 2014. DREI, reportedly written in two weeks, followed the piano album Klavirni, released on the label in January 2015; My Heart Bleeds Melody follows the opener {{ <track_link source="emika-battles.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The Electricity Club named it one of the highlights of DREI, an excellent track that rewarded repeated listening. Give it that second listen, and a third with the a cappella.
