## Genre and Mood

**Weval** made **The Most** for their first record, and it is electronic music on its way to the dance floor. No review describes this track's mood on its own; the feelings named here, warm and dreamy, are this page's reading of what Exclaim! called the bright and lush debut EP it comes from.

## Song Summary

The Most is the third track of the Half Age EP, which Atomnation released on 19 March 2013 as the duo's debut. The EP's own note introduces the opener, Out of the Game, as an anthem in waiting, then says the other tracks gradually move more towards the dance floor, glued together by chopped-up minimalistic vocals, analogue sounds and ever-evolving chord schemes. The Most is one of those other tracks; it is credited to both members, Harm Coolen and Merijn Scholte Albers.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }} is the order in which chords follow one another, and an ever-evolving one changes the harmony under the parts that repeat. That is how a track built from short fragments can still feel like it is going somewhere.

The EP also carries its own {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}: The Most (David Douglas Remix) closes the original digital release. Play the two back to back and listen for what Douglas kept.

Ten years on, Weval released The Most as a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} from HERE at Outernet in London, on their Remember tour of 2023, mixed and mastered by the duo themselves. A track made by two producers in a studio becomes something performed in a room.

## Artistic Context

Coolen, born in 1986, and Scholte Albers, born in 1989, came to music from the film industry and began producing together in 2010. After Half Age, Detian from the same EP was used in a Schweppes commercial in 2014 and reached number five on the French iTunes electronic chart. The duo later released on Kompakt and then on Technicolour, a Ninja Tune imprint; their album Remember was nominated for an Edison in 2024. ## Impact and Reception

The Most has had a long life in the duo's own catalogue: a remix on the EP, a place on its 2014 vinyl reissue and a slot in their live set a decade later. Start with the 2013 version, then watch the 2023 one.
