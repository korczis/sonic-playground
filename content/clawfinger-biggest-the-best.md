**Clawfinger** unleashes a fierce sound with their track **Biggest & The Best**,

"Biggest & The Best" exemplifies Clawfinger's signature style, combining
heavy guitar riffs with rap verses. The track delivers a sharp critique of
societal values that prioritize fame and ego, all set against a backdrop of
high-energy instrumentation.
