## Genre and Mood

**FRENCHGRL** takes **Seven Nation Army**, the White Stripes' garage rock riff that became a stadium chant, and makes it a short electronic single; Apple Music files it under electronic. No review of this version has been published, so the feelings named here, hypnotic and bold, are a reading of what any version of this riff carries, not a quotation. What the track adds to them is for your ears to decide.

## Song Summary

The facts come from the release itself. Sunflower Records, a division of Inside Music, released Seven Nation Army as a single on 3 December 2025. It runs 2:08. The credits name FRENCHGRL as producer and Jack White as composer and lyricist, so the words of the original are part of the cover. Jack White wrote the song as a little experiment, a compelling song without a chorus, built on a seven-note riff he played on a semi-acoustic guitar pitched an octave down so that it sounded like a bass.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of someone else's song. The credits show how FRENCHGRL works: on Sunflower Records the same name has released takes on Toxic and Katy Perry's I Kissed A Girl, each credited to the original writers, alongside originals such as Let's Vibe and collaborations with PLÜM. Compare the 2021 dance cover {{ <track_link source="gaullin-julian-perretta-seven-nation-army.md" /> }}, which keeps the riff and rebuilds everything around it.

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} is a short, repeated figure that carries a song, and this one is among the most famous: seven notes on five pitches, a held note, four syncopated notes and two closing ones. Because it works without words, it moves easily between genres and arrangements, which is why so many covers of it exist.

## Artistic Context

FRENCHGRL's catalogue on Apple Music runs from 2024 onward, mostly singles filed under electronic and dance: Stolen Dance and Riptide with PLÜM, Let's Vibe, Mr Mystery, I'll Go Blue. Stolen Dance came out as an Inside Records release, and Sunflower Records itself is a division of Inside Music. Hear the original first in {{ <track_link source="the-white-stripes-seven-nation-army.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The White Stripes' recording won the Grammy for Best Rock Song and became one of the world's best-known sports anthems; FRENCHGRL's is one of its newest covers. Play the original, then this, and count the seven notes in both.
