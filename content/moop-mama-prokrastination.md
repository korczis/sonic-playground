## Genre and Mood

**Moop Mama** call what they play urban brass, and **Prokrastination** shows what that means: hip-hop and rap in German, with the beat played not by a drum machine but by a brass band and two drummers. The mood is playful and groovy, a song about not getting things done that is in no hurry itself.

## Song Summary

The title is the German word for procrastination, and the song is about exactly that. Reviewing its album for laut.de, Alexander Austel heard the band take the lead first and create a musical feeling of procrastination, before the rapper Keno describes the clinical picture of endless postponing; Keno himself, the reviewer adds, does not seem to suffer from it. At more than five minutes, the studio track gives the band room to set the scene before the words arrive.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the track part by part beyond its opening and its verses, so this outline stays short.

### Intro

The horns and drums play on their own first. In Austel's reading, this is where the subject is set up: the music does the dawdling before anyone names it.

### Main Section

Then Keno comes in and the song becomes a description of the condition, delivered in rhymes over the band. Laut.de praised his wordplay across the album and noted that the brass sometimes pushes him to pick up speed to keep pace.

## Concepts in Focus

Most hip-hop is built on loops: a sample or a programmed pattern repeated under the voice. Laut.de stressed that Moop Mama's wind players open up new hip-hop territory entirely without looped samples, so the {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} has to do what a loop would: hold the groove, leave space for the voice and change when the song needs it. The band's founder, the tenor saxophonist Marcus Kesselbauer, is also its composer and arranger, and he shares the writing credit here with Keno Langbein. Compare the horns of {{ <track_link source="macklemore-ryan-lewis-thrift-shop.md" /> }}, which come from a chopped-up loop.

The {{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} works against that live backing: Keno's voice is the one instrument that is not brass or drums, and the review hears the band driving him.

Moop Mama are a stage band first, and the song exists as a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} too. Prokrastination (Live 2019), on the 2020 album Live Vol. 2, credits every player of that line-up: trumpets, saxophones, trombones, a tuba, two drummers and Keno.

## Artistic Context

Moop Mama formed in Munich in 2009 around Kesselbauer, Keno and Lukas Roth, and made their name with unannounced guerrilla concerts in parks and pedestrian zones, Keno rapping through a megaphone over the horns; some of those shows ended in trouble with the police over noise. M.O.O.P.Topia, their third studio album, appeared in 2016 on Mutterkomplex and reached number 23 in Germany. Keno left the band in 2022.

## Impact and Reception

Laut.de called M.O.O.P.Topia the soundtrack to the coming festival season. In March 2020, as people were asked to stay at home, the band put the instrumentals of three albums online and posted a karaoke video of Prokrastination, inviting fans to imitate Keno at home. Play it on a day you have been putting something off.
