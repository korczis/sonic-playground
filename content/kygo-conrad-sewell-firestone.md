## Genre and Mood

**Kygo** and **Conrad Sewell** made **Firestone** one of the records that defined tropical house, filed by Wikipedia under that name and deep house. Its reviewers reached for the beach: Dancing Astronaut heard "beachy nirvana", Music Times a soundtrack for pool parties. It is relaxed, dreamy and carefree, and it never raises its voice.

## Song Summary

Sewell wrote the song first. He told Songfacts he was writing for a Swedish House Mafia pitch and imagined a big EDM anthem in the vein of Don't You Worry Child; the title came from a huge sign for the tyre company Firestone that he passed while driving to Venice. Kygo, who said he fell in love with Sewell's voice, sent it back "all tropical". Sewell disliked it at first, then on the third listen was won over by what he called "the polar opposite" of his idea. Kygo produced it; Sewell, Kygo and Martijn Konijnenburg wrote it.

## Concepts in Focus

Firestone is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} record in which the guest brought the song and the producer brought the sound. Compare Sewell's description of what he wrote with what you hear.

It is also a lesson in the missing {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }}. Dancing Astronaut noted that it departs from the drop-and-breakdown structure common in other electronic genres; instead of tension and release, the track keeps one temperature.

The album version runs 4:32; a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} of 3:24 was cut for airplay. Kygo's later {{ <track_link source="kygo-maty-noyes-stay.md" /> }} shows the same approach a year on.

## Artistic Context

Kygo, Kyrre Gørvell-Dahll, grew up in Bergen, started piano lessons at six and considers himself more a pianist than a DJ; he taught himself production with Logic and a MIDI keyboard from YouTube tutorials. Firestone, released on 1 December 2014, was his first original single after a run of remixes, and it reappeared on his debut album Cloud Nine in 2016.

## Impact and Reception

Firestone went to number one in Norway, number two in Sweden, three in Germany and eight in the UK, where it topped the dance chart; France certified it diamond. Its video, starring Rachel Echelberger in a chase through doors into forests, parties and a dry Los Angeles riverbed, passed a billion views. Wikipedia counts Kygo among those who brought tropical house into the commercial mainstream, and this is where it started. Put it on at dusk and let it stay at one temperature.
