## Genre and Mood

**Alok** and **Bruno Martini** made **Hear Me Now** with the singer **Zeeba**, and it became the song the world knows Alok by. It sits between Brazilian bass, house and indie pop, and its label introduced it not with a beat but with a guitar lick, melodic chords and a flute. The mood is nostalgic and heartfelt, and in the end uplifting, especially once you have seen the video.

## Song Summary

As its title says, Hear Me Now is a plea to be heard, sung by Zeeba. Spinnin' Records, which released it on 21 October 2016, described its sound as a smooth guitar lick and melodic chords, enhanced by a flute. The blog Noiseporn filed it under Brazilian bass, and it is also listed as house and indie pop: a dance record that also found its way onto pop radio. All three artists share the writing credit, and Alok and Martini produced it.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, written and directed by Caio Amantini and Rapha Pampiona, is what most people met the song through. A grown man travels to his parents' home and relives memories with his father, then wakes in the taxi: it was a dream, and he runs to hug his father. By January 2026 the video had passed 673 million views. It gives a plea to be heard a second meaning, about family and time.

Zeeba is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: the Brazilian-American singer is credited after the two producers, as is usual in dance music, though the voice is what listeners remember. Zeeba is also a co-writer, which is not always the case for a featured singer.

The track's success brought a long line of versions. Each {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds the song for a different floor: Spinnin' released a radio mix by EDX and Nora En Pure in March 2017, and in October 2023 a remix EP with new versions by Alok and by Martini themselves.

## Artistic Context

Alok, born Alok Achkar Peres Petrillo in Goiânia in 1991, is the son of the DJs Swarup and Ekanta, pioneers of psytrance in Brazil and founders of the Universo Paralello festival in Bahia; his name means light in Sanskrit. House Mag had named him the best DJ in Brazil in 2014 and 2015; Hear Me Now made him a name worldwide. Since 2021 he has ranked fourth in DJ Mag's Top 100 DJs four years running, and third in 2025.

## Impact and Reception

The song reached number 6 on Billboard Brasil's pop airplay chart and charted across Europe: number 14 in Finland, 40 in Austria and Denmark, 50 in France and Germany, and number 7 on the Czech radio chart.
