## Genre and Mood

**Jain** wrote **Take a Chance** for The Fool, her third album, and it is pop with a dance floor under it: OnesToWatch called it disco-riddled and pure pop. Uplifting and playful are this page's words for that, and romantic because Jain describes the album as a love story and this song as a fated encounter.

## Song Summary

Jain introduced the song in two lines: the fated encounter, and the audacity to seize a chance away from the marked path. She wrote the words with Jason Glasser and the music with Yodelice, who arranged and produced it. Matt Chamberlain plays drums, Yodelice guitar and bass, Johan Dalgaard keyboards and synthesizer, Thibault Mortegoute trombone. It was recorded by Yodelice and Michael Harroch, mixed by Bob Clearmountain and mastered by Alex Gopher.

### Groove

The disco pulse OnesToWatch heard is the frame: a song built to move, in under three and a half minutes.

### Band

The credits name a player for every part, from Chamberlain's drums to Mortegoute's trombone.

### Lyric

The story is the one Jain gave it: a meeting that feels fated, and the nerve to step off the expected road for it.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes a recording from song to sound. Yodelice, the French musician Maxim Nucci, discovered Jain's demos and produced her debut; here he is co-writer, arranger, producer, guitarist, bassist and recording engineer at once.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} balances the recorded parts into one stereo picture. Bob Clearmountain, the engineer credited with the mix, decides how the drums, bass, keyboards, trombone and voice sit against each other.

{{ <concept_link name="mastering" /> }} is the last step: the finished mix is prepared for release, its loudness and tone made consistent across the album. Alex Gopher, a French mastering engineer and electronic musician who was part of the French touch of the 1990s, mastered this one.

## Artistic Context

The Fool came out on 21 April 2023 on Columbia and Sony Music France. Jain built it around the Tarot de Marseille, which her mother taught her, and the card of the Fool, about the willingness to be foolish that any journey asks. OnesToWatch reports that it was born during the pandemic in a room in Marseille and is more guitar-led than her earlier, vocally driven work; BroadwayWorld names Kate Bush and Stevie Nicks among its influences.

## Impact and Reception

Take a Chance came out on 24 March 2023 with a lyric video, after the title track, and Jain performed both on the French television show Quotidien. Wikipedia's discography lists it as a single without chart positions; the title track, by contrast, charted. Play it with the album around it, and hear the chance it takes.
