## Genre and Mood

**Dreadzone** put **Iron Shirt** together from reggae's past and the dance floor's present. Discogs files the album under breakbeat, dub and breaks; Wikipedia calls the band dub, reggae and electronic. The mood is defiant and spiritual, a six-minute track with a borrowed battle cry at its core.

## Song Summary

The core is Max Romeo's Chase the Devil, released by Island on War Ina Babylon in 1976, written and produced by Romeo and Lee "Scratch" Perry. Romeo sings of putting on an iron shirt to chase the devil out of the earth; in a 2010 BBC interview he said the devil is everything negative in our minds and the iron shirt is our strength of spirit. Dreadzone, in the Financial Times' words, snipped off a patch of that song for Iron Shirt, and a Rebel Base reviewer heard its refrain as a reworking of the Romeo classic. The track runs 6:02 on Once Upon a Time and was released as a single in 2006.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the beat or the arrangement of Iron Shirt section by section; the sources establish the refrain, the voices and the players, and this breakdown stays with them.

### Chorus

The refrain is where Chase the Devil comes back, sampled and reworked rather than covered, so a 1976 roots record speaks from inside a 2005 production.

### Main Section

Two vocalists are credited on the recording, Earl Sixteen and MC Spee; which of them takes which part no source says. Steve Roberts, the brother of founder Greg Roberts, is credited on guitar, bass and keyboards; Discogs credits the production, arrangement and electronics to Greg Dread and Ben Balafonic.

## Concepts in Focus

Iron Shirt is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Chase the Devil is among reggae's most borrowed records: The Prodigy sampled it for Out of Space in 1992 and Jay-Z's Lucifer took its opening line. Dreadzone use it to anchor the refrain.

{{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }}, the Jamaican art of remixing a record through the mixing desk, is the band's foundation; their sound system ran the Dubwiser club night in London.

{{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} is the other half: drum patterns built from broken, syncopated loops rather than a steady four-on-the-floor kick, the style Discogs gives the whole album.

## Artistic Context

Dreadzone formed in London in 1993 around the former Big Audio Dynamite drummer Greg Roberts. Earl Sixteen joined in 1995 and MC Spee in 2000. Once Upon a Time came out in 2005 on the independent Functional label, recorded by Greg Roberts, Ben Balafonic, Spee, Sixteen and Steve Roberts, who died in October 2006.

## Impact and Reception

Rebel Base heard a rougher Dreadzone on this album and called it a record for every reggae lover's collection. Iron Shirt is also on the 2006 live album Live at Sunrise, whose reviewer singled out its refrain. Three years later Earl Sixteen covered Chase the Devil himself. Turn it up and listen for the shirt being put on.
