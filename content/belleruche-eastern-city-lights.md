## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** tagged **Eastern City Lights** on their own Bandcamp under blues, electronic, soul and turntablism, and Deezer files the single that carried it under R&B and soul and funk. No critic seems to have written about the song itself. Introspective and haunting are borrowed, honestly, from the words the band's label used for Rollerchain, the album whose streaming edition now ends with it: introspective yet aggressive, with haunting vocals. Whether this track fits those words, no source says.

## Song Summary

Eastern City Lights runs 3:29. Tru Thoughts released it on 10 October 2013 as the second of two tracks on a digital single, after Minor Swing, and included it eighteen days later as the last of 28 tracks on the band's Best Of. On Spotify it closes Rollerchain (Bonus Track Version), fourteen tracks against the thirteen of the 2012 album. The label posted it on its YouTube jukebox channel that November. ## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005, released their first records on their own Hippoflex label and signed in 2007 to Tru Thoughts, the Brighton label that put out all four of their albums. Rollerchain, the fourth, came out on 7 May 2012; the label described it as darker and more bass-heavy than before, with the soulful touch kept. Eastern City Lights came after it, one of the last new tracks the trio released.

## Impact and Reception

On the eve of the Best Of, the band announced that they were saying farewell, or at least see you in a bit; in June 2025 they announced new material. The song has no documented reception. Play it late, with the city outside.
