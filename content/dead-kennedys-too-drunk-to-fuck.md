## Genre and Mood

**Dead Kennedys** deliver a punchy and rebellious sound in **"Too Drunk to Fuck,"** a 
track that blends punk rock energy with biting social commentary. The song is a 
quintessential example of the band’s ability to tackle serious subjects 
with humor and sarcasm.

## Song Summary

"Too Drunk to Fuck" examines the consequences of excessive drinking, especially 
in the context of relationships. The lyrics convey a mix of frustration and humor, 
embodying the punk ethos of challenging societal norms.

As the song progresses, the tempo remains high, and the rhythmic guitar and

## Artistic Context

**Dead Kennedys** were known for their provocative lyrics and satirical 
approach to social issues, making them iconic figures in the punk rock 
scene of the late 1970s and early 1980s. "Too Drunk to Fuck" is emblematic of 
their style, blending humor with critical commentary on contemporary life.

## Impact and Reception

"Too Drunk to Fuck" has maintained its status as a classic punk anthem, 
celebrated for its clever lyrics and high-energy performance. The song 
resonates with fans for its unabashed critique of societal behaviors and 
the punk rock spirit.
