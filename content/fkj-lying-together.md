## Genre and Mood

**FKJ** introduced himself with **Lying Together**, a track that mixes deep house, electronic production and funk warmth. Its mood is romantic, groovy and chill, a slow-burning piece of music that sounds like a lazy, sunlit afternoon. There is nothing aggressive here; even the beat feels like it is lounging.

## Song Summary

"Lying Together" is built from a small set of warm elements: chopped vocal fragments, filtered chords, a soft but steady beat and an elastic bassline. FKJ loops and layers them with the patience of a musician who plays every instrument himself, and the result feels intimate rather than club-sized. The memorable detail is the way the vocal snippets drift in and out, like overheard words from a conversation in the next room. The groove sways rather than pounds, and the sound has the slightly worn, analogue texture of a record played on a good system in a small room. It is house music built for headphones and late breakfasts as much as for clubs.

## Musical Breakdown

The track unfolds gently over four and a half minutes, building its groove one layer at a time.

### Intro

Filtered chords and a soft beat open the track, warm and slightly hazy, setting the relaxed tempo.

### Build-up

The bassline joins, rounded and funky, and percussion fills out the groove as the filter slowly opens.

### Main Section

Chopped vocal fragments and the full chord progression take over, the groove settling into a warm, hypnotic loop.

### Bridge

A breakdown pulls back the beat, leaving the chords and voices floating before the groove returns.

### Outro

The layers fade back to the filtered chords, ending the track as softly as it began.

## Concepts in Focus

Lying Together runs on a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}, but a softened one. The steady pulse of house is there, yet the beat is gentle, the bass is funky rather than pumping, and the chords carry the warmth of soul records. That mix is what the label called neo-house.

The main source of movement is the {{ <concept_link name="filter sweep" /> }}. The chords enter muffled and hazy, and as the filter opens they brighten, so the track seems to come closer without getting louder. Listen to the intro and the build-up for the clearest version of it.

Underneath, everything is a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}: short chord, bass and vocal phrases repeated and layered one at a time. FKJ later made this method visible on stage, building tracks live from loops he plays himself; for a contemporary from the other side of the world working with the same warm, intimate palette, compare {{ <track_link source="chet-faker-gold.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

FKJ, short for French Kiwi Juice, is the alias of the French multi-instrumentalist Vincent Fenton from Tours, who plays guitar, bass, keys and saxophone and is known for building tracks live, layer by layer. "Lying Together" came out in November 2012 on Roche Musique, the label the DJ and producer Cezaire (Jean Janin) founded that year, and the label's history names it as the neo-house track that put both on the map; a second release followed in 2014. FKJ's second Roche Musique EP, *Time For A Change*, came a year later, and his debut album *French Kiwi Juice* (2017) went gold internationally. Roche Musique's catalogue of French producers mixing house, funk and soul gave him a natural home, and his later records and live shows developed the same blend into something more ambitious, often with him moving between instruments on stage.

## Impact and Reception

The track helped establish FKJ and the Roche Musique sound, a blend of French house, funk and soul, and it remains a staple of his live sets years later. It earned its place here for its warmth and intimacy. Put it on when you want to slow everything down.
