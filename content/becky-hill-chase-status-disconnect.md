## Genre and Mood

**Becky Hill** and **Chase & Status** made **Disconnect** a drum and bass single for the charts, and Hill described it exactly that way: a true dance-floor record that walks the line between the rave and the radio. It is energetic and intense, and short, at 2:44: Riff Magazine heard distorted synths and quick, hard-hitting beats, and Songfacts a moody atmosphere around them.

## Song Summary

The song is about switching off. Songfacts reads the lyric as Hill stuck in a rut, heading to the club to disconnect from her thoughts and feelings and coming out recharged; Melomaniacs Mag hears a song about isolation. Hill wrote it with her long-standing collaborator Karen Poole and the drum and bass artist Emily Makis, together with Chase & Status, Saul Milton and Will Kennard, and Kieron McIntosh, who co-produced it with the duo and played keyboards.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track; the reviews describe its elements rather than its sections.

### Vocal

Riff Magazine called this some of Hill's strongest belting, and Melomaniacs Mag heard her voice stand out from the production rather than sink into it.

### Beat and Synths

Under her, Chase & Status bring what Riff Magazine called distorted synths and quick, hard-hitting beats, the drum and bass engine that sets the song's pace.

### Outro

At 2:44 the song ends quickly; an extended mix followed two weeks after the single.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="distortion" /> }} is what happens when a sound is pushed past what a circuit or a plug-in can carry cleanly, so that it grows harsher and fuller. In Riff Magazine's description the synths here are distorted, which is where much of the track's intensity comes from, set against a voice that stays clear.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a track from its parts. Tiësto's remix, released on 4 August 2023, replaced the original's moody atmosphere with revved-up house beats, in Songfacts' words. An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} goes further: on 10 September 2023 Hill released an orchestral acoustic version, filmed at the Canopy hotel in London. For another British pop voice carried into drum and bass, hear {{ <track_link source="john-newman-kove-love-me-again-kove-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Disconnect came out on 14 July 2023 on Polydor, the second single from Hill's second album, Believe Me Now?, released on 31 May 2024. It was her second drum and bass record, a decade after Afterglow with Wilkinson; she said she had dreamed of working with Chase & Status since her teens, and that the song had already been going off at festivals before its release. She first played it at Creamfields South in May 2023.

## Impact and Reception

Disconnect reached number six in the UK and number two on the UK Dance chart, Hill's sixth top-ten single and Chase & Status' fifth, and it is double platinum in the UK. Hill and the duo performed it at the BRIT Awards on 2 March 2024. Two minutes forty-four; play it loud.
