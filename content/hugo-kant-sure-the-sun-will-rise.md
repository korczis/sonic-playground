## Genre and Mood

**Hugo Kant** made **Sure the Sun Will Rise** for a planet that does not exist. It is French trip-hop on the border of downtempo and nu-jazz, the kind Scratched Vinyl called cinematic instrumental hip-hop with lush orchestration and funky beats. The mood is atmospheric and warm, with a groove underneath: Radio Študent heard in the album's acoustic sounds a feeling of warmth and homeliness, even in the jungles of a distant world.

## Song Summary

The track is the fourth of eleven on Far From Home, released on 16 October 2020 by the label Bellring, and runs 4:55. Kant composed and produced every track himself, and Scratched Vinyl noted that, unlike on earlier projects, there are no guests. Radio Študent chose this track as its example of one side of the album's sound, a mix of cultures that felt like a dub party in India: very organic sounds of classical instruments combined with equally classic electronic beats.

## Concepts in Focus

Far From Home is a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }} told almost without words. Scratched Vinyl traced the story: the previous album, Out of Time, followed an astronaut lost in space, and here he is stranded on an alien planet. Radio Študent read the track order as his day there: the hour grows late, then not one sun rises but two, and two tracks follow, each devoted to its own sun: The Second Sun and this one.

The sound rests on {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Radio Študent pictured the astronaut arriving with a MIDI controller full of samples, noted that most of them come from acoustic instruments, and that Kant puts them on his website free for other musicians.

What holds it together is {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }}. Benzine described his method as an ant's work: he chooses melodic or rhythmic bases he likes, then enriches and polishes them bit by bit until the texture is right.

## Artistic Context

Hugo Kant is the alias of the French producer and multi-instrumentalist Quentin Le Roux. Far From Home, his fourth album, was about two years in the making, according to Benzine. Parapet Musical listed keyboards, bass, guitars, percussion, flugelhorn, clarinet and flute across the album; on stage, Radio Študent noted, the flute is the only acoustic instrument he brings.

## Impact and Reception

Benzine picked Far From Home among its twelve albums of October 2020, Parapet Musical gave it 8/10 as a varied but coherent instrumental journey, and Scratched Vinyl called it another excellent album from a special talent. Put it on at dawn and listen for the second sun.
