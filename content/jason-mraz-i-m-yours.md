## Genre and Mood

**Jason Mraz**'s **I'm Yours** is pop with a reggae lilt, the work of a singer-songwriter in no hurry: carefree, warm and uplifting. Critics have filed it under reggae, folk-pop, soft rock and Jawaiian, the Hawaiian blend of Jamaican reggae with local acoustic pop and folk.

## Song Summary

Mraz wrote it in San Diego in the summer of 2004, just before his first visit to Hawaii. He told American Songwriter it came so quickly and joyfully that he thought he might save it for a children's record. He wrote it on an electric guitar, but, as he told Ukulele Magazine, what he heard in his head was a ukulele rhythm and the lightness that instrument brings. Although many hear a love song, he has described it as singing to the great unknown, to God or a higher power or universal love.

### The Groove

As Mraz kept returning to Hawaii, he told Hawai'i Magazine, the rhythm of the Islands began to shape the way he played the song. Ukulele Magazine credits time in Jamaica for its reggae influence.

### The Chords

The published sheet music gives four chords in B major, B, F-sharp, G-sharp minor and E, at a moderate tempo.

### The Band

Martin Terefe produced the album version and plays bass guitar, piano and guitar on it, alongside Mraz's vocal and guitar, Nikolas Torp Larsen's keyboards, Kristoffer Sonne's drums and a group of backing singers.

A {{ <concept_link name="demo" /> }} of I'm Yours came out first, in 2005, on the limited EP Extra Credit, promoting Mraz's second album Mr. A–Z. By then it was already a crowd favourite at his 2004 and 2005 shows, and the island feel grew over those years of playing it.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, which debuted on 14 March 2008, was shot on Oahu and Kauai with the director Darren Doane.

## Artistic Context

I'm Yours was released on 12 February 2008 as the first single from We Sing. We Dance. We Steal Things., Mraz's third album, which followed on Atlantic in May and reached number three on the Billboard 200. It was recorded mostly in London and San Diego.

## Impact and Reception

The song peaked at number six on the Billboard Hot 100 and stayed 76 weeks, a record at the time. It became the first song to top the Triple A, Adult Top 40, Mainstream Top 40 and Adult Contemporary charts, went to number one in Sweden, Norway and New Zealand, and earned Grammy nominations for Song of the Year and Best Male Pop Vocal Performance. In 2021 it passed a billion streams on Spotify. Pick up a guitar or a ukulele and play along.
