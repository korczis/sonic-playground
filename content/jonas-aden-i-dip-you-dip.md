## Genre and Mood

**Jonas Aden** built **I Dip You Dip** for the dark end of the dance floor. It is tech house, so house at heart and electronic all the way through, but its mood is closer to a warning than a party: dark, groovy and a little mysterious. Spinnin' Records, which distributed it, said its effects and bass bring an obscure but exciting vibe to the floor.

## Song Summary

The track is short, 2:24 in its streaming version, and it works by contrast. It opens on dark chords and ominous vocals, then turns into a tech-house groove of twisting sound effects and raw bass; the label called it a moody club piece and a strong DJ tool. The title is the hook most listeners will remember, but no source explains it, so it is best taken as sound.

## Concepts in Focus

I Dip You Dip is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. The record had been creating a stir through the summer of 2018 before it was released. At Tomorrowland that year Don Diablo heard it in the set of the producer Brooks on the first weekend, contacted Aden within minutes to ask for it for his label Hexagon, and by the second weekend played it from the main stage to a crowd of more than 80,000.

The {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} exists for those DJs: about a minute longer than the streaming version, giving them more room to mix the track in and out of a set.

E minor, if Discogs is right, puts the track in a {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }}, which fits the dark chords at its start.

## Artistic Context

Jonas Aden is a Norwegian producer who first made his name between 2015 and 2017 with releases on Heldeep and Hexagon; {{ <track_link source="jonas-aden-young-god.md" /> }}, a 2016 free download, comes from the same years, and Don Diablo picked his track Temple for Hexagon's Generation HEX EP in 2016. He was also, his agency says, the first Norwegian to sign with Spinnin' Records. I Dip You Dip, released on 26 October 2018, ended an 18-month break in which he released no new music.

## Impact and Reception

EDMLI wrote in 2019 that Aden had dominated 2018 with it, and Aden called seeing one of his favourite artists play his track in front of so many people insane. His agency says it overtook Fisher's Losing It on a trending chart within 24 hours. Turn it up and wait for the bass.
