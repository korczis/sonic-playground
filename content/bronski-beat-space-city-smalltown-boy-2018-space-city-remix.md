## Genre and Mood

**Bronski Beat** and **Space City** meet on **Smalltown Boy 2018 (Space City Remix)**, the last of four remixes on the second Smalltown Boy 2k18 single, released on 6 April 2018. Discogs files the single under synth-pop, hi-NRG and Euro-disco, the dance music the song came from in 1984. The mood belongs to the song itself: a melancholic story of leaving home, and in 2018 a nostalgic return to a record already thirty-four years old.

## Song Summary

Smalltown Boy tells of a young man who is bullied and decides to leave home. Jimmy Somerville, Steve Bronski and Larry Steinbachek wrote it, and it first came out in May 1984. The 2018 title points to a later Bronski Beat: in 2016 Steve Bronski revived the band with Ian Donaldson and a new singer, Stephen Granville, and in 2017 they re-recorded most of their debut album as The Age of Reason. Space City's remix runs 7:59. No official upload of it was found, so the page has no video.

## Concepts in Focus

Two kinds of new version meet in this title. Smalltown Boy 2018 is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same song recorded again by a band carrying the same name, with a different singer and, on The Age of Reason, keyboards and programming by Steve Bronski and Ian Donaldson. Space City's {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} then reworks the song as another producer's track. The single gathers four of them: Dario Trapani at 4:17, Dirty Disco's Mainroom Remix at 8:22, Babert at 5:30 and Space City at 7:59. A first 2k18 single, in February, had four more. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat-arnaud-rebotini-smalltown-boy-arnaud-rebotini-remix.md" /> }}, made a year earlier from the 1984 recording.

## Artistic Context

Bronski Beat formed in London in 1983, and Smalltown Boy, produced by Mike Thorne, began as an attempt to cover the Sex Pistols' Pretty Vacant. The reformed band released The Age of Reason on 28 July 2017 through Strike Force Entertainment, a name of Cherry Red Records, and The Age of Remix on 30 March 2018. Wikipedia's discography places the two 2k18 singles with those albums. Discogs gives Space City no profile; its credits are remixes, most of them of records by Dirty Disco, and a Hard Rain remix on The Age of Remix. Steve Bronski died in December 2021.

## Impact and Reception

No chart or review records the 2k18 singles. The original reached number three in the UK and number one on the US dance chart, and it is still the reason anyone remixes the song. Play {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat_small_town_boy.md" /> }} first, then this, and listen for what survives.
