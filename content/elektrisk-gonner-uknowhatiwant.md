## Genre and Mood

**Elektrisk Gønner** sits where electro meets indie rock, and **Uknowhatiwant** is the track that shows it most bluntly. The French review that covered its album heard it as energetic, punchy electro-rock; the title, run together into one word, already sounds like a demand rather than a question, and the rest of the song's story leans provocative.

## Song Summary

Uknowhatiwant appeared as a single in spring 2011 and then as the third of thirteen tracks on Yer Fire, the project's debut album, released in France in May 2011 by the label Platinum. The streaming credits name Benjamin Stasola as its composer and lyricist. Little has been written about the song itself: no published source explains its lyric, so this page leaves it to the listener.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through this track section by section, and this page does not guess at its structure. What can be said comes from the album review.

### The Drive

Muzzart's reviewer singled the song out as one of Yer Fire's strongest, a punchy electro-rock track built for energy rather than atmosphere.

### The Palette

Across the album the same review heard heavy basslines, bouncy synths and distorted riffs, with guitars on some tracks, and a female voice alternating or blending with a deeper male one on several songs. How much of that is in this track is yours to hear.

## Concepts in Focus

A song can travel through a {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} placement far beyond its label's reach. On his own YouTube upload of the track, the artist lists it as featured in the third season of HBO's Girls and the second season of the supernatural drama Lost Girl, a route by which a French independent release could reach American and Canadian television audiences.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} has its own story. The same upload says the official video was censored by YouTube and points viewers to Vimeo instead; that upload is not the video and runs a little longer than the album track, so this page plays the album track from the label's Topic upload.

## Artistic Context

Discogs lists an earlier Elektrisk Gønner vinyl on Platinum in 2010, with remixes by Bikini Machine and Bumblebeez, whose sleeve calls the project the dog that ate your birthday cake. Muzzart names Benjamin Gonner as its leader, working with four collaborators, and notes his earlier work with Oslo Telescopic and Lozninger. Compare the French dance-rock of {{ <track_link source="cassius-toop-toop.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Muzzart placed Yer Fire among Platinum's best releases, beside Bikini Machine and Rubin Steiner. Turn it up and decide for yourself what it wants.
