## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**'s **Dogs Are In This House** sits where her catalogue's labels overlap: Discogs files its single under deep house and techno, the album it comes from under electro, electroclash and synth-pop, and her own Bandcamp tags it with house, techno, disco and punk rock. Playful and groovy are the feelings chosen here; the groove is the one thing its label promised.

## Song Summary

The track is credited to Pilocka Krach alone, as composer, lyricist and producer, and runs five minutes six. It came out first on 10 March 2017 as a three-track single on the Berlin label Katermukke, with a remix by Sacha Robotti and one by Dirty Doering and Sascha Cawa. In April it reappeared on her double album Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, on her own label Greatest Hits International. No source describes its arrangement or sets out its lyric, so this page does not paraphrase either.

## Concepts in Focus

Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos is a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }} in its label's own words: a conceptually framed double album and a diary of her last two years on tour, with Mexico, Detroit and Berlin as fixed points. Each half has its own origin. The tracks of The Lost Amigos, this one among them, were written and produced in Tulum, in Guadalajara, at Submerge in Detroit and in Berlin-Marienfelde in 2015 and 2016. On the vinyl the track is the third of The Lost Amigos; on the CD it opens the album straight after Pilocka's Intro.

The single framed it with two of the club's readings. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by Sacha Robotti runs six minutes eleven, the one by Dirty Doering and Sascha Cawa five thirty-eight.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, published on Katermukke's channel on 30 March 2017, is a shorter cut of 3:44, made by lupeclupe.com and supported by Initiative Musik with federal funds.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick and grew up in Berlin-Mitte, and she runs her own label, Greatest Hits International. In 2016 a Musicboard Berlin grant took her to Detroit, where she connected with Underground Resistance. Her label calls her the charming queen of Berlin's post-techno underground and says she is Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos herself, her band and all at once.
