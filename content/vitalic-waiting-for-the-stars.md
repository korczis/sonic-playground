## Genre and Mood

**Vitalic**'s **Waiting for the Stars** is electropop with a disco heart: groovy, nostalgic by design and, in its singer's delivery, a little melancholic. The Electricity Club called it an unabashed nod to the French producer's favourite songs of the 1970s and 1980s; Beat heard a Bowie-like ennui inside a chic groove.

## Song Summary

The track was the first single from Voyager, Vitalic's fourth album, released as a single on 21 October 2016 ahead of the album on 20 January 2017, both on Clivage Music. It is sung by David Shaw, credited with his band as David Shaw and The Beat. Vitalic's own page for the album calls it an irresistible disco-pop hit and the album a cosmic space odyssey, the most disco of his four records. The version here, three minutes and fifty-three seconds, also appears on the French compilation Technikart 05 - Le printemps c'est maintenant.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review maps the song section by section, so this breakdown follows what critics described rather than a structure.

### Main Section

A Moroder-esque beat drives the track, in The Electricity Club's words, under squelchy, engaging electropop that is in places deliberately out of tune. The Playground heard a solid beat complemented by infectious electronics and called it intergalactic disco.

### Music Video

The official video runs about half a minute shorter than the album version. It was directed by David Hugonot Petit, with art direction by Charlie le Mindu.

## Concepts in Focus

David Shaw is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: the track is Vitalic's, and the "ft." credit names the voice that carries it. The Irish Times singled out his contribution to the album as well judged.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the other lead. Critics compared the beat to Giorgio Moroder's and the album's sound to retro, shimmering disco; the deliberate detuning The Electricity Club noticed is a choice, roughening the shine of a polished pop song.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} shows how a single is cut down for the screen: compare it with the full version to hear what the edit removes. For another French tribute to Moroder, see {{ <track_link source="daft-punk-giorgio-by-moroder.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Vitalic is Pascal Arbez-Nicolas from Dijon. His early records include raw electro such as {{ <track_link source="vitalic-cocaine.md" /> }}, and his albums OK Cowboy, Flashmob and Rave Age came before Voyager. He has said he changed his mind during the album's production and added beats.

## Impact and Reception

Beat called it Voyager's chart-friendly single, The Electricity Club its standout track. Listen first for the beat, then for the notes that are allowed to sit slightly off pitch.
