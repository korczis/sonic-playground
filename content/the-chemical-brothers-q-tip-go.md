## Genre and Mood

**The Chemical Brothers** and **Q-Tip** meet again on **"Go"**: electronic dance music with big beat's punch and a hip-hop voice in front, energetic from the start and built to tip into euphoria at the chorus.

## Song Summary

"Go" was the second single from Born in the Echoes, the duo's eighth album, and it premiered on Annie Mac's BBC Radio 1 show; the release followed on 4 May 2015. Tom Rowlands and Ed Simons wrote it with Kamaal Fareed, better known as Q-Tip; Mark Ralph plays bass and Yolanda Quartey sings backing vocals. Mixmag heard the duo's more uplifting side in it.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Beat

The Music Network describes "a driving, effortless beat held together by a powerful bass line". In the Singles Jukebox, Alfred Soto found nothing wrong with "the bass and Latin percussion", and Mo Kim praised its build-up of tension.

### Verses: Q-Tip

Brad Shoup hears Q-Tip as "a man half his age, twitchy and eager to get the party over", and adds: "The bass has his back."

### Chorus

For The Music Network, the bassline "explodes in the chorus with intergalactic synths". No source we found describes how the track opens or ends.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} brings a voice and a history. Q-Tip had already fronted "Galvanize", the duo's earlier top-ten single, and critics kept measuring "Go" against it: Treble's Adam Blyweiss heard him "showing off his Galvanize battle scars". Compare how the duo used Fatlip in {{ <track_link source="chemical_brothers_the_salmon_dance.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is what nearly every description returns to: it holds the beat together, carries the rapper, and is the part that bursts open when the synths arrive.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} is by Michel Gondry, who also made the train-window film of {{ <track_link source="chemical_brothers_star_guitar.md" /> }}. Seven women in vintage sci-fi outfits, which The Line of Best Fit called "sci-fi lederhosen", walk and twirl poles through the brutalist towers of the Front de Seine district in Paris. The video is shorter than the 4:20 album track.

## Artistic Context

Born in the Echoes came out on 17 July 2015 on Virgin EMI, and on Astralwerks in the US, and topped the UK albums chart; AllMusic heard a return to the duo's big beat roots. Guests across the album included St. Vincent, Beck and Cate Le Bon. Some critics found "Go" poppy and a departure from those roots.

## Impact and Reception

The Singles Jukebox panel was split, from "a very corny mash-up" to praise for its riffs. The single reached 46 in the UK in 2015 and was nominated for Best Dance Recording at the 58th Grammy Awards. It then lived on screen, in Need for Speed, Baywatch and The Bad Guys, until its use in the 2026 film Apex took it to number 4 in the UK and number 1 on the UK dance chart, the duo's first top-ten single since "Galvanize".
