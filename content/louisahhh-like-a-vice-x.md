**Louisahhh**, a Los Angeles-born, Paris-based producer, has carved a niche in the underground techno scene with her signature blend of raw intensity and poetic depth. Known for her collaborations with techno heavyweights and her independent work on the label **RAAR**, Louisahhh’s music is unapologetically visceral, addressing themes of empowerment, rebellion, and vulnerability. **Like A Vice** is a shining example of her ability to fuse gritty production with deeply emotional storytelling.

## Genre and Mood

**Louisahhh**’s track **Like A Vice** channels raw, industrial techno energy infused with a darkwave aesthetic. The song carries an intense, almost hypnotic rhythm that feels like an exploration of primal emotions. Its pulsating beats and brooding lyrics create an atmosphere of both tension and release, speaking to themes of desire, control, and personal liberation.

## Lyrical Walkthrough

The lyrics of "Like A Vice" weave a narrative of temptation, control, and eventual release, perfectly synchronized with the music's progression. Each line builds in intensity, paralleling the escalating tension of the track.

### Opening Lines: "It pulls me in, like a vice / Tight, relentless, can't think twice."

The opening verse introduces the central metaphor of the song—a vice—representing something that is both alluring and suffocating. Louisahhh's breathy, restrained delivery is matched by minimal percussion and a repetitive synth loop, creating a sense of quiet desperation. The subdued arrangement mirrors the internal struggle hinted at in the lyrics.

### Pre-Chorus: "Breaking skin, I’m bound and free / This is how it has to be."

As the lyrics explore the paradox of feeling simultaneously bound and liberated, the music begins to swell. Layers of distortion and industrial textures creep in, heightening the tension. The fragmented rhythm amplifies the emotional complexity, emphasizing the dichotomy between control and surrender.

### Chorus: "It’s like a vice, it grips, it bites / A beautiful pain in the dead of night."

The chorus explodes with raw energy, echoing the visceral imagery of the lyrics. The pounding beats mimic the tightening grip of the vice, while Louisahhh’s voice becomes more commanding, cutting through the cacophony of layered synths and percussion. Each beat feels like a tightening screw, driving the metaphor home.

### Second Verse: "Every step closer, I lose control / Every breath deeper, it claims my soul."

In the second verse, the lyrical imagery intensifies, delving into the consequences of succumbing to the vice. The music mirrors this descent with denser, glitchy textures and an unsettling progression in the synth line. The beats quicken slightly, evoking a sense of spiraling into chaos.

### Bridge: "Hold me tighter, don't let go / I need this more than you could know."

The bridge introduces a moment of vulnerability. The lyrics take on a pleading tone, and the music briefly strips back to allow for introspection. A haunting melody emerges, creating a stark contrast to the relentless energy of the previous sections. This pause feels like a moment of surrender before the final climax.

### Final Chorus: "It's like a vice, my darkest friend / It’ll break me, but I’ll ascend."

The final chorus is a triumphant blend of all the song’s elements, driving home the themes of destruction and transcendence. The music swells into a full-blown industrial assault, with Louisahhh’s vocals taking on a defiant tone. The relentless bassline and distorted synths reach their peak, embodying the cathartic release described in the lyrics.

### Outro: "Let it crush me, I am whole."

The outro brings the song full circle, with the lyrics suggesting a reconciliation with the vice. The music fades into a dark, ambient hum, leaving the listener with a sense of closure and introspection.
