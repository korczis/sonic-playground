## Genre and Mood

**Kelis** made **Millionaire** with **André 3000** of OutKast, who produced it and sings on it. It sits between R&B, funk and electronic pop: Wikipedia lists it as synth-funk, electronica and swing pop, and the mood is playful, groovy and confident, carried by a quick, spare track.

## Song Summary

Millionaire is a duet built by its guest. André 3000 produced it and is credited with vocals, drums, music programming and keyboards, with Kevin Kendricks adding keyboards and piano. It contains excerpts and samples from La Di Da Di, the 1985 record by Doug E. Fresh and Slick Rick, and both are credited as writers alongside Kelis and André. ## Musical Breakdown

### The Beat

The Houston Press's Craig D. Lindsey pictured André drifting in on a pink cloud, tinkering with his Casio and coming up with a tune of fast-paced R&B minimalism. That is the clearest description any source gives: quick, spare and keyboard-made.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} usually arrives with a verse. Here the guest is also the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}: André 3000 made the track at Stankonia Recording and DARP Studios in Atlanta and then sang on it. A feature then becomes a collaboration, with the guest's sound shaping the whole record rather than one part.

The song is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} across eras. Excerpts from a 1985 hip-hop record sit inside a 2003 R&B single, and the older record's writers, Doug E. Fresh and Slick Rick, share its credits.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Giuseppe Capotondi, features neither singer: children play the duo as their younger selves.

## Artistic Context

Tasty, Kelis's third album, came out on 5 December 2003 on Star Trak and Arista, with producers including the Neptunes, who made its lead single {{ <track_link source="kelis-milkshake.md" /> }}, as well as Raphael Saadiq, Dallas Austin and André 3000. Reviewers singled out his contribution: Exclaim! called it sophistofunk and Tinnitist electro-Prince soul. Millionaire became the album's third single on 18 October 2004, released in the UK by Virgin; restructuring at Arista meant it was never released in the US.

## Impact and Reception

Millionaire entered the UK Singles Chart at number three, Kelis's third consecutive top-five single from Tasty, and topped the UK Hip Hop/R&B chart. It reached number eight in Ireland, 11 in Finland, 23 in Australia and 27 in New Zealand, and was certified gold in the UK. The album drew a Metacritic score of 80.
