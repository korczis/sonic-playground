## Genre and Mood

**Basement Jaxx** have always made house music that refuses to behave, and **Galactical** is a short, colourful blast of it. House, dance and electronic pop collide in a mood that is playful, euphoric and cosmic, the soundtrack to a party that has accidentally drifted into orbit.

## Song Summary

The track is a celebration with a science-fiction costume on, a three-minute burst of bounce and shouted joy led by longtime Jaxx vocalist Vula. Felix Buxton and Simon Ratcliffe pile up bright synths, chunky drums and vocal hooks in the restless, maximal way that marks their best work. The memorable detail is its sense of fun: nothing here is cool or restrained, and that is the appeal.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The track launches quickly, with a burst of synth colour and a beat that is already bouncing before you have found your feet. There is no slow fade in; the party is already on.

### Verse 1

Vula's voice cuts through with personality and attitude, riding the groove rather than floating above it, and the production keeps throwing small sounds at the edges of the mix.

### Chorus

The hook is built for shouting along, big, simple and slightly absurd in the best Jaxx tradition, with the beat pushing hard underneath.

### Bridge

A brief breakdown thins the arrangement to a few elements, a moment of weightlessness before the rocket fires again.

### Outro

The last section piles everything back in and then cuts away almost abruptly, which suits a track this short and this hyperactive.

## Artistic Context

The Brixton duo Basement Jaxx have been one of British dance music's great eccentrics since the nineties, with hits such as "Where's Your Head At" and "Red Alert". "Galactical" is a bonus track on the special edition of *Junto*, their seventh studio album, released in 2014 on their own Atlantic Jaxx label. Its video, directed by Hadaya Turner, features the Japanese dance troupe Wrecking Crew Orchestra in glowing, neon-striped costumes, a perfect visual match for the music.

## Impact and Reception

*Junto* marked a return after several years away and showed that Basement Jaxx still knew how to throw a party. "Galactical" was a bonus, not a headline single, but its psychedelic video and pure bounce made it a favourite for fans who like the duo at their silliest. It is on this playground for anyone who needs three minutes of unfiltered joy. Turn it up, dim the lights and float.
