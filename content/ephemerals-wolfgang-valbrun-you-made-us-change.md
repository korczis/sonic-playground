## Genre and Mood

**Ephemerals** and their singer **Wolfgang Valbrun** made **You Made Us Change** as a soul record in the full sense: a band song carried by an R&B lead voice, soulful and a little nostalgic, because its album was made, in its singer's biography's words, as classic soul with a modern twist.

## Song Summary

The song was the highlight of the band's debut album, Nothin Is Easy, and the notes of its Bandcamp EP describe it as a soul record that blends Hillman Mondegreen's production with Valbrun's vocals. Mondegreen wrote it and leads the band; Valbrun, who was born and raised in New York State and built his career in Paris, was invited to be its lead singer. We do not quote the lyric here, and no source we found explains what, or who, made "us" change.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

You Made Us Change was the album's calling card. The album was recorded in seven days at a London studio and engineered by Joe Leach. No source we found walks through this song part by part, so this review does not invent its structure; listen for the voice first and for how the band frames it.

### Overall Vibe

In a 2020 interview the band described their music as a fusion of funk, soul and psychedelia, with Mondegreen favouring polychords and ambiguous tonality over plain major and minor moods. The interview was about the band's later work, so treat it as a lens for this song rather than a description of it.

## Concepts in Focus

You Made Us Change shows how a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can widen a song's world. Jalapeno's November 2014 EP put the original beside four reworks: the Dutch disco-house producer NSFW pitching the vocal down over a bass groove, AOTOA's atmospheric double-time dub, and a mid-tempo electronica remix and a dub by Antenna Happy.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Jan Hillman, is built around choreography by Natalie Adams.

## Artistic Context

Nothin Is Easy first came out on Mondegreen Soho through Bandcamp's pay-what-you-can service and was reissued by Jalapeno Records in autumn 2014. Before Ephemerals, Valbrun sang with the Parisian funk band Marvellous and toured with the French producer GUTS.

## Impact and Reception

Valbrun's biography calls the song the band's breakthrough: the French producer Kungs reworked it into a worldwide airplay hit, which took Valbrun to the Victoires de la Musique. That track, I Feel So Bad, released in 2016 and credited to Kungs featuring Ephemerals, reached No. 3 in France. Start with this original.
