## Genre and Mood

**Hayden James** made his name with **Something About You**, a house record that its maker wanted to be a song first. Complex heard a slower French house influence in it and called it funky but chill; Wonderland traced it to the house and disco of the French club scene. Groovy, sultry and catchy fit: a driving bassline, what Wonderland called seductive vocals, and a hook Dancing Astronaut found infectious.

## Song Summary

The track runs 3:43 and was written by Alexander Burnett, Jessica Higgs and Hayden Luby. According to Wikipedia, James said in an interview that he wrote it in two or three hours in 2014 but took weeks to produce it, and played it first to the band Rüfüs for their opinion. He sings the chorus himself; in the verses his voice is blended with that of George Maple, the stage name of Jessica Higgs. It made sense to him, he said, to write a song and not a dance track.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times, so this guide follows the parts the reviews and James himself describe.

### The Bassline

Wonderland heard a driving bassline, funky and catchy, and linked it to James Blake, Jamie xx and Daft Punk.

### The Verses

Two voices, James and George Maple, mixed so that they read as one.

### The Chorus

James's own voice on what Dancing Astronaut called a hooky topline, with playful lead work around it.

### The Texture

Complex heard the whole track through haze: reverb-heavy textures, disco-inspired and unhurried.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part a song is built to leave in your head. Here there are two: the sung chorus line and the bass under it. One way to read James's wish for a song rather than a dance track: something you could hum away from the club.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} carries the groove in house, and here it carries the funk too. Wonderland singled it out as funky and catchy and heard bouncy synths beside it.

{{ <concept_link name="reverb" /> }} puts sound in a space. The hazy, reverb-heavy textures Complex heard are why a track this rhythmic still feels soft at the edges; Complex said it simply feels like summer.

## Artistic Context

Hayden James is a Sydney producer, DJ and songwriter on Future Classic, active since 2013, when he released his debut EP; he went on to support Disclosure and ODESZA on tour. The song first appeared on 19 December 2014 on the label's compilation Teen Idols, then as a digital single on 22 May 2015, with a remix package following in August. His debut album, Between Us, came only in 2019.

## Impact and Reception

Dancing Astronaut called it a delicate and infectious take on where house was heading. The song reached number 46 in Australia and charted in France, Germany and Italy; it went gold in Australia, double platinum in New Zealand and silver in the UK, and was nominated for Best Dance Release at the 2015 ARIA Awards, losing to Rüfüs. Play it, then the ODESZA remix, and hear what survives.
