## Genre and Mood

**Joe Goddard** and **Mara Carlyle** made **She Burns** a deep house track that never raises its voice. Electronic Rumors heard it as lazy, dreamy and rolling, a vocal house track that gets under your skin; Passion of the Weiss heard disco-house grooves as effective as anything by Goddard's band, Hot Chip. Its haunting side owes as much to the video as to the music.

## Song Summary

The track splits its work cleanly: Goddard handles the beat, Carlyle the voice. Under her, The Line of Best Fit heard post-dub bass bursts and swirls; Electronic Rumors heard rumbling synth bass, sporadic electronic pulses, spitting 808 beats and otherworldly synth work. Over that ruminative track, as SPIN called it, Carlyle sings about what Promo News called regrettable recent events, a confession of not believing what she has done. The version on the Taking Over EP runs eight minutes; the video uses a cut of under four.

### Verse 1

### Main Section

Across the long track the bass rumbles and bursts in dub-like swirls, and the voice turns, in Electronic Rumors' words, uplifting at times and soulful at others.

## Concepts in Focus

She Burns credits Carlyle as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: the producer's track, the singer's song. The Line of Best Fit praised Goddard for complementing her emphatic tones.

The {{ <concept_link name="roland tr-808" /> }} sound that Electronic Rumors noticed sets the pulse, and the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} does the moving: a rumbling synth bass that The Line of Best Fit heard in bursts.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Soandsau (Sophie Diess and Jean-Christophe Saurel) and shot in a day in a Peckham flat, shows Carlyle as a corpse while two morticians inject her with embalming fluid and parts of her body briefly come back to life. SPIN wrote that the couplet becomes infinitely more haunting when a corpse sings it.

## Artistic Context

Joe Goddard, born in 1979, is a member of Hot Chip and co-founded the label Greco-Roman in 2007. She Burns was the first track heard from his Taking Over EP, released by Greco-Roman on 17 June 2013, with DFA issuing the single in the US; the EP also holds the nine-minute Bassline '12. Mara Carlyle, raised in Shropshire, sang for Plaid and on Matthew Herbert's Goodbye Swingtime before her own albums The Lovely and Floreat.

## Impact and Reception

Black Plastic found the EP's other tracks more interesting but granted She Burns its crystalline beauty. The song returned in remixes by Leo Invisible and Animalist, and Carlyle went on to work with Justice on Woman.
