## Genre and Mood

**Avicii** made **Addicted To You** a dance song that does not sound happy about its subject. Wikipedia files it as dance pop and soul; AllMusic called it seductive, with Audra Mae getting sultry, and Avicii's own team later wrote that her voice gave the song a sense of longing and desire, of being helplessly drawn to someone. Melancholic is this page's word for what Dancing Astronaut heard: a sorrowful story.

## Song Summary

On True, Avicii's 2013 debut album, Addicted to You is the fourth track and runs only 2:28. It was written by Tim Bergling, Arash Pournouri, Josh Krajcik and Mac Davis, the Texan songwriter behind Elvis Presley's In the Ghetto and A Little Less Conversation, who is also credited with guitar. Avicii and Pournouri produced it; MusicBrainz credits Bergling with the mix. The lead vocal is Audra Mae's, though the title credits Avicii alone.

## Musical Breakdown

No source maps the song section by section, so this page claims no tempo, key or structure. What critics did describe is the contrast at its heart. Dancing Astronaut heard a sorrowful story painted with instruments more often used for brighter sounds, and a vocal reminiscent of the singer's southern-bred roots. PopMatters heard Mac Davis's hand intensify the country influences that run through the whole album. Hot Press called it catchy electro lifted by Mae's smoky, soulful voice.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} question is plain here: Audra Mae sings the whole song, yet the single is credited to Avicii. Hot Press called that a shame. Compare {{ <track_link source="avicii-feeling-good.md" /> }}, where the two worked together again.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Sebastian Ringler and released on 14 February 2014, adds a story the lyric never tells: a 1930s crime romance inspired by Bonnie and Clyde, ending in a shooting. Some French channels blurred it for its violence.

The single also came out as a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} package in March 2014: Avicii's own Avicii by Avicii version and remixes by David Guetta, Sick Individuals, Albin Myers, Ashley Wallbridge and Bent Collective, running from 4:51 to 5:57, about twice the album length.

## Artistic Context

True, recorded between 2011 and 2013 and released by PRMD Music and Island in September 2013, was a deliberate turn: Avicii said he did not want to replicate Levels, and Wikipedia files the album under house and EDM beside country, bluegrass and soul. Audra Mae, born in Oklahoma City in 1984 and a great-great-niece of Judy Garland, sang three of its songs.

## Impact and Reception

Released to Australian radio on 27 November 2013 as the album's fourth single, it reached number one on the US Dance Club Songs chart, number five in Australia, six in France and Germany, and fourteen in the UK, with four-times platinum in Australia and platinum in the UK. Listen to the voice first, then to what the music around it does with so short a song.
