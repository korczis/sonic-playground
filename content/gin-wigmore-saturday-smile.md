## Genre and Mood

**Gin Wigmore**'s **Saturday Smile** is the quiet room on a loud album. Beat heard Gravel & Wine as gritty, attitude-filled blues rock; this is a ballad, filed under soul for the album it belongs to, and the mood is romantic and introspective rather than bold.

## Song Summary

Saturday Smile is the ninth track of Gravel & Wine and runs 3:22. Wigmore wrote it with Eric Rosse, who plays piano on it. She explained it to That Music Magazine in 2013: "I wrote that song for someone pretty special to me." She liked, she said, the definitiveness of knowing that one person is your everything, and the song sets that beside being unsure whether what you feel is love, lust or everything else that goes with it, the question people ask themselves when they try to stay in a long relationship.

### The Piano

Rosse's piano is the one instrument a source ties to this song alone: Wikipedia's personnel list credits him with piano and production on Saturday Smile and on no other track.

### The Voice

Wigmore's voice carries the song's double feeling, sure of the person, unsure of the name for it.

### Around Them

MusicBrainz also credits Butch Walker on guitar, with Jake Sinclair engineering and Claudius Mittendorfer mixing.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="ballad" /> }} in pop is a slow song that puts the melody and the singer in front. The New Zealand Herald's review heard two of them on Gravel & Wine and wrote that Wigmore is also capable of a stunning ballad, "which is exactly what If Only and Saturday Smile are there for." Compare the other one, {{ <track_link source="gin-wigmore-if-only.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} credit is worth a closer look here. Butch Walker produced the album, and the label's metadata on the song's official upload names him as its producer. Wikipedia's personnel list instead credits Rosse with production on this track. Both can be true: a co-writer can shape one song while the album's producer oversees the whole record.

## Artistic Context

Gravel & Wine, Wigmore's second album, was recorded in Santa Monica, California, in the second quarter of 2011, with Butch Walker producing and his band, The Black Widows. That Music Magazine reported that she spent time in Clarksdale, Mississippi, while writing it, which fed her interest in the blues. Universal released it in New Zealand on 7 November 2011 and in the United States on 2 April 2013.

## Impact and Reception

Saturday Smile was not a single; the album's singles were Black Sheep and Man Like That. Gravel & Wine went to number one in New Zealand, and the New Zealand Herald gave it four and a half stars, naming Saturday Smile as one of the two ballads that show Wigmore's range beyond the sly, dramatic songs. Hear it after {{ <track_link source="gin-wigmore-happy-ever-after.md" /> }}, the way the album sets it.
