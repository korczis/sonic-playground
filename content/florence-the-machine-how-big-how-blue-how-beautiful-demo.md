## Genre and Mood

**Florence + The Machine** recorded **How Big, How Blue, How Beautiful** twice, and this is the earlier version, the demo. It is indie pop with an alternative band's weight, and its mood is longing, romantic and uplifting at once: Fluxblog heard it as a love song about cities, and Los Angeles in particular, sung through the strain of a long-distance relationship.

## Song Summary

This page is the demo, not the album recording, {{ <track_link source="florence-the-machine-how-big-how-blue-how-beautiful.md" /> }}. The demo closes the deluxe edition of the album, released by Island Records on 29 May 2015, as the last of five bonus tracks, three of them demos. Welch wrote the song with Isabella Summers, the Machine of the band's name. Summers produced the demo and is credited with programming, drums, synths, bass, keys, Rhodes and percussion; Ali Helnwein plays strings and Alex Beitzke guitar. It runs 4:32, against 5:34 for the album version produced by Markus Dravs.

### Main Section

Fluxblog warned not to mistake this demo for anything minimal or skeletal: it is fully produced, with a string section. The difference, as that reviewer heard it, is scale. The album version has even more strings and goes bigger still, while here the song feels big without every element of the arrangement competing with Welch's voice.

### Outro

The album version ends on an extended string coda, which Fluxblog did not care for. No source describes the demo's ending; listen for how it closes a minute sooner.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="demo" /> }} is usually a quick sketch made to try a song out. This one breaks the rule: it sounds finished, which makes it a rare chance to hear a song produced twice, by the co-writer and then by the album's producer.

A {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }} adds to an album beyond its standard track list. The deluxe edition adds two new songs and three demos: Which Witch, Third Eye and this one. Placed last, it lets the listener end where the song began.

What separates the two versions is {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }}. The song and the singer are the same; the demo has Summers's programmed parts and Helnwein's strings, while the album version adds more strings, brass and a long coda. Listen for what each change does to the voice.

## Artistic Context

How Big, How Blue, How Beautiful was the band's third album, made after Welch's year-long break from music. She wanted something big but with gentleness and warmth, built more on live instruments, almost band-led, rather than a second Ceremonials. The album went to number one in the UK and the US and was shortlisted for the 2015 Mercury Prize.

## Impact and Reception

Fluxblog's Matthew Perpetua wrote about the demo when the album came out. He preferred this less ornate version, found the album mix oddly sterile, and called the song one of the best things Welch has written. Hear the demo, then the album version, and decide which sky looks bigger.
