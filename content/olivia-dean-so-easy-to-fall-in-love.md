## Genre and Mood

**Olivia Dean** sings **So Easy (To Fall In Love)** as a bossa nova with jazz pop manners: drums, cocktail piano, a trumpet and layered backing voices around her lead. The mood is confident, romantic and playful, a love song in which the singer is less worried about being chosen than sure she is worth choosing.

## Song Summary

Dean told Elle that she had seen friends go on first dates so anxious about being liked that they forgot to ask whether they liked the other person. The song turns that around: she presents herself as the perfect mix of Saturday night and the rest of your life, and she called it a pat on the back on the way out of the door to a first date. Critics heard her flirting and persuading, and excited about a new relationship. Dean wrote it with Amy Allen, John Ryan and Max Wolfgang; Zach Nahome, Ryan and Julian Bunetta produced it, with Allen as additional producer and Wolfgang as vocal producer.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song part by part, and this page gives no timings; what the reviews describe is its sound as a whole.

### Main Section

The Independent called it sweet-strummed and heard backing vocals layered like chiffon while cocktail piano notes shimmy around a trumpet indebted to Bacharach and David. Billboard's Thomas Smith heard lithe percussion under a vocal that seemed the album's most forthright attempt at reviving Astrud Gilberto.

### Vocal

The Skinny heard a wide range of vocal inflections, endearing, confident or flirtatious, against a playful assortment of instruments and backing vocals.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="phrasing" /> }} is where this song lives. Billboard's comparison with Astrud Gilberto is about a way of singing rather than a tune, and The Skinny's list of inflections, endearing, confident, flirtatious, describes one melody shaded three ways by how Dean delivers the words.

Behind her, {{ <concept_link name="vocal harmony" /> }} does quiet work: the backing voices the Independent heard layered like chiffon. Critics compared the style to the Supremes and to Carpenters-style middle-of-the-road pop, two sounds built on stacked voices.

The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is small: drums, piano, trumpet and voices. The player above is the official lyric video, which carries the album recording; the music video, directed by Jake Erland, was released separately on the same day.

## Artistic Context

Dean, born in 1999 in Tottenham and raised in east London, studied at the BRIT School and broke through with her 2023 debut, Messy. The Art of Loving, released by Capitol and Polydor on 26 September 2025, takes its title from Mickalene Thomas's exhibition All About Love, itself a response to bell hooks. So Easy was its fourth single, released with the album.

## Impact and Reception

The song climbed to number two on the UK Singles Chart, where it spent two weeks behind Dean's own duet with Sam Fender, Rein Me In, and reached number five on the Billboard Hot 100; it has been certified platinum in both countries. Rolling Stone heard the glamour of Diana Ross in it. Put it on before you go out, as Dean intended.
