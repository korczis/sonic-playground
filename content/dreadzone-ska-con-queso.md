## Genre and Mood

**Dreadzone** put **Ska Con Queso** in the middle of their 2005 album Once Upon a Time: track five of twelve, seven minutes long, and no singer. Discogs files the album under breakbeat, dub and breaks, and the Belgian reggae site Rebel Base counted this track among the two instrumentals that keep the ambient flavour and film samples of the band's first records. Atmospheric and groovy are the feelings that description suggests; no review walks through the track itself, so this page stays close to what is documented.

## Song Summary

The title is Spanish for ska with cheese, and it names the Jamaican dance music that came before rocksteady and reggae. Whether the track borrows ska's rhythm, no source says, and this review does not claim it. Discogs lists it at 7:03 on the original Functional Breaks CD; the album credits production, arrangement and electronics to Greg Dread and Ben Balafonic, with Balafonic mixing. The guitarist Steve Roberts is credited on four other tracks, not this one, and the album's vocalists, Earl Sixteen and MC Spee, belong to its vocal songs.

### The Atmosphere

Rebel Base heard the ambient flavour of the band's earlier albums surviving only in the instrumentals. Listen for sound that sets a mood rather than a melody to sing.

### The Samples

The same review heard film samples kept in these tracks. Which films, no source says; listen for voices or sounds that seem to come from somewhere else.

### The Rhythm

The album's styles are breakbeat and dub: broken drums set inside the space and low end of the Jamaican mixing desk.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }} is the Jamaican art of remaking a record at the mixing desk, stripping it toward drums and bass and opening space with echo. Discogs places Once Upon a Time in dub, and an instrumental is where that space can stay open for seven minutes.

{{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} swaps reggae's drum patterns for broken, syncopated drums from funk and dance music. It is the other half of the album's classification.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} lifts sound from other recordings; here the review heard film samples. Compare how a sample carries a sung refrain in {{ <track_link source="dreadzone-iron-shirt.md" /> }}, the track just before.

## Artistic Context

Dreadzone formed in London in 1993 around Greg Roberts, the former Big Audio Dynamite drummer billed as Greg Dread; their one UK Top 40 hit, Little Britain, came in 1996. Once Upon a Time, their fifth studio album, came out in 2005 on Functional; Rebel Base heard a rougher sound than before, shaped by the members' work as DJs on the English club circuit. The band's own Dubwiser Records reissued the album in 2020.

## Impact and Reception

Ska Con Queso was never a single and has no recorded chart life. Its value, for the one critic who named it, is continuity: an instrumental that keeps the older Dreadzone audible on a more vocal album.
