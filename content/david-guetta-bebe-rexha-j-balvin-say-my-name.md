## Genre and Mood

**David Guetta**, **Bebe Rexha** and **J Balvin**'s **Say My Name** is dance-pop with a Latin accent: Billboard called it a Latin-inspired dance-pop beat, and Wikipedia adds reggaeton and moombahton to its genres. The mood is bold and sultry, a love song that is really a test of loyalty, delivered with heat rather than hurt.

## Song Summary

Rexha sings to a lover she suspects of being unfaithful and, in the chorus, demands that he prove it: say my name, if you love me. Balvin raps his verse on the same theme in Spanish. The title and the doubt recall Destiny's Child's 1999 hit of the same name, which Billboard heard the song nodding to. Guetta produced it with Giorgio Tuinfort and Boaz van de Beatz, and described the result as pop, reggaeton and electronic parts at once, and very minimal.

## Musical Breakdown

No source follows the song section by section or gives its order, so this breakdown stays with what the credits, Songfacts and the producer say.

### Chorus

The title line does the work: Rexha's short demand, say my name, if you love me, repeated. This is the part built to stay in your head; Mitch Allan is credited with producing the vocals.

### Main Section

Two voices take turns on one subject: Rexha's suspicion in English and Balvin's verse, rapped in Spanish. Under them, listen for how little plays. Guetta told Billboard that making a record from a very small number of elements was challenging, and the credits list piano by Tuinfort, bass by Quincell Adolphin and additional keyboards by Matthew Holmes and Philip Leigh.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} here is a sentence anyone can sing back, and it is also the title.

Three names share the credit. Rexha had been credited on Guetta's 2014 hit Hey Mama while still barely known, and said it was nice to start again with him; this was his first collaboration with Balvin. Each {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} brings an audience, and here one brings a language. Songfacts reports that Guetta and Balvin first recorded the song with Demi Lovato.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Hannah Lux Davis, puts the three on a neon-lit dancefloor under black light, cut with jungle flowers and animals.

## Artistic Context

Say My Name appeared on Guetta's album 7 in September 2018 and was released as its seventh single on 26 October 2018. Compare another DJ-producer's single sung by a guest, {{ <track_link source="robin-schulz-francesco-yates-sugar.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

It reached number two on Romania's Airplay 100 and is certified Diamond in France and Gold or higher in fourteen more countries. Its lesson is in the restraint Guetta described: very few sounds, one line, three voices. Play it and count what you hear.
