## Genre and Mood

**Me Succeeds** wrote **Rongorongo**, and Arp Aubert remade it as **Rongorongo (Arp Aubert Remix)**: electronic music for the club, a techno remix of a song by a band that calls itself an experimental pop duo. The record store Oye Records, selling the remix album, described this version as a driving techno track. The feelings follow from that description and from Arp Aubert's own sound, which the band's Bandcamp page calls melancholic: energy in the beat, melancholy around it. No official upload of the remix is on YouTube, so this page carries no video.

## Song Summary

Rongorongo is the title track of Me Succeeds' album of December 2011, which the band released on its own imprint; the original runs a little over four minutes. Less than two years later the Hamburg duo returned on Ki Records with Rongorongo Remixed, a whole album of remixes by eleven producers, and Arp Aubert's version of the title song opens it as side A, track one of the double vinyl. At 7:15 it is close to twice the length of the original. Oye Records named the band's lead singer, Mohna, in the same breath as this remix.

### The Long Form

What can be said is arithmetic and genre. Seven minutes and a quarter, against the original's four, is the length of a track made for DJs, with room to bring a beat in slowly and take it out again. Listen for what fills the extra time, and for how much of Mohna's voice is kept.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version made by reworking the parts of a recording. Here the change is in genre as well as length: a pop duo's song turned, in Oye Records' word, into driving techno. Comparing the two shows how much of a song lives in its rhythm.

A remix album sits close to a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}: it gathers many producers' work around one source, here the songs of a single album. Rongorongo Remixed gives the title song two readings, Arp Aubert's on the first side and Christian Löffler's at the start of the second record, followed by remixes of Seventeen, Faxekondi, That's Why I Cast a Shadow, Books and Our Charm.

## Artistic Context

Me Succeeds are from Hamburg; their Bandcamp page lists releases from The Rooms We Left Behind in 2005 through Rongorongo in 2011 and Into Randomness in 2016. Arp Aubert is Stephan Lorenz, founder of the Mirau label, whom the band's page calls its regular remix supplier; he and the band had traded remixes before, and he remixed their Projection of Light on Water as late as 2026. Ki Records released the remix album in the summer of 2013, MusicBrainz dating the vinyl to 29 July and the CD following in September.

## Impact and Reception

No chart, review or award is recorded for this remix, and with no official video, the way in is through the remix album itself. Find Rongorongo Remixed, put the original next to it, and listen to what Arp Aubert does with the extra three minutes.
