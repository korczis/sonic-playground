## Genre and Mood

**AYYBO**'s **BASS GLIDE** is tech house at its most compact: a house single of about two and a half minutes that Apple Music files under electronic and Gray Area counts among his releases emphasising dark house grooves. Dark and groovy is the brief, and the title says where the weight sits.

## Song Summary

There is no written review of BASS GLIDE, so this page stays close to what is documented. The single came out on 22 February 2023 on GDB Records, the label AYYBO runs himself, with an extended mix released the same day. The credits name one composer, Aaron Bonnema, which is AYYBO's own name. The visualizer on his channel uses artwork by Kristopher Lenz. AYYBO describes the sound he was making at the time as bassline-heavy tech house, mysterious-sounding, in his words a good evil.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the low, repeated figure that carries a house track's movement, and in tech house it often matters more than any melody. Heavy basslines run through AYYBO's productions, and BASS GLIDE puts the idea in its title, which is a clear invitation to listen low.

The {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is the version for DJs: longer, with room to blend into and out of the next record. Comparing it with the single shows how one track exists in two shapes, one for streaming and one for the booth, as with the club mix of {{ <track_link source="camelphat-elderbrook-cola-club-mix.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the feel that makes a body move, made of timing and weight rather than notes. Gray Area's phrase for this track, a dark house groove, names the feel rather than any part.

## Artistic Context

AYYBO is Aaron Bonnema, a house producer and DJ from Anaheim, California. Car rides with his father, playing Outkast's B.O.B. and the Chemical Brothers, got him into house music; he learned to DJ on a $150 deck with YouTube tutorials, studied film, and sold his filming equipment to pay for a studio. He signed to Zeds Dead's Deadbeats label in 2018 under an earlier alias, launched AYYBO in spring 2021 with tech-house edits, and uses TikTok as a sounding board, posting snippets of unreleased music for his followers to judge.
