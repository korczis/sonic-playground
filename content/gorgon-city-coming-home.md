## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** open their 2014 debut album Sirens with **Coming Home**, a house song sung by Maverick Sabre. It sits in deep house by its pulse but not by its usual promises: Complex heard no big build and no heavy drop, only a melancholy piano line and light percussion. Melancholic and longing are the feelings, and Skiddle called the result muted house.

## Song Summary

Coming Home runs 4:06 and is the first of the album's tracks. Its writers are Matt Robson-Scott and Kye Gibbon, the two members of Gorgon City, with Jonny Coffer and Michael Stafford, the singer known as Maverick Sabre; Gorgon City and Coffer produced it, and Mark "Spike" Stent mixed it. Six backing singers stand behind the lead, with Patsy McKay credited as vocal arranger. Complex's James Keith called it an emotive song that acknowledges the success of all three musicians, and heard in it gratitude to fans and a longing for home.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times or follows the track section by section, so this guide follows what three reviews heard.

### The Piano

Complex put a melancholy piano line at the centre, with only light percussion around it.

### The Groove

mxdwn's Jeremy Rellosa heard what he took as typical of much of the album: multi-layered throbs of bass, punchy keys and plenty of clapping, a slap-clap that works like a more distinct snare.

### The Voice

Skiddle's Jimmy Coultas called Maverick Sabre's rasp unmistakable; this is where it first enters the album.

### No Drop

Where a dance record would build and release, Coming Home stays level, by Complex's account.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} usually visits once. On Sirens, Skiddle noted, Maverick Sabre is the only singer trusted with more than one appearance: he opens the album here and closes it on {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-hard-on-me.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is where a house track keeps moving when its melody is still. The layered bass mxdwn heard is what carries this one forward under a piano line that holds back.

The {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} is dance music's release after a build-up. Its absence is the point here: Complex heard a song that never builds to one, a restraint that suits the gratitude it heard in the lyric.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City are Matt "RackNRuin" Robson-Scott and Kye "Foamo" Gibbon, two producers who had built their own reputations before working together. By 2014 they had a summer hit with Ready for Your Love, featuring MNEK. Maverick Sabre had his own chart hit in 2011 with Let Me Go. Sirens came out on 6 October 2014 on Virgin EMI and Black Butter.

## Impact and Reception

Coming Home was not among the album's singles, but Complex premiered a video for it in September 2014, shot in a home-video style without computer effects or polished studio performance. Sirens reached number 10 on the UK Albums Chart, topped the UK Dance Albums Chart and went gold. Skiddle heard the whole album as the sound 2014's dance mainstream would be remembered by. Start the album here, then skip to its end for Hard On Me.
