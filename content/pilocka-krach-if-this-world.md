## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** opens her 2017 double album with **"If This World"**, electronic music that Discogs files under electroclash, electro and synth-pop and that her label sums up as pure electro post-rave simplicity. The feelings are playful and gritty, the album's own: Der Vinylist called it funky, provocative, dirty and funny.

## Song Summary

If This World is the first of nine tracks on Sugar Cane, the half of the album named after the artist's alter ego, and it runs 4:31. Her Bandcamp credits say every Sugar Cane track was written and produced by Pilocka Krach in Biesenthal, Sternhagen Gut and Bohemia in 2014 and 2015; Qobuz lists her as composer and lyricist. No source we found quotes or explains its lyric, describes its arrangement or says how it ends, so this page does not guess at them. What the sources do give is its place in a story.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes this track on its own. Here is what is documented about its place and its materials.

### The Opening

On the vinyl edition it is A1 of the Sugar Cane record, the very first track; on the CD edition, which sequences the album differently, it comes eleventh of twelve.

### The Materials

Her label says each sound on the album is either a raw sampling cut or deep, growling synthesis, and Der Vinylist hears the result as punchy and dry.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }} is held together by a story or idea rather than a set of singles. Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos is framed, her label says, as a diary of two years on tour, with Mexico, Detroit and Berlin as fixed points. Der Vinylist reads it as a narrative: at the start Sugar Cane is at the end of her tether, playing cards against death; later the Lost Amigos turn the tide and bring her back to life. That makes the opening track the low point the story starts from.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" text="Sampling" /> }} is one pole of her sound: recorded fragments cut and reused, raw rather than polished. The other is the {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }}, used for deep, growling tones. Compare how both work in {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach_la_luna-y-el-sol.md" /> }}, from the same half.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach is a Berlin artist, born in Köpenick and a veteran of the club Bar25, who releases her music on her own label, Greatest Hits International. Her label calls her the charming queen of Berlin's post-techno underground. Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos came out in April 2017 as a double vinyl album with a CD, two years after her Best Of; the Sugar Cane half was also released on its own digitally.

## Impact and Reception

Der Vinylist's Enwie Kej wrote that Pilocka makes noise, but fine techno noise, picked Clarita's Song as the album's earworm and ended with one instruction: listen to it. No review singles out If This World. Start the album where she starts it.
