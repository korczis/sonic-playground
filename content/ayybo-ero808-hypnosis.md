## Genre and Mood

**AYYBO** and **ero808** made **HYPNOSIS** the breakout of AYYBO's house career, a catchy, groovy two-and-a-half-minute single from the side of tech house that likes to play. Gray Area places AYYBO in the bass-heavy and tech varieties of house; HYPNOSIS shows the lighter, more playful end of it, led by a voice and a saxophone rather than by darkness.

## Song Summary

The track was built backwards. ero808, a singer-songwriter, sent AYYBO his raw vocals, and AYYBO wrote the beat from that topline, following ero808's catchy hook with a swinging saxophone melody, as Gray Area tells it. The lyric is a few lines about a woman dancing slowly and leaning in, with the singer playing along and admitting she has him in a hypnosis. It came out in July 2022; the official audio runs 2:39, and an extended mix exists for DJs.

## Musical Breakdown

The lyric sheet AYYBO posted with the official audio labels its parts, and this walk-through follows it; no source describes the beat underneath bar by bar.

### Intro

ero808 opens alone with the lines the verses will repeat, setting the scene before the groove takes over.

### Verse

The verse restates those lines with small changes, plus ad-libs in Spanish, 'ay dale, mami'.

### Pre-Chorus and Chorus

The title line comes four times in a row, and the chorus answers it with a single word, 'dale', around the hook.

### The Saxophone

The saxophone melody follows the vocal hook, a second hook without words; AYYBO later joked that it was what summoned Pitbull.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} usually arrives once a song is written. Here it came first: ero808's vocal topline existed before the beat, and everything else was arranged to serve it.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can become a different song. Pitbull's producer Sermstyle asked for the instrumental; Pitbull wrote a hook over it the same day, and in January 2023 the result came out as Right or Wrong (Hypnosis), credited to Pitbull, AYYBO and ero808.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Tom Bender, takes the title literally: a stage hypnotist, John Brown, puts people under, and they then dance to the track.

## Artistic Context

AYYBO is Aaron Bonnema, a house producer and DJ from Anaheim, California, who studied film, sold his camera gear to pay for a studio and launched the AYYBO alias in spring 2021 with tech house edits. In 2022 he released an original single almost every month; HYPNOSIS was the one that broke through. A version of the video on Helix Records' channel credits the release to Helix Records and Funkshway.

## Impact and Reception

DJs including Pete Tong and MistaJam played it, Gray Area reported over eleven million Spotify plays and half a million TikTok views, and then came the Pitbull version. For a track that began as a raw vocal, that is quite a journey. Listen for the saxophone answering the voice.
