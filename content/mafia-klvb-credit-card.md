## Genre and Mood

**Mafia Klvb**'s **Credit Card** comes from the underground end of hip-hop and electronic music; the act tag their debut as future bass, future beats and lifestyle rap. Its mood is edgy, gritty and provocative, a track about money and excess delivered from inside a character.

## About the Artist

**Mafia Klvb** present themselves as R!CH BU$¥ B!TCH and DJ KA$H, an act from Naples with a motto, The Ultimate Lifestyle, and their self-titled debut, released on 25 February 2022, is pitched as an insight into their underground lifestyle. Behind the persona the credits are precise: every track was written by Guadalupe Carranza del Castillo and the Berlin-based artist Pilocka Krach, produced by Felix Zoepf, with Pilocka Krach co-producing two of the five tracks, and the album came out under the name Greatest Hits International with distribution by Morr Music.

## Song Summary

"Credit Card" closes the five-track album. It is credited to Mafia Klvb with Pilocka Krach and Mommo G, and it takes the most ordinary object in a wallet and turns it into a symbol of a lifestyle: spending, excess and the attitude that comes with them. The album as a whole describes itself as a postcard-like middle finger from the realm of revenge and resistance, and the song carries that mix of swagger and defiance.

## Sound Breakdown

### Intro: Setting the Tone

The track sets its tone from the start, Felix Zoepf and Pilocka Krach's production framing the voices before they take over.

### Verse One: The Hard Truth

The verse builds the character's world, the underground lifestyle the album promises.

### Chorus: The Catchy Hook

The title phrase anchors the song, the credit card returning as its central image.

### Verse Two: Diving Deeper

Pilocka Krach and Mommo G are credited alongside Mafia Klvb, so the song is shared between the persona and its guests.

### Bridge: Moment of Reflection

Billie Mendrix is credited on guitar for this track, a played instrument inside an electronic production.

### Final Chorus: The Climax

The song ends where it began, on its title and its attitude, the last word of a short debut.

## Concepts in Focus

Mafia Klvb are built on a {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }}. The names, the motto and the talk of an underground lifestyle are part of the work, just as a mask is part of Claptone or cartoons are part of Gorillaz. The credits tell a second story, of a songwriting and production team, and the gap between the character and the people behind it is part of what makes the project interesting.

The song also depends on its {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credits. It is billed as Mafia Klvb with Pilocka Krach and Mommo G, so a co-writer and co-producer steps in front of the microphone too. Reading credits like these is a good habit: they show who made a track, not just whose name is on the cover.

## Overall Vibe

"Credit Card" is **edgy** and **gritty**, a small, provocative piece of underground rap that works best heard as the last chapter of the Mafia Klvb debut. Play the five tracks in order and the persona, and the joke inside it, come into focus.
