## Genre and Mood

**DJ Clipps** released **Sweet Dreams** in its Deep Summer Mix in 2017 with three guests, Bthelick, 9ts and Tasia. Apple Music files the single under dance. No published review describes it, so its two feelings rest on its own titles: dreamy for Sweet Dreams, carefree for a mix named after a deep summer. Read both as context, not as a listener's account.

## Song Summary

The single came out on 15 September 2017 as one track of five minutes and thirty-four seconds, distributed by DistroKid with the copyright line Mixrupt Media Collabs. The art track that YouTube generated from it lists DJ Clipps, Tasia, Bthelick and 9ts, then Ble3azy and Johnny Jay, and three legal names, Benjamin Christopher Blease, Dinah Natasha Flannigan and Emaechi John Ekubia, without saying who did what. No lyrics are published, so this review does not describe the words.

The title will make many listeners think of Eurythmics. The credits, however, name neither Annie Lennox nor Dave Stewart, so the release does not present itself as a cover, and no source says whether it borrows from the 1983 song.

## Artistic Context

Apple Music lists dozens of releases by DJ Clipps, Bthelick and 9Ts in changing combinations from May 2017 onwards, mostly under the names Mixrupt Collabs and Mixrupt Music, filed under house, dance, trance and other genres, and later a run of singles labelled Clipps Edit. Several carry the titles of well-known songs, such as In the Air Tonight and Summertime Sadness.
