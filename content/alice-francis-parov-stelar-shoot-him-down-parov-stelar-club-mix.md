## Genre and Mood

**Alice Francis** wrote **Shoot Him Down** as a 1920s-styled revenge song; **Parov Stelar**'s club mix takes it to the dance floor. Discogs files this release under house and electro swing, the album under jazz, swing and electro. The feelings named here, defiant and theatrical, come from the lyric and Francis's flapper persona; no review describes how the remix itself sounds.

## Song Summary

The song is a woman's answer to a man who called her his baby girl while in bed with someone else: by the chorus she has nothing left but heartache and a gun. Francis wrote it with the brothers Johann and Robert Niegl for her debut album, St. James Ballroom. The club mix opens Shoot Him Down (The Parov Stelar Versions), four tracks released on 25 May 2012 by Etage Noir Recordings, the Austrian producer's own label: his club and radio versions, then a club and a radio version by Milk & Sugar. The YouTube release credits the production to Marcus Füreder, Parov Stelar's real name.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version built from an existing recording's parts, often by another producer. Here that producer was already the best-known name in electro swing: Parov Stelar, from Linz, whom Wikipedia counts among the style's pioneers. Play it next to {{ <track_link source="alice-francis-shoot-him-down.md" /> }}, the album version, and notice that Discogs shelves them differently: swing for the album, house for the EP.

The EP also shows two kinds of version side by side. The club version is the {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} of the pair, the long cut made for DJs who need room to blend one record into the next. The radio version is a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}, cut to under three minutes for airplay. One remix in two lengths serves two audiences, the club and the radio.

## Artistic Context

Alice Francis was born in Timișoara, Romania, lives in Cologne and performs in the costume and manner of a 1920s flapper. She says she got to know Parov Stelar in 2012 and credits him with introducing her band to the scene; they also played some of his tour dates as support. Parov Stelar, born Marcus Füreder in 1974, founded Etage Noir in 2003 and released his double album The Princess the same year as this remix. The Parov Stelar Versions came out more than three months before St. James Ballroom itself, which followed on 7 September 2012.

## Impact and Reception

In Francis's own words, Parov Stelar's remix of Shoot Him Down pretty much went through the roof. Electro Swing Thing lists the song among her hits and touring with Parov Stelar among her milestones.
