## Genre and Mood

**Dada Life** are known for electro house with a sense of humour, and **Arrive Beautiful Leave Ugly** is electro house too, energetic, intense and bold. But it is an instrumental, and the critics who heard it in October 2012 could not agree whether it was the duo turning things down or turning them up.

## Song Summary

The track comes from The Rules of Dada, the Stockholm duo's second album, released on So Much Dada in October 2012. Your EDM noted that it was the first previously unreleased track in the album's running order, placed straight after two songs fans already knew. There are no words: Elektro Daily heard it resting on a sharp synth melody, and Your EDM heard a deep sawtooth bassline as its backbone.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A gliding saw lead plays the melody from the start, with intermittent bass hits under it, and the track drops quickly into its groove.

### Main Groove

The bassline drives everything here, with the hard lead on top of it all the way. Hi-hats and claps fill out the beat, and a few breaks use chord stabs to point up the melody.

### Breakdown

In the main breakdown the lead is slowly filtered back in, while bass hits and chord stabs add energy.

### Build-up and Return

A large build-up leads back into the main groove, and the bassline drives on to the end.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the engine. In a track with a vocal, the bass usually supports a singer; here it does the work a singer would do, and Your EDM described the sawtooth bass, a bright, buzzing synth wave, as the piece everything else hangs on.

The {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} and the {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} are the shape of the track: the groove is pulled away, tension is rebuilt, and the return lands harder for the gap. The way the lead comes back is a {{ <concept_link name="filter sweep" /> }}: the sound starts muffled and opens up as the filter lets more of its upper frequencies through, so you hear the melody arrive rather than switch on.

## Artistic Context

Olle Cornéer and Stefan Engblom formed Dada Life in 2006 and named it in homage to the Dada art movement, and humour runs through their shows. They also release audio plugins, most famously the saturator Sausage Fattener. The Rules of Dada reached the lower part of the Billboard 200 and the top 20 of the UK dance album chart. Compare {{ <track_link source="aronchupa-little-sis-nora-i-m-an-albatraoz.md" /> }}, Swedish electro house from the same years with its joke up front.

## Impact and Reception

Opinion split. Your EDM called the track explosive but more understated than the duo's usual maximal style; Magnetic Magazine called it huge from start to finish; Dancing Astronaut grouped it with Boing Clash Boom as tracks that change things up but felt too hard or disconnected from the rest. Days after release, Magnetic's account of the album launch at Avalon Hollywood took the title as its headline: its writer arrived beautiful and left ugly, soaked in champagne. Turn it up and decide which reviewer was right.
