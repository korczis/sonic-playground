## Genre and Mood

**AYYBO** and **Preme** made **DRENCH** for the darker end of tech house: a heavy, rolling bassline, crisp drums and a rap vocal riding on top. It is groovy and dark at once; Mixmag praised the way it merges darkness and funk while keeping the energy high. AYYBO himself has called his sound a good evil.

## Song Summary

DRENCH is a dance record built around a borrowed voice. AYYBO took a topline from Preme's earlier song Pause Play and flipped it into a funk-infused roller, as Mixmag put it, so a hip-hop vocal becomes the hook of a club track. The arrangement is lean: bass, drums and Preme's punchy lines. The detail that stays with you is the tension at the start, when the bass is there but held back, and the release when it finally drops.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The track opens on heavy bass passed through a filter, so it sounds muffled and coiled. Crisp beats and Preme's vocal sit over the low end while the tension builds.

### Drop

When the bass drops, the full track arrives. The drum programming stays simple, which is the point: Mixmag heard it as simple yet highly effective, with nothing in the way of the groove.

### Breakdown

In the breakdown Preme's lyrics take centre stage. Mixmag reported that this was the part already drawing strong reactions on dance floors.

### Outro

No source describes how the track ends; listen for how the single closes in a little over three minutes. An extended mix exists for DJs.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the engine here, as in most of AYYBO's work; Gray Area describes his sound as tech house anthems with enveloping basslines.

That is the job of the {{ <concept_link name="filter sweep" /> }} in the intro. Closing a filter cuts the bright upper part of a sound and leaves a dull thump; opening it lets the full tone back in. Holding the bass behind a filter makes the listener wait, so the drop lands harder.

The {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} is the opposite move: the beat steps back and Preme steps forward. Crediting him as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is more than courtesy, because his voice is what the track is organised around. Compare the bass-first approach of {{ <track_link source="ayybo-bass-glide.md" /> }}, released the same year.

## Artistic Context

AYYBO is Aaron Bonnema, an Anaheim-based producer who came to house through the Chemical Brothers, taught himself to DJ, sold his filming equipment to build a studio and signed with Zeds Dead's Deadbeats label in 2018. DRENCH came out in May 2023 on Funkshway, a Los Angeles house label and events brand, as the follow-up to his first track there, HYPNOSIS. Preme, formerly known as P Reign, is a Toronto rapper and producer active in hip-hop since 2008, with tracks alongside Drake, A$AP Rocky, Meek Mill and French Montana.

## Impact and Reception

Mixmag welcomed it as a funk-infused roller from a tech house prodigy, and Gray Area noted that AYYBO took his decks underwater for the video. Put it on loud and wait for the bass to open.
