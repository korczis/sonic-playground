## Genre and Mood

**Emika**'s **Miracles Prelude** is a short piece of electronic music between trip-hop and the experimental. The feelings named here come from the reviews: Spectrum Culture heard a dissonant trip-hop duet, and Ibiza Spotlight, writing about Miracles, a haunting duet of Emika with her own lowered voice, on an album that same review heard as a dark and brooding world.

## Song Summary

At 1:49 on Emika's Bandcamp, Miracles Prelude is the third track of DREI and leads into the fourth, Miracles. Glamglare noted that Miracles is split in two parts and that the first includes a male voice Emika duets with; Ibiza Spotlight wrote that in Miracles she pitches her voice down for a duet with herself. Spectrum Culture heard in the prelude Emika pointing to freedom as a source of unexpected rewards. A fan on her Bandcamp page called it the darker prelude to Miracles. Emika wrote the words and the music, and she produced and mixed the album herself.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the prelude bar by bar, and in under two minutes there is little room for sections. The reviews give its elements.

### Intro

The album has just come out of My Heart Bleeds Melody. Listen to how quickly the prelude changes the mood.

### Main Section

The duet is the heart of it: two voices, or one voice split in two, in what Spectrum Culture called dissonance. Listen to whether the lower voice answers the higher one or sings with it.

### Outro

The prelude does not resolve on its own; it hands over to Miracles. Play the two tracks together, as the album does.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="interlude" /> }} is a short piece between an album's main songs, and a prelude is one that announces what comes next. Here the announcement is literal: the next track carries the same name. Splitting Miracles in two lets Emika set up the mood before the song arrives.

The prelude also exists as an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: MusicBrainz lists Miracles Prelude (instrumental) on Drei (Instrumentals & A Capellas), released in December 2017. Without the voices, what is left is the setting the duet was sung over.

## Artistic Context

Emika is Ema Jolly, the Berlin-based producer and singer born in Milton Keynes. She announced DREI in March 2015 with a statement that it is ultimately about freedom and its limits, the madness of being alone and the shock of a miracle, and FACT reported that she wrote it in a single two-week period and produced and mixed it herself. It came out on her own label, Emika Records, on 4 May 2015, opening with {{ <track_link source="emika-battles.md" /> }}; the prelude leads into {{ <track_link source="emika-miracles.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

A prelude is rarely reviewed on its own, and only Spectrum Culture gave this one a sentence. It is here because the owner played it often enough for it to make a yearbook. Listen to it with the lights low, and let it run straight into Miracles.
