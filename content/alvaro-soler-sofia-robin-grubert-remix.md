## Genre and Mood

**Alvaro Soler**'s **Sofia - Robin Grubert Remix** is one of three remixes released with the 2016 single. The original is filed under folk pop and Latin pop; this version lays a second producer's reading over it. The mood is upbeat on the surface and bittersweet underneath, because the sunny summer single is, in its Spanish words, about a love that has ended.

## Song Summary

Sofia is a breakup song that decides to keep walking. The Italian weekly Sorrisi read its lyric as a story of defeat after a love affair, torn between a feeling that lingers and a person who is no longer there; the video sets that against dancers and musicians in the sun. Soler wrote it with Simon Triebel, Ali Zuckowski, Jakke Erixson and Nadir Khayat, better known as RedOne, who produced it with T.I. Jakke. Grubert's remix runs 3:21, nine seconds shorter than the 3:30 single.

### The Song Underneath

Listen first for what survives from the original: Soler's voice, the Spanish lyric about letting go, and the name in the title, sung to someone who has gone.

### The Remix

Grubert's version sits on the remix release beside the B-Case Remix (3:16) and the Oovee Remix (3:44): three producers, three readings of the same hit, each close to radio length.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} takes the parts of a finished recording and rebuilds them into a new track, usually for a different audience or setting. That is why this page carries the original artist's name and the remixer's credit, and why it is a recording in its own right rather than a footnote to the single.

Each remix is also an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} of the song: the same writing credits and words, a different production. Comparing it with the single is the clearest way to hear what production does.

The credits show the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} twice over. RedOne and T.I. Jakke produced Sofia; Grubert, who has written and produced for others for years, put his own production on top of theirs.

## Artistic Context

Álvaro Soler was born in 1991 in Sant Cugat del Vallès near Barcelona to a German father and a Spanish-Belgian mother, spent his teens in Japan and went solo in 2014 while living in Berlin. El mismo sol made his name in 2015, and Sofia, released on 15 April 2016, was added to the international edition of his album Eterno agosto. Robin Grubert, a German-Swedish composer and producer based in Berlin and Los Angeles, co-wrote and produced the Eurovision 2014 winner Rise Like a Phoenix, worked on Keri Hilson's I Like and records his own songs as Little Ashes.

## Impact and Reception

Sofia went to number one in Italy, Switzerland and Flanders and topped the airplay charts of the Czech Republic, Poland and Slovakia; it was certified eight times platinum in Italy and diamond in Poland, and it was still on Hungary's year-end dance chart in 2025. No review of the Grubert remix itself turned up. Play it after the single and decide which summer you prefer.
