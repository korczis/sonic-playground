## Genre and Mood

**DJ Fresh** made **Gold Dust** as drum and bass, and **Shy FX** cut it down into **Gold Dust (Shy FX Re-Edit)**, the version that became the 2012 single. Discogs files the release under drum and bass; Dancing Astronaut heard the re-edit as drum and bass, jungle and even reggae. The feelings, energetic and carefree, belong to the song's whole family, a dance record whose best-known video is all skipping ropes, skateboards and free running.

## Song Summary

Gold Dust began on 16 August 2008 as a 7:51 instrumental 12-inch on Breakbeat Kaos. In August 2010 it returned with a vocal by Ce'cile, a pairing Tropical Bass described as drum and bass meeting ragga-jungle, and in 2012 a version with Ms. Dynamite went onto DJ Fresh's album Nextlevelism. Shy FX then made a re-edit for radio, and Ministry of Sound released it on 2 December 2012 as the lead track of a six-track download. At 3:02 it is the shortest Gold Dust of all.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the re-edit section by section, so this breakdown keeps to what the sources say.

### Main Section

Dancing Astronaut wrote that Shy FX had dusted off the original and made it a real drum and bass, jungle and even reggae tune. Listen for all three: the fast breakbeat of drum and bass, the rolling, chopped drums of jungle, and the reggae lilt that the 2010 version already pointed toward.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shortens a track for the airwaves. Shy FX did more than trim: he reshaped the song for radio, and his version runs almost five minutes shorter than the 2008 original.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to another producer. On this release, five more producers followed Shy FX; compare his three minutes with {{ <track_link source="dj-fresh-kutz-gold-dust-kutz-full-vocal-mix.md" /> }}.

Gold Dust is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: instrumental, Ce'cile vocal, Ms. Dynamite vocal, re-edit, remixes. The song is a family of recordings rather than one.

## Artistic Context

DJ Fresh is Dan Stein, Gold Dust's sole credited writer and producer. Shy FX is the London DJ and producer Andre Williams, whose 1994 ragga jungle breakthrough Original Nuttah, with vocals by UK Apache, made him a mainstay of the jungle and drum and bass scene. With T Power and the singer Di he reached UK number seven in 2001 with Shake Ur Body; in 2005 he and T Power started the Digital Soundboy label, and in 2012 he produced Light Up (The World) for Yasmin, featuring Ms. Dynamite.

## Impact and Reception

The re-edit reached number 22 in the UK, two places above the 2010 version, and 39 in Ireland; Dancing Astronaut noted it had been played often on Annie Mac's Radio 1 show. All versions together were certified triple platinum in the UK, though none reached the top twenty. Play the re-edit, then the instrumental it came from.
