## Genre and Mood

**Magnetic Man** and **Katy B** made **Perfect Stranger** a drum and bass record built for the pop chart: electronic, energetic, euphoric and romantic at once. It is a club track that wants to be sung along to, and it sits on the line between the dubstep scene its producers came from and the radio.

## Song Summary

The lyric is about spotting someone on the dance floor and falling for them on the spot. Digital Spy's Nick Levine admitted the subject was well worn, then gave the single five stars anyway, praising a production "stately and spacious", clattering without feeling cluttered, and Katy B's enchanting vocal. Resident Advisor's Andrew Ryce called it a rolling junglist anthem and by far the album's peak. The album version heard here runs 5:57; the version on Katy B's own album is 3:13.

### Main Section

The weight of the song is in its chorus. Ryce wrote that it "launches into the stratosphere, propelled by a deceptively simple drum loop". PopMatters' Timothy Gabriele heard something less heavy: house-inflected pop with skronky bass, a track that strikes softer than the album's harder moments.

No source describes the ending of the album version either. What is documented is the group's care over it: they told The Guardian they spent two weeks getting the original version right.

## Concepts in Focus

Perfect Stranger is a clear case of the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}. Magnetic Man are three producers and DJs who brought in guest singers for their album; Katy B gives this track its voice and its story, and the song appears on both her debut and theirs.

The engine is a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}: a short drum pattern repeated under the chorus. Resident Advisor's point is that its simplicity is deceptive, the repetition giving the vocal its lift.

That chorus is the song's {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}, the part designed to stay with you after one listen.

The single also shows how a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} extends a song: the download added versions by Benga and Steve Angello, and the group said Benga could live so closely with a track and then do something completely different with it.

## Artistic Context

Magnetic Man are Benga, Skream and Artwork, dubstep producers and DJs who first met in the late 1990s at the Big Apple Records shop in Croydon. Their debut album Magnetic Man came out in October 2010 and reached number 5 in the UK. Perfect Stranger, its second single, was released on 1 October 2010 on Columbia. Katy B, born in Peckham in 1989, had released Katy on a Mission earlier in 2010, and she sings on a second album track, Crossover. Ms Dynamite, heard on {{ <track_link source="ms-dynamite-dy-na-mi-tee.md" /> }}, sings Fire on the same record.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 16 on the UK Singles Chart and number 29 in Scotland, and reached the Ultratip charts in both parts of Belgium. The album around it drew mixed to positive reviews, with a Metacritic score of 68. Play it loud and wait for the chorus.
