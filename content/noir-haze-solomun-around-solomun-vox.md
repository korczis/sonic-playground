## Genre and Mood

**Noir & Haze** wrote it, but **Solomun** made **Around (Solomun Vox)** the deep house anthem of 2011. It is house music at its most emotional: a rolling electronic groove that is hypnotic in the body and melancholic in the heart, carried by a voice full of longing.

## Song Summary

At its core, "Around" is a simple, aching song about someone who is no longer there, sung by Haze with a soft, slightly smoky tone. Solomun's vocal mix wraps that voice in a slow-building, bass-heavy groove: a deep, warm bassline, crisp but restrained drums and dark, moody chords that rise and fall like breathing. The result feels intimate and huge at the same time, a sad song you can dance to for seven minutes.

## Musical Breakdown

The mix is built for the long arc of a DJ set, taking its time before letting the vocal bloom.

### Intro

A steady kick and shuffling hi-hats open the track, with the bassline creeping in low and round.

### Build-up

Moody chords and small melodic details gather above the groove, and fragments of the vocal begin to surface.

### Verse 1

Haze's voice arrives in full, soft and close, delivering the lyric with a weary tenderness that cuts through the beat.

### Main Section

The groove locks in completely, the bass rolling under the vocal and the chords swelling, a hypnotic stretch that made dancefloors sing along.

### Bridge

A breakdown pulls the drums away, leaving the voice and chords suspended, the most emotional moment of the mix.

### Climax

The kick returns and everything lands together, the bassline at its heaviest and the vocal at its most yearning.

### Outro

The layers ease away, leaving the drums and bass to roll out into the next record.

## Artistic Context

Noir is a Danish producer and the founder of the Noir Music label; Haze wrote and sang the lyrics. "Around" was released on Noir Music in June 2011, with the Solomun Vox as its lead mix, and was later licensed to Defected. For Solomun, the Hamburg-based producer behind the Diynamic label, the remix became a breakthrough moment, the record that carried his name from the underground to the biggest clubs in the world.

## Impact and Reception

The Solomun Vox was inescapable throughout 2011 and 2012, hailed as one of the remixes of the year and described at the time as the deep house anthem of its season. Years later, Mixmag counted it among Solomun's most iconic moments, with tens of millions of streams. It is here because it is the perfect sad-happy dance record. Play it loud, close your eyes and sing along.
