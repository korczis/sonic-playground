## Genre and Mood

**Des Rocs** plays rock in the old sense of the word, and **Maybe, I** belongs to the hard, glam-tinged side of alternative rock that runs through his records. Its mood comes from its words: dark, confessional and defiant, a song that admits the worst about its narrator and refuses to apologise.

## Song Summary

Maybe, I is a song of self-doubt turned into a challenge. Its narrator feels a fever burning inside, waits for demons, and wonders whether he is still a lamb waiting for the slaughter. The refrain answers with a string of maybes: maybe he is a total wreck, maybe he will not repent, maybe he will live a lie and let the vultures in. That last phrase gave its name to Let the Vultures In, the debut EP released on 2 November 2018. The official audio had appeared on Des Rocs's YouTube channel two months earlier, on 8 September 2018. Danny Rocco is credited as the writer and produced the song with Katalyst.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part of a song that sticks. Here it is a phrase rather than a riff: Maybe, I, repeated at the start of line after line, so that the title is what you remember and its doubt becomes the song's identity.

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} lets an artist revisit his own song. In May 2021 Des Rocs released Maybe, I by Moonlight on his channel, shorter at 2:26, while promoting his This Is Our Life EP and Alive headline tour. Comparing the two is the clearest way to hear what the original's arrangement adds.

## Artistic Context

Des Rocs is the solo project of Danny Rocco, born in Rockville Centre, raised on Long Island and based in New York City. He began in the band Secret Weapons, which opened for Panic! at the Disco, Fall Out Boy and Weezer, before it went on hiatus. He names Elvis Presley, Roy Orbison, Jimi Hendrix, Queen and Muse as influences; Let the Vultures In was his solo debut.

## Impact and Reception

Des Rocs went on to open for Muse and the Rolling Stones and, after signing with Sumerian Records in 2023, to reach number six on Billboard's Mainstream Rock chart with I Am the Lightning. Maybe, I came first. Start here.
