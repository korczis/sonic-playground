## Artistic Context

Landhouse & Raddantze are a house and downtempo duo from Karlsruhe: Benjamin Landhäußer, who also records alone as Landhouse, and Gino Raddant. Their releases go back at least to the Den Grønne Katten EP of 2018 on the label Molt. Landhouse is the busier name, with solo albums in 2024 and 2025 and a 2026 Spirit Dance collection, Journey Through The Night with Landhouse, that includes the duo's Nicht Mit Jup Dieter. In 2019 a Landhouse track appeared on Horizon, a compilation from Iboga Records, the label of {{ <track_link source="be-svendsen-circle.md" /> }}.
