## Genre and Mood

**Catastrophe**, a French pop collective, recorded **Smalltown Boy - Nova Version** as a cover and named it after a radio station, then released it on their 2020 vinyl selection Fizzy. The song itself is a melancholic dance record, a story of longing and escape often called a gay anthem, and this is a pop band's reading of it.

## Song Summary

Bronski Beat released Smalltown Boy in 1984 as their debut single. Written by Steve Bronski, Jimmy Somerville and Larry Steinbachek, it tells of a bullied young man who leaves home, and Bernard Rose's video shows a boy who is gay-bashed and then leaves his hometown for London. Catastrophe's version is the seventh of eight tracks on Fizzy, 3:47 long. Its title points to Radio Nova: in October 2019, after an interview about exile and intimacy between the novelists Loo Hui Phang and Blandine Rinkel, Catastrophe played the song live in Richard Gaitet's Nova Book Box. The release does not say whether the Fizzy track is that session's recording.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes this version's arrangement, so this section says what to listen for rather than what happens.

### Verse

Listen to how the story is told: the original sets it to a pulsing synth-pop backing, and every cover has to decide whether to keep that pulse or let the words stand more exposed.

### Refrain

The refrain is where the song's advice lives, the "Run away, turn away" that Radio Nova chose as the caption of its video. Listen to who sings it and how many voices join in.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} keeps a song's melody and words and is free to change the rest. Smalltown Boy has been covered, sampled and remixed many times, from Real McCoy to Orville Peck; hear Catastrophe's take against {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat_small_town_boy.md" /> }} and the piano-led {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat-smalltown-boy-reprise-2014.md" /> }}, in which Somerville himself took the song out of the club.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} of Smalltown Boy is unusual: it is not a boast or a love line but an instruction to leave. Because those few words carry the whole story, the song survives being moved into almost any arrangement.

## Artistic Context

Catastrophe began in 2016 around Pierre Jouan and Blandine Rinkel, who is also a novelist; Arthur Navellou and others joined later. They announced themselves with an article in Libération, wrote a book in a single night, La nuit est encore jeune, and released a self-titled album on Bertrand Burgalat's label Tricatel in January 2018, music that Cafébabel heard moving between jazz, pop, classical and hip-hop. Étienne Daho invited them to the Théâtre National de Bretagne, where they later became associate artists.

## Impact and Reception

Radio Nova's verdict on the session was simple: magnificent. Fizzy gathered eight of the band's tracks on vinyl for Record Store Day 2020, a way in, the band said, to the world of Catastrophe. Play it after the original and listen for what a quieter room does to the words.
