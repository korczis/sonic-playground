## Genre and Mood

**SAINt JHN** and **Imanbek**'s **Roses - Imanbek Remix** is the version of Roses that conquered the charts: slap house, as Wikipedia files it, a pitched-up voice over a house beat. It is upbeat, catchy and carefree, which is remarkable because the song underneath was none of those things. Sound On Sound described the 2016 original as dark, brooding, medium-tempo trap.

## Song Summary

Roses first came out in July 2016, written by Carlos Phillips, the rapper's real name, with Lee Stashenko and produced by Fallen. In spring 2019 Imanbek Zeikenov, a 19-year-old in Aksu, Kazakhstan, studying engineering and working as a railway signalman, remixed it as a hobby. He told Sound On Sound it took about two hours, on a ten-year-old Acer laptop running FL Studio 12, monitored on cheap earbuds. He uploaded it in May 2019 and, by his account, forgot about it.

## Musical Breakdown

### Tempo and Key

According to Sound On Sound, Imanbek raised the tempo from 102 to 122 beats per minute and the key from C minor to F minor, pitching it up step by step while experimenting.

### Beat and Bass

He first edited the original into a new structure, then added a kick, clap, hi-hat and crash and a bass line. His effects were a Cableguys Kickstart plug-in, FL Studio's own EQ and reverb, and one mastering plug-in he declined to name.

### The Voice

Saint Jhn's vocal is pitched far up; Wikipedia notes it is higher than in the original. Imanbek himself put the appeal down to a track that was "unusual" and "really positive", with a pitched-up voice he called "infectious". No source maps the song section by section.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} usually keeps the vocal and changes everything around it, and this one proves how far that can go: almost nothing is new except drums and a bass line, yet the mood flips from brooding to sunny. Imanbek made it without Saint Jhn's involvement, after his attempts to contact the rapper on Instagram went unanswered; only after Kirill Lupinos of Effective Records contacted him in August 2019 was the sample cleared with Saint Jhn and his manager.

{{ <concept_link name="pitch shifting" /> }} does the rest. Raised five semitones, from C minor to F minor, Saint Jhn's voice becomes the element Imanbek himself called infectious. Compare {{ <track_link source="hotel-ugly-shut-up-my-moms-calling-sped-up.md" /> }}, a faster, higher version that also travelled on TikTok.

## Artistic Context

Saint Jhn, born in 1986 and raised between Georgetown, Guyana, and Brooklyn, wrote songs for other artists before his solo career. The remix was officially released in September 2019 through a partnership between Effective Records and his label Hitco, and later appeared on his album While the World Was Burning. A Snapchat filter and TikTok carried it abroad.

## Impact and Reception

The remix reached number four on the Billboard Hot 100 and number one in the UK, Australia, Canada, Ireland, the Netherlands and New Zealand. In March 2021 it won the Grammy for Best Remixed Recording, Non-Classical, making Imanbek the first Grammy winner from Kazakhstan. Listen with the 2016 original next to it.
