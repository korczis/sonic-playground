## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz**' **Broken** is synth-pop from an electronic album, melancholic and introspective, and one of the Plastic Beach tracks with no guest credited: Damon Albarn sings it himself. Stereogum's Ryan Leas called it melancholic, one of the record's more world-weary tracks.

## Song Summary

Broken is the eleventh of Plastic Beach's sixteen tracks and runs 3:17. Gorillaz are credited with writing and producing it, Jason Cox with recording and mixing, Stephen Sedgwick with recording and programming. It was never a single.

It is also one of the oldest songs on the album. On 14 January 2009, more than a year before release, Albarn presented Zane Lowe's BBC Radio 1 show with members of Gorillaz and aired three demos, Electric Shock, Broken and Binge, the song that became Stylo. Stylo was heavily reworked; Broken remained mostly unchanged.

### The Voice

Leas counted the album's highlights as the songs where Albarn's presence was clearest, and heard Broken, with Rhinestone Eyes, as a more strung-out rendition of a similar Albarn aesthetic.

### The Words

Erik Thompson of The Line of Best Fit heard it continue the candour of On Melancholy Hill, with Albarn turning his stark appraisal towards the fall of man.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="demo" /> }} is usually a sketch to be replaced. BBC Newsbeat called the three aired in 2009 rough tunes, a sizeable departure from the first two albums, with fewer vocals; that Broken survived almost as it was says the song was largely finished early.

Plastic Beach is a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }}: its title is a floating island in the South Pacific made of humanity's debris, inspired by marine pollution such as the Great Pacific Garbage Patch. Albarn said he wanted listeners to feel the essential melancholy of a ready meal in its plastic packaging, and Broken is where Stereogum heard that melancholy most plainly.

As a {{ <concept_link name="deep cut" /> }}, Broken is heard mostly by listeners who play the record through. Leas still heard it as a more sophisticated extension of the sound first sketched on the debut, the one that began with {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-del-the-funky-homosapien-clint-eastwood.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Plastic Beach, the third Gorillaz album, came out on 3 March 2010 on Parlophone, and on Virgin in the United States. Albarn produced it himself, recording from June 2008 to November 2009 in London, New York and Damascus, with guests from Snoop Dogg to Lou Reed.

## Impact and Reception

The album debuted at number two in the UK and on the Billboard 200 and scored 77 on Metacritic. Sean Highkin of Beats Per Minute called On Melancholy Hill and Broken the album's most pop-friendly one-two. Play them in that order and hear one song hand its mood to the next.
