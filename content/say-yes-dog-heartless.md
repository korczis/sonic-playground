## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog**'s **"Heartless"** is electro-pop and synth-pop at its most compact: barely more than two minutes, warm and heartfelt, a small song in the middle of an album about travelling far.

## Song Summary

"Heartless" is the fifth of ten tracks on Voyage, the trio's second album, released on 10 May 2019 by Diskodogs Records. It is built around a single sentence: the German blog Shitesite wrote that its central, and only, line is "I don't want to be heartless", and that with this sound the three of them need not worry. That is the song's whole lyric, a wish rather than a story, and the music around it carries the rest.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found walks through "Heartless" part by part, so this breakdown stays with what critics said about the sound of Voyage and with the one line Shitesite quoted.

### Main Section

The line is the song. Listen for how a short phrase turns into the hook simply by coming back, and whether the arrangement shifts beneath it while the words stay the same.

### The Sound Around It

Shitesite heard more analog sounds, more warmth and more feeling on Voyage than on the debut. Soundmag heard synthesizers and beats mixed with analog instruments such as bass guitar and drums, electro-pop at once warm and cool.

### Outro

The song stops after about two minutes and hands over to "Feel Better". No source describes its ending.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the short idea a song is built to stick with you, and it usually earns its place through repetition. "Heartless" takes that to its limit: with only one line, the hook and the lyric are the same thing, and the only variation left is in the voice and the arrangement.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is where Shitesite heard Voyage change most: the bass is unusually prominent and creative, and often shapes the songs more than the beat does. The critic said that about the album, not this track in particular, so listen for whether the bass leads here as it does in {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-lies.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

**Say Yes Dog** are Aaron Ahrends (vocals), Paul Rundel (bass) and Pascal Karier (drums), a German-Luxembourgish trio. After their 2015 debut Plastic Love they toured festivals in India, Korea and Vietnam and travelled to Japan and Ghana, and announced that their second album would be shaped by those impressions. Shitesite found little pentatonic or Afrobeat influence on it; instead, the band had refined their electro-pop. Ahrends is credited as the producer of "Heartless".

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of Voyage were friendly but measured. Bedroomdisco heard clear synth-pop with funky bass and minimal drums, but songs that often sound alike; Soundmag found no obvious hit, but a self-contained sound world to get lost in; MusikBlog called it a beautiful, occasionally slightly monotonous album. "Heartless" asks for two minutes. Give them, and see whether one line is enough.
