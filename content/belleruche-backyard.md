## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche**'s **Backyard** comes from The Express, the album their Bandcamp tags as blues, electronic and soul and this page also files under trip-hop, as it does the trio's other records. No review describes this song by itself; groovy and warm are this page's reading of what critics said about the whole album, smoky vocals, bluesy guitar and a beat from the decks.

## Song Summary

Backyard is the second of eleven tracks on The Express, which Tru Thoughts released on 13 October 2008, and it runs 3:15. The credits say the three members wrote, produced and engineered the album themselves in a place they call Flat 2, and that Jason Howes mastered it at The Premises. No source describes the lyric, so this page leaves the backyard of the title to the listener.

### Main Section

The parts are the band's: Kathrin deBoer's voice, Ricky Fabulous's guitar and DJ Modest's turntables. Basic Soul heard deBoer's vocals on the album as smooth and smoky, Fabulous's rhythm guitar as harmonic and Modest's sampling and electronic beats as well placed. ## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} makes the record player an instrument. On The Express, the label wrote, the trio used their turntable trickery more subtly than before, leaving a wider space for melodies and ideas, so the decks colour the song rather than lead it.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} gave the track a second life. The producer Kidkanevil reworked it, and Tru Thoughts put his version on the 2010 Fuzz Face single, two years and one album later. Music Is My Sanctuary called it a nice little downtempo remix; MusicBrainz lists it again on the label's Shapes 11:01 and on the band's Best Of in 2013.

The original travelled through the {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}: MusicBrainz and Discogs list it on lounge collections such as Wagram's Saint-Germain-des-Prés Café Volume XI and High Note's Beijing 2, both from 2009.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005 and signed to the Brighton label Tru Thoughts in 2007, the year their debut Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut in the label's history. The Express followed in October 2008; the label wrote that it kept the trio's simple formula and gave it a more raw, bluesy edge. Compare {{ <track_link source="belleruche-anything-you-want-not-that.md" /> }}, the track that opens the album just before it.

## Impact and Reception

Wikipedia says The Express gained daytime support on BBC 6Music. The label's own upload of Backyard went up on its TruThoughtsJukeBox channel in 2012. Play it next to the Kidkanevil remix and hear how much room the original leaves.
