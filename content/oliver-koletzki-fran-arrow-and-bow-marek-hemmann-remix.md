## Genre and Mood

**Oliver Koletzki** and **Fran** wrote **Arrow and Bow** as synth-pop; in **Marek Hemmann**'s hands it became house, deep and melodic, a remix that keeps the romance of a love song and stretches it into something warm and long. Their label called it a deeply romantic house affair, fit for the educated dancefloor, and that is the frame for listening to it.

## Song Summary

The original Arrow and Bow is a four-minute song about love, the first single from Lovestoned, the album Koletzki made with Fran on his label Stil vor Talent. Hemmann's remix keeps the song's subject and its singer but not its shape. According to the label, he kept some of the best parts of the vocal and coloured them with beautifully melodic synthesizer patterns. This page is about Arrow and Bow - Marek Hemmann Remix: fewer words, more melody, more time.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version of a finished recording, built from its parts. This one shows the idea clearly because the original is on the playground too: put {{ <track_link source="oliver-koletzki-fran-arrow-and-bow-original-version.md" /> }} next to it and hear what a remixer chooses to keep. The single put Hemmann's version first, ahead of Koletzki's own club remix and two by Channel X.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} does the colouring. In the original the label heard synthesizers taking the song back to 1980s pop; here it hears melodic patterns that turn the same vocal into house.

Length is the third idea. A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} blends tracks into one continuous set, and DJs need room at both ends to do it. MusicBrainz lists this remix on DJ mixes such as The Place Ibiza, Volume 1 in 2011 and Body Language, Vol. 11 in 2012.

## Artistic Context

Oliver Koletzki, born in Braunschweig in 1974, founded Stil vor Talent in 2005. Marek Hemmann, born in Gera in 1979 and based in Jena since 2005, releases mainly on Freude am Tanzen, and his own melodic minimal is in {{ <track_link source="marek-hemmann-gemini.md" /> }}. The digital single came out on 16 August 2010, catalogue number SVT051, with Hemmann's remix as its first track; Koletzki and Fran's earlier single is {{ <track_link source="oliver-koletzki-fran-hypnotized.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No review of the remix itself has been found; the label's notes are the only description of its sound. It went onto the 2011 label compilation 6 Years Stil vor Talent and several DJ mixes. Listen to it after the original, and hear a pop song turned into seven minutes of house.
