## Genre and Mood

**Maelstrom** and **Louisahhh** released **Rough & Tender** on Brodinski's label, which Louisahhh said was all about sinister club music, "so my music fits right in". Vice heard her records of that year as dark, sexy, undeniably French house and techno, and those words give this page its genres and two of its feelings, dark and sultry; bittersweet is this page's reading of a title that holds both halves of a relationship.

## Song Summary

The track is the second of two on Bromance #15, out on 17 March 2014 and credited on the label's upload to Louisahhh!!! & Maelstrom; the first is Night Clubbing. Vice described its opening as spoken lines about protecting a fragile heart and balancing toughness with tenderness. One of them came from a book by her ex: "I hated him for a while, because his writing is so good," she told Vice. She called the song "a moment of standing on the edge and having to push through it for spiritual progress."

## Musical Breakdown

No published review walks through Rough & Tender part by part, so this breakdown follows what the sources do describe.

### Intro

The voice comes first, speaking rather than singing. Listen for how long the words have the room before the beat takes it over.

### Main Section

Spin heard its sister track, Night Clubbing, as a hard, steady bass beat with glistening synth hits and arpeggiated sequencers. No source says whether Rough & Tender is built the same way; listen for what it shares and what it holds back.

### Outro

At just under five minutes, the track is short for a club record. Listen for how it ends.

## Concepts in Focus

Louisahhh's voice here works as {{ <concept_link name="spoken word" /> }}: talked, and low, rather than sung over the beat. Vice compared it to the icy vocals of Ladytron. Spoken words at the head of a dance track sound like a confession made on a dancefloor, and the title names the two halves those words try to balance.

On 2 June 2014 Bromance issued a deluxe edition, Traces, with three new versions: two of Night Clubbing by Perc and a Gothic Stripped Down Mix of Rough & Tender by Dave Clarke. Each {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands the track to another DJ's taste and sends it into other DJs' sets.

## Artistic Context

Louisahhh, New York-bred, moved to France in 2013; Brodinski suggested she meet Maelstrom, Spin reported. Maelstrom recalls that she came straight from the airport to his house in Nantes and that in that first week they recorded her first solo Bromance EP, Transcend. He had been signed early on by The Hacker to Zone, and the two later co-founded RAAR. For her start on Bromance, hear {{ <track_link source="brodinski-louisahhh-let-the-beat-control-your-body.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The pair kept the song: MusicBrainz lists Rough & Tender (Provocation) on their 2024 album May the Rage Burn a Path for Joy, ten years on. Start with the words, and listen to what the beat does to them.
