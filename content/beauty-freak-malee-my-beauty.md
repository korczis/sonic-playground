## Genre and Mood

**Beauty Freak** and **MaLee**'s **My Beauty** is electro-swing as pop: a playful, nostalgic song in which the label hears the 1960s, dressed for the dance floor. Its video takes that theatrical streak into a mansion.

## Song Summary

My Beauty, released on 1 December 2017 by the label Ego, was the debut single of Beauty Freak, a band from Lille formed by three French DJs, Stéphane Chenu, Ludovic Piquemal and Yann Brunissen, who wrote it together. Chenu and MaLee share the vocals. The label describes a song about the mystery of every love story that doubles as a cry for tolerance and acceptance, built from symphonic and electronic music with jazzy touches.

## Musical Breakdown

The sources describe the song's sound and image rather than its structure, so this breakdown follows what they say, not a bar-by-bar map.

### Intro: A Retro Frame

The label's pitch is an atmosphere that brings us back to 1960s sounds, with Propellerheads and Amy Winehouse named as reference points.

### Main Section: Two Voices

Chenu and MaLee sing it between them; the label credits MaLee's vocal strength with lifting the track.

### The Message: A Call for Acceptance

The label likens the song to a chorus bound to broadcast a cry for tolerance and acceptance, the message the video spells out.

### The Arrangement: Symphony Meets Club

The band's stated idea is a meeting of symphonic and electronic music, in the label's words a world where the waltz meets electronic music; no source says how that plays out bar by bar.

## Concepts in Focus

MaLee is credited as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, the label for a guest beside the main act. Here the credit is modest for a singer whose voice the label calls the highlight; credits describe contracts and billing as much as who carries a song.

The song found a second home as a {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }}. In 2019 the soundtrack album of Luc Besson's film Anna, otherwise Eric Serra's score, included Beauty Freak's My Beauty, in a version called the My Soul Edit. A film can give a song an audience it never had as a single.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was shot in a mansion-like set and follows the daily life of two opposite families, in a nod to the Addams Family. The video turns the plea for tolerance into a story about two households that do not fit together.

## Artistic Context

Beauty Freak sit in the electro-swing line that {{ <track_link source="caravan-palace-lone-digger.md" /> }} made famous from Paris: older pop and jazz styles reworked with electronic production. My Beauty kept growing after its release: Beauty Freak's 2019 album Mon amour carries it again as the My Soul Edit 2018 and as an orchestral mix.
