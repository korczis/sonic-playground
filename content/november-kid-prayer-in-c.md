## Song Summary

Prayer in C was written by Nili Hadida and Benjamin Cotto of the French folk-pop duo Lilly Wood and the Prick and released in 2010 on their album Invincible Friends. In 2014 Robin Schulz remixed it from a CD of that album, and his version reached number one in France and around twenty other countries. NOVEMBER KID's cover came out on 20 June 2025 on One Seven Music and lasts 2:02. ## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song by someone other than its original artist. That is what separates this track from the hit most listeners know: Schulz's {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworked Lilly Wood and the Prick's own recording, keeping Hadida's voice, as the page on {{ <track_link source="lilly-wood-and-the-prick-robin-schulz-prayer-in-c-robin-schulz-radio-edit.md" /> }} describes. The pairing also shows how far a song travels from its first form: a folk-pop album track, a deep house remix, then a two-minute cover eleven years later.
