## Genre and Mood

**La Fleur** made **Flowerhead** as a house track, and **Spencer Parker's Gun For Hire Remix** stretches it to eleven minutes and twenty-five seconds for the club. Discogs files the 2011 release that first carried it under house and tech house. No review of the remix has been found, so groovy and hypnotic are this page's reading of those styles and of a version built to be long, not a description of how it sounds.

## Song Summary

Flowerhead is the work of Sanna Engdahl, who records as La Fleur, and Tore Jarlo; Discogs credits both as writers and producers. The original came out in May 2010 on Flowerhead EP, the first record of her own vinyl label, Power Plant. In 2011 Whatpeopleplay, the label of the Hamburg company Wordandsound, licensed the track for Flowerhead Revisited, with this remix on the B side; Discogs credits Parker with the remix and additional production. Its distributor praised the original's fresh, piano-driven sound.

## Concepts in Focus

This {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} came out of the licensing. La Fleur told MEOKO that Wordandsound asked to license her vinyl-only EP; she agreed to license only Flowerhead, they suggested a remix by Spencer Parker, and it felt meant to be, because he had been one of the first DJs to support the record and had asked to remix it. The remix's name is also the title of Parker's debut album, released by Saved Records in September 2011.

The {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} then put it in new company. What People Play House Compilation 1 places it fifteenth among thirty tracks, beside Todd Terje, Pantha du Prince, Roman Flügel and {{ <track_link source="nicolas-jaar-time-for-us.md" /> }}, and adds two continuous DJ mixes; Format:B's {{ <track_link source="format-b-fran-liquid-feat-fran-original-mix.md" /> }} is there too.

## Artistic Context

La Fleur told Dancing Astronaut that no label wanted the Flowerhead tracks, so she started Power Plant to release them, against the advice of most colleagues; the cover art is by Olaf Hajek. Spencer Parker, an English house producer, moved to Berlin; in 2016 Mixmag described him playing Panorama Bar, best known on Rekids and running his own label, Work Them.

## Impact and Reception

La Fleur told MEOKO that she had thought of buying all 500 copies of the first pressing herself, and that it sold out quickly. Flowerhead returned on Power Plant in December 2016 as Flowerhead Reprise, with remixes by Cassy and Dana Ruh and her own 2016 rework. Give Parker's version its full eleven minutes.
