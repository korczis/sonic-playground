## Genre and Mood

**Highly Sedated**'s **Slip Away** is electronic music that grows into progressive house: energetic, uplifting and, by the time its synths are all in, euphoric. BroadwayWorld picked it as the high-energy track that best shows the band's production abilities on their debut album.

## Song Summary

Slip Away is the fifth of twelve tracks on All We Have Is Now, which the Swedish band released on 28 July 2017 through Steve Angello's SIZE Records. It runs four minutes and thirty-eight seconds. There is no full sung lyric at its centre; instead, in BroadwayWorld's words, it is built around underpinned snippets of sampled lyrics, set over uplifting chords and a driving bassline, and it escalates into a plethora of full-bodied synth patterns.

The layers carry it all the way to what the article calls a progressive house anthem.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the engine from the first bars: a driving low part under the uplifting chords, there before the synth layers arrive.

The vocals are an example of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} used as texture. Short fragments of words, rather than a verse and a chorus, give the track a human voice without tying it to a story.

The {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} is the shape of the whole piece as BroadwayWorld describes it: a quick escalation, synth pattern upon synth pattern, until the track becomes an anthem. Compare another Swedish take on progressive house, {{ <track_link source="dada-life-don-t-stop.md" /> }}.

Slip Away also exists as a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }}. In 2019 SIZE published a version recorded live in Vilnius with the Lithuanian Chamber Orchestra, six minutes and fifty-two seconds long, more than two minutes longer than the album cut.

## Artistic Context

Highly Sedated formed in 2015 among old friends, after one of them, Leo, invited the others to play a festival in Lithuania; they had expected a one-off. They called themselves a rock and roll set-up making electronic dance music, "as analogue as digital can get", with Moog synthesisers and string instruments, and named Radiohead, Wu-Tang Clan, Underworld and Massive Attack among their influences. SIZE, the Stockholm label Angello founded in 2003, released their early singles, among them Make Love, Got Some Water and Last Call.

## Impact and Reception

Before the album came out, the label announced a Midsummer concert with the Lithuanian Chamber Orchestra in Vilnius on 21 July 2017, and Last Call became a fixture of Angello's residency on BBC Radio 1. The band called the album "a timeless album with a contemporary feel". Turn it up and wait for the synths to pile in.
