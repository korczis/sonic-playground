## Song Summary

Matroschka E.P. came out in June 2006 on Ostwind Records, catalogue number OW006, as a single vinyl record. Side A carries Out Of My Space; side B opens with Kosmonautentraeume and closes with Minikombikosmonautenmix, a remix by Marlose. Discogs credits the writing and production of the two originals to Kombinat 100 and the mastering to Helmut Erler at Dubplates & Mastering in Berlin. ## Concepts in Focus

On vinyl, a {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} is the second face of the record, and on a club twelve-inch it can carry a track as long and as important as the A-side. Here side B holds both the original Kosmonautentraeume and a reworking of it, so a DJ could choose between two versions without changing records.

That reworking is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} placed on the release from the start: Marlose's Minikombikosmonautenmix, whose title folds Kombinat and Kosmonauten into one word. It returned in 2024 on Ostwind's Remix Compilation, Vol. 1, and the original appeared on Ostwind Compilation Volume 1 in 2008.

{{ <concept_link name="mastering" /> }} is the last step before a record is cut, and the credit here is specific: Helmut Erler at Dubplates & Mastering in Berlin. For a vinyl release, mastering also prepares the sound for being cut into the groove.

## Artistic Context

Discogs lists the members of Kombinat 100 as Ronny Mollenhauer, known as Mollono.Bass, Henry Dieckmann, known as Commander Love, Mark Vogler and Jens Lembke. Their own Bandcamp notes describe four men from Mecklenburg whose tracks are born on stage and made for the stage, drawing on techno, house, dub, pop and jazz, with accordion, melodica, congas and Hammond organ in the hand-played moments. Mollono.Bass, a founding member, also founded the labels Acker Records in 2006 and 3000° two years later. The album Wege Übers Land, which the band's Bandcamp calls a retrospective of its countless live gigs, followed on Acker Records at the end of 2007.

## Impact and Reception

The names share one vocabulary: a Kombinat was a union of industrial plants into one large enterprise in the socialist states, East Germany among them; Kosmonaut is the Soviet word for an astronaut; a Matroschka is the Russian nesting doll.
