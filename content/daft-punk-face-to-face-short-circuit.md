## Genre and Mood

**Daft Punk** at full power on stage: **Face to Face / Short Circuit** is electronic music as a stadium event, a house and dance medley that feels euphoric from the first bar. It is celebratory in the purest sense, the sound of a crowd and two robots in a pyramid of light sharing the same energetic rush, and it shows how the duo could turn their own back catalogue into something even bigger live.

## Song Summary

This is a live mash-up rather than a single song. Daft Punk take the bright, filtered house bounce and chopped vocal of Face to Face, the Discovery track sung by Todd Edwards, and weave it into the twitchy, glitching robot funk of Short Circuit from the same album. The cheers of the audience are part of the arrangement, swelling every time a familiar hook returns. The memorable detail is the way the two tracks seem to argue and then agree, one sunny and human, the other mechanical and stuttering.

## Musical Breakdown

Like the whole Alive 2007 set, the segment is built from overlapping loops that rise, collide and resolve.

### Intro

A filtered loop creeps in under the crowd noise, the cutoff slowly opening as thousands of people recognise what is coming.

### Build-up

Drums lock into a hard four-on-the-floor while elements of Short Circuit start flickering through the mix, building pressure.

### Main Section

The vocal of Face to Face bursts through, bright and catchy, riding a bouncing house groove that sounds even fuller in the arena.

### Climax

The two tracks collide head on, the jagged synth stabs of Short Circuit punching through the melody while the crowd roars over the top.

### Outro

The energy is dialled back into stuttering, glitching fragments that lead straight into the next part of the set without pausing.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} in the fullest sense: captured at the Palais Omnisports de Paris-Bercy on 14 June 2007, with the crowd left in the mix as part of the sound. There is no gap between tracks, because the set was conceived as one continuous performance; what you hear is the night itself, not a studio version with applause added.

The performance is built from the {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}. Daft Punk ran Ableton Live on custom-built computers and played loops, samples and filters from their own records in real time, so Face to Face and Short Circuit can sound simultaneously, one layered over the other: a live {{ <concept_link name="mashup" /> }} of two of their own tracks, made possible because both share a tempo and a groove. The same loop-based logic runs through the studio track {{ <track_link source="daft-punk-around-the-world.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="filter sweep" /> }} is their main tool for tension. Using Minimoog Voyager synthesizers and mixers in the pyramid, they could close a loop's filter until it is a muffled thud and open it again as the crowd roars; listen to the intro, where the opening of the filter is the build.

## Artistic Context

Alive 2007 documents Daft Punk's celebrated pyramid tour, and this recording comes from their show at Bercy in Paris on 14 June 2007. Track 7 of the album, it runs 4:55 and is credited to Thomas Bangalter, Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo and Todd Edwards. Both source tracks come from Discovery (2001), and the tour reworked them alongside material from Homework and Human After All into one continuous megamix. Thomas Bangalter and Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo used the concert to show how sampling and looping their own records could create something new.

## Impact and Reception

Alive 2007 is widely seen as one of the great live electronic albums: Pitchfork called it "the Ultimate Daft Punk Mixtape", and it won the Grammy for Best Electronic/Dance Album at the 51st Grammy Awards. The pyramid shows became legendary, and their influence on the scale and spectacle of later dance-music festivals is hard to overstate. This segment is here because it captures that euphoria in under five minutes. Play it loud, close your eyes and imagine the pyramid lighting up.
