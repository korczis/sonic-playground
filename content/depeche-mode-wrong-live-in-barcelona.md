## Genre and Mood

**Depeche Mode** take **Wrong (Live in Barcelona)** through synth-pop, electronic music and the muscle of alternative rock. It is intense, dark and defiant, a catalogue of failures delivered with the swagger of a band that knows exactly how to own a stadium.

## Song Summary

"Wrong" is a breathless list of everything that can go wrong in a life: wrong place, wrong time, wrong choices, recited with grim relentlessness. On record it is a jagged, minimal piece of electronic menace; live it becomes heavier and more physical, with real drums pounding under the synths and Dave Gahan's voice carrying the litany with theatrical gusto. The audience turns its bleakness into something like a celebration. Martin Gore's synth parts sound rawer and more distorted on stage, and the rhythm section gives the song a pounding forward motion that the studio cut deliberately withheld. It is a fine example of how Depeche Mode's electronic songs grow teeth in front of a crowd.

## Musical Breakdown

The live version keeps the song's tight structure but adds weight and crowd energy.

### Intro

A distorted, lurching synth figure cuts through the arena, instantly recognisable and greeted with a roar.

### Verse 1

Gahan launches into the rapid-fire lyric, each line hammered home over a stuttering, mechanical rhythm.

### Chorus

The title word lands like a slammed door, the full band behind it, drums and synths locking together.

### Bridge

The arrangement briefly opens up, letting the synth textures swirl before the litany returns.

### Outro

The song crashes to an abrupt stop, leaving the Barcelona crowd's cheers to fill the space.

## Artistic Context

"Wrong" was the lead single from Sounds of the Universe, Depeche Mode's 2009 album. The band's world tour that followed was filmed and recorded over two nights in Barcelona in November 2009, released in 2010 as Tour of the Universe: Barcelona 20/21.11.09. The live line-up, with drummer Christian Eigner and keyboardist Peter Gordeno alongside Gahan, Martin Gore and Andy Fletcher, gives the song a punch the studio version only hints at.

## Impact and Reception

"Wrong" was a reminder that Depeche Mode, three decades into their career, could still sound abrasive and urgent. This live version captures the band in full flight, with a crowd that clearly knows the new song as well as the classics. It belongs here for the way it turns a bleak list into a shared, cathartic shout. Play it loud and imagine the arena lights.
