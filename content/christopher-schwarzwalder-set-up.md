## Genre and Mood

**Christopher Schwarzwälder**'s **Set up** is made for the club. Discogs files the EP it comes from under tech house, the record shop Oye under house and tech house, and the label's Bandcamp page tags it minimal and electronic: styles built on a steady pulse and slow change rather than on verses and choruses. The feelings are dark and hypnotic because that is what the label promised from this side of the record: Setup and The List, its description says, will take you to darker and more hypnotic places.

## Song Summary

Set up is the third of five tracks on Leaves, an EP released by Denature Records on 6 April 2018 as a download and a 180-gram 12-inch, catalogue number DR004, and repressed on vinyl in 2023. It runs six minutes and four seconds; the Bandcamp page spells it Setup. Schwarzwälder is credited as its composer and producer. Around it are Catjammer and Slow Down, made with Jo.Ke, the lead singer of Feathered Sun, which the label hears as slow and melancholic, then The List and the title track, described as an endless ballad into the sunset.

### Main Section

No published review describes Set up section by section, and this page does not invent one. What the sources give is its place and its colour: the opening of side B, the turn from melancholy to darkness. A tech house track of this length usually begins and ends so a DJ can mix it, develops by adding and removing layers over a steady kick, and leaves room for a few changes to matter. Listen for where Set up follows that pattern and where it goes its own way.

## Concepts in Focus

The foundation of tech house is the {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}: a kick on every beat, off-beat hi-hats and claps, at a tempo a room can dance to for hours. Everything else in a track like Set up happens on top of that groove.

Volumo's biography of Schwarzwälder describes his style as somewhat wistful and dark, dominated by a {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }}, yet always uplifting. No source names the key of Set up, but the idea helps with the label's darker places: a minor key is what most listeners hear as shadow.

## Artistic Context

Schwarzwälder, according to the same biography, is a trained chef who began playing hip-hop records in Frankfurt am Main and moved to Berlin, cooking full time while learning the city's parties. He records with Mira as Mira & Chris Schwarzwälder, belongs to the collective Feathered Sun, heard in {{ <track_link source="feathered-sun-saubohnen.md" /> }}, and has worked with Nu and Acid Pauli. Compare his earlier {{ <track_link source="christopher-schwarzwalder-iannis-ritter-high-original-mix.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Set up is not a chart record but a club track from a small label, repressed five years on. Play it late, after the melancholy of the EP's first side.
