## Genre and Mood

**Farolfi & Gambafreaks vs Moloko** is the credit, and the "vs" says how **A Style Suite** was made: two Italian producers meeting the English-Irish duo Moloko through a sample. The record is filed under house and tech house; its mood comes from what it borrows, a disco single, and the groovy, uplifting pull of that song is what the producers carried onto the dance floor.

## Song Summary

A Style Suite is built on Moloko's The Time Is Now, the 2000 single by Róisín Murphy and Mark Brydon that became the duo's biggest UK hit. The German Kontor pressing states it plainly: the record contains a sample of The Time Is Now, courtesy of Chrysalis UK, and credits Murphy and Brydon as writers. The Italian maxi-single credits Murphy's vocals and Alex Farolfi's arrangement. This page is about the Gambafreaks vs Farolfi Mix, the main version.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

No review we found describes this mix in detail, so we will not invent a structure for it. What the record itself tells you is its length, 5:16, a DJ's version with room to mix in and out.

### Main Section

The source is better documented than the remake. The Time Is Now opens on acoustic guitar, adds a string quartet and a high, Bee Gees-like vocal, and fades on a looping piano; Brydon said the band wanted an acoustic dance track. A Style Suite takes Murphy's voice from it into a house and tech house frame.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} here is legal as well as musical: the German sleeve notes that the sample is used courtesy of Chrysalis, which also publishes the song, and the writing credit stays with Moloko. That is why the record is "vs Moloko" rather than a cover.

The 24 Records 12-inch shows how club records come in an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} for every use: this 5:16 main mix, a 3:46 radio mix, and two mixes by other producers, Alle Vacchi and Fedo.

The German edition added another {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}, by ATB, made at ATB Studio 5 and running 7:45. Compare how {{ <track_link source="ash-naila-sing-it-back.md" /> }} brings a different Moloko song into deep house by remaking it rather than sampling it.

## Artistic Context

The 24 Records 12-inch, catalogue number TW 54, came out in Italy in January 2005, with Red Music holding the recording rights; Cassagrande released it in Spain and Kontor in Germany that spring. The same year it appeared on compilations including Kontor's Top of the Clubs and House 2005: The Vocal Session. The digital release this upload comes from is dated 17 May 2007.

## Impact and Reception

We found no critical review of A Style Suite, so we cannot tell you how it was received in print. What the databases do show is how far it travelled in 2005, onto German, Spanish, Italian and Australian dance compilations. Put it on after The Time Is Now and listen for what stayed.
