## Genre and Mood

**Curses** wrote **Surrender** as post-punk for the dance floor, and **Jennifer Cardini** remixed it into something faster in spirit and harder at the edges. This is a remix of a new wave and post-punk song made for electronic DJ sets: energetic on the surface, with the haunting post-punk song still underneath.

## Song Summary

The original Surrender opened Romantic Fiction, Curses' debut album, released by Dischi Autunno on 26 October 2018. Its label says the song broke a long writer's block and set the whole album in motion, carried by a driving bassline, raw percussion and haunting vocals; XLR8R, premiering it, called it a seductive slice of post-punk. On 19 April 2019 the label released the Surrender EP: the original and four reworkings by Djedjotronic, David Vunk, Eva Geist & Steve Pepe, and Cardini, the label's own boss. Hers, 6:43 long, closes the digital release; the vinyl left it off.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts. The Surrender EP shows the range of the form in one place: the label described a dark EBM version by Djedjotronic, a tripped-out take by David Vunk, a stripped-down one with live percussion by Eva Geist & Steve Pepe, and Cardini's version in overdrive.

The original runs on a {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} that its label called driving, and a prominent bass is one of the marks of {{ <concept_link name="post-punk" /> }}, the tense, stripped-down music that followed punk from the late 1970s. Remixing such a song for clubs means deciding what to do with that engine.

## Artistic Context

Curses is Luca Venezia, a New Yorker who once made breaks and basslines as Drop the Lime and moved to Berlin in 2015, the year the Curses alias began. Romantic Fiction, Beatportal wrote, sits between dub-disco and post-punk. Cardini grew up in Monaco, held residencies at Rex Club and Le Pulp in Paris, then moved to Cologne and Berlin; her labels are Correspondant and Dischi Autunno.

## Impact and Reception

No review of this remix was found; its home is the DJ set rather than the press. The Ransom Note later announced his second album, Incarnadine, for the same label. Start with the 4:17 original, then let Cardini's version run.
