## Genre and Mood

**Be Svendsen** opened his 2013 Masquerade EP with **Let Them Not Weep**, six and a half minutes that Discogs files under tech house, and its digital edition under deep house and minimal techno as well. The mood is groovy and dreamy, the way the label, 3000° Grad, describes it: a minimal groove with melodies turning around a voice. No official upload of the track is on YouTube, so this page carries none.

## Song Summary

The label's text is the fullest description of the track. Based on a minimal groove, it says, the Danish producer whirls around tunes and melody samples, and a kaleidoscopic pattern shapes around the swinging voice of a female singer. Discogs credits the writing to Be Svendsen alone and does not name the singer, and neither does any other source. The title, a plea that someone be spared tears, is the only words of the track this review can report.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track section by section; this breakdown keeps to the three elements the label names.

### Groove

The base is a minimal groove, a rhythm with few parts that has to carry six and a half minutes.

### Samples

Around it Be Svendsen sets tunes and melody samples in motion, the label's image of whirling them around the beat.

### Voice

At the centre is a female singer's voice, which the label hears as swinging and around which the samples form their kaleidoscopic pattern.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the felt quality of a rhythm that makes people move. A minimal groove makes that feeling from very few parts, and here it carries six and a half minutes.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} reuses fragments of recordings as material. The label speaks of melody samples rather than played parts: here the fragments become the colours of the track's kaleidoscope.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} stood next to the original from the start. The EP's second track is Mollono.Bass's version of Let Them Not Weep, which Kompakt describes as adding extra push through many small details, and the B-side has Be Svendsen remixing his own Solo Para Mi.

## Artistic Context

Be Svendsen is a deep house and downtempo DJ and producer based in Copenhagen. 3000° Grad, the label in Wustrow, Germany, released Masquerade on 2 September 2013 on 12-inch vinyl, distributed by Kompakt, and digitally, with Leone completing the record. A year later came {{ <track_link source="be-svendsen-circle.md" /> }} on Iboga Records.

## Impact and Reception

The label's pitch put him outdoors: his stylistic open-mindedness, it said, is just perfect for open-air scenarios. The vinyl has since sold out at Kompakt. Find the EP on the label's Bandcamp and listen for the voice inside the pattern.
