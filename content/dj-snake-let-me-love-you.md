## Genre and Mood

**DJ Snake** made **Let Me Love You** a dance record with a ballad's heart. It sits between tropical house and electro-pop: Rolling Stone heard the tropical electro-pop vibe of Justin Bieber's Sorry, and Time heard a summery, tropical EDM track. The mood is longing, and the hook is catchy enough to carry that longing onto a dance floor.

## Song Summary

The song is a plea. Bieber, the featured singer, asks to be allowed to love someone, and Rolling Stone summed it up as lovelorn lyrics over booming synth pads and choppy vocal loops. It was written by DJ Snake (William Grigahcine), Bieber, Andrew Watt, Ali Tamposi, Brian Lee and Louis Bell, produced by DJ Snake with Watt and Bell, and recorded in April 2016 at Studio 713 in Houston. Interscope released it on 5 August 2016 as the third single from DJ Snake's debut album, Encore.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times; the parts below follow what the Singles Jukebox critics heard in August 2016.

### Verses

Bieber sings close and earnest over the pads. Will Adams heard him pushing from the chest instead of just breathing out the words, and Edward Okulicz called the result plaintive.

### Chorus

Brad Shoup heard Bieber pleading and consoling, breaking off a cadence on the chorus that ought to keep the song a hit all summer.

### Drop

Then the track hands over to DJ Snake: filtered bass lines and chopped vocal sounds, which Ryo Miyauchi heard as a comedown follow-up to Lean On. Adams noted the curious choice of making the drop the least hard-hitting part of the song.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit tells you whose record it is. This is DJ Snake's single, from his album, with Bieber as the guest voice: the producer's sound frames the singer, not the other way round.

A {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} usually releases a build with the song's hardest hit. Here, critics noticed, it eases off, and the energy comes from texture and rhythm instead of weight.

That texture is built from a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} of chopped vocal fragments. Shoup heard DJ Snake turn Bieber into a trumpet, the voice cut up and played like an instrument.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by James Lees and released on 29 November 2016, follows an outlaw couple, played by Tommy O'Brien and Emily Rudd, through a high-speed chase and a run-in with a mob boss; Rolling Stone called it Bonnie and Clyde with a twist. The twist is that the story is a virtual-reality game, played by a kid and a grown man.

## Artistic Context

DJ Snake is a French DJ and producer whose earlier hits included Turn Down for What with Lil Jon and Lean On with Major Lazer and MØ. Encore, his first album, also featured Skrillex, Young Thug, Travis Scott and Migos among others. Bieber had just appeared on Major Lazer's Cold Water, and a Singles Jukebox critic noted that the two came out in the same week.

## Impact and Reception

Let Me Love You entered the Billboard Hot 100 at number 12 and Digital Songs at number one, with 113,000 downloads, and climbed to number four. It reached number one in Finland, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Scotland and Switzerland, and won the Radio Disney Music Award for Best Crush Song in 2017. Critics were split: the Singles Jukebox scores ran from 2 to 7 out of 10. Listen for the moment the chorus gives way to the drop and decide whether easing off was the right call.
