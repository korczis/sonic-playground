## Genre and Mood

**Robag Wruhme**'s **K.T.B.** comes from an album of minimal electronic dance music, yet it is the short, sung exception on it. Discogs files the single under house, downtempo and minimal, and its long afterlife on lounge compilations says how listeners took it: relaxed and intimate rather than built for peak time.

## Song Summary

K.T.B. is the seventh of nine tracks on Wuzzelbud KK, released by the Jena label Musik Krause in June 2004. Robag Wruhme wrote and produced the album; on this track the singer Delhia supplies both the voice and the words, which the CD credits to Franziska Grohmann and other editions to Delhia, and the producer Marlow recorded it.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes K.T.B. section by section, so this review gives what the credits and the running time show rather than a map. It is a song with a guest voice inside an album of instrumental club tracks, and at three minutes forty it is one of the shortest pieces there; only the two-and-three-quarter-minute Pelagia is shorter. Listen for how much it does in that time with one voice set into the album's minimal production.

## Concepts in Focus

Delhia is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} in the plainest sense: MusicBrainz lists her as the guest vocalist on a producer's record, and Discogs credits her for the words as well, so the song's lyric is hers, not Wruhme's. On an album otherwise carried by rhythm and texture, her voice is what turns K.T.B. into a song.

The track also shows how a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} single spreads a song. In November 2004 the Berlin label Sonar Kollektiv licensed the original from Musik Krause and released it on a 12-inch with three new versions: the Wighnomy Brothers' Against Moplakk Terror remix, a human beatbox version by Delhia and Mischer, and the Ruhig Brauner remix by Daniel Paul and Zky, which later turned up on Luke Fair's Balance 011 mix. For another track from the same Thuringian circle, hear {{ <track_link source="marek-hemmann-hooray.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Robag Wruhme is Gabor Schablitzki, born in Rudolstadt in 1974 and based in Jena. With Sören Bodner he formed the DJ duo Wighnomy Brothers and in 1998 founded the label Freude am Tanzen; he has released as Robag Wruhme since 2002. According to the German Wikipedia, Wuzzelbud KK, his first LP, earned wide critical praise and made his name in the scene; his first tour of Japan followed in 2005, then South America.

## Impact and Reception

laut.de wrote that hardly a DJ was without Wuzzelbud KK in their record case. K.T.B. travelled further than the club tracks around it: MusicBrainz lists it on compilations such as City Lounge 2, Berlin Lounge and The Best of Lounge Music. Musik Krause put it on YouTube in 2015. Play it late, and let the voice lead.
