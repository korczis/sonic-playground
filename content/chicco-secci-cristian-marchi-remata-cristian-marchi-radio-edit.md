## Genre and Mood

**Chicco Secci** and **Cristian Marchi** made **Remãta** as house for the hours when a dancefloor is still warming up. Spinnin' Records described laid-back drums, sweet melodies and smooth rhythms, with a vibe that makes it hard to keep still, so relaxed and groovy are the feelings this page hears in that description. This review covers Marchi's radio edit, the shorter of the single's two versions.

## Song Summary

Remãta came out on Spinnin' Records on 29 August 2016, introduced by the label as the work of two Italians joining forces on a timeless tune. The digital single holds two versions: Chicco Secci's Tribe Mix, about 3:10, and a second cut of 2:50 that YouTube titles simply Remãta and Charts in France lists as Remãta - Cristian Marchi Radio Edit. The credits name Cristian Marchi, Federico Secci and Paolo Sandrini as writers and producers. No source explains the title or describes a vocal, so this page does not guess at either.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} trims a dance track to a length a radio programmer will play, with a quicker start and fewer repeats. Here the single itself sets the comparison: one version named after each artist, Secci's longer Tribe Mix and this short edit by Marchi.

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady pulse house music is built on. Spinnin's description suggests a relaxed version of it, drums that sit back rather than drive. Compare the brighter, piano-lit groove of {{ <track_link source="axwell-max-c-i-found-u-radio-edit.md" /> }}.

Three names stand behind the credits, which shows how a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} can be invisible on the cover: Paolo Sandrini is not a billed artist, but Panorama reported that he co-runs the label Violence Rec with Marchi.

## Artistic Context

Chicco Secci started as a DJ and radio engineer at Radio Deejay, was a resident at Ibiza's KU from the mid-1980s and moved to Miami in 1993; his productions include Whip of the Rhythm and Tarantella. Cristian Marchi, born in Mantua in 1976, is a progressive house DJ whose breakthrough came with Love, Sex, American Express after the 2007 and 2008 singles Feel the Love and We Are Perfect. I WANT EDM reports that Secci's last release before an eight-year pause came in 2017.

## Impact and Reception

The single left no reviews behind; Spinnin' also gave the Tribe Mix an official music video. Put the radio edit on early in the evening, then play the Tribe Mix after it.
