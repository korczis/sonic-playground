## Genre and Mood

**Imany** and **Filatov & Karas** turn **Don't Be So Shy (Filatov & Karas Remix)** into a sleek meeting of deep house, pop and soul. The mood is alluring and hypnotic, a romantic slow-burn that became a dance-floor record across Europe.

## Song Summary

At its heart the song is an invitation, sung with confidence and a sly smile: a call to drop the hesitation and give in to desire. Imany's voice is the star, a deep, smoky contralto that sounds unhurried even when the beat picks up. The Russian duo Filatov & Karas wrap it in a clean, pulsing house groove with a melodic synth figure that loops like a whispered promise. The remix keeps the intimacy of the original while giving it a body that moves, and at about three minutes it is as compact as a pop single.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix is built on patience: it lets the groove and the voice circle each other rather than chasing a big drop.

### Intro

A soft kick, a rounded bass note and the first hint of the synth motif set a low-lit, sensual scene.

### Verse 1

Imany enters close and confiding, her voice almost spoken in places, the groove staying discreet underneath.

### Chorus

The title line arrives over the full beat, the melody lifting just enough to feel like a dare.

### Main Section

The instrumental hook takes over, a looping, slightly melancholic synth line that gives the track its hypnotic pull.

### Outro

The layers thin out until the voice and bass fade together.

## Concepts in Focus

This is the story of a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} outgrowing its original. Imany's version is a slow, intimate song; Filatov & Karas kept her voice and the melody, added a steady house beat and a synth hook, and made it short enough for radio. That version, not the original, topped the charts in France, Germany, Austria, Poland, Russia and Slovenia and became her biggest hit.

The song began as {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} music. Imany wrote the score for Audrey Dana's 2014 film Sous les jupes des filles, released in English as French Women, and Don't Be So Shy was used in it in two versions, one sung by Sherika Sherard and one by Imany as a work in progress. A placement gave the song its first audience; a remix gave it a continent.

## Artistic Context

Imany, born Nadia Mladjao in Martigues near Marseille to a family from the Comoros, worked as a model in the United States for seven years before starting to sing in 2008; her debut album The Shape of a Broken Heart (2011) went platinum in France and triple platinum in Poland. She wrote Don't Be So Shy with Stéfane Goldman. Filatov & Karas, a Russian DJ and production duo, reworked it in 2015, and the remix later appeared on her 2016 album The Wrong Kind of War, alongside a remix EP. The video on this page is the lyric video released in Britain by Ministry of Sound.

## Impact and Reception

The remix became a pan-European hit and one of the clearest examples of the mid-2010s wave in which a deep-house remix carried a soulful vocal far beyond its original audience. It belongs here because the balance is right: the voice keeps its mystery, and the groove gives it momentum. Put it on late in the evening and let it work slowly.
