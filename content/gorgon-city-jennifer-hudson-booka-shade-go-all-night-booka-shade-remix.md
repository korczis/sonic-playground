## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City**, **Jennifer Hudson** and **Booka Shade** meet in **Go All Night (Booka Shade Remix)**, a deep house reading of a house single. It is electronic dance music of the patient kind, hypnotic and groovy, with Hudson's soulful voice still running through it.

## Song Summary

The original Go All Night, from Gorgon City's 2014 debut album Sirens, cast Jennifer Hudson as a classic club diva; Complex called it one of the album's biggest surprises, a soulful throwback with Hudson channelling her inner house diva. For the single's remix package the German duo Booka Shade made their own version. Complex, posting it after Annie Mac premiered it on BBC Radio 1, heard them lift some of the original's atmosphere, its grooving bassline and well-timed parts of Hudson's vocal, and throw them all into a deeper house cut. The remix runs seven minutes and two seconds.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} decides what to keep. The single's digital remix package carried versions by Wilkinson, Booka Shade, Illyus & Barrientos, Drew Hill and Aquilo, each a different answer to the same material. Booka Shade's answer, in Complex's account, keeps the groove and treats the famous voice as one element among others.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the thread between the two versions. Because the remix keeps it, the song stays recognisable even when the vocal steps back; compare it with {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-jennifer-hudson-go-all-night.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady pulse both versions share. Deep house stretches it out: at seven minutes there is room for a long mix-in and mix-out on either side of the vocal moments.

## Artistic Context

Booka Shade are Walter Merziger and Arno Kammermeier, veterans of the Frankfurt electronic music scene. In 2002 they helped found the label Get Physical, and in 2005 their Body Language with M.A.N.D.Y. became a global club hit. Complex noted they had been making music since the mid-1990s. The remix appeared on the Go All Night remix single in 2014 and again as track six of Sirens (Remixes) in 2015, next to remixes by Weiss, DJ Anna, CLOSE and Deetron.

## Impact and Reception

Complex welcomed the remix, saying the duo's decades of experience shone through in the rework. The original single, meanwhile, reached number 14 in the UK and topped Billboard's Dance Club Songs chart. Give the remix its full seven minutes and let the bassline lead you in.
