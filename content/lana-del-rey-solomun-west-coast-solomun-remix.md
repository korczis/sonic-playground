## Genre and Mood

**Lana Del Rey** and **Solomun**'s "West Coast - Solomun Remix" is deep house with a dark heart: haunting in its low end, dreamy in the voice that floats above it, a song about California carried into a club after midnight.

## Song Summary

The original, the lead single of Ultraviolence in 2014, written by Del Rey and Rick Nowels and produced by Dan Auerbach, is a melancholy love song about a woman torn between love and ambition, famous for a chorus that slows by nearly 60 beats per minute. Del Rey commissioned a whole set of remixes for its release, from Four Tet, MK, Camo & Krooked, Zhu and others; Solomun's was one of them. It runs past eight minutes, and in 2016 it was collected on his Diynamic compilation Selected Remixes 2009-2015.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through this remix section by section, so this breakdown stays with the elements the 2014 coverage describes.

### The Groove

Dancing Astronaut heard Solomun's "thrumming, deep house-rooted beats" colliding with Del Rey's "impossibly sultry vocals", a groove built for a long night rather than for radio.

### The Bass

The same preview describes a thick layer of dark vibrato under her lyric and a sonorous, shifting bass: the remix's weight sits low.

### The Voice

Del Rey stays at the centre, with what the preview calls jazzy additions around her ethereal vocal.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts. Here the vocal stays and nearly everything beneath it is Solomun's, which is why the credit carries two names.

Its foundation is the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}, the dark, shifting bass that Dancing Astronaut singles out, and a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} built to hold a dancefloor. A house track normally keeps one tempo, while the original slows down for every chorus; the sources do not say how Solomun handles that shift, so listen for it yourself. For the producer's touch on another voice, compare {{ <track_link source="noir-haze-solomun-around-solomun-vox.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Solomun, born Mladen Solomun in Travnik and raised in Hamburg, founded the label Diynamic in 2005. His sound has been described as house with deep, funky basslines, euphoric melodies and emotionally charged vocals. His remix of Noir & Haze's "Around" was Resident Advisor's remix of the year in 2011, and in 2012 Mixmag named him DJ of the year.

## Impact and Reception

THUMP picked it among the best remixes of the single, calling it a "spooky house remix" with "real Yuma Tent vibes". Give it the full eight minutes.
