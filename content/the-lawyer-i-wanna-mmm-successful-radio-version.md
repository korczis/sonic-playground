## Genre and Mood

**The Lawyer** released **I Wanna Mmm...** in 1999, and Discogs files every pressing under house and progressive house, the polished club sound of the late 1990s. Dance is where the record travelled: onto radio, into jukeboxes and onto compilations across Europe. Playful and upbeat are a reading of its title, a hum where a word should be; no critic's description of the track could be found.

## Song Summary

**I Wanna MMM... - Successful Radio Version** is the short cut of the single, three and a half minutes against club mixes of six and more. On the Italian maxi-single on Time it sits beside the Successful Mix, the Original Club Mix and the Put Some Tobacco Mix; the European CD licensed to Sony added edits and a dub by Foundation and an edit by Doug Laurent. No source sets out the lyric or the arrangement, so this page does not paraphrase them. The sleeve says the track was "written, rolled, mixed and shaked" at Time Studio 1.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is the version cut for broadcast, and this single shows the system at work: one song, released at once as a short version for radio and long mixes for DJs. Compare {{ <track_link source="chicco-secci-cristian-marchi-remata-cristian-marchi-radio-edit.md" /> }}, a later Italian house record.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands the song to someone else: for the European release, Foundation and Doug Laurent each made their own.

The song's longest life was on the {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}: MusicBrainz lists dozens from 1999 on. One was Hit Mania Dance 2000, mixed by Mauro Miclini in December 1999 and opened by Gigi D'Agostino's Another Way; his {{ <track_link source="gigi-d-agostino-bla-bla-bla.md" /> }} is from the same year.

## Artistic Context

Discogs calls The Lawyer simply an Italian project, with Nick Ferrando and Rossano Prini as members. The sleeves credit writing and mixing to Ferrando, Prini, also known as DJ Ross, and Estefansinho "Mamo DJ"; the UK CD names Prini as producer. According to Italian Wikipedia, Prini became A&R manager for Time Records in 1998, running its Spy Records imprint, and produced I Wanna Mmm there, before later work as DJ Ross and at the radio station m2o.
