## Genre and Mood

**Cléa Vincent** takes on **All that she wants**, the Ace of Base hit, and turns it into a homespun cover of breezy French synth-pop. It is playful and dreamy, steeped in nostalgia for early-nineties radio but delivered with an indie, bedroom-pop lightness that makes it feel brand new.

## Song Summary

The original is a dark little pop song about a restless woman for whom all she wants is another baby, whatever that word means, set to a reggae-pop shuffle that conquered charts worldwide. Vincent keeps the famous melody but softens its strut, replacing the glossy production with warm, slightly lo-fi keyboards, a relaxed drum machine and her own airy voice. The memorable detail is that charm of the handmade: it sounds like a song recorded in an apartment on a lazy Sunday, and that is exactly its appeal.

## Musical Breakdown

The cover follows the original's shape but trades punch for haze.

### Intro

Soft synth chords and a gentle drum-machine pattern establish a sunny, slightly woozy groove.

### Verse 1

Vincent's voice enters light and nonchalant, singing the familiar lines with a wink rather than a sneer.

### Chorus

The well-known hook arrives in a gentle wash of keys, instantly recognisable yet dreamier and more intimate than the original.

### Bridge

A short instrumental passage lets the synths drift, adding a little French-pop melancholy to the mix.

### Climax

The last choruses layer vocals and keyboards for a warm, swaying finale.

### Outro

The groove fades softly, leaving a lingering hum of synths and the urge to put the original on next.

## Artistic Context

Cléa Vincent is a Parisian singer, songwriter and keyboardist associated with the label Midnight Special Records, which operates a small studio in an apartment. In 2014 she released two EPs, Non Mais Oui 1 and 2, mixing re-recorded songs from her earlier work with covers and remixes; her version of Ace of Base's 1992 single All That She Wants appeared on the first, released in March 2014. The project helped establish her as a rising name of the new French pop scene.

## Impact and Reception

The cover became one of the calling cards of Vincent's early career, a playful, affectionate reading that won over listeners who never expected to hear Ace of Base sound this tender. It is here because it shows a great cover can reveal the sweetness hidden inside a familiar hit. Put it on and sing along softly.
