## Genre and Mood

**Team**'s **Reklama na ticho** is Slovak pop rock of the late 1980s, the genre Slovak Wikipedia gives the album alongside hard rock. Its mood today is nostalgic and iconic: the best-known hit of the record that, by Czech daily Deník's account, made the band so popular it overshadowed even the legendary Elán.

## Song Summary

Reklama na ticho, an advertisement for silence, is the second of eleven tracks on Team, the band's 1988 debut album on the label Opus, and runs three minutes and 42 seconds. Slovak Wikipedia credits the music to Július Kinček, who produced the album, and the words to Daniel Hevier; the band's new singer Pavol Habera sings it. The credits on the Opus upload differ, naming Kinček as lyricist and Milan Dočekal as composer, so they are worth reading with care.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the recording part by part, so this breakdown stays with the album's line-up and history.

### Main Section

Listen for a six-piece band: Habera's voice, Dušan Antalík's guitar, Ivan Válek's bass, Ivan Marček's drums and two keyboard players, Dočekal and Bohuš Kantor. The guest Vašo Patejdl, who also programmed a LinnDrum, is credited on other tracks of the album, not on this one.

### Outro

How the song ends is not described in any source. Notice how a closing passage might work as an encore in a hall, where the song has ended concerts for decades.

## Concepts in Focus

After the album's success, Team recorded it all over again in Esperanto, and their new manager Stano Marček, an Esperantist, took them to Esperanto meetings abroad, mainly on the British Isles. That is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} on the scale of a whole record: the same songs and the same band, a different language and a different audience.

A song that stays in a band's set for decades also gathers a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} or two. Team's 2003 concert album Live in Praha includes it, and it was an encore on the band's tours of 2012, 2016 and 2019.

## Artistic Context

Team formed in Martin in 1980 around Antalík and Válek, lost two years to military service and released its first single in 1985. Habera, previously with Avion, joined at the start of 1988, when most of the debut was already prepared. The band won the Zlatý slávik poll in 1989, 1990 and 1991; Habera went on to a solo career. Compare {{ <track_link source="richard-muller-banket-po-schodoch.md" /> }}, another Slovak hit of those years.

## Impact and Reception

According to Deník the debut album sold a quarter of a million copies, with Reklama na ticho, Malá nočná búrka and Nároční as its hits, and sparked a hysteria around the band. English Wikipedia singles out this song in the album's success. Listen to it as the start of that story.
