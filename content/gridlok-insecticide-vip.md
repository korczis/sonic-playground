## Genre and Mood

**Gridlok** made **Insecticide VIP** as drum and bass of the neurofunk kind, the style the American producer's releases are filed under. The feelings are the original's: a bio of Gridlok called Insecticide paranoia-inducing and the creepiest track of 2011. Dark and eerie, the VIP is a variation on that track and keeps its name.

## Song Summary

Insecticide first appeared in December 2011 as the A-side of a 12-inch on Gridlok's own label, Project 51, backed with Dirtball, written and produced by Ryan Powell, Gridlok's real name. The bio describes its cold-blooded, fidgety sounds finding their way into raves across the world. The VIP came out on 22 February 2015 on RAMLIFE Drum & Bass, a RAM Records compilation presented and mixed by the producer Audio, and RAM Records' own upload calls it a RAMLIFE exclusive. As a standalone track it runs 5:12.

### The Original

Cold-blooded and fidgety, in the bio's words: a description of texture and mood rather than of melody.

### The VIP

A VIP mix, by definition, is the producer's own variation: new drops, sounds or rhythms, or a rebuilt arrangement. Play the 2011 original and the 2015 VIP back to back and listen for which of those Gridlok changed.

### In the Mix

On the compilation, the VIP is track nine of the digital release and also part of Audio's continuous 61-minute mix, alongside tracks by Noisia, Mefjus and Ed Rush & Optical.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="vip mix" /> }} is a version a producer makes of his own track. The term has roots in DJ-exclusive dubplate culture and is now often read as Variation In Production: a self-remix rather than a remix by someone else.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} blends tracks into one continuous set. RAMLIFE Drum & Bass gives both: Audio's mix and, on the digital release, every track on its own.

That makes it also a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}: twenty-six tracks by many artists chosen around one label, RAM Records, founded in 1992 by Andy C with his friend Ant Miles.

## Artistic Context

Ryan Powell grew up in Florida's Tampa Bay area and trained as a classical violinist before turning to production in his early teens. He founded Project 51 Recordings in 2003, later based himself in San Francisco, and has released on RAM, Playaz, Commercial Suicide and Moving Shadow. In 2013 Complex called him the main American drum and bass producer and DJ still holding his relevance.
