## Genre and Mood

**Acid Pauli**'s **Requiem for a Loop - Be Svendsen Remix** is house with a light step; Sound Shelter files the remix EP under house, and Be Svendsen works in deep house and downtempo. The mood the release notes describe is groovy and playful: wild caprioles, then a joyful push forward.

## Song Summary

The original is a short piece from Acid Pauli's 2012 debut album mst, which XLR8R heard bouncing with a ragtime skank and xylophone trills, close to early acid-jazz downtempo. Be Svendsen, in the words of the release notes, straps its swaying piano chords to a swinging bass line. The remix first appeared in 2014 as the opening track of his Alchemie EP on 3000grad. This recording is the 5:28 edit, issued in January 2017 on Ouïe beside the full version, and used on the soundtrack of the film Es war einmal Indianerland.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can change a piece's genre while keeping its identity. Be Svendsen keeps the piano that defines Requiem for a Loop and changes what moves beneath it, so a downtempo sketch becomes a house track.

{{ <concept_link name="swing" /> }} is the word both the release notes and the original's critics reach for. XLR8R heard a ragtime skank in the original; the remix answers with a swinging bass line, so the lilt survives the change of genre. Compare the swinging groove of {{ <track_link source="solomun-friends.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} is where this edit found a second life. Acid Pauli wrote the music for İlker Çatak's 2017 film Es war einmal Indianerland, and its soundtrack album on Ouïe includes this edit.

## Artistic Context

Acid Pauli is Martin Gretschmann, born in 1973 in Schongau, the former programmer of The Notwist and the man behind Console. He runs the Berlin label Ouïe with Nico Stojan. Be Svendsen is a Copenhagen producer; the Alchemie EP, remixes of Acid Pauli, Electronic Swing Orchestra, The Micronaut and The Segment, came out the same year as his single {{ <track_link source="be-svendsen-circle.md" /> }}.
