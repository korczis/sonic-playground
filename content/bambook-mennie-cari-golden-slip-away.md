## Artistic Context

Bambook is a Stockholm duo, Eiad Sayegh and Hannes Netzell, whom Resident Advisor called a cut above the average deep house practitioners after their Kindisch single Galopp in July 2014. Mennie is the Italian DJ Francesco Bertelli. Cari Golden is an American vocalist; Amsterdam Dance Event lists her collaborations with Fur Coat, Groove Armada, Pan-Pot and Noir, and calls You and I, her track with Fur Coat, one of the biggest hits of 2012. Beatport's 2014 year-end chart placed her 34th among all electronic artists.

## Impact and Reception

No critic wrote about the single, but its route is documented: in 2015 it appeared on Hed Kandi: Miami, Defected in the House: Miami 2015 and Toolroom Radio presents: In at the Deep End. Play it the way those compilations did, as one long track in a night.
