## Genre and Mood

**Pablo Nouvelle** and **Fiona Daniel**'s **Is It Ok** is downtempo built from samples, music that one profile said might a decade and a half earlier have been termed trip-hop, or more recently chillout. Its mood is melancholic and intimate: Pablo Nouvelle himself described the direction of his songs as a sort of melancholic pop music, and Fiona Daniel sings torchily.

## Song Summary

Is It Ok was Pablo Nouvelle's debut single, released in May 2012. It began as a sample: he wanted to work with the Swiss singer-songwriter Fiona Daniel, a friend of his girlfriend, and when she was not convinced he sampled her own song, sketched his ideas around it and showed her the result. That persuaded her, and they made the track together. Its original, Is It Ok?, appears on her debut album Drowning.

### Main Section

Listen for the voice against the beat. Fiona Daniel sings torchily, and Pablo Nouvelle said his drums are always still quite heavy, a habit from years of making hip-hop beats. Somewhere between the two sits the sampled material from her original song.

## Concepts in Focus

The track is a case of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} turned into a partnership. When the artists he wrote to did not answer, he said, he ended up sampling a lot of the artists he liked; with Fiona Daniel the sample became the pitch that won her over, and German Wikipedia lists the result in her discography as an Is it Ok remix.

That is why she is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} rather than an anonymous sample: her name stands beside his on the single, and she worked with him again on later songs.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was not shot for the song. It was a short film that a friend made, and the music was put to it; one profile heard in it the feel of a stylish, existentially vague French film starring Jean Seberg.

## Artistic Context

Pablo Nouvelle is Fabio Friedli from Bern, a musician and animation-trained film-maker whose alias comes from a love of the French nouvelle vague. He started the project in June 2011. Fiona Daniel, born in Zurich in 1987, released Drowning in 2010. Is It Ok opens his self-titled EP, which MusicBrainz dates to June 2013 on Feraltone. Compare {{ <track_link source="wax-tailor-charlotte-savary-our-dance.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

God Is In The TV introduced him in June 2012 on the strength of this single, and his debut album, All I Need, followed on Armada Music in 2016. Listen to the voice first, then to what is built around it.
