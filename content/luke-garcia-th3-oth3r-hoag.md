## Artistic Context

Luke Garcia grew up in Madrid's club scene, started his career in the early 2000s and made his name in Ibiza, Miami and South America; he was a resident at Pacha Madrid. Th3 Oth3r is his frequent partner. Individually they had released on labels such as Defected, Chapter 24 and King Street Sounds; together on MoBlack, Dantze, Asymmetric Recordings, Omeni and Dear Deer.
