## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** called her 2020 twelve-inch **Joy**, after its A-side, and the feelings on this page, joyful and playful, take her at her word and at her label's: its text for the record ends with coming out laughing, like children. Discogs and Sound Shelter file the record under electro; URSL's own page tags the track leftfield house. No review describes how it sounds, so this page keeps to what the release says.

## Song Summary

Joy runs 6:06 and has side A of the record to itself at 45 rpm; Rain in the Desert, featuring Roderic, and Ice Cream on Ice share side B at 33⅓. URSL released it in August 2020, with a mini-poster. The label's text reads like a postcard in fragments: map coordinates, 26 degrees, a sunny breeze, empty streets, a climb up "the holy mountain of joy", rock bell sounds, a stream of sounds hitting mountains, a temple of joy. It describes a journey, not a track, and no source ties any of its images to a moment in the music.

### Main Section

Six minutes is a club length, room for a DJ to mix in and out. Whether the rock bells of the label's text are in the mix is something only your ears can answer.

## Concepts in Focus

A twelve-inch single has two sides, and the {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} of this one holds its two companion tracks, among them {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-roderic-rain-in-the-desert.md" /> }}. Joy is the title track and takes a side by itself; the record is named after it.

Electro, the genre its listings name, is defined, in Wikipedia's account, by the prominent use of the Roland TR-808 {{ <concept_link name="drum machine" /> }}: synthetic beats and machine-made rhythm, born in the early 1980s from funk and early hip-hop. No source names the machines Pilocka Krach used on Joy, so listen for whether its beat sounds programmed and steady in that tradition.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick and runs her own label, Greatest Hits International; with a grant from Musicboard Berlin she travelled to Detroit in 2016 and connected with the techno collective Underground Resistance, according to her publisher. URSL describes itself as a platform for electronic music founded in 2011 that connects Hamburg and Berlin; it released Joy under the catalogue number URSL030. Earlier in 2020 she and Roderic had given {{ <track_link source="roderic-pilocka-krach-palmenhaus.md" /> }} to a benefit album for the Berlin club Kater Blau.
