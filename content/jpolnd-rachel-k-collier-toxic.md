## Song Summary

Toxic is the fourth of seventeen tracks on #FreeBritney, released on Bandcamp on 12 November 2021, and runs 3:28. Each track is a Britney Spears song reworked by a different act, nineteen female-identifying and non-binary artists in all, according to Grimy Goods. The original Toxic, from Spears's 2003 album In the Zone, was written by Cathy Dennis, Henrik Jonback, Christian Karlsson and Pontus Winnberg; the cover's own producers and players are not credited on its Bandcamp page.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of someone else's song. Toxic is a demanding one to cover: the original won Spears the Grammy for Best Dance Recording, her only Grammy, and its hook is built on strings and a South Asian flavour that a new version has to keep, replace or drop.

The album sits between a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} and a tribute: seventeen acts on one release, which its Bandcamp page tags as a compilation, all singing one artist's songs.

That makes the whole record a kind of {{ <concept_link name="protest song" /> }} built from someone else's songs. It came out on the day a Los Angeles judge ended Spears's conservatorship, which had begun in 2008, and Grimy Goods heard Toxic and Circus as pointing at the media's treatment of her.

## Artistic Context

JPOLND is one of the project names of the songwriter Jessica Vaughn, who told Vydia she started Head Bitch Music in 2019 as an imprint for her own songs; Hypebot describes the company, run with Ryan Vaughn, as helping independent artists, mainly women and LGBTQIA+ creatives. Rachel K Collier is an electronic producer and performer from Swansea who also remixed JPOLND's The End for the label. Hear her earlier work on {{ <track_link source="ryan-blyth_once-twice-feat-rachel-k-collier.md" /> }}.
