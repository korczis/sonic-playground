## Genre and Mood

**Jonas Aden** released **Young God** in March 2016, when, according to EDMTunes, the 19-year-old Norwegian seemed to be everywhere. Apple Music files it as house, and it came out on Future House Music, named after the style he worked in: a high-energy, upbeat branch of house built on its drops and its bass.

## Song Summary

Young God is a dance single in two versions: a 2:54 mix, called Original Mix on YouTube's auto-generated channel and plain Young God on Apple Music, and an extended mix of 3:57. Both came out on 23 March 2016, credited to Jonas Aden as composer, and the label's YouTube upload that day pointed listeners to a free download. No review we found describes the track itself, so this page describes the genre around it and says plainly where the sources stop.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through Young God part by part. What follows is the frame the genre gives it, not a description of the recording.

### Beat

Wikipedia describes future house as typically around 126 to 128 BPM on a 4/4 beat, high in energy and bass-heavy.

### Drop

The genre's signature, in the same description, is a metallic, elastic-sounding drop over frequency-modulated basslines, with a muted melody around it.

### Versions

The extended mix runs about a minute longer than the original mix; how that minute is used, no source says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is the short cut of a dance track, made for radio and playlists. Young God's 2:54 version plays that role, and it is the one Future House Music put on YouTube and streaming services play.

An {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is made for DJs, who need room to blend one record into the next. Listen to both versions in a row and you hear what three minutes leaves out.

{{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" /> }}, a kick drum on every beat, is the foundation of house and of the 4/4 beat Wikipedia attributes to future house. The term was coined by the French DJ Tchami for a 2013 remix, and Oliver Heldens's hits took the style into the mainstream in 2014.

## Artistic Context

In January 2016 EDMTunes described Aden's sound as bouncy future house with grungy synths and a fat bassline, noted his remix of Lean On by Major Lazer and DJ Snake, and reported that Don Diablo had chosen his track Temple for the first Generation HEX EP. Young God followed two months later. Eska's 2023 profile of the same musician, which traces his career back through that Lean On remix, gives his name as Jonas Engelschiøn Mjåset and lists Spinnin' Records, Hexagon and Musical Freedom among his labels.

## Impact and Reception

According to Eska, his music passed 100 million streams and he entered DJ Mag's top 150 in 2020, before he stepped away and returned as the singer-songwriter Aden Foyer; YouTube's auto-generated channel for Young God now carries that name. Play the original mix first, then the extended one.
