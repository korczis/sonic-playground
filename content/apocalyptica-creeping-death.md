## Genre and Mood

**Apocalyptica** play **Creeping Death** as four cellists with no guitars, no drums and no singer, between neoclassical chamber music and symphonic metal. It is intense and dark: A Green Man Review wrote that the band strips the anger from Metallica's music but leaves the darkness, and called this track incredible.

## Song Summary

Creeping Death comes from Metallica's 1984 album Ride the Lightning, written by James Hetfield, Lars Ulrich, Cliff Burton and Kirk Hammett. Its narrator is the Angel of Death, and its subject the tenth plague of Egypt from the Book of Exodus, the death of the firstborn. Apocalyptica recorded it as the sixth of eight covers on their 1996 debut. This page's version comes from the album's 20th-anniversary edition of 22 July 2016: the same 5:08 recording, remastered, which MusicBrainz lists on both the 1996 and 2016 releases.

### Main Section

Eicca Toppinen, Sputnikmusic noted, arranged every song on the album with one formula: three cellos recreate the band and one plays the vocal line. A Green Man Review heard the four playing melody, lead guitar, bass line and rhythm. Teen Ink described this track as blending slower rhythms with hard, heavy cello beats.

### Bridge

Metallica built the Die by my hand section around a riff from Die by His Hand, a song by Hammett's former band Exodus. No source describes how the cellos play it on record; reporting a 2017 concert, Super Conductor noted that the audience still shouted its Die.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} can keep every note and change every sound. Here a thrash song becomes chamber music for four bowed instruments, and its words simply disappear.

The song is driven by a {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }}, a short repeated figure; its most famous one even came from another band. When the riffs move to cellos, listen for how much of their bite survives the bow.

Finally, {{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }}: a remaster processes an existing recording again. That is what happened to Creeping Death in 2016, while {{ <track_link source="apocalyptica-battery.md" /> }} on the same album was a new recording.

## Artistic Context

Apocalyptica began as four classically trained cellists from Helsinki's Sibelius Academy: Max Lilja, Antero Manninen, Paavo Lötjönen and Eicca Toppinen. After a 1995 show, a label employee invited them to record, and Mercury released the album on 10 May 1996. It went platinum in Finland and gold in Germany and Poland.

## Impact and Reception

Super Conductor remembered the debut blazing through Creeping Death with gusto and glee; Get Ready to ROCK! called the reissue a worthy return to the band's roots. Metallica's original is among their most loved songs. Hear it first, then this.
