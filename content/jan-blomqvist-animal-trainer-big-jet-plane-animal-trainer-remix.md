## Genre and Mood

**Jan Blomqvist** sings and **Animal Trainer** remix on **Big Jet Plane (Animal Trainer Remix)**, a deep house reworking of a much-loved folk song. As a remix it keeps the original's tenderness but sets it in motion, and the mood is melancholic, dreamy and full of longing, the sound of watching city lights recede from a night-time window seat.

## Song Summary

Big Jet Plane is a song about wanting to fly away with someone, a gentle fantasy of escape. Blomqvist's version keeps his hushed, close-miked vocal at the centre, and Animal Trainer wrap it in a rolling, late-night groove: warm sub bass, soft percussion and pads that swell and recede like breathing. The memorable detail is the contrast between the fragile voice and the steady, propulsive beat beneath it.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix runs well over six minutes and takes its time getting airborne.

### Intro

A soft kick and gentle shakers establish a patient pulse, with airy pads hovering above.

### Build-up

The bassline rises out of the mix and the percussion tightens, a slow taxi down the runway.

### Verse 1

Blomqvist's voice enters, intimate and slightly weary, floating over the groove rather than riding it.

### Chorus

The familiar melody of Big Jet Plane lifts on swelling chords, the moment the remix feels truly in flight.

### Bridge

The beat falls away and the vocal hangs in reverb, a quiet, almost weightless passage.

### Climax

Drums and bass return with more weight, and layered synths push the emotion to its peak without breaking the hypnotic flow.

### Outro

The arrangement thins until only the pulse and a final vocal echo remain, like a plane disappearing into cloud.

## Artistic Context

Big Jet Plane was written and first recorded by Angus Stone and later became one of Angus & Julia Stone's best-known songs. Jan Blomqvist, a Berlin-based singer and producer celebrated for his live electronic performances, made it his own with a brooding electronic cover. Animal Trainer, a production duo who made their Anjunadeep debut in 2015, delivered this remix, which also appears on the compilation Faze #53: Animal Trainer.

## Impact and Reception

The remix became a regular in melodic house and deep house sets, a favourite for sunrise and closing slots where a recognisable song can gather a whole crowd. It is on this playground because it bridges folk intimacy and club hypnosis so gracefully. Listen to it on a night flight, or just imagine one.
