## Genre and Mood

**Gang Starr**'s **Full Clip** is hip-hop of the old New York kind, rap as a duo of one MC and one DJ-producer. The mood is defiant and confident: Metro Silicon Valley called it "an angry newborn" that slanders sellouts, and MTV heard Guru playing the role of hip-hop's wise man.

## Song Summary

Full Clip was one of the new songs recorded at D&D Studios in Manhattan in 1999 for the double compilation that took its name, released as its lead single on 8 June 1999. Guru condemns those who rap "childish nonsense" and lie about who they really are, without naming names. DJ Premier and Guru produced it. The memorable detail comes before the first verse: Premier shouting "Big L, rest in peace" for the Harlem rapper shot dead that February.

## Musical Breakdown

The sources describe the song's opening, its words and one element of its beat; this breakdown keeps to those.

### Intro

On the album, an excerpt from a March 1999 tribute concert for Big L leads in, with the audience chanting in call and response. MTV placed Premier's shout in the seconds before Full Clip; Premier says he put it at the start.

### Verse 1

Guru takes the stand as an elder of the form, aiming at rappers who fake their lives and their moves.

### Main Section

Under him, MTV heard DJ Premier offer a jazz-inflected guitar strum. No source we could find names the record behind it or describes the arrangement further, so listen for what repeats and what is cut in.

## Concepts in Focus

Premier's dedication is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }}. He told Music Week that when they got the news of Big L's death he decided to say "Big L rest in peace" at the start, because every DJ would cut that part up and play it. A phrase written for scratching becomes part of every DJ set that uses it; The Vinyl Factory calls that intro a DJ favourite and its writer's double copies worn out.

Premier's craft is also built on {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Exclaim!, reviewing the compilation, charted his growth from early jazzy sounds to arguably hip-hop's best sonic auteur, "consistently underscored by his precise scratching and obscure sampling". Which records lie under Full Clip, none of our sources says.

## Artistic Context

Guru, born Keith Elam in Boston, and DJ Premier, born Christopher Martin and raised in Texas, came together as Gang Starr in New York in 1988. Full Clip: A Decade of Gang Starr, out on Virgin on 13 July 1999, gathered their singles from 1989 to 1998 with three new songs; Premier said they made Full Clip the day after Big L died, a friend they had just been hanging out with.

## Impact and Reception

The compilation reached number 33 on the Billboard 200 and went gold within weeks. The Vinyl Factory calls Full Clip still a party starter and probably the group's best-known song, and Premier says it always drives the crowd crazy live. Turn it up from the first second: the dedication is part of the song.
