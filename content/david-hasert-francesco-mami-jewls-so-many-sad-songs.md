## Genre and Mood

**David Hasert**, **Francesco Mami** and **Jewls** made **So Many Sad Songs** a house track in the long form: about seven and a half minutes in its original mix, which Apple Music files under house. The title promises melancholy; Hasert's own sound, as Electronic Groove describes his sets, is energetic and dreamy at once, and a sad song for the dance floor sits right between those words.

## Song Summary

The music is credited to Hasert and Mami, and the words to Jewls, who sings them as the featured voice. The track came out on 27 October 2017 on Dantze, Baby II, a ten-track compilation from the Dantze label. In February 2018 Hasert's channel added an official video, produced by Klein + Frank, in a version of 4:50.

The original mix runs about 7:39, the length of a track made for DJs, with room to blend in and out.

A vocal with written lyrics makes this a song as well as a club track, and the title phrase is the line to wait for.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit tells you whose voice you are hearing and who the track belongs to. Here the producers come first and Jewls, who wrote the lyrics, is the guest, the same arrangement as in {{ <track_link source="oliver-koletzki-fran-hypnotized.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} usually gathers old recordings, but a label compilation can also be where new music first appears. Dantze, Baby II gathered ten tracks by different artists, and MusicBrainz lists no earlier release of this one: the compilation was its first home.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} also meant a new edit: a club track nearly eight minutes long became a sub-five-minute film credited to Klein + Frank.

## Artistic Context

David Hasert is a DJ and producer based in Cologne, where he moved from the Sauerland in the early 2000s; he founded the label LIKE Records and became the face of the club Reineke Fuchs. He has released on Beats In Space, Stil vor Talent, Kompakt (as DAMH, with Michaela Dippel) and Dantze. Before electronic music he played in bands, and he names Andrew Weatherall, DJ Koze and James Holden as early influences because their music has emotion. Resident Advisor lists Dantze as a Berlin label founded in 2006.
