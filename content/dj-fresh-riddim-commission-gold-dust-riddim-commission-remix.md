## Genre and Mood

**DJ Fresh** made **Gold Dust** as drum and bass, and **Riddim Commission** remade it as **Gold Dust (Riddim Commission Remix)** for the 2012 single. This page files it under remix and electronic music rather than a narrower style, because no source describes how this version sounds. The feelings, energetic and carefree, belong to the song: a dance record whose video is all skipping ropes, skateboards and free running.

## Song Summary

Gold Dust has lived many lives. It began on 16 August 2008 as an instrumental 12-inch on Breakbeat Kaos. On 1 August 2010 it came back with a vocal by Ce'cile and reached number 24 in the UK. In 2012 a version with Ms. Dynamite went onto DJ Fresh's album Nextlevelism, and in December Ministry of Sound released Shy FX's re-edit as a single, with remixes by TC, WestFunk & Steve Smart, Kutz, Riddim Commission and Max Polyphonic. Ministry of Sound put this remix on its YouTube channel on 19 November 2012, about two weeks ahead.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to another producer, and a remix package is a way to send one song into several scenes at once. Dancing Astronaut heard Shy FX's lead version turn Gold Dust into drum and bass, jungle and even reggae; Riddim Commission came from somewhere else. Insomniac's 2017 profile describes the duo's music as a meeting of grime and house, built on big drums and a hip-hop attitude to house, with Kenny Dope and Todd Terry among their heroes.

Gold Dust is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: instrumental, Ce'cile vocal, Ms. Dynamite vocal, re-edit, five remixes. Compare this one with {{ <track_link source="dj-fresh-kutz-gold-dust-kutz-full-vocal-mix.md" /> }} from the same single.

## Artistic Context

DJ Fresh is Dan Stein from Worthing, a member of the drum and bass group Bad Company, with two UK number ones from Nextlevelism behind him by the end of 2012. Riddim Commission are Marco Del Horno and Swerve. Insomniac tells how they began: Black Butter Records signed Marco's tune Ho Riddim and asked Swerve for a version, which Marco liked better than his own, so the two made the project a joint one. Swerve is a radio DJ; Marco manages bass acts. Their debut album, Riddim.Bass.Life, followed in 2017.
