## Genre and Mood

**Connor Price** and **Nic D** released **Too Easy** under hip-hop and rap, the genre their release carries. The title promises confidence, and the way Price sells his music, through comic skits with his friends and family on TikTok, suggests the playful side; Billboard Canada calls his songs clean and catchy. No review describes how this particular track sounds, so this page keeps to what the credits and the release tell.

## Song Summary

Too Easy came out as a single on 24 January 2025, with its official video posted on Price's channel the same day. Four months later it opened ICONIC, an eight-track EP that Price and Nic D released on 30 May 2025. The song lasts two minutes and thirteen seconds. Its credits are short too: production by 100 Graham, mixing and mastering by Christian Sagum, and the copyright held by Price's own company, 4 of Clubs LLC, together with FRDi Entertainment.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in rap often builds the whole instrumental. Here the producer credit goes to 100 Graham, and Billboard Canada's profile names GRAHAM as the producer Price works with. Whatever you hear under the voices is the producer's part of the song.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" text="Mixing" /> }} is the next step: balancing those parts and the voices into one finished track, then mastering it for release. The credit goes to Christian Sagum, and Billboard Canada describes Price's brother-in-law Christian as the person who mixes and masters his music, part of a team built on friends and family.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was shot by Cakes Mitchell, its credits say. For Price, video is part of the work rather than an afterthought: Billboard Canada reports that he often edits his own videos and studies how long viewers stay with them.

## Artistic Context

Price, born in 1994 in Markham, Ontario, began as a child actor, in Cinderella Man and later the series Being Human and X Company, before turning to rap. His Spin the Globe series, collaborations with rappers from wherever a spun globe landed, made his name on TikTok. He releases mostly through his own label and says independence gives him ownership and creative control. Nic D is his most frequent collaborator: the two made the 2023 mixtape Till Next Time, and in 2026 the duo album Paperboys.
