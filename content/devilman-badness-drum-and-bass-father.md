The mood is energetic and confident, and the title says how confident, a man naming himself the genre's father.

The track appears on Devilman's State of Mind, released in 2014 on SIKA Records, the label Devilman co-runs; the streaming upload gives the release date as 10 January 2014 and a running time of 3:41. The credits name Devilman under his real name, spelled Mickell Edwards there, as lyricist and Jay Barrett as composer, and bill the producer, DJ Looney, as an artist beside the MC. A music video filmed and edited by the YouTube channel JDZmedia came out in August 2014 under the title Devilman ft. Badness.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} in British club music grew out of the MC, who hosts and hypes over a DJ's records. Grime and drum and bass move at different speeds, and an MC crossing from one to the other has to find a flow for the faster beat. Compare two MCs sharing a drum and bass beat in {{ <track_link source="bru-c-mr-traumatik-tsuki-wavey-one.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit varies from listing to listing: the video names Badness as the guest, while the streaming upload credits Devilman and DJ Looney.

That second credit shows how a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in electronic music is often billed like a performer: the person who built the beat stands next to the voice. In 2021 a track called Drum & Bass Father vip 2, credited to Dj Looney Uk, appeared on the release Blast from the Past 2.

## Artistic Context

Devilman is Mikel Edwards, a grime MC born in Birmingham. He started in the Vortex crew, then joined Dark Elements, and with Deadly and Mayhem was among the first Birmingham grime artists on BBC 1Xtra and among the first grime MCs from outside London. A 2006 clash with Skepta on Lord of the Mics made him widely known. His albums include Devilman's Diary (2012), and in 2021 he released Future Proof.

## Impact and Reception

NTS Radio, which has played Devilman since January 2015 on shows including Slackk's, lists the track among his songs it has broadcast, and the title has outlived the release: a December 2026 night with Devilman and Logan D is billed as Drum & Bass Father Xmas.
