## Genre and Mood

**FM Belfast** made **I Can Feel Love** the third track of their debut album, and it is electro-pop with an indie pop heart: playful and joyful, carried by two voices and a cowbell this song made its own. It aims at the dance floor without taking itself too seriously.

## Song Summary

"I Can Feel Love" runs three and a half minutes on How to Make Friends, released in 2008 on World Champion Records and later in Iceland by Kimi Records and abroad by Morr Music. It is credited to Árni Rúnar Hlöðversson, Árni Vilhjálmsson and Lóa Hlín Hjálmtýsdóttir, who also produced it. Reviewers singled it out briefly: Music Ravings said it makes even the cowbell hip, and Incendiary heard in it, as in Lotus and Par Avion, something of the Eltons or Scissor Sisters.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track part by part. These are the elements the reviews name, some of them for the album as a whole.

### The Beat

A dance pulse with the cowbell Music Ravings singled out, a sound often played for laughs.

### The Voices

A man's and a woman's voice, Árni Rúnar and Lóa, the band's founding duo; Boom Boom Chik heard their harmonies across the album as spot on.

### The Chorus

The hook of the song. Music Ravings found the basic quintessence of joy in almost every chorus of the album.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="vocal harmony" /> }} is the band's simplest tool. Two voices singing different notes at once make a chord of their own, and here a male and a female voice give a simple arrangement width; Boom Boom Chik wrote that the pairing added depth to the simple music underneath.

{{ <concept_link name="mastering" /> }} is the last stage of making a record, normally left to a specialist. According to Wikipedia, FM Belfast recorded the album in New York and Iceland and did the recording, mixing and mastering themselves, along with the artwork. Music Ravings wished for more post-production layering; the homemade finish is part of the record's character.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} parts are light rather than lavish. Incendiary traced the album's plinky synths back to the Human League.

## Artistic Context

FM Belfast began in Reykjavík in late 2005 as a duo of Árni Rúnar Hlöðversson, also known as Plúseinn, and Lóa Hlín Hjálmtýsdóttir. Árni Vilhjálmsson and Örvar Þóreyjarson Smárason, also of múm, joined, and the studio project became a live act at Iceland Airwaves in 2006, with anywhere from three to eight members on stage. For the scene around them, try {{ <track_link source="gusgus-over.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The song travelled beyond the record: in October 2009 the band played it into Frequency at RÚV during Iceland Airwaves, broadcast by KEXP in Seattle, and Morr Music put it on The Singles Club in 2013. Play it loud and wait for the cowbell.
