## Genre and Mood

**SBTRKT** and **Little Dragon** met on **Wildfire**, a 2011 single that sits where electronic music, UK garage and dubstep blurred into what the press then called post-dubstep. SoulBounce used the buzzword and added that such labels are irrelevant; it heard a storming slice of futuristic soul. The mood is sultry and groovy, with an edge: Alternative Magazine called it a mean groove and heard Yukimi Nagano singing with an edgy attitude.

## Song Summary

The song is built as a conversation. Beats Per Minute described a synth-heavy track whose bassline and vocal trade phrases, and argued that the vocal melody is just as necessary as the instrumental one. SBTRKT keeps the voice sparse and nearly unprocessed, which, in that review's words, gives the electronics a sense of human feeling. Aaron Jerome wrote it with Nagano, Erik Bodin and Fredrik Källgren Wallin of Little Dragon and with Arild Werling, and produced it alone.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times, so the parts below follow what the reviews describe rather than a timeline.

### Intro

Nagano's voice cuts in during the first few bars, a snappy interjection Alternative Magazine put down to the influence of James Blake.

### Main Section

The beat draws on Detroit techno, the same review says, while a rising synth line borrows from acid house. Over it the bass and the voice take turns, neither one simply accompanying the other.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} usually adds a voice to a producer's track; here the credit names a whole band. Nagano is the voice, but three members of Little Dragon are among the writers, so the feature is closer to a co-write than a guest spot.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the other lead. Listen for the bass as a melody that answers the singer, not as a floor beneath her: that exchange is what Beats Per Minute singled out.

The single paired the original with a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} on which Drake added a verse, released on 23 May 2011 a month before the album. FACT reported that he had jumped on the track, with Nagano still on it.

## Artistic Context

SBTRKT is Aaron Jerome Foulds from Cambridgeshire, who used the name and a series of ceremonial-style masks to stay anonymous; he has said he would rather let the music speak for itself. Before the album he remixed M.I.A., Radiohead, Basement Jaxx and Underworld. Little Dragon, from Gothenburg, had appeared on Gorillaz' Plastic Beach in 2010 and released Ritual Union in July 2011. Wildfire is the third track of SBTRKT's self-titled debut, released by Young Turks on 27 June 2011, whose other voices included Sampha and Jessie Ware. Sam Pilling directed the video.

## Impact and Reception

Alternative Magazine picked it as the album's likeliest radio track; Beats Per Minute called it particularly stunning. The album scored 76 on Metacritic, won DJ Magazine's album of the year, placed 42nd on Pitchfork's best albums of 2011 and was certified silver in the UK. Listen for the moment the bass stops leading and the voice answers.
