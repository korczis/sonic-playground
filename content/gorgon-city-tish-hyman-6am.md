## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** and **Tish Hyman** made **6AM** the hour the club has not yet let go. It is house, close to the deep house of the duo's other album tracks, but harder at the edges: electronic, groovy and built for the dancefloor, with a vocal that is sultry and longing at once. The title says when it is meant to be heard.

## Song Summary

6AM is the tenth of thirteen tracks on Sirens, Gorgon City's debut album of October 2014, and it runs 3:51. It was written by the duo, Kye Gibbon and Matthew Robson-Scott, with Latisha Hyman, the Bronx singer and rapper who performs it as Tish Hyman. Writing for Broadly when the album came out, Zel McCarthy called it classic vocal house, with a bossy bassline and a sultry vocal, and heard in it the ambiguity of longing and late-night sexiness.

Around the voice, the track runs on its bassline, with synth fills and bright, high percussion on top. Gibbon and Robson-Scott produced, programmed the drums, engineered and mixed it themselves, according to the credits of the official upload.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited beside the main act. Sirens is built around changing guest voices, from MNEK to Katy Menditta and Maverick Sabre; here the guest is also a co-writer, so the words and the delivery are hers.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} carries much of this track, and McCarthy's word for it, bossy, is a good guide: listen for how it leads rather than sitting politely under the song.

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} is rhythmic speech over a beat; in Hyman's verse it alternates with singing, so listen for where one turns into the other. Compare the guest singing on {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-hard-on-me.md" /> }}, which closes the same album.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City is the North London duo of Kye "Foamo" Gibbon and Matt "RackNRuin" Robson-Scott. In 2014 they had UK hits with Ready for Your Love, which reached number four, and Here for You, which reached number seven; Sirens, released by Virgin EMI and Black Butter, reached number 10 on the UK Albums Chart. 6AM was not one of the album's singles. Hyman, from the Bronx, had drawn attention the year before with a cover of Killing Me Softly.

## Impact and Reception

Reviewing Sirens for EarMilk, Steel Hanf called 6AM the clubbiest track on the album and a relatable anthem for late-night partiers. The same piece quotes Gibbon saying he was quite proud of it, because it represents the clubbier, dirtier vibe of the record. Play it late, as the title asks.
