## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz** wrote the song and **Hot Chip** rebuilt it: **Kids with Guns (Hot Chip Remix)** is electronic music and indie dance made from an art-pop original, and as a remix it changes the mood more than the words. Where the Demon Days track is cold and haunting, critics hear this one as hypnotic, atmospheric and, surprisingly, joyful.

## Song Summary

Damon Albarn wrote Kids with Guns after a child at his daughter's school brought a knife in to show his friends, and the original version, with Neneh Cherry, sets that story to a clanking, mechanical groove. Hot Chip keep Albarn's performance, which the release credits, and by the critics' accounts change much of what surrounds it. Electronic Beats called their version a faster rework that leans into the band's pop and house vocabularies, a joyful reimagining of a serious original.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the remix part by part, and at just over seven minutes it is longer than most of the descriptions of it. What critics single out is the texture, not the structure.

### Main Section

Upbeat synth stabs and indie rock guitar take the place of the original's melancholy, lackadaisical riffs, in Electronic Beats' words. For Klaverson, the electronic artist who picked it for Clash, soft synth layers, 808-style drums and a driving rhythm build a soundscape that is constantly in motion.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can tidy a song for the radio or take it apart. This one keeps the song's words and voice and rewrites its emotional colour: the same lyric about violence becoming ordinary now moves over something bright and danceable, and the contrast is what makes it unsettling in a new way. Play the original first, then this.

The drums are described as 808-style, a reference to the {{ <concept_link name="roland tr-808" /> }}, the 1980 drum machine whose booming kick, snappy snare and cowbell run through electro, hip-hop and everything since. No source names the machine Hot Chip actually used; the term describes the sound, not the gear.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} does two jobs here: short, upbeat stabs that push the rhythm, and the soft layers underneath that make the long track feel, in Klaverson's words, like it pulls you deeper the longer you listen.

## Artistic Context

Hot Chip formed in London around Alexis Taylor and Joe Goddard and by 2006 were signed to EMI in the UK and DFA in the US; their album The Warning was shortlisted for that year's Mercury Prize. The remix first appeared on the Kids With Guns / El Mañana remix single in April 2006, carries a 2006 Parlophone copyright, and was collected on disc two of D-Sides, the 2007 set of Demon Days B-sides and remixes.

## Impact and Reception

D-Sides reached number two on the US dance albums chart, and the remix turned up again on The Inbetweeners soundtrack in 2009. Nearly two decades later it still makes lists of the best Gorillaz remixes. Give it the full seven minutes.
