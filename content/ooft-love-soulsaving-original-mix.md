## Genre and Mood

**OOFT!** made **Love & Soulsaving (Original Mix)** for the slow end of the dance floor. He files it under deep house, disco and house, and adds two more tags that say how it moves: pitch down and slomo. The mood is groovy and, as the title promises, soulful, a title about love and saving souls on a track in no hurry to get anywhere.

## Song Summary

Love & Soulsaving is side A, track 1 of The Pitch Down Jamz EP, a 12-inch catalogued FOTO-007 on Foto Recordings and released on 1 October 2012. The EP gathers four producers, OOFT! beside La Tuerie, Luvless and Cole Medina, and its title echoes the tag OOFT! gives his own track: pitch down. No review describes the track in detail; what we know of its sound comes from its maker's own tags and from how he works.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is everything in a slow-motion house track. When a record is pitched down, it gets slower and lower at once, and a groove that would race at full speed starts to sway. OOFT! has described the method on another track: "playing it a lot slower and pitched down than the original source." Listen for that heavy, unhurried swing here.

The Special Intro Version is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same track by the same artist, offered only on Bandcamp beside the digital copy of the 12-inch. Comparing the two shows how much a change at the start can shift the way a track lands.

## Artistic Context

OOFT! is Ali Herron, a DJ and producer from Glasgow who runs Foto Recordings and has held residencies in some of the city's best clubs. Bolting Bits calls him a "Glasgow house music veteran" with "low-slung deep house leanings" and numerous edits; The Skinny calls them his "signature disco-heavy edits", and he speaks of moving on "from my early disco editing days" towards more original work. His debut album, Intricacies of Modern Life, followed in 2018.

## Impact and Reception

Love & Soulsaving is the track that opens its label's various-artists 12-inch, and it has stayed in circulation on OOFT!'s own Bandcamp. For a disco-flecked deep house cousin, try {{ <track_link source="session-victim-we-want-to-thank-all-our-friends.md" /> }}. Play this one slow and late.
