## Genre and Mood

**JANIS**'s **Hypnotized** is electro-pop: NOWNESS presented it in 2015 as the electro-pop debut of a Paris-based producer. The title sets the mood of this page, hypnotic and a little mysterious, and the video took the title literally.

## Song Summary

Hypnotized was a comeback. The French singer Janis Sahraoui, born in Saint-Étienne in 1988, had been known as Sliimy, and in the spring of 2015 returned under a new name with this single. MusicBrainz dates the release to 13 March 2015 on Y&I Records and lists the track at 4:08, the audio version this page uses. Sahraoui performed it on the French television show Le Grand Journal on 5 May 2015.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }} is the name and image under which an artist performs. Sahraoui has had three: Sliimy until 2015, Yanis from 2015 to 2023, and Janis since 2023, which is why this 2015 single, first released as Yanis, is credited to JANIS today. Each name marks a stage of the music too: French Wikipedia describes the move from the pop of the Sliimy years to the electro-pop of the Yanis records.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Ludovic Zuili and produced by Instant T and Frenzy Paris, filmed dancers under the influence of hypnosis; its credits name a hypnotist, Julian, and a cast that includes the actress Charlotte Le Bon. NOWNESS premiered it under the headline that dancers lose control in it.

The video runs longer than the 4:08 recording, and the artist's channel published both, the clip and an audio upload. That makes each an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} of the release, and the yearbook's copy is the audio one.

## Artistic Context

As Sliimy, Sahraoui shared songs on Myspace in the late 2000s, signed with Warner and released the album Paint Your Face in April 2009; a cover of Britney Spears's Womanizer drew attention after Perez Hilton featured it, and Sliimy opened for Britney Spears in Paris and toured with Katy Perry in 2009. After Hypnotized came Crave later in 2015 and L'Heure Bleue in February 2016, the first record under the name Yanis. In 2023 Sahraoui played Orlando in Paul B. Preciado's film Orlando, My Political Biography.

## Impact and Reception

Hypnotized was a fresh start more than a hit: NOWNESS, a channel for short films, gave its video a premiere, and the single opened the road to L'Heure Bleue. Let it play twice, once without the pictures.
