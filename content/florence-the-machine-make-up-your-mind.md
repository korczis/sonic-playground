## Genre and Mood

**Florence + The Machine** added **Make Up Your Mind** to the deluxe edition of their third album. It belongs to the band's alternative and indie pop, with the art-rock reach of the album around it. Its feelings here, emotional and introspective, come from what Florence Welch said about that album, because no review found for this page describes the song itself.

## Song Summary

Make Up Your Mind runs 4:01 and is credited to Florence Welch and Tom Hull, better known as Kid Harpoon. Charlie Hugall produced and mixed it, with Kid Harpoon as co-producer. The credits are the clearest picture of how it is built: Orlando Leopard plays piano, bass, harmonium and organ and is named co-arranger, Kid Harpoon adds acoustic guitar and piano, Rob Ackroyd a second acoustic guitar, and Hugall the percussion. Welch sings the lead and the backing vocals.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }} is a song added to one edition of an album. The deluxe How Big, How Blue, How Beautiful added two finished new songs, Hiding and Make Up Your Mind, and demos of Which Witch, Third Eye and the title track. The two new songs came from different teams: {{ <track_link source="florence-the-machine-hiding.md" /> }} was written with and produced by James Ford.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes how a song sounds, and Markus Dravs produced most of the album. This song has Hugall at the desk and Kid Harpoon as co-producer, an old partner of Welch's: he co-wrote Shake It Out, Never Let Me Go and Leave My Body with her for Ceremonials in 2011.

An {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} decides who plays what and when. Here it is, on paper, three kinds of keyboard and two acoustic guitars, with Leopard as co-arranger: test that against what you hear.

## Artistic Context

How Big, How Blue, How Beautiful came out on Island on 29 May 2015, recorded in London and Los Angeles. Welch said its songs dealt with reality rather than escape, in a turbulent time for her; it is more stripped-down than the band's earlier records and draws on folk, blues and gospel. Kid Harpoon later co-wrote and produced Harry Styles's Grammy-winning Harry's House.

## Impact and Reception

Make Up Your Mind was never a single, and no review found for this page discusses it. The album around it went to number one in the UK and on the Billboard 200 and earned five Grammy nominations.
