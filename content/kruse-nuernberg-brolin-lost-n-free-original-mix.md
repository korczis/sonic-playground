## Genre and Mood

**Kruse & Nuernberg** made **Lost N Free** with the British singer **Brolin**, and THUMP called it a gorgeous serving of deep house. It is deep house built around a voice. The feelings named here, introspective and uplifting, are the label's own words: OFF Recordings hears lyrics and melody swirling across an uplifting instrumental and calls the track the soundtrack for introspective, early-morning moments.

## Song Summary

Lost N Free is the title track and A side of the Lost N Free EP, catalogue number OFF109, released by OFF Recordings in March 2015. The label credits Brolin, a British producer and songwriter, with writing the vocal, which it calls heart-melting; THUMP heard it as melty and sensual. The EP also holds Doin It, its B side, and a remix of the title track by Sasse and ED ED. No lyric source says what the song is about beyond its title, so this page does not guess.

### Main Section

A vocal that melts into the mix, an uplifting instrumental under it, and a glow THUMP heard in the production. The original mix runs about six and a quarter minutes, long enough for DJs to blend it in and out.

## Concepts in Focus

Brolin is the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: credited after the duo, but carrying the song as its writer and singer. The pairing worked. When the duo followed it later in 2015 with Yokohama Nights on Exploited, Kruse told THUMP that their first collaboration had gone extremely well and that Brolin has a very special sound in his voice.

Doin It is the EP's {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }}, and the label says it takes the release underground with pulsing synths and piano stabs that build over contagious basslines and swung drums. Hearing it after the title track shows one EP serving two moods.

THUMP premiered the {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} in February 2015 and praised its soft palette of pastel pinks, blues and greens, which it said mirrors the duo's glowing production. The video uses a cut of about four minutes, not this full original mix.

## Artistic Context

Florian Kruse and Nils Nuernberg grew up in a rural area between Kiel and Hamburg and both studied audio engineering; Nuernberg came from drum and bass, Kruse from hip-hop and deep house. By 2015 they worked between Hamburg and Berlin, and OFF Recordings, the established German label they had a close relationship with, called them part of the underground electronic scene, known for tastefully crafted house.

## Impact and Reception

No chart entry is documented for the EP. The label published feedback from DJs: Jonas Rathsman called it a wicked release, Purple Disco Machine said he loved the EP, and Veerus & Maxie Devine wrote that Lost N Free is amazing.
