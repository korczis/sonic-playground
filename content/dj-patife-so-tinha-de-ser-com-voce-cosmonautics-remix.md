## Genre and Mood

**DJ Patife** placed the Cosmonautics remix of **Só Tinha de Ser Com Você** fourth on Cool Steps, his 2001 mix album, and it sits exactly where drum and bass, bossa nova and Brazilian song overlap. The mood is groovy and relaxed: a Cliquemusic reviewer heard Patife favouring subtler, less frenetic grooves that still work on a dance floor, and named this track as a good example.

## Song Summary

The song is by Antônio Carlos Jobim and Aloysio de Oliveira, recorded by Elis Regina and Jobim on Elis & Tom in 1974. In 2001 three Brazilian producers, DJ Marky, DJ Patife and ESOM, recut it for drum and bass with the singer Fernanda Porto, and the remixer Cosmonautics reworked their version. The review describes the result as bossa nova guitar and Porto's subtle vocal blended with a supersonic beat. Some releases spell the title Só Tinha Que Ser Com Você.

### Main Section

Three layers carry the track in the review's account: the bossa nova guitar, the quiet voice and the fast beat under both. On The Brazilian Job, the UK 12-inch that also carries this remix, the original mix credits Ulysses Rocha on acoustic guitar and Benjamim Taubkin on Rhodes; the Cosmonautics mix credits only the remixer and Mad Zoo for additional production, so which of those parts survive is for your ears to judge.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} of a {{ <concept_link name="cover version" text="cover" /> }}, two steps away from Jobim. The Brazilian Job, released on Movement in 2001, put the Original Lounge Mix on one side and the Cosmonautics Mix on the other, billed as an exclusive from a forthcoming album. Compare both with Patife's {{ <track_link source="dj-patife-sambassim-dj-patife-remix.md" /> }}, the bonus track on the same CD.

Cool Steps is a {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }}, a CD of fifteen tracks compiled and mixed by Patife, where Cosmonautics appear three times: this remix, a remix of Technozoide's Esfera, and 81 Is The Number with Patife himself. No source we found says who is behind the name.

## Artistic Context

DJ Patife, born Wagner Ribeiro de Souza in 1976, has been active since 1995 and moved to London in 1998. Cool Steps came out in Brazil on 5 October 2001 on Trama and SambaLoco. DJ Marky's Wikipedia entry names Só Tinha Que Ser Com Você, with LK, among the releases through which Brazilian producers forged a new drum and bass sound that became popular worldwide. Fernanda Porto, born in Serra Negra in 1965, is known as a singer of drum 'n' bossa.

## Impact and Reception

The Movement single entered the UK charts on 27 October 2001. It reached only number 87 on the Official Singles Chart, but number 10 on the Dance Singles Chart, where it stayed 22 weeks, and number 14 on the Independent Singles Chart over 29 weeks. Play it loud enough to hear the guitar under the drums.
