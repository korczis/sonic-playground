## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** and **Anne-Marie** made **Elevate** one of the brightest corners of Sirens. It is house with a UK garage lilt, upbeat and energetic, a dance song with a real singer at its centre rather than a club tool with a vocal sample. The title promises a lift, and the reviews that singled it out heard exactly that.

## Song Summary

Elevate is track eleven of Sirens, Gorgon City's debut album, released on 6 October 2014 by Virgin EMI and Black Butter. It runs 3:41 and is written by the duo, Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott, with Ed Thomas and Martyna Baker; the duo produced it and, per the release credits, also mixed it and programmed the drums. It was not one of the album's singles. Skiddle's Jimmy Coultas called it one of the most upbeat new moments on the record.

## Musical Breakdown

The reviews describe the track as a whole and give no times, so the parts below follow what they heard rather than a timeline.

### Groove

Coultas heard a snappy mutation that starts from classic New Jersey garage swing.

### Bassline

From there, he wrote, it moves toward the UK's bass-drenched version of that sound. Earmilk's Steel Hanf heard Elevate and the following Take It All closing the album in big and elegant fashion, both carrying the signature Gorgon City bass lines.

### Vocal

Anne-Marie carries the song; Coultas wrote that she props it up, and he heard the sound of 2013 merging with 2014 in her presence.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is how most of Sirens works. Every track has a guest singer, and Kye Gibbon told Earmilk that the duo look for voices you would not normally hear on a dance record. Here that voice belonged to Anne-Marie, months before her first solo EP.

{{ <concept_link name="swing" /> }} is the rhythmic idea behind the garage Coultas heard. When pairs of beats are played slightly unevenly, a programmed pattern starts to lilt; garage made that lilt its signature.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the UK half of the story: the low end that, in Coultas's account, drenches the American swing and turns it into a British club record.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City are Kye "Foamo" Gibbon and Matt "RackNRuin" Robson-Scott from North London. Anne-Marie, from Essex, featured on songs by Magnetic Man, Gorgon City and Raized by Wolves before going solo, and in 2014 Rudimental asked her to replace a departing vocalist; she toured with them for about two years. Her solo career followed: the Karate EP in 2015, Alarm in 2016 and the lead vocal on Clean Bandit's Rockabye. The deluxe Sirens adds a second song with her, Try Me Out.

## Impact and Reception

Sirens reached number 10 on the UK Albums Chart, topped the UK Dance Albums chart and went gold. The Guardian's Harriet Gibsone heard a pop-house powerhouse that was not especially innovative but scintillating. Elevate itself has no chart record; it is an album track that rewards the listen to the end of the record.
