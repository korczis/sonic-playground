## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **Fascination (Remastered Version)** is the gentle side of her debut. Buzzbands.LA called it a sweet, acoustic number, which places it between folk and the world-music shelf where her album was filed, and the mood the blog describes is sultry and relaxed rather than dramatic.

## Song Summary

Fascination is the third track of Handmade, Zahra's debut album of 2010, where it runs 3:39. The metadata of the single's official upload credits her alone as composer, lyricist and producer. The Los Angeles blog Buzzbands.LA, posting the song in September 2011 as the album made her American debut, heard it as a showcase for her soothing yet textured vocals and compared her sultry flair to Sade and Billie Holiday. This page reviews the remastered version from the 2011 reissue of the album; what the lyric says, no source tells.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }} prepares an existing mix again for release: level, tone and balance can change, but the performance stays the same. Handmade was issued in a remastered version on 4 February 2011, and this upload comes from it. No source says what changed, so the honest exercise is comparison: play this version beside the 2010 album if you have it.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is usually a shorter version for radio. Oursoul Records released Fascination (Radio Edit) as a single on 25 February 2011, and Wikipedia lists Fascination among Zahra's releases of that year. Yet the edit's official upload is listed at 3:38, the same as this remastered version, so whatever the edit changes, it is not much length.

## Artistic Context

Hindi Zahra was born in Khouribga, Morocco, in 1979 and moved to Paris at fifteen to live with her father. She is a self-taught multi-instrumentalist who sings mostly in English and sometimes in Shilha, as on {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-imik-si-mik-remastered-version.md" /> }}. Handmade came out on Blue Note; Songlines heard in it Berber traditions, desert blues, jazz, folk and reggae, and a Billie Holiday influence.

## Impact and Reception

Handmade won the Prix Constantin in November 2010 and the Victoire de la Musique for world music album in February 2011, reached number 17 in France and was certified gold there. No source found gives Fascination a chart place of its own; it travelled as a single and as an American introduction.
