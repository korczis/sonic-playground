## Genre and Mood

**WHTKD** keeps it smooth and steady on **Say to Me**, a deep house track that leans on warmth rather than force. The electronic groove is soft-edged and rolling, the house rhythm never in a hurry, and the vocal gives it a soulful core. The mood is hypnotic with a gentle longing running through it, perfect for the hour when afternoon turns into evening.

## Song Summary

The song circles a simple emotional request, asking someone to finally say what they feel. A soulful male vocal, credited to Myron Avant, is chopped, looped and spread across the track so that its pleading phrases become part of the rhythm. Underneath, a warm bassline, soft kicks and gently swinging percussion keep everything moving. The memorable detail is how the vocal loops lock into the groove, repeating until they feel less like a lyric and more like a mantra.

## Musical Breakdown

Say to Me follows the patient logic of deep house, introducing elements slowly and letting the loop do its work.

### Intro

A muffled kick and soft percussion set a relaxed pulse, with hints of the vocal drifting in through a filter.

### Build-up

The bassline enters, round and warm, and the filter opens bit by bit as chords and hi-hats join.

### Main Section

The full groove settles in with the vocal loop on top, its soulful phrases repeating over the steady beat and the swinging percussion.

### Bridge

The drums briefly drop away, leaving the voice and chords hanging in space before the kick returns.

### Outro

The arrangement thins back to rhythm and fragments of the vocal, fading out as gently as it began.

## Artistic Context

WHTKD is the alias of German producer Leo Carvelli, whose work blends soulful vocals with deep, melodic house production. Say to Me became his breakthrough after it was featured on the YouTube channel Lithuania HQ, one of the influential channels that shaped the deep house boom of the 2010s. Those channels turned unknown producers into names with huge audiences almost overnight, and the track fits squarely in that warm, vocal-led strand of the genre. Its official video was later published through Kontor.TV.

## Impact and Reception

That exposure lifted Say to Me far beyond the clubs, spreading it through streaming playlists and a set of remixes, including versions by 5&Dime and T2. It became the track most listeners associate with WHTKD and a staple of chilled deep house mixes. It is here because it does something simple beautifully: it finds a soulful loop and lets it glow. Put it on as the light changes and let it carry you into the evening.
