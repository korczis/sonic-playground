## Genre and Mood

**Mista Trick** and **Spencer Ramsay**'s **Taking a Bath (Splish Splash)** puts a 1958 rock and roll novelty on a drum and bass chassis. It is playful, energetic and carefree, the same joke Bobby Darin told, now over drum and bass, with saxophone and piano keeping one foot in the past.

## Song Summary

Splish Splash is Darin's story of a Saturday-night bath. He steps out, wraps a towel round himself, opens the door and finds a party in full swing in his living room, so he jumps back in. Mista Trick's version keeps the words and the hook and builds a chanted section out of them. The credits are short: production by Mista Trick and Spencer Ramsay, a new vocal by Andrew Bourn, saxophone and piano by Joe Rodwell, mastering by Kevin Tuffy.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

No source describes how the track opens; the lyric sheet begins directly with Darin's first verse.

### Verse 1

Bourn sings Darin's opening verse, from the bath to the party behind the door. The lyric sheet breaks the line off there, on "There was a...", and the chant follows.

### Chorus

The chant turns the song's own words into a hook: "Splishin' Splishin'", "Movin' Movin'", with "Rockin' and a-rollin'" between them, repeated like a refrain.

### Verse 2

The second verse brings in the party guests, named after other hits of the day, before the chant returns.

### Outro

The words end on Darin's own closing lines, a run of rhymes on splashing, rolling, strolling and grooving.

## Concepts in Focus

Mista Trick calls the track a drum and bass {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} of Darin's song, but the credits point to a {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} as well: the vocal is newly sung by Andrew Bourn, and the saxophone and piano are newly played. The difference matters: a remix reworks an existing recording, a cover records the song again. This track does the second in the style of the first.

Splish Splash itself is a model {{ <concept_link name="novelty song" /> }}. Murray Kaufman, the DJ Murray the K, bet Darin that he could not write a song from a bath-time line suggested by Kaufman's mother, Jean; Darin did, Kaufman was credited as "Jean Murray", and the single on Atco reached number 3 in the US and 18 in the UK in 1958. A joke with a hook that strong survives any genre.

## Artistic Context

Mista Trick is a Bristol producer who mixes vintage, vaudeville-flavoured material with hard-hitting drum and bass; he runs the Swing & Bass club night at Hootananny in Brixton and its label, and his debut album You came out on This Is Freshly Squeezed in 2021. A first version of Splish Splash with Spencer Ramsay appeared on his YouTube channel in May 2023; this one followed as a single on Virgin on 23 February 2024. No source describes Spencer Ramsay's own work.

## Impact and Reception

On his own site Mista Trick calls it his viral hit, a remix that "has stormed the internet". Compare its old-into-new move with {{ <track_link source="caravan-palace-black-betty.md" /> }}, then put it on and wait for the door to open.
