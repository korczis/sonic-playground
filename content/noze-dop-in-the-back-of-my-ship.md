## Genre and Mood

**Nôze** and **dOP** meet on **In the Back of My Ship**, an electronic track by a house duo that one review heard as reggae. The feelings named here, playful and groovy, follow the reputation of both acts for tongue-in-cheek, live-played dance music, since no review describes this song beyond a single line.

## Song Summary

The track is the fifth of nine on Dring, the album Nôze released on Get Physical Music in spring 2011, and it runs about five and a half minutes. The guests are dOP, a Paris trio that Resident Advisor described, like the Hamburg group Wareika on the same album, as a live ensemble. Its one written verdict is harsh: the French blog Des oreilles dans Babylone called it "le reggae faiblard", a feeble reggae, made with Nôze's "potes", their pals from dOP.

## Concepts in Focus

dOP are the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} here, and an unusual one: not a single singer adding a hook, but a whole group with its own sound. Textura described their 2010 debut Greatest Hits as soulful house blending funk and jazz, made by a trio with backgrounds in rock, jazz, hip-hop, reggae, classical and African music. That list makes the reggae turn of this collaboration less surprising.

Reggae is built on the {{ <concept_link name="off-beat" /> }}, the points halfway between the main beats, where its guitar and keyboard chops usually sit. No source describes the pattern of this track, so take it as a listening exercise: count the beats and hear whether the accents land on them or between them.

## Artistic Context

Nôze are Ezechiel Pailhès and Nicolas Sfintescu, and Dring was their first album since Songs on the Rocks in 2008. Fact reported that it was more instrumental and more improvised, with a stronger jazz influence, and that its players included Thibaut Frisoni on guitar, Alexandre Authelain on saxophone and clarinet and Matthias Mahler on trombone. The press release, Resident Advisor wrote, spoke of klezmer, reggae and cumbia. dOP, formed in 2006 and signed to the Circus Company label, were on Resident Advisor's lists of top live acts in 2010, 2011 and 2012.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of Dring were mixed. Groove said Nôze had become a real band but heard too much folkloric mixing; Des oreilles dans Babylone found the album a good vintage, steeped in brass, with this track among its weaker moments. Get Physical nonetheless uploaded it in 2020 for a playlist of the label's dancefloor classics. Nôze's remix work, heard on {{ <track_link source="dapayk-padberg-caro-island.md" /> }}, shows the club side of the same duo. Listen, and decide whether the critic was right.
