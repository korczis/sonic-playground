## Genre and Mood

**Tiësto** and **The Chainsmokers** made **Split (Only U)** for the main stage. The Nocturnal Times called it a big-room production, Dancing Astronaut heard a big-room blitz, and EDM House Network a trap-infused beat after the breakdown. It is energetic and euphoric in the way festival records are meant to be: one melody, a long wait, then everything at once.

## Song Summary

The track carries two names. Split is the Croatian city that hosts Ultra Europe, where Tiësto brought The Chainsmokers on stage to premiere it in July 2015, and the name stuck. Only U points to its melody: We Rave You found it very similar to Ashanti's Only U, the 2004 lead single of her album Concrete Rose, and Wikipedia's article on that song says the producers used a sample of it. We Rave You called the result a hard-hitting track, as main-stage as they come.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track; the reviews describe its parts. The embedded video is the official music video, which runs shorter than the 4:16 single.

### Intro

Dancing Astronaut heard the track open with main-stage chord progressions, a calm before what follows.

### Breakdown

EDM House Network described a powerful breakdown that turns into a trap-infused beat, setting up what comes next rather than releasing it.

### Drop

Here, in Dancing Astronaut's words, comes the big-room blitz of thunderous percussion and an arena-sized synth assault. We Rave You found the synths incredible and the drop simply out of this world.

## Concepts in Focus

The title already admits the debt. {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} means building new music from a piece of an existing recording, and here the borrowed piece is the melody of an R&B single of 2004. Note how the sources word it: We Rave You says the melody is very similar, while Wikipedia and The Nocturnal Times say sample. Tiësto's {{ <track_link source="tiesto-grapevine.md" /> }} raised the same question about a Motown song three years later.

The {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} is the moment a festival track releases what its build has held back, and the premiere reviews judged Split almost entirely by it. That is why it was played on festival stages for weeks before anyone could buy it: a drop is tested on a crowd.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, released on 3 September 2015, was directed and edited by Joe Zohar for Moving Box Studios.

## Artistic Context

Tiësto, the Dutch DJ born Tijs Verwest, released the track on 31 August 2015 through Beatport on Musical Freedom, his own label. The Chainsmokers, a New York duo, were then known for lighter, pop-minded records; Dancing Astronaut called this a far cry from their indie-tinged originals, with the duo taking notes from Tiësto. We Rave You noted that both acts had already been playing it at main-stage festivals before the release.

## Impact and Reception

Split (Only U) did not enter the Flemish chart but reached number 63 on the Walloon Ultratip. It travelled further on festival stages and on the soundtrack of the 2016 action film Collide, and EDM House Network counted more than 100 million streams by its ninth anniversary. Wait for the drop; that is what it was built for.
