## Genre and Mood

**Whilk & Misky** pull soul, funk and indie songcraft together on **I Want Your Lovin'**, and the **Edit** trims it to a tight, radio-ready length. The mood is groovy and romantic, with a thread of longing pulling through every line, the sound of someone who knows exactly what they want and is not ashamed to ask.

## Song Summary

The song is a direct plea for love and attention, sung with warmth rather than desperation. Its strength is its groove: a swinging rhythm, a bassline that rolls rather than thumps, and arrangement touches that nod to classic soul records while keeping a modern, polished finish. The vocal sits at the centre, smooth and slightly smoky, repeating its wish until it becomes a hook you hum for the rest of the day. The memorable detail is how understated it all is: the song seduces by staying relaxed.

## Musical Breakdown

The edit keeps the structure lean, getting to the hook quickly and leaving nothing extra.

### Intro

A warm groove arrives straight away, inviting and unhurried, with a hint of vintage soul in its tone.

### Verse 1

The voice enters close and conversational, setting out the song's desire in simple, honest terms.

### Chorus

The title line becomes the hook, smooth and repetitive, as easy to sing along to as it is to sway to.

### Bridge

A short change in texture lets the groove breathe before the last return of the chorus.

### Outro

The groove rides out gently, ending with the same easy charm it began with.

## Concepts in Focus

I Want Your Lovin' runs on its {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}: a swinging rhythm and a bassline that rolls rather than thumps. The beats are programmed by the duo themselves, but the feel is loose and human, which is why the song sways instead of pumping.

Its {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the title, a single wish repeated in the chorus until it becomes the thing you hum afterwards. A hook this simple depends on delivery; sung smoothly and without strain, it sounds confident rather than desperate.

## Artistic Context

Whilk & Misky are a London duo formed in 2012 by Charlie Dickens and Nima Khaste, known for blending folk, soul, blues and electronic textures into cinematic, heartfelt songs. Their debut EP *The First Sip* (2015) was supported by Zane Lowe, Huw Stephens and Pete Tong, and "Clap Your Hands" and "Babe I'm Yours" reached the top five of Spotify's Viral 50. "I Want Your Lovin'", their first single for Island, was the first track released from a new set of sessions and opened a series of monthly releases. The credits name Charles Dickens and Masoud Hasounizadeh as writers, Hasounizadeh as producer, Matt Doughty as recording engineer, David Wrench, known for his work with Jamie xx, FKA Twigs and Caribou, as mixing engineer, and Dan Baldwin as mastering engineer.

## Impact and Reception

The song found a natural home on soulful and chill-out playlists, where its warmth and low-key confidence stand out. It shows a side of the duo that is more playful and groove-led than their moodier, more dramatic material, and it makes a good introduction to their catalogue for anyone who likes their soul with a little modern polish. Put it on for a slow dance in the kitchen, the kind nobody else needs to see.
