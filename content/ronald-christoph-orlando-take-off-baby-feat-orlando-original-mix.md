## Genre and Mood

**Ronald Christoph** made **Take Off, Baby!** with **Orlando**, a Belgian tech house singer, and Discogs files the single under techno and tech house: styles built on a steady pulse and slow change rather than on verses and choruses. Groovy and hypnotic is this page's reading of what those styles aim for; no published review describes how this track feels.

## Song Summary

Take Off, Baby! is the second release of the German label EVAMORE MUSIC, catalogue number EMM002, issued in April 2012 as a 12-inch and as a download. Its original mix, credited to Ronald Christoph featuring Orlando, opens side A and runs six minutes and forty-two seconds. Beside it the record carries the SiD & Spoony Talker Edit of the same track and two versions of Last Party, the original and Ronald's BMX. The title promises a take-off; the sources do not quote or describe the lyric, and this page does not guess at it.

## Concepts in Focus

The credit itself teaches something. A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is named after the main artist, and here Orlando appears only on the original mix: the SiD & Spoony Talker Edit and the Last Party tracks are credited to Ronald Christoph alone.

The track also shows how one recording lives as an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. The same 12-inch gives it to SiD & Spoony for an edit, and later in 2012 the French label Wagram Music put a version of about four minutes on its compilation Saint-Germain-des-Prés Café 14, the series that also carried {{ <track_link source="dj-disse-will-buhrkall-break-on-through.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Take Off, Baby! belongs to a {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} of small labels, vinyl and download stores. Discogs describes Ronald Christoph as a Berlin-based techno and house producer who has worked professionally since the early 1990s and passed through many genres, from classical and jazz to hip hop and metal, before house and techno. EVAMORE MUSIC released his Underground Limbo 2013 the following year.
