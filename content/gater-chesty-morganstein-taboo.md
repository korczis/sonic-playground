## Genre and Mood

**Gater** and **Chesty Morganstein** made **Taboo** at the height of electroclash, the style that fused 1980s electro, new wave and synth-pop with techno and, as Wikipedia puts it, put the emphasis on songwriting, showmanship and a sense of humour. Discogs files the EP under electro. Provocative and playful are the feelings named here, because the song is built on a double meaning: it sounds like one thing and, according to its author, is about another.

## Song Summary

Taboo is the first track of Gater's debut EP, which Dekathlon Records released in Germany in September 2002. Brian Suarez wrote and produced it in New York, and Chesty Morganstein is credited with the vocals. Radio Nova, which played the song a lot in the early 2000s, describes a lyric that plays on the ambiguity of its words, and then the reveal: in an interview Gater said the song is really about the board game Taboo, of which he and Chesty are big fans. The version embedded here is the same recording, reissued digitally in 2014 by the label RemuteD.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }} was named by DJ Hell in Munich and popularised in New York by Larry Tee's Electroclash festival in 2001. Taboo connects both cities: written in New York, released on a Munich label, and included on Ministry of Sound's 2003 Electroclash compilation next to Chicks On Speed, whose {{ <track_link source="chicks-on-speed-euro-trash-girl.md" /> }} shows the Munich side of the scene.

Chesty Morganstein is credited as the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, and here the guest is the song's face: the producer stays behind the machines while the voice delivers the joke. Gater's Discogs profile adds its own humour, claiming he studied to be a priest and was influenced by house, electro, freestyle and pornographic movies.

## Artistic Context

Gater is Brian Suarez, a producer from New York. Dekathlon was founded in Munich in 2002 by Florian Senfter, known as Zombie Nation, and Wolfram Gottardt; Senfter started it after leaving DJ Hell's International DeeJay Gigolo Records, and Radio Nova notes that Taboo appeared on the label when it was brand new. The EP also carried a Zombie Nation remix of the title track, made at Zombie Studios in Munich.

## Impact and Reception

Taboo travelled through European compilations, among them Ministry of Sound's Electroclash and Radio Nova's Nova Tunes, and Nova later revisited it as one of its own classics. Listen once for the double meaning, and once knowing it is about a board game.
