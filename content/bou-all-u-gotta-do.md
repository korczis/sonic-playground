## Genre and Mood

**Bou**'s **All U Gotta Do** is drum and bass, the style Discogs files it under, released by one of the scene's labels, DnB Allstars Records. No published review describes how the track itself sounds, so the two feelings on this page come from how Mixmag described Bou's style in 2022: the dynamism of jump-up and jungle sunk into the hypnotic momentum of a roller. Energetic and hypnotic are the two halves of that sentence.

## Song Summary

All U Gotta Do came out on 25 November 2022 as a single of three minutes forty-five, catalogue number DNBAR043; the label posted it on its YouTube channel the day before. Shazam names Amine Bouguenna, Bou's own name, as its writer, and no guest is credited. Beyond those facts the sources are silent: no interview or premiere I could find explains the title, the vocal or how the track was made.

## Concepts in Focus

Bou is his own {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. Wikipedia says he was inspired to produce after hearing jump-up on SoundCloud and makes his tracks in FL Studio; his later hit Closer took him about forty minutes. Mixmag adds that he had been producing since college and runs his own label, Gossip, for quality control. Here he is credited alone.

Drum and bass is built on the {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }}: Wikipedia describes the genre, born in Britain in the early 1990s, by its fast breakbeats and deep bass. Mixmag hears jungle in Bou's sound too, and the breakbeat is what the two styles share.

## Artistic Context

Bou, born Mohamed Amine Bouguenna, lived in France and in Ashford, Kent, before growing up in Monsall, Manchester; Mixmag names his French heritage, his Muslim upbringing and Manchester as his inspirations and High Contrast as a long-time idol. His first single, Movements, came out in 2015. In 2021 he featured on Bru-C's Streetside, which reached number 84 in the UK and went gold. All U Gotta Do sits just before the turn: in 2023 Closer, with Slay, reached number 24 and Baddadan, with Chase & Status, number five. Hear Chase & Status that same year in {{ <track_link source="becky-hill-chase-status-disconnect.md" /> }}.
