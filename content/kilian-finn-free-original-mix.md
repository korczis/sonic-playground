## Genre and Mood

**Kilian & Finn** released **Free** with the singer NewShoes in 2014, and this is its original mix. Apple Music files the single under dance and the compilations that later licensed it under electronic. No published review describes the track, so its two feelings rest on where labels put it: dreamy on the evidence of Sommertraum, a 2015 German compilation whose title means "summer dream" and which carries this mix, and groovy as the plainest word for the dance classification. Read both as context, not as a listener's account.

## Song Summary

Free is a four-track single that Exposure Music and Dream Team released in Germany on 1 August 2014: this original mix of four minutes and fifty-two seconds, a radio mix, and a remix and remix edit by LCAW. YouTube's art track credits Kilian Bohn and Finn Bohn as composers and Edition EXP as publisher. NewShoes is the credited guest singer. I found no published lyrics, so this review does not describe the words.

## Concepts in Focus

NewShoes is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}: the track belongs to Kilian & Finn, and the "feat." credit names the guest voice. The blog Acid Stag introduced the pairing that way: Hype Machine preserves the opening of its post of 3 April 2014, about German siblings teaming up with a Hamburg-based guest.

The single also shows how one song is cut for different uses. The Radio Mix, at three minutes and thirty-one seconds, is the {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}, short enough for airplay; the original mix is about a minute and twenty seconds longer. LCAW's {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} goes the other way, to six minutes and twenty-eight seconds, with an edit of it at four minutes.

## Artistic Context

EARMILK introduced Kilian & Finn in November 2014 as the heads of Exposure Music, which it called one of the hottest new dance labels coming out of Germany. Free travelled through licences: Bang Record and Pirames International released it in Italy in October 2014, FuturePlay in France, and in 2015 it appeared on compilations such as Sommertraum and Urban Deep House, Vol. 1. Compare {{ <track_link source="alle-farben-graham-candy-sometimes.md" /> }}, another German house track of that year built around a guest singer.

## Impact and Reception

Free did not chart, as far as any source records, and its reception is the list of labels that took it on. Start with the original mix, then try the radio mix and hear what a minute and twenty seconds removes.
