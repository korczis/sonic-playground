## Genre and Mood

**Gin Wigmore** drives **Man Like That** with the stomp of blues, the warmth of soul and the swagger of rock. It is bold, confident and gritty, a short, fierce song that knows exactly what it wants.

## Song Summary

The song is a declaration of desire, a list of qualities in the kind of man the singer is after, delivered with a wink and a growl. Wigmore's voice is the main event: raspy, powerful and full of attitude. The arrangement is built for stomping, with heavy drums and twangy guitars, played by producer Butch Walker and Fran Capitanelli, giving it a vintage feel while the production stays punchy and modern. At under three minutes it hits hard and leaves quickly. There is humour in it too: the lyric is a playful shopping list of manly virtues, and Wigmore sings it with the relish of someone who knows she holds all the cards.

## Musical Breakdown

The track wastes no time, building quickly from a lean groove to a full-throated finish.

### Intro

A stomping beat kicks things off, raw and rhythmic, with a guitar line snarling on top.

### Verse 1

Wigmore enters with a playful, teasing delivery, setting out her terms with a grin you can hear.

### Chorus

The hook bursts open, her voice soaring and cracking with energy as the band hits harder behind her.

### Bridge

The arrangement drops back for a moment, building tension before the last chorus.

### Outro

The song ends with a final punch, as sudden and confident as everything before it.

## Concepts in Focus

Man Like That is a compact example of {{ <concept_link name="verse-chorus form" /> }}. The verses set out the list, the chorus states the demand, a short bridge holds back, and the last chorus lands harder for the wait. Nothing is wasted: the whole form fits in under three minutes, which is part of why the song feels so assured.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is less a melody than a voice. Wigmore's rasp, the way she leans into the title and roughens it, is what makes the chorus stick. Compare the same voice on {{ <track_link source="gin-wigmore-black-sheep.md" /> }}, the album's lead single, where it carries a darker message with the same bite.

## Artistic Context

Gin Wigmore is a singer-songwriter from New Zealand whose husky voice set her apart early in her career. "Man Like That" comes from her second album, Gravel & Wine, released in New Zealand on 7 November 2011. Before recording it, she spent two months in Mississippi and Alabama looking for Southern inspiration; the album was then made in Santa Monica with Butch Walker, who produced it with his band The Black Widows and co-wrote this song with her. Gravel & Wine went to number 1 in New Zealand and was certified double platinum there.

## Impact and Reception

"Man Like That" was released as the album's second single on 1 February 2012, after "Black Sheep". It reached its widest audience through a Heineken advert tied to the James Bond film Skyfall, in which Wigmore performs the song. Its retro energy and instantly recognisable vocal made it a natural fit for screens and playlists, and it showed how a contemporary artist could channel old blues and soul swagger without sounding like a pastiche. It is here for its sheer attitude, under three minutes of grit and charisma. Play it loud and stomp along.
