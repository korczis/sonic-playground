## Genre and Mood

**Flava D**'s **Cats** is drum and bass with a grin. It is playful, humorous and energetic at once: Hospital Records, which released it, introduced it as a tongue-in-cheek, bass-infused tribute to her much-loved cats, and also as a drum and bass belter meant for raves and festivals.

## Song Summary

The joke and the weapon are the same track. On top sit vocals that the label calls irresistibly catchy and humorous, sung by Flava D herself; underneath, what it calls futuristic sound design, made of tearout basslines and punch-packed drums. The single came out on Hospital Records on 22 September 2023, and the label's description says it was already taking raves, festivals and dance floors by storm before release.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review takes Cats apart section by section. The label's description names its three layers, and this breakdown follows them rather than inventing a structure.

### The Vocal

Flava D's own voice carries the hook, the part that turns a cat song into something a crowd can shout.

### The Bassline

Tearout basslines, in the label's words. Listen for the bass taking over the lead between the vocal lines.

### The Drums

Punch-packed drumwork holds the speed and drives the track under the bass.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} in drum and bass often carries the tune as much as the low end. Cats leans on what Hospital calls tearout basslines, which is how a light-hearted song can still hit hard in a club.

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} does not have to be serious to work. Here it is a short vocal joke, sung by the producer, and the label credits it with making the track catchy. Compare a hook built for a festival crowd in {{ <track_link source="chase-status-selecta.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="sound design" /> }} is the craft of shaping the sounds themselves rather than the notes. Hospital's description puts it at the centre of Cats: the futuristic character lies in how the bass and drums are built.

## Artistic Context

Flava D is Danielle Gooding, a DJ and producer born in Bournemouth who learned to produce on Ableton while working in a record shop as a teenager. She made her name in UK garage and bassline, on the label Butterz and in the trio TQD with Royal-T and DJ Q. Her first drum and bass single, Return To Me, came in 2019, and she told Beatportal the switch was a massive learning curve: she thought she was a good producer until she started making drum and bass. When Cats came out, the label noted she was fresh from Glastonbury, Hospitality On The Beach, Parklife and Bassfest, and still releasing tracks such as Truth with Sammy Virji, among her recent bassline and UK garage cuts. Her debut album, Here & Now, followed on Hospital in 2025.

## Impact and Reception

On YouTube, the drum and bass channels UKF Drum & Bass and DTDNB carry Cats alongside Hospital's own upload. Play it loud, and listen to how seriously the bass takes the joke.
