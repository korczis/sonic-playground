## Genre and Mood

**DJ Fresh** made **Gold Dust** as drum and bass, and **TC** remixed it for **Gold Dust - TC Remix**, one of the versions that followed the song's 2012 return. Discogs files the release under drum and bass. The feelings, energetic and uplifting, belong to the song's whole family: the BBC's Fraser McAlpine called the 2010 version life-affirming and astonishingly chipper.

## Song Summary

Gold Dust began in 2008 as a 7:51 instrumental 12-inch on Breakbeat Kaos, returned in 2010 with a vocal by Ce'cile, and appeared in 2012 with Ms. Dynamite on DJ Fresh's album Nextlevelism. Ministry of Sound released a six-track download on 2 December 2012, led by Shy FX's re-edit for radio; TC's remix is track two. Discogs times it at 4:49 and Wikipedia's track list at 4:56, against Shy FX's 3:02. Dan Stein, DJ Fresh's real name, is the song's sole credited writer and producer.

At close to five minutes, the remix is longer than the radio versions and shorter than the original instrumental. Listen for how it uses the time: whether it starts with a long run-in for DJs, where the vocal enters, and whether the drop comes sooner or later than in the versions you know.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to another producer. On this release, TC was one of five remixers to follow Shy FX; play his version next to {{ <track_link source="dj-fresh-shy-fx-gold-dust-shy-fx-re-edit.md" /> }} and hear the same song cut two ways.

Gold Dust is a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: an instrumental, two vocal versions, a re-edit and a set of remixes. The song is a family of recordings rather than one.

TC also shows the many sides of a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in drum and bass: he sang and co-produced Plasticworld on Pendulum's 2005 album Hold Your Colour and sang on Sub Focus's Follow the Light.

## Artistic Context

TC is Tom Casswell, also known as Tommy Boy, an English drum and bass producer, singer and DJ who grew up in St Paul's in Bristol and started out inspired by the city's scene. His debut album, Evolution, came out in 2007, and by 2012 he was releasing on Don't Play. In 2014 DJ Fresh reworked Casswell's unreleased Make You Bounce into Make U Bounce, with vocals by Little Nikki, released by Ministry of Sound. The Gold Dust remix came two years earlier.

## Impact and Reception

Dancing Astronaut listed TC among the reworks of the 2012 package, which itself reached number 22 in the UK through Shy FX's re-edit; all versions of Gold Dust together were certified triple platinum in the UK. Play TC's version, then the re-edit, and judge what nearly two extra minutes buy.
