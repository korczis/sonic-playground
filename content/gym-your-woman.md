## Genre and Mood

**GYM**'s **Your Woman** is a cover, electronic and tagged by its label as synth-pop and 8-bit. The song it covers is longing and bittersweet by design: Billboard's J'na Jefferson described White Town's original as a despondent sample set against upbeat energy.

## Song Summary

Your Woman was written and recorded by Jyoti Mishra as White Town and released in 1996, then reissued by Chrysalis in January 1997. Its hook is a muted trumpet played by Nat Gonella on Lew Stone and his Monseigneur Band's 1932 recording My Woman, which Mishra found through the BBC series Pennies from Heaven. He said he wanted a pop song with several perspectives, whose narrator is not what a casual listener might expect. GYM's version runs a little over four minutes.

### Main Section

The label's Bandcamp page tags the track 8-bit, electronic, pop and synth-pop, the same tags it gives a track from GYM's own album PYGME of December 2013. Whether the cover keeps Gonella's trumpet, and how close it stays to Mishra's arrangement, no source says; that is for your ears.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of someone else's song. Your Woman makes covering hard in an interesting way, because so much of its identity sits in a single borrowed sound. Covers by Tyler James, Princess Chelsea, Cats on Fire and British India have each had to decide what to do with it.

That sound is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Mishra did not sample Al Bowlly's vocal on the Lew Stone record, only the trumpet, and built a 1990s song around a fragment of a 1932 one, on free sequencing software and a cheap multitrack cassette recorder. A cover can sample the same fragment again, replay it or drop it; each choice changes the song. For another French act making a 1990s hit its own, compare {{ <track_link source="clea-vincent-all-that-she-wants.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

GYM, credited as G Y M on the label's Bandcamp, releases on Play It Loudly, an independent label in Lille run by We Are Enfant Terrible. This cover is the first track of the label's compilation Play it Loudly #1 / Covers, released on 29 September 2014, and also came out as a single in France the same year.

## Impact and Reception

White Town's original reached number one in the UK, the first gender-reversal song to top the chart there, and number 23 in the US. GYM's cover had no chart life and no reviews that we could find. Play the two versions back to back.
