## Genre and Mood

**Bob Sinclar**'s **The Beat Goes On** is house with a disco heart. Discogs files its parent album under house and disco, Wikipedia under house and electroclash, and Muziekweb heard Sinclar keep the disco in his house while adding electro loops. Uncut found the album frothier and camper than ever. Groovy and playful, then, and only three minutes long.

## Song Summary

The credits tell how the track was made. Bob Sinclar, the stage name of the French producer Christophe Le Friant, made the loops and all of the beats. Alain Wisniak played keyboards and wrote the vocal arrangement, Lene Lovich wrote the words, and Linda Lee Hopkins sings the lead, with backing vocals credited to Bob Sinclar's Angels. Despite the title, this is not Sonny Bono's 1967 song of the same name, heard on this site in {{ <track_link source="jojo-effect-the-beat-goes-on.md" /> }}.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track or describes it section by section; the review of the single describes its strengths.

### Vocal

Resident Advisor's Jai Anderson wrote in 2003 that the track has vocals that leave a lasting impression, Hopkins in front and Sinclar's Angels behind.

### Groove

The same review found the bassline impressive, under the loops and programmed drums that the single's credits give to Sinclar himself.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} is a section of sound repeated seamlessly, the building block of house. Sinclar is credited with the loops and the drum programming, so the track's frame is his; the voices and Wisniak's keyboards are laid over it. Daft Punk took the same principle to its limit in {{ <track_link source="daft-punk-around-the-world.md" /> }}.

The single came out as a radio edit and a club mix, and remixes followed. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a track for another setting: Resident Advisor recommended Roger Sanchez's mix and BT's Goin On Vocal Mix to funky house DJs. The {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}, shortened for airplay, was the version the reviewer liked least, calling it watered down.

## Artistic Context

The single appeared in France in 2002 on Yellow Productions, the label Le Friant runs, with EastWest; the 2:59 version became the first track of his third album, III, released in February 2003. Lovich and Wisniak had both written songs for the French disco producer Cerrone, and a BBC Music reviewer heard the album as Sinclar's homage to him. The embedded upload, the same 2:59 length as the album version, comes from a 2024 Yellow Productions compilation.

## Impact and Reception

The Beat Goes On reached number 22 in France, 25 in Wallonia, 29 in Italy and, released on Defected, number 33 in the UK in January 2003, with number two on the UK Dance chart. III reached number 24 in France, though reviews were mixed: the BBC found it average and at times dull. Start with the bassline.
