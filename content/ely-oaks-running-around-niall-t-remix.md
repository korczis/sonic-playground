## Genre and Mood

**Ely Oaks** released **Running Around** in early 2025, and within weeks came this version, the **Niall T Remix**. It belongs to the same world as the original: short, streaming-era dance music that Songfacts calls a pulsating EDM anthem, and that German Wikipedia places between commercial electronic pop, tech house and techno. The mood is energetic on the surface and bittersweet underneath, because the song is a complaint set to a beat. This page reviews the remix; where the sources describe only the original, it says so.

## Song Summary

The song is sung by an uncredited vocalist who, in Songfacts' words, sings about her romantic exasperation: a partner who keeps seeing other people, the running around of the title. Songfacts also reports that the track samples So Good by Nosi, the alias of the Luxembourg DJ and producer Eric Nosbusch. The Niall T Remix keeps the song and its voice and runs a little longer, about 2:18 on the streaming services against roughly 2:05 for the original; the upload on Ely Oaks's own channel is a few seconds shorter still.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a track from its parts. Released within weeks of the original, as this one was, it extends a single's life: a new version for new playlists, while the original is still climbing. Niall T did the same in 2025 for BOVSKI, whose {{ <track_link source="bovski-low-again.md" /> }} he remixed weeks after it came out.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} sits under the song itself. According to Songfacts, Running Around samples Nosi's So Good, so even the original is built partly from another record; the remix is one more layer on top.

## Artistic Context

Ely Oaks is Elias Grauß, an Austrian DJ and producer with Italian roots who moved to Berlin and signed to Jinx Music there. German Wikipedia notes that he releases mainly on streaming services and promotes his music on TikTok and Instagram, and lists collaborations with Niklas Dee and the DJ Alok. Running Around was his breakthrough; this remix single appeared in the UK under licence to Robots & Humans, a division of Sony Music UK.

## Impact and Reception

The success belongs to the song as a whole: in the UK, Running Around reached number 28 on the Official Singles Chart and number 5 on the Official Dance Singles Chart, where it stayed twenty weeks. It also charted in Germany and Austria. Play the original and the remix back to back.
