## Genre and Mood

**AronChupa** and **Little Sis Nora** made **I'm an Albatraoz** as a joke that turned into a worldwide hit. Wikipedia files it under electro house, electro swing and Melbourne bounce, and AronChupa put it more simply: he wanted to mix an old-school 1920s swing style with modern EDM. The result is playful and openly humorous, energetic from the first bar, and short at two minutes and forty-seven seconds.

## Song Summary

The song is a mock-tough boast. Nora Ekberg, Aron's younger sister, tells the story of a mouse named Lorry, dismisses her rudely and answers with the title line, sung like a badge of pride. Songfacts describes the music as vaudeville-style swing over a bouncy electro beat, and EDM Sauce's Kristopher Novak picked out three things that made it stand out: the insanely bouncy drop, the early-1930s piano breakdown and the catchy sing-along lyrics. The single release on 8 August 2014 also carried a 4:28 extended version and a one-minute shortened one.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A French announcer opens the track, presenting AronChupa and the Albatraoz like a variety act.

### Verse 1

Nora sings the story of Lorry the mouse in a sing-along voice, built for crowds to chant back.

### Drop

The electro half arrives as the bouncy drop EDM Sauce described, the moment the track turns into a club record.

### Breakdown

The piano break in an early-1930s style is the swing half, a deliberate step back in time. The sources do not say exactly where in the track it falls, so listen for the moment the beat gives way to the piano.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} is the release a dance track builds towards, and here it carries the modern half of AronChupa's idea. A {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} thins the music out; this one swaps the electronic beat for an old-time piano, so the contrast between the two decades is heard rather than explained.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} did much of the rest. Directed by Aron Ekberg and Sertaç Yıldızhan and shot at the Paramount Theatre in Oakland, it puts Nora at the centre, with the actresses Daniela Nieves Walker and Willa Holland as backing dancers in dressing gowns, AronChupa at a turntable, an older drummer and saxophone players. ## Artistic Context

AronChupa is the Swedish DJ and producer Aron Ekberg, and Little Sis Nora is his sister Nora Ekberg; they wrote the song together and he produced it. He told Songfacts that the plan was just to show it to friends. It was released by Sony, and the official channel later posted a sequel, I'm an Albatraoz Again.

## Impact and Reception

The song reached number one in Sweden and Denmark, two in Australia, Austria and the Netherlands, four in Germany and the Czech Republic, eight in Canada and 25 in the UK. It went seven times platinum in Sweden and quadruple platinum in Canada, and by January 2025 its video had passed 1.5 billion views on YouTube. Listen for the moment the piano takes over, then for the drop that pulls the track back to the club.
