## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** put **16 Minutes** fourth on Rollerchain, their darkest album, and it is one of its slowest and strangest moments: trip-hop and soul at a downtempo pace, haunting and hypnotic. Brighton Source called it a downtempo curio, one of the moodier tracks that haunt the record.

## Song Summary

The song's pull, according to the reviews, comes from two things. The NZ Herald heard it drawing you in with mesmerising repetition, one of the few places on the album where the production's ingredients fully came together for its critic. The French blog La Toile de Pandore was struck by something else: after the omnipresent bass of Get More, the track before it, 16 Minutes surprised with male backing voices of an enveloping softness. Tru Thoughts released it on 25 June 2012 as the album's second single, with a video directed by David Yeo.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through 16 Minutes section by section, so this breakdown stays with the elements the reviews name.

### Repetition

The track works by returning to the same material, and the NZ Herald heard that return as the thing that draws a listener in.

### Voices

Kathrin deBoer sings lead; around her, La Toile de Pandore heard soft male backing voices, a texture that took the blog by surprise. Who sings them, the sources do not say.

### Mood

Brighton Source placed the song among Rollerchain's moodier tracks, the ones that haunt, and called it downtempo; how its sections are arranged, no source describes.

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is the band's founding idea. Belleruche are Kathrin deBoer on vocals, Ricky Fabulous on guitar and DJ Modest on turntables, and their debut album was called Turntable Soul Music. What the turntables contribute here, no source describes, but laut.de heard Rollerchain as a perfect symbiosis of electronica and guitar, and Brighton Source heard the trio drifting away from their earlier turntable soul.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005 and signed in 2007 to Tru Thoughts, the Brighton label. Rollerchain, their fourth, came out on 7 May 2012; the three members wrote and recorded it over five months in late 2011 and are credited together as writers, producers and engineers. The label described the result as darker and more bass-heavy, keeping the band's melodic, soulful touch. The next track on the album is {{ <track_link source="belleruche-reach-for-the-bottle.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Rollerchain divided critics: the NZ Herald gave it three stars and found its cold production hard to connect with, while laut.de called it the band's darkest and possibly best album. Even the cooler NZ Herald review singled 16 Minutes out. The band announced its end in October 2013. Turn the lights down and let the repetition work.
