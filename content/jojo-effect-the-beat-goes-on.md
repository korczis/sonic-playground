## Genre and Mood

**Jojo Effect**'s **The Beat Goes On** is a cover that moves a sixties pop hit into the lounge. It belongs to nu-jazz and the electro-swing compilations that later gathered it, and its mood is playful and groovy, where the original's lyric was topical.

## Song Summary

Sonny Bono wrote, composed and produced The Beat Goes On for Sonny & Cher. It was recorded on 13 December 1966 at Gold Star Studios in Hollywood, entered the Billboard Hot 100 in January 1967 and peaked at number six. The lyric deals mainly with the events of its day; the refrain answers them by saying the beat goes on. Jojo Effect's version runs five minutes and a quarter on their album Not With Me, credited to Bono as lyricist and composer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} keeps the song and changes the setting. The 1966 session used the Wrecking Crew, nineteen musicians by Wikipedia's count, and bassist Carol Kaye says she devised its syncopated bass line in place of the walking bass written into Harold Battiste's arrangement. Covers have taken the song in every direction since: {{ <track_link source="buddy-rich-big-band-the-beat-goes-on-live-at-the-chez-club-hollywood-1967.md" /> }} turned it into big-band jazz the same year, and Orbit made an electro version in 1982.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} extended this cover's life in turn. In 2016 Club des Belugas, the project Soundcharts links with Jojo Effect producer Maxim Illion, remixed it for the release Nine on Glamjazz Records.

## Artistic Context

According to Last.fm, Jojo Effect was founded in November 2005 by the German producers and multi-instrumentalists Jaycay and Maxim Illion, with the singers Anne Schnell and Brenda Boykin. Not With Me came out on ChinChin Records; MusicBrainz dates the French release to October 2006, and the YouTube release gives January 2007. It was album of the month on WDR 5 in January 2007 and received heavy rotation on Ibiza Global Radio.

## Impact and Reception

The track found its audience on compilations. MusicBrainz lists Jojo Effect's The Beat Goes On on Café d'Afrique, Cigar Lounge and Absinth sets in 2006 and 2007, and later on Electro Swing Fever and Electro Swing Essentials, in this and shorter edits. Play Sonny & Cher first, then let the beat go on.
