## From Reluctant Pianist to Synth Enthusiast: My Musical Odyssey

Picture a small suburban village, where a young boy from a middle-class family 
begrudgingly sits at a piano, his fingers itching to change the classical sheet 
music to the raw energy of Eminem, Guano Apes, Offspring, and Nirvana. That boy 
was me, and little did I know that this forced piano practice was the first 
step in a lifelong love affair with music.

![A serene and peaceful sketched illustration](/images/man_1.png)

As time passed, my musical palate expanded exponentially. I dove headfirst 
into the mosh pits of heavy metal, crowdsurfed through punk shows, headbanged 
to rock anthems, and found solace in classical compositions. Jazz improvisation 
captivated me, techno beats moved me, R&B grooves soothed me, pop choruses 
stuck in my head, and folk melodies transported me to distant lands.

But it was the allure of synthesizers that truly captured my heart. The warm, 
pulsating sounds of 80s synths, the infectious rhythms of house music, the 
electrifying energy of French house, the edgy attitude of electroclash, and 
the nostalgic charm of synth-pop – these genres reignited my passion for the 
keyboard. Suddenly, that piano I once despised became a gateway to a world 
of electronic music production.

My obsession with synths led me down a rabbit hole of vintage gear, modern 
hardware, and software emulations. I've spent countless hours (and a small 
fortune) crafting sounds, tweaking parameters, and creating music that 
resonates with my soul. While I've dabbled with guitar, bass, and harmonica, 
synths in all their glorious shapes and sizes remain my true love.

![A serene and peaceful sketched illustration](/images/combo_1.png)

As a person who appreciates the finer things in life – from the flowing lines 
of calligraphy to the bold structures of architecture – I find beauty in the 
unconventional. My background as a programmer and hacker, combined with my 
experiences traveling the world, has cultivated a taste for the darker, more 
ironic, and less harmonious corners of the musical universe.

This blog is my sanctuary, a place to share the hidden gems I've discovered 
throughout my musical journey. Here, you'll find a curated selection of 
tracks that push boundaries, challenge norms, and ignite the imagination. 
While you might encounter a few familiar names, expect to discover a 
treasure trove of artists operating outside the mainstream.

Join me as we explore the fringes of electronic music, unearth forgotten 
classics, and celebrate the artists who dare to be different. Whether you're 
a seasoned audiophile or a curious newcomer, there's something here for 
everyone who appreciates the power of sound to move, inspire, and transform.

**Welcome to my world of sonic exploration. Let's embark on this adventure 
together.**
