## Genre and Mood

**FM LAETI**'s **Sanctuary** is soul-flavoured pop with a light step: APAR.TV called it a catchy ode to love, a gentle romantic revolt, and Aficia heard in its simple words a need to take flight and find freedom. Romantic, liberating and joyful: the reviews heard all three.

## Song Summary

Sanctuary is the tenth of twelve tracks on For the Music, the album FM Laeti released in 2014. Shazam credits the song to FM Laeti and François-Marie Dru. Aficia, which gave the album four stars, called it colourful, pop and truly rooted in the present, a record about love and joy, and named Sanctuary its favourite. Its video, directed by Jérôme Bernard, carries the same feeling into images.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song part by part, so this guide follows what the reviews say about its words, its hook and its video.

### Main Section

The reviews agree on lightness: an enchanted interlude in APAR.TV's words, a song about taking flight in Aficia's, sung by a voice Shazam's biography places between R&B, jazz and pop.

### Music Video

The video shows FM Laeti with her musicians, singing and dancing with a communicative joy, filmed by the cinematographer Baptiste Chesnais.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the short idea a song is built to leave in your head, and APAR.TV's warning that Sanctuary is catchy is the plainest description of one. A pop-soul hook does not need to shout; here it rides the song's sense of lightness.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} does not tell a story. Aficia described it as music, dance, freedom and a smile, the singer and her band sharing a moment, and that choice turns the lyric's longing for freedom into something you can see. Compare the very different, playful video of {{ <track_link source="jain-makeba.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

FM Laeti was born in 1981 in Karukera, Guadeloupe; her father, a drummer, had co-founded the zouk group Kassav'. The multi-instrumentalist François-Marie Dru spotted her and worked with her on her debut album, It Will All Come Around, released by Wagram in late 2011 to critical acclaim, and Shazam's biography notes that she has been called one of France's most exciting soul talents. For the Music followed in 2014; Discogs and MusicBrainz credit it to Pigalle Production, Shazam lists the digital release under Wagram Music's W Lab, and Ludovic Bruni plays guitar on the album.

## Impact and Reception

Sanctuary was pressed as a promotional CD single, and its video arrived in January 2015 to warm French press. It is here for its lightness. Put it on when you need a little sanctuary of your own.
