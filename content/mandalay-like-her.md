## Artistic Context

Mandalay formed in London in 1995, when Nicola Hitchcock answered Saul Freeman's advertisement for a singer in Melody Maker. Their 1996 single Flowers Bloom was Melody Maker's single of the week, and V2 signed them for a full recording deal. Their debut, Empathy, came out in 1998, produced by Guy Sigsworth. Instinct followed in 2000, and in 2002 the duo split over "irreconcilable personal and professional differences".

## Impact and Reception

Instinct's singles were Deep Love, remixed by Nitin Sawhney, and Not Seventeen; Like Her was not among them. The Guardian called the album "resolutely elegant, affecting pop", and AllMusic gave it four stars out of five. In the United States, Solace, a 2001 compilation drawn from both albums, sold more than 44,000 copies. Listen to Like Her in the album's own order, between Don't Invent Me and Deep Love.
