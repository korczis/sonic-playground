## Genre and Mood

**Claptone** and **Say Yes Dog** meet on **Before I Lose My Mind**, a house track that leans towards pop: a groovy club pulse with a song on top, and a title that promises longing before a note is played.

## Song Summary

The track first appeared as the B-side of Heartbeat, the 2016 single from Claptone's debut album Charmer. Claptone's own channel announced it as a previously unreleased B-side featuring the Berlin indie-pop outfit Say Yes Dog. EDMtunes called it an all-new original, a blended piece with pop and house influences that showcased Claptone's skills at the keys, and found it quite refreshing.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes Before I Lose My Mind section by section, so this breakdown stays with the few things the coverage does name.

### The Keys

Claptone plays keyboard on the track, and EDMtunes heard his playing as the showcase of the piece.

### The Vocals

Say Yes Dog, in EDMtunes' words, lend their voices on the final layer of the EP. Who in the trio sings which line, the sources do not say.

### Two Lengths

The video here carries the 5:36 version that Exploited released on 28 March 2016. The yearbook's recording is the 3:25 version that Different Recordings released on 8 April 2016, with its own ISRC.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} brings something the main artist does not have. Here it is a band: Say Yes Dog, a German-Luxembourgish trio of Aaron Ahrends, Paul Rundel and Pascal Karier, at home in synth-pop rather than in clubs. Claptone did the same with an American indie band on {{ <track_link source="claptone-clap-your-hands-say-yeah-ghost.md" /> }}.

The two releases show the logic of the {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}: the same track cut from more than five and a half minutes to under three and a half for radio and streaming rather than for DJs. What the shorter cut leaves out, no source spells out. Compare {{ <track_link source="clara-moto-gone-by-the-morning-radio-edit.md" /> }}.

Claptone's {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }} is a golden beaked mask resembling a plague doctor's, worn with white gloves. According to Wikipedia it conceals his identity and arouses intrigue, and little is known about who is behind it; there are even rumours that Claptone is more than one person.

## Artistic Context

Claptone is based in Berlin and gained recognition in 2013 with No Eyes, featuring Jaw. Charmer came out on Different Recordings in October 2015, and its single Heartbeat, sung by Nathan Nicholson of The Boxer Rebellion, came back in 2016 with a Doorly remix of Anything and this new B-side. Say Yes Dog had released their debut album, Plastic Love, the year before.

## Impact and Reception

Beyond the EP coverage, no review of the track on its own could be found, which is the usual fate of a B-side. It travelled anyway: in December 2016 it was also included on the Exploited compilation Shir Khan Presents Exploited 2016 Ffwd Rwd.
