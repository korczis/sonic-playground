## Genre and Mood

**Chilly** remixed their own suite and released it in 2013 as **For Your Love Suite 2014 full version - Chilly Remix**: nine and a half minutes of disco that is a remix, a cover and a revival all at once. The groove is the point, and so is the nostalgia, since the song underneath it was already old when Chilly first recorded it in 1978.

## Song Summary

For Your Love began as a 1965 single by the Yardbirds, written by Graham Gouldman at eighteen and built on a harpsichord, bongos and a bowed bass. In 1978 the German producer Bernt Möhrle made it the title piece of Chilly's debut album, For Your Love: the song followed by a suite, arranged with Christian Kolonovits, to which Möhrle added new lyrics and music under the title I Walk Alone. The track ran almost twelve minutes and became an international disco hit. This remix, credited to Chilly, appeared on the release For Your Love new mix 2014 and lasts 9:30. No review we found describes what the remix changes, so the page says less about its sound than about its history.

## Concepts in Focus

The track stacks two ideas that are often confused. A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of someone else's song, which is what Chilly's 1978 For Your Love was. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks an existing recording, which is what this 2013 release says it is, made by the group itself. The song has had other lives too: DJ Hell remixed Chilly's version on his album Munich Machine, D-Classics released an edit called 4 Love in 2006, and Chocolate Puma recorded a version with Colonel Red in 2011.

The long form belongs to the {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }}. Disco suites stretched a song well past radio length so that DJs could keep a floor moving; the 1978 original runs almost twelve minutes, this remix nine and a half.

## Artistic Context

Chilly was a German Eurodisco and rock group active from 1978 to 1983, created and produced by Möhrle, with Ute Weber and Brad Howell as singers and four dancers. After For Your Love came Come to L.A., Showbiz, Johnny Loves Jenny, Secret Lies and Devils Dance. Compare {{ <track_link source="boney-m-sunny.md" /> }}, another German-made Eurodisco cover of a 1960s song.

## Impact and Reception

The 1978 suite spent seven weeks on Billboard's dance club chart in the United States and peaked at number 38 in April 1979. No source reports how this remix was received. Start with the Yardbirds' single, then let this one run.
