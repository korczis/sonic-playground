## Genre and Mood

**Alec Troniq** and **Gabriel Vitel** made **Old Tortures** for the club, but not only for it. Their label, Broque, described the EP it comes from as swinging yet narrative house music, real songs that tell stories, clocked right for the dancefloor, and tagged it house, tech house, pop and vocal. Hypnotic is what a long house track aims for; the introspective side comes from a title, and a voice, that sound like more than a dance tool.

## Song Summary

Old Tortures is the third of six tracks on The Aviating, released by Broque in November 2015 under the catalogue number brq 108, and at eight minutes and forty-eight seconds it is the longest. The label credits every song to the two of them as writers and producers. According to Broque, the EP's stories are about life on the road, about clubs, festivals, airports and hotels; it does not say which story this track tells, and this page does not guess.

## Concepts in Focus

The ground floor is the {{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" /> }} kick, a beat on every count of the bar. It lets a DJ blend one record into the next and lets dancers stay with a track for nine minutes, and it is what a vocal has to sit on without fighting it.

The track belongs to a particular {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}: a small German label, two musicians who play as a live act, and early feedback from DJs and radio hosts rather than charts. On Broque's page, one radio DJ predicted it would bang clubs all over the world, and another thought it could even be tried on radio.

Gabriel Vitel's own channel carries a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} of the song with the Dortmunder Philharmoniker. A live version with an orchestra is a different recording of the same song, and a good test of what the studio version's machines were doing.

## Artistic Context

According to Discogs, Alec Troniq is a live act and electronic producer from Dresden, and Gabriel Vitel, from Schwäbisch Hall and now in Dresden, has been a songwriter since 2012 and an indie dance live act since 2013, playing live with Troniq. The Aviating was their first joint release on Broque. Compare {{ <track_link source="alec-troniq-gabriel-vitel-faces.md" /> }}, the track just before it.

## Impact and Reception

Old Tortures is not a chart record. On the label's feedback page one listener called it his favourite on the EP, and it earns that the slow way. Give it the full nine minutes.
