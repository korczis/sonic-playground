## Genre and Mood

**Beltran**'s **Smack Yo'** is tech house at its most stripped and physical. Its label, Solid Grooves Raw, calls it a murky and twisting peak-time cut armed with punchy drums, warped vocal samples and weighty low end, and that is the whole recipe: dark in colour, energetic in purpose, made for the hour when a club is fullest.

## Song Summary

The track began away from any studio. Beltran told Beatportal that the idea came to him while he was waiting for the bus to work: he could not get the vinyl scratches and the bassline out of his head, so he took out his phone and recorded himself singing the beat. He then rebuilt the idea in his production software, and said the beat came out exactly as intended and the vocal fit like a glove.

### Drums and Bass

The track is built for DJs, and its foundation is rhythm: the punchy drums the label names and the bassline Beltran heard first, carrying the weight the label calls peak time.

### The Vocal

The warped vocal samples turn a voice into a hook, bent and repeated over the groove rather than sung as a story. How the track opens and closes, no source describes.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the repeating low part that carries a dance track, and here it is where the idea started: Beltran named it, with the vinyl scratches, as the sound stuck in his head at the bus stop.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} usually means borrowing a piece of another recording; in Smack Yo' the label hears vocal samples warped until they are more texture and rhythm than words. Compare a vocal taunt used the same way in {{ <track_link source="nils-ohrmann-in-ya-face-original-mix.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} shaped the record's path. Solid Grooves' co-founders Michael Bibi and PAWSA test unreleased tracks in their sets, and the label described Smack Yo' as a firm favourite of Bibi's and a stand-out of his 2022 sets before it came out.

## Artistic Context

Beltran is the Brazilian DJ and producer Juliano Beltran, from a small town in southern Brazil; Nylon reports that he worked in IT security before music took over, and that his first official gig was the party Basement in Porto Alegre. He told Beatportal he had sent promos to Michael Bibi for almost three years before Smack Yo' became his debut on Solid Grooves Raw, the UK label's sister imprint, in 2022.

## Impact and Reception

Smack Yo' became Beltran's first Beatport number one, reaching the platform's overall top spot, and Nylon called it a runaway hit. Its life went on in other people's mixes: MusicBrainz lists it on Ministry of Sound's Ibiza Sessions 2023 and on a 2023 One Mix by A-Trak. Turn it up and follow the bass.
