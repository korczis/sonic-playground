## Genre and Mood

**Matt Lange** released **Pure Ruin** as a single track of 5:34, the length of a record made for DJs rather than for radio. Traxsource files it under techno and Apple Music under electronic. No published review describes it, so its two feelings, dark and intense, rest on those classifications, on the title and on how Vice summed up Lange's music in 2015: dark, driving, futurist house and techno. Read them as context, not as a listener's account.

## Song Summary

Pure Ruin came out on 13 December 2019 on isorhythm, the label Lange runs, and on the same day he posted it on his own YouTube channel with the line ℗ 2019 isorhythm. It is a one-track release, and his Wikipedia discography counts it among his non-album singles. I found no lyric, no credits beyond his name and no description of its sound, so this review does not say whether it has a voice or how it is built.

## Concepts in Focus

Pure Ruin is close to a {{ <concept_link name="self-release" /> }}. Lange founded Isorhythm in 2011 as a platform for his own music, so the ℗ line names a company, but the company is his. An artist-run label gives the same freedoms as releasing alone: he chooses when a track comes out and in what form, here a single track between two larger projects.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the instrument his work is known for. Wikipedia credits his use of modular synthesis and of isorhythms; EARMILK traced the label's name to that technique of the fourteenth and fifteenth centuries, built on repeated rhythmic patterns. No source says which synthesizers or which patterns are heard on Pure Ruin; listen for the sounds themselves and whether their patterns repeat at different lengths.

## Artistic Context

Lange was born in Manhattan and works from Los Angeles. He started on piano at four, sang in a boys' choir, played guitar in a hardcore band and graduated from Berklee College of Music. His early records were progressive and deep house, beginning with a single on Armada Music; deadmau5 then signed his debut album Ephemera to Mau5trap in 2015, and from that year he moved towards techno. In 2019 he returned to Anjunabeats with Every Word and the Isn't It Lovely EP, a few weeks before Pure Ruin. His second album, Isolated, followed on Isorhythm in 2020.
