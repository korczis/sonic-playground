## Genre and Mood

**Emmit Fenn** made **Control** from almost nothing: one line, a low voice and a groove. It sits in indie electronic and electronic music, and its mood is hypnotic and groovy, but also introspective, because the line it repeats is an instruction to yourself, to let the feelings take control.

## Song Summary

Control is the middle track of The Last Dance, a three-song EP released on 28 March 2019 by TH3RD BRAIN, between Higher and Overflow. At 2:50 it is the shortest of the three. Writing for We Are: The Guard that May, Calvin Paradise heard the song as a mantra: a phrase repeated so often that it stops being heard consciously and starts working on the listener underneath.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is usually the line you remember. Here the hook is almost the whole song, and the review's point is that repetition changes what it does: after enough returns the words become part of the rhythm, and you are listening to a groove that happens to be made of a sentence. Compare {{ <track_link source="infinity-ink-infinity.md" /> }}, which also lives on one half-buried vocal line.

Fenn is a singer and a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}, and the second role has reached further than many listeners realise. He co-wrote and co-produced Billie Eilish's 2018 single Bitches Broken Hearts with Finneas O'Connell, the first Eilish song produced by someone other than Finneas, and is credited there with drum programming, piano, synthesizers, mixing and mastering as well.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} turns the EP into one film in three parts. Control is part two, published on Fenn's channel in April 2020: two dancers, Jessie Lee Thorne and Matt Luck, directed and shot by Conner Bell.

## Artistic Context

We Are: The Guard called The Last Dance Fenn's first sustained follow-up to his 2017 debut EP Prologue, and noted that he also produces for artists such as Billie Eilish and RY X and had played Coachella that spring. The Last Dance became a series: a second part followed in 2022 and a third in 2025, both on TH3RD BRAIN.

## Impact and Reception

Control travelled beyond its EP: remixes of it later appeared as singles, among them a Dallin Dance Remix in 2023. Critical coverage is thin; the one full review we found was enthusiastic, ending on the feeling that you want to dance and never want the song to stop. Play it twice in a row and see whether you notice the second start.
