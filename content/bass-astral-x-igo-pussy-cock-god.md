## Genre and Mood

**Bass Astral x Igo**'s **Pussy Cock & God** is electronic music with an alternative edge. Apple Music files the album under electronic, and a Polish reviewer heard it as minimalist electronics, putting this track among the songs that form an electronic alternative around a strong voice. The mood is energetic, and the title alone is provocative.

## Song Summary

The track is the eighth of ten on Discobolus, the duo's debut album, released on 24 June 2016 by Iglo Records, the label Igor Walaszek founded. Its title puts sex and God in one line; no source discusses the lyric, so this page does not guess at it. What the sources do describe is how the album was made: Kuba Tracz produced it at home, and Walaszek sang over his tracks.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through this track; the notes below come from a review of the album and from the producer's own account.

### Intro

Writing on allaboutmusic.pl, Dominika Mrówczyńska described Suspicious Mind as opening on sounds that later return only in certain parts, and put Pussy Cock & God in the same group. Listen for which opening sounds come back, and where.

### Main Section

Walaszek's strong voice comes to the front; the same review heard a fair amount of unease in his singing across the album. Underneath, Tracz said, the debut was built mainly with two Native Instruments plugins, the Massive synthesizer and the Battery drum sampler, used minimally on purpose.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in a duo like this is also the composer: Tracz wrote the music in Ableton, and Walaszek's voice completed it.

A {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} no longer has to be a keyboard. Massive is software, and Tracz said he wanted to squeeze as much as he could out of the tools he already had.

{{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" text="Four on the floor" /> }} is the bass drum on every beat. Tracz said that in his rock band the drummer hated playing it, and only in this duo could he put it in every song.

## Artistic Context

Tracz and Walaszek met in the Kraków rock band Clock Machine and started the duo in 2015, at first to play a few seaside gigs. A concert at Spirala in Gliwice, filmed and shared online, became their breakthrough. Three albums followed until 2021: Discobolus, Orell and Satellite.

## Impact and Reception

Discobolus and Orell both went gold in Poland. At the duo's sold-out farewell concert in Poznań in September 2021, Radio Meteor reported that the real frenzy came with the hits Bikini and Pussy Cock & God. Turn it up and see why.
