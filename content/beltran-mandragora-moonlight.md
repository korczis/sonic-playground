## Genre and Mood

**Beltran & Mandragora**'s **Moonlight** is minimal house built for the small hours: SubSoul, posting it the day after release, called it a minimal deep tech groover, and Apple Music files it under house. It is groovy in the plainest sense, a track that lives on its beat, and catchy because of one thing above it, a lead vocal that Electronic Groove heard as catchy and psychedelic.

## Song Summary

Moonlight came out on 8 December 2023 on the label Revival New York as a single of five and a half minutes. Electronic Groove listed its ingredients: the psychedelic lead vocal, powerful kicks, deep sub-bass and captivating synths. By then, the same write-up reported, it had already drawn attention from the DJs Marco Carola, Luciano and Dennis Cruz.

### Main Section

No timed analysis of Moonlight exists that I could find, and I could find no official upload to point you to, so this page keeps to what the write-ups describe. The weight sits low: the kicks and, under them, a deep sub-bass. Above them run the synths, and over everything the vocal that gives the track its hook. ## Concepts in Focus

In a track like this the {{ <concept_link name="kick drum" /> }} is not just timekeeping: Electronic Groove picked out the kicks as powerful, one of the four things worth naming. Underneath, the sub-bass works as a {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} you feel as much as hear, which is why minimal tracks can sound almost empty on a phone and full on a club system.

With so few elements, the vocal becomes the {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}. A single vocal line heard as psychedelic can carry a dance track the way a chorus carries a pop song. Compare the vocal taunt in {{ <track_link source="nils-ohrmann-in-ya-face-original-mix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Beltran is the Brazilian DJ and producer Juliano Beltran, from a small town in southern Brazil; his first official gig was a party called Basement in Porto Alegre. He describes his sound to Nylon as minimal references, some indie, some house, and likes the words trippy and weird for it; labels once turned him down because his sound was too weird. His Smack Yo', sung into his phone while waiting for a bus, became his first Beatport number one in 2022 on Solid Grooves Raw, and his next record, Warning, was announced for Revival New York. Mandragora's own channel has posted a live version that runs Moonlight together with Smack Yo'. For more Brazilian house on a label abroad, hear {{ <track_link source="funky-fat-and-digitaria-you-bring-me-down.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No chart placing is recorded for Moonlight; its reception is the one club tracks have, the attention of the DJs who play them. Find a system with real bass and let the vocal do the rest.
