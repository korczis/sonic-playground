## Genre and Mood

**White Town** is Jyoti Mishra, and **Your Woman** is the indie pop song he made at home that went to number one. It is synth-pop with a borrowed past: bittersweet and full of longing in its words, and catchy enough to be sung along to before anyone noticed what the words said.

## Song Summary

Mishra has said he was trying to write a pop song with more than one perspective: written in the first person, but the character behind the voice is not necessarily who a casual listener would expect. Among its themes he lists a straight man in love with a lesbian, a gay man in love with a straight man, and the hypocrisy that results when love and lust meet highbrow ideals. The music sets a muted trumpet, sampled from a 1932 recording, against an upbeat groove, and the whole record was made with free MIDI sequencing software on an Atari ST and a cheap cassette multitrack.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The song opens on the trumpet: a phrase from My Woman by Lew Stone and his Monseigneur Band, played by Nat Gonella. Mishra found it through the BBC series Pennies From Heaven.

### Main Section

Under the vocal runs a rhythm that Billboard's Larry Flick traced to Chic's Good Times, with jangly guitar licks, a bouncing backbeat and distorted vocals. Mishra hears George Clinton-style funk, Depeche Mode-inspired electro pop and 1990s boom-bap in it. Slant Magazine heard his plaintive tenor turn the refrain, never being the right kind of girl, into one of the most subversive moments in 1990s pop.

### Outro

No source describes how the song ends; listen for whether the trumpet has the last word.

## Concepts in Focus

Your Woman shows what {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} can do to mood. The sampled record was sung by Al Bowlly, and Mishra called his song a flip of its anti-woman theme; the trumpet keeps the 1930s melancholy while the beat moves forward. Dua Lipa later sampled the same Lew Stone record on Love Again.

It is also a {{ <concept_link name="lo-fi" /> }} success story: the EP's title, >Abort, Retry, Fail?_, is the MS-DOS error Mishra met when his hard drive failed during mixing.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Mark Adcock on a 16 mm Bolex and shot largely in Derby, imitates black-and-white silent film, with expressionist gestures, circular masks and a door scene borrowed from Un chien andalou.

## Artistic Context

Mishra, born in Rourkela, India, in 1966 and raised in Derby, started White Town as a band in 1989 and ran it alone from 1990. Your Woman first appeared in 1996 on the US indie label Parasol Records; after Mark Radcliffe played it on BBC Radio 1, Chrysalis, Brilliant! and EMI reissued the EP in January 1997. It led his second album, Women in Technology, released on 24 February 1997.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number one in the UK, Iceland, Israel and Spain, and Wikipedia calls it the first gender-reversal song to top the UK chart. Q, Pitchfork, Slant and Billboard have ranked it since.
