## Genre and Mood

**renforshort** takes **feel good inc.** out of Gorillaz' cartoon world and into alternative pop. The lower-case title is hers, and so is the attitude: bold, energetic and a little sharper than the 2005 original, with a guest guitar solo that pushes the cover toward rock.

## Song Summary

Gorillaz' Feel Good Inc., the lead single of Demon Days, is built around a bass line, a sung chorus and De La Soul's rap. That last part was the problem. renforshort told Dork the song was hard to get right because it is rap-heavy and she is not a rapper, so she spent a long time on the arrangement. Jeff Hazin produced it, and the Brazilian guitarist Mateus Asato, credited as featured artist, plays the solo. Dork called the result a razor-sharp pop belter and an alt-glitching delight. It came out on 11 December 2020 through Interscope.

## Musical Breakdown

The sources describe the voice and the solo in detail; they do not walk through the rest of the arrangement, so neither does this breakdown.

### Main Section

The song is carried by renforshort's voice rather than by a rapper's, which is the cover's central decision. How exactly she reworks De La Soul's verse no source spells out; listen for it yourself against the original.

### Guitar Solo

Guitar World's Matt Owen wrote that Asato is not afraid to crank up the overdrive for his feature. The solo opens with rapid-fire hammer-ons, settles into a tasteful pentatonic line marked by whole-note bends and his sliding style, and climaxes with a tremolo-picked run in the manner of Beat It. renforshort's verdict: Asato killed the guitar.

## Artistic Context

renforshort is a Toronto singer-songwriter who was 18 when Spill introduced this cover. She signed with Interscope and released her debut EP teenage angst on 13 March 2020; Mike Shinoda of Linkin Park remixed its song i drive me mad. She told Dork the idea for this cover came when Rhinestone Eyes was blowing up on TikTok. Mateus Asato, from Campo Grande in Brazil, became known through videos of his playing posted on Instagram.

## Impact and Reception

The cover closed a strong year for renforshort, which Dork marked with nostalgic (luvsick) and afterthoughts, and she called it one of her favourite releases in a long time. Guitar World covered it for Asato's solo alone. Play it straight after the Gorillaz original and listen for where the rap used to be.
