## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** filed **Bought and Sold**, printed Bought & Sold on the album, on their own Bandcamp under blues, electronic, soul, abstract and turntable, and Tru Thoughts introduced the trio's sound in the band's own words, handmade hip-hop blues soul. No critic seems to have written about this song on its own, so trip-hop, soul and blues are the album's frame rather than a verdict on the track, and soulful and nostalgic are this page's reading of how the label sold the record: a trawl through a dusty basement full of the best records you have never heard of.

## Song Summary

Bought & Sold is the eleventh and last track on Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts on 16 July 2007. At 2:39 it is the shortest of the eleven songs; every other one runs past three minutes. The album credits all three members, Ricky Fabulous, Kathrin deBoer and DJ Modest, with the writing, production, engineering and recording, done at a place they called Flat 2, and J. Howes mastered it at The Premises. Neither the Bandcamp page nor any review gives the lyric or explains the title, so this page does not guess at it.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005: Kathrin deBoer, Ricky Fabulous and DJ Modest. Their first records were limited 7-inch singles on their own Hippoflex label before the Brighton label Tru Thoughts signed them in 2007; they stayed until 2013 and made four more records there, from The Express in 2008 to Rollerchain in 2012. The band announced its end in October 2013, and in 2025 it returned on Hippoflex with new material.

## Impact and Reception

Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut album in Tru Thoughts' history. XLR8R called it sweet, sweet soul; Basic Soul found the set consistent throughout but lacking a particular standout moment, though worth investigating. Neither review mentions the closing song. In 2012 the label posted it on its YouTube jukebox channel, four years after the live film. Let the album run to its end and hear how it signs off.
