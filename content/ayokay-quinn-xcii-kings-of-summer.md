## Genre and Mood

**ayokay** and **Quinn XCII** made **Kings of Summer** for the last warm evenings of the year. It is indie pop with electro-pop sparkle and an easy pop hook, nostalgic, carefree and uplifting, a song about youth that already knows it is looking back.

## Song Summary

The song remembers summers spent with friends, when every night felt like it mattered and nobody worried about what came next. ayokay's production is bright and roomy, built from shimmering synths, a light, bouncing beat and plucked melodic hooks, and Quinn XCII sings with a relaxed, conversational warmth that makes the memories feel shared. The memorable detail is the way the chorus lifts like a car window rolling down. Nothing in the production is heavy or busy; every element sounds like it was chosen to keep the mood light, which is exactly why the nostalgia lands so gently. It is a song that smiles more than it dances.

## Musical Breakdown

The track follows a clean pop structure with a producer's ear for texture.

### Intro

Glimmering synths and a gentle pulse set a sunlit scene before the voice arrives.

### Verse 1

Quinn XCII's vocal enters relaxed and close, sketching the friendships and late nights the song looks back on.

### Build-up

The beat tightens and layers stack up, building quiet anticipation for the hook.

### Chorus

The title hook opens out into a bright, singable refrain, the moment the nostalgia turns into a celebration.

### Bridge

A pared-back passage lets the melody breathe before the final chorus returns.

### Outro

The synths shimmer out slowly, like the last light of a long evening.

## Artistic Context

ayokay is an American producer known for melodic, pop-leaning electronic music with a warm, hand-played feel. "Kings of Summer" features his childhood friend and frequent collaborator Quinn XCII, and it was released as a single on Columbia Records in 2016, after an earlier free release online. The track helped set the template for both artists: warm, nostalgic, synth-touched pop with a strong sense of place and friendship.

## Impact and Reception

The song became a viral success and reached the Billboard Hot Dance/Electronic Songs chart, giving both artists a breakthrough moment and a lasting fan favourite. It still turns up on every summer playlist worth its sunscreen. Put it on during a drive with the windows down, preferably with the people you would call your own kings of summer.
