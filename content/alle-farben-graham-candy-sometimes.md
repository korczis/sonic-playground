## Genre and Mood

**Alle Farben** and **Graham Candy** found a perfect match on **Sometimes**, a deep-house pop song with an electronic shimmer and a summer heart. It is bittersweet, dreamy and uplifting all at once, the sound of a sunny day with a little cloud in it.

## Song Summary

The song is about the days that do not go to plan, the ups and downs everyone knows, and the quiet hope that things will turn around. Alle Farben builds a soft, bouncing groove of warm bass, gentle kicks and bright melodic touches, and Graham Candy's high, fluttering voice carries the melody with a fragility that makes the simple lyrics feel personal. The memorable detail is that voice: once heard, it is impossible to mistake. The production never fights it. Instead the beat stays light and springy, leaving the voice room to wobble and soar, and the whole song feels like a walk along a canal on a warm afternoon when your thoughts drift somewhere between happy and homesick.

## Artistic Context

Alle Farben is the alias of Berlin DJ and producer Frans Zimmer, whose melodic, playful house became a staple of German festivals and radio in the mid-2010s. "Sometimes" appears on his debut album Synesthesia, released in 2014, the album's title echoing the colour-and-sound idea behind his name, which means "all colours". Graham Candy, a singer from New Zealand, became one of his key collaborators, also appearing on "She Moves (Far Away)".

## Impact and Reception

"Sometimes" became one of Alle Farben's breakthrough songs, spreading quickly across German-speaking radio and earning a run of remixes, from club mixes to versions by LCAW and others. It helped define a moment when gentle, melodic house crossed into the pop mainstream. Put it on for a slow summer evening and enjoy its little shot of melancholy.
