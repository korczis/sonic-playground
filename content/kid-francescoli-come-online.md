## Genre and Mood

**Kid Francescoli**'s **Come Online**, credited with **Julia Minkin**, is short electronic pop from the Marseille project's 2017 album Play Me Again. It is filed here as sultry and intimate, following Radio Nova, which introduced Kid Francescoli as a maker of soft, sensual pop; we found no review of this particular song, and the page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

Come Online is the tenth of the album's eleven tracks and runs 2:38. The label's metadata credits the music to Mathieu Hocine, the musician behind Kid Francescoli, and Simon Henner, and the words to Julia Minkin. Those are the same three writers as on Moon (And It Went Like), the track just before it. No source we found explains the lyric, so this review does not guess at it.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or interview describes the arrangement part by part, so this breakdown keeps to what the album itself documents.

### Coming Out of Moon

Moon is the longest track on Play Me Again at 6:30. Come Online follows it at well under half that length, the shortest song on the record apart from the 28-second Intermezzo.

### The Recording

Henner and Hocine recorded and produced the album in Marseille; Antoine Thibaudeau mixed it at Rumble Sound Studio and Darius van Helfteren mastered it at Amsterdam Mastering. Listen for what a two-and-a-half-minute pop song needs, and what it leaves out.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is usually a guest. Minkin is something else: Hocine, quoted by Frontview Magazine, said she was very involved in the composition, writes her own lyrics and melodies, and would tour with him because they work as a duo. The credit next to the project's name is the nearest the release format comes to saying that.

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} can also be an author. Simon Henner, who records as French 79, produced Play Me Again with Hocine and shares the writing credit on this song and on Moon.

## Artistic Context

Mathieu Hocine founded Kid Francescoli in Marseille in 2002, naming it after the Uruguayan footballer Enzo Francescoli. Hocine met Minkin, a singer from Chicago, at a concert in New York in 2009; their album With Julia followed, and she moved to Marseille. Play Me Again continues that story: the romance had ended, and it had turned into a musical partnership.

## Impact and Reception

Come Online was not among the album's singles, but Kid Francescoli played it live: at Radio Nova on 20 March 2017, as one of two songs chosen from the new album, and at Light FM in Beirut in early 2018. Its neighbour Moon went viral on TikTok in 2020 and became the project's biggest hit. Play the two together.
