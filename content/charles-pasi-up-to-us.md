## Genre and Mood

**Charles Pasi** opens his record Uncaged with **Up to Us**, and the record is where, by Libération's account as Wikipedia reports it, he moved beyond the blues of his first release towards "more soul and funky textures". That sentence is where the genres and the two feelings on this page come from: soulful and groovy, with the blues he came from still in the frame. No review describes Up to Us itself, so this page says what can be known and no more.

## Song Summary

Up to Us is Pasi's own song: the distributor's upload names him as author and composer. It runs three minutes fifty and stands first in the running order of both versions of Uncaged, the 2009 EP and the twelve-track album of 2011, so it is the song he chose, twice, to open the record. Beyond that, the sources are silent: no interview explains the title or how the song was made.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="blues" /> }} is Pasi's starting point. He won prizes at the Blues sur Seine contest and at the FestiBlues international de Montréal in 2005, and his first release, the self-produced EP Mainly Blue of 2006, was English-language blues. Uncaged, as its title suggests and as Libération heard it, was the step out of that frame.

There is a second recording to compare: an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} filmed as an acoustic session by the webzine Le Cargo! and published in March 2011, the season the album came out. Le Cargo! introduced it with a single line of praise for what he can do with a harmonica. Hearing the song stripped down next to the record is the clearest way to tell the song from its production.

## Artistic Context

Pasi was born in Paris to an Italian father and a French mother. He grew up on Ray Charles, Little Richard and Percy Sledge, joined a gospel choir at seventeen, took up the harmonica after hearing Bob Dylan and Neil Young, and studied jazz in Rome and Paris. The Uncaged EP won him a competition at the Printemps de Bourges that brought digital distribution by Believe; the 2011 album added the saxophonist Archie Shepp on two tracks. Stereofox later called the harmonica his favourite of the instruments he plays. Hear another harmonica player between blues and soul in {{ <track_link source="son-of-dave-devil-take-my-soul.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No chart placing or review is recorded for Up to Us on its own. Pasi's path after it is documented: guitar for Carla Bruni, a nomination as revelation of the year at the 2014 Victoires du Jazz, the opening slot on Neil Young's French tour in 2016 and the album Bricks on Blue Note in 2017. Start the record where he started it, with this song.
