## Genre and Mood

**Extrawelt**'s **Fernweh** is an instrumental from the meeting point of techno, minimal and tech house, the styles Discogs files its first 12-inch under. Its title gives the mood: Fernweh is the German word for a longing for faraway places, built from fern, far, and Weh, pain, as the opposite of Heimweh, homesickness. Longing and a hypnotic pull are the feelings named here; no published review describes the track's sound in detail, so this page says less than it would like to.

## Song Summary

Fernweh was the Hamburg duo's second release. Arne Schaffhausen and Wayan Raabe wrote and produced it, Marcus C. Maichel is credited with additional programming, and it came out on 17 May 2006 as the A-side of a 45 RPM 12-inch on Kompass Musik, backed with Drehfehler and mastered at Dubplates & Mastering in Berlin. The record was repressed in May 2008, reissued digitally by Cocoon Recordings in December 2009 and, remastered, chosen to open Extra Welt Hits in 2020.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }} goes back to a finished mix and prepares it again for a new release: level, tone, the balance of low and high end. Extra Welt Hits was presented as a box of newly remastered tracks, so the 2020 Fernweh is the same composition through a newer final stage. Compare a German club classic remastered for an anniversary in {{ <track_link source="m-a-n-d-y-booka-shade-body-language-original-remaster.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} also makes an argument. Extra Welt Hits gathers fifteen years in fifteen tracks, and the release's own text calls its opener, Fernweh, a monumental masterpiece; that is a label's praise, not a critic's verdict.

Long before that, Fernweh travelled through the {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }}. Discogs lists it on mixed CDs from 2006 to 2008, among them King Unique and NuBreed's Electric_02 and DJ Dani Gygax's Street Parade 2008: Underground.

## Artistic Context

Schaffhausen and Raabe began making music together in the winter of 1998-99 and played live as Spirallianz and Midimiliz, a trance-rooted project. They founded Extrawelt in 2005 and debuted on Border Community with the Soopertrack EP; Fernweh followed a year later, with Doch Doch and Titelheld right after. Resident Advisor's review of their 2008 debut album heard the duo's more refined tech house still carrying the dark-corridor depths of their past.

## Impact and Reception

Fernweh has been repressed, reissued and remastered across two decades, which says more than any review could. Play the remaster loud, and let the title do the rest.
