## Genre and Mood

**Kungs** and **Cookin' on 3 Burners**' **This Girl** is a remix that became the song most people know: a soul record from Melbourne turned into French house. Groovy and upbeat, it keeps the warmth of a band playing in a room while it moves to a beat built for clubs and radio.

## Song Summary

The original This Girl, written by the trio's Jake Mason, Lance Ferguson and Ivan Khatchoyan and sung by Kylie Auldist, was released as a single in June 2009 from the album Soul Messin'. Kungs told iHeartRadio that he found it during a YouTube session of soul and blues tracks and knew at once that he wanted to remix it. He released his version on 19 February 2016 as the lead single of his debut album Layers, where it is the second track, three minutes and fifteen seconds long.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found walks through the remix part by part, and no source gives times. Kungs' own account says what he did, so this guide follows it.

### Main Section

He kept the trumpets, the bass and the funk, soul and blues energy of the original and built a dance track around them, with Auldist's voice on top.

### What Was Cut

He said he removed the parts he found boring and kept the ones he liked most. The original runs three minutes and forty-five seconds; the album version is half a minute shorter, and an extended mix of about four minutes came out on the EP.

## Concepts in Focus

This Girl shows what a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can be: not a new song, but an edit and a rebuild of an existing recording, credited as Kungs vs. Cookin' on 3 Burners. Kungs said the band were unsure at first, then liked the track and agreed to release it.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Matt Larson for La Main Productions and released on Kungs' Vevo channel on 24 March 2016, was filmed on the Greek island of Milos: a couple meet on a cruise ship, dance in their cabin, explore the island's lakes and hills and end up around a bonfire in a cave.

{{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }} carried it further. Wikipedia lists Apple advertisements and the Shot on iPhone challenge, a 2016 Littlewoods campaign in Britain and a 2017 Cricket Wireless commercial.

## Artistic Context

Kungs is Valentin Brunel, born in Toulon in 1996, who began posting his own music online at seventeen and built a following with remixes of Bob Marley and Lana Del Rey. Cookin' on 3 Burners are a Melbourne funk trio with Mason on Hammond organ and Khatchoyan on drums.

## Impact and Reception

The remix reached number one in France, Germany and Wallonia, number two in Britain and number twenty-six on the Billboard Hot 100, won the NRJ Music Award for best dance or electro single and was nominated for an ARIA Song of the Year. Press play and listen for the trumpets.
