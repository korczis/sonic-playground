## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**, **The Lost Amigos**, **Sunru** and **Nereo II** are joined by **Roderic** on **One Day Left - Roderic Remix**, a house and electro rework of a 2017 track. The release's own text gives it four words, a dirty funky beach shake, and the feelings chosen here follow them: groovy for the funk and the shake, gritty for the dirt.

## Song Summary

One Day Left began as a seven-minute track on Pilocka Krach's double album Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, with words by its two singers, Sun Ru Carter and Nereo II, and music by Pilocka Krach. In September 2021 she released One Day Left, a record of five remixes, two instrumentals and the original. Roderic's version is the third track, 6:15 long, and it keeps the featured singers in its title. No source publishes the lyric or describes the remix beyond that one line, so this page stays close to it.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording from its parts. A remix release like this one lets you hear how differently five producers read the same material: Franz Alice Stern made what the release calls an epic synth monster, OID added rocking beats and a new vocal by the Russian artist Chikiss, DJ B2B a slow sundowner, and Roderic a beach shake.

That word, shake, points to the {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}: the rhythmic feel that makes a body move, more than any single sound. In funk, much of the groove lives in syncopated accents and the gaps between them. Compare it with the disco feel the label hears in {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-the-lost-amigos-sunru-nereo-ii-one-day-left-original.md" /> }}.

Each remix is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} with its own title and length; this one is 45 seconds shorter than the original.
