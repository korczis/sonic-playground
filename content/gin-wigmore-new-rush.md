## Genre and Mood

**Gin Wigmore** comes out swinging on **New Rush**. It is alternative rock with a pop hook and a rock core, built on pounding drums and a chorus made for shouting along, and its feelings are energetic, empowering and bold: the sound of someone kicking a door open.

## Song Summary

The song is about the thrill of a fresh start, the jolt of adrenaline that comes with leaving something behind. Wigmore's voice, husky and commanding, rides a muscular arrangement of big drums, gritty guitars and darker, more modern production than her earlier vintage-styled records. The chorus is the payoff, a chant-like hook that turns a personal feeling into a collective one.

## Musical Breakdown

The track is compact and driven, designed to build momentum from the first bar.

### Intro

A heavy, stomping beat and a menacing low end set a tense, dramatic tone straight away.

### Verse 1

Wigmore enters low and controlled, her raspy voice coiled like a spring over the pounding rhythm.

### Build-up

The tension rises as guitars and layered vocals gather, the drums hitting harder with every bar.

### Chorus

The chorus explodes into a full-throated, chant-along hook, her voice at its most powerful and the band at full volume.

### Bridge

A short drop in intensity strips things back, letting the voice and beat breathe before the final surge.

### Climax

The last chorus is the biggest of all, with stacked vocals and crashing drums giving it the feel of a stadium anthem.

### Outro

The song ends sharply, leaving a ringing silence where the rush was.

## Artistic Context

"New Rush" was released in April 2015 as the lead single from Blood to Bone, the third album by the New Zealand singer-songwriter, issued on Island Records. After the retro soul and rock and roll of Gravel & Wine, the album pushed her sound towards a darker, heavier and more contemporary style, and this song, her first new single in years, was its calling card, a clear signal that she had changed direction.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 39 on the New Zealand chart, while Blood to Bone became her third consecutive number-one album there. The song found an even wider audience when it was picked for the FIFA 16 soundtrack, putting her voice in front of millions of players around the world. It is here for its sheer energy. Put it on before a workout, a big day or anything that needs a push.
