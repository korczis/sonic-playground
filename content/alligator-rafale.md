## Genre and Mood

**Alligator**'s **Rafale** is French synth-pop, electronic and small in scale. The music-data service Soundcharts describes the duo's world as lo-fi and touched by drifting house, with voices over spectral melodies, keyboards and drum machines and a retro-futuristic feel. The lyric gives the mood its direction: someone left in despair, brooding for the night. Melancholic in its words and dreamy in its haze, it still moves.

## Song Summary

Rafale means a gust or a burst, of wind or of gunfire. The song runs three and a half minutes and is the fourth of ten tracks on Octets, the duo's first album, released by the label La Souterraine on 3 November 2017. Its lyric, printed with the video on the duo's own channel, speaks to a woman alone in the shadows who broie du noir ce soir, an idiom for brooding in the dark, and promises to take her dancing.

## Musical Breakdown

### Two Versions

An eight-track Octet appeared earlier, in December 2016, and MusicBrainz lists its Rafale at more than five minutes. On the 2017 album the same title runs three minutes and twenty-nine seconds, the length of the version La Souterraine also placed on its compilation Pays vaincus that year.

### The Sound

No review walks through the track, so what to listen for comes from the duo's profile: keyboards and drum machines, spectral melodies and a lo-fi grain. Listen for how those parts sit together under the voices.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="drum machine" /> }} is an instrument that plays programmed rhythms; in synth-pop it replaces the drummer and gives a song its steady, mechanical pulse. Alligator's profile names drum machines among the tools of its sound, so listen to the beat of Rafale as something set rather than played.

{{ <concept_link name="lo-fi" /> }} is a sound with its rough edges left in: less polish, more grain, often by choice. Soundcharts uses the word for Alligator's whole world, and a {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} heard this way sounds warm and slightly worn rather than glossy. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="mount-sims-ashes.md" /> }}, synth-pop with a darker, sharper edge.

## Artistic Context

Alligator formed as a duo in 2017 around Octets, and La Souterraine, which released the album, had already placed Rafale on its compilation Pays vaincus a few months earlier. In 2022 the duo released a second album, Torrents, which Soundcharts describes as an exploration of melancholic tension.

## Impact and Reception

Soundcharts singles out Rafale as the track that stood out and was widely broadcast; it is the duo's most played song. It is here because it was among the owner's most played songs of 2022. Put it on late, and let the gust carry you.
