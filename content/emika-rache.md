## Genre and Mood

**Emika**'s **Rache** is German for revenge, and the album lists it as Rache (Revenge). It belongs to the electronic, experimental electro-pop of DREI, which Ibiza Spotlight heard as dark dub pop and Berlin-influenced electro with a subtle refinement from her classical training. Dark, defiant and celebratory all apply at once, because Spectrum Culture heard a celebration under the darkness: beneath its dark exterior, it wrote, Rache is a song of celebration.

## Song Summary

Rache is the seventh track of DREI, released on 4 May 2015 on Emika's own label, Emika Records, and runs four minutes and thirty-nine seconds. According to FACT, she wrote the album in around two weeks and produced and mixed it herself; Ibiza Spotlight added that she writes, produces, mixes and sings everything on her records. Spectrum Culture read Rache as Emika indulging in her revenge against the corporate monolith that is the music industry, and heard a feminist prowess adorning the album's messages without addressing it directly.

## Concepts in Focus

Emika did the {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} of DREI herself. Mixing decides how loud each part is, where it sits and how it sounds against the others, so when the songwriter is also the mixer, nobody stands between the idea and the finished balance. For a song read as revenge on the music industry, that independence is part of the point; Spectrum Culture quotes her wish to produce fat, hard beats without needing anyone.

Rache also exists as an instrumental, an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} released on her own channel. Hearing the track without its voice is a way to separate what the music says from what the words say. Compare the album's opening, {{ <track_link source="emika-battles.md" /> }}, where its theme of limits and freedom is first set out.

## Artistic Context

Emika is Ema Jolly, born in Milton Keynes, classically trained and holding a degree in music technology, who worked as a sound designer for Native Instruments in Berlin while developing her sound. DREI came after her self-titled debut of 2011, the album Dva and the piano record Klavirni. She now works from a studio in the Black Forest, and she releases on her own label.

## Impact and Reception

Rache was not a single, and most of the reviews cited here only list it; Spectrum Culture is the one that reads it. Listen to it as the sound of an artist doing everything herself, and enjoying it.
