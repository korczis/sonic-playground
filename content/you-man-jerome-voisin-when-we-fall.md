## Genre and Mood

**You Man** describe themselves as deviant, hypnotic and futuristic electro disco, and **When We Fall** shows what they mean. It sits between deep house, the style Discogs files their album under, and the nu-disco of the duo's own Bandcamp tags, and it is dark, hypnotic and melancholic: a house beat on purpose left rough, under a melody that sounds older than the club it plays in.

## Song Summary

The duo told Konbini that the track started from a piano riff and that the whole structure came to them almost on its own. What it lacked was a voice with an unusual, slightly deviant timbre, to match music whose sounds were deliberately imprecise. They thought at once of Jérôme Voisin, known as Fonzie, a singer from the group GYM whom they knew a little; the next morning he sent them almost final vocal takes. Voisin wrote the words, which open by asking how many nights it will take. It was the last song the duo composed for their first album.

## Musical Breakdown

No timed analysis is published; the parts below follow what the duo and the press describe.

### Intro

The piano comes first in the accounts: Tsugi heard melodious piano chords, and the riff is the seed of the whole track.

### Main Section

A beat joins the piano, which Tsugi heard as a 1990s electro bass beat and the duo call a house beat that is not very clean. Over it runs the paradox the duo built the song on, a fairly dark, baroque melody played on steel drums. Voisin's voice carries the lyric through it.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} is the root here: one short piano figure from which, by the duo's account, the rest of the track grew.

As a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Voisin was not a vocalist hired for a finished track but the missing piece of one, chosen for his timbre. The duo said he seemed completely inhabited by the song, beyond what they had expected.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, written, directed and edited by Quentin Tavernier, was You Man's first narrative video after two DIY clips. Tsugi describes a man dressed all in white searching for his ideal, in poetic and mystical images shot partly by drone; Cavalcade, which lists the film in its portfolio, calls it a metaphor for a break-up, mixing Hauts-de-France realism, Greco-Roman imagery and Magritte-like surrealism.

## Artistic Context

You Man is a duo from Lille, credited by their label as a project by Tepat Huleux and Giac Di Falco. Spectrum of Love, eleven tracks produced and recorded by the duo and mastered by Mainro, came out on Alpage Records, first as a limited vinyl in October 2016 and digitally on 18 November 2016; When We Fall is its second track.

## Impact and Reception

Alpage Records posted the song as a new single in September 2016, two months before the album. Konbini called the album very good, and the video followed in March 2017. Listen for the steel drums, and ask why they sound so far from sunshine here.
