## Genre and Mood

**Kygo** and **Maty Noyes** shape **Stay** from tropical house, electronic textures and pop melody. It is dreamy and romantic, with a thread of longing running through its soft, sunny surface.

## Song Summary

The song is a plea for someone to stay just a little longer, sung with warmth and vulnerability. Kygo's production is gentle and airy: plucked melodic hooks, mellow piano, a relaxed beat and a drop that blooms rather than explodes. Maty Noyes brings a smooth, slightly husky vocal that keeps the emotion close and intimate, so the track feels like a quiet conversation set against a sunset. Nothing is rushed: the arrangement leaves room between notes, the drums stay soft and the melodic hooks drift in and out like waves on a warm shore. It is music for the last hour of a summer day, when you would rather nothing ended.

## Musical Breakdown

The track follows a classic tropical-house arc, swelling softly rather than building to a peak.

### Intro

Soft piano chords and ambient textures open the song, setting a calm, reflective mood.

### Verse 1

Noyes enters with a hushed, tender delivery, the arrangement staying sparse around her.

### Build-up

The beat begins to swell, with light percussion and rising pads hinting at the release to come.

### Chorus

The vocal hook blossoms over the beat, the emotion lifting without losing its gentleness.

### Main Section

Kygo's signature plucked melody takes over in the drop, bright and buoyant, carrying the song's longing on a breezy groove.

### Outro

The melody and vocal fade slowly, leaving the warm atmosphere hanging in the air.

## Concepts in Focus

Stay shows the tropical house version of the {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }}. In big-room EDM the drop is a wall of bass and drums after a build-up; Kygo's drop is a plucked synth melody that blooms over a relaxed beat. The release is still there, the moment the track opens up after tension, but it arrives as melody rather than impact, which suits a song that asks for tenderness.

Maty Noyes is credited as the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, but here the feature began the song. She wrote it with the Norwegian producer William Wiik Larsen, discovered Kygo through his remix of Marvin Gaye's Sexual Healing, and sent him the demo; he finished his production within a few days and called it one of the fastest songs he ever completed. The feature brought together a Norwegian producer and an American singer who had just sung with The Weeknd.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Jason Beattie and released on 18 February 2016, follows the up-and-down relationship of a lesbian couple, giving the plea of the lyric a specific story. For the record that helped start the tropical house wave two years earlier, listen to {{ <track_link source="faul-wad-pnau-changes.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Kygo, the Norwegian producer Kyrre Gørvell-Dahll, became the face of tropical house in the mid-2010s with his mellow, piano-led, melodic approach to dance music. Stay, written by Kygo, Maty Noyes and William Wiik Larsen and produced by Kygo and Larsen, was released by Sony on 4 December 2015 and became the fourth single from his debut album, Cloud Nine, in 2016. A week after release, on 11 December 2015, Kygo and Noyes gave its first live performance at the Nobel Peace Prize Concert in Oslo, a sign of how quickly his sound had moved into the mainstream. The album version runs 3:59, the radio edit 3:10.

## Impact and Reception

Stay reached number 2 in Norway and number 3 in Sweden, number 11 in Switzerland, number 14 in Portugal and number 20 in the UK, where it reached number 7 on the dance chart. It showed that dance music could be soft, emotional and still deeply catchy. It is here for the sense of calm it creates, a few minutes of warmth that feel like holding on to a perfect moment. Put it on as the evening sets in and let it stay with you.
