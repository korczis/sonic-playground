## Genre and Mood

**KlangKuenstler** put **Bummelbahn** ninth on That's Me, his 2014 album for the London label Sunday Best. The label describes the album's sound as warm and hypnotic and says he stays true to his tech house roots while drawing on classical music, jazz and 1960s film scores. Those are the label's words for the whole record; no published review describes Bummelbahn itself, so this page takes its genres and feelings from that copy and claims nothing more about how it sounds.

## Song Summary

The title is a colloquial German word for a slow train that stops at every station, built from bummeln, to dawdle, and Bahn, railway. The track runs five minutes and twenty-one seconds and carries no featured guest. MusicBrainz credits the composition to KlangKuenstler alone. There are no published lyrics and no official video or audio upload I could find, so this review cannot tell you whether a voice appears or how the track unfolds.

## Artistic Context

KlangKuenstler is the alias of Michael Korb, born in 1990 in Füssen in the Allgäu. He moved to Berlin in the summer of 2012, released Das bin ich on Kallias in 2013 and That's Me on Sunday Best, the independent label founded by Sarah Bolshi and Rob da Bank, in 2014. He played mainly house, including deep and tech house, until 2018, and has since turned increasingly to hard techno.
