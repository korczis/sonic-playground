## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** called their music handmade hip-hop blues soul, and **Natural Self**, a producer from the same label, gave their song **13.6.35** a new body in this remix. The page files it under soul, electronic music and remix, with soulful and groovy as its feelings. Those are a reading of the band's tags and the remixer's background, not a quotation: no review describes this version.

## Song Summary

The song first appeared as 13:6:35, the seventh track of Belleruche's debut Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts in July 2007 and running 4:08. Four years later, on 4 April 2011, Tru Thoughts issued 3 Amp Fuse, a five-track EP built around a song from the band's third album, 270 Stories. Next to a radio edit and an instrumental of the title track, it carried three remixes, of Scratch My Soul by Loki, of Fuzz Face by Pollyn and this one, 13.6.35 (Natural Self Remix), which runs 4:28. The label posted it to its YouTube jukebox channel in July 2012.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version made from the parts of an existing recording, usually by another producer. Here it also does a label's work: putting a remix of a 2007 song on a 2011 EP gives an older track a second release and a new audience. ## Artistic Context

Belleruche were Kathrin deBoer on vocals, Ricky Fabulous on guitar and DJ Modest on decks, a London trio who released limited 7-inch singles on their own Hippoflex label before signing to Tru Thoughts in 2007. Natural Self is the project of producer, vocalist and DJ Nathaniel Pearn, a Tru Thoughts artist since his 2001 debut single, Shakedown/The Soul Step. The label's biography traces his sound from The Bomb Squad's productions for Public Enemy through crate-digging funk, soul, jazz and Latin music to tropical and bass culture, and notes that he has remixed many artists.
