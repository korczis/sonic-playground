## Genre and Mood

**Rachel K Collier** built **Don't Know What** out of somebody else's record. It is electronic pop with a sample at its core: what she called funky vocal chops, synths and programmed drums, groovy and playful rather than heavy, the sound of a producer enjoying a puzzle.

## Song Summary

The track began as Collier's entry in an episode of Andrew Huang's YouTube series 4 Producers Flip the Same Sample, made with the sample platform Tracklib and published on 22 August 2022 alongside versions by Ocean, Tommy Trash and Huang himself. Every producer received the same record: You Don't Know What You're Doing by Philly Groove's Sound Experience. Collier released her flip as a single three days before the episode, on 19 August 2022, and her own breakdown video walks through how she made it.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes the finished track passage by passage; what follows is the method Collier described, which tells you what to listen for rather than when.

### Main Section

The song is built on the chopped vocal. Collier separated the voice from the band, cut out the phrases she liked best and retimed them; she told Tracklib she spent absolutely ages on that. A bassline, drums and synths carry the chops.

### Breakdown

She experimented with structures, breakdowns and B-sections in Ableton Live's session view, so the track moves between sections rather than looping one idea. Where each section falls, no source says; listen for the changes.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} usually means borrowing a loop. Here it means rebuilding: Collier isolated the vocal of You Don't Know What You're Doing with an acapella extractor, chopped its best phrases in Ableton Live and ran them through Max for Live's Buffer Shuffler. A flip travels far from its source while keeping what made it worth taking.

Then came {{ <concept_link name="sound design" /> }}. She reharmonised the material with the glitch section of the Microcosm effect and with OVox, which she used to turn the vocal into a pad, so the sampled voice ends up playing chords it never sang. Her breakdown video gives separate chapters to sound design, vocal textures and synth work.

The decisive {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} choice came last: the slow bassline she started with felt too plain for funky vocal chops, so she added drums and tried out different structures. Compare {{ <track_link source="rachel-k-collier-earthquake.md" /> }}, made for the same series three months later, whose breakdown is organised around its drops.

## Artistic Context

Collier is a producer, singer and songwriter from Swansea who studied creative music technology at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama. She first became known as a featured singer, on Ray Foxx's Boom Boom (Heartbeat), a UK number 12 in 2013, on Mat Zo's Only for You and on tracks such as {{ <track_link source="ryan-blyth_once-twice-feat-rachel-k-collier.md" /> }}. She is now known for multi-instrument live performances of her own songs, teaches as an Ableton Certified Trainer and shares her process on YouTube. Don't Know What later became track seven of her album Songs in the Key of Internet, released on 29 September 2023.
