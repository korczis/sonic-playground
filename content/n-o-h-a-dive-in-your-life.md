## Genre and Mood

**N.O.H.A.**'s **Dive In Your Life** comes from a band that describes its own music as Ethno Bass: drum and bass crossed with world music, Balkan and Latin influences. It is electronic music played by a live band, and the feelings the band's own descriptions promise are energetic and groovy, energy-loaded grooves in their words, a living, breathing tornado on stage. This is the song's radio edit, the short form made for airplay.

This version, the Radio Edit, Album Cut, came out on 27 June 2008, also on Unique Records, and runs 3:40. ## Artistic Context

N.O.H.A., Noise of Human Art, was founded in 1995 by Philip Noha, born in Prague, and Chevalier Hadley, MC Chevy, from New York. Noha is the band's composer, producer and saxophonist. Their debut album Noise of Human Art came out in 1997, followed by No Slack, Next Plateau, Dive In Your Life, Respect the Menu and Circus Underground, and after a seven-year pause Process of Living in 2020. The Czech Wikipedia records a legal dispute with Minerva over the band's name after she left.

## Impact and Reception

The band names Dive In Your Life among its biggest hits, with Di Nero, Tu Café and Balkan Hot Step, the songs that, in its own words, made N.O.H.A. resonate across major European festivals.
