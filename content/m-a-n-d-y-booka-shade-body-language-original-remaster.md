## Genre and Mood

**M.A.N.D.Y.** and **Booka Shade** made **Body Language** in 2005, and **Body Language - Original Remaster** is the version their label issued in 2023. It is house music that Discogs also files under electro and tech house: euphoric when the hook takes over, playful in its melody and hypnotic over more than seven minutes.

## Song Summary

There is no story and no singer credited. Body Language is built on chunky drums, a warm bass and, as Get Physical puts it, a gorgeous melodic hook that rides up and down the scale. Those soft-focus notes carry what the label calls subtle but playful soul, and they are why the record has never stopped bringing crowds together. It became one of the defining club records of the mid-2000s, a track that worked in small clubs and on festival main stages alike.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The foundation is the drums, fleshy and full, with the warm bass beneath them, a sound the label still hears as coming from the future.

### Main Section

The hook does the work: it rides up and down the scale in the soft-focus notes the label describes. Listen for how often it returns.

### Outro

At more than seven minutes, the record leaves DJs room to mix out of it, the length of a track made for clubs first.

## Concepts in Focus

This version is a {{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }}. For Get Physical's 21st year the label prepared the 2005 recording again at the mastering stage and released it on vinyl and, as the label put it, digitally and on streaming platforms for the first time ever, with new remixes by HOSH and Patrice Bäumel. A remaster changes the overall sound, loudness and balance, but not the arrangement: what you hear is the original track.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} here is instrumental. With no singer credited, the melody does what a chorus does in a song, and it is simple enough to remember after one hearing.

Body Language has also lived on through {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. will.i.am used it in Get Your Money on his album Songs About Girls, and in 2016 Jax Jones sampled its bassline for You Don't Know Me with Raye, which reached number three in the UK.

## Artistic Context

M.A.N.D.Y. are Patrick Bodmer and Philipp Jung; Booka Shade are Walter Merziger and Arno Kammermeier, veterans of the Frankfurt scene. Together with DJ T. they founded the Berlin label Get Physical Music in 2002. The single came out on 25 April 2005 as GPM 027, taken from M.A.N.D.Y.'s mix compilation Body Language Vol. 1, and it was the collaboration that broke both acts to a global audience. Compare how a reviewer heard the Get Physical sound in {{ <track_link source="lulu-rouge-lulu-theme.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Body Language was named Ibiza Track of the Season at the 2005 DJ Awards, Resident Advisor called it one of the biggest releases of the year, and DJ Magazine made Get Physical its label of the year. It is here as a house classic. Turn it up and wait for the hook.
