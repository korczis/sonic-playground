## Genre and Mood

**Funky Fat & Digitaria** bring a slower, funk-flavoured energy to **You Bring Me Down**, combining house rhythms with electro and funk elements. Resident Advisor heard the pair's collaborations as having "a slower tempo and an electro slant", and the mood follows: groovy and energetic, with an emotional, slightly downcast edge that the title spells out.

## Song Summary

"You Bring Me Down" is the second collaboration between the two Brazilian acts on their 2012 Masochist EP. Its distributor described it this way: "Soaring synths swarm the mind with grumbling electronic stabs and a true injection of funk leaving the blissful vocals to tell the story." The tension is between the light, breathy voice and the heavier synth work underneath, a song about being let down set to a groove that refuses to stop.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro: Captivating Groove

The song opens with the groove: bass and rhythmic percussion establish a funk-inflected house pulse before anything else arrives.

### Verse 1: Emotional Lyrics

The vocals enter, breathy and light, carrying the story of being brought down by someone, set against upbeat instrumentation that contrasts with the words.

### Chorus: Catchy Hook

The title line returns as the hook, the driving beat and synths underlining it.

### Bridge: Instrumental Exploration

Midway through, the instruments take the lead, with soaring synth lines and grumbling stabs trading space over the beat.

### Climax: Dancefloor Energy

The layers stack up and the groove is at its fullest, the funk elements and electronics pushing together.

### Outro: Reflective Conclusion

The track eases back into the groove it opened with, a clean ending for a DJ.

## Concepts in Focus

The track is a small study in what a {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} can do in one arrangement. The "soaring synths" are sustained, melodic sounds that fill the space above the beat; the "grumbling electronic stabs" are short, punchy chords placed on the rhythm like a horn section. Both may come from the same kind of instrument, but one works as atmosphere and the other as percussion. Listen to the main section and try to follow each separately.

Underneath sits a funk-inflected {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}. Funky Fat's name is a statement of intent, and the "true injection of funk" the distributor heard comes from how bass and percussion lock together at a relaxed tempo. A slower groove gives the vocal room and makes the track feel heavier, which is the electro slant Resident Advisor noticed. ## Artistic Context

Funky Fat are a Brazilian trio based in Belo Horizonte, Guilherme Santoro, Rafael Cury and Thiago Guimaraes, credited as writers and producers of this track. Digitaria are the Brazilian duo of Daniel Albinati and Daniela Caldellas. Their joint Masochist EP came out in May 2012 on Hot Creations, the label of Jamie Jones and Lee Foss, catalogue HOTC020, with You Bring Me Down on the B-side next to Digitaria's Crazy Life; the A-side carried the title track and Digitaria's Paradise. Above Board described the whole EP as "a funky fusion of all things electronic, from deep house and disco to indie-pop and electro".

## Cultural Impact

Hot Creations was one of the defining labels of the early-2010s house revival, and a release there put both Brazilian acts in front of an international club audience. Resident Advisor's review opened by praising "some astute A&R" in the Hot Creations camp. The track later appeared on compilations such as the Austrian series Fruit 8: Grape, a sign of how far a B-side can travel.
