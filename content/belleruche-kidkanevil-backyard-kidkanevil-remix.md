## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** and **Kidkanevil**'s **Backyard - Kidkanevil Remix** is the London producer's take on a song from Belleruche's second album, which the one blog that reviewed it called a nice little downtempo remix. This page files it under downtempo, trip-hop and soul, where the trio's turntable soul has always lived. Relaxed comes from that same review, which recommended it for a Saturday morning; warm is this page's reading of a slow remix of a smoky soul song.

## Song Summary

The original Backyard is the second track of The Express, the album Tru Thoughts released in October 2008, written, produced and engineered by Kathrin deBoer, Ricky Fabulous and DJ Modest. Two years later the label put this remix on the Fuzz Face single, the second single from the band's third album, 270 Stories. Discogs lists the promo CD with two tracks, Fuzz Face and this remix at 3:40. No source describes the lyric or the arrangement, so this page keeps to what can be shown.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version built from a recording's parts, usually by another producer. Here a beatmaker slows a song down rather than stretching it for the dance floor; compare it with {{ <track_link source="belleruche-backyard.md" /> }} to hear what an outside ear changes.

The remix sits where the {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} used to: the second track of a single whose lead song is new. Pairing a new song with a reworked older one can send listeners of the new album back to the previous one.

From there it travelled by {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}. MusicBrainz lists it on the label sampler Shapes 11:01 in October 2011 and on a Tru Thoughts R&B and soul collection, and the band put it on their Best Of in October 2013.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche were a trio of voice, guitar and turntables on Brighton's Tru Thoughts; 270 Stories, released in October 2010, was their third album, and Hypebeast reported that Fuzz Face, its second single, came out worldwide on 6 December. Kidkanevil is Gerard Roberts, a London producer whose label Project Mooncircle traces his music to hip-hop, anime soundtracks and computer game music; he is a Red Bull Music Academy alumnus and works with the Japanese producer Daisuke Tanabe as Kidsuke. Hear the band's own sound of that year in {{ <track_link source="belleruche-clockwatching.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Tru Thoughts uploaded the remix to its TruThoughtsJukeBox channel, and Music Is My Sanctuary, writing in March 2011, admitted it had overlooked it in the label's steady flow of releases and filed it as a future classic. Put it on slowly, on a morning with nothing planned.
