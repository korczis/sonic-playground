## Genre and Mood

**Black Rose** is two producers, the German Henrik Schwarz and the house DJ Jesse Rose, and **Sky** is the track they made with the singer **Luisa Gerstein**. Stamp The Wax called it a soulful, joyous, Latin-flavoured track; it is house, deep house to the listeners the magazine pointed it to, and the feelings are its words: soulful, and openly happy.

## Song Summary

Sky was known before it was released. Fans first heard it in Henrik Schwarz's Boiler Room set in November 2012, where Stamp The Wax found it one of the highlights of a highly accomplished mix. The official release came in summer 2013 on Sunday-Music, the label Schwarz runs for his own productions and those of musician friends, with a Dub on the same release. The memorable detail is in the description itself: a track that, the magazine said, sounds even better in summer.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} can launch a track. Schwarz played Sky in his Boiler Room set months before release, so by the time it came out, many listeners already knew it from the mix.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit marks Gerstein as a guest on a producers' record. Black Rose's earlier Anthem EP of 2011 had sampled the reggae singer Barrington Spence, and Resident Advisor found it hokey; Sky gave the duo a singer of their own.

The Dub is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} by the same producers, without the guest credit: the same idea, built for the moment in a DJ set when a voice would be too much.

## Artistic Context

Henrik Schwarz, born in 1972 in Bergatreute in southern Germany, has released music since 2002 and is known for joining electronic music with jazz and classical work, later with the Metropole Orkest. Gerstein sang in Lulu and the Lampshades, the Bristol band that formed at the university there in 2009 and revived a Carter Family song as Cups; they later became Landshapes. Compare {{ <track_link source="marek-hemmann-gemini.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Sky kept travelling after its release: Defected put it on its 2014 House Masters collection of Schwarz's work, and it appeared on the Bermuda 2013 compilation from Berlin. Stamp The Wax said it sounds better in summer; play it on a warm evening and test the claim.
