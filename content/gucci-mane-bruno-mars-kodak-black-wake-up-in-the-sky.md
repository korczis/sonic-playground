## Genre and Mood

**Gucci Mane**, **Bruno Mars** and **Kodak Black** made **Wake Up in the Sky** a meeting of hip-hop, pop and soul with trap underneath. It is a celebratory song about feeling good and enjoying life, and NME's Jordan Bassett heard euphoria in it: the most commercial Gucci Mane had ever sounded, he wrote, but who could deny him that?

## Song Summary

Wake Up in the Sky came out on 14 September 2018 on Guwop and Atlantic as the third single from Gucci Mane's album Evil Genius, released that December. It was written by the three artists, Radric Davis, Peter Hernandez and Bill Kapri, with Tarentino, DY and Tre Pounds of the production team 808 Mafia, and produced by 808 Mafia and Mars. Serban Ghenea mixed it.

### Intro

The song starts with Mars singing the word fly high in his register, before the hook begins.

### Chorus

Mars carries the R&B hook. Pitchfork's Alphonse Pierre heard him steal the spotlight with a silky hook, and Bassett heard it shimmer.

### Verses

A trap beat comes in for the verses of Gucci Mane and Kodak Black, described on Wikipedia as more sensual and romantic. Pierre heard Gucci Mane sound rejuvenated as he ad-libs over the bright instrumental.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part of a song built to stick, and here it belongs to the guest: the verses ride trap drums, the hook floats above them in Mars's R&B voice.

That makes it a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}. The single comes from Gucci Mane's album, but its best-known part is sung by a guest. Compare {{ <track_link source="eve-gwen-stefani-let-me-blow-ya-mind.md" /> }}, where another pop singer carries a rapper's hook.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Florent Dechard and Mars and released on 31 October 2018, stages the three as a vintage soul trio in glittering suits, singing into old microphones in front of a theatre curtain. Gucci Mane hands a rose to a woman in the audience and Kodak Black does his Zeze dance move.

## Artistic Context

The collaborators had history: Gucci Mane joined Mars on the 2017 remix of That's What I Like, and first worked with Kodak Black on Vibin in This Bih in 2016. In 2026 the song also appeared on Mars's compilation Collaborations. Exclaim!'s Clayton Tomlinson named it one of the best tracks on Evil Genius and said it felt like a Rick Ross song that had been hitting the gym.

## Impact and Reception

The song peaked at number 11 on the Billboard Hot 100 after the video came out, tying Gucci Mane's highest-charting lead single in the US, reached number five on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and topped the Rhythmic airplay chart. The RIAA certified it six times platinum, and it won Most Performed Song at the 2020 ASCAP Pop Music Awards. Pitchfork's Evan Rytlewski, though, found it prim.
