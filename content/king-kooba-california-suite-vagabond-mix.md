## Genre and Mood

**King Kooba** released **California Suite** as a downtempo song with trip-hop programming, and the Vagabond Mix is the version other DJs carried into their mixes. Exclaim! called the original smooth, lush and undeniably seductive; the feelings here, sultry and relaxed, come from that review and from the small-hours setting the mix ended up in.

## Song Summary

California Suite first appeared on Enter the Throne Room, the duo's second album, in 1999. Denise Benson of Exclaim! heard programming reminiscent of early Massive Attack, rich, chunky bass, and a track that builds gorgeously around the stunning vocals of Esther Phillips. The Vagabond Mix is a different recording: MusicBrainz lists it at six minutes and two seconds against a little under six for the album version. No source we found names who made the mix or describes what it changes, so this page does not guess.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes the Vagabond Mix part by part. What can be said comes from the original's review and from where the mix was placed.

### Main Section

If the mix keeps what Exclaim! heard, the bass and the voice carry it; listen with that review in mind and decide where the two versions part.

### In the Late Night Tales mix

Nightmares on Wax placed it fifth in his selection, after The Rootsman's Show Me Some Love and before Quincy Jones's Listen (What It Is), so in the continuous mix it is heard shorter and blended into its neighbours.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} keeps a song's identity under a new treatment. The suffix in the title is the only sign here, and the lengths in MusicBrainz confirm that the Vagabond Mix and the album track are separate recordings.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} turns a track into a moment in someone else's set. Mark Farina used the Vagabond Mix in Mushroom Jazz 3 in 2001; two years later it appeared in Late Night Tales, a series whose guests, in Wikipedia's words, are asked to make the ultimate late-night mix.

Those releases are also each a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}, which is why the 2024 reissue lists the track again at full length beside the mix. Compare {{ <track_link source="focus-having-your-fun.md" /> }}, picked for the same set.

## Artistic Context

King Kooba are the London duo of the producer Matt Harris, also known as DJ Shuff, and Charlie Tate, who has played bass for Neneh Cherry. Exclaim! described them working with a live band and guest singers on a blend of cinematic future funk. California Suite returned on their third album, nufoundfunk, released by Om Records in 2000.

## Impact and Reception

The song travelled through other people's selections. Late Night Tales: Nightmares on Wax came out on Azuli on 12 May 2003; Cherwell heard funky, tasteful selections with hip-hop leanings, and the label reissued it on vinyl in June 2024. Put on the album version, then this one, and hear what changed.
