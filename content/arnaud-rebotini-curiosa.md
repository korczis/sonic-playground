## Genre and Mood

**Arnaud Rebotini** wrote **Curiosa** as the title piece of his score for Lou Jeunet's film of the same name, so it belongs first to the soundtrack and then to the electronic music Rebotini is known for. The feelings named here, romantic and sultry, come from the film's subject, a love affair played out through erotic photography, not from any description of this cue; no source describes how it sounds.

## Song Summary

The cue runs 4:11. Rebotini posted it on his own channel on 5 April 2019, two days after the film opened in France, with a note that the music was composed by him and released by Blackstrobe Records as BSR030. The film follows Marie de Heredia, played by Noémie Merlant, who loves the writer Pierre Louÿs but marries the wealthy Henri de Régnier to ease her father's debts, and who finds her own literary voice through the games the three of them play.

## Artistic Context

Rebotini, born in Nancy in 1970, founded Black Strobe with Ivan Smagghe in 1997 and has released his own records on Blackstrobe Records since his 2011 album Someone Gave Me Religion. His film work grew out of his scores for Robin Campillo, Eastern Boys and 120 Battements Par Minute, which won him the César for best original music; you can hear that period in {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat-arnaud-rebotini-smalltown-boy-arnaud-rebotini-remix.md" /> }}.
