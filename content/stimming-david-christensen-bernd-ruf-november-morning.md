## Genre and Mood

**Stimming** made **November Morning** as deep house with a classical heart: eight and a half minutes of blue chords and a quiet climax. The mood is melancholic and emotional, hypnotic in the way a grey morning can be, still and full at once. He has called it one of his most emotional tracks.

## Song Summary

The release notes describe the original in one line: blue and melancholic house chords with a typical Stimming climax, not obvious but effective. Around them he places a pseudo violin, field recordings and his own whistle. Those details are the point. Stimming is known for hand-made sounds and for recording the world around him, and here they turn a club track into something closer to a diary entry. On streaming services the track is credited to Stimming, David Christensen and Bernd Ruf, names that on the EP belong to its orchestral twin.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }} can carry a whole track when the chords themselves are the mood. The release notes put the chords first and say the other sounds round things up: the harmony sets the feeling, and the rest colours it.

The {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} here leads to what the release notes call a climax that is not obvious but effective: a peak that does not announce itself the way a club drop does. Compare the same patience in {{ <track_link source="yoko-duo-stimming-close-these-curtains-stimming-remix.md" /> }}.

Then there is the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. The November Morning EP pairs this original with a version for more than 60 players of the Brandenburgisches Staatsorchester Frankfurt, conducted by Bernd Ruf and orchestrated by David Christiansen, with no artificial sounds added afterwards. In September 2024 Stimming performed it again at the Beethovenfest in Bonn with the NDR Vokalensemble.

## Artistic Context

Martin Stimming, born in 1983 in Giessen and based in Hamburg, played violin, piano and drums from the age of ten before studying electronic music production. He has released on Diynamic since meeting its founders Solomun and Adriano in 2006. November Morning first appeared on his Window Shopping EP for Diynamic in March 2012; the November Morning EP followed on 26 November 2012 on 2DIY4, the sublabel that also issued {{ <track_link source="solomun-friends.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

An orchestra, a choir and a festival stage have all returned to this track, and it remains one of Stimming's defining pieces. It is here because it shows how much feeling house music can hold in a few chords and a whistle. Play it on a grey November morning.
