## Genre and Mood

**Stavroz** are a band, not a laptop act, and **Silent Spring** sounds like it in the way its label describes it: deep house played with bass guitars and a piano. It belongs to the electronic house world, but its feeling is uplifting and playful rather than dark, a seven-minute track that Moodfamily, the band's own label in Ghent, presented as ready for sunscreen.

## Song Summary

Silent Spring is the title track of the Silent Spring EP, released on 30 April 2015, just in time for summer, as the band's teaser put it. The label's notes describe how it is built: frivolous bass guitars accompany a broad-shouldered melody and gently carry the track to glory, while a snug piano hides in the breaks and builds a contrast. The memorable thing is that balance between a playful low end and a melody with weight.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is where Silent Spring gets its character. In much house the bass is a synthesizer; in Stavroz two members play bass, and the label's word frivolous describes a part with a personality of its own rather than a mere foundation. Listen for how much of the track's good mood comes from it. Compare the carefully shaped bassline of {{ <track_link source="nils-penner-the-thing.md" /> }}, made for the same warm season.

A {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} is the passage where a dance track strips back before the groove returns. Moodfamily's notes put the piano there, hidden in the breaks, so the quietest moments are also the ones with a new colour, and a contrast like that is what makes a groove's return feel bigger.

## Artistic Context

Stavroz formed in Ghent in 2011, when IJsbrand De Wilde and Gert Beazar, both DJs and sound engineers, started the project; Maxim Helincks and Pieter De Meester soon joined them live and in the studio. Their Discogs profile credits them with an ethnic, acoustic and organic vibe in electronic music, and lists releases on Délicieuse, Kindisch, Bedrock and Moodfamily. Wikipedia describes their style as organic house, electronica and downtempo, and says they founded Moodfamily because they felt there was too little emotion in the music. Silent Spring was their first EP on it, catalogue number MF005, with two more tracks, Great Day to Fly a Kite and Should've Brought an Umbrella.

## Impact and Reception

Stavroz went on to the debut album Mindibu in 2022. Silent Spring is here as an early sign of what they do best: dance music that sounds played by hands. Put it on when the first warm days arrive.
