## Genre and Mood

**AYAWAKE & Be Svendsen**'s **Scarecrow** is electronic music dressed for a western. Svendsen's label filed it as the second original on his 2017 release and called it equally alluring; a later write-up of the track heard it as cavernous and atmospheric, yet immediate and intimate. Svendsen himself calls his music Tarantino techno, and here the reference is older: the westerns scored by Ennio Morricone.

## Song Summary

The song began with AYAWAKE, who wrote and recorded the vocals and passed them on to Be Svendsen. Svendsen, in the label's words, honoured it as the crown work of his cowboy-tech tribute to the Ennio Morricone soundtracks of the late 1960s. The full track runs just over nine minutes on Svendsen's Twilight in Tankwa EP, released on Rebellion, the sister label of Crosstown Rebels; the single this page is about is a version of about three and a half minutes, dated April 2017.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

No timed analysis of Scarecrow exists that I could find, so this page does not map its minutes. The sources describe what it is made of: AYAWAKE's voice at the centre and, around it, a mass of parts. When Svendsen chose it for a binaural remix with the audio company Polygon in 2020, he explained that there were so many different elements that would be interesting to place, and the mix counted more than 70 sounds ranging from the orchestral to the electronic and the abstract.

## Concepts in Focus

The track lives twice inside the idea of the {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }}. It was made as a tribute to film music, Morricone's scores for westerns, and then became film music itself: Milan Records put it on The New Pope (Original Soundtrack from the HBO Series) in February 2020 as track 12, in a version of 3:27; the series is Paolo Sorrentino's. No source I found says which scene uses it.

The short single works like a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}. No release calls it that, but the listings show the same song at about three and a half minutes on the single and the soundtrack and at just over nine on the EP; the video embedded here is the full version.

## Artistic Context

Be Svendsen is the Copenhagen producer Lasse Bruhn Svendsen, who broke through in 2014 with productions and live sets at festivals such as BOOM, Fusion, Burning Man, Symbiosis, Rainbow Serpent and AfrikaBurn. Europavox describes a sound built from samples, live instruments, field recordings and vocal snippets. Hear him reshape another Danish voice in {{ <track_link source="the-crooked-spoke-be-svendsen-black-tower-hotel-be-svendsen-mix.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The New Pope carried Scarecrow from the festival fields where Svendsen made his name into a television drama, and three years after release he went back to it for the binaural remix. Put on headphones and listen for the cowboy in the machine.
