## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **Beautiful Tango (Remastered Version)** is a song where world music, folk and the intimacy of a singer-songwriter meet in a slow, swaying dance. It is romantic and soulful, sung so close and so softly that it feels like a private confession shared across a small, candlelit room.

## Song Summary

Despite its title, this is not a tango in the ballroom sense but a love song that moves like one, two people circling each other, drawn together and pulled apart. A lilting acoustic guitar with a hint of desert blues, soft hand percussion and gentle bass leave plenty of air around Zahra's husky, jazz-tinged voice. The memorable detail is her phrasing, unhurried and sensual, bending each line as if she were improvising it on the spot.

## Musical Breakdown

The arrangement is spare and organic, letting the voice lead every step.

### Intro

A gently plucked guitar sets a lilting, slightly melancholy rhythm, warm and inviting.

### Verse 1

Zahra's voice enters low and smoky, almost conversational, with light percussion keeping time underneath.

### Chorus

The melody opens up, her voice rising with a soft ache as the guitar strums more fully, the dance at its most intimate.

### Bridge

A short instrumental passage lets the guitar and percussion take over, the groove swaying like a slow walk at dusk.

### Climax

The final refrains bring a little more intensity, layered voices and fuller rhythm without ever losing the song's tenderness.

### Outro

The instruments fall back and the song closes on a gentle, lingering phrase, as if the dancers are reluctant to let go.

## Artistic Context

Hindi Zahra is a Moroccan-born, Paris-based singer and songwriter whose music draws on Amazigh roots, jazz, soul and folk. Beautiful Tango was the lead single and opening track of her debut album Handmade, released in January 2010 on Blue Note, and its video was directed by filmmaker Tony Gatlif. The remastered version presented here gives the original recording a fresh polish without changing its character.

## Impact and Reception

Handmade made Zahra one of the breakthrough voices of French world music: it won the Prix Constantin in November 2010 and the Victoires de la Musique award for best world music album in February 2011, with Beautiful Tango as its calling card. It sits on this playground as one of the most seductive songs in the collection. Dim the lights and let it sway you.
