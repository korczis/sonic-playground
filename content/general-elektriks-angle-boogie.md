## Genre and Mood

**General Elektriks** is the project of the French keyboard player, singer and producer Hervé Salters, and **Angle Boogie** is funk with electro and pop folded in: groovy, playful and catchy. It is a short, bright track of just over three minutes, built to move bodies rather than to tell a long story.

## Song Summary

Angle Boogie is the second track on To Be a Stranger, the album Salters wrote in Berlin and released on 29 January 2016. Reviewing the album, Les Oreilles Curieuses wrote that he treats listeners to funky little bombs, as he knows so well how to do, and named Angle Boogie among the hit-like ones. The same review heard its title as a playful wink at Kool & the Gang's famous Jungle Boogie. The sources describe the album more than this track, so this review says less about its details than about its spirit.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track or walks through its sections, so the parts below follow what the reviews and the official video let a listener hear.

### Main Section

The groove carries the track. Les Oreilles Curieuses described the album as a blend of electronica, funk, jazz, pop and soul, made with vintage clavinets and organic arrangements; Hop Blog heard electro rhythms and sounds that are a little cheap but very warm, on a record made to spread good cheer and swing.

### Music Video

The official clip, published on the VEVO channel on 15 January 2016, two weeks before the album, was directed by Drew Tyndell, produced by Rhiannon Leifheit Tyndell and filmed in Portland, Oregon.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the repeating rhythmic feel that makes a body move, and Angle Boogie is built around one. Salters came up as a session keyboard player, and the reviews describe this album first by its pulse: funky and swinging. Listen for the parts that repeat and lock together rather than for the melody on top. The title, if the critic is right, points the same way, back to Kool & the Gang's funk.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} came first. Releasing an official clip before the album gave the track a face two weeks ahead of the record, the way the earlier single {{ <track_link source="general-elektriks-whisper-to-me.md" /> }} had announced it months before.

## Artistic Context

Salters played keys in Paris for Femi Kuti, -M- and DJ Mehdi before moving to San Francisco in 1999, where he joined the Quannum collective and worked with Blackalicious. As General Elektriks he released Cliquety Kliqk, Good City for Dreamers and Parker Street before To Be a Stranger, which came out on Wagram Music and 3ème Bureau; the live album Punk Funk City followed the same year. Les Oreilles Curieuses heard the Berlin album as a return to his musical roots.

## Impact and Reception

Les Oreilles Curieuses gave To Be a Stranger 8.5 out of 10 and singled out Angle Boogie among its most hit-like tracks; Hop Blog liked the album's good humour and swing. Put it on loud and listen for the boogie in the title.
