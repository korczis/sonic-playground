## Genre and Mood

**MC Lars** and **K.Flay** slow everything down for **The Game**, the fourth of seven tracks on their 2009 EP Single and Famous. It is hip-hop and rap at a stroll. The blog Hipster, please!, the only outlet found to review the EP, called it a street-level banger that doubles as a less-than-explicit sex jam, and a slow and steady head-bobber. Sultry and playful are the feelings named here, and both come from that description.

## Song Summary

The song sits right in the middle of the EP, and the critic heard it as the record's halftime show, a break that also stands on its own. Its risk, he wrote, was its pace: with a plodding vocal delivery and a slightly wavering beat, it could easily have slid into lackadaisical rap. Instead it comes off as relaxed and sure of itself. The beat is K.Flay's; the review calls it another K.Flay-produced track. At 3:54 it is one of the longer songs on a record whose shortest runs under two minutes.

### Sound Breakdown

The beat moves slowly and not quite evenly; the critic's word is wavering. That slight looseness is what keeps a slow tempo from feeling stiff.

### Main Section

Both artists rap, and neither hurries. The delivery the review calls plodding is the point: the voices sit back on the beat, suggest more than they say and let the groove carry them.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} is often judged by speed and density, but The Game shows the other side of the craft: holding a slow beat without losing the listener. The critic's worry that it might sound lazy, and his verdict that it does not, is a useful test for any relaxed verse.

The track is also a reminder of what a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} does in rap: builds the beat the rappers answer. The upload on MC Lars's channel credits the EP to both artists as producers, with help from the Rondo Brothers and the Dust Collector, and the review names K.Flay as producer of this one.

Finally, listen for the {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}, the steady pulse the head-bob follows. Compare the driving guitar of {{ <track_link source="mc-lars-k-flay-single-and-famous.md" /> }}, which closes the same EP.

## Artistic Context

MC Lars, born Andrew Nielsen in 1982, studied English at Stanford and calls his style post-punk laptop rap; he founded Horris Records in 2004. K.Flay, born Kristine Flaherty in 1985, enrolled at Stanford in 2003 and released her mixtape Suburban Rap Queen in 2005. Single and Famous, their first collaboration, came out on Horris on 8 September 2009. She signed to RCA in 2012 and in 2016 to Interscope, as the first artist on Night Street Records.

## Impact and Reception

The EP was a small independent release with one review found, and its critic heard The Game as a head-bobber sure to please. MC Lars posted it on his YouTube channel in 2015. Put it on and let your head find the beat.
