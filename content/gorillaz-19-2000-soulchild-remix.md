## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz** were always a genre-blending project, and **19-2000 (Soulchild Remix)** shows it at its most cheerful. This remix takes the alternative, dub-tinged original and turns it into electronic pop with a hip-hop bounce. The mood is playful and upbeat, utterly carefree, the soundtrack of a cartoon band speeding down a sunny road.

## Song Summary

Where the album version of 19-2000 is lazy and lopsided, the Soulchild Remix tightens everything up: a brighter, punchier beat, a bouncing bassline and sparkling synths. 2-D's laid-back vocal floats over the top with its odd, half-nonsensical lines, and the famous call to get the cool shoeshine, sung by Miho Hatori as the voice of Noodle, is impossible to forget. That chorus is the memorable detail, a sweet, childlike chant that sticks for days.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix keeps a tight pop structure, swapping between verses and that chant without wasting a second.

### Intro

A bright synth riff and a crisp beat kick in immediately, setting a sunny, energetic tone.

### Verse 1

2-D sings in his relaxed, slightly detached way, the beat bouncing beneath him with playful synth flourishes.

### Chorus

The shoeshine chant arrives in a high, sing-song voice, the hook of the whole track and its happiest moment.

### Bridge

A short breakdown strips the beat back to a few synth and vocal fragments before the groove snaps back in.

### Outro

The chant repeats over the full beat before the track closes quickly, leaving you wanting another play.

## Artistic Context

Gorillaz are the virtual band created by Damon Albarn and artist Jamie Hewlett, and 19-2000 comes from their 2001 debut album Gorillaz. The Soulchild Remix became the single version and the one most people know, and it appeared in the football video game FIFA Football 2002, which carried it to a huge new audience. It was later included on The Singles Collection 2001-2011, which gathers the band's first decade of hits. The animated video, with the band racing through a strange futuristic landscape, was as much part of the appeal as the song.

## Impact and Reception

The remix became one of the biggest early Gorillaz singles and a defining sound of the turn of the millennium, loved by fans of indie, hip-hop and pop alike. For many listeners it was the first time they heard the band, thanks to the video game. It is here because it is simply joyful. Put it on, turn it up and join the chant: get the cool shoeshine.
