## Genre and Mood

**Soulwax** released **E Talking** as a single from their 2004 album and then rebuilt it themselves. This is the Nite Version, six minutes and seven seconds long, from the 2005 remix album Nite Versions: electro and electronic dance music in a remix the band made of their own song. Drowned in Sound heard a cheeky electro banger driven by low-slung bass and hyperactive handclaps, which is a fair summary of how energetic and playful it is.

## Song Summary

The original E Talking was the second single from Any Minute Now, released on 17 January 2005. The Nite Version keeps the song and changes its job. Exclaim wrote that the band were subtly but effectively rewiring it with a perfectly monotone female vocal, and compilations of 2005 catalogued on MusicBrainz credit this version "feat. Nancy Whang", the LCD Soundsystem singer. The memorable thing is that deadpan voice against a restless rhythm section.

## Musical Breakdown

No source maps the track's sections or times, so this follows what reviewers describe.

### The Groove

The bass sits low and drives everything, and the handclaps above it never settle.

### The Voice

The female vocal is flat on purpose, a cool counterweight to the busy rhythm.

### On the Album

On Nite Versions the tracks run together as one seamless club set; on its own, as here, the version runs its full six minutes.

## Concepts in Focus

Nite Versions is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} album the band made of their own record. Drowned in Sound described the method as breaking Any Minute Now down into its elements and reassembling it into a pounding, seamless club set, the songs rearranged in Cubist ways and recognisable only up close.

The name is a nod to Duran Duran's Night Versions, extended dance remixes intended for nightclub play, and this is an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} in that sense: about a minute and a half longer than the album original.

Its engine is the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}. Reviewers name the bass before anything else, and Mixmag called the song a bangin' electro-rocker with a driving riff.

## Artistic Context

Soulwax are the Ghent brothers Stephen and David Dewaele, who formed the band in 1995 and also DJ as 2manydjs; the song is credited to both. Any Minute Now came out on PIAS in August 2004, and Nite Versions followed on 26 September 2005. A hidden 51-second track in its pregap is an alternate version of the original E Talking's guitar riff.

## Impact and Reception

The original reached number 27 in the UK, the band's highest placing, and the BBC put it on its list of songs that shaped dance music, crediting it with bringing EBM into the mainstream. Treble called E Talking the album's huge club hit, Nite Versions went double silver in Europe, and it was reissued for its fifteenth anniversary in 2020. Start with the bass and let the handclaps pull you in.
