## Genre and Mood

**Branko**, **SKIP&DIE** and **Fellow** forge **Made of Gold** from electronic music, world rhythms and dance-floor energy. It is vibrant, bold and celebratory, a global club track that feels like a street party spilling across borders.

## Song Summary

"Made of Gold" is a celebration of self-worth and shared energy, carried by voices trading lines over a gleaming, bass-heavy groove. Branko's production blends deep, rolling bass, chattering percussion and bright synth textures, drawing on the Afro-Portuguese, Latin and electronic sounds that define his work. SKIP&DIE bring their own mix of South African and Dutch influences, while Fellow adds another vocal colour, so the track feels like a conversation between scenes and cities. It is warm, confident and made to move bodies, the kind of track that turns strangers on a dance floor into a crowd.

## Artistic Context

Branko, the Lisbon producer João Barbosa, is a founding member of Buraka Som Sistema, the group that helped bring kuduro-inspired club music to international audiences. "Made of Gold" comes from ATLAS, his debut solo album, released on his own Enchufada label in September 2015. The album grew out of his journeys through cities including Amsterdam, New York, Cape Town and São Paulo, collaborating with local artists along the way, and the Amsterdam-based SKIP&DIE are part of that map.

## Impact and Reception

ATLAS was received as a vivid snapshot of global club music, a record that treated borders as an invitation rather than a limit. "Made of Gold" stands out as one of its most direct, feel-good moments, ideal for festival stages and summer playlists. It is here for its warmth and its sense of community through rhythm. Put it on, turn up the bass and let it shine.
