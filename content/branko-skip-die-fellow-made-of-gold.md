## Genre and Mood

**Branko**, **SKIP&DIE** and **Fellow** forge **Made of Gold** from electronic music, global rhythms and dance-floor energy. It is vibrant, bold and celebratory, a club track that feels like a street party spilling across borders.

## Song Summary

"Made of Gold" is a celebration of self-worth and shared energy, carried by guest voices over a gleaming, bass-heavy groove. Branko's debut album is often described as a course in global club music, moving through kuduro, baile funk and hip-hop with pop and dance sensibilities, and this track brings that approach to Amsterdam. SKIP&DIE, a Dutch and South African band, and Fellow add their own colours, so the track feels like a conversation between scenes and cities. It is warm, confident and made to move bodies, the kind of track that turns strangers on a dance floor into a crowd.

## Musical Breakdown

The track builds steadily, layering voices and rhythms into a full, glowing groove.

### Outro

The groove rides on before fading, leaving the bass ringing in your chest.

## Concepts in Focus

The credit line tells the story: Branko featuring SKIP&DIE and Fellow. A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is more than a guest; on ATLAS every track pairs Branko with artists he met in one city, so the features are the album's map. Here the producer provides the frame and the guests bring the voices, and the song only exists because they met.

Branko comes from {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}, not from a band tradition. With Buraka Som Sistema he helped carry Lisbon's kuduro-inspired club sound, rooted in Angolan dance music, to international stages, and Made of Gold is made the same way: for DJs, sound systems and crowds rather than for headphones alone.

What makes such a track work is its {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}, the way bass and percussion lock together until the body knows what to do. Listen to how the voices ride on top of that pulse instead of driving it; the groove is the ground everything else stands on.

## Artistic Context

Branko, the Lisbon producer João Barbosa, is a founding member of Buraka Som Sistema. "Made of Gold" comes from ATLAS, his debut solo album, released on his own Enchufada label on 4 September 2015. The album grew out of journeys to Amsterdam, São Paulo, Cape Town, New York and Lisbon, filmed by Red Bull Music Academy as the series Atlas Unfolded, in which Branko works with local artists in each city. SKIP&DIE and Fellow both appear in its Amsterdam chapter, and the album's other collaborators include Mr. Carmack, Princess Nokia and Jillionaire of Major Lazer.

## Impact and Reception

ATLAS was received as a lesson in global club music, a record that treated borders as an invitation rather than a limit. "Made of Gold" is one of its most direct, feel-good moments, ideal for festival stages and summer playlists. It is here for its warmth and its sense of community through rhythm. Put it on, turn up the bass and let it shine.
