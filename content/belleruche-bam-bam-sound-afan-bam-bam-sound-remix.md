## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** are a London trio who mix soul, electronics and turntables, and **Afan - Bam Bam Sound Remix** is one of their tracks reworked by others: electronic music built from a track by a trip-hop-leaning soul band, released on a single by their label. This is the Bam Bam Sound Remix, not the album version. Its feelings here, dark and intense, come from the original and the album around it, because no review describes the remix itself.

## Song Summary

The original Afan is the seventh track of Rollerchain, Belleruche's fourth album, released by Tru Thoughts on 7 May 2012, and it runs just 2:20. The French webzine La Toile de Pandore heard it as synth moans and drum rolls with the effect of an electric shock. The remix is more than twice as long, 4:57, and was the second track of the 16 Minutes single, behind the album's title song and before remixes of Stormbird by V.C. and of Wasted Time by Ross Wakefield.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts, usually by someone outside the band. Here the remix takes a short, sharp album track and extends it to more than double its length, a common direction for a remix aimed at DJs rather than at album listeners.

The {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }} tradition explains where it lives. The 16 Minutes single carries the title song first and then three remixes of other Rollerchain tracks, so a listener who bought it for one song met three more of the album in new versions. Hear the lead track, {{ <track_link source="belleruche-16-minutes.md" /> }}, first.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche are Kathrin deBoer on vocals, Ricky Fabulous on guitar and DJ Modest on turntables, formed in North London in 2005 and signed to the Brighton label Tru Thoughts from 2007 to 2013. They wrote and recorded Rollerchain in the last five months of 2011, and the album's notes call its sound darker and more bass-heavy than their earlier work. The Discogs profile of Bam Bam Sound describes two DJs, Duck and Johnny Clash, residents at London's Big Chill Bar, who had played alongside Belleruche among many others; this remix is the only release Discogs credits to them.
