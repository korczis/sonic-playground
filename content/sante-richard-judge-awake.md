## Genre and Mood

**Santé** teams up with vocalist **Richard Judge**, credited as J.U.D.G.E, on **Awake**, a track that sits between deep house and techno. It is hypnotic and introspective, carried by a soulful voice, and it feels like the first clear thought after a long night out.

## Song Summary

As the title hints, Awake is about coming to, about the moment the haze lifts and things look different. The original mix runs just over six minutes and is built on a deep, rolling bassline, tight, dry drums and dark chords that shift slowly underneath. J.U.D.G.E's vocal brings the human element, delivered with a grave, soulful calm that contrasts with the steady machine pulse. The memorable detail is that voice against the darkness of the groove.

## Musical Breakdown

The structure is club-functional, but the vocal gives it the arc of a song.

### Intro

Kick, hats and a low bass throb set a serious, dark-room tone from the first bar.

### Build-up

Percussion layers stack and a moody chord progression creeps in, raising the tension slowly.

### Verse 1

J.U.D.G.E enters with a measured, soulful delivery, his voice sitting deep in the mix like a thought spoken aloud.

### Main Section

The groove locks in at full strength, bass and chords cycling hypnotically while vocal phrases return like a mantra.

### Bridge

The drums drop out and the chords and voice hang alone, a brief, almost spiritual pause.

### Climax

The beat slams back with the full chord stack and vocal on top, the track's most emotional and powerful stretch.

### Outro

Elements are pulled back one by one until only the rhythm remains, ready for the next record.

## Artistic Context

Santé is a German DJ and producer who runs his own label, Avotre, where Awake came out as the 12-inch AVOTRE 013 in late 2014. The EP paired the original with a remix by French techno heavyweight Agoria on the B-side; that Agoria version also travelled widely with an official video. Reviewers noted how the release showed the label boss reaching for deeper shades of house and techno with a strong vocal at its centre.

## Impact and Reception

Awake became one of Santé's best-known productions, a track that DJs could play both in peak hours and in more reflective moments, and it introduced many listeners to J.U.D.G.E's distinctive voice. It earns its place here for that rare balance of club power and soul. Play it as the night turns into morning.
