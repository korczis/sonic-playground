## Genre and Mood

**One Direction**'s **Drag Me Down** is mid-tempo pop rock, uplifting in what it says and defiant in its title: a song about someone who keeps the singer standing, from a band that had just lost a member.

## Song Summary

Drag Me Down was the first single after Zayn Malik left the group in March 2015, and the lead single of the album Made in the A.M. The lyric thanks someone who stood by the singer when no one else was behind him: a heart of fire, a soul like a river, and without that person nothing left. The Daily Telegraph called it a love song to the band's fans. Jamie Scott, John Ryan and Julian Bunetta wrote it, and Bunetta produced it, in C minor at 138 beats per minute.

### Intro

The song is built on a guitar figure: Alfred Soto of The Singles Jukebox calls it a two-note riff, and the Los Angeles Times hears springy guitars like the Police's.

### Verse

### Chorus

Soto hears claps and massed vocals with the hooks up front. In the 18-second chorus clip the band released before the single, Niall Horan sings the second half and Harry Styles reaches for a high note.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} can be as small as two notes. Here it is what the critics remembered first, and the reason they reached for the Police, Maroon 5 and Magic!.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} lives in the chorus, where the voices are massed together; The Verge compared its melody to Taylor Swift's I Knew You Were Trouble.

The song sits in a {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }}, C minor, which keeps a grateful lyric from sounding simply sunny.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Ben and Gabe Turner, sends the band into astronaut training at NASA's Johnson Space Center; it won the fan-voted Best Video at the 2016 Brit Awards and passed a billion YouTube views.

## Artistic Context

Syco released the single worldwide on 31 July 2015. Liam Payne announced it on Twitter with no advance promotion, and the band played it live for the first time that night in Indianapolis. Billboard's Jason Lipshutz heard a more mature sound; Digital Spy wrote that One Direction were far from down and out.

## Impact and Reception

Drag Me Down set a Spotify record of 4.75 million streams in one day, debuted at number three on the Billboard Hot 100 and topped the charts in twelve countries, the UK among them. Not every critic was won over: the Los Angeles Times found it all too accurately titled. Listen for the two notes underneath everything.
