## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City**, **Katy Menditta** and **WEISS** come together on **Imagination (Weiss Remix)**, a house remix that pulls the original toward the deeper, groovier end of the club. It is deep house with a swinging, bass-heavy edge, and the mood is groovy and hypnotic, with an energetic pulse that keeps the floor moving from the first bar to the last.

## Song Summary

The original Imagination is a sleek vocal house song about the pull of attraction and the stories we tell ourselves about the people we want. WEISS reimagines it for the dance floor: he strips the song back, puts a rolling, swinging bassline and shuffling drums at its centre and lets Katy Menditta's voice flicker in and out of the mix. The memorable detail is how the vocal becomes a texture rather than a lead, appearing in teasing fragments that make each full return of the hook feel like a reward.

## Musical Breakdown

As a club remix, the track stretches out over six minutes, giving each element room to grow.

### Intro

Crisp, shuffling drums and a filtered bass set a tight groove, built for DJs to mix in.

### Build-up

The bassline grows fuller and more swinging, with small synth details and fragments of the vocal starting to appear.

### Main Section

Katy Menditta's voice arrives over the full groove, the bass and percussion locked into a hypnotic, rolling pattern.

### Bridge

A breakdown pulls the drums away, leaving the vocal and atmospheric textures suspended before the groove returns.

### Climax

The full beat crashes back in with the vocal hook on top, the remix at its most energetic.

### Outro

The arrangement strips back to drums and bass, rolling out cleanly for the next track.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City are the London production duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott, central figures in the UK's mid-2010s wave of house music that crossed over into the charts. Imagination, featuring vocalist Katy Menditta, comes from their debut album Sirens, released in 2014, and this remix by the UK producer WEISS appears on the companion Sirens (Remixes) release. WEISS is known for groove-led, bass-heavy house, and his take shifts the song from radio toward the underground club.

## Impact and Reception

The Weiss Remix became a favourite among DJs, valued for its rolling groove and its clever, sparing use of the vocal, and it sits comfortably alongside Gorgon City's own club records such as Hard On Me, also on this playground. It is here because it shows how a good remix can transform a song while keeping its soul. Play it loud, and let the bassline do the talking.
