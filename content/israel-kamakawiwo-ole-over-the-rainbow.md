## Genre and Mood

**Israel Kamakawiwo'ole** sings **Over The Rainbow** with a ukulele and nothing else. It is an acoustic cover, Hawaiian in its sound and its singer. The mood is serene and warm, with a bittersweet edge: the voice is gentle, and it is hard to hear now without knowing the singer died at 38.

## Song Summary

Harold Arlen and Yip Harburg wrote Over the Rainbow for The Wizard of Oz in 1939, where Judy Garland sang it as Dorothy; it won the Oscar for Best Original Song. In 1988 Kamakawiwo'ole called a Honolulu studio at three in the morning and was given fifteen minutes. The engineer, Milan Bertosa, set up microphones, did a quick sound check and rolled tape: he played and sang, one take, and it was over. This page plays the cut version titled Over the Rainbow, released on Alone in IZ World in 2001.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} can make a famous song new by subtraction. Garland's version had a film orchestra; this one has a ukulele, and according to the music publisher EMI it became the most requested version of the song.

It began as a {{ <concept_link name="demo" /> }}. Bertosa gave the singer a copy and filed the tape away; only in 1993 did he play it to the producer Jon de Mello, who put it on Facing Future as a medley with What a Wonderful World.

That single take exists in more than one {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. Facing Future has the medley; Alone in IZ World has the cut version, which Wikipedia names without saying what was cut. The label's chapters list only the parts of Over the Rainbow. Kamakawiwo'ole also recorded an upbeat Jawaiian take of the medley for his 1990 album Ka ʻAnoʻi. Compare {{ <track_link source="henry-mancini-moon-river-vocal-audrey-hepburn.md" /> }}, another Oscar-winning film song sung small.

## Artistic Context

Born in Honolulu in 1959, Kamakawiwo'ole was a founding member of the Makaha Sons of Niʻihau before going solo in 1990; Facing Future became Hawaiʻi's first platinum album. He promoted Hawaiian sovereignty. He died of respiratory failure in 1997, aged 38, and lay in state at the Hawaii State Capitol, the only person so honoured who was not a government official.

## Impact and Reception

The cut version became a sleeper hit: number one in Germany and France in 2010 and 2011, and Germany's best-selling single of 2010. Alone in IZ World topped Billboard's World Albums chart. The Library of Congress added the medley to its National Recording Registry, announced in March 2021. Listen to Garland first, then to this.
