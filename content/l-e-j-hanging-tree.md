## Genre and Mood

**L.E.J** recorded **Hanging Tree** as a cover, and their version sits between pop and the orchestral: three voices, a backing choir and a string section in place of a film score. No review describes this recording, so its feelings, haunting and melancholic, are the song's own, the eerie murder ballad Suzanne Collins wrote for her novel Mockingjay.

## Song Summary

The song asks someone to come to a hanging tree at midnight, words Collins wrote and the Lumineers' Jeremiah Fraites and Wesley Schultz set to music for the 2014 film. L.E.J open their first album, En attendant l'album, released on 4 December 2015, with it. The album's credits list an arrangement by the group, strings and voices arranged by Marie Jeanne Serrero, seven string players and seven backing singers: a small orchestra and a choir around three friends.

## Artistic Context

Lucie Lebrun, Elisa Paris and Juliette Saumagne were born in 1993 and grew up together in Saint-Denis; the group's name is their initials. Lucie sings and plays synthesizers and saxophone, Elisa sings and plays drums and percussion, Juliette sings and plays cello and bass. They broke through with Summer 2015, a mashup of summer hits posted on YouTube that August, which reached number one in France.

## Impact and Reception

As a single, Hanging Tree peaked at number 99 in France; the album, all covers except their first original song La Dalle, reached number 4 and went multi-platinum. Listen to how three voices carry a song written for a crowd.
