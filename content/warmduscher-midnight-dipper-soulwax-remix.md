## Genre and Mood

**Warmduscher**'s **Midnight Dipper (Soulwax Remix)** takes a punchy, funky London band song and pushes it into disco, six minutes of groove with a leer on its face. It is groovy, provocative and irreverent in equal measure: Fluxblog heard the remix make a song that was already strange and silly even stranger and sillier.

## Song Summary

The original Midnight Dipper, three minutes long, was the first single from Warmduscher's 2019 album Tainted Lunch. Joyzine's Ioan Humphreys heard a throbbing beat destined for indie discos and festival tents and a vocal that mixed a horizontal delivery with sleaze; the lyric he called tongue-in-cheek, sleazy and explicit. Soulwax keep the groove and the vocal much as they were, Fluxblog wrote, but stretch the song out and dial the disco way up.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} does not have to rebuild a song from scratch. Here Soulwax change surprisingly little of the material, by Fluxblog's account, and still change its meaning: the original presented its sleazy character as a farce, while the stretched, disco-heavy version made the same lewdness feel genuinely pervy, like wandering into a swingers' club and not knowing whether to leave or stay.

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is what makes that possible. A groove that Humphreys called the most funky, driving pulse he had heard for ages can carry six minutes as easily as three; extending it shifts the weight from the joke to the dancefloor. Compare how Soulwax treat {{ <track_link source="metronomy-love-letters-soulwax-remix.md" /> }}.

The No Intro cut is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same remix, framed differently for a different use.

## Artistic Context

Warmduscher formed in London in 2014, fronted by Craig Higgins as Clams Baker Jr.; Tainted Lunch, released on 1 November 2019 on The Leaf Label, was produced by Dan Carey. Soulwax, the Ghent group built around the brothers David and Stephen Dewaele, are as well known for remixes as for their own records. MusicBrainz dates the digital single to February 2020; the remix also opened European Cowboy, the Record Store Day 2020 12-inch of remixes by Soulwax, Decius and Savage Gary, limited to 1,000 copies.

## Impact and Reception

The Line of Best Fit called the original an instant 6 Music favourite that oozes funk and gave Tainted Lunch nine out of ten. Fluxblog's verdict on the remix was more ambivalent and more vivid: stuck between wanting to get out quickly and staying to see how much weirder it gets. Put it on and find out which way you lean.
