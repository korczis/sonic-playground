## Genre and Mood

**Glasser**'s **Tremel (Jamie xx Remix)** turns an art-pop song into an electronic remix that ends somewhere near house. Quirky is The FADER's verdict in other words: totally strange, it wrote, like another entity entirely from either Glasser or The xx. Dreamy belongs to the ending, a wordless vocal over keys that melt away.

## Song Summary

This is the third of four tracks on the Tremel single Young Turks released on 29 March 2010, about four minutes long against the original's three. Cameron Mesirow wrote and sings the song; the single credits her with Ariel Rechtshaid, Daniel Hart, David Moyer and Garrett Ray as performers, and Jamie xx as remixer. The FADER streamed the remix on 14 April 2010, and Matthew Schnipper's short post is the fullest description any source gives: Jamie, he wrote, totally reconstitutes Tremel.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times, so this guide follows the order of The FADER's description.

### Opening

Mesirow's vocals arrive dispersed, The FADER wrote, across hiccuping bass. Listen for her voice in fragments rather than whole lines.

### Main Section

Under the scattered voice runs what the post called a loose ramble of keys and drum rolls. Follow the keys: they carry the track to its end.

### The Last Minute

For the last minute and more, the track relents into the blueprint for an early-'90s house song, with Glasser wordlessly singing la-la.

### Ending

Then, in The FADER's words, it collapses into low, melting keys.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can keep a song's shape or break it apart. Jamie xx does the second. The FADER called the result a surprisingly fruitful exquisite corpse, after the surrealist method in which each contributor adds to a work while seeing only part of what came before. Hear the whole song first in {{ <track_link source="glasser-tremel.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the rhythm of house music, a kick on every beat with hi-hats between. The FADER heard only its blueprint in the closing minute; listen for how much of that groove arrives before the track falls apart.

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes other people's records; remixes let one show his own taste. The FADER noted that Jamie xx, producer of The xx, had begun to swerve away from his group's sound in his remixes. Compare {{ <track_link source="the-xx-crystalised.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Jamie xx is James Smith, born in London in 1988. Wikipedia lists Glasser among the artists he remixed after The xx's first mixtape, beside Florence and the Machine, Adele and Jack Peñate; the 2010 Elevate festival named the Tremel remix as his latest that year. Mesirow released her debut album Ring that September.

## Impact and Reception

Within a year Jamie xx had produced We're New Here, a whole remix album of Gil Scott-Heron's I'm New Here. Play the original, then this, and listen for what survives.
