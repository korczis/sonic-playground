## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** tagged **13:6:35** on their own Bandcamp under blues, electronic, soul, abstract and turntable, and Deezer files its album under R&B and soul and funk. Tru Thoughts introduced the trio's sound in the band's own words, handmade hip-hop blues soul. No critic seems to have written about this song on its own; soulful and introspective are this page's reading of what reviewers heard across the album, smoky blues vocals over hip-hop beats, and not a description of the track.

## Song Summary

13:6:35 is the seventh of eleven tracks on Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts on 16 July 2007, and runs 4:08. The album credits all three members, Ricky Fabulous, Kathrin deBoer and DJ Modest, with the writing, production, engineering and recording, done at a place they called Flat 2. The song is older than the album: Basic Soul names The Itch/13:6:35 as one of two singles the band had put out on their own Hippoflex label, and Wikipedia dates that single to 2005, listing it as 13:6:34. No source explains the title or gives the lyrics.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005; their Bandcamp tells how Fabulous and Modest met deBoer in a London market. Their first records were limited 7-inch singles on Hippoflex, sold in independent shops and at gigs, before Tru Thoughts, the Brighton label, signed them in 2007. The same page lists their influences from Sarah Vaughan to Cut Chemist; deBoer had also sung with DJ Vadim and Bonobo's live band.

## Impact and Reception

Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut album in Tru Thoughts' history. XLR8R compared deBoer to Róisín Murphy and a latter-day Sarah Vaughan; Basic Soul found the set consistent but lacking one standout moment. In 2012 the label posted the song on its YouTube jukebox channel, together with a remix of it by Natural Self. Put it on with the album around it.
