## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" text="Rapping" /> }} is speech set rhythmically against a beat, and on this album it is the whole performance on record. Listen for where he places the stresses, and how much space he leaves between lines, rather than for melody.

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes a recording from idea to finished track, and in rap the producer often builds the whole instrumental. Smoov-E, the Bay Area rapper and producer Eli Meltzer, made all of Teardrops on My Tombstone and released it on R.E.A.L. Records, the label of his own albums. In its 2020 review of Speak of the Devil, Legends Will Never Die called him a good match for Avalon because his sounds complement Avalon's debauchery; that album is heard in {{ <track_link source="mickey-avalon-eli-ultraviolence.md" /> }}, and Meltzer's own work in {{ <track_link source="eli-faster-you-fall.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Mickey Avalon, born Yeshe Perl in 1975, is a rapper from California. His self-titled debut came out in 2006 on Interscope with the singles Jane Fonda and Mr. Right; Loaded followed in 2012. In November 2016 he announced his third album, Teardrops on My Tombstone, for Valentine's Day 2017. Wikipedia lists it as self-released, while the release itself carries R.E.A.L. Records on its copyright line and came out digitally, on CD and on vinyl.
