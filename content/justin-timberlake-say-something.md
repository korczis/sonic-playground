## Genre and Mood

**Justin Timberlake**'s **Say Something**, sung with **Chris Stapleton**, is a pop record with country at its centre and soul in its harmonies. Wikipedia files it as an upbeat country pop and country rock ballad, and the label fits: the tempo moves while the words look inward. It is filed here as reflective for its lyric and soulful for its voices and harmonies.

## Song Summary

The lyric is about reflection and regret, about the pressure to have something to say and not being sure you do. The song blends the electronic structures of the album's earlier singles, Filthy and Supplies, with Stapleton's country blues. It is credited to Timberlake, Timothy Mosley (Timbaland), Nate Hills (Danja), Stapleton and Larrance Dopson, produced by Timberlake, Timbaland and Danja, and recorded at Conway Recording Studios in Hollywood, with Elliott Ives and Stapleton on guitar.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times or a full structure, so this follows what the critics heard.

### The Groove

Joe's Will Lavin heard hip hop-inspired drums under a country-driven guitar riff. CMT's Lauren Tingle named driving acoustic rhythm guitars, horns and syncopated percussion.

### The Verses

Stapleton told Billboard he went to Los Angeles only to write songs with Timberlake, and was surprised when Timberlake asked him to take a verse. That is how the song became a duet.

### The Bridge

Pitchfork's Jamieson Cox singled out the bridge's conclusion, that saying nothing can be the greatest way of saying something.

## Concepts in Focus

Stapleton is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} who is also one of its writers. The two first sang together at the 2015 CMA Awards.

The contrast between them is {{ <concept_link name="melisma" /> }} against clarity: Tingle heard Timberlake's high tenor and Stapleton's gravelly melisma, his voice running through several notes on one syllable.

The {{ <concept_link name="bridge" /> }} usually brings contrast before the last chorus; here it states the song's moral, and critics argued about whether that moral says anything.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Arturo Perez Jr. with La Blogothèque, was filmed in one continuous take at the Bradbury Building in Los Angeles and recorded live with 17 musicians and a 60-person choir. It runs 6:23, so it is a different performance from the 4:38 album recording embedded here.

## Artistic Context

Man of the Woods came out on RCA on 2 February 2018, two days before Timberlake's Super Bowl halftime show. Say Something was its third single, released with the video on 25 January. Timberlake and Timbaland had written together before, on Cry Me a River in 2002, the song {{ <track_link source="gabriella-cilmi-cry-me-a-river.md" /> }} covers.

## Impact and Reception

It reached number 9 on the Billboard Hot 100, Timberlake's 19th solo top ten and Stapleton's first, number 9 in the UK and number 1 in Slovakia and Slovenia. Uproxx heard a surprisingly danceable groove; Pitchfork's Marc Hogan wrote that the two have nothing to say. The video won Best Live Video at the 2018 UK Music Video Awards, and the song was nominated for a Grammy. Hear the album version, then the one-take video.
