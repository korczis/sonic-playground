## Genre and Mood

**Dynoro** and **Gigi D'Agostino**'s **In My Mind** is deep house with a pop hook; Wikipedia files it under deep house and slap house, and Dynoro's own upload tags it future house. The mood is catchy and quietly sad, a festival-sized track in which one critic heard an unspoken sadness, and nostalgic too: its hook is about as old as its producer.

## Song Summary

In My Mind is a mashup. Dynoro, a Lithuanian teenager, combined In My Mind, a 2012 progressive house song by Ivan Gough and Feenixpawl sung by Georgi Kay, with the hook of D'Agostino's L'amour toujours. He first released it under his own name; copyright problems took it out of the stores, and it returned on 8 June 2018 on B1 Recordings, a joint venture with Sony Music, with D'Agostino credited. The memorable detail is the swap: where the 2012 original had a synth chorus, this version has a simple vocal hook.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times for this track; the parts below follow Production Music Live's 2019 analysis, which places it in F-sharp major.

### Intro and Verse

The vocal sits over a sawtooth, brass-like bass that plays the root notes of the chords.

### Build-up

A rising loop or drums with pitch bent upward lead into the drop.

### Drop

A future house bass, swung and loosely timed, runs over a four-on-the-floor kick, claps and percussion; in the second half a hi-hat pattern enters and a gliding synth lead takes over.

### Second Drop and Outro

The bass stutters, the claps and hi-hats change, and the track closes on a vocal chop with heavy delay.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="mashup" /> }} lays parts of existing records on top of each other. That is how this track began, and its history shows the cost: the first release vanished over copyright and returned with D'Agostino credited.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} helped it travel: D'Agostino's melody was already known, so the track arrived half-familiar.

{{ <concept_link name="swing" /> }} is what Iain Mew heard when he called it stadium dance music made to swing. The analysis explains why: the bass notes are swung and left unquantized, slightly off the grid, so a straight kick drum carries a bassline that lilts. Compare another stitched-together record in {{ <track_link source="controller-7_get-ready-for-the-young-folks.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Dynoro is Edvinas Pechovskis, born in Vilnius on 25 December 1999; D'Agostino is the Italian DJ behind L'amour toujours. The song Dynoro reworked had its own history: the 2012 original came out on labels including Axtone, and Axwell's mix of it was nominated for a Grammy for Best Remixed Recording.

## Impact and Reception

In July 2018 In My Mind reached number one in Germany and Austria, and it topped the charts in Sweden, Switzerland, Norway, Finland, Hungary, the Czech Republic and Slovakia. It reached number five in the UK, the first UK top-five hit for both artists, went diamond in France and double platinum in the UK and the US. The Singles Jukebox was less impressed, averaging 3.57 out of 10. Play it and listen to how the bass leans against the kick.
