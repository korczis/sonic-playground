> Roman Flügel's remix of "Idle Eyes" takes C.A.R.'s original track and strips it down to its most delicate essence. The title suggests a detached gaze, mirrored by Flügel's minimal approach, which lets the music float weightlessly. The remix becomes a meditative experience, where subtle textures and layers of sound build a dreamlike atmosphere.

With its delicate, almost haunting quality, this remix amplifies the mysterious and introspective vibe of the original, a minimalist track where every sound feels deliberate. Roman Flügel, one of the most respected names of German electronic music, makes a remix that is as spacious as it is hypnotic, distant and intimately close at the same time.

## Genre and Mood

**C.A.R.'s** "Idle Eyes," remixed by **Roman Flügel**, falls into the realm of **minimal electronic** with a heavy emphasis on atmosphere. The track radiates a **hypnotic** and **ethereal** vibe, made for late-night listening where every sound has room around it.

## About the Artists

**C.A.R.** is the solo project of Chloé Raunet, the former singer of the London band Battant. Where Battant had a spiky punk edge, C.A.R. leans toward electro and cold wave, and her vocals carry a cool, detached tone.

**Roman Flügel** is a German producer whose work, under his own name and many aliases, is known for its precision and restraint. His remixes, like his original records, often lean toward minimalism and slow-building atmosphere.

## Song Summary

Roman Flügel's remix of "Idle Eyes" opens with a subtle, almost imperceptible pulse and unfolds slowly. Resident Advisor called it the clear pick of the remix package: a rattlesnake bassline, later joined by spiralling keys and pads that swell into a euphoric climax. Each sound is deliberate, and the result is a meditative space built from very few parts.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts, and this one shows how far that can go. C.A.R.'s original, in Resident Advisor's words, sets a blank-eyed vocal over gently building bass and soft background sounds in a slick electro and cold-wave frame; Flügel keeps the mood of detachment but changes the format completely, from a song into a long, slow-burning club track.

The engine is the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}. With the drums kept sparse, the rattling bass carries the groove and much of the melody, and its repetition is what makes the track hypnotic.

The payoff is a {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} without the usual tricks. There is no snare roll and no sudden silence; instead keys and pads enter one after another and swell, so the tension grows by accumulation until the climax. Compare the more direct club rebuild in {{ <track_link source="metronomy-love-letters-soulwax-remix.md" /> }}.

## The Lyric

The words of "Idle Eyes" are built on images of clockwork, a wound-up toy soldier and a threat of vandalism, delivered in C.A.R.'s detached voice. In the remix the vocal becomes one more texture over the bass, which suits a lyric about something mechanical being set in motion.

## Artistic Context

"Idle Eyes" came out on the label Kill The DJ on 29 September 2014, a month ahead of C.A.R.'s debut album, with a remix package by Roman Flügel, Planningtorock, Clara 3000 and rRoxymore. Flügel's remix was also issued on vinyl by Les Disques De La Mort.
