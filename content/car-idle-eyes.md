## Genre and Mood

**minimal electronic** with a heavy emphasis on atmosphere. The track radiates a

## About the Artists

**C.A.R.** (Chloé Raunet) is an electronic artist known for her ability to blend 
cold wave, post-punk, and minimal synth into a distinct, introspective sound. Her 
vocals often carry a raw, vulnerable tone, exploring themes of solitude and 
self-reflection.

**Roman Flügel**, a legend in the electronic music world, is celebrated for his 
ability to create lush, experimental soundscapes that push the boundaries of 
electronic music. His remixes, like his original work, are meticulous, often 
leaning towards minimalism and deep atmospheric exploration.

## Song Summary

Roman Flügel’s remix of “Idle Eyes” opens with a subtle, almost imperceptible 
pulse, drawing listeners in with its minimalist approach. The track unfolds 
slowly, layering delicate synth lines and pulsating beats that give the remix its 
hypnotic and atmospheric feel. Each sound is deliberate, creating a meditative, 
dreamlike space for the listener to get lost in.
