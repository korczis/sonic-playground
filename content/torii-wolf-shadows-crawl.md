## Genre and Mood

**Torii Wolf** sings **Shadows Crawl** over a beat by DJ Premier, and it is hip-hop of the most classic kind with a singer instead of a rapper at its centre. New Lease Music heard the heavy influence of late-1980s and early-1990s boom bap in it; XXL heard Wolf glide over an ominous mix of piano, Premier's signature drums and occasional scratches. The mood is dark and haunting, and it is all over in two and a half minutes.

## Song Summary

The song is about a night-time fear: that secrets will spill out while the singer sleeps, and that the person beside her will learn them. Its beat was not made for her. DJ Premier said, as RAPstation reported, that he had made it for Yuna's new album, but she went for another demo; Torii Wolf then wrote Shadows Crawl on the spot and cut the vocals. It was released in July 2016 as the second track from her coming album Flow Riiot, after "1st".

## Musical Breakdown

### Sound Breakdown

No source describes the song section by section, so this page does not either. The parts the reviews name are few and clear: a piano, Premier's drums and his scratches, the three elements XXL lists. Listen for how they share the space with the voice.

### The Voice

New Lease Music called Wolf's voice a throaty yet delicate instrument. Here it carries a lyric about being afraid of one's own sleep, sung, not rapped, over drums of the kind Premier has made for rappers from Gang Starr to PRhyme.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in hip-hop is often the author of the music itself, and DJ Premier is one of the genre's best known. XXL wrote that Flow Riiot would be the first project for a female artist produced entirely by him, and quoted praise of him as a tailor who caters his sound to an artist while still sounding like Premier; Earmilk later noted contributions from King Of Chill, araabMUZIK and Mike Zombie on the album. A beat first cut for someone else and reused here shows how a producer's sound travels between voices. Hear his work for Gang Starr in {{ <track_link source="gang-starr-full-clip.md" /> }}.

The scratches are {{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }}: the DJ playing a record as an instrument. In Shadows Crawl they are occasional, accents rather than a solo.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} turns the lyric into an image: a woman lies asleep while Wolf's face floats around a dark room, flashing across the screen when she sings, as if her consciousness were wandering while she sleeps.

## Artistic Context

XXL introduced Torii Wolf in 2016 as a New York singer-songwriter who writes, sings and plays guitar and drums. She signed to Premier's TTT (To The Top) imprint, and Flow Riiot, an album of fifteen tracks, was released on 29 September 2017.

## Impact and Reception

Reviewing the album, New Lease Music picked Shadows Crawl as the track for the serious hip-hop heads' collection. Play it late at night, and listen for how much space the beat leaves her voice.
