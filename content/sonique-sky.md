## Genre and Mood

**Sonique**'s **Sky** is dance music with a pop song inside it: electronic, close to trance, uplifting and euphoric in the way a big vocal hook over a club beat can be. Its singer was also a DJ, and its co-writer a pop songwriter whose credits, Pop Rescue noted, run from Belinda Carlisle to Madonna.

## Song Summary

Sky was written by Sonique and the songwriter Rick Nowels, and produced by Nowels with Chris Cox and Barry Harris. It was the third single from her debut album Hear My Cry, after It Feels So Good. Few critics wrote about the song itself. Pop Rescue, reviewing the album years later, called it "a great little light trance track" that "sits perfectly amongst It Feels So Good and I Put A Spell On You", and AllMusic's Stephen Thomas Erlewine found much of the album "exceptionally produced", with Sonique's singing "usually compelling". This page is the album version, 4:26.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes Sky section by section, so this breakdown follows its versions rather than its bars.

### The Album Version

On Hear My Cry the song runs 4:26, the length Wikipedia and MusicBrainz give for the album recording. The official video, which Armada uploaded in 2025, runs about as long.

### The Radio Edit

For radio and most CD singles the song was cut to 3:59.

### The Remixes

For clubs it was rebuilt by Sharam Jey and by The Conductor & The Cowboy, and Sonique made her own six-and-a-half-minute remix, which closes some editions of the album.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shortens a track so a station will play it. Sky's runs 3:59 against the album's 4:26, so most listeners in 2000 heard less than this version.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a song for another setting. The 12-inch carried two remixes beside the album version, records for DJs as much as for fans.

All of these are an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} of one recording: the same song cut, stretched or rebuilt for radio, album and club.

## Artistic Context

**Sonique** is Sonia Marina Clarke, a London singer, musician and DJ who first came to attention in S'Express in the early 1990s. Hear My Cry came out on Serious and Universal in February 2000. In the United States Sky went to radio on 19 June 2000; in Britain it was released on 4 September.

## Impact and Reception

Sky reached number two in the UK and number one in Croatia and Romania, and was certified silver in Britain. In 2001 Sonique won the Brit Award for British female solo artist. Armada, which took over her catalogue in 2025, says Sky "further solidified her status as a global dance music icon". Hear the album version, then the radio edit, and listen for the difference.
