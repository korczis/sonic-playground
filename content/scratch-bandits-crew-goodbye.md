## Genre and Mood

**Scratch Bandits Crew** are DJs first, and **Goodbye** is their hip-hop at its quietest: downtempo, built from records, with no guest MC credited. Its feelings are melancholic, romantic and calm, and on an album full of guest MCs and switching styles, reviewers singled it out as the moment the noise drops.

## Song Summary

Goodbye is the seventh of eleven tracks on Stereo 7, the crew's 2015 album for Chinese Man Records, and one of five without a guest vocalist. Its centre, according to La Grosse Radio, is a sampled crooner whose voice conjures suits and champagne flutes; the same reviewer heard a romantic melancholy that charmed them and a serenity that stood out on the record. Les Oreilles Curieuses wrote that the track impresses with its gentleness and slight melancholy. No source names the record the voice comes from, so this page does not guess.

### Main Section

A crooner's voice, lifted from an old record, is the element the reviews remember, and the track stays gentle enough that they heard it as serene. Around it, Stereo 7 as a whole mixes seventies samples, hip-hop beats, electro bass and jazz instrumentation, in La Grosse Radio's description, and Froggy's Delight heard styles shift inside single tracks.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" text="Sampling" /> }} is the heart of this track. A sampled voice brings its own era with it: the reviewer's images of suits and champagne are what the crooner's timbre suggests, not anything the crew had to play. Lifting a fragment of someone else's record and placing it in a new frame changes what it means: an old singer's voice becomes part of a 2015 farewell.

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" text="Turntablism" /> }}, playing records as an instrument, is the crew's craft and the reason for their name. La Grosse Radio credits Syr with digging up the samples and handling the turntables, and Discogs files the album under cut-up and turntablism. No source describes the scratching on Goodbye itself; compare the hand-built beats of {{ <track_link source="c2c-feat-pigeon-john-because-of-you.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Scratch Bandits Crew came out of Lyon as a group of DJs; by Stereo 7, reviewers described the core as the duo Supa-Jay, the founder, and Syr. The album, mixed by Michel "Mitch" Vincent and released on 18 May 2015, was their first for Chinese Man Records. YouTube's listing credits Goodbye to Jérémy Delorme, Cyril Vigan and Pierre Vadon.

## Impact and Reception

French reviews of Stereo 7 were warm: Les Oreilles Curieuses called the record more urban and more varied than their debut and its blend of sounds a strength, and Froggy's Delight praised its technical mastery and sample digging. Two of them stopped to single out Goodbye. Put it on late and let the crooner say his piece.
