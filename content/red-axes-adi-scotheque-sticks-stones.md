## Genre and Mood

**Red Axes** and **Adi Scotheque**, the name the streaming release credits, made **Sticks & Stones** for an album that Resident Advisor described as a mix of post-punk, new wave and '90s club sounds. Its label called the track a certifiable club hit, fuelled by sassy vocals. It is energetic by design: the duo said they wanted to give their guest a more energetic track than last time. Defiant and playful are a reading of its refrain, which turns a schoolyard taunt into a shrug.

## Song Summary

The guest is Adi Bronicki, credited by name as featuring, writer and vocalist on the LP and in the official video, and as Adi Scotheque on the streaming release, whose composer credits name Adi Bronicki beside Dori Sadovnik and Niv Arzi. Her lyric, printed under the video, tells of Rami from the block, thirteen and taught by pain to be mean and quick, then pivots to a refrain that calls itself "a love song for geometry", at peace with asymmetry, and for geology, because "dirt won't ever hurt me".

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track's sections, so the breakdown follows what the duo and the lyric document.

### Main Section

The base is a jam: Dori Sadovnik's brother on bass and his nephew on a drum machine. Over it sits what Red Axes called the weird sound, a Korg guitar synthesizer.

### Verses and Refrain

Bronicki's verses tell Rami's story; the refrain about geometry and geology answers it, and the printed lyric ends on a list of bears, rattlesnakes and mosquitoes to fear.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit usually names one guest one way. Here it shows how credits drift between formats: Adi Bronicki on the vinyl and in the video, Adi Scotheque in the streaming credit. Red Axes had already featured her on their album The Beach Goths.

The track began as {{ <concept_link name="improvisation" /> }}: a family jam, recorded and shaped into a song. Their earlier {{ <track_link source="red-axes-caminho-de-dreyfus.md" /> }} shows the psychedelic house that came before; {{ <track_link source="curses-surrender-jennifer-cardini-remix.md" /> }} is another post-punk club record of the period.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Sergei Rostropovich and starring Lea Prinz, appeared on 19 March 2020 with the album's announcement and runs 4:21, longer than the 3:46 album track.

## Artistic Context

Red Axes are Dori Sadovnik and Niv Arzi, a Tel Aviv duo who met at fourteen and started in a psychedelic rock band. Their self-titled third album came out on Dark Entries on 8 May 2020, marking ten years of collaboration. Magnetic reported it was the first they conceived as a full-length, and the label heard it brimming with jagged guitars, spacy arpeggios and hypnotic vocals. Sticks & Stones was its fifth track and first single.

## Impact and Reception

No chart entry could be found. Magnetic's Ryan Middleton heard the album delivering something new at every turn. Turn it up and listen for the guitar synthesizer.
