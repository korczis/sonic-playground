## Genre and Mood

**Glass Candy** were a Portland, Oregon duo who moved from post-punk towards disco, and **Naked City** belongs to that later sound: disco and synth-pop made electronic, hypnotic, glamorous and a little mysterious. It is short, just over two and a half minutes.

## Song Summary

Naked City is a song that waited. When Italians Do It Better put it on its YouTube channel at the start of October 2018, Aaron Vehling of Vehlinggo called it a previously unreleased gem from around 2008, with Glass Candy's trademark minimalist melodicism and disco backbeat, led by Ida No's otherworldly voice. Johnny Jewel produced and mixed it. The lyric, printed with the label's upload, is a chain of short images of the city after dark, "smoke & mirrors" and neon, the city as an illusion, pleasure and danger in the same breath.

### Main Section

A disco backbeat and a sparse, melodic arrangement: in Vehlinggo's description, the duo's familiar minimalism.

### Verse 1

Ida No sings the images one after another, in short lines, more incantation than story.

### Outro

The song ends after about 2:36. Later on the EP it returns twice more, once without the voice and once with nothing but the voice.

## Concepts in Focus

The Naked City EP is a small lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. Besides the song and two other tracks, Good Time and The Message, it carries an {{ <concept_link name="instrumental" /> }} version and an a cappella one. Releasing a song in pieces like this invites DJs and producers to rebuild it, and it lets a listener hear exactly what each half brings: the beat's patience on one side, the voice's chant on the other.

It is also a record about the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in a duo. Johnny Jewel is credited with producing and mixing the track, while Ida No's voice is the face of it. Glass Candy's sound comes from that division of labour, and the stripped-down versions make it audible. For another cool look at glamour, hear {{ <track_link source="felix-da-house-cat-madame-hollywood.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Ida No and Johnny Jewel formed Glass Candy in Portland in 1996. Their early music drew on no wave, post-punk and art rock; later they moved towards synth-pop and Italo disco. Jewel co-founded Italians Do It Better with Mike Simonetti in 2006, and the label released B/E/A/T/B/O/X in 2007 and the singles that followed, up to a cover of Herb Alpert's Rise in 2014. Naked City and The Beat's Alive, both issued digitally in 2019, were the duo's final EPs; Naked City came out on 11 January.

## Impact and Reception

Months before the EP came out, Exclaim! reported, citing Gorilla vs. Bear, that the song soundtracked Chanel's Spring/Summer 2019 show at Paris Fashion Week, and the video's thumbnail carried the Chanel logo redesigned in Jewel's and No's image. Play it with the lights low, then play the a cappella.
