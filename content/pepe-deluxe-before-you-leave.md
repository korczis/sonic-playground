## Genre and Mood

**Pepe Deluxé**'s **Before You Leave** is breakbeat, funk and big beat from Helsinki by way of a Brighton label: a groovy, playful record that Hot Press called a fine piece of musical breakbeat funk.

## Song Summary

The song began as another one. When Levi's wanted the band's Woman in Blue for a jeans campaign, the band says, EMI and Sony quoted £500,000 to clear its Nina Simone and Tony Hatch samples. Sony offered a deal on the Tony Hatch part, provided EMI got nothing, so instead of paying for both, Pepe Deluxé hired a musicologist, rewrote the lyrics and melody, and released the result as Before You Leave. It is still built on a Tony Hatch composition: the closing track of his 1970 album with the Satin Brass, Sounds of the 70's. Its release credits name Jari Salo, the band's James Spectrum, and Tony Hatch, and split the publishing between Catskills and Sony ATV.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

No review we found walks through the track, so this page describes less than usual. What the sources give is its frame: a breakbeat-funk groove, the Tony Hatch material underneath, and a new vocal line where Nina Simone's voice used to be. The album version runs about five and a half minutes; the official video, directed by Miikka Lommi in 2001 with Saimi Hoyer as the Woman in Blue, uses an edit of about three and a half.

## Concepts in Focus

Before You Leave is a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} and its price. The band's debut, Super Sound, was so heavily sampled that clearance problems forced a new version of the album, with many tracks re-created from cleared samples or played on live instruments.

The song is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} of their own Woman in Blue: same band, same vibe, as they put it, new melody and words. And what is embedded here is a {{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }}: the 25th anniversary remaster of 2025, which changes the overall sound of the album recording but not its arrangement. For the voice that had to go, hear {{ <track_link source="nina-simone-felix-da-housecat-sinnerman.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Pepe Deluxé formed in Helsinki in 1996: DJ Slow (Vellu Maurola), JA-Jazz (Tomi Castrén) and James Spectrum (Jari Salo), experimenting with hip-hop, big beat, breakbeat and downtempo. Their demo EP Three Times a Player was chosen downtempo album of the month by the British magazine Muzik and led to a contract with Brighton's Catskills Records. Later albums turned to psychedelia, baroque pop and surf rock.

## Impact and Reception

Levi's used the track in its worldwide campaign of 2001, which won the Gold Lion for best use of music at the Cannes advertising festival. The single reached number 20 in the UK, giving Catskills the chart hit Hot Press had predicted, and the band says Sony ended up licensing the whole album. In 2025 they reissued Super Sound for its 25th anniversary and put Before You Leave out again with new remixes. Listen for the record that a sample bill rewrote.
