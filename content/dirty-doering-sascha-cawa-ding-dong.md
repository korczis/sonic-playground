## Genre and Mood

**Dirty Doering** and **Sascha Cawa** released **Ding Dong** on their own label, Katermukke, and Discogs files it under deep house. No review describes the track, so the feelings on this page, groovy and hypnotic, are a reading of that genre and of the label's sound rather than a critic's verdict.

## Song Summary

Ding Dong is the title track of the Ding Dong EP, catalogue number KATER088, released digitally on 24 December 2014, Christmas Eve. The original runs 5:09, and the EP also carries a dub mix of 5:17 and a radio version of 3:38. The distributor credits two composers, Velten Doering, Dirty Doering's real name, and Sascha Winkler. Beyond those facts, the sources are silent on what the track sounds like, and this page will not invent it.

## Concepts in Focus

The EP is a small lesson in versions. A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} trims a club track for radio and streaming, usually with a quicker start and fewer repeats; here it brings 5:09 down to 3:38. A {{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }} mix, a name taken from Jamaican studio practice, usually strips a track towards drums and bass and treats the rest with echo and drop-outs. What exactly this dub mix removes, no source says, so it is worth hearing the three side by side.

All of it grows out of Berlin {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Dirty Doering was a resident at Bar 25 and later at Kater Holzig, and the Amsterdam Dance Event lists Sascha Cawa, a DJ since the early 1990s, as a resident at Kater Holzig too, known above all for his joint productions with Doering.

## Artistic Context

Velten Doering, born in Leipzig in 1978, has lived from DJing since 2004; German Wikipedia describes his style as ranging from tech house and deep house to minimal techno. The label itself says Doering and Cawa founded Katermukke together in 2011, and the same two composers are credited on the label's first release, {{ <track_link source="dirty-doering-casino-aquatique.md" /> }}. Ding Dong came eighty-seven catalogue numbers later.

## Impact and Reception

No chart run or review is recorded for Ding Dong. What the sources show is a long shelf life inside the label: MusicBrainz lists the track on Katermukke Playground I in December 2015 and again on Katermukke Playground III in December 2016, two years after release.
