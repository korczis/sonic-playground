## Genre and Mood

**Martin Waslewski** made **Gerd** for the dance floor and for someone he loved. It is deep house in the long club form, electronic but built, as its label describes it, from warm parts such as piano chords and a guitar riff. The mood is groovy and uplifting, and because of its dedication it is also heartfelt: a track meant, in its label's words, for smiles on the dance floor.

## Song Summary

Mother Recordings introduced Gerd as an homage to Waslewski's best friend, his grandfather. The label described a long build-up with driving percussion, piano chords, groovy shakers and a catchy guitar riff, a warm, jazzy vibe, and a drop after the long break that it said no words could describe. The dedication lives in the title and in the warmth of the arrangement. The memorable detail is patience: Gerd takes seven minutes, with a long build-up and a long break before its drop.

## Concepts in Focus

Gerd is a clear example of a {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} in deep house. The label speaks of a long build-up and names what it is made of: driving percussion, shakers, piano chords and a guitar riff. A build-up that does not rush lets the groove feel earned.

Its centre is what the label calls the long break, a {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }}. In a breakdown the beat drops out or thins, and dancers are left with the melody and their own anticipation. Length matters: a short pause is a breath, a long one is a story.

That story ends in the {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }}, the moment the full beat and bass return. Compare the patient, loop-based warmth of {{ <track_link source="whtkd-say-to-me.md" /> }}, another German deep house record of those years.

## Artistic Context

Martin Waslewski is a German producer and DJ from Halle (Saale). According to his Amsterdam Dance Event profile, he grew up on hip-hop and discovered electronic music in 2010; he has released on Hommage, Monaberry, Heulsuse, Exploited Ghetto and the Berlin label Mother Recordings, which he first sent a demo in early 2015. Mother released the Gerd EP, catalogue number MOTHER039, on 28 September 2015, two tracks: Never Leave You and Gerd.

## Impact and Reception

Gerd is a club record; the label premiered it on its YouTube channel and the producer uploaded it to his own. It belongs here as a reminder that an instrumental can carry a personal story. Wait for the long break, and let the drop arrive.
