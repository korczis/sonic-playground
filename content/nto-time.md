## Genre and Mood

**NTO**'s **Time** is melodic techno with a minimal frame, electronic music from the French producer who released it as N'to. Its feelings come from the style Wikipedia describes in his work: repetition that turns hypnotic, and an atmospheric pull from melodies that keep evolving over a bass-driven pulse.

## Song Summary

Time runs six minutes and four seconds and came out on 13 March 2015 as the A-side of HM09, the ninth release of Hungry Music, paired with Chez Nous. Hungry Music put it on YouTube the same day. No review we could find describes the track itself, so this page tells you where it comes from and what is said about its maker's style, and leaves the details to your ears.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} is the natural unit of music built on repetition. Instead of a song form, a few patterns run again and again, and change comes from layering, removing and varying them, which is what evolving motifs means in practice.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the other half. Music described as bass-driven puts the lowest line at the centre: it carries the groove against the kick drum and often holds the track's identity as much as any melody above it.

## Artistic Context

NTO was born Anthony Favier in Marseille in 1985; his friends' southern-accented way of saying his nickname "Antho" gave him the name N'to. He started on guitar as a teenager, discovered computer production at sixteen or seventeen and released his first EPs on Basilic Records from 2008. In 2013 he co-founded Hungry Music with Worakls and Joachim Pastor, whose {{ <track_link source="joachim-pastor-eternity.md" /> }} came out in the same series, and in 2015 the label toured France with its artists. He cites Mediterranean landscapes, Marseille and Corsica, as inspiration.

## Impact and Reception

Time was later included on the label's compilation Hungry 5 in 2018. In 2020 Favier left Hungry Music on good terms and continued as NTO, releasing the album Apnea in 2021 and Forever Friends with the pianist Sofiane Pamart in 2024. Play it as six minutes of patience and listen for what changes.
