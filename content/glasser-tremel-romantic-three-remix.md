## Genre and Mood

**Glasser**'s **Tremel (Romantic Three Remix)** moves an art-pop song onto a darker dance floor. Stereogum's Brandon Stosuy heard it keep the original's chill but replace its tropical feel with layers of darkwave disco: electronic and cold, a remix that changes the song's genre more than its mood.

## Song Summary

This is the second of four versions on the Tremel single that Young Turks released digitally on 29 March 2010, side A's second track on the 12-inch, after the original. It runs about six and a half minutes, more than twice the original's three. Cameron Mesirow wrote and sings the song; the single credits her with Ariel Rechtshaid, Daniel Hart, David Moyer and Garrett Ray as performers, and Romantic Three as remixer. Stereogum posted it on 13 May 2010, via FACT, and its short note is the only description any source gives.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times or walks through the remix, so this guide follows Stosuy's comparison with the original and leaves the structure to your ears.

### The Starting Point

He had described the original as building its dreamy momentum from overlapping percussion, vocals, synthesizers and electronics, with a feel both tropical and frozen. That is the material Romantic Three worked from.

### The Disco Layer

The tropical half goes. In its place come layers of darkwave disco: the cold, synthetic mood of darkwave, a genre born of post-punk and new wave, set to a disco beat.

### The Voice

The lyric stays with water, tides and drowning, and Stosuy noted that here things get a little choppier. Listen for where the song is cut and rearranged rather than played straight.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can keep a song's mood and change its body. Romantic Three keeps the frozen half of Stosuy's description and swaps the tropical half, where Jamie xx, in {{ <track_link source="glasser-tremel-jamie-xx-remix.md" /> }}, scatters the voice in pieces, as The FADER heard it. Hear the source first in {{ <track_link source="glasser-tremel.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is where the name turns up again. Discogs credits Romantic Three, together with Glasser, with mixing FACT Mix 174, a 41-minute mix published under her name in August 2010. It opens with Cluster, the German duo LA Weekly had heard in Tremel the year before, and includes an edit of the Jamie xx remix. Beyond this remix and that mix, no source says who Romantic Three are.

## Artistic Context

Mesirow, born in Boston and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area, released Tremel on Young Turks six months before her debut album Ring. Discogs files the single under styles from ethereal to disco and UK garage, and this remix is where the disco lives.

## Impact and Reception

The remix reached listeners through blogs: FACT shared it, Stereogum reposted it, and the 12-inch put it beside the Jamie xx and Astronomer versions, the latter nine minutes long. Play the original, then this, and listen for what turns colder.
