## Genre and Mood

**Be Svendsen**'s **Circle** is deep house at an unhurried pace; Discogs files it under deep house and tech house, and the label tagged its upload organic house and downtempo. The mood is the one Iboga Records described when it released the track: groovy summer vibes and organic vibes "in spirit of celebration".

## Song Summary

Circle was the title track of a two-track digital single that Iboga Records, a Danish label, released on 15 July 2014, catalogue IBOGADIGITAL147, with Bones as its second track. The label's upload introduced Be Svendsen as a producer out of Copenhagen and the single as his first for the label, and it set the two tracks against each other: Circle the celebration, Bones "an unsolved desert mystery". The original mix runs 6:12.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the felt quality of a rhythm, the way its parts lock together and make people move. In Circle that is the label's whole pitch, groovy summer vibes: notice what its parts do to keep that going for six minutes.

Circle also lived on in the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. Mollono.Bass made a remix of 6:00, and Zen Baboon a mix of 7:41 that Iboga posted on its channel. Comparing them with the original shows how other producers heard the same material: what they kept, and where they took it.

## Artistic Context

Be Svendsen is a deep house and downtempo DJ and producer based in Copenhagen. Before Circle he released the Catchpenny & Cosmos EP on Acker Records in 2011, then Gross Kelle and Masquerade, and the Alchemie EP came out the same year; later came Skywalker, The Bar Tender and, in 2021, Nemrut on Cercle Records. Cercle filmed him playing live at Mount Nemrut in Türkiye. Hear him three years before Circle in {{ <track_link source="the-crooked-spoke-be-svendsen-black-tower-hotel-be-svendsen-mix.md" /> }}.
