## Genre and Mood

**Flume**'s **What You Need** is the quiet end of his debut album: electronic, downtempo, dreamy and intimate. PopMatters called it a particularly delightful and glitchy slow-jam, and XLR8R heard slick dynamics and tender vibes in it.

## Song Summary

What You Need is the fourteenth of fifteen tracks on Flume, the self-titled debut that Harley Streten released on Future Classic on 9 November 2012, and it runs four minutes and ten seconds. Wikipedia credits him alone as its writer and producer, and it has no guest singer: PopMatters counted it among the album's instrumentals. Flume posted it on his own YouTube channel in November 2012, the month of the album.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track section by section, and none times its parts; this follows what reviewers heard in it.

### The Pace

PopMatters' "slow-jam" is the first thing to hear: a track that takes its time.

### The Texture

The same review called it glitchy, the word for sounds that stutter, skip or break apart on purpose. Listen for where the surface frays and where it stays smooth.

### The Shape

XLR8R grouped it with Change and Ezra as tracks whose slick dynamics and tender vibes have a charm that cannot go unnoticed, even if they sounded to the writer like a younger sibling of the music of Lapalux and Shlohmo. Listen for where the track swells and where it draws back.

## Concepts in Focus

Flume is a one-person record, so the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} is the whole band. Streten wrote and produced every track, and on the instrumentals there is no singer to share the attention: every sound is his choice. Compare {{ <track_link source="flume-stay-close.md" /> }}, which XLR8R praised in the same paragraph for its confident studio craft.

The track found its widest audience as a {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} placement. Grand Theft Auto V's Worldwide FM, hosted by Gilles Peterson, plays house, jazz and worldbeat, and What You Need is on the playlist Grand Theft Wiki lists for the game's next-generation editions, beside Four Tet, Mount Kimbie and Portishead.

## Artistic Context

Flume is the Sydney producer Harley Streten. His debut topped the Australian albums chart, won the J Award for Australian Album of the Year and went double platinum in Australia; its singles were Sleepless, On Top and Holdin On. Q called its collage approach the starting point for a captivating album of pop electronica.

## Impact and Reception

What You Need was never a single, and PopMatters, which preferred the vocal tracks, still singled it out among the instrumentals; a Salacious Sound reviewer ranked it third among his favourite tracks on the record. Put it on late, after Warm Thoughts, and let it play through to Star Eyes.
