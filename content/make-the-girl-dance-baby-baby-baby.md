## Genre and Mood

**Make The Girl Dance**'s **Baby Baby Baby** is electro and electro house for the dance floor, playful and catchy on record and provocative on screen. Most people met it through its video, and the song's story is hard to tell without it; no review we found takes the music apart, so this page says what was written about it and leaves the rest to your ears.

## Song Summary

Baby Baby Baby was the duo's first single, released on 12 May 2009 by the Paris label Roy Music. The credits name Gregory Cauzot and Pierre Mathieu as composers and Honorine Crosnier as lyricist. Writing two days later, Synthtopia heard "electro beats" and earworm synth hooks, the German Ohrwurm in its words. This page embeds the original mix, the 4:32 version of the single that carried it with remixes.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the order of events in the track, so this breakdown follows the parts that were written about.

### The Hooks

The synth hooks are the track's calling card: short riffs made to repeat in your head after it ends.

### The Beat

Synthtopia heard the clip as a reply to Matt & Kim's Lessons Learned, with "more electro beats" than the song it answered.

### The Video

The official video is one take of about four minutes on the rue Montorgueil in Paris's 2nd arrondissement. Three women walk naked down the street, mouthing the song, with the lyric on screen and black rectangles over the nudity.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} made the song. Its idea was a reply to Matt & Kim's Lessons Learned, made with three women in a Paris street. A single continuous shot makes the stunt feel live and unedited, and Synthtopia's headline question, sexy or sexist, kept people talking about it.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} had to hold the attention the video won. An earworm synth riff is the electro producer's chorus: a few notes that return until you know them.

{{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }} gave the song a further life. Ultra Records' upload of the video notes that it was featured in a Victoria's Secret commercial, Hello, Bombshell. Compare {{ <track_link source="cassius-toop-toop.md" /> }}, Paris dance music of the same decade that also leaned on rock attitude.

## Artistic Context

Make The Girl Dance is a Paris duo active since 2008: the musician and producer Greg Kozo and Pierre Mathieu, who had presented Plus vite que la musique on M6. French Wikipedia files them under electro house, French touch and electronic rock. Singles such as My Name's Breezy and Kill Me followed, then the albums Everything Is Gonna Be Ok in the End in 2011 and Extraball in 2015, both on Roy Music.

## Impact and Reception

The single entered the French chart on 13 June 2009, peaked at number 26 a week later and stayed for 22 weeks. Le Parisien reported that the clip was Dailymotion's most watched video of 2009, and by October the single had reached Spain through EMI.
