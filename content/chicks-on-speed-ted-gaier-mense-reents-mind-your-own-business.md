## Genre and Mood

**Chicks On Speed** recorded **Mind Your Own Business** with **Ted Gaier** and **Mense Reents** and put it on their 2000 debut album, which Wikipedia files under electroclash and Discogs under electro, house and synth-pop. It is a cover with a defiant title and a playful spirit, a late-1990s electronic group taking up a song from the first wave of post-punk.

## Song Summary

The song belongs to Delta 5, a Leeds band whose debut single it was, on Rough Trade in 1979, and it remains their best-known song. Its writers are the band's Julz Sale, Ros Allen, Bethan Peters and Kelvin Knight. Chicks On Speed's version was one of the singles they put out in 1998 and 1999, before any album; the recording appeared in 1999 on Pop Tics, a compilation on the Bungalow label, and then on Chicks On Speed Will Save Us All!, where it runs 3:10. Discogs notes that the album sleeve misspells the title as Buisness.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes this cover passage by passage, so this section sets it against the original, as its sources allow.

### The Original

Pitchfork heard Delta 5 as a tense, barbed kind of dark pop: an unusual, dub-heavy rhythm section with two bass players, and three women's voices, deadpan and sarcastic, trading lines.

### The Cover

Discogs credits Chicks On Speed, Gaier and Reents as both producers and performers. How the parts are shared out is undocumented; listen for what replaces the two basses and how the three voices are split.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} says something about the source it chooses. Covers were central to Chicks On Speed's early singles, from The Normal's Warm Leatherette to Cracker's Euro Trash Girl and this one, and the album added The B-52's and Malaria!; Robert Christgau heard the whole record as a revival of early-1980s taste.

{{ <concept_link name="post-punk" /> }} is where the song comes from: Delta 5 came out of the same Leeds art-school scene as Gang of Four and the Mekons and were active in Rock Against Racism, combining feminist politics with a two-bass funk-punk sound.

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }} is where it went. The album is considered to epitomise that German movement, which looked back to the electronic sounds of the early 1980s. Hear the same approach applied to Malaria! in {{ <track_link source="chicks-on-speed-kaltes-klares-wasser.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Chicks On Speed formed in Munich in 1997 at the Academy of Fine Arts, a feminist art collective as much as a band, and their first label, Go Records, counted its catalogue numbers down from ten to zero. Ted Gaier and Mense Reents play in Die Goldenen Zitronen, the left-wing Hamburg punk band; Reents is its keyboard player and drummer.

## Impact and Reception

Critics of the album praised its reinterpretations of 1980s songs, and Christgau gave it an A-minus. The original kept travelling too, into Sex Education and an Apple advert. Play both versions and decide whose business it is.
