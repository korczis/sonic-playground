## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** filed **Alice** on their own Bandcamp under blues, electronic, soul, abstract and turntable, and MusicBrainz listeners tag it trip hop and downtempo. No critic seems to have written about the song on its own, so soulful and nostalgic are this page's reading of how the label sold the album: a voice you cannot quite pin down, singing hooks over what sounds like a trawl through a dusty basement full of records you have never heard.

## Song Summary

Alice is the eighth of eleven tracks on Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts on 16 July 2007, and runs 3:40. The album credits all three members, Ricky Fabulous, Kathrin deBoer and DJ Modest, with the writing, production, engineering and recording, done at a place they called Flat 2. Unlike 13:6:35, which the trio had already put out on their own label, Alice appears neither on their earlier singles nor on the 2006 Four Songs EP; its first release is this album. Who Alice is, and what the lyric says, no source tells.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is in the album's title and in the band's tags for the song. Fabulous and Modest began by playing bizarre turntable-and-guitar sets in London bars, by the band's own account; the label promised scratches aplenty, and XLR8R heard a dusting of turntablism over jazzy guitars and handcrafted beats.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} gathers songs from several records. In October 2013, the month the band announced its end, their Best Of opened its second disc with Alice. Compare the song just before it on the album, {{ <track_link source="belleruche-13-6-35.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005, when Fabulous and Modest met deBoer in a market and she sang for them over a cup of tea. Their first records were limited 7-inch singles on their own Hippoflex label before Tru Thoughts, the Brighton label, signed them in 2007. The label summed up the sound as Sarah Vaughan, Charlie Christian and Cut Chemist stuck in a dusty second-hand record store with wine and a sampler.

## Impact and Reception

Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut album in Tru Thoughts' history. XLR8R heard in it the vocal-pop appeal that once made Massive Attack and Portishead exciting, though the standouts it named were Northern Girls and Minor Swing; Basic Soul found the set consistent but lacking one standout moment. In 2012 the label posted Alice on its YouTube jukebox channel. Put it on with the album around it.
