## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song someone else made famous, and the credits are where a cover shows itself: here the songwriters of the original are named, and Bleu Azur only as the performer.

The original is famous for its {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }}. In F minor, its intro and verse cycle through D♭maj7, C7♭9, Fm7 and E♭m7 to A♭7, which the analysis cited by Wikipedia reads as the first four bars of {{ <track_link source="bobby-hebb-sunny.md" /> }} reordered at double time. Whether this version keeps those chords no source confirms; listening for them is the obvious test.

## Artistic Context

Bleu Azur is known from streaming catalogues rather than the press. Deezer lists singles such as Greedy, Flowers, In the Name of Love, Keep It Simple and Don't Feel Right, and Apple Music a 2026 single, i wanna run, filed under House. We found no biography.

The song itself was written by MacDonald and Salter, who made the instrumental track, and Withers, who wrote the words. Grover Washington Jr. recorded it with Withers singing, Richard Tee on electric piano, Steve Gadd on drums, Eric Gale on guitar and Marcus Miller on bass, for the album Winelight, released by Elektra on 24 October 1980.
