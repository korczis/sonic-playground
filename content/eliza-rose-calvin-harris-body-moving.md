## Genre and Mood

**Eliza Rose** and **Calvin Harris** made **Body Moving** as house music cut to pop length: a dance single of two and a half minutes. Euphoria Zine heard the two artists' signature styles combined in a house track; EDMTunes heard disco-infused beats and basslines. The feelings are energetic and groovy, and the lyric, by Euphoria Zine's account, is about little more than the urge to dance, set in the nightlife it describes.

## Song Summary

Body Moving came together after Rose played at Harris's show at Ushuaïa in Ibiza on 30 June 2023, and it was released by Ministry of Sound on 17 November 2023. Rose wrote it with Adam Wiles, Harris's own name, and Jessie Reyez; Harris produced it. Rose said her goal was a track that captured the essence of summer while igniting dance floors. Its most memorable detail is a horn that steps in where the voice would be.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The track opens on a repetitive handclap pattern, and more instruments join with the first verse.

### Verse 1

Rose sings over a beat that alternates between disco and a heavier bassline. Just before the first chorus her voice softens and lets the production briefly take centre stage.

### Chorus

In the chorus her vocal and the production meet as equals. In the chorus and post-chorus a horn plays in place of some of the lyrics, on the very notes Rose would have sung. The track ends well before three minutes, which Euphoria Zine credited with keeping its catchiness from wearing thin.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the idea a song is built to stick with you, and Body Moving splits its hook between a voice and a horn: you learn the line from Rose, then hear the horn sing it back without words.

Underneath is a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}: claps from the first second, then a disco beat alternating with a heavier bassline. House is also the genre Rose works in as a DJ and producer.

Harris is the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} here, credited as producer and co-writer, while the record carries Rose's name first. Compare {{ <track_link source="calvin-harris-rag-n-bone-man-giant.md" /> }}, where he builds house again around another artist's voice.

## Artistic Context

Eliza Rose was born in London's East End in 1991 and raised in Dalston; she worked at Flashback Records as a teenager and began DJing in her late teens. In 2022 B.O.T.A. (Baddest of Them All), made with Interplanetary Criminal, reached number one in the UK, making her the first female DJ to top the chart since Sonique in 2000 ({{ <track_link source="sonique-sky.md" /> }}).

## Impact and Reception

Body Moving reached number 34 in the UK, 11 on the UK dance chart and 30 on the US Hot Dance/Electronic Songs chart, topped radio airplay charts in Latvia and Lithuania, and was certified silver in the UK. Harris later included it on his compilation 96 Months. Turn it up and listen for the horn.
