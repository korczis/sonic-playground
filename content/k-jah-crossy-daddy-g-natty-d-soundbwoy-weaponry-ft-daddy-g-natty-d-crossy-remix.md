## Genre and Mood

**K Jah**'s **SoundBwoy Weaponry**, here in the **Crossy** remix with the MCs **Daddy G** and **Natty D**, is jungle made in the English Midlands and remixed in the West Country. Jungle and drum and bass are the labels its own release carries. Energetic and defiant are this page's words for a dance-floor track with a title about a soundboy's weapons; no review of the remix turned up, so what follows is what the releases document and what the genre is built from.

## Song Summary

The original, spelt Soundboy Weaponry on its streaming release, came out on 23 September 2019 on South Central Recordings, K Jah's own Birmingham label, with Brass Knuckles as its other track; the label's preview credits it to K Jah featuring MC Daddy G and MC Natty D. A year later the label's sixth release, on 10 August 2020, paired this remix by the Bristol DJ Crossy with Jinx's remix of K Jah's Have Some Fun. Its full title there is SoundBwoy Weaponry Ft Daddy G + Natty D (Crossy Remix), and it runs 5:14, the same length as the original.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes this remix section by section, so this guide names only what jungle is made of and where to listen for it.

### The Breaks

Jungle runs on rapid, heavily syncopated breakbeats, often sampled from funk and jazz records and cut into new patterns.

### The Bass

Under them sits the genre's other half: deep basslines from dub, reggae and dancehall.

### The Voices

On top are the two MCs. Natty D's biography describes his choppy ragga style as a natural fit for jungle.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} here stays inside the genre: Crossy, a jungle DJ, rebuilds a jungle track rather than moving it into house or pop.

Daddy G and Natty D are each a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, credited in the title. Both are MCs rather than singers: Natty D's biography calls him an MC, host and lyricist.

The {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} is the engine of jungle: a sampled drum break, sped up and rearranged.

The remix also shows how a {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} reuses tracks. In January 2021 it reappeared on Aries & Kelvin 373 present Born On Road x Jungle Cakes: Welcome To The Jungle, both in full and as a one-minute excerpt inside the continuous mix.

## Artistic Context

K Jah is Kieron Anderson, a jungle and drum and bass producer from Birmingham who has released on labels such as Hocus Pocus, Criminal and Ruffneck Ting and owns South Central Recordings, founded in 2018. Crossy is Ben Cross, a Bristol DJ who grew up on Kenny Ken and DJ Randall and takes inspiration from the Bristol collective Born On Road. Natty D is a Birmingham MC, host and lyricist raised on rave, dub and reggae; for Daddy G, billed MC Daddy G in 2019, no biography could be found.
