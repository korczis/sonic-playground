## Genre and Mood

**Session Victim** made **We Want To Thank All Our Friends** at the meeting point of deep house and disco, the two styles its first release was filed under. The mood is groovy in the most literal sense, a track built to keep bodies moving, and nostalgic in its materials, which come from the duo's love of old records.

## Song Summary

The title reads like a thank-you note. Session Victim are known for building tracks through intuitive sampling sessions, mixing samples with live instruments and organic grooves, and for a taste for dusty, forgotten vinyl. At seven minutes, this one is made for DJs and long nights rather than for radio.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} is how Session Victim work. Their label describes intuitive sampling sessions in which fragments of records meet live instruments, with influences from late-1990s trip hop, jazz, soul and dusty vinyl. The method aims at texture rather than quotation: the grain and warmth that can make a new house record sound older than it is.

Deep house rests on a {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }}, a kick on every beat and a pulse steady enough to hold a dance floor for seven minutes. Disco is house music's ancestor, and a disco-flecked groove like this one wears that family resemblance openly. Compare the longer, glowing disco-funk groove of {{ <track_link source="the-supermen-lovers-moments.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Session Victim are the German duo Hauke Freer and Matthias Reiling, the track's credited composers. We Want To Thank All Our Friends first appeared in 2010 on Paw to the Floor, a four-track EP by the British label Wolf Music Recordings (catalogue number WOLFEP006), alongside Dead Rose Music Company, Francis Inferno Orchestra and Greymatter. The duo's own single release followed on 1 September 2010. Their underground hit Good Intentions came in 2011 and their debut album, The Haunted House of House, in 2012; textura called it club-friendly mood music. Later albums followed on Delusions of Grandeur and, with Needledrop, on Night Time Stories.

## Impact and Reception

The track belongs to the early chapter of a duo who went on to run their own vinyl label, Retreat, and to release four albums. It has kept circulating: in 2019, nine years after release, the YouTube channel Délicieuse Musique put it up for a new audience. It is here for its warmth. Put it on with friends.
