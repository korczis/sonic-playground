## Genre and Mood

**Sweat - Darkovski Extended Remix**, by **Groove Armada**, **Brodanse** and **Cari Golden**, is deep house by every label it has been given: Discogs files the remix release under that style alone. It is also the version that left the clubs: in 2014 and 2015 it appeared on compilations of chill-out, lounge and sunset music, which is why relaxed and groovy are the feelings here.

## Song Summary

Sweat began in 2013 as the first part of a three-part collaboration between Groove Armada and the London brothers Austen and Scott Smart, who record as Brodanse, released on the brothers' own Danse Club Records with the vocalist Cari Golden. Armada Music licensed it that year. In January 2014 Danse Club issued six remixes that Discogs notes as the winners of a Beatport remix competition; Armada's Sweat (Remixes) followed within weeks. Darkovski's is one of the six. This page reviews its long version, 6:25, which Armada's streaming release calls the Extended Remix and the record listings simply the Darkovski Remix; a 3:36 radio edit sits beside it.

## Musical Breakdown

No review, label note or interview describes how the Darkovski remix sounds or how it is built, so this breakdown stays with what the releases show and leaves the rest to your ears.

### The Long Version

At six and a half minutes the extended remix has nearly three minutes more than its radio edit: the space DJs use to mix in and out.

### The Voice

Golden is credited as featured artist and co-writer. How much of her vocal the remix keeps, no source says.

### The Setting

Kontor's Nassau Beach Club Ibiza 2014 and Sunset Chill 2014, La Nuit's Rare Lounge and Chilled House Grooves and Ambient Lounge 19 all carry this remix.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts. A remix competition opens that to anyone: the act releases the parts, producers send their versions, and the winners are released. Six of them made the 2014 release.

An {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is the long version made for clubs and DJs; the radio edit cuts it down for broadcast and playlists. Play both and hear what the shorter one leaves out.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is credited beside the main acts, often as the voice. Golden is the voice of {{ <track_link source="bambook-mennie-cari-golden-slip-away.md" /> }} too.

## Artistic Context

Groove Armada, Andy Cato and Tom Findlay, formed in London and active since 1996, are known for At the River, I See You Baby and {{ <track_link source="groove-armada-superstylin.md" /> }}. The original 12-inch credits both of them with Brodanse and Golden as writers; the Armada release names Findlay. Sweat later went into Groove Armada's own 2015 mix, Little Black Book. Of Darkovski, Discogs lists little beyond a 2013 release of his own, Tension/Uptown.

## Impact and Reception

The original came in a Club Mix and a Deep Mix; a record shop's listing of the 2014 follow-up, Advancing Man, says Sweat topped the Beatport charts and got Radio 1 airplay from Pete Tong. No review of the Darkovski remix has surfaced. Start with this long version, then the original, and listen for what the remixer kept.
