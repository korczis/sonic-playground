## Genre and Mood

**Moomin**'s **The Story About You** is deep house in a sombre mood: electronic music for the club that does not hurry anyone onto the floor. Resident Advisor called it the funereal title track of a pensive album, and the feelings it leaves are melancholic and introspective, held in place by the hypnotic patience of a track that takes seven minutes to tell its story.

## Song Summary

The track is the title piece of the debut album by Moomin, the name under which the German producer Sebastian Genz releases music. Its official upload credits Genz alone as composer and lyricist and dates the album to 31 October 2011 on Smallville. In Nick Connellan's review for Resident Advisor, the title track rests on dignified piano and guitar, with a mournful humming that often joins what he called the sober tale. It is an Original Mix, the producer's own full-length version, at a little over seven minutes.

### The Humming

A voice without words, mournful, joining the instruments rather than leading them; listen for how often it returns and what it leaves when it goes.

### The Long Form

Across the album the same review heard cyclical chords, dry percussion and simple melodic themes stretched over long stretches. Listen for how little changes, and how much that little matters.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady rhythmic floor of house music, a kick on every beat with the hi-hats and claps around it. Deep house keeps that floor but lowers the temperature, and here the interest moves up from the rhythm to the piano and guitar above it.

A {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} is a passage repeated without a seam. Resident Advisor heard Moomin's chords as cyclical and his themes as stretched out, which is what a loop does in house: the change comes from what is added or taken away, not from a new section. The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is where that happens. With few elements, every entrance counts: the humming arriving over the piano is an event in the way a chorus would be in a pop song. Compare how {{ <track_link source="stimming-the-song.md" /> }} builds a long track from very little.

## Artistic Context

Genz emerged as Moomin in late 2010, sharing the twelve-inch Hardmood/Joe McDaddy with Oskar Offermann, and three singles followed in 2011 before this album, Resident Advisor reported. The record store Oye Records, selling a 2022 repress of the double LP, wrote that he never intended to reinvent house music. The album's balance, in the review's words, sets small bursts of jubilance against periods of introspection.

## Impact and Reception

The album was repressed on double LP in 2022, more than ten years after its first release. No chart or award is recorded for the track. Give it the full seven minutes, ideally late, and listen for the moment the humming comes in.
