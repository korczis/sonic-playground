## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **He Needs Me - Unplugged** takes up a song she did not write, from a 1955 film about jazz musicians, in a session where acoustic folk and jazz meet. It is filed as intimate because of what an unplugged session is, a voice close to the listener, and romantic because it is a love song. We found no review of this take, and the page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

He Needs Me was written by Arthur Hamilton, the songwriter best known for Cry Me a River. It is one of three songs he contributed to Pete Kelly's Blues while under contract to Jack Webb, who directed and starred in the film. Peggy Lee sang it there, playing Rose Hopkins, an alcoholic jazz singer, a role that earned her an Academy Award nomination. Nina Simone recorded it in late 1957 for her debut album Little Girl Blue, released by Bethlehem in 1959. Zahra's take, produced by herself, runs 2:12.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or session note describes the performance part by part, so this breakdown stays with what is documented.

### The Session

The take comes from the Unplugged Session, six tracks recorded at Studio Ferber in Paris on 28 March 2011, according to the deluxe CD's notes. He Needs Me is the fifth, after Ahiawa, The Man I Love, Beautiful Tango and Oursoul.

### The Song in a Small Room

Simone's version is a trio: her voice and piano, Jimmy Bond on double bass, Albert "Tootie" Heath on drums. The deluxe notes do not list who plays with Zahra here. Listen for what an unplugged setting keeps of a song from a jazz film, and what the shorter length leaves out.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song first recorded by someone else, and He Needs Me has passed through three very different voices: a film singer in character, a jazz pianist at the start of her career, and a Moroccan-born songwriter in a Paris studio. Compare this take with Simone's on Little Girl Blue.

An {{ <concept_link name="unplugged" /> }} recording strips a song back to voice and acoustic instruments.

It is also a {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }}: absent from the 2010 album, it arrived on Handmade (Bonus Version) on 29 April 2011 and on Until the Next Journey in July. Hear the session's last song, {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-don-t-forget-unplugged.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Hindi Zahra was born in Khouribga, Morocco, has lived in France since 1993 and began her career in Paris as a soul backing singer. She produced, arranged and mixed Handmade herself; Blue Note released it in 2010. In 2014 she returned to Simone's repertoire, singing Just Say I Love Him on the tribute album Autour de Nina.

## Impact and Reception

Handmade won the Prix Constantin in November 2010 and the Victoire de la musique for world music album in February 2011, weeks before this session. Play Simone's version first, then this one, and listen to how little the song needs.
