## Genre and Mood

**Mike Posner**'s **Cooler Than Me - Single Mix**, the version credited with the producer **Gigamesh**, is electro-pop with a grudge. It is catchy and playful on the surface, and the lyric underneath is about a slight: wanting someone who acts as if they are above you. The tone sits between complaint and tease.

## Song Summary

Posner wrote and first recorded the song as a student, in his dorm room on what he called an extremely cheap microphone. He told Blues & Soul in 2010 that it was about a girl in his class he had a crush on, and about a type of girl found everywhere: the song captures that moment of feeling snubbed. The single mix is the version that became his debut single in April 2010 and opened the way to his album 31 Minutes to Takeoff.

### Main Section

DJBooth, writing in June 2010, described the single mix as quickly paced and synth heavy, with a semi-whispered vocal and a summery mood. The label's credits add a second voice: Eric Hölljes sings background vocals, and he is a co-writer with Posner.

## Concepts in Focus

The song grew through {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. In October 2009 a Gigamesh Remix appeared on Posner's mixtape One Foot Out the Door, with production largely identical to the final song but a different structure and no verses; an acoustic version with Big Sean appears on the mixtape A Matter of Time. The hit kept Gigamesh's sound and added the verses.

That makes the single mix an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} that became the main one, and it is why the credits matter. The release lists Gigamesh, the Minneapolis producer and DJ Matthew Masurka, as producer, programmer and remixer, with Serban Ghenea mixing and John Hanes engineering. DJBooth, by contrast, wrote that the single mix dropped Gigamesh's production and was Posner's own. This page follows the label's credits, which also match Wikipedia, and notes the disagreement, a reminder that the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} of a hit is not always obvious from outside.

## Artistic Context

Posner released the single on J Records on 16 April 2010; it was the lead single of 31 Minutes to Takeoff, released that August. The writing credits also include Sean Anderson (Big Sean) and Jeffrey Oh. The video, directed by Jason Beattie, follows Posner into a party at the Roosevelt Hotel on Hollywood Boulevard that comes to life, and changes, each time he puts on a different pair of sunglasses.

## Impact and Reception

Cooler Than Me reached number 6 on the Billboard Hot 100, number 5 in the UK and Canada, number 4 in Australia and number 3 in New Zealand, and it is certified six times platinum in the US. Posner's later single I Took a Pill in Ibiza looks back on it as a pop song people forgot, and in 2019 a deep house cover, {{ <track_link source="lucky-luke-cooler-than-me.md" /> }}, gave it a second life. Hear the single mix first, then the cover, and listen for what survives.
