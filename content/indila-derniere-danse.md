## Genre and Mood

**Indila**'s **Dernière danse** is French pop with one foot in chanson and the other in world music. Charts in France heard it as a blend of variété and world music carried by a warm voice. The mood is melancholic and intense, and the choirs behind it give it a haunting, almost sacred weight.

## Song Summary

The title means "last dance". It was Indila's first solo single, released in November 2013 by Capitol and AZ as the lead single of her only album, Mini World, which followed in February 2014. Indila wrote the words and melodies; Skalpovich, who produced the whole album, wrote the music with her. She told Charts in France that on this song there was nobody but herself, after years of singing hooks on other artists' records. Its memorable detail is the build: a crescendo melody that keeps climbing.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives times for Dernière danse, so this guide follows what its first reviewers heard.

### The Voice

The song stands or falls on Indila's voice. Listeners compared her to Najoua Belyzel, Marie Laforêt and Natacha Atlas; she answered that what unites such artists, Piaf among them, is emotion.

### The Melody

Charts in France called the melody crescendo and catchy, a tune that grows instead of circling.

### The Choirs

Behind it, the same review heard religious choirs holding the song up, a church-like backdrop for a pop single.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }} is the order of a song's chords. When Charts in France told Indila the song drew heavily on Charles Aznavour's Parce que tu crois, she said Skalp handled the production, that she would have to ask him whether it was homage or pure chance, and that the song rests on a progression that passes through time and renews itself. A 2023 article in L'Éclaireur Fnac went further and called it a sample of Aznavour's song; the credits name only Indila and Skalpovich.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, released in December 2013, shows Indila thrown out of her home and wandering Paris among indifferent passers-by while her mood raises a storm over the city; her voice finally calms it. In May 2023 it became the first video of a French-language song to pass a billion views on YouTube.

For the song's tenth anniversary in January 2024, Indila released a techno {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by the producer Bennett.

## Artistic Context

Born Adila Sedraïa, Indila first sang on records by French rappers such as Rohff, Soprano and Youssoupha; her hook on Youssoupha's Dreamin' made her name. Mini World was produced by Skalpovich and won the Victoire de la Musique for album revelation of the year in 2015.

## Impact and Reception

Dernière danse reached number two in France and in Wallonia and number one in Greece, Israel, Romania and Turkey; in Greece it spent thirteen weeks at the top of Billboard's digital chart. Its life online has been longer still, much like {{ <track_link source="jain-makeba.md" /> }}. Watch the video once, then listen again with your eyes closed.
