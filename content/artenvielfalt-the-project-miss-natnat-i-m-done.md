## Genre and Mood

**Artenvielfalt**, **The Project** and **Miss NatNat** made **I'm Done** as a song, not a club tool: Discogs files the release under deep house and house, but the version here is three minutes thirty-nine, with the long mixes left to the rest of the EP. This page files it as groovy and relaxed, the register of a short vocal deep house track rather than of a peak-time one.

## Song Summary

I'm Done is the first of four tracks on an EP that the French label Délicieuse Records lists in September 2015. The credits that come with the recording name Lennart Vonau of Artenvielfalt as producer, arranger, engineer and bass player, Jörg Müller-Lornsen as singer, and Bartosz Strzelecki and Günter Haas on guitar. Müller-Lornsen, Strzelecki and Vonau wrote the music; Müller-Lornsen and Natalie Katharina Strenge, the Miss NatNat of the title, wrote the words.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes I'm Done part by part, so this review gives the frame rather than a map. It is a short vocal version with live guitars and bass in the credits, released alongside a Club Mix of 6:15 and two remixes of over six minutes each. Listen for what the short version keeps: the voices and the song.

## Concepts in Focus

Miss NatNat is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, credited on the title next to the two acts and in the credits as a co-writer of the lyric. The credits also name a second singer, so I'm Done carries more than one voice.

The EP shows the difference between an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} and a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. The Club Mix is credited to the same artists and is simply longer, made for DJs; the remixes by Marlon Hoffstadt with HRRSN and by Björn Störig are other producers' readings of the song. For another vocal deep house record on the same label, hear {{ <track_link source="parra-for-cuva-anna-naklab-fading-nights.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Artenvielfalt is a German producer duo of Benjamin Sachau and Lennart Vonau, whose other releases came out on labels such as Ostfunk and No Brainer. Délicieuse, which put out I'm Done, is a French label whose YouTube channel Délicieuse Musique carried the original mix as early as September 2013, two years before the date Discogs and MusicBrainz give the EP.

## Impact and Reception

The track travelled through DJ mixes first. Discogs lists I'm Done, credited to The Project, on five compilations of 2014, among them Nassau Beach Club Ibiza 2014 on Kontor and Club Privè Formentera on Sony Music, and MusicBrainz shows the Marlon Hoffstadt and HRRSN remix on the Nassau Beach Club mix that April. Start with this short version, then try the Club Mix.
