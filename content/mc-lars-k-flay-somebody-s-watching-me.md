## Artistic Context

MC Lars, Andrew Nielsen, born in 1982, studied English at Stanford and Shakespeare at Oxford, and is often called a pioneer of lit-hop and a key figure of early nerdcore. Horris Records began in 2004 as an imprint and later became self-distributed. K.Flay, born Kristine Flaherty in 1985, also started making music at Stanford. Single and Famous was their first collaboration; three years later she signed to RCA, and in 2016 she became the first artist on Night Street Records at Interscope.
