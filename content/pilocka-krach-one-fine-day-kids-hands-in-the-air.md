## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** is a Berlin techno artist, but **Hands in the Air**, made with the **One Fine Day Kids**, was filed elsewhere: Discogs lists the 7-inch under pop and African music, Bandcamp under world. No review describes how it sounds, so this page does not guess at its tempo or its beat. Joyful and uplifting are a reading of how and why it was made, a song written with children for their own arts club, not a description of the mix.

## Song Summary

In February 2018 Pilocka Krach travelled to Nairobi to write a song with the One Fine Day music club. Political unrest in Kibera, the settlement Bandcamp calls the largest slum in East Africa, cut the planned six days to three. In a dusty school classroom the children worked on their own lyrics and rehearsed the choir parts, and on the fourth day they recorded in a studio. Pilocka Krach is credited as writer and producer, the children as featured artists.

## Concepts in Focus

Hands in the Air is a {{ <concept_link name="charity record" /> }}: all proceeds of the single go to One Fine Day's work in Nairobi, where sales and benefit concerts help pay for weekly arts clubs. The 7-inch was pressed in an edition of 100 and sold only through Bandcamp, a release sized for its cause rather than for the charts.

The credit "feat. One Fine Day Kids" uses the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} convention of pop and hip-hop, usually reserved for a star guest. Here it names a choir of children who wrote their own lines, and puts them in the release's title beside the producer.

## Artistic Context

One Fine Day is a charity founded in 2008 by Marie Steinmann and Tom Tykwer; with the British partner Anno's Africa it runs workshops in dance, ballet, theatre, music, acrobatics, creative writing and fine arts at schools in Nairobi's slums. The music project began in 2015, when the South African singer Dear Reader, Cherilyn MacNeil, made Smoke Machine with children there. Hands in the Air was the second song, released with it on 1 June 2018. Pilocka Krach, born in Berlin-Köpenick, runs her own label, Greatest Hits International, named on the 7-inch beside City Slang; hear her own records on {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-my-name-is-fame.md" /> }}.
