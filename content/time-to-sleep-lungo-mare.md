## Genre and Mood

**Time To Sleep** made **Lungo Mare** for the dancefloor, but not for a polished one. It sits between indie dance and electronic dance music, and the one detailed account of it hears a groove with grit: a funky bass line, a bleeping synth and a glitch left in on purpose. The warmth is in the title, a walk by the sea at sundown.

## Song Summary

The track takes its name from what its premiere called one of Croatia's most beautiful coastal promenades, a path of twelve kilometres with sundowners and views over the Adriatic Sea. The bass line is funky, almost rock-ish; a bleeping lead synth rides over it; and a glitch adds, in the premiere's words, even more rawness and edge. What makes it memorable is the contradiction the same writer heard: roughness with beauty shining through.

The bass is the engine: funky and almost rock-ish at once.

## Concepts in Focus

Lungo Mare is a clear case of a {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} that carries a track's identity. Here the premiere singles it out first, funky and almost rock-ish, and that edge sets the mood. Compare the disco bass of {{ <track_link source="poni-hoax-olga-kouklaki-budapest.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} plays the opposite role: a bleeping lead, light and electronic, against the physical push of the bass. The glitch is an error kept because it adds texture.

The single also exists as an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} of 7:24 on Disco Halal, almost twice the length of the 4:08 version reviewed here. Longer versions are made for DJs, who need room to mix in and out.

## Artistic Context

Time To Sleep is a producer from Pula, on the Croatian coast, and Lungo Mare was his debut on the Disco Halal Singles Club, released on 1 May 2020. Disco Halal is the label of Moscoman, a Tel Aviv DJ and producer now based in Berlin; in September 2019 he launched the Singles Club as a monthly digital series meant to shine a light on new music from young, unknown artists. Torture the Artist premiered the track the day before release.

## Impact and Reception

No chart run or wider critical response to Lungo Mare is documented; its public life began with that premiere and a place in a label series built for newcomers. Put it on near the water if you can, and listen for the moment the glitch breaks the calm.
