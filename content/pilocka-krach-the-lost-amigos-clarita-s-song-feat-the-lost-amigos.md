## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** and **The Lost Amigos** put out **Clarita's Song** as electronic music that refuses a single label: its Bandcamp page tags it electronic, elektro, hip hop, house, punk, techno and world all at once. The feelings here are playful and quirky, the second taken from the one listener's description on record, a Bandcamp supporter who called it oddball German electronic funk.

## Song Summary

Few sources describe the song itself. Its credits are clearer: the music is by Pilocka Krach, the lyrics by Clara Ruiz Almanza and Sun Ru Carter, and the official video names the music as Pilocka Krach featuring Sun Ru and Clarita, the name in the title. It came out on 10 February 2017 as a single on her label Greatest Hits International, with a short piece called Zammmmmma and a DJ Zündstoff Remix beside it, and as a 7-inch of 100 individually screenprinted, numbered copies. No source sets out the lyric, so this page does not paraphrase it.

## Concepts in Focus

The credit line is an odd case of the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}. A feature normally brings in a guest, but her label's text says Pilocka Krach is Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos herself, her band and all at once. Here the featured name is a second identity, while the guests, Sun Ru and Clarita, appear only in the video's credits and the lyric credits.

The song also has a place in a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }}. In April 2017 it reappeared as track ten of Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, a double album that Bandcamp describes as conceptual and framed, a diary of two years of touring between Mexico, Detroit and Berlin. Clarita's Song opens the Lost Amigos half; streaming services carry that half as its own eight-track album, The Lost Amigos.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, published on her channel in February 2017, was directed by Alfonso Garrido and Andrea Granja de Alcatraces with Pilocka Krach as co-director, produced by Gorila Films and One Day Production, and supported by Initiative Musik with federal culture funds.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick, grew up in Berlin-Mitte and releases her music on her own label. In 2016 a Musicboard Berlin grant took her to Detroit, where she connected with Underground Resistance. By the album's credits, the Lost Amigos tracks were written and produced in Tulum, in Guadalajara, at Submerge in Detroit and in Berlin-Marienfelde in 2015 and 2016.
