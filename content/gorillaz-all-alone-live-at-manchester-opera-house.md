## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz** played All Alone at the Manchester Opera House in November 2005, and **All Alone (Live at Manchester Opera House)** keeps the song's two moods: the gritty hip-hop of Roots Manuva's rap and the ethereal turn of Martina Topley-Bird's singing. On stage both guests stood in front of a full band and a string section.

## Song Summary

The concerts were built to recreate Demon Days, the album released that May, and All Alone came tenth, as it does on the record. Both of its studio guests came to Manchester: Roots Manuva is credited with the guest rap, Martina Topley-Bird with the vocals. John Murphy of musicOMH, reviewing the shows, heard the two duetting on a sumptuous All Alone. The recording runs three minutes and fifty-two seconds.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes this performance in detail or gives times, so these notes rest on the credits and on one review of the shows.

### The Rap

Roots Manuva takes the verse he co-wrote and recorded for the album, where PopMatters heard a dirty, skittery hip-hop track; how his delivery changed in the hall, no source says.

### The Duet

Martina Topley-Bird joins him, and the song turns towards her voice, the part musicOMH's word sumptuous seems to describe.

### The Band

Behind the guests played a band led by Damon Albarn on vocals, piano and melodica, with Mike Smith as musical director, guitars, bass, drums, percussion, a turntablist, backing singers and strings; musicOMH counted a fourteen-piece string section.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} captures a performance rather than a studio construction, and this one comes from five nights, 1 to 5 November 2005, compiled for the film. Gorillaz had kept themselves hidden: the reviewer noted that on earlier occasions the band had played behind a screen, and here Albarn sat at his piano in silhouette under a constantly changing video montage. On the audio release, that staging is gone and only the sound remains, which the same reviewer called terrific.

On a record a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a credit; in Manchester the guests were the show: guest artists performed under spotlights while the band stayed in silhouette, so the people you hear are also the only ones you would have seen clearly. Compare the studio version, {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-martina-topley-bird-roots-manuva-all-alone.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

The Manchester shows were a commission ahead of the first Manchester International Festival, which took place in 2007. The film, directed by David Barnard and Grant Gee, came out on DVD from Parlophone on 27 March 2006, and an audio-only version followed in 2011. The same November, Gorillaz put a live All Alone on the Dirty Harry single; MusicBrainz lists it as a separate recording, and no source I found says where it was made.

## Impact and Reception

The DVD reached number three on the UK music video chart, went gold in the UK and was nominated for the Grammy Award for Best Music Film. All Alone was never a single in any form. Listen to it after the studio version and hear what a room full of strings and a live crowd do to it.
