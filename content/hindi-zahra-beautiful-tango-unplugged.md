## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **Beautiful Tango - Unplugged** takes the song that opened her debut album and sets it in an acoustic session, where world music and folk meet with little between voice and listener. It is filed here as intimate and warm because of what an unplugged take is and what the song already was; we found no review of this particular recording, and this page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

Beautiful Tango is Hindi Zahra's best-known song. It came out as a single in 2009 and opened Handmade in 2010, where the studio version runs 3:56 with guitars by Zahra and Romain Vaugeois and percussion by Zahra and Thierry Moutoussamy. The streaming metadata credits the composition to Zahra and Vaugeois and the words to Zahra, who also produced it. She told the Khaleej Times that its rhythm comes from Berber handclapping, and accepted the magazine Wire's comparison of the song to a cross between Django Reinhardt and Billie Holiday. This unplugged take is a separate recording, made two years later.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or session note describes the performance part by part, so this breakdown stays with what is documented.

### The Session

The take comes from the Unplugged Session, six tracks recorded at Studio Ferber in Paris on 28 March 2011 according to the deluxe CD's notes. Beautiful Tango is the third of them, after Ahiawa and The Man I Love.

### The Length

It runs about 4:25, nearly half a minute longer than the studio original. Listen for where that time goes: a slower pace, longer phrases, or more room between lines.

### The Voice

With the studio arrangement gone, the voice carries the song. Compare its phrasing with the version you know.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="unplugged" /> }} recording strips a song back to acoustic instruments and voice. Colin Irwin of the BBC, reviewing Handmade in 2010, found its production overly sophisticated and suspiciously smooth, and wrote that Zahra sounds really special when she bares her teeth; a session like this is one answer to that kind of criticism.

It is also an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} of a song with a fixed public image: Songlines' Nige Tassell noted that Beautiful Tango had been used in a television advertising campaign, whose income gave her time to make the album.

And it is a {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }}: absent from the 2010 album, it arrived on Handmade (Bonus Version) on 29 April 2011 and on Until the Next Journey that July. Hear the session's opening song too, {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-ahiawa-unplugged.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Hindi Zahra was born in Khouribga, Morocco, into a family of Chleuh Berber artists from the Souss, and began her career in Paris as a soul backing singer. She produced, arranged and mixed Handmade herself.

## Impact and Reception

Handmade won the Prix Constantin in November 2010 and the Victoire de la musique for world music album in February 2011, weeks before this session. Play {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-beautiful-tango-remastered-version.md" /> }} first, then this one.
