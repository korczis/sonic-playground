## Genre and Mood

**Toploader**'s **Dancing in the Moonlight** is a cover played as pop rock with a soul organ in it, carefree and feel-good by design. The Musical Hype hears a warm, vintage sound and a sing-along chorus; the song's own writer imagined a peaceful and joyful celebration of life.

## Song Summary

Sherman Kelly wrote the song in 1969 while recovering from an assault by a St. Croix gang, picturing an alternate reality. His band Boffalongo recorded it first, in 1970, and King Harvest made it a hit: number 13 in the US in early 1973. Toploader, a rock band from Eastbourne, recorded their version in 1999, with Joseph Washbourn singing lead.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the recording; the notes below follow The Musical Hype's description.

### Intro

The track opens on the song's signature instrumental introduction, different from King Harvest's lush intro but with the same effect.

### Main Section

Mallet percussion, exuberant keyboards and an organ give the arrangement its warmth, over a soulful groove and a robust bass line. Where King Harvest had a guitar solo, Toploader lean on keys. Washbourn's vocal is expressive without overdoing it, with melodic tweaks and ad-libs of his own.

The sing-along chorus is the section The Musical Hype calls the one to beat.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} can stay faithful or change a song's sound, and this one keeps the tune and mood while swapping King Harvest's guitar for keyboards and organ. Four recordings are worth knowing: Boffalongo's original, King Harvest's hit, Toploader's cover and, closing the 2009 Best Of, the band's own acoustic version.

That acoustic take is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same band, the same song, a different arrangement, a way to hear how much of the cover's lift comes from its production.

The song's second life is a case of {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. Sainsbury's used it in television adverts, and it appears in A Walk to Remember (2002), Four Lions (2010) and The Umbrella Academy (2019).

## Artistic Context

Toploader formed in 1997 and signed to S2, a Sony Music subsidiary. Dancing in the Moonlight, released on 21 February 2000, was the third single from their debut album Onka's Big Moka, produced by Dave Eringa and released in May 2000; the album reached number four in the UK. The band split in 2003 and re-formed in 2009.

## Impact and Reception

The single first peaked at number 19. Re-released with added production from Stargate after heavy use in a Sainsbury's campaign, it reached number seven in the UK and in Ireland, and the BPI has since certified it multi-platinum. In 2017 Vice ran an essay titled Toploader's Dancing in the Moonlight Saved My Life, Sort Of, and in 2023 the band played it on Ant & Dec's Saturday Night Takeaway. Turn it up at a summer party.
