## Genre and Mood

**NTEIBINT** and **Σtella** made **The Owner** as electronic pop with disco in its bones: Blackplastic called it a steady-paced electronic track with disco flourishes, and Eskimo Recordings an electrifying slice of electronic pop. The feelings here are romantic and relaxed, because the review heard an easygoing love song rather than a peak-time tune.

## Song Summary

The Owner is a love song of just under four minutes, sung by Σtella over NTEIBINT's production. What makes it memorable, in the only review that walks through it, is the chorus: vocal hooks and muted guitars that really hold the track together. The label praised her angelic voice, the review her gorgeous vocals. The single came out on Eskimo in June 2015 with a beatless version, an extended mix and remixes by Anoraak and Justin Faust.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the phrase a listener carries away. In The Owner the review credits two of them working together, a vocal line and a muted guitar figure, so the chorus is hooked twice: once in the voice and once in the rhythm of the guitars.

The release also shows how one song multiplies. An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} comes from the song's own makers: here a beatless version, which takes the drums away and leaves the song itself, and an extended mix for DJs. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is another producer's reading. Justin Faust added what the review called a chunky elastic bassline and pushed the guitars further forward; Anoraak, the review's favourite, layered effects and strings into a disco feel it compared to Everything But The Girl around Temperamental.

## Artistic Context

NTEIBINT is the Greek DJ and producer George Bakalakos, part of the roster of Eskimo Recordings, the Ghent label founded in 2000 and known for nu disco and cosmic disco. Σtella is Stella Chronopoulou, a singer from Athens who later released albums on Arbutus Records and Sub Pop. The two had already made Never Without You for Eskimo in 2014, and in 2018 they returned with A State Nearby, which they called a sci-fi disco ballad.

## Impact and Reception

The label called Never Without You highly successful, and in 2018 Do You Like That Song? still named The Owner among the pair's excellent songs. For a parallel in quietness, try {{ <track_link source="the-xx-crystalised.md" /> }}. Then play The Owner and wait for the chorus, where the voice and the damped guitars meet.
