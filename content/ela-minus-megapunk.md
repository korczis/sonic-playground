## Genre and Mood

**Ela Minus** made **megapunk** as electronic music with the bones of synth-pop and the attitude its title promises, defiant and intense. Stereogum heard a greyscale electronic track built on a throbbing, insistent pulse, with Minus singing in a flattened, spectral hush; Paste heard a brooding-then-explosive middle ground between New Order and Aphex Twin. The punk is in the stance, not in guitars.

## Song Summary

Released on Domino on 30 July 2020, megapunk was the second single from her debut album acts of rebellion, after they told us it was hard, but they were wrong. As Stereogum summarised it, the verses voice an inability to stay silent and a fear of running out of time to defend rights, and the chorus is a repeated refusal to be stopped. Minus wrote it in 2019 and worried that a late release would strip it of its context; in her statement she said she could not have been more wrong, ending with the words "ánimo y fuerza", courage and strength.

The pulse and the voice carry everything. Stereogum heard the track as more direct and urgent than its predecessor; Paste called it the album's most thrashing entry, brooding and then explosive. Listen for where the brooding turns, and how the hushed voice holds steady while the electronics grow.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="protest song" /> }} usually raises its voice. Megapunk keeps its voice low and lets the pulse do the pushing. Paste, writing two weeks before the 2020 US election, heard an uncompromising reminder to rise up even under threats of violence and jail, and called the song something of a thesis statement for the album.

The album was made entirely on analog gear in her Brooklyn home studio, according to Wikipedia, and Paste stresses that no computers are involved, only hardware synths. An {{ <concept_link name="analog synthesizer" /> }} shapes sound with circuits played in real time, which suits Minus, who worked building synthesizers at Critter & Guitari in New York after studying at Berklee.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} is credited to DR.ME and Ela Minus, and its description opens with the same words: ánimo y fuerza.

## Artistic Context

Gabriela Jimeno, born in Bogotá in 1990, drummed in the Colombian hardcore band Ratón Pérez from the age of 11 to 18, then studied jazz drumming and music synthesis at Berklee. In 2019 she became the first Latin American artist signed to Domino. Acts of rebellion followed on 23 October 2020; later she toured North America with the producer behind {{ <track_link source="caribou-odessa.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The album was widely praised and playlisted by the BBC; Paste called megapunk the record's apex, and in September 2021 she played it at the Pitchfork Music Festival. Turn it up and listen to how quietly it refuses.
