## Genre and Mood

**Faul & Wad** and **PNAU** meet on **Changes**, a house track with a deep, soulful glow and a big pop heart. The mood is uplifting and nostalgic, with a euphoric lift: dance music about growing up that sounds like the last warm night of summer.

## Song Summary

Changes is built around the refrain of Pnau's song Baby, sung by a children's choir, which the French duo turn into the emotional centre of a smooth house groove. Around that voice they place a warm bassline, a steady four-on-the-floor kick and a mellow saxophone line that became the track's signature. The combination of innocence and dancefloor polish is what makes it stick: it is bittersweet and joyful at the same time.

## Musical Breakdown

The original mix runs close to six minutes, giving the groove time to settle before the hook takes over.

### Intro

A soft kick, crisp percussion and warm chords open the track, establishing a relaxed, sunlit tempo.

### Build-up

The bassline enters and the groove fills out, with hints of the sampled vocal floating in and out of the mix.

### Main Section

The choir's refrain arrives in full over the beat, the children's voices giving the track its tender, nostalgic character.

### Chorus

The saxophone takes the lead, a smooth, catchy melodic line answering the vocal and giving the song its most memorable moment.

### Bridge

The beat drops back and the vocal and chords breathe on their own, a moment of calm before the groove returns.

### Climax

The kick, bass, sax and choir come together at full strength, the track at its most euphoric.

### Outro

The layers ease away gradually, leaving the rhythm and a last echo of the refrain.

## Artistic Context

Faul & Wad Ad are a French production duo, and Changes, credited to Faul & Wad Ad vs. Pnau, first appeared online in 2013 before its official release that November. It draws on Baby, a song from Pnau's self-titled album; Pnau, the Australian duo of Nick Littlemore and Peter Mayes, are also known for their collaborations with Elton John, and Littlemore is one half of Empire of the Sun.

## Impact and Reception

Changes became a major European hit, reaching number one in Germany and Belgium and climbing into the UK top three. It remains one of the defining deep house crossover records of its moment. It is on this playground for its rare balance of tenderness and groove. Turn it up on a summer evening and let it take you back.
