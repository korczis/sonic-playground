## Genre and Mood

**Bou** and **Inja**'s **Cous Cous** is drum and bass with a grin. UKF's Dave Jenkins announced it as "a joyously bumpy bassline muck-about" from Bou, with Inja on vocal duties, and that is where the two feelings on this page come from: playful in its spirit, energetic in the way the genre always is.

## Song Summary

Cous Cous is a club track that had a life before its release. Bou told UKF he had held it for at least a year and a half and that it was one of the tunes that "really kicked off in the raves before lockdown". Inja, he said, "laid out this sick vocal on it and it fit perfectly", and "really brought the project together". It came out on 7 May 2021 as the first release on Gossip, Bou's own label, launched with a livestream and a video premiere; the label called it a "rave ready banger" when it posted the video.

## Concepts in Focus

In drum and bass the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is half the genre's name, and here it is the joke and the hook at once. Wikipedia describes drum and bass by its heavy bass and sub-bass lines; UKF's word for this one, bumpy, points to a bass that bounces rather than rumbles.

Under it runs the {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }}: drum and bass is built on fast breakbeats, typically between 165 and 185 beats per minute, and from its beginnings DJs have played with MCs beside them, drawing on hip-hop and ragga. Inja's vocal belongs to that tradition.

Bou is his own {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}, and here also his own label. He told UKF that Gossip was "a very personal label", "a place for my special tunes", not "a throwaway label"; Beatportal reported a year later that three of its first four releases had reached the top of Beatport's chart.

## Artistic Context

Bou, born Mohamed Amine Bouguenna in Algeria, grew up in France, Kent and finally Manchester, where he found his place through the Bloc2Bloc station. He was 24 when Cous Cous came out and had just won Best Producer at the Drum and Bass Awards for the second year running. Inja is a drum and bass MC turned DJ and producer, who signed to Hospital Records and whom Mixmag credits with "unrivalled lyricism, positive energy, and a trademark charisma". Hear Bou on his own in {{ <track_link source="bou-all-u-gotta-do.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No chart placing or full review is recorded for Cous Cous itself; its proof is the dance floor it was tested on and the label it opened. Two years later Bou's Closer and Baddadan were in the UK charts. Play it loud and follow the bass.
