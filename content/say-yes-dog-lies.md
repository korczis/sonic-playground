## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog**'s "**Lies**" is synth-pop with a dance pulse, and on the trio's second album it is the groovy, upbeat one: Cool Hunting called it an upbeat and satisfying electro-pop tune with an unwavering groove.

## Song Summary

"Lies" was the second single from Voyage, the band's second album, released with its video in April 2019. The reviews of Voyage agree on its sound: this time the band gave itself over fully to synthesizers and sank deep into the electro-pop of the 1980s. Within that sound, Bedroomdisco heard Lies as the track that relies more on the bass and sounds funkier than the rest. What the lyric says about lies, none of the sources used here discusses, so this review leaves it to the listener.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes this song section by section, so the breakdown follows the parts the reviewers do name.

### The Bass

Paul Rundel's bass is what sets the song apart. On an album Bedroomdisco described as clear synth-pop with funky bass and minimal drums, Lies is where the bass comes furthest forward.

### Synths and Drums

soundmag.de named Lies, with Sugar, as an example of the record's nostalgic aesthetic: 80s-style synthesizers and synthetic-sounding drums. Around them, the same review noted, the band still plays many analogue instruments.

### The Video

The video, directed by Stephen Korytko, follows a man in a parachute tracksuit and headphones dancing at the back of a concert crowd.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} palette can define a whole album, and on Voyage it does: the reviews describe one consistent sound of 80s synths, which Bedroomdisco found turning monotonous over the full length.

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} can give one song its own identity inside a shared sound. ## Artistic Context

**Say Yes Dog** is a German-Luxembourgish electro-pop trio: Aaron Ahrends sings and plays keyboards, Paul Rundel plays bass and Pascal Karier drums. They met studying sound engineering and jazz at the conservatory in The Hague. Voyage followed their 2015 debut Plastic Love four years later and came out on 10 May 2019 on Diskodogs Records; its title, Bedroomdisco wrote, comes from the band's travels in Japan, Vietnam and South Korea. Hear where they started with {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog_a-friend.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Voyage drew mixed reviews, three out of five from Bedroomdisco, and soundmag.de heard clever electro-pop with both warmth and coolness, comparing it to Metronomy and Hot Chip. When critics picked out songs, Lies was among the ones they named. It is here for that bass: play it next to {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-deep-space.md" /> }}, the album's opener, and hear how much one part can change a band's colour.
