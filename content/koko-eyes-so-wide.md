## Genre and Mood

**KOKO**'s **Eyes So Wide** is alt-pop from Bristol, somewhere between indie pop and electro-pop, the kind Notion called subversive. It is filed as sultry and confident: sultry because the band describe it as a song about lust, confident because the one review that describes its sound called it swaggering.

## Song Summary

The band explained the song in a statement with its release: it is about lusting after someone from the moment you meet them and wanting to drop your inhibitions with them, in a mood that swings between highs and lows. They pictured it being played at 4am in a field. Indie Is Not A Genre heard smooth vocals over a pumping bass line, capturing the moment between the party and the after-party comedown. The Topic release credits five writers: Oliver Garland, Harry Dobson, Daniel Ashley Clark and the producers Charlie Russell and Couros Sheibani.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} can set a song's attitude before the singer does. Here the reviewer's two words, pumping for the bass line and swaggering for the whole song, point to where the attitude comes from.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} is part of the writing team on this record. Russell is credited with production, mixing and programming, Sheibani with production and programming, and both as composers, while John Greenham mastered the single.

Three weeks after the single, on 31 January 2020, KOKO released Eyes So Wide A.M., an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} running 3:24 against 2:38, mixed by Russell and mastered by Tim Debney. ## Artistic Context

KOKO are Garland on vocals, Dobson on bass and synth and Ashley C on synths and lead guitar. They met at a party in Bristol in 2017 and began jamming in Garland's father's garage. Eyes So Wide followed their debut single Freak, released in November 2019, and came out on 10 January 2020 on OKOK Recordings. Their debut EP Follow followed in March 2020, and NBHAP heard in them a mixture of Billie Eilish, British indie and hyperactive electro.

## Impact and Reception

Eyes So Wide was a new band's second single, and its reception is in the blogs that introduced KOKO in January 2020: Gems & Secrets called their sound unique, exciting and undeniably infectious. Put it on late, and listen to the bass.
