## Song Summary

Rain in the Desert is the first track on the B-side of Joy, a three-track twelve-inch that URSL released at the end of August 2020 with a mini-poster. The title track plays at 45 rpm on side A; Rain in the Desert and Ice Cream on Ice share side B at 33⅓ rpm. It runs 5:03 and is credited to Pilocka Krach featuring Roderic. The label's text for the record reads like a travel diary: a set of map coordinates, a sunny breeze, empty streets, a climb up the holy mountain of joy, rock bell sounds and a stream of sounds hitting mountains, coming out laughing like children. It describes the whole record, and no source ties a line of it to this track.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick, grew up in Berlin-Mitte and runs her own label, Greatest Hits International; her publisher calls her the electric rock star from Berlin. URSL, which describes itself as a platform for electronic music founded in 2011 to connect Hamburg and Berlin, released Joy under the catalogue number URSL030. Roderic had put out his albums Perfect Mirror and It All Depends on Katermukke, the Berlin label that also released her Ay Caramba; compare {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-the-lost-amigos-ay-caramba.md" /> }}.
