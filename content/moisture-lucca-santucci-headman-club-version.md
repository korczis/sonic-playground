> The **Headman Club Version** of **Moisture**, Headman's song with the singer **Luca Santucci**, is a long, entrancing dance mix: steady rhythm, synthesizers and a soulful vocal stretched out for late-night floors.

Driven by rhythmic precision and lush textures, the club version gives the song's groove room to breathe. Deep basslines and synthesizer lines frame Santucci's vocal, and the extended form makes the track as useful to a DJ as it is pleasant to listen to.

## Genre and Mood

This version of "Moisture" sits between **deep house**, nu-disco and **electronic** club music, blending a **soulful** vocal with a **hypnotic** beat. It is smooth and steady, made to keep a floor moving rather than to explode.

## About the Artists

**Headman** is the alias of Robi Insinna, a producer, DJ and designer from the Zurich electronic scene who founded the label Relish Records. His music blends house, pop, disco and rock and is associated with the nu-disco movement; his debut *It Rough* (2003) and *Catch Me* (2008) came out on the Munich label Gomma, and he has remixed Franz Ferdinand, The Gossip, Roxy Music, Zombie Nation and Tiga. **Luca Santucci** sings on Moisture and wrote its lyrics; Insinna composed, arranged and produced it.

## Song Summary

The track opens with a subtle, driving beat and atmospheric synthesizers. When Santucci's voice comes in, the production builds around it, and the long form lets each part arrive in its own time. *ON* first came out in 2006, with collaborators from Soulwax and The Rapture; on it the song runs about three minutes. This club version, released on 30 September 2016 as a bonus track on the album's deluxe edition, runs 5:21.

## Sound Breakdown

### Intro: Building the Atmosphere

The track starts with minimal percussion and a deep, rolling bassline that builds anticipation, an opening built for mixing.

### First Verse: Soulful Vocals

Santucci's vocal glides over the beat, warm and unhurried.

### Chorus: Hypnotic and Dynamic

Layered synthesizers and a deeper bass take centre stage, turning the song into a hypnotic dance-floor groove.

### Bridge: Space to Breathe

The beat strips back so the voice can come forward, a contrast that prepares the return of the groove.

### Final Chorus: Full Energy Unleashed

The full arrangement returns, bass and synthesizer patterns together, and the track rides its groove to the end.

## Concepts in Focus

This is an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} in the classic club sense: the same song, lengthened from about three minutes on the album to over five, with a beat-led intro and more room for the instrumental groove. Those extra minutes are what a DJ needs to blend one record into the next.

Because Headman made it himself, it is also an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} rather than an outside remix: the producer re-reading his own song for a different setting. In an interview he described his remix aim as finding what is danceable and highlighting it, and that is exactly what this version does with Moisture.

What holds the five minutes together is the {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}: bass, drums and synthesizers locked into a steady, unhurried pattern that keeps moving under the voice. For another disco-rooted house track built around a soulful lead, compare {{ <track_link source="the-supermen-lovers-moments.md" /> }}.

## Overall Vibe

"Moisture (Headman Club Version)" is **hypnotic**, **soulful** and **dynamic**, a late-night track for dancing or for headphones.

## Conclusion

With this club version, Headman shows how a producer can stretch his own three-minute song into a dance-floor piece without losing its voice. It is a good entry point into the nu-disco sound of Relish and Gomma.
