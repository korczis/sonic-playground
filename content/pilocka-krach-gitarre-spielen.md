## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**'s **Gitarre Spielen** is techno and electronic music that makes fun of rock's most earnest gesture. The feelings are playful, ironic and humorous: a refrain that wishes it could play guitar like Bob Dylan, set to a machine beat, and then, against all expectation, a guitar solo.

## Song Summary

Gitarre Spielen means "playing guitar". Pilocka Krach wrote and produced it; the vocal is by Max Spohn and the guitar by Felix Zoepf. Monika Enterprise's press text, written by Daniel Meteo, hears the album's pop codes reach a first summit here, in the refrain's delusionally amusing wish to play like Dylan, the man with a guitar in every photo who stood for the singer-songwriter as the voice of a generation. Underneath, the text describes a keyboard melody in the manner of Popcorn, a clear techno beat full of electronic cymbals, nothing naturalistic, and finally a real guitar solo.

### Main Section

The refrain carries the track: the Dylan wish, which HHV Mag's Kristoffer Cornils heard as a spoken sample. Under it run the techno beat with its electronic cymbals and the Popcorn-style keyboard line.

### Guitar Solo

Then the joke turns real: "Oh, yeah, then there actually is a guitar solo!!!", as the label's text puts it.

## Concepts in Focus

The refrain is the {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}, and a sly one. A hook is the idea that sticks, and here what sticks is a confession of ambition: a techno track that would rather be a folk singer. It works because it is repeated and because it is slightly absurd.

The {{ <concept_link name="guitar solo" /> }} is the punchline. In rock it is the moment of virtuosity; in a techno track by a producer who mocks authorly gestures it becomes a quotation, answering the refrain's wish.

Best Of is a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} disguised as a debut: eleven tracks from 2009 to 2014, several first released elsewhere. Gitarre Spielen first appeared in 2009 on her EP Delusions of Grandeur on Save to Disc Recordings, with the same guitarist and singer credited. Compare {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-my-name-is-fame.md" /> }}, another track the album collected.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach is a Berlin producer and performer who, the label says, emerged from Vienna's noise avant-garde and then Berlin's techno-hippie post-rave scene, with Bar25, Fusion Festival and Nation of Gondwana among her first stages. She has released tracks since 2007, on Katermukke and her own Greatest Hits International among others. Best Of came out on 22 May 2015 on Monika Enterprise, with sleeve design by The Designers Republic.

## Impact and Reception

Cornils found the track rather more humorous than intended, while granting that Krach is really trying to deconstruct the beer-serious earnestness of Berlin's scene. Groove's Christin Bolte enjoyed hearing her live hits gathered on one album; DMC World's Ben Hogwood gave it four out of five and warned not to expect a straight face. Listen for the solo, and decide whether the wish came true.
