## Genre and Mood

**RAC** and **K.Flay**'s **Heartbreak Summer** sits on EGO, an album Spill Magazine hears as upbeat, synth-driven pop with strong melodies and a summer sound. Synth-pop and electro-pop, then, but with a catch the same review names: the record often pairs heartbreaking lyrics with bright, happy melodies. The title says it in two words, and the feelings follow from it, bittersweet and melancholic.

## Song Summary

Heartbreak Summer is a 3:52 song from EGO, RAC's album of 14 July 2017 on Counter Records. Apple Music credits it to André Allen Anjos, the man behind RAC, and Kristine Flaherty, K.Flay's real name, as composers; Anjos produced it, Andrew Maury mixed it and Joe LaPorta mastered it. K.Flay sings it as the featured artist. No source we found discusses the lyric beyond its title, so this page does not either.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found walks through the track part by part. Two reviewers each give it a sentence, and this breakdown is built on those.

### Main Section

Spill Magazine names Heartbreak Summer as one of the few songs that break the album's formula: it slows the tempo down. Diandra Reviews It All hears the album's arrangements as bright paths for darker undertones that build towards more lavishly placed chords, and gives this song as the example. Listen for the pace first, then for where the harmony thickens.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} lends a voice to someone else's record. EGO is built that way: Rivers Cuomo of Weezer, Rostam Batmanglij, St. Lucia and KNA are among its guests. K.Flay is more than a voice here, since she shares the writing credit with Anjos, so the song is a meeting of two writers rather than a producer's track with a hired singer.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes how a recording sounds, and RAC came to producing through remixing: Anjos started the Remix Artist Collective in 2007 as a student in Illinois, and his remix of {{ <track_link source="bob-moses-tearing-me-up.md" /> }} won the Grammy for Best Remixed Recording in 2017.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} is a separate job from producing. Andrew Maury balanced the finished parts into one stereo recording; Joe LaPorta then mastered it, preparing that mix for release.

## Artistic Context

Anjos was born in Portugal in 1985 and is based in Oregon. EGO came three years after his album Strangers and after the remix work that made his name; Sir Sly, whom K.Flay would later remix, had remixed his Let Go in 2013. K.Flay, born in Wilmette, Illinois, in 1985, had released Every Where Is Some Where, with {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }}, that April.

## Impact and Reception

We found no sign that Heartbreak Summer was released as a single, and no chart record for it. The two reviews of EGO we found differ in tone: Spill found the album solid but short of variety and this song less lustrous than the standouts, while Diandra Reviews It All heard the record as a celebration of how much life humbles us. Play it in the album's order and decide whether the slowdown is a dip or the moment the summer turns.
