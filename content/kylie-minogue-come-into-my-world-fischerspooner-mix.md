## Genre and Mood

**Kylie Minogue**'s **Come into My World**, in the **Fischerspooner** mix, is a remix of a pop single by an electroclash duo from New York. The song's mood is longing and sultry: a plea to a lover, in a voice that AllMusic's Jon O'Brien, writing about Fever, called a seductive purr. How far the mix pulls the song toward electroclash, no source used here says.

## Song Summary

Cathy Dennis and Rob Davis wrote and produced Come into My World for Fever, Kylie Minogue's eighth studio album. Wikipedia describes it as a dance-pop song in which the singer pleads with her lover to come into her world; PopMatters' Jason Thompson heard a plea for love. O'Brien called the original chilled-out dream pop. The mix's official upload lists Fischerspooner as mixer next to the original team, with Kylie Minogue on lead vocals and Cathy Dennis on backing vocals and additional keyboards.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to someone else to rebuild, and in 2002 such mixes travelled on singles. This one was on the DVD single as audio, the Benelux and Canadian two-track CD, the French maxi-single and the US 12-inch, always beside the radio edit.

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }} was the scene of the late 1990s and early 2000s that fused 1980s electro, synth pop and new wave with punk attitude and a taste for performance, around labels such as International DeeJay Gigolo and clubs in New York, Berlin and Paris. Fischerspooner were an electroclash duo and performance troupe formed in 1998 by the classically trained musician Warren Fischer and the video artist and experimental theatre performer Casey Spooner; the duo's debut album #1, first released on International DJ Gigolo, included {{ <track_link source="fischerspooner-emerge.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Come into My World was released on 21 October 2002 as the fourth and final single from Fever, after Can't Get You Out of My Head, In Your Eyes and Love at First Sight. Michel Gondry's video multiplied Kylie Minogue at a street intersection in Boulogne-Billancourt, outside Paris. Christopher Smith of Talk About Pop Music notes that the mix appeared on the Canadian and French singles before Boombox, Parlophone's remix album of 2008. It is also on the bonus disc of Fever's special edition.

## Impact and Reception

The song reached number eight in the UK and four in Australia and won the 2004 Grammy for Best Dance Recording, Kylie Minogue's first Grammy. The mix had a life of its own: on the KylieX2008 tour the song was performed in its Fischerspooner version in the fifth act, Naughty Manga Girl.
