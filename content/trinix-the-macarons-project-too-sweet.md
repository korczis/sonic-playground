## Genre and Mood

**Trinix** and **The Macarons Project** share the credit on **Too Sweet**, a cover of Hozier's 2024 hit that Deezer files under electro, electro-pop and pop. It is a song passed from hand to hand: an Irish singer's retro-soul single, an Indonesian duo's cover, then a French electronic duo's version for the dance floor. Playful and upbeat are this page's reading of a flirtatious lyric made to move quickly.

## Song Summary

Hozier's lyric is a portrait of two people who live at different speeds. He is the night owl who takes whiskey and black coffee; she keeps healthy hours and is, as the title says, too sweet for him. Trinix released their version on 28 November 2025 through the Paris label Inside Records, and MusicBrainz lists the recording as a cover. Deezer credits Hozier and his co-writers as its authors and The Macarons Project as a main artist beside Trinix; the video on the duo's channel calls them featured.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or interview we could find describes this version part by part, so this page draws no map of it and claims no tempo or key.

### Main Section

What the sources do give is its shape: 2:44 on the single, against 3:33 for The Macarons Project's own cover, which they released on YouTube in July 2024, performed by Ree and Dito and produced by Jacob Montague. The lyric is Hozier's; the sound around it is Trinix's, and how much of the duo's 2024 recording survives inside it, no source says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song by someone other than its first performer. This one is a cover twice over. Hozier recorded Too Sweet for his EP Unheard; The Macarons Project, a Canada-based Indonesian brother-sister duo in which Ree sings and Dito plays guitar, covered it; Trinix then made their own version with them. Compare the guitar-and-voice world of {{ <track_link source="angus-julia-stone-big-jet-plane.md" /> }}, which Trinix also turned into a club hit.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest performer named in a recording's credit. Here the guest credit goes to The Macarons Project, while the songwriting credit stays with Hozier and his six co-writers: performing a song and writing it are separate credits, and a cover keeps the second.

## Artistic Context

Trinix are Josh Chergui and Loïs Serre, two childhood friends from Lyon who formed the duo in 2012. Wikipedia describes them as an EDM and electro house act whose catalogue is full of reworkings: She Said, built on Angus & Julia Stone's Big Jet Plane, passed 100 million Spotify streams in 2024, and Beautiful Day grew from a Rushawn a cappella. In 2026 they followed Too Sweet with a Deluxe Edit.

## Impact and Reception

Hozier's original went to number one in the UK and on the Billboard Hot 100, his first number one in each. No chart run is documented for this version. Put it on after the original and listen to how quickly the night goes by.
