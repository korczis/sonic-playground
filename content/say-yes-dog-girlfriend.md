## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog** put **Girlfriend** on their first album as synth-pop and electro-pop, played on real synthesizers with live drums. The feelings here, melancholic and groovy, follow the German webzine Terrorverlag, which heard a melancholic streak across the album, and Renowned for Sound, which picked this song out for its funky keystrokes.

## Song Summary

Girlfriend was one of the two singles of 2015, with Stronger, from Plastic Love, the trio's debut album, released on 4 September 2015 on Diskodogs Records with Cargo. The band put the song online in June, ahead of the album, noting that it was recorded by Say Yes Dog and Shai Solan at Helmbreker Studios in Haarlem and mixed by Ash Workman. The reviews describe it in a line each: for Terrorverlag it is a greeting from the era of the Neue Deutsche Welle, the German new wave of the early 1980s.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the centre of Say Yes Dog's sound, and Terrorverlag stressed that the trio play real ones, with live drums rather than programmed beats. Terrorverlag did not say what reminded it of the Neue Deutsche Welle; listen for whether it is the synthesizers.

BlackPlastic heard the album's bouncy electronic melodies as being there to support the band's {{ <concept_link name="vocal harmony" /> }}, and named Girlfriend among the songs where the record's sophisticated pop ambitions show. Listen for whether the voices stack in the choruses.

## Artistic Context

Say Yes Dog are a German-Luxembourgish trio: Ahrends and Rundel grew up in Berlin, Karier comes from Luxembourg, and all three studied sound engineering and jazz at the conservatory in The Hague. Their 2013 EP {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog_a-friend.md" /> }} came first; Plastic Love followed two years later.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews were warm but measured. Renowned for Sound counted Girlfriend among the songs with a distinct edge; BlackPlastic felt the album's ambitions, hinted at here and on {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-stronger.md" /> }}, were never quite realised in full, but called the record a touching mission statement from a band of huge promise. Put it on and decide which side you are on.
