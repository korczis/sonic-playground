## Genre and Mood

**The Prodigy**'s **The Day Is My Enemy** is the door into the album of the same name and does not open gently. It is electronic music with an industrial edge, dark, haunting and intense: Sonicabuse called it industrial strength, and Metal Hammer heard a dark, brooding title track that would not sound out of place on a Nine Inch Nails album.

## Song Summary

Two borrowed things made the track. Liam Howlett said the idea came when Olly Burden gave him a guitar riff, which became its foundation; and its title, like the album's, comes from the lyrics of Cole Porter's "All Through the Night", in the Ella Fitzgerald recording that first inspired the track. Martina Topley-Bird sings, with additional vocals by Paul "Dirtcandy" Jackson, and the Swiss group Top Secret Drum Corps plays the drums. It was the last track Howlett produced for the album, which it opens.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track section by section or gives times; the reviews describe its voice and its beat.

### The Voice

The Line of Best Fit wrote that Topley-Bird's smoky vocals open the album. Songfacts reports that her singing was first recorded for a different Howlett track that he did not like, and that he told Spin he tucked it away for the right moment.

### The Beat

Exclaim!, writing on the day the track came out, heard a menacing monster of a beat and a creepily innocent-sounding hook. Metal Hammer called it meticulously paced, forebodingly kicking off a record its makers called the most aggressive they had ever made.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="interpolation" /> }} re-sings words or a melody from an older song instead of sampling its recording. Mixdown described exactly that here: Topley-Bird interpolates Ella Fitzgerald's "All Through the Night", and Cole Porter is credited as a writer of the track.

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} can be the seed of a whole track, and this one came from outside the band: Howlett asked Burden's permission to build on it.

As a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Topley-Bird, known for her work with Tricky and Massive Attack, brings a trip-hop voice into a record built for attack. Hear her in another frame on {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-martina-topley-bird-roots-manuva-all-alone.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

The Day Is My Enemy, The Prodigy's sixth album, was written and recorded over several years, largely on tour and at the band's Tileyard Studios, and released on 30 March 2015 by Take Me to the Hospital and Cooking Vinyl. Howlett said "violent" was the word that kept coming up. The title track was released as official audio and a promotional single on 26 January 2015, two weeks after the lead single {{ <track_link source="the-prodigy-nasty.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 30 on the UK Dance chart, and the album debuted at number one in the UK. Drowned in Sound wrote that the title track really is as good as it gets here; The Line of Best Fit felt Topley-Bird's voice did not fit the barrage around it. Start the album where it starts.
