## Genre and Mood

**Sir Sly**'s **&Run - K.Flay Remix** hands an indie pop and synth-pop song to **K.Flay**, an artist better known for writing and singing her own songs than for remixing others. The original is, in Atwood Magazine's words, bold and genre-defiant, catchy while it borrows a little of everything; its feelings are introspective and bittersweet, a narrator who feels free and trapped in the same breath.

## Song Summary

&Run comes from Don't You Worry, Honey, the trio's second album, released by Interscope on 30 June 2017. Landon Jacobs, Jason Suwito and Hayden Coplen wrote and produced it. Atwood reads the album as Jacobs's journey through grief, as he opens up about his mother's death and his recent divorce, and hears in &Run a man who cannot outrun the burden of memory and knows he is kidding himself. K.Flay's version came out as a single on 19 January 2018 and runs 3:36, about ten seconds shorter than the album version.

## Musical Breakdown

No review we found describes the remix itself, so this breakdown says what the sources establish and leaves the listening to you.

### The Song

Earmilk calls the original an eccentric, synth-heavy track that barrels on with drum and bass at its centre. That is what any remix of it starts from: Jacobs's vocal, the hook and the drive.

### The Remix

Apple Music credits K.Flay as remixer and as studio personnel and keeps the trio as writers and producers, so the words and melody are Sir Sly's and the frame around them is hers.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording's parts into a new version. This one belongs to a swap: Sir Sly's channel posted K.Flay's remix of &Run at the same moment, on the evening of 18 January 2018, as K.Flay's channel posted Sir Sly's remix of her song Giver. Each act lends its sound to the other and each song reaches the other's listeners. Hear K.Flay's own songs remixed by someone else in {{ <track_link source="k-flay-aire-atlantica-blood-in-the-cut-aire-atlantica-remix.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes how a recording sounds. Sir Sly produce themselves: the credits name all three members. A remixer is a producer invited in afterwards, working on finished parts rather than on a song still being written.

## Artistic Context

Sir Sly, formed in Orange County, California, and active since 2012, released their debut album, You Haunt Me, in 2014. K.Flay, born Kristine Flaherty in Wilmette, Illinois, in 1985, had released Every Where Is Some Where in April 2017 on Night Street and Interscope, the album of {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }} and of Giver. The ties lasted: K.Flay's 2026 side project, 2XT, includes Jason Suwito.

## Impact and Reception

The original &Run reached number 3 on Billboard's Alternative Songs chart, ranked 13th on its 2018 year-end list and was on the FIFA 18 soundtrack. We found no chart record or review for the remix. Play the album version first, then K.Flay's, and listen for what she keeps.
