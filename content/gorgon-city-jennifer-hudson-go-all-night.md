## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** and **Jennifer Hudson**'s **Go All Night** is house and dance music in its most classic shape: a producers' track with a great singer on top, euphoric, energetic and soulful. VICE heard it rooted in classic American house, packed with the verve of a bygone era and yet current and fresh.

## Song Summary

Go All Night comes from Sirens, the 2014 debut album of the English production duo Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott. They wrote it with the singer Kiesza, and it nearly did not happen: Robson-Scott told VICE they were not going to use it until their manager kept insisting that Hudson was doing it. She sent her vocal the Saturday before the album had to be mastered, recorded on her own, and the duo finished the production around it. In Robson-Scott's words, she made it into a really good tune.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track in order; these are the parts its reviewers singled out.

### The Piano and the Bass

SoulBounce heard sleek, piano-laced rhythms that work with Hudson rather than against her, with the duo staying in the background. Dancing Astronaut heard her voice blending with the piano chords and the bassline.

### The Vocal

Hudson takes the lead with her signature runs and, in SoulBounce's words, does not hold back; VICE cast her as the club diva over restrained hooks.

### The Ad-libs

Around the main line sit her ad-libs, the part Robson-Scott singled out when he listened to the stems separately.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} can change a song's fate. Here a guest voice, credited as a feature, turned a track its makers had nearly shelved into a single.

{{ <concept_link name="melisma" /> }} is what the reviewers meant by her runs: several notes bent across one syllable, a technique of gospel and soul. SoulBounce heard echoes of 1990s house acts such as La Bouche and Robin S, a music long built on polished production paired with strong singers.

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is the steady dance-floor pulse of that tradition; every reviewer placed Go All Night inside it. Compare the deeper remix of the same album's {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-mnek-will-saul-ready-for-your-love-close-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Sirens came out on 6 October 2014 and entered the UK Albums Chart at number 10, with vocals from Katy B, MNEK and others. Go All Night followed on 14 December 2014 as its fifth single on Virgin EMI, with remixes by Wilkinson, Booka Shade, Illyus & Barrientos, Drew Hill and Aquilo.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 14 in the UK and number 2 on the UK dance chart, Hudson's first British top-20 single since Spotlight in 2008, and topped Billboard's Dance Club Songs chart in the US. It was certified silver in the UK in 2019. Press play and let the piano carry you in.
