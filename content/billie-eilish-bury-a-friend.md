## Genre and Mood

**Billie Eilish**'s **bury a friend** is electro-pop and synth-pop pulled toward trip-hop and industrial noise. It is dark, eerie and haunting, but quietly so: the menace comes from a whisper, not a shout.

## Song Summary

Eilish sings from the point of view of the monster under your bed, which, she said, could be anything, even someone you love so much that it takes over your life. The refrain is a run of questions to the listener, and its last one, when we all fall asleep, where do we go, gave her debut album its title. She wrote the song with her brother Finneas O'Connell, who produced it; they made it in Chicago with a shuffle beat in mind, and she drew him a black monster to show how it should sound.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A voice calls her name: the rapper Mehki Raine, then known as Crooks, recorded it on his phone and sent it to her.

### Verse 1

Her vocals are soft and treated with layers of effects, a near-whispered duet with a distorted version of herself, as she put it, over a galloping hip-hop beat that critics compared to Kanye West's Black Skinhead.

### Chorus

The melody turns almost innocent, likened to the Doors' People Are Strange and called as innocuous as a childhood rhyme, against the dark lyric.

### Bridge

No source describes the bridge beyond its place: it arrives straight after a verse rather than after the penultimate chorus. Around it the song is full of what critics called rumbling percussion, scattered synths and screams.

### Outro

The song closes on an instrumental passage of ilomilo, the next track on the album.

## Concepts in Focus

The song is written in G harmonic {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }}, the minor scale with a raised seventh note, at a moderately fast 120 beats per minute. Listen to how the chorus melody that critics heard as childlike sits on that dark ground.

It also bends {{ <concept_link name="verse-chorus form" /> }}. Charlie Harding of Vox described a broken song form with strange alternate verses and a bridge placed after a verse, an effect he found destabilising and yet still accessible. The Harvard Crimson heard the pauses as the quick breaths of someone in a panic.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Michael Chaves in a single day, shows Eilish under Mehki Raine's bed, then pulled by black-gloved hands and stabbed with syringes; reviewers compared it to Suspiria and The Shining. For another dark voice in a trip-hop fog, hear {{ <track_link source="kosheen-addict.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

bury a friend was released on 30 January 2019 through Darkroom and Interscope as the third single from WHEN WE ALL FALL ASLEEP, WHERE DO WE GO?, announced the day before. Eilish said it set the tone for the whole album.

## Impact and Reception

It reached number 14 on the Billboard Hot 100, number six in the UK and number one in Sweden and Latvia, and became her first number one on a Billboard airplay chart, the Alternative Songs chart. NME called it a sizeable middle finger to anyone expecting a twinkly ballad. Listen in the dark, if you dare.
