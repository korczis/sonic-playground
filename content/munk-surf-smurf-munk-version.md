## Genre and Mood

**Munk** made **Surf Smurf (Munk Version)** a house track that sounds as if it had been found in a garage: gritty, groovy and played for fun. It sits between genres that rarely meet. The base is house, yet the song underneath belongs to surf rock and rockabilly, and the label's own notes say so plainly.

## Song Summary

Surf Smurf came out on Gomma on 23 January 2014, as a single shared by Munk, the alias of Mathias Modica, and Rebolledo of Pachanga Boys. The label describes the song as a dirty 1960s surf rock song gone electronic. It exists in two versions by its two makers. Munk's, 5:59 long, is what the notes call a dirty rockabilly house track that brings back analogue vibes and kaput drum machine sounds; Rebolledo's runs 11:34. A third track, Got It Baby, completes the record.

## Concepts in Focus

This is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} in the clearest sense: the two people behind the song each finished it their own way and released both on one single. Rebolledo turned it into a twelve-minute dub techno piece that flows around the basic guitar lick; Munk kept it under six minutes and dirty.

That lick is a {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }}, the short, repeated figure that anchors a song. Surf rock is built on such guitar figures, and here one carries a house track.

The {{ <concept_link name="drum machine" /> }} matters as much. Its machine-steady time is house's foundation, but the label stresses that these sounds are kaput, rough rather than clean, which suits a song that wants to sound found rather than polished.

## Artistic Context

Mathias Modica trained as a jazz pianist and is the son of a classical composer. He started Gomma in 1999, ran it with Jonas Imbery and released bands such as WhoMadeWho (hear {{ <track_link source="whomadewho-keep-me-in-my-plane.md" /> }}); in 2012 he launched Toy Tonics. He also records as Kapote. Rebolledo has been a Munk fan from the start, and Modica has often played as a DJ in Mexico, especially at Rebolledo's club Topaz de Luxe.
