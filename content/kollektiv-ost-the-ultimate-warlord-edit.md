## Genre and Mood

**Kollektiv Ost** released **The Ultimate Warlord Edit** in March 2022, and it sits where nu-disco meets deep house: a long, steady electronic track for the dance floor. The mood its label promised for the year's first release was soothing, hypnotic and euphoric, and the track's own description gives the hypnotic part to its vocals.

## Song Summary

The single came out on Datscha Music on 4 March 2022 with two versions: this edit at 6:20 and a radio edit at 3:25. Datscha Music's release text is the only published description of it. It speaks of electro-disco magic in the bass and kick combination, a focus on the groove and the hypnotic vocals, and pads and atmospheric elements that gently morph and mutate to create their own universe, one where the only necessary item is a dance floor. No review, interview or premiere describes the track further.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} cuts a club track down for radio and streaming. Here the single itself holds both: the full 6:20 edit and a 3:25 radio edit. Hearing them side by side is the quickest way to find out which parts a DJ needs and a radio listener does not.

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is what the label puts first, and in this kind of track it lives in the relationship between the bass and the {{ <concept_link name="kick drum" /> }}. The kick marks the beat and the bass moves with it; that pairing is where to listen first.

An edit is a kind of {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}: a producer reshapes an existing record rather than writing a new one. The release text does not name the source, but MusicBrainz credits this recording to The Immortals with Kollektiv Ost as remixer. The Ultimate Warlord was a late-1970s disco record by The Immortals, written and produced by Will Morrison and Ian Guenther, which one record shop describes as dark electronic disco with demonic vocals. What the edit keeps of it, no source says.

## Artistic Context

Kollektiv Ost are Sandro Schäufler and Marcel Schulz, both from Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. According to their own biography, the name was first used at the end of 2003 for underground dance parties before it became their joint project; Marcel later moved to Berlin and, after more than fifteen years, returned, while Sandro stayed. They have released on Katermukke, Bar25, Dantze and 3000Grad.
