## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog** captivates with their track **A Friend,** delivering 
a lively mix of synth-pop and indie dance. This catchy tune 
invites listeners to embrace the warmth of friendship and the 
joy of connection.

## Song Summary

The track embodies a spirit of celebration, intertwining upbeat 
synths and infectious melodies that resonate with listeners. 
It captures the essence of camaraderie and the happiness 
found in friendships, making it a perfect anthem for social 
gatherings and joyful moments.
