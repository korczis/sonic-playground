## Genre and Mood

**Kid Francescoli**'s **The Player**, credited with **Julia Minkin**, is electro-pop from the Marseille project's 2017 album Play Me Again, filed by Wikipedia under electropop and indie pop. Its feelings here, playful and romantic, follow what Mathieu Hocine said the album is about: love, its doubts and joys, and a game of cat and mouse. No review of this song describes how it sounds, and the page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

The Player is the third of the album's eleven tracks and runs 4:04. Bandcamp's credits give it to Hocine and Minkin as writers, and Shazam names Minkin as the singer. Kid Francescoli posted it on his YouTube channel on 13 January 2017, seven weeks before the album came out on Yotanka on 3 March. No source explains the lyric, so this review does not guess at it.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or interview maps the song part by part, so this breakdown keeps to what its makers and its releases document.

### The Pace

When Made in Marseille asked whether the new album kept the old texture, because The Player suggested a change, Hocine answered that With Julia had already been far more danceable than his earlier work, that the rhythmic songs of the new one were even more so, and that its average tempo was higher while the album stayed fairly calm.

### The Recording

Henner and Hocine recorded and produced the album in Marseille; Antoine Thibaudeau mixed it and Darius van Helfteren mastered it. Hocine named Simon of Nasser and Mathieu of Oh! Tiger Mountain among the people who helped with bass lines.

## Concepts in Focus

Minkin is credited as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, but she is not a guest. Hocine told Frontview Magazine that she was very involved in the composition, writes her own lyrics and melodies, and would tour with him because they work as a duo.

The {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} is where his songs begin. Hocine told MyProvence that he comes to the studio every day to record what he calls loops, and that by working on them they turn, almost by magic, into songs. Listen for what repeats in The Player and what was added around it.

## Artistic Context

Hocine founded Kid Francescoli in Marseille in 2002 and named it after the Uruguayan footballer Enzo Francescoli. He met Minkin, an American singer, after a concert in New York; their album With Julia came first, and Play Me Again continued the story, the romance turned into a musical bond. Wikipedia calls it the project's most successful album; its track Moon later went viral.
