## Genre and Mood

**K.Flay**'s **High Enough - Seattle Sessions** takes her alternative-rock and indie breakthrough into orchestral territory. Announcing it, she described the Seattle Sessions as a reimagining of some of her favourite songs from the album, using live string, horn and choir sections. The feelings are the original's, reflective and uplifting; no source describes how the new setting shifts them.

## Song Summary

The song is the same one: K.Flay has said High Enough began as a reply to the many songs about getting high, asking what if she is already high enough, and it compares love to a drug while wanting to feel exactly what she feels. What changes is the setting. The deluxe edition's announcement called the five Seattle Sessions brand new orchestral versions of album tracks. This recording runs 3:34, shorter than the studio original.

### The Length

## Concepts in Focus

This is an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} in the strict sense: the same artist recording the same song again, with a different sound, rather than another producer remixing her tracks. Set it beside {{ <track_link source="k-flay-high-enough.md" /> }} and you hear how much of a song's mood lives in its production.

The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is the whole point here. Melody and words stay; the parts written for strings, horns and choir decide what the song feels like. The sources do not name the arranger or the players.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} was Budo. K.Flay called him an old friend and an incredible producer who helped her bring the vision to life.

## Artistic Context

Every Where Is Some Where, K.Flay's album on Interscope and Dan Reynolds' Night Street imprint, came out on 7 April 2017 and earned a Grammy nomination for Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical. Its deluxe edition, released digitally on 12 January 2018, added five Seattle Sessions: Blood In The Cut, Giver, Black Wave, High Enough and Mean It. MusicBrainz also lists them as a Seattle Sessions EP dated 18 May 2018, and KFlayVEVO published this video the day before.

## Impact and Reception

No review of this version was found. The original reached number 4 on Billboard's Alternative Airplay chart and was certified platinum in the US. Play the two back to back, original first.
