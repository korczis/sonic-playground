## Genre and Mood

**Lilly Wood and The Prick** wrote the song, and **Robin Schulz** turned it into a global hit with **Prayer in C (Robin Schulz Radio Edit)**. The track fuses deep house, dance and folk roots, and its mood is melancholic, energetic and deeply bittersweet: a sad song you dance to with your eyes closed. It is a perfect example of how a remix can change a song's meaning without changing a word.

## Song Summary

The lyric is bleak, dealing with loss, abandonment and a world that seems to be falling apart, sung by Nili Hadida in a cool, almost resigned voice. Robin Schulz keeps that voice and the looping acoustic guitar riff, then sets them over a bouncing deep-house beat and a warm bassline. The contrast between the dark lyric and the bright, propulsive groove is exactly what makes the remix so addictive. Schulz's touch is light: he adds space, a gentle swing and a warm low end, and resists the temptation to overload the arrangement. The guitar riff, simple and instantly memorable, does the rest, looping like a thought you cannot shake off.

## Artistic Context

Lilly Wood and The Prick is a French duo of singer Nili Hadida and multi-instrumentalist Benjamin Cotto. The original "Prayer in C" appeared on their 2010 album *Invincible Friends* as a stripped-back folk-pop song. In 2014 the German DJ and producer Robin Schulz remixed it, and the remix appears on his debut album *Prayer*, released the same year. Schulz had built a following with remixes that brought acoustic and vocal tracks into gentle, melodic house, and this one became the template for a whole wave of similar summer remixes.

## Impact and Reception

The remix became one of the biggest dance hits of 2014, topping charts in several countries, including Germany and the UK, and it made Robin Schulz an international name. It is here for that perfect balance of sadness and sunlight. Let the guitar riff pull you in.
