## Genre and Mood

**KANT** and **Leo Stannard** stretch out on **Close to the Wire (Club Remix)**, a seven-minute version that leans fully into deep house and electronic club territory. Its mood is melancholic, hypnotic and emotive, the kind of track that makes a crowded dance floor feel suddenly reflective without ever losing its pulse. It is house music with a songwriter's heart.

## Song Summary

The song is about living on the edge of something, emotionally stretched and close to breaking. Leo Stannard's voice, soft but strained, carries that tension, and KANT's club arrangement gives it room to breathe. Piano chords with a touch of melancholy sit at the centre, while a steady four-to-the-floor beat and patient percussion turn the song into a long, rolling journey rather than a radio single. Nothing is hurried: the track trusts its chords and its singer, and lets repetition do the emotional work.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix is built for DJs: long, gradual and carefully layered, saving its emotional peak for the moment the groove is fully established.

### Intro

A clean kick and shuffling hats open the track, giving DJs a long runway before any melody appears.

### Build-up

A deep bassline and soft pads join, with piano fragments drifting in to hint at the song's mood.

### Main Section

The full piano chords and Leo Stannard's vocal arrive, heartfelt and close, floating over the groove so that the melancholy and the momentum feel inseparable.

### Bridge

The drums drop away for a breakdown, leaving voice and piano exposed, a moment of stillness that makes the return of the beat hit harder.

### Climax

The groove comes back at full strength with the vocal hook repeated, the emotional and rhythmic peak of the track.

### Outro

Elements fall away one by one until only the beat remains, ready to be mixed into whatever comes next.

## Artistic Context

KANT is a producer known for melodic, emotive house, with releases on labels such as Black Butter Records, and Leo Stannard is a British singer-songwriter whose voice has fronted several dance collaborations. "Close to the Wire" was premiered in June 2016, with reviewers pointing to its piano-led melancholy and singer-songwriter feel. This club remix comes from the *Close to the Wire* EP.

## Impact and Reception

Its premiere coverage compared the piano melancholy to Moby, and the club remix gives DJs a longer, more patient way into the song. It earns its place here for the way it balances sadness and drive. Listen through to the breakdown and feel the beat bring you back.
