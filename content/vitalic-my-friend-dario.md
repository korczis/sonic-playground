## Genre and Mood

**Vitalic** made **My Friend Dario** sound like a rock single assembled by machines. It belongs to electroclash and the electro house of its album, but behaves like a short pop song with a story: energetic and intense on the surface, dark underneath, because the story ends in a crash.

## Song Summary

Dario, Pascal Arbez-Nicolas has said, is a concept name for a part of his own personality: a reckless figure who drives like a brainless kid. In the song Dario dies in a crash, which Arbez called unreal because it is just a song. The narrator is Brigitte, a voice-synthesis program, and this was the first Vitalic single to use it. Adam Blyweiss of mxdwn heard guitar-driven electroclash with vox-box lyrics that sum up the tale of a nihilistic driver; Jeff Terich of Treble heard a buzzing cybernetic Jesus and Mary Chain sounding track that revs like a Harley.

## Concepts in Focus

My Friend Dario is a textbook case of {{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }}, the scene that took 1980s electro and synth-pop, added punk attitude and deadpan vocals, and grew around labels such as International DeeJay Gigolo. Here the deadpan voice is literally a machine.

It is also a lesson in what a {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} can impersonate. Reviewers described guitars; the album's official description says everything was synthesized and that the only thing Vitalic cannot fake is the emotion. A timbre, not an instrument, makes this sound like rock.

Finally, {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }} carried the song far: it is on the soundtracks of FIFA 06, Need for Speed: Carbon and Riders Republic, and it played at Versace's and Chanel's Fall/Winter 2005 shows. A song about a doomed driver in a racing game is a neat fit.

## Artistic Context

Vitalic is Pascal Arbez-Nicolas, born in 1976, from Dijon. The Hacker encouraged him to send tracks to DJ Hell at International DeeJay Gigolo, which released the Poney EP in 2001; compare its raw electro in {{ <track_link source="vitalic-cocaine.md" /> }}. My Friend Dario came out on 29 March 2005 as the first single from his debut album OK Cowboy, recorded in Dijon in 2003 and 2004 and released on 4 April 2005 by Citizen Records and PIAS.

## Impact and Reception

OK Cowboy was well received: Metacritic gives it 82, Pitchfork placed it 184th among its top 200 albums of the 2000s, and in 2012 IMPALA certified it gold for European sales of at least 75,000 copies. The single's video, directed by Marco Laguna, has the band miming on air guitar and air drums. Put it on loud and decide whether you are hearing a guitar.
