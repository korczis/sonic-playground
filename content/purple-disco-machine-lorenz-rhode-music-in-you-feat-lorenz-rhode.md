## Song Summary

Music In You is the first of thirteen tracks on Soulmatic, the debut album of Tino Piontek, the Dresden producer behind Purple Disco Machine. Sweat It Out released it on 20 October 2017, and the track runs four minutes 30 seconds. The album credits name Lorenz Rhode as featured artist and as its producer, arranger, performer and talk box player, and Rhode and Piontek as its writers. Monte mixed it at Trigonomics in Hamburg. The video followed on Purple Disco Machine's channel on 8 January 2018.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is usually a guest voice. Rhode's credit goes further: writer, producer, arranger and player of the instrument at the centre, the talk box. A talk box sends the sound of an instrument through a tube into the player's mouth, where it is shaped like speech and picked up by a microphone; German Wikipedia calls the talk box with a synthesizer Rhode's specialty. Compare the machine voice of {{ <track_link source="daft-punk-around-the-world.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} came almost three months after the album, keeping the opener in view. A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by David Penn later appeared on The Soulmatic Remixes.

## Artistic Context

Piontek, born in Dresden in 1980, began producing in 1996 and released his first productions in 2012; a 2023 Grammy for his remix of Lizzo's About Damn Time came later. Rhode, who grew up in Remagen, has led the Rundfunk-Tanzorchester Ehrenfeld since 2016 and worked with Jamie Lidell. The pair met again on Up & Down and At The Disko.
