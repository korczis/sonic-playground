## Genre and Mood

**Hermanos Inglesos** made **Wanderland** with **meme** on the voice, and the databases do not agree on what to call it: Discogs files the duo's own digital release under electro and house, and a PIAS promo of the same single under indie rock. That split suits a pair of club DJs putting out a sung single. This page files it as dreamy and groovy, a reading to test against the track.

## Song Summary

Wanderland is the 2010 single of the Belgian brothers Cedric and Didier Engels, written, produced and mixed with the producer Ramon Tapia according to the credits that come with the recording. It also opens their debut album, The Wander Of You, released by PIAS Belgium the same year. The singer credited as meme is Lara Chedraoui, who was then just starting out with the band Intergalactic Lovers.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes Wanderland part by part, so this review gives the frame rather than a map. The digital single lists four versions: this radio edit at 3:34, the Original at 5:36, a Clashes remix at 7:31 and a Superlijm remix at 3:25. Discogs credits meme on the edit and on both remixes; for the Original it names only the duo, and whether that version carries her voice the listing does not say.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} cuts a track down for radio and streaming. Here the cut is large: the edit runs about two minutes shorter than the Original, a length made for airplay rather than for a DJ's mix.

Chedraoui appears as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, but under a pseudonym. The Dutch Wikipedia notes that she sang on Wanderland as meme in 2010, a year before Intergalactic Lovers released their first single, Fade Away; in 2015 she worked with the brothers again on Self Control. When Wanderland came out again in 2024, the release named her as Lara Chedraoui.

The single's two {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} versions pull in opposite directions, one longer than the Original and one shorter than the edit. For another vocal single from a Belgian label, hear {{ <track_link source="nteibint-tella-the-owner.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Engels is the Dutch word for English, hence the name: English brothers, in Spanish. Their Discogs profile describes former Culture Club residents and Diesel U-Music winners who absorbed Belgium's club tradition, from the R&S label and the club Boccaccio to Eskimo Recordings and Culture Club, and who build eclectic DJ sets from their own edits, remixes and tracks.

## Impact and Reception

No chart entry or review of Wanderland was available to this review. What the record shows is a long life: a 2010 single, the edit on YouTube since 2014, and a new release fourteen years after the first. Start with this edit, then hear the Original for what the extra two minutes hold.
