## Genre and Mood

**Asaf Avidan & the Mojos** sang it and **Wankelmut** remade it: **One Day / Reckoning Song (Wankelmut Remix) [Radio Edit]** is the short cut of the house remix that Wikipedia files under house, folktronica and trip hop, and that one review said had all the hallmarks of classical minimal techno. The mood stays bittersweet and nostalgic: Wankelmut heard in the original a settling of accounts with a fading love that still sounds positive, and the remix carries it at dance-floor pace.

## Song Summary

Wankelmut, a young Berlin DJ, heard Reckoning Song on a trip to the United States. In the winter of 2011 he reworked it in Ableton Live, cutting and filtering the recording and adding a simple four-four groove, and posted it on SoundCloud without Avidan's permission. Avidan learned of it on Facebook and replied that it sounded nice but was not his style. The track spread through blogs and clubs; Four Music, which held the rights to the original, released it in June 2012, and this radio edit runs about three and a half minutes.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can be made from nothing but the finished song. Wankelmut did not re-record anything: he cut and filtered the {{ <track_link source="asaf-avidan-the-mojos-reckoning-song-one-day.md" /> }} and gave it a simple four-four groove.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is the short version for broadcasting: 3:30 or 3:33 against 7:16 or 7:18 for the full mix, depending on the format.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Daniel Franke and Sander Houtkruijer, follows three performance artists through a city by night. This page embeds the second, daylight version, now on Wankelmut's VEVO channel.

## Artistic Context

Wankelmut is Jacob Dilßner, born in 1987 and raised in Friedrichshain, East Berlin, where he took piano lessons. He was studying philosophy and political science and DJing at Berlin clubs such as Sisyphos when he made the remix; his name is the German word for fickleness. Compare {{ <track_link source="lilly-wood-and-the-prick-robin-schulz-prayer-in-c-robin-schulz-radio-edit.md" /> }}, the same route two years later.

## Impact and Reception

The remix topped the charts in Germany for seven weeks, Austria for six and Switzerland for seven, and reached number one in Belgium, the Netherlands and Italy and number 30 in the UK. In Germany alone it sold about 1.2 million, and it was nominated as hit of the year at the 2013 Echo Awards. Avidan later called it very important for his career.
