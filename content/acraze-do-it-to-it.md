## Genre and Mood

**ACRAZE** presents an electrifying soundscape with **Do It To It**. This high-energy
dance track encapsulates the vibrant atmosphere of nightlife, combining house beats 
with nostalgic R&B influences, resulting in a song that feels both fresh and familiar.

"Do It To It" showcases ACRAZE's skillful production and Cherish's captivating vocal 
delivery. The track is a celebration of music and dancing, featuring lyrics that 
emphasize the joy and freedom found on the dance floor, creating a contagious party vibe.
