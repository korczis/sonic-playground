## Genre and Mood

**DJ S.K.T** looks back to the golden age of vocal house on **Must Be A Reason (Radio Edit)**, a dance track with modern polish and a heart rooted in the late 1990s. The mood is uplifting and groovy with a warm, nostalgic glow, a record that feels instantly familiar even on the first play, because in a sense it is.

## Song Summary

The track is built around one vocal phrase, the line that asks why the singer must be king of the castle, taken from Wamdue Project's "King of My Castle", the Chris Brann song with Gaelle Adisson's vocal that topped the UK chart in 1999. Rather than use the original master, the release credits a sample replay by Mark Summers, a faithful re-recording made for the purpose, and DJ S.K.T sets it over a bright, bouncing house groove. The radio edit trims the club version down to its essentials: a tight beat, a bubbling bassline, warm chords and that hook. The memorable detail is how the vocal loops into the groove, catchy enough for radio and still built for dancing.

## Musical Breakdown

As a radio edit, the track gets to its best moments quickly while keeping the shape of a club record.

### Intro

A punchy kick and crisp hi-hats set a bright pulse, with filtered chords hinting at the melody to come.

### Build-up

The bassline enters and the filter opens, as fragments of the vocal begin to appear over the rising groove.

### Main Section

The full hook arrives over warm chords and a bouncing bass, the track settling into its sunny, feel-good stride.

### Bridge

A short breakdown pulls the drums away, leaving the vocal and chords floating before the beat crashes back in.

### Outro

The groove rides out with the hook repeating, wrapping up neatly inside a radio-friendly running time.

## Concepts in Focus

The track is an example of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} with a twist. The vocal comes from "King of My Castle", but the credits name Mark Summers for "sample replay production": instead of licensing the 1990s master recording, a studio recreated it so closely that it does the same job. That is why the original writers, Chris Brann and Gaelle Adisson, share the writing credit with Stephen Kenny Tempest, DJ S.K.T's real name. The song is borrowed; the recording is new.

The borrowed line works because it is a ready-made {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}. In 1997 Brann wrote it around Freud's idea that the ego is not master in its own house; in 2017 it is a short, sung question that can be looped, filtered and repeated without wearing out. Listen to how the build-up teases fragments of it before the main section lets the whole phrase through.

This page is the {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}, about three minutes long, released alongside a longer club mix. The edit keeps the arc of the club version, intro, build, hook and breakdown, but shortens the long, beat-only stretches that DJs need for mixing, so the hook arrives sooner and the whole track fits a radio slot.

## Artistic Context

DJ S.K.T is Stephen Kenny Tempest, an English producer, DJ and label manager who started entering DJ competitions at twelve and made his name with house and tech house remixes, among them a 2014 Ministry of Sound release of his mix of the Nightcrawlers' "Push the Feeling On". His 2015 single "Take Me Away", featuring Rae, reached number 19 in the UK. Must Be A Reason came out on DFX on 18 August 2017 in a club mix and this radio edit, part of a wave of releases that revisited 1990s house and garage hooks with cleaner, heavier modern production.

## Impact and Reception

Must Be A Reason became one of DJ S.K.T's best-known records: Defected included the club mix on its Most Rated 2018 compilation, it was played on Defected Radio shows hosted by Sam Divine and Sonny Fodera, and its a cappella was issued for other DJs to use. For listeners who remember the original late-90s classic, it offers a warm hit of nostalgia; for newer ears, it is simply a sunny, effortless groove. It is on this playground because it bridges those two worlds so smoothly. Put it on at the start of a night out, or any time you need a lift.
