## Genre and Mood

**C2C** collaborates with **Pigeon John** to create an uplifting fusion of hip-hop, electronic, and soul in 
**Because of You**. Known for their genre-blending style, C2C delivers a feel-good anthem, while Pigeon John's engaging 
vocals bring warmth and sincerity to the track. The song’s joyful energy and catchy rhythm make it a celebration of 
gratitude and positivity.

## Song Summary

*Because of You* explores themes of appreciation and positive influence. The lyrics highlight the power of connection 
and the impact people can have on each other’s lives. Through vibrant production and heartfelt lyrics, C2C and Pigeon 
John craft a track that resonates with anyone who has felt inspired or supported by others.
