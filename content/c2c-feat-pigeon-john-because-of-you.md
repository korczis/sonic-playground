## Genre and Mood

**C2C** collaborates with **Pigeon John** on **Because of You**, a bright fusion of hip-hop, electronics and soul. The four French DJs supply a beat assembled on turntables, and the Los Angeles rapper brings a warm, easy voice that turns it into a song. The energy is upbeat and friendly, a feel-good track that wears its technique lightly.

## Song Summary

*Because of You* is built around its title, a phrase addressed to someone who has made a difference. A contemporary review of the album called the track Gorillaz-esque, and the comparison fits: it has the same mix of hip-hop rhythm, pop hook and cartoon brightness. What sets it apart is how the music is made, by four DJs cutting and layering records by hand.

## Lyrics Breakdown

### Intro: Setting the Vibe

The song opens on the groove, a beat with the slightly rough, hand-placed feel of sounds cut from records. It sets a welcoming tone before the voice comes in.

### Verse 1: Recognizing the Impact of Others

Pigeon John's delivery is relaxed and melodic, between rapping and singing. The beat leaves him room, so the verse feels conversational rather than crowded.

### Chorus: The Heartfelt Declaration

The chorus is where Pigeon John sings "because of you", the hook the whole track hangs on. Short and repeated, it is the part a listener takes away.

### Verse 2: Embracing the Journey

The second verse continues in the same easy voice while the beat keeps its forward motion.

### Bridge: A Moment of Reflection

Between the vocal sections the turntables step forward: this is where C2C's craft is easiest to hear, as fragments are cut, repeated and re-placed against the groove.

### Final Chorus: A Joyful Celebration

The final chorus returns the hook and closes the track on the same bright note it began with.

## Concepts in Focus

C2C are a lesson in {{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }}: playing the turntable as an instrument. The group won the DMC World DJ Championships in the team category four years in a row, from 2003 to 2006, and each member uses the decks to imitate drums, bass, guitar riffs or brass. On Tetra they turned that competition technique into songs.

Every turntablist track is also an exercise in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. The sounds come from records, and the art is in choosing the fragment, cutting it at the right moment and placing it so it becomes rhythm or melody. Here the fragments serve the song rather than show off the hands that move them.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the guest's job. The title phrase, sung by Pigeon John and repeated in the chorus, gives an instrumental crew's track a shape a listener can follow and remember.

## Artistic Context

C2C, also known as Coups2Cross, formed in Nantes in 1998. Its members are DJ Greem and 20syl, founders of the hip-hop group Hocus Pocus, and DJ Atom and DJ pFeL of Beat Torrent. Because of You is the second track on their debut album Tetra (stylised Tetr4), released on 3 September 2012 by On And On Records. The album topped the French album chart, and at the 2013 Victoires de la Musique it won all four awards it was nominated for, including best new artist, best music video and best live show. Pigeon John is a Los Angeles rapper known for melodic, good-humoured hip-hop.

## Impact and Reception

Tetra was one of the French breakthrough records of 2012 and won a European Border Breakers Award. Because of You was not one of its main singles, which were Down the Road, The Cell and Happy, but it shows the album's method at its most accessible: four turntablists, one guest voice, one hook. Listen once for the song and once only for the decks.
