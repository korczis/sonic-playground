## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog**'s **Golden Nights** is synth-pop and electro-pop turned down low: dreamy and relaxed, one of the quiet songs on an album otherwise built to move.

## Song Summary

Golden Nights is the fourth of the ten tracks on Voyage, the trio's second album, released on 10 May 2019 by Diskodogs Records. The Topic upload credits Aaron Ahrends, Pascal Karier and Paul Rundel beside the band and Ahrends as producer. Two German reviews singled it out. Soundmag's Eric heard, beside the danceable pieces, "ruhig-spacige Songs", quiet, spacey songs like Golden Nights and The Same that recall Air a little. MusikBlog's Sebastian found the album's songs, each a pearl on its own, sounding alike over its length, and still named Golden Nights his tip, the track to play first.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes Golden Nights part by part. What follows is what they say about its character and about the sound of the album it sits in.

### The Mood

Quiet and spacey, in Soundmag's words, the opposite of the album's dance-floor numbers such as Lies and Sugar.

### The Sound

The album's palette, as the reviewers heard it: nostalgic synths, falsetto hooks, funk basslines and clapping drums for MusikBlog; vibrating synths and pulsing rhythm in a minimal frame for Bedroomdisco. How much of that this track uses, the reviews do not say.

### The Comparison

The reference point is Air, the French duo. Soundmag offers it lightly, "ein wenig", a little; listen for what earns it.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the centre of Voyage. Bedroomdisco wrote that on this album the band gave themselves over fully to synths and to space-pop, sinking deep into the electro-pop of the 1980s. On a quiet track the same instrument does a different job: not driving a beat but colouring the air around the voice.

An {{ <concept_link name="ambient texture" /> }} is sound heard as a surrounding rather than a line. A song that reminds a critic of Air is a song where texture matters as much as melody; compare it with the album's opener, {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-deep-space.md" /> }}, which drifts the same way.

## Artistic Context

**Say Yes Dog** are two Berliners and a Luxembourger who met studying sound engineering and jazz at the conservatory in The Hague. Voyage came four years after their debut Plastic Love; Bedroomdisco wrote that its title comes from the band's travels through Japan, Vietnam and South Korea, though the music looks back to the 1980s rather than east.

## Impact and Reception

The album's reviews were warm and wary at once: Soundmag found no obvious hit but a self-contained sound world it was happy to float through, and Bedroomdisco wished for more variety. Golden Nights is the song one of those critics picked out of the uniform whole. Start the album here, as MusikBlog suggests.
