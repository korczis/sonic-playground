## Genre and Mood

**We Are Enfant Terrible** made **Filthy Love** the first single from their debut album, and it shows the trio's formula at its most compact: electro-pop and synth-pop with an alternative edge, laced with the sounds of old game consoles. The mood is sultry and playful at once, a voice one critic found especially sexy over game-console electronics, and a refrain built to stick.

## Song Summary

The song runs a little over three minutes. The Line of Best Fit heard it pick up the pace after the album's downbeat opener and called it a catchy little number. Drowned in Sound, in an otherwise lukewarm review, credited it with a reasonable bass line and a stuttering title refrain with an element of catchiness. The Interrobang found Clo Floret's vocal especially sexy here. Around those three things sits the band's signature: European synth-pop married with samples from 8-bit video games.

### The Electronics

Synths and console sounds set the colour. Onstage the band ran a Game Boy emulator on a Nintendo DS; in the studio, they told Journal du Geek, the emulator ran on a computer.

### The Bass Line and the Beat

The bass line is what Drowned in Sound singled out, under the drums Cyril Debarge plays in the band.

### The Refrain

The title comes back in a stutter, the hook the song is built around.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="chiptune" /> }} is the sound of game consoles turned into an instrument. We Are Enfant Terrible do not make pure chiptune; The Interrobang heard a sound that goes beyond the genre, with 8-bit samples inside synth-pop. The members said that between six and twelve they listened only to video-game music, and Drowned in Sound noted that reviewers kept invoking Sonic the Hedgehog.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} divides opinion. Drowned in Sound liked the one in Filthy Love, while The Line of Best Fit felt the lack of a bass across the album. Listening for it is a good test of your own ear.

## Artistic Context

We Are Enfant Terrible are Clo Floret, Thomas Fourny and Cyril Debarge, three Lille natives. Explicit Pictures, recorded at Fourny's house in Lille, came out in April 2011 on the Canadian label Last Gang, under licence from Pil Records. The same city later produced {{ <track_link source="gym-your-woman.md" /> }}, also tagged 8-bit synth-pop.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of the album were mixed: The Skinny gave it two stars and found few tunes catchy enough, while The Line of Best Fit called it well made and suspected the band's best work was still to come. Drowned in Sound and The Line of Best Fit both singled out Filthy Love as catchy. Turn it up and listen for the console sounds.
