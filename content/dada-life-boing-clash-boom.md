## Genre and Mood

**Dada Life**'s **Boing Clash Boom** is electronic dance music at full size: electro-house built on a heavy bassline, a vocal that runs through the whole track and a title made of three cartoon sound effects. It is energetic and intense, but it is also playful, a track that sets out to surprise a crowd as much as to flatten it.

## Song Summary

The song does what its title says: it bounces, crashes and booms. Elektro Daily heard in it the most typically Dada lyrics and the strongest vocalist on the album, and a breakdown unlike anything the duo had tried before.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The voice comes first, within a few seconds, before the beat has fully arrived; then the kick enters and the sawtooth bass and synth chords lock in behind it.

### Verse 1

The first verse rides that groove for a few measures. Elektro Daily described the low end as a staggering melody at a thumping, garage-like pace.

### Drop

A short, gargantuan breakdown leads into the drop for the second verse. The vocals carry on, and a huge lead synth joins them for extra energy.

### Breakdown

This is the passage the reviewers singled out. The rhythm changes completely, the beat slows a little, and three kicks hit at intervals against a heavily distorted bass while the voice keeps going. If you are not expecting it, Your EDM warned, it might surprise you.

### Outro

Then bassline, backing synths and lead combine for one last frenzied drive, and the track closes on another of what Your EDM called its Dadastep breakdowns, an ending Elektro Daily found abrupt.

## Concepts in Focus

In most dance tracks a {{ <concept_link name="breakdown" /> }} is a pause in which the beat thins out. Here the main breakdown is no rest: the beat slows and spaces out, so the floor has to change how it moves.

The {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }} is more conventional: a short breakdown builds tension, and the second verse lands with the full beat and a lead synth on the voice.

{{ <concept_link name="distortion" /> }} is what keeps the slow breakdown from feeling empty. A heavily distorted bass fills the gaps between those three kicks with grit, the rough, saturated sound Elektro Daily called the duo's usual sausage-fattened one. Compare how {{ <track_link source="dada-life-arrive-beautiful-leave-ugly.md" /> }}, an instrumental from the same record, handles its own breakdowns.

## Artistic Context

Dada Life are the Stockholm duo Olle Corneér and Stefan Engblom, named after the Dada art movement. Boing Clash Boom is track ten of their second album, The Rules of Dada, released on 16 October 2012 on So Much Dada and Universal, produced by the pair and written with Jon Asher and Anton Hård. Elektro Daily summed the album up as loud, catchy dance music that never takes itself too seriously.

## Impact and Reception

Released as the album's fourth single on 1 June 2013, the track came with remixes by Major Lazer, Bingo Players and Pierce Fulton. Critics disagreed about the original: Elektro Daily called it the album's standout and Your EDM one of the duo's most original tracks to date, while Dancing Astronaut found it too hard or disconnected from the rest. Listen to the breakdown and pick your side.
