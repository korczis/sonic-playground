## Genre and Mood

**Jain** recorded **Son of a Sun** with the same partner and the same small set of instruments as the songs that made her name, the pop, world and electro-pop of her debut album Zanaka. The feelings chosen for this page, playful and warm, are the album's; no published review describes this particular song, so how it carries them is for you to hear.

## Song Summary

Son of a Sun was released on 25 November 2016, a year after Zanaka, as one of the additions to the album's deluxe edition, and at the same time on a six-track release of its own, the Zanaka EP, where it is the second track. It runs three minutes and thirty-one seconds. Shazam credits the words and music to Jain alone, and the arrangement, production, recording and mixing to Yodelice, with mastering by Herb Powers. Jain plays the keyboards; Yodelice plays guitar and bass guitar.

## Concepts in Focus

Yodelice is credited not only as producer but as recording and {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} engineer. Mixing is the stage after recording, where each part is balanced, placed in the stereo field and shaped in tone; when the producer mixes his own recording, one pair of ears decides both what is played and how it is heard. Mastering, here by Herb Powers, is the final polish of the finished mix for release.

The Zanaka EP shows how a release can be built around an album's success. Next to three new songs, City, Son of a Sun and Dynabeat, it carries an extended version of Come and two {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} versions: Come by Femi Kuti and {{ <track_link source="jain-makeba.md" text="Makeba" /> }} by Dirty Ridin'. The new songs sit beside the hits they follow.

## Artistic Context

Jain is the French singer and songwriter Jeanne Galice, born in Toulouse, who grew up in Dubai, the Republic of the Congo and Abu Dhabi and learned music programming in Pointe-Noire. Yodelice discovered her demos and produced Zanaka, released on 6 November 2015; the title is Malagasy for child, a tribute to her mother's Franco-Malagasy roots. The song's ISRC belongs to the same 2015 series as City and Makeba.

## Impact and Reception

Son of a Sun was never a single, and its place on the playground is a listener's choice rather than a chart's. It is a chance to hear Jain without the weight of a hit around her. Listen to it with the credits in mind.
