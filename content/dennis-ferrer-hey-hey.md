## Genre and Mood

**Dennis Ferrer**'s **Hey Hey** is a house record that sounds like a warning before it sounds like a party. It is soulful in its chorus, hypnotic in its pulse and intense once the full beat lands, a track built for the dance floor that kept its edge when it crossed over to radio.

## Song Summary

According to TechnoMood, the track sat as a forgotten project on Ferrer's hard drive until he heard his daughter chanting "hey hey" around the house and decided it would be a hit. The finished record puts that chant next to a sung chorus by Vivien Goldman, and keeps everything else lean. The title is the hook, and Ferrer sings it himself.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A review in 34th Street Magazine in spring 2010 described the beat as "startling in its simplicity", opening on "an ominous, pulsating synth and a stream of quiet snares". Nothing yet sounds like an anthem.

### Chorus

"From that first chorus on, everything changes": Ferrer adds what the same review called a "metallic, warehousey layer of kicks", and Goldman "belts out an unexpectedly soulful chorus, peppered with Ferrer's own 'hey hey' chants". The reviewer heard in her a Crystal Waters-like 90s house diva.

### Bridge

The vocals stay unassuming until the bridge, where Goldman's brief scatting meets the kicks; the review found that pairing "arrestingly powerful".

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} does not need to be a melody. Here it is two syllables, chanted rather than sung, the same two a child repeated at home; the chorus gives it a soulful partner.

The {{ <concept_link name="kick drum" /> }} is the hinge of the arrangement. Before the first chorus the beat is all synth and soft snares; when the kick arrives with its metallic, warehouse weight, the track turns from tension to release.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Johny Mourgue, avoids the usual club footage. Defected presented it as "a highly original story" meant to deepen the listener's understanding of the song. ## Artistic Context

Ferrer is a New York producer and DJ, a veteran of soulful house who has worked with Masters at Work and Louie Vega, founded the Objektivity label and was nominated for a Grammy for a Dido remix. Hey Hey came out first on Objektivity, in late 2009, and was then licensed to Defected, which released it in 2010 with a remix EP by Crookers ({{ <track_link source="crookers-mad-kidz.md" /> }}), Vandalism, Tom De Neef and others.

## Impact and Reception

It went almost unnoticed until the Winter Music Conference in March 2010; by May it was on BBC Radio 1's A list, and the chart run that began in June took it to number 11 on the UK dance chart, number 3 on the UK independent chart and number 55 on the singles chart. Wikipedia calls it his most famous single, and TechnoMood counts it among Defected's staple records. Wait for the first chorus, and feel the floor change.
