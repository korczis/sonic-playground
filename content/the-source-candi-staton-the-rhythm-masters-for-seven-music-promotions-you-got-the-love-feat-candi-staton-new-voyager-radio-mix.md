## Genre and Mood

**The Source** featuring **Candi Staton**, "**You Got The Love**", New Voyager Radio Mix: house built around a soul and gospel voice, uplifting by nature and euphoric by design, a remix made to put a fifteen-year-old club anthem back on the radio.

## Song Summary

The song began in 1986 as a soul, disco and gospel track written by Anthony B. Stephens, Arnecia Michelle Harris and John Bellamy, recorded by Candi Staton for a direct-to-video documentary about a man trying to lose weight. Its fame came later and from the clubs. This version is its third top-ten revival: in 2006 Now Voyager remixed the song again and called the result the New Voyager Mix, which Positiva released on CD on 6 February 2006. The radio edit was the first track of the UK single, ahead of the Shapeshifters' mixes, and the record reached number 7 in the UK, number 6 in Ireland and number 8 in the Dutch Top 40.

### The Voice

What every version shares is Staton's vocal. The 2006 mix, Wikipedia notes, uses more of her lyric than the first two hit versions did.

### The Radio Edit

This is the short version, about three minutes, made for radio; the UK single also carried an Extended New Voyager Mix for the clubs.

## Concepts in Focus

The song's history is a history of the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. In the late 1980s the DJ Eren Abdullah laid Staton's a cappella over Frankie Knuckles' version of Jamie Principle's house track Your Love, a bootleg made by mixing two records. John Truelove released it on his label in 1991 as The Source featuring Candi Staton, and it reached number 4. In 1997 the Now Voyager Mix reached number 3, the song's highest position. Now Voyager was a band Truelove started in 1996.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shortens a track for radio: here the remix is cut to around three minutes so that a club record could be played between pop songs.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit is unusual here. Staton is not a guest on someone else's song: the song is hers, and the record is built around her voice.

## Artistic Context

**Candi Staton**, born in Hanceville, Alabama, in 1940, sang gospel as a girl with the Jewell Gospel Trio, became the First Lady of Southern Soul at Rick Hall's FAME Studios, topped the US R&B chart in 1976 with Young Hearts Run Free and returned to gospel in 1982. You Got The Love became her biggest-selling record in Europe. The streaming copy named here sits on a 2008 compilation, Pure Dance Party, which adds a credit to The Rhythm Masters for Seven Music Promotions; Wikipedia names the Rhythm Masters among the remixers of the 1997 release.

## Impact and Reception

The same vocal has now reached the UK top ten in three different mixes, in 1991, 1997 and 2006. Florence + the Machine took the song to number 5 in 2010, and {{ <track_link source="the-retrosettes-sister-band-you-got-the-love.md" /> }} shows it surviving far from the club. Play the 2006 radio edit loud.
