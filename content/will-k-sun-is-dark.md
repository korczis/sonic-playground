## Artistic Context

WILL K is a producer and DJ born and raised in Melbourne. His Monstercat biography says he started making music at twelve and was releasing on major electronic labels in his early teens. In 2020 he made Flip Reset with Kaskade, heard on this site remixing {{ <track_link source="mark-farina-kaskade-dream-machine-kaskade-mix.md" /> }}. The BIGSOUND festival's 2025 profile names Sun Is Dark first in his catalogue, alongside the Beatport chart-topper OoooH and Flip Reset, and mentions sets at Tomorrowland, Creamfields and Parookaville.
