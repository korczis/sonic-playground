## Genre and Mood

**Ben Khan**'s **do it right** is R&B and soul passed through electronics, and it is one of his most relaxed and groovy songs. The Line of Best Fit heard a seductive R&B and soul song with the lag of a video game: still inside Khan's jittery, glitchy world, but tilted towards a summer-sunset feeling, without the anger his glitches usually carry.

## Song Summary

Released on 11 May 2018, do it right was the second single from Khan's self-titled debut album, after 2000 Angels had announced his return in April. Khan told Complex that the song is "all about wandering", and he resisted a darker reading: it does not, he said, carry a sense of doom for him. The Line of Best Fit heard a balance of soul and electronica: a soft voice over a beat that keeps shifting. It lasts just over two minutes.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is the feel that makes a listener move, often born of tension between a steady pulse and what happens around it. Here that tension is in miniature: lag in the beat, a relaxed voice above, and a groove from the way the two settle together.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} is the stage where the recorded parts are balanced, placed and shaped into one sound. According to DIY, the album was mixed by Spike Stent and mastered by Dave Cooley, and Khan told Complex he spent two weeks with the producer Flood on two of his tracks. A busy beat under a soft vocal is the kind of balance a mix protects.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, released on 21 May 2018, was a co-direction between Khan and a friend, Salim, as Khan told Complex; he had directed the 2000 Angels video himself. He did not want it read as dystopian.

## Artistic Context

Ben Khan is a London producer and multi-instrumentalist; his last major release had been the 2015 EP 1000. After a three-year pause he returned in April 2018 with {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-2000-angels.md" /> }}, and do it right showed what The Line of Best Fit called a softer side of his sound. The album followed on 10 August 2018 on Dirty Hit, where slower moments such as {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-the-green.md" /> }} carry the same easy mood.

## Impact and Reception

The Line of Best Fit put the single on its New Music Friday playlist, and Complex premiered the video; reviewing the album, XS Noize gave it 8/10 and called do it right frankly wonderful, a track that captures a laidback groove. Put it on late and let it wander.
