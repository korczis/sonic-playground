## Genre and Mood

**Nôze** and **Wareika** meet on **"Exodus"**, an electronic house track that the one review to mention it called bluesy. The feelings named here, soulful and groovy, follow that word and the dance-floor roots of both acts, because no source describes the song in more detail.

## Song Summary

"Exodus" is the fourth of nine tracks on Dring, the album Nôze released on Get Physical Music in April 2011, and it runs just over four minutes. Before the album, a radio edit came out on 7 March 2011 on the single Dring Dring / Exodus. The guests are Wareika, whom Resident Advisor counted, like dOP on the same album, among Nôze's fellow live ensembles. The French blog Des oreilles dans Babylone gave it one sentence: the bluesy "Exodus", with the Hamburg group Wareika, is also fairly successful.

## Artistic Context

Nôze are the Parisian duo Ezechiel Pailhès and Nicolas Sfintescu. Fact reported that Dring was more instrumental and improvised, with a stronger jazz influence; Groove heard a real band with guitar, clarinet, saxophone, trombone and drums. Wareika formed in Hamburg in 2007, Jakob Seidensticker, Florian Schirmacher and Henrik Raabe, and, as Circus Company, one of their labels, describes them, play live without computers, improvising around their own material.

## Impact and Reception

Reviews of Dring were mixed: Groove heard too much folkloric mixing, while Des oreilles dans Babylone found the album a good vintage and this track one of its successes.
