## Genre and Mood

**Belleruche** put **Balance** on their 2007 debut, and it shows the London trio at their slowest and most settled. The band called their sound handmade hip-hop blues soul. HNL Hip Hop heard Balance as one of the record's slow burners, the songs that let the band really settle into their individual roles. Soulful, relaxed and reflective are this page's words for that: a song that takes its time and leaves room around each player.

## Song Summary

Balance is the fifth of eleven tracks on Turntable Soul Music, released by Tru Thoughts on 16 July 2007, and runs 4:08. The album credits all three members, Ricky Fabulous, Kathrin deBoer and DJ Modest, with the writing, production, engineering and recording, done at a place they called Flat 2. The song is older than the album: MusicBrainz lists the same recording as the last of four tracks on the Four Songs EP, which the band put out on their own Hippoflex label in 2006. HNL Hip Hop's review says deBoer sings about the polarized nature of the human experience; the lyric itself is not published, and this page quotes none of it.

## Musical Breakdown

One review describes Balance, and this guide keeps to it, claiming no tempo, key or structure beyond it.

### Main Section

According to HNL Hip Hop, the song rests on a popping riff from Ricky Fabulous under deBoer's vocal, while DJ Modest keeps time with a simple kick and snare. The same review heard him add fills of a gentle horn scratch between the verses. Where each part enters, and how the song ends, no source describes.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is in the album's title and in this song's quietest detail. The trio had no drummer: DJ Modest's decks gave them their rhythm, and here they also give them a second voice. A scratched horn between the verses works like a reply to the singer.

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} is a short figure that anchors a song. In Balance it belongs to the guitar: the popping line the review hears beside deBoer's voice. Listen for how little else it needs around it.

{{ <concept_link name="self-release" /> }} is how Belleruche began. Before Tru Thoughts signed them, they released limited 7-inch records on their own Hippoflex label and distributed them by hand at gigs; the 2006 Four Songs EP on Hippoflex already carried Balance. Compare {{ <track_link source="belleruche-13-6-35.md" /> }}, another song that made the same journey.

## Artistic Context

Belleruche formed in North London in 2005. Tru Thoughts compared their sound to Sarah Vaughan, Charlie Christian and Cut Chemist; XLR8R heard sweet soul built around jazzy guitars, handcrafted beats and a dusting of turntablism. The band released three more albums on the label before announcing its end in October 2013.

## Impact and Reception

Turntable Soul Music became the fastest-selling debut album in Tru Thoughts' history. HNL Hip Hop called it a fantastic cool-out experience that deserves repeated listening and named Balance among the slow burners where the trio's roles come clearest. In 2012 the label posted it on its YouTube jukebox channel. Listen for the scratch between the verses.
