## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** wrote the song; **My Name Is Fame - Be Svendsen Remix** is the Danish producer Be Svendsen's electronic rework of it, one of three remixes that came with her 2013 single. No review or premiere describes how this remix sounds, so the feelings, playful and quirky, are those of the song it reworks, a track DMC World cited for her humour.

## Song Summary

The original My Name Is Fame is a spoken word track: DMC World's Ben Hogwood heard a deadpan voice over a brilliant, weighty kick drum. Pilocka Krach released it on 11 February 2013 on Fame, a numbered picture-disc 7-inch on her own label Greatest Hits International, with a second track, David Bowie Will Never Die. The vinyl came with a download code for its tracks and for remixes by Sven Dohse, Philip Bader and Be Svendsen. On the digital release Be Svendsen's remix is the fourth of five tracks and lasts 3:53, against 3:30 for the radio mix, 6:41 for Sven Dohse's remix and 6:09 for Philip Bader's club edit. No source says which parts of the original it keeps.

## Concepts in Focus

## Artistic Context

According to the Amsterdam Dance Event's biography, Be Svendsen made melancholic downbeat music in the early 2000s with the Danish electronica artist Rumpistol in Luftkastel, was part of the progressive trance act Psyless Groove, founded in 2002, and in 2009 released a swing-tech EP, Caravan Adaption, under the alias Benny Berigan. Hear his house remix of an Acid Pauli piece in {{ <track_link source="acid-pauli-be-svendsen-requiem-for-a-loop-be-svendsen-remix.md" /> }}, and his own Masquerade EP from the same year as this remix in {{ <track_link source="be-svendsen-let-them-not-weep.md" /> }}. Pilocka Krach, born in Berlin-Köpenick, later put the original on Best Of (2015).
