## Genre and Mood

**Chuck Roberts** was called the voice of house music, and **In the Beginning** carries the words that earned him the name. It belongs to house as its scripture rather than as a dance track: a spoken sermon, delivered with the cadence of church, about what house music is. The mood is spiritual, uplifting and, by now, iconic.

## Song Summary

The speech began on My House, a 1987 track by Rhythm Controll. Mixmag tells how the Chicago house figure Tony Lewis phoned Roberts in the middle of the night: he had finished a track and wanted someone with a church background to explain what house music is. Roberts opened with "In the beginning, there was Jack", and the sermon that follows, as Michaelangelo Matos summed it up, presents house music as a universal language spoken and understood by all. In 2011 Trax Records put a recording titled In the Beginning, credited to Roberts, first on its 25th anniversary collection; MusicBrainz times it at under two minutes. No official upload of this recording could be found, so this page has no player.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes the 2011 recording, and no source says whether it is the 1987 vocal, a new reading or a new mix, so this breakdown stays with the speech, which every source describes.

### The Opening

The first words are the title: in the beginning, there was Jack. Jack was a key word of early Chicago house, and the sermon makes it the origin of everything.

### The Sermon

What follows is a definition of house music spoken like scripture by a man who had started singing at religious events at the age of six.

### The Place on the Album

Placed as track one, the speech works as an overture to the songs Trax chose for its anniversary.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="spoken word" /> }} here is not deadpan but preaching. Roberts does not sing a melody; he testifies, and the authority of the voice is the point.

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} made the speech immortal. 5 Magazine called it the most sampled speech in house music history, used in hundreds of songs; Resident Advisor names Fingers Inc.'s Can You Feel It and records by Todd Terry, Tuff Jam and Julio Bashmore. Compare a later spoken voice over house in {{ <track_link source="zwicker-dragon-fly-feat-billy-oden-album-version.md" /> }}.

{{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} is the world the sermon speaks to, the dancers who know what jack means. In 2018 Roberts set out his own 10 Commandments of House for 5 Magazine, from courtesy on the floor to supporting local artists.

## Artistic Context

The Chicago label Trax Records released the collection on 18 October 2011 as a two-disc set with a documentary, The House That Trax Built; Jorge Cruz curated it. In 2017 Roberts surprised the Chosen Few Picnic during Terry Hunter's set, which led to a tour with Hunter and to In The Beginning (There Was Jack), produced by Hunter with Monique Bingham, on Ultra.

## Impact and Reception

Roberts died on 6 June 2024, aged 66. Defected remembered him as the writer of one of house music's most iconic vocals. Find the speech and listen to how many records you already knew it from.
