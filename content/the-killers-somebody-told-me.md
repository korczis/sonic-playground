## Genre and Mood

**The Killers**' **Somebody Told Me** has been filed as alternative rock, new wave, dance-rock and indie dance. Blender heard a superb blaze of synthesizers and guitars, Rolling Stone a nightclub anthem in the making: energetic, catchy, and playful in the way good gossip is.

## Song Summary

Brandon Flowers told Rolling Stone that the song is the story of trying to meet someone in a club. The narrator has heard a rumour, and the chorus delivers it as a punchline Blender called the year's best one-liner, a tangle of boyfriends and girlfriends. All four members are credited as writers; the band recorded it with Jeff Saltzman for its debut album, Hot Fuss.

## Musical Breakdown

The song is in B-flat minor; the chords below come from published analysis, and no source times the sections.

### Intro

A lone B-flat minor chord states the key before the band locks in.

### Verse 1

A short B-flat minor, E-flat minor, B-flat pattern, played twice; the pre-chorus lifts through G-flat and A-flat and falls back, winding the spring.

### Chorus

B-flat minor, G-flat, A-flat, F. The first time through, the final F is cut off, a small jolt that keeps the song from settling.

### Bridge

After a second verse and chorus, the bridge strays well away from the home key before the last chorus.

## Concepts in Focus

Each section has its own short {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }}, and the change from one loop to the next is what you feel as a lift; the missing F shows how much a progression can do by leaving something out.

The structure is plain {{ <concept_link name="verse-chorus form" /> }}, with the pre-chorus raising the harmony so the chorus lands as a release.

Flowers' voice carries a short {{ <concept_link name="delay" /> }}: engineer Mark Needham recalled that Saltzman used the Echo Farm plug-in's default setting, a slight overdrive and an 84 ms delay, on nearly the whole record.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Brett Simon in February 2004, shows the band playing in a moonlit desert before a giant LED screen. It pays homage to New Order's Crystal, whose fictional on-screen band gave the Killers their name.

## Artistic Context

Hot Fuss, released by Island Records in June 2004, draws mostly on new wave and post-punk; Somebody Told Me was its second official single, after Mr. Brightside. The album topped the UK chart and had sold more than seven million copies by 2012. It shares its year with {{ <track_link source="franz-ferdinand-take-me-out.md" /> }}, another debut that pulled guitar music toward dancing.

## Impact and Reception

First released in March 2004, the single reached number 28 in the UK, the band's first top-40 hit; re-released in January 2005, it climbed to number three. Billboard found it as addictive as nickel slots, and Paste and American Songwriter later ranked it among the band's best songs. Play it loud and count the chords.
