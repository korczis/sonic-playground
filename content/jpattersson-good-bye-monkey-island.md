## Genre and Mood

**JPattersson**'s **Good Bye Monkey Island** is the seventh track of his 2020 album Mood, which Discogs files under dub, downtempo and deep house. The feelings here, relaxed and dreamy, follow the album's own description, which runs from tropical tipsiness to hypnotic depth. No review describes this track on its own, so this page stays close to what the record and its makers say.

## Song Summary

Good Bye Monkey Island runs 5:58 on the album and, like all of Mood, was written and produced by Johann Beger, the man behind JPattersson. The album's notes set the scene rather than the music: bare feet in warm sand, a breeze, the moment before a jump into clear water. They also say that his trumpet and his voice still run through his sound, while this third album sounds new again. The track has a second life on the same record: Mood closes with a remix of it by Kermesse.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }} is the root of JPattersson's style. It began in Jamaica as a way of remixing records down to drums and bass, with echo and drop-outs; he calls his own version progadub, because, as his press text puts it, he is not from Jamaica but from East Germany. Hear how he applied it four years earlier on {{ <track_link source="jpatterson-elevator.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} usually comes out after the original. Here both are on one album: the original as track seven and Kermesse's remix, at 6:49, as track nine. The remix appeared again on Mood - Remixes in October 2020, beside versions of other album tracks by Miret, Dole & Kom and others.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} on the artist's own channel, published in September 2020, is an animation by Julian Quitsch, with artwork by Elli Jäger, who is also credited with the album's artwork. It runs about five seconds longer than the album track.

## Artistic Context

JPattersson is the Leipzig producer Johann Beger. Mood, his third album, came out on CD and digitally on 3 July 2020 on 3000° Grad, with guests on two other tracks, Lore and Guacáyo. Dole & Kom, who had remixed Elevator in 2016, did the pre-mastering. Its notes call it an album to hear from beginning to end, and at the same time made for the dance floor.
