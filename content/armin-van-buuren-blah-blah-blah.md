## Genre and Mood

**Armin van Buuren**'s **Blah Blah Blah** is trance turned blunt. Wikipedia files it as psychedelic trance, Apple Music as trance, and EDM Sauce called it minimalist psytrance: energetic and intense, with a playful streak that comes from a refrain made of nonsense.

## Song Summary

The idea fits in one breath. A child's voice chants that all we ever hear from you is blah blah blah, and that we don't care what they say; the track loops that phrase over a hard beat for three minutes. The voice belongs to Aidan, the younger son of co-writer Andrew Bullimore, who wrote it with Armin and Josh Record. Asked what inspired it, Armin replied with a deliberately nonsensical answer built from the same three words.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A State of Trance, Armin's own platform, described a foreboding intro, and EDM Sauce found the chant a fitting way in.

### Main Section

The weight sits low: what A State of Trance called a devastating kick-bass combination, under a beat EDM Sauce heard as driving, relentless, bouncing and pounding. The child's voice does not go away; it is looped throughout.

### Outro

No source describes how the three-minute single ends; the extended mix, at 6:06, gives DJs a longer run at the same material.

## Concepts in Focus

The track is a lesson in how a {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} can be almost no melody at all. A short chanted phrase with a sneer in its words is easy to remember and to shout, and A State of Trance reported fans going wild to it from Miami to Amsterdam to Sydney.

The hook is also a {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }}. EDM Sauce noted that the sample of the child's voice is looped throughout and stays constant, and confessed it grew a bit annoying past the halfway point. That is the trade-off of loop-based dance music: insistence that can hypnotise a festival field or tire a listener at home.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} package followed, with versions by Bassjackers, Brennan Heart and Toneshifterz, Alyx Ander, Kid Comet, Zany and Tru Concept, plus an a cappella of the chant alone. Each takes the same voice into another style, much as the trumpet-and-psytrance meeting of {{ <track_link source="timmy-trumpet-vini-vici-thunder.md" /> }} bends a kick-and-bass formula.

## Artistic Context

Armin van Buuren, five times voted the world's number one DJ, premiered the track at Ultra Music Festival in Miami in 2018, after his psytrance collaboration Great Spirit with Vini Vici. Armada Music released it on 18 May 2018 as the lead track of the Blah Blah Blah EP, with The Last Dancer, Just As You Are and Popcorn, and it later appeared on the album Balance.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 11 in Germany and topped Hungary's dance chart, where it was the dance song of 2019, and earned platinum in France and Denmark. Ajax plays an excerpt after goals at its training ground, the trailer for Michael Bay's 6 Underground used it, and the official lyric video has passed 800 million views. Play it loud once and decide whether the chant is a joke, a provocation or both.
