## Genre and Mood

**Ben Khan**'s **2000 angels** sits where electro-pop meets funk and soul, and the critics who first heard it reached for the future to describe it. Grimy Goods called it dripping with futuristic-soul grooves; the same writer heard it, together with the single that followed, as a neo-80s cinema soundtrack. Groovy and futuristic are the feelings here: a body-moving rhythm in sounds from a film set decades ahead.

## Song Summary

The song came out in April 2018 with its video and announced Khan's debut album. No review spells out its lyric, so this page does not guess at it. The sources agree on the sound and the package: a groove-led track that opens the album, and an eerie, dystopian video Khan directed himself. The Guardian, reviewing the album, suggested that 2000 angels was the one song on it with the hammer blow of a big pop melody, the natural way into the record.

## Concepts in Focus

The track is built on a {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}: the interlocking feel of beat, bass and the other rhythm parts that makes a body move before the mind has caught up. It is the thread from Khan's earlier funk singles to this album.

The album credits show the role of the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. Hot Press and Grimy Goods both reported that Khan played every instrument himself, yet the record was produced with Flood, known for PJ Harvey, Depeche Mode and Nine Inch Nails, mixed by Spike Stent and mastered by Dave Cooley. A producer shapes the sound and the decisions, not only the playing. Hear another album track made the same way in {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-monsoon-daydream.md" /> }}.

Khan directed the {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} himself. Noisey called it an almost auteur-like piece of work and compared its futuristic, dystopian look to Justin Timberlake's comeback of the same year.

## Artistic Context

Ben Khan is a London singer, songwriter and producer whose earlier singles include {{ <track_link source="ben-khan-savage.md" /> }}, from his 2014 1992 EP. 2000 angels arrived on 13 April 2018 via Dirty Hit as the lead single of his self-titled debut, which was released on 10 August 2018 and opens with this song.

## Impact and Reception

The album divided critics, from 8.5 out of 10 at The Line of Best Fit to three stars at The Skinny, but they agreed on this song. The Skinny named it among the three tracks to hear, XSNOIZE called it now iconic, and the Grimy Goods writer named it probably her favourite Khan track yet. Play it, then the album from the start.
