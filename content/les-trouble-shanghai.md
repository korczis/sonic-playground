## Concepts in Focus

Shanghai is a clear case of {{ <concept_link name="self-release" /> }}. There is no label in its credits. The artist holds the rights to the recording, the composition has no publisher, and a digital distributor places the file on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, where it appears as an automatically generated upload on a Topic channel. That route lets an artist put out singles on its own schedule, without a label's release plan.

The same credits make Les Trouble its own {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. On a label release, the producer is often a second person who shapes the sound of someone else's song. Here writer, producer and owner are one name, so every decision about the sound belongs to the artist. Compare a similar solo route in {{ <track_link source="findmyname-rebel.md" /> }}.
