## Genre and Mood

**HVOB**'s **Always Like This** is a cover that moves a British indie song into deep house and electronic music. The label's notes describe warm synth pads over soft beats; melancholic and warm are this page's two words for that pairing, and both come from those notes rather than from a sound the page claims to have measured.

## Song Summary

The song belongs to Bombay Bicycle Club. Jack Steadman wrote it, and the London band released it on 13 April 2009 as the first single from their debut album I Had the Blues But I Shook Them Loose; it reached number 97, their first entry in the UK singles chart. Four years later the Viennese duo HVOB recorded it for their self-titled debut album, released by the Berlin label Stil vor Talent on 8 March 2013, where it is the fifth track and runs 5:25. In the words of the announcement, Anna Müller's distinctive voice puts an entirely new spin on the melancholy of the original.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} keeps a song's words and melody and changes everything around them. Here a guitar band's single becomes an electronic track, and HVOB credit the writing to Steadman while producing it themselves.

The single, the duo's third from the album, came out on 26 April 2013 with two versions for the dance floor: a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by Oliver Koletzki, the label's co-founder, which the announcement calls powerful, emotive and melodic, and one by Andhim.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was made by lichterloh.tv, the duo's regular visual collaborators, with Andrew Mezvinsky as artistic researcher and co-director. It is built on drawings by Claude Lorrain, Hieronymus Bosch, Francisco Goya, Rembrandt, Albrecht Dürer and Jan Both. The track was also filmed live for They Shoot Music at the Pratersauna club in Vienna, with live drums and lichterloh's visuals.

## Artistic Context

HVOB, short for Her Voice Over Boys, are Anna Müller and Paul Wallner, who formed the duo in Vienna in 2012 after playing in the band Herbstrock. Oliver Koletzki found them through their SoundCloud uploads and signed them to Stil vor Talent. On this record Müller composed, produced and sang, and Wallner composed and did the final production. Compare {{ <track_link source="hvob-azrael.md" /> }}, from their second album.

## Impact and Reception

Andhim's remix went on to mix compilations such as Get Physical's Body Language Vol. 14 and Saint-Germain-des-Prés Café Vol. 16, according to MusicBrainz. Start with the original album version, then hear how the remixes pull it back to the club.
