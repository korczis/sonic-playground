## Genre and Mood

**Andreas Henneberg** released **Skirmish** in 2013, and Discogs files the release under tech house and deep house; Apple Music's later listing calls it house. No published review describes the track, so its two feelings rest on that classification and on how Henneberg describes his own music: groovy as the plainest word for a house track, hypnotic for seven minutes of club music between techno, minimal and house. Read both as context, not as a listener's account.

## Song Summary

Skirmish is the second of two tracks on Driss EP, released by the Berlin label Heinz Music on 3 October 2013 with catalogue number HM010, as digital files only. The title track runs seven minutes and fourteen seconds, Skirmish seven minutes and five. YouTube's art track credits Henneberg alone as composer. The track has no lyrics that any source records.

## Concepts in Focus

A track of seven minutes is long for a song and normal for a {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }}, the continuous set in which a DJ blends one record into the next. Skirmish travelled the way such tracks do: in 2014 it appeared on On #4, a compilation selected by Marcus Meinhardt for the label Hensen, and in 2019 on Heinz Music Best Of, Vol. 1, the label's own retrospective.

It also belongs to {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} through its maker. Henneberg's biography says he sums up techno, minimal and house in his live and DJ sets in a very compatible and functional way: music judged on the floor, where a track like this one has to keep a room moving rather than tell a story.

## Artistic Context

Henneberg was born in Berlin and has worked there since the mid-1990s as a producer, label owner, mastering engineer, DJ and live act. He founded the labels SNOE, Voltage Musique, Hennon and Cascandy and is part of The Glitz. Compare {{ <track_link source="dirty-doering-casino-aquatique.md" /> }}, another Berlin tech house track of the early 2010s.

## Impact and Reception

Skirmish did not chart, as far as any source records; its reception is the list of compilations that took it on and the reissue twelve years later. Play it from the start and let the full seven minutes run.
