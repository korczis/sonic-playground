## Genre and Mood

**KlangKuenstler** and **Alice Phoebe Lou** end the new material of That's Me with **Amsterdam**; only a remix follows it. Sunday Best, the London label that released the album, describes its sound as warm and hypnotic, says KlangKuenstler stays true to his tech house roots while drawing on classical music, jazz and 1960s film scores, and calls the result upbeat electro house. Those words are the label's, and they describe the whole album: no published review describes Amsterdam on its own, so this page takes its genres and feelings from that copy and claims no more.

## Song Summary

Amsterdam is the tenth of eleven tracks on That's Me, released on Bandcamp on 18 November 2014, and runs five minutes and eleven seconds. It is credited as featuring Alice Phoebe Lou, the singer of the album's first single Man On The Moon. MusicBrainz credits the song to both of them as composers and to Lou as the lyricist. No lyrics are published with the release, so this review does not describe what she sings.

No published review or timed analysis describes how Amsterdam is built, so this review gives no map of its parts. ## Artistic Context

KlangKuenstler is the alias of Michael Korb, born in 1990 in Füssen in the Allgäu, who played mainly house until 2018 and has since turned increasingly to techno, usually described as hard techno. Alice Phoebe Lou, born in 1993 in Kommetjie near Cape Town, took a gap year in Europe after school, living first in Amsterdam and then in Berlin, where she busked. Her own debut album Orbit (2016) has a song called Amsterdam credited to her; no source I found says whether the two share more than a title.
