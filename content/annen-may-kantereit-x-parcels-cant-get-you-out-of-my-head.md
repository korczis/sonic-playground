## Genre and Mood

**AnnenMayKantereit** and **Parcels** recast Kylie Minogue's 2001 pop hit **Can't Get You Out of My Head** as a band performance. Their cover strips away the cool electronic pulse of the original and puts voices and plain instrumentation in its place, so the song becomes more melancholic and reflective while keeping its obsessive core.

## Song Summary

The collaboration brings together two bands with Berlin and Cologne in common but very different sounds: AnnenMayKantereit, a Cologne rock band built around Henning May's distinctive rough voice, and Parcels, an Australian electropop group based in Berlin and known for close, three-part vocals. The arrangement is minimal and lets the lyric's fixation come forward. The result is more intimate than the original, a pop classic sung as if at the end of a long night.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro: A Soft Beginning

The song opens gently, with a relaxed, intimate tone. The acoustic band sound immediately distinguishes this version from the electronic original.

### Verse 1: Soulful Vocals

Henning May's rough, resonant voice takes centre stage, bringing a raw, unpolished quality to lines first sung with breathy restraint. The backing stays minimal so the voice can carry the song.

### Chorus: A Haunting Interpretation

As the chorus unfolds, Parcels' voices join and surround the lead, giving the familiar refrain a haunting, communal quality. The simplicity of the instrumentation draws attention to the repeated lines.

### Bridge: Building Emotion

The song's darker middle section, with its talk of a secret it cannot let go, gains weight in this slower setting before the arrangement returns to its stripped-back sound.

### Outro: A Fading Echo

The cover ends quietly, voices and instruments easing away, leaving the title phrase hanging in the air.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} keeps the song, its melody and words, and is free to change everything else. Here the change is total: Kylie Minogue's record, written by Cathy Dennis and Rob Davis, is famous for its cold, hypnotic electronic production, and this version replaces it with a band in a room. Listen for how the same lyric of fixation sounds less like seduction and more like confession.

{{ <concept_link name="vocal harmony" /> }} is what Parcels bring. Their sound leans on close three-part vocals in the spirit of seventies disco and soft rock, and set against Henning May's grainy lead, those smoother voices make the obsession sound shared, almost like a chorus of people who cannot stop thinking about the same person.

The original's most famous part is its wordless {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}, the la-la-la line that does not need lyrics to stick. A good test of any cover is whether that hook survives; here it does, carried by human voices instead of a synthesizer, which makes it warmer and a little sadder.

## Lyrical Themes

The lyrics remain faithful to the original, reflecting themes of:

- Obsession and fixation on someone
- The inability to move on from a deep emotional connection
- The all-consuming nature of desire

The cover's more introspective delivery brings out the melancholic side of these emotions.

## Cultural Impact

Kylie Minogue's **Can't Get You Out of My Head**, the lead single from her 2001 album Fever, became one of the defining pop hits of the early 2000s. AnnenMayKantereit published this cover on their YouTube channel in December 2019, filmed by Lenny Rothenberg and Martin Lamberty of Cardinal Sessions and recorded and mixed by Fabian Langer. It fits a habit of the band: they sing mostly in German but occasionally release English covers, and in 2022 their version of Suzanne Vega's Tom's Diner with Giant Rooks gave them their first UK chart entry.

## Legacy

The cover shows how a pop classic can take on another temperament entirely. By turning a high-gloss electronic single into an unvarnished band performance, AnnenMayKantereit and Parcels prove that a great melody can carry a song through any change of costume. Play the original straight after it and notice which version stays in your head.
