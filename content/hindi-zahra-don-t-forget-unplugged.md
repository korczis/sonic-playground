## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **Don't Forget - Unplugged** takes a quiet song from her debut album and records it again without the studio around it, where world music, folk and acoustic performance meet. It is filed here as intimate and longing: intimate because of what an unplugged session is, longing because the song is a plea not to be forgotten. We found no review of this particular take, and the page says so wherever it matters.

## Song Summary

Don't Forget first appeared as the tenth of the eleven songs on Handmade in 2010, where it runs 3:47. The streaming metadata credits its words and music to Zahra, who also produced it. M Music & Musicians heard her on that album as a teacher of less experienced lovers, with Don't Forget teaching tenderness. This take is a separate recording, made a year later for the deluxe edition, and at 2:39 it is 68 seconds shorter.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or session note describes the performance part by part, so this breakdown stays with what is documented.

### The Session

The take comes from the Unplugged Session, six tracks recorded at Studio Ferber in Paris on 28 March 2011, according to the deluxe CD's notes. Don't Forget is the sixth and last of them, after Ahiawa, The Man I Love, Beautiful Tango, Oursoul and He Needs Me.

### The Song Without Its Frame

On the studio version Camille Ablard played cello, Abdoulaye Traore, Thomas Naïm and Zahra played guitars, and Zahra and Thierry Moutoussamy played percussion. The deluxe notes do not list the session's players. Listen for which of those colours survive, and what the shorter length removes.

### The Close

As the final track of the deluxe Handmade, this take ends the whole album.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="unplugged" /> }} recording strips a song back to acoustic instruments and voice. The BBC's Colin Irwin, reviewing Handmade in 2010, called the studio Don't Forget lilting and heard a touch of the McGarrigles in it, but found the album's production overly sophisticated; a session like this is one answer to that.

It is also an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same song, a different performance. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-don-t-forget-remastered-version.md" /> }}, the studio original.

And it is a {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }}: absent from the 2010 album, it arrived on Handmade (Bonus Version) on 29 April 2011 and on Until the Next Journey that July.

## Artistic Context

Hindi Zahra was born in Khouribga, Morocco, in 1979, has lived in France since 1993, and began her career in Paris as a soul backing singer. She produced, arranged and mixed Handmade herself.

## Impact and Reception

Handmade won the Prix Constantin on 12 November 2010 and the Victoire de la musique for world music album on 9 February 2011, weeks before this session, and it went gold in France. Play the studio version first, then this one, and listen to how much of the song is still there.
