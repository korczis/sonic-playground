## Artistic Context

According to Eskimo Recordings, Tepat Huleux and Giac Di Falco used to slip out of their homes in Lille around the turn of the millennium, crossing the Belgian border to dance all night at the parties that gave birth to that label. In a 2016 Liveurope interview the writer called them former members of the Auto Band, and the pair said they try to stay as naive as possible when they compose: a track can take a month or an hour. Spectrum of Love came out on Alpage Records in October 2016, their first album.
