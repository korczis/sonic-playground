## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**'s **Laughter In Cairo** is six minutes and twenty-one seconds of electro and electronic music, the second-longest of the seventeen tracks on the Bandcamp edition of her 2017 double album. The feelings named here, gritty and playful, follow how her label describes her style rather than any review: no critic we found wrote about this track.

## Song Summary

Laughter In Cairo is the third track of Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, and the distributor's listing places it on Sugar Cane, one of the album's two halves, dated 17 April 2017 and issued on her own label, Greatest Hits International. According to the album's Bandcamp credits, every Sugar Cane track was written and produced by Pilocka Krach herself in 2014 and 2015, in Biesenthal, at Sternhagen Gut and in Bohemia, while the Lost Amigos half came from places that include Tulum, Detroit's Submerge and Berlin-Marienfelde. No source explains the title or prints a lyric, so this page does not say whose laughter it is.

### Main Section

Her label sums up the record's sound as electro post-rave simplicity, built either from raw sampling cuts or from deep, growling synthesis. Over six minutes, listen for which of the two leads, and for how a track of this length keeps moving with few elements. Whether there is a voice, or any laughter at all, is for you to hear.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" text="Sampling" /> }} is one pole of the sound her label describes: cut, recorded fragments with rough edges, reused as material. The other is the {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }}, here meant less for melody than for weight and growl. The same pair runs through {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach_la_luna-y-el-sol.md" /> }}, from the same album.

The album itself is a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }}: her label calls it a conceptually framed double vinyl and a diary of two years on tour, with Mexico, Detroit and her Berlin roots as fixed points. The vinyl-and-CD edition even plays the material twice, in two running orders, so Laughter In Cairo appears both as track 3 and as track 20.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick and grew up in Berlin-Mitte; her publisher calls her the electric rock star from Berlin. With a grant from Musicboard Berlin she went to Detroit in 2016 and connected with the techno collective Underground Resistance, and she releases her music on Greatest Hits International. Two years before Sugar Cane, the Berlin label Monika Enterprise had released her Best Of, with tracks such as {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-my-name-is-fame.md" /> }}. Her label presents Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos as her band and as her own alter ego.

## Impact and Reception

No review of the album or of this track turned up; what survives is label copy and record-shop listings, one of which files the vinyl under house and names Acid Pauli and Britta Arnold as similar artists. The Lost Amigos name returned in 2020 on a single of its own, {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-the-lost-amigos-ay-caramba.md" /> }}. Laughter In Cairo remains a long, unexplained title in the middle of a travel diary. Play it loud and decide what the joke is.
