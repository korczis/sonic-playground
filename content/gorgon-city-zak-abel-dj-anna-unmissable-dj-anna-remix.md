## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** wrote **Unmissable** as a bright, soulful pop-house single with **Zak Abel** on vocals, and **DJ Anna** takes it somewhere much darker in her remix. Deep house warmth, a house pulse and a techno producer's sense of pressure meet in a mood that is hypnotic, groovy and full of longing, the original's romance recast for a club at two in the morning.

## Song Summary

The song is about someone you simply cannot overlook, and the original delivers that feeling with an upbeat pop shine. Anna slows the excitement into a heavy, rolling bassline and stretches the track to almost eight minutes, using Zak Abel's soulful voice in fragments and echoes rather than as a full pop performance. The memorable detail is the bass, deep and relentless, carrying the whole remix on its shoulders.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A long, DJ-ready opening lays down kick, hats and a dark atmosphere, patient and focused, with no hint yet of the pop song inside.

### Build-up

The rolling bassline arrives and the groove deepens, each loop adding a little more weight and a little more tension.

### Main Section

Fragments of Zak Abel's vocal float in, chopped and echoed, turning the hook into a memory drifting through the mix rather than a chorus to sing along to.

### Bridge

A breakdown pulls the drums away and lets the voice and pads hang in the dark, a moment of longing before the floor gets its release.

### Climax

The bass and drums slam back together with the vocal snippets layered on top, the remix at full force, hypnotic and physical.

### Outro

The elements peel away slowly, leaving a long rhythmic tail for the next record to blend into.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City are the London production duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott. "Unmissable" was the fourth single from their 2014 debut album *Sirens*, and this remix appeared on *Sirens (Remixes)*, released in April 2015 on Virgin EMI. DJ Anna, the Brazilian DJ and producer now known simply as ANNA, has built her reputation on deep, driving techno and house, and she brings that sound here.

## Impact and Reception

When "Unmissable" came out as a single, reviewers noted that the ANNA remix led the pack of alternative versions, pulling the track back toward deep house with its heavy bassline while the original stayed pop-leaning. It is on this playground as a perfect example of a remix that finds a second song inside the first. Give it the full eight minutes and let the bassline do the talking.
