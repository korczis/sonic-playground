## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** wrote **Unmissable** as a bright, soulful pop-house single with **Zak Abel** on vocals, and **DJ Anna** takes it somewhere much darker in her remix. Deep house warmth, a house pulse and a techno producer's sense of pressure meet in a mood that is hypnotic, groovy and full of longing, the original's romance recast for a club at two in the morning.

## Song Summary

The song is about someone you simply cannot overlook, and the original delivers that feeling with an upbeat, radio-friendly shine. Anna brings it, in Your EDM's words, back to the deep end of house music with a heavy bassline and stretches it to almost eight minutes, using Zak Abel's soulful voice in fragments and echoes rather than as a full pop performance. The memorable detail is the bass, deep and relentless, carrying the whole remix on its shoulders.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A long, DJ-ready opening lays down kick, hats and a dark atmosphere, patient and focused, with no hint yet of the pop song inside.

### Build-up

The rolling bassline arrives and the groove deepens, each loop adding a little more weight and a little more tension.

### Main Section

Fragments of Zak Abel's vocal float in, chopped and echoed, turning the hook into a memory drifting through the mix rather than a chorus to sing along to.

### Bridge

The drums pull back and let the voice hang in the dark for a moment of longing before the floor gets its release.

### Climax

The bass and drums return together with the vocal snippets layered on top, the remix at full force, hypnotic and physical.

### Outro

The elements peel away slowly, leaving a long rhythmic tail for the next record to blend into.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording's parts into something new, and this one shows how far a remix can move a song along the line from radio to club. The original, written by Gorgon City with Jonny Coffer and James Napier, is a deep-house and garage-flavoured pop single of three and a half minutes; ANNA keeps the vocal but treats it as raw material.

What replaces the pop structure is the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}. Heavy and rolling, it is the remix's real hook: the part that moves bodies and holds the attention while the vocal comes and goes.

The length makes it an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} in spirit: long, sparse intro and outro sections exist so that a DJ can blend it with the records before and after. Compare how another remixer handles a Gorgon City single on the same remix album in {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-mnek-will-saul-ready-for-your-love-close-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City are the London production duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott. "Unmissable" was released on 28 August 2014 on Virgin EMI as a single from their debut album *Sirens* and reached number 19 in the UK and number 5 on the UK dance chart. This remix appeared on *Sirens (Remixes)*, released on 7 April 2015. DJ Anna, the Brazilian DJ and producer Ana Miranda, now known simply as ANNA, grew up in Amparo in São Paulo state, where her father owned a club, began DJing as a teenager and started producing after moving to the Czech Republic in 2009; she later became one of techno's leading names, releasing on Kompakt and Drumcode.

## Impact and Reception

When "Unmissable" came out as a single, Your EDM noted that the ANNA remix led the pack of alternative versions, contrasting its heavy bassline with the original's pop-ish vibe. It is on this playground as an example of a remix that finds a second song inside the first. Give it the full eight minutes and let the bassline do the talking.
