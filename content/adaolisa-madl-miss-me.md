## Genre and Mood

**adaolisa** and **madL** made **Miss Me** an R&B song with soul in its voice: catchy on the surface, defiant underneath, and empowering in what it says. FM4 introduced the singer in 2019 as Ada, the best and newest R&B discovery, and called this track fresh and crisp, "euer bester Trip in den Kopf-Sommer", your best trip into a summer of the mind.

## Song Summary

Miss Me is a single from 2019, a little under three minutes long. FM4's Soundpark described it as a deep and catchy song that producer madL conjured up, one that puts the voice of a strong, independent woman in the foreground. The lyric leaves no chance, in FM4's words, for dull machos who apologise afterwards for the same nonsense every time. There is no official upload of the song available to embed here, so this page shows its artwork and relies on what FM4 wrote.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes a recording from idea to finished track. In R&B the producer often builds the whole instrumental around a singer, and here the credit says so openly: the song is billed to adaolisa and madL together. They are school friends who discovered the R&B of the late 1990s and 2000s together, and their first recordings were made with her singing in his home studio.

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the idea that makes a song stick. FM4's word for Miss Me was eingängig, catchy, and for a break-up song that matters: a line that is easy to sing back turns private resolve into something shared. Compare the bite of {{ <track_link source="luca-malina-fckrz.md" /> }}, another Viennese song about the darker side of a relationship.

## Artistic Context

adaolisa is the daughter of an Austrian and a Nigerian. She grew up in Johannesburg, lived in Salzburg for a few years and moved to Vienna to work on her music and study art. Her first single, Bae Privileges, came out in 2019. With madL she later found a home at Villa Lala in Vienna, where the two write and produce songs for other musicians as well as their own, in styles her biography names as neo-soul, pop and R&B.

## Impact and Reception

FM4 picked up Miss Me twice in November 2019. Two years later her single Fineboy went to number 1 on the FM4 charts and she was the station's Soundpark Act of the Month, a singer who, FM4 noted, decidedly refuses to play in front of an audience. It is here because it was among the owner's most played songs of 2021. Find it under her name and listen for the voice up front.
