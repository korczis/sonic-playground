## Genre and Mood

**Elderbrook** works where electronic dance music meets songwriting, and **Old Friend** shows both sides: a tech-house groove underneath, an electro-pop vocal hook on top. He called it a more upbeat song, and it is, but the nostalgia in its subject keeps it from being simply cheerful.

## Song Summary

Elderbrook described Old Friend as "a more upbeat song about seeing and being around people you used to know", and said the lyric is about how, when that happens, you get pulled back into old habits you thought you had left behind. It came out at the end of November 2018 as the title track and single of a four-track EP, with Sleepwalking, Capricorn and Bird Song, the last written with Totally Enormous Extinct Dinosaurs. The Line of Best Fit's Thomas Harvey heard tightly crafted songwriting and rhythmic vocal melodies, with layered vocals pushing and pulling the emotion from section to section. The video was directed by Harry Butt.

## Musical Breakdown

EDM Reviewer described the track in order; no source gives times.

### Intro

Old Friend starts with Elderbrook's voice. A repetitive but catchy melody adds to the harmonies, and without much complexity the opening runs swiftly towards the drop.

### Drop

The drop is tech house: a shuffling bassline with a riffing synth as its companion, and percussion adding spice throughout. EDM Reviewer heard the same distinctive tone as his earlier hit Cola, yet fresh.

### Crescendo

The release announcement described production that builds layers of sound until it reaches a thumping crescendo.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part you cannot shake off, and here it is the vocal melody, insistent in the release's own word and repeated until it becomes the song's identity. For a lyric about being pulled back into old habits, a melody that keeps coming back is apt.

The {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} is short. The opening heads straight for the {{ <concept_link name="drop" /> }}, the point where the track turns from song to dance music and the groove takes over from the voice.

That groove runs on its {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}. A shuffling bass rolls with an uneven, swung feel rather than sitting squarely on the beat, which keeps a simple pattern moving. Compare {{ <track_link source="camelphat-elderbrook-cola-club-mix.md" /> }}, where a bouncing bass line carries his voice in a similar way.

## Artistic Context

Elderbrook is Alexander Kotz, an English electronic musician and DJ born in 1992, who started in an indie band and as a folky acoustic singer-songwriter before university drew him into electronic dance music. His breakthrough was Cola with CamelPhat, a number one on the US Dance Club Songs chart and a Grammy nominee for Best Dance Recording. Through 2018 he toured as a guest of Rüfüs Du Sol, Jungle and Bonobo. Parlophone later released his debut album, Why Do We Shake in the Cold?, in 2020.

## Impact and Reception

EDM Reviewer scored Old Friend 78 out of 100 and called it a radio hit that could also impress club audiences; The Line of Best Fit heard a tasteful blend of electronic music and left-of-centre pop. His YouTube channel also carries an official MK remix and a live acoustic version. Hear the original first, and notice how quickly it gets to the point.
