## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz**' **Clint Eastwood**, with **Del the Funky Homosapien** on the verses, is the song that introduced the virtual band in 2001, and Wikipedia describes it as a mix of electronic music, dub, hip-hop and rock. It is groovy and a little dark: a loping beat, a melodica line out of a western, a cemetery in its video.

## Song Summary

The song is named after the actor because of its similarity to the theme of The Good, the Bad and the Ugly, and the melodica's notes are based on the famous yell in that theme. Albarn added that the band, recording in Jamaica and listening to dancehall, imagined Clint Eastwood as a cool moniker.

The British group Phi Life Cypher had recorded the verses, but Dan the Automator asked Del to write his own during the Deltron 3030 sessions; Del told SPIN he did it in thirty minutes while reading How to Write a Hit Song.

## Musical Breakdown

No source gives a timed analysis of the album recording, so the parts below follow what the recording notes and interviews describe.

### The Beat

The rhythm is the first preset of a Suzuki Omnichord; in 2023 Albarn showed Zane Lowe that it is Rock 1, then pressed the fill button. A drum machine plays the drums, a Moog Rogue and a Roland JV the bass, with piano and a Solina String Ensemble.

### Verses and Chorus

Del raps the verses and Albarn sings the chorus, with the melodica, the track's main instrument, carrying the western melody; Kid Koala is credited on turntables.

### Outro

The album version runs about 5:42, and roughly its last 1:10 is the backing track with no vocals before a fade. The 4:32 video edit is slightly slower and ends after the last sung "My future".

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="drum machine" /> }} needs no drummer and, here, no programmer either: Albarn switched on the Omnichord and the first preset was the beat.

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} sits beside this one: some single releases carried the original take with Phi Life Cypher, and a version with De La Soul and Bootie Brown was recorded for the 2005 Demon Detour shows. {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-space-monkeyz-a-fistful-of-peanuts.md" /> }} is a remix, not a new performance.

The {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }} is the whole premise. In Jamie Hewlett and Pete Candeland's animated video, Albarn's voice belongs to 2-D, and Del appears as a blue phantom rising from Russel's cap.

## Artistic Context

Gorillaz were created by Albarn, of Blur, and Jamie Hewlett. Clint Eastwood came out on 5 March 2001 as their debut single, from their self-titled album, produced with Dan the Automator; the song returned on The Singles Collection 2001–2011 in November 2011. This page embeds the full album recording from the Gorillaz - Topic channel, not the video edit.

## Impact and Reception

The single peaked at number four in the UK, number one in Italy and number 57 on the Billboard Hot 100, and was certified double platinum by the BPI; Rolling Stone ranked it 38th among the best songs of the 2000s. Listen past the last chorus: the song keeps going without its singers.
