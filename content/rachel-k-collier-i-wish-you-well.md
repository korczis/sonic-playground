## Song Summary

I Wish You Well came out as a single in 2021, credited on its release to NiF Records, with Rachel K Collier as producer and Rachel Karen Collier as composer. Two years later it became the second of ten tracks on her album Songs In The Key Of Internet, released on 29 September 2023. The album's Bandcamp page sums up the credits in one line: written, performed and produced by Rachel K Collier.

## Concepts in Focus

Collier is her own {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. The producer chooses the sounds, shapes the arrangement and judges the performances; here writer, performer and producer are one person, so every choice is hers. Ableton describes her as starting out as a top-liner writing melodies and lyrics for other acts, then making her own music to take creative control.

The song also has a life on stage. In August 2021 she published a performance and walkthrough of I Wish You Well on a Roland MC-707, a Juno synthesizer and a MIDI foot pedal. Roland calls the MC-707 a groovebox with eight tracks of recording, sequencing, synthesizers and effects, able to run a live set without a computer. Its {{ <concept_link name="sequencer" /> }} holds the parts that repeat, so her hands and feet are free for what changes.

## Artistic Context

Rachel K Collier is a Welsh electronic producer and performer from Swansea who studied creative music technology at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama. Before her own releases she was a featured singer, on Ray Foxx's Boom Boom (Heartbeat), a UK top 20 single in 2013, and on Mat Zo's Only for You. Ableton, which certifies her as a trainer, notes that she builds her live set around Push 2.
