## Genre and Mood

**Gabry Ponte** and **LUM!X** built **We Could Be Together** on a melody many European listeners already carry around: the tune of Daddy DJ's song of the same name, a hit at the turn of the millennium. It is dance music with a pop shape and a house pulse, euphoric and upbeat, and its nostalgia is the point rather than a side effect.

## Song Summary

The track keeps the original melody and builds a new song around it. The Discogs entry for the Spinnin' Records release notes that it contains elements of Daddy DJ, and the credits show how: David Le Roy and Jean-Christophe Belval, who wrote Daddy DJ, share the writing with Ponte, Giacomo Uber and a team of vocal writers, and the new lead vocal is by Ásdís. Daddy DJ are credited as featured artist and co-producer.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} and borrowing sit close together here. Wikipedia's page on Daddy DJ says the 2022 track samples the song; Discogs says it contains elements of it. Either way, the original writers are credited, which is how a new record can legally reuse an old tune.

As a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Daddy DJ are not the lead voice, which is credited to Ásdís; the credit marks the act whose song is being reused, and Discogs also lists them among the producers.

The melody itself is the {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}. The original song topped the charts in Sweden, Norway and Wallonia and spent ten weeks at number two in France, and Basshunter had already sampled it for DotA in 2006. Compare how Ponte reworks another familiar song in {{ <track_link source="timmy-trumpet-gabry-ponte-mad-world.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Ponte co-founded Eiffel 65, whose Blue (Da Ba Dee) topped charts worldwide in the late 1990s, before going solo in 2005. LUM!X, the Austrian-Italian producer Luca Michlmayr, born in 2002, had worked with him on Monster in 2019, and represented Austria at Eurovision 2022 with Halo a few weeks before this single came out on 3 June 2022.

## Impact and Reception

Ponte's Wikipedia page calls it a summer hit, already gold in Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium and France. Italian radio received it on 10 June 2022. Play it for someone who knows the original and watch them recognise it.
