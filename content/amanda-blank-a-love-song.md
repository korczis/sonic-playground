## Genre and Mood

**Amanda Blank**'s **A Love Song** is the moment her party record I Love You stops to think. The Philadelphia rapper's album is mostly loud, Diplo- and Switch-made club music; here, in the words of Exclaim!, Blank slows the pace down considerably. It is a hip-hop love song, introspective and full of longing, built almost entirely from other people's songs.

## Song Summary

The track reworks LL Cool J's 1980s ballad I Need Love from a woman's point of view: Exclaim! called it an introspective, gender-reversed redo. NME heard Santigold's I'm a Lady chorus creamily mashed into it, and Wikipedia also lists an interpolation of K-Otix's Love Song. Diplo produced it, and the credits name ten writers, from Blank and Santi White (Santigold) to James Todd Smith, better known as LL Cool J.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track; the reviewers describe what it borrows.

### Verse 1

The verses carry the I Need Love material, now from a woman's point of view. That reversal is the whole idea of the song.

### Chorus

The chorus comes from Santigold's I'm a Lady. The album's track listing credits Santigold as featured, and Dork's lyric page gives the refrain to both singers.

### Main Section

NME heard Diplo's production as a cheesed-down version of the LL Cool J original. PopMatters noted that a verse from the track had already appeared on Diplo and Santigold's 2008 mixtape Top Ranking.

## Concepts in Focus

A Love Song is a study in {{ <concept_link name="interpolation" /> }}. Wikipedia and PopMatters describe interpolations: the borrowed lines and melodies are performed again rather than sampled. That is why the credits grow so long: LL Cool J and his co-writers of I Need Love, the writers of I'm a Lady and of K-Otix's Love Song all share the song with Blank. PopMatters put it exactly: covers, or interpolations, really.

Santigold's part shows how a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} can bring her own song with her. The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} of A Love Song is the chorus of I'm a Lady, so the guest's voice and the guest's song arrive together. Compare how another love song is carried into hip-hop in {{ <track_link source="method-man-mary-j-blige-i-ll-be-there-for-you-you-re-all-i-need-to-get-by-puff-daddy-mix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Amanda Blank, born Amanda McGrath in Philadelphia, came up through the city's scene with Spank Rock, Diplo and Santigold. Her debut album, I Love You, came out on Downtown Records on 4 August 2009, produced by Diplo, Switch, XXXChange, Dave Sitek and Jacknife Lee. A Love Song is track seven, next to the club track {{ <track_link source="amanda-blank_dj.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The track divided critics. Exclaim! valued the change of pace, NME found the mash-up creamy, and PopMatters thought the idea worked better on the mixtape than on the album. I Love You reached number 3 on Billboard's Top Heatseekers chart. Play it after DJ and hear the same voice ask for love instead of volume.
