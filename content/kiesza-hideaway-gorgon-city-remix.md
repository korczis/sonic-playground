## Genre and Mood

**Kiesza**'s **Hideaway (Gorgon City Remix)** takes a deep house hit back into the club through the hands of a London house duo. Complex called it a house and bass number. Energetic fits what one critic heard as an even more kinetic thump; romantic belongs to the song itself, an invitation to hide away together.

## Song Summary

Kiesza wrote Hideaway with its producer Rami Samir Afuni in New York in the summer of 2013, in about an hour, drawing on the 1990s dance music both loved. The original is built on a thick vintage Chicago house bassline and a cracking drum machine, with Kiesza singing high and urgently. Gorgon City's remix runs 5:11, a minute longer than the 4:11 single. Heather Phares heard it give the song an even more kinetic thump and some wobbly dubstep bass.

## Musical Breakdown

The reviews describe the remix's beat and bass, not its order of events, so this guide claims no structure or times.

### Main Section

The beat hits harder than the original's, and the bass is the big change: where the single rests on a house bassline, the remix has what Phares called wobbly dubstep bass, and That Drop called it enchanting and bassy with just the right amount of wub.

### The Voice

Kiesza's vocal is the remix's link to the original: her high verses and pre-chorus, and her guttural yelps in the chorus. How much of the performance survives, and where, no review says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can travel ahead of its song. Complex posted this one on 13 February 2014, weeks before Kiesza's debut EP and two months before the single went to number one, and predicted it would be massive that summer.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is where the two versions part. The original's bassline comes from vintage Chicago house; the remix swaps it for a wobbling bass from dubstep, so the same melody sits on a different floor.

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} gave the remix a second home. In April 2015 Gorgon City collected it on Sirens (Remixes), beside their remix of Jessie Ware: {{ <track_link source="jessie-ware-gorgon-city-say-you-love-me-gorgon-city-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Kiesza, born Kiesa Rae Ellestad in Calgary in 1989, trained as a ballerina and served as a naval communicator in the reserve of the Royal Canadian Navy. Gorgon City are the London duo Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott; their debut album Sirens reached number ten in the UK in October 2014. The two sides worked together: Kiesza co-wrote the album's {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-jennifer-hudson-go-all-night.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Hideaway debuted at number one on the UK Singles Chart, selling over 136,000 copies in its first week. The Gorgon City remix appeared on the US Target edition of the Hideaway EP and then on Sirens (Remixes). Play the single first, then this, and listen to the floor change under Kiesza's voice.
