## Genre and Mood

**Sofa Surfers** have spent their career between rock and electronic music, and the German Wikipedia places them between trip-hop, dub and acid jazz; their later albums added the weight of guitars and drums. **Broken Together** comes from that later period. Its title sets a melancholic, introspective frame, and the band's own history suggests the rest: patience from downtempo, density from rock.

## Song Summary

Broken Together comes from Superluminal, which the band released on 14 September 2012 through Monoscope Productions. It is sung by Mani Obeya, the band's singer since 2004, and the label metadata credits the writing to him, as Emmanuel Obeya, and to the four other members of the time, Markus Kienzl, Michael Holzgruber, Wolfgang Frisch and Wolfgang Schlögl. The streaming release lists him as a featured artist, a detail that says more about how platforms credit voices than about the band.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes Broken Together passage by passage, and this one will not invent a map. What can be said from the sources is the frame: a song of about four and three-quarter minutes from a band that, by Austrian Music Export's account, always kept a noise-rock side next to its downtempo roots, and whose previous album Blindside was its heaviest and most riff-driven. Listen with that history in mind, and decide for yourself which side of the band the song leans toward.

## Concepts in Focus

The credit "feat. Mani Obeya" is an interesting case of the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}. Obeya joined Sofa Surfers as their singer in 2004, so he is not a guest; the credit tells listeners whose voice they hear, but it makes a band member look like a visitor.

Sofa Surfers are also a working {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} band. They wrote the music for the Brenner crime films adapted from Wolf Haas, Komm, süßer Tod in 2000, Silentium in 2004, Der Knochenmann in 2009 and Das ewige Leben in 2015, and Austrian Music Export credits their film work with atmospheric soundscapes. Compare how {{ <track_link source="kosheen-addict.md" /> }}, from another band rooted in 1990s trip-hop, carries that sound into the 2010s.

## Artistic Context

Sofa Surfers formed in Vienna in 1997 and came out of the city's downtempo scene of the late 1990s, where, as Austrian Music Export put it, they stood out as a proper band among DJs and remixers. Richard Dorfmeister's remix of their early track Sofa Rockers appeared on Kruder & Dorfmeister's The K&D Sessions in 1998. Their 2005 album was the first with guitar and drums, and Blindside in 2010 was their most rock-oriented record. Superluminal followed in 2012; founding member Wolfgang Schlögl left in 2014.

## Impact and Reception

Superluminal reached number 32 in the Austrian album chart, and Falter's review said it cemented the band's reputation as the coolest band collective in Vienna. Sofa Surfers won Amadeus Austrian Music Awards in 2010 and 2013. Broken Together is an album track rather than a hit single, and it is best heard in its place on the record.
