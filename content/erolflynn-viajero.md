## Genre and Mood

**Erolflynn**'s **Viajero**, made with the duo New Gang, is deep house that its own label describes by what it is not: not cold, not clinical. Warm and relaxed are the words for what remains, an electronic track with a guitar at its centre.

## Song Summary

96 Musique released Viajero on 27 April 2015 as a single with two versions, the original at 5:24 and a radio edit at 3:24, and posted an official video the same day. The label calls it a deep house record that feels like an exhale, built on live guitar strumming and an unhurried, sun-soaked groove, natural and human where so much deep house is synthetic. The songwriting is credited to K. Bellone, A. Rizzo and P. Bougarel. The title is Spanish for traveller; no source explains why it was chosen.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes the track section by section or gives times, so this part rests on the label's description and leaves the order of events to your ears.

### The Guitar

The guitar is what the label hears first: strummed, played live rather than programmed. New Gang's page introduces Pauline as the duo's guitarist and singer; no source we found says whether her voice is used on Viajero.

### The Groove

Around the guitar sits the groove 96 Musique calls unhurried. New Gang's other member, Alfio, plays keys and machines and builds live loops, and the duo describes its own music as a mix of electronica, jazz and house.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit can hide a full partnership. New Gang are featured here and on Your Last Song from December 2014, so Viajero was not a one-off meeting but a second collaboration.

The {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} released alongside the original cuts two of its five and a half minutes. In a track built on patience, that is a real change: listen to both and notice which one you would call unhurried.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} gave Viajero a second home. The full version made its way onto We Go Deep, Saison 2, a 96 Musique compilation mixed by The Avener and released on 13 July 2015; that is the upload embedded here. The same series later carried {{ <track_link source="the-avener-ane-brun-to-let-myself-go-don-rimini-sweet-home-version.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Erolflynn is Kevin Bellone, a DJ and producer from the French Riviera who started playing at 14 in venues along the coast and works mainly between Cannes and Nice. His label lists deep house, tech house, hip-hop and indie among his foundations. He signed his first contract, with 96 Musique, in 2014. The Avener, the Nice producer whose Fade Out Lines topped the charts in Germany and Austria, compiled the We Go Deep series for the same label.

## Impact and Reception

No independent review of Viajero turned up. The label counts it among its most celebrated releases and says it has gathered millions of streams, a claim we could not check. What can be checked is its persistence: a single, a radio edit, an official video and a place on a DJ-mixed compilation. Put on the full version and let it travel.
