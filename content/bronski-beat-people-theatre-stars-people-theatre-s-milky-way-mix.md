## Genre and Mood

**Bronski Beat** and **People Theatre** meet on **Stars (People Theatre's Milky Way Mix)**, the tenth track of the second disc of The Age of Remix, a three-disc box released on 30 March 2018. Discogs files the album it remixes under synth-pop, hi-NRG and Euro-disco. The mood comes from the song's history: a celebration of a 1979 disco anthem, and a nostalgic return to it by a band first formed in 1983.

## Song Summary

Stars, the title track of the San Francisco disco singer Sylvester's 1979 album, was written by Patrick Cowley and released as a single in January 1979. In 2016 Steve Bronski revived Bronski Beat with Ian Donaldson and a new singer, Stephen Granville. On The Age of Reason, released on 28 July 2017, they reworked their 1984 debut, and its second disc opens with Stars in five versions. People Theatre's Milky Way Mix of that recording runs 6:53. No official upload of it was found, so this page has no video.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or interview describes this remix, so this page does not describe its sections.

### Main Section

Listen for what remains of the 2017 recording: Stephen Granville's lead vocal, Cowley's melody, the keyboards and programming credited to Steve Bronski and Ian Donaldson. Then listen for what People Theatre adds, and for whether the title's Milky Way is heard as much as it is named.

## Concepts in Focus

Stars is a {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} before it is anything else. SuperDeluxeEdition describes the song on The Age of Reason as a cover of Sylvester's Stars; the band had covered disco before, as on {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat-i-feel-love.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} then adds a producer's layer. That second disc already carried five versions: a radio edit (3:24), an extended version (5:10), two mixes by Sordid Soundz and an 8:45 Cosmic Energy Mix by QUBIQ. The Age of Remix adds two by People Theatre.

The second, the Happiness Mix, appears only inside a {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }}: the box's third disc is a continuous mix compiled and mixed by Sordid Soundz, the name under which Ian Donaldson works as a DJ.

## Artistic Context

People Theatre is the project of the French producer Peter Rainman, who, according to Discogs, handles vocals, keyboards and programming himself. Patrick Cowley, who wrote Stars, is regarded as the creator of hi-NRG, the uptempo strain of disco under which Discogs also files The Age of Reason. Steve Bronski died in December 2021. Hear another remix from the same months on {{ <track_link source="bronski-beat-space-city-smalltown-boy-2018-space-city-remix.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No chart or review records The Age of Remix. Sylvester's Stars reached number four on Billboard's dance club chart, together with Body Strong and I (Who Have Nothing), and that original is still the place to start: play it, then the band's 2017 version, then this remix, and listen for what each one keeps.
