## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach**'s **Bird In A Cage** comes from an album that Discogs files under electroclash, electro and synth-pop and that her own Bandcamp tags with house, techno, punk and hip-hop beats. Playful and quirky are the feelings chosen here, after the label's own words for her style: pure electro post-rave simplicity. No critic has described this track, so take them as a place to start.

## Song Summary

Bird In A Cage runs three minutes thirty-seven and belongs to Sugar Cane, the first of the two records that make up her 2017 double album Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos on her label Greatest Hits International. The album's notes say that every Sugar Cane track was written and produced by Pilocka Krach in Biesenthal, Sternhagen Gut and Bohemia in 2014 and 2015. The digital release credits her as composer and names no lyricist. No source describes how the track is built or what it sounds like, so this page leaves the description to you.

### Main Section

On the vinyl, Bird In A Cage opens side B of Sugar Cane, after Toodrei, and leads into Flight To Paris, which lasts only a minute and twenty-two seconds. Listen to the two in a row and decide where one ends.

## Concepts in Focus

The double album is a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }} by its label's account: a conceptually framed record and a journey diary of her last two years on tour, with Mexico, Detroit and Berlin as fixed points. The two halves keep their own origins. Sugar Cane was made in Biesenthal, Sternhagen Gut and Bohemia in 2014 and 2015; The Lost Amigos in Tulum, Guadalajara, Detroit and Berlin in 2015 and 2016, as in {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-dogs-are-in-this-house.md" /> }}.

Bird In A Cage is a {{ <concept_link name="deep cut" /> }}. It was not a single, and the twelve-track CD edition, which sequences the album anew, leaves it out; so does the second running order of the vinyl-plus-CD set. You meet it only on the full double album or on Sugar Cane alone.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick and grew up in Berlin-Mitte, and she releases her music on her own label. Her label calls her the charming queen of Berlin's post-techno underground, says she is Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos herself, and describes her sounds as either raw sampling cuts or deep growling synthesis. Two tracks later on the same side comes {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach_la_luna-y-el-sol.md" /> }}.
