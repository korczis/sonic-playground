## Genre and Mood

**Yael Naim**'s **"New Soul"** is pop with an indie-folk heart, uplifting and carefree on the surface. PopMatters heard a "lighthearted and easily recognizable melody"; underneath is a lyric about finding life harder than expected.

## Song Summary

Naim wrote "New Soul" after a conversation with a friend about reincarnation. As she told NPR in 2008, she had once thought she was an old soul who knew life, then found she was completely new, made a lot of mistakes and decided it was fine to start again. In the song she arrives in "this strange world" hoping to learn how to give and take. She sings and plays guitar and piano, Laurent David plays bass and guitar, David Donatien drums and Sebastien Llado trombone; Naim and Donatien produced it.

### Verses

The verses carry the story of the new soul. The published sheet music sets the song in C major, in common time at 100 beats per minute, with a vocal line spanning two octaves, from A3 to A5.

### The La-La-La Section

The hook has no words at all: a "la la la la" passage that Wikipedia names as the song's most prominent part.

### The Arrangement

Thomas Hauner of PopMatters credits much of the song's success to its "droll trombone and baritone accompaniments", which shape its frame.

## Concepts in Focus

"New Soul" is a textbook case of {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. In January 2008 Apple used it in the first advertisement for the MacBook Air, and NPR reported that Steve Jobs picked it himself. Donatien told NPR they had turned down earlier requests, chocolate among them, but agreed to a computer; Naim added that computers are a tool that helps them make music.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the la-la-la passage, wordless and easy to sing back after one hearing, on an album that moves between English, French and Hebrew.

The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is small and homemade. Over two years Naim and Donatien arranged and recorded thirteen songs in a studio in her Paris apartment, and Hauner wrote that Donatien's production and drumming shaped much of the album's sound.

## Artistic Context

Naim was born in Paris in 1978 and grew up in Ramat HaSharon, Israel, where she later sang as a soloist in the Israeli Air Force Orchestra. Her self-titled second album came out on Tôt ou Tard on 22 October 2007, and in North America on Atlantic in March 2008.

## Impact and Reception

After the advert, "New Soul" entered the Billboard Hot 100 at number 9 and rose to number 7, making Naim the first Israeli solo artist with a US top-ten hit. It reached number 7 in Canada, number 30 in the UK and number 1 in Belgium, and was used in the films The House Bunny and Wild Target. Listen past the advert to the song itself.
