## Genre and Mood

**Etienne de Crécy** made **Smile** with **Alex Gopher** as a groove, and the Vocal Mix hands it to the American rapper **Asher Roth**. It is French house leaning on funk, carefree and groovy: the Arts Desk heard a low funk groove, Ibiza Spotlight a fresh, breezy, smooth track built on one of de Crécy's funky basslines.

## Song Summary

The original Smile, four and a half minutes long, is the fifth track of Super Discount 3, the album de Crécy released in January 2015. That summer Ultra Records issued Smile (Vocal Mix) in the US, with Roth's vocal on top and an extended version beside it; this is the vocal mix itself, about three and a half minutes. De Crécy told the story of the guest: he discovered Roth through the video for Tangerine Girl, felt his laid-back funk suited the track, and Roth recorded his part in a matter of hours at Gopher's studio.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Groove

The reviews of the original describe its foundation: a low funk groove and a funky bassline, smooth and breezy. No source maps its sections, so listen for how the bass carries everything else.

### The Vocal

Over that groove, the Vocal Mix adds Roth's voice. No review describes his part in detail beyond the laid-back funk de Crécy heard in him; listen for how a voice changes the groove of the album track.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} is a second finished recording of a song by the same artist. Smile has several: the album track, this Vocal Mix and its extended version, then six remixes by Don Dada, Moonbootica, Clyde P, Round, Mijo and Malikk. Comparing the album track with this one shows what a voice adds to the same groove.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited on another artist's track. Super Discount 3 was the first de Crécy album with singers, and this version adds one more: Roth, an American hip-hop artist whose first single as lead artist, I Love College, came out in 2009.

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} is the low line that carries a groove, and in Smile Ibiza Spotlight heard one of de Crécy's funky basslines.

## Artistic Context

De Crécy, born in Lyon in 1969, went to the same college in Versailles as Alex Gopher and the duo Air, and co-founded the label Solid with Gopher. As a sound engineer in Paris he met Philippe Zdar, heard here with Cassius in {{ <track_link source="cassius-toop-toop.md" /> }}, and they recorded as Motorbass. Super Discount 3, the third album of his Super Discount series, combined the samples of the first with the synthesizers of the second, with songs built to play in his DJ sets.

## Impact and Reception

Ibiza Spotlight called Smile the standout of Super Discount 3. The Vocal Mix got its own video, directed by de Crécy in Atwater Village, Los Angeles: a morning commute down Glendale Boulevard from behind the wheel, opening on a radio introduction by KCRW's Jason Bentley, with Roth appearing in it. Put it on for a drive.
