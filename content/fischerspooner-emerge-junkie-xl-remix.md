## Genre and Mood

**Fischerspooner**'s **Emerge (Junkie XL Remix)** takes one of the defining records of electroclash and hands it to a Dutch producer from the world of big-beat and trance. The genres here are electroclash, remix and electronic music; the feelings, energetic and bold, are an interpretation rather than a critic's verdict: no review we found describes the remix itself in detail, so this page says less about its sound than about where it came from and where it went.

## Song Summary

The remix is the long version of Emerge. On Fischerspooner's US CD single it runs 8:52, beside an album version of 4:48, and on the 2002 double 12-inch from Capitol Records it fills the whole first side. The song itself is unchanged in its credits: music by Warren Fischer, lyrics and vocals by Casey Spooner, additional vocals by Lizzy Yoder, produced by Nicholas Vernhes and Fischerspooner. What Junkie XL changed is the arrangement and the pacing around them.

### Main Section

The length is the clearest fact. Nearly nine minutes gives a DJ room to bring the track in and take it out, and gives the vocal space to arrive and return. Listen for where the remix lets the beat run alone and where it brings Spooner's voice back.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version of a recording built from its parts by someone other than the original producer. Emerge collected them: its releases carry versions by Dave Clarke, DFA, Adult., Naughty and others, and this one was credited to Junkie XL, the name Tom Holkenborg used, in the same year his remix of Elvis Presley's A Little Less Conversation became a worldwide hit.

As a {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }}, the remix travelled further than most club records. It was included in SSX 3, the 2003 snowboarding game, whose licensed soundtrack won an award from the Academy of Interactive Arts and Sciences. Compare {{ <track_link source="felix-da-house-cat-silverscreen.md" /> }}, which reached players through the same game.

The original belongs to {{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }}, the early-2000s revival of 1980s electro and synth-pop; {{ <track_link source="fischerspooner-emerge.md" /> }} tells that story.

## Artistic Context

Fischerspooner, the New York duo of Warren Fischer and Casey Spooner, released Emerge as their debut single in 2001 on International DeeJay Gigolo, and it was re-released in the following years by Ministry of Sound and by Capitol. MusicBrainz lists this remix on Capitol's US single and on editions of the debut album #1 from 2003. Holkenborg himself had moved from trance to electronica and big beat, and later became a film composer.

## Impact and Reception

In 2020 the band put out an updated edit and remaster of Emerge, noting on its own channel that the song had lived on as an underground classic and turned up most recently in the HBO series Westworld; they re-released the Junkie XL remix with it.
