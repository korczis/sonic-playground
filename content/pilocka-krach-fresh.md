## Song Summary

Fresh is a 3:53 track on The Lost Amigos, the second half of Sugar Cane & The Lost Amigos, which Pilocka Krach released in April 2017 on her own label, Greatest Hits International. The streaming credits name two composers, Pilocka Krach and Gerardo Martinez Angeles, and her alone for the lyric; the vinyl credits add that Martinez Angeles played additional synthesizer on it. No source publishes the words or describes the arrangement, so this page keeps to the credits and to one line of the album's own text: pure old-school sounds never sounded that fresh.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} makes sound electronically, and two players on one track can split the work: one shapes the bass or the beat, another plays on top. The credit for additional synthesizer says exactly that Martinez Angeles added to what Pilocka Krach had built.

Fresh also belongs to a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }}. Her label calls the record a conceptually framed double vinyl album and a diary of two years on tour, with Mexico, Detroit and her Berlin roots as fixed points. The halves have names: Sugar Cane, made in Biesenthal, Sternhagen Gut and Bohemia in 2014 and 2015, and The Lost Amigos, made in Tulum, Guadalajara, Detroit and Berlin in 2015 and 2016. Fresh sits on the second; {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach_la_luna-y-el-sol.md" /> }} is on the first.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach is a Berlin DJ and producer, born in Berlin-Köpenick and raised in Berlin-Mitte, whom her publisher calls the electric rock star from Berlin. A Musicboard Berlin grant took her to Detroit in 2016, where she connected with Underground Resistance. Her label says she is Sugar Cane and The Lost Amigos herself, her band and all at once. The Lost Amigos side also holds {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-the-lost-amigos-sunru-nereo-ii-one-day-left-original.md" /> }}.

No review of Fresh has been found. It is one of seventeen tracks on the double album, and on streaming services it sits on an album called simply The Lost Amigos. Play it with the rest of The Lost Amigos, in order, as the diary it was framed to be.
