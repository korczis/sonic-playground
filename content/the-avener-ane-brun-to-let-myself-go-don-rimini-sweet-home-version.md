## Genre and Mood

**The Avener** and **Ane Brun**'s **To Let Myself Go - Don Rimini Sweet Home Version** is a remix from the house end of electronic music, credited to a producer whose French Wikipedia entry files him under electro house, French touch and ghetto house. The song underneath stays what it was: melancholic, and liberating in its title.

## Song Summary

This is a version of a version. Ane Brun wrote and recorded To Let Myself Go for her 2005 album A Temporary Dive; The Avener reworked it for his 2015 debut The Wanderings of the Avener; Don Rimini then remixed that rework. His version runs 5:48 and was released on 15 July 2016 as track 15 of We Go Deep, Saison 3 (Mixed by The Avener), a 96 Musique compilation. No source we found explains the name Sweet Home Version.

### Main Section

At 5:48 against roughly 4:14 for The Avener's version, it is about a minute and a half longer. The credit keeps Ane Brun as artist alongside The Avener. How Don Rimini uses that time and that voice is for you to hear.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks an existing recording. Here the chain has three links: Brun's folk recording, The Avener's slow house rework of it, and Don Rimini's remix of that. The Avener made his name this way, with Fade Out Lines, his rework of a Phoebe Killdeer & The Short Straws song; Don Rimini's own catalogue includes remixes of Young MC, The Shamen and Lil Louis's French Kiss.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is a continuous sequence of tracks blended by a DJ. We Go Deep, Saison 3 comes in both forms: fifteen separate tracks and a continuous mix of more than an hour by The Avener, which is why a long remix like this one suits it. Hear the version it starts from in {{ <track_link source="the-avener-ane-brun-to-let-myself-go.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Don Rimini is the alias of the French producer Xavier Gassemann, active since 2006, with releases on Rise Recordings, Mental Groove and No Brainer before he founded his own label, BunkaBall, in 2017. The Avener is Tristan Casara from Nice, whose 2014 rework Fade Out Lines topped the charts in Germany, Austria and Spain.
