## Genre and Mood

**Hotel Ugly** wrote **Shut Up My Moms Calling** as indie pop with an R&B and bedroom-pop softness, a longing, bittersweet song about someone who will not come home. This page is about its official sped-up edit, released in September 2022: the same song, faster and higher, and the version that carried it on TikTok.

## Song Summary

The original was self-released on 10 February 2020, written by the brothers Christopher and Michael Fiscella and produced by Hotel Ugly. Its narrator, as Wikipedia summarises the lyric, is trying to keep a relationship alive and keeps asking a partner to come home. On 14 September 2022 the song was re-released together with this sped-up version, licensed to Amuseio AB. It became a sleeper hit in the second half of 2022, largely through TikTok and mainly in this form.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Edit

A sped-up version plays the recording faster, and the pitch rises with the tempo, so the voice sits higher than in the original. The edit runs about 2:15, roughly half the 4:23 Wikipedia lists for the original; no source says how much faster it is or what was trimmed.

### The Plea

The lyric is unchanged: the repeated request to come home is still the centre. SoundCloud's editors called the song tailor-made for the sped-up treatment.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="sped up" /> }} version raises tempo and pitch together, almost like a record played at the wrong speed. Wikipedia traces it to nightcore, a name two Norwegian DJs coined in 2001 for their sped-up dance tracks; MusicRadar's Matt Mullen also points to Dutch bubbling and chipmunk soul.

That makes this release an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}, not a remix: the same recording by the same act, reframed. In the early 2020s labels and artists began releasing official sped-up versions after fan edits went viral, and Wikipedia notes some put out normal, sped-up and slowed versions together.

MusicRadar offers one reason for the trend: listeners may want to reach the {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} or chorus faster. A shorter, quicker version gets there sooner.

## Artistic Context

Hotel Ugly formed in Houston in 2019 as a duo of the Fiscella brothers; in 2022 Chris left and it became the solo project of Mike Fiscella, who performs as Mike Vince. The debut album Ugly Duck followed in March 2023.

## Impact and Reception

The song entered the Billboard Hot 100 in January 2023 and peaked at number 68; it reached 36 in the UK and 64 on the Billboard Global 200, and went platinum in the UK. By December 2025, Wikipedia counts over 1.5 billion Spotify streams for the original and over 608 million for this edit. Play both versions back to back and decide which one sounds like the song.
