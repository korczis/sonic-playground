## Genre and Mood

**Paji**'s **Six o'clock - Original Mix** is electronic music that Discogs files under two styles at once, deep house and techno. The feelings we give it, relaxed and hypnotic, are our reading of where the track travelled rather than a critic's verdict: a DJ mix called Deephouse Lounge and a Buddha-Bar compilation, two homes for unhurried, late-evening listening.

## Song Summary

Six o'clock opens Six O'Clock E.P., a 12-inch that Kittball Records released in Germany on 21 November 2013 as KITT-055-6; the digital release followed on 10 December. Pajwan Faroch, who records as Paji, is credited as its writer and producer. The original mix takes the A1 slot at six minutes and twenty-three seconds, followed by a remix by Basti Grub, with a second original, Viola, on the B side.

## Concepts in Focus

The label Original Mix only makes sense next to a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. On this record the two sit on the same side: Paji's own version first, then Basti Grub's, nearly the same length. A remix reworks the parts of a recording, so the pair invites a direct comparison of what a second producer keeps and what he replaces.

A {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is a continuous set in which one track blends into the next. MusicBrainz lists Six O Clock (original mix) inside Deephouse Lounge 2014.1, a mixed compilation of March 2014, and a version on Buddha-Bar: The Ultimate Experience in 2016. A mix places a track in a sequence and a mood chosen by someone else, which is often how a club record finds listeners outside the club.

## Artistic Context

Discogs describes Paji as a German musician and producer from Aachen living in Berlin. Kittball Records, the home of this EP, calls itself electronic music from Germany since 2005: Tube & Berger started it to put out their own records and their friends', Juliet Sikora joined as partner and label manager, and the office moved from Solingen to Dortmund. Discogs labels it a German tech house label; its own site counts 300 releases. The connection lasted: Kittball's ADE showcase in Amsterdam in October 2026 lists Paji in its line-up.

## Impact and Reception

We found no review and no chart entry for Six o'clock. Its reach shows in its afterlife, in a mixed deep house compilation within months and on Buddha-Bar three years later. It is here because it stayed in the owner's yearbook. Give it its full six minutes and listen for where the techno and the deep house meet.
