## Genre and Mood

**Solee**'s **Phoenix - Unique Repeat Remix** is tech house, the style the remixing duo **Unique Repeat** name as their base. The feelings chosen here, groovy and playful, are theirs too: they describe their music as one huge, irresistibly catchy groove with a taste for quirky, cartoon-like sounds. No review of this remix was found to confirm how much of that it shows.

## Song Summary

Phoenix is a track by the German producer Solee, first released in October 2014 on a single with Shanti, at just over ten minutes. Parquet Recordings followed in February 2015 with a remix single, catalogue number PARQUET098: Johannes Brecht's remix, Fabio Giannelli's and this one by Unique Repeat, which runs eight minutes and 27 seconds. MusicBrainz dates it to 9 February; the YouTube listing gives 23 February.

Unique Repeat, the friends Dan and Sinan, describe a style in which the shuffled groove of frisky tech house meets cartoon-like, sometimes even kafkaesque sound effects, with neo-trancy melody layers and quotes from retro synths, in a constant morph of styles and atmospheres. ## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} takes the parts of a finished track and rebuilds it, often for a different floor or mood. A label's remix single gathers several remixers at once, so each finds a different use for the same material and DJs can choose the one that fits their set.

Phoenix also shows the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} at work. Within a year of the original came Solee's own video edit of four minutes, the three remixes on PARQUET098, a remix by AKA AKA and Thalstroem in June 2015, and in September a Timelapse mix and a Slow Motion version on his album Out of Nowhere.

## Artistic Context

Solee is a German producer releasing on Parquet Recordings. Unique Repeat formed in 2011, when, in their own account, two best friends whose music had always been too different for a joint project found their creative vibes falling in sync.
