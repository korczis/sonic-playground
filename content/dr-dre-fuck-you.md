## Genre and Mood

**Dr. Dre**'s **Fuck You** is West Coast hip-hop from the opening stretch of his 1999 album 2001, a rap track with two guests and a title that tells you exactly how subtle it means to be. It is provocative by design, playful in tone and irreverent toward everything, good taste included.

## Song Summary

Fuck You is the third track of 2001 and runs 3:25. It features Devin the Dude and Snoop Dogg, and Wikipedia credits its writing to Andre Young, Brian Bailey (the rapper Hittman), Calvin Broadus and Devin Copeland. Edge Induced Cohesion called it rather profane and not particularly romantic, an early example of the stealing-your-girl rap song, told by a cheater who wants sex and nothing more. Okayplayer's Peter A. Berry heard "juvenile, and probably irresponsible West Coast fun", carried by flagrant obscenities, a stylish soundscape and a hook shared by Devin and Snoop.

### Intro

On the album, Edge Induced Cohesion notes, a phone call from a woman comes just before the song begins.

### Verses

Berry wrote that parts of the verses push the limits of blatant misogyny, but so casually that they blend into the song's silliness. Behind the rappers, Wikipedia credits Camara Kambon and Scott Storch on keyboards, Sean Cruse on guitar, Mike Elizondo on bass and Taku Hirano on percussion.

### Hook

Devin the Dude and Snoop Dogg sing the refrain together, a "delirious joint hook" in Berry's words.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} can end up owning a song. Devin the Dude, a Houston rapper then on Rap-A-Lot, is only a guest here, yet Wikipedia notes that Fuck You remains his highest-charting song in the United States.

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part a listener carries away. Here it is shared by two voices, the detail Berry singled out.

The {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} explains the second title. The edited version is called F\*\*\* You, and the label's official uploads carry both, with Devin credited on the clean one as Devin a/k/a The Dude.

## Artistic Context

2001 came out on 16 November 1999 on Aftermath and Interscope, produced mainly by Dr. Dre and Mel-Man, who are credited for this track too. It was recorded at Encore Studios in Burbank, Larrabee West and Dre's own studio. Wikipedia says the album expanded on the G-funk of The Chronic with sparse beats and fewer samples; for this track it lists none. Compare the crowded party of {{ <track_link source="dr-dre-let-s-get-high.md" /> }} later on the album.

## Impact and Reception

Fuck You was released in 1999 as a promotional 12-inch with Xxplosive on the other side. It reached number 61 on Billboard's Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and 52 on R&B/Hip-Hop Airplay, and was certified platinum in New Zealand in 2023. Okayplayer ranked it seventh of 22 album songs; in Soul In Stereo's retrospective, Ronald Grant placed it seventh and Edd Bowser fifteenth. Listen for the hook, and decide where you land.
