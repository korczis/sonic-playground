## Genre and Mood

**Oliver Koletzki** and **Fran**'s **Hypnotized** is electronic music written as a pop song: electro-pop that OYE Records files under house, from an album its distributor says moves between catchy pop, modern house grooves and 1980s synthesizer pop. The mood is romantic and catchy, the two words its own release notes keep returning to: an electronic love story and a catchy pop anthem.

## Song Summary

The song, the label's text says, is about the moments during a night that decide where it is going to end: love, passion and the energy between two people, the first dance, the first kiss, the moment the night turns into day. Fran wrote the words and sings them; Koletzki composed and produced the music, which the notes describe as a lovely piano melody over a very smooth and poppy electronic beat. The album version runs five minutes and 41 seconds.

### Main Section

Two things carry it, according to the label: the piano melody and the beat under it, smooth rather than hard. Over them sits Fran's voice, which the notes call crystal clear. Listen for how the piano and the voice share the melody.

### Outro

The sources do not describe the ending. The song is about night turning into day; notice whether the music marks that moment.

## Concepts in Focus

Fran was a newcomer credited as the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Oliver Koletzki feat. Fran. The partnership grew: in 2010 they released the album Lovestoned as Oliver Koletzki & Fran, which reached number 87 in Germany, and the 2010 singles Arrow and Bow and Echoes.

The 12-inch, released in August 2009 ahead of the album, paired the original with a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by Format B, described as happy techno-pop with an added guitar loop and a syncopated bass line, and the house track Warschauer Straße. Compare it with Format B's own {{ <track_link source="format-b-fran-liquid-feat-fran-original-mix.md" /> }}, sung by Fran.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Kai Kurve, uses a special video version of 4:36, a minute shorter than the album; it was the first of three videos from Großstadtmärchen.

## Artistic Context

Koletzki, born in Braunschweig in 1974, broke through in 2005 with Der Mückenschwarm and founded Stil vor Talent that year. Großstadtmärchen, his second album, came out in September 2009 with guests including Mieze Katz of MIA and Axel Bosse; Hypnotized is its sixth track.

## Impact and Reception

MusicBrainz lists the album version on compilations from Kontor's House of House to the 2012 anniversary set 15 Jahre radioeins. In 2014 the hr-Sinfonieorchester performed it with Koletzki and Fran in Frankfurt's Jahrhunderthalle, conducted by John Axelrod in arrangements by Gunter Papperitz.
