## Artistic Context

Klaas is Klaas Gerling, a DJ and producer born in Cologne in 1981. His remix of Guru Josh's Infinity topped the Belgian chart in 2008, his own Our Own Way reached the French top 10 in 2009, and since 2016 he has remade past club hits such as Robert Miles's Children with Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike and MATTN. In 2019 I've Been Thinking About You, with Londonbeat, reached number one on a US Billboard dance chart. Wikipedia lists Silence among more than two dozen releases of his in 2024, three of them remixes by Roman with Rocco.
