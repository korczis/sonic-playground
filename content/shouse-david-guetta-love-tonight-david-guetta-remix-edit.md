## Genre and Mood

**Shouse** wrote the song and **David Guetta** made the remix, and **Love Tonight (David Guetta Remix Edit)** is the shortest form of it: house built for a crowd, dance music whose hook is a choir. It is euphoric and celebratory by design. Ed Service of Shouse has said the song is about unity, togetherness and community; in 2021 he called it a hymn of liberation, arriving as Europe came out of lockdown.

## Song Summary

Shouse, the Melbourne duo of Jack Madin and Ed Service, recorded Love Tonight in a warehouse in Brunswick with a choir of the two of them and eighteen friends, amateur singers and figures from Melbourne's underground scene, as a tongue-in-cheek homage to Band Aid and We Are the World. Madin wrote the words; the song, he said, is about the love of your friends. It came out on 14 December 2017 on Hell Beach, to a lukewarm reception. Guetta's remix followed on 11 June 2021, in this 2:38 edit and a three-minute version, with an extended remix beside them.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts. Guetta told Songfacts that he knew the a cappella from underground DJs but felt the production did not fit his style, so he made a bootleg for himself, just a DJ tool. The reaction to it in his sets convinced him it needed to be released.

That origin is pure {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}: a vocal passed from DJ to DJ, reworked for one set, tested on a floor before anyone sold it. Guetta said the Shouse world reminded him of his early years in house clubs and early-1990s raves.

This page's version is a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}. At 2:38 it is shorter than the remix itself, made for radio and streaming rather than for mixing, where the extended version gives a DJ room to blend.

## Artistic Context

Ed Service moved to Melbourne from New Zealand's Hutt Valley in 2011 and met Jack Madin, a musician from folk and rock, in a nightclub. Before Guetta, remixes by Oliver Huntemann and Vintage Culture had already made the song a sound on TikTok. The duo later wrote Live Without Love with Guetta, released in May 2023. Compare his earlier dance-pop on {{ <track_link source="david-guetta-bebe-rexha-j-balvin-say-my-name.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The remix took the song into the upper reaches of national charts across Europe; Wikipedia cites Music Week as saying it accounted for 65 percent of the song's UK sales during its surge. The remix itself won Best Independent Dance, Electronica or Club Single at Australia's AIR Awards in 2022. Play it loud and listen to the choir.
