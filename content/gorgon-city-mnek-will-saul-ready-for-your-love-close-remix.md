## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City**, **MNEK** and **Will Saul** reshape **Ready For Your Love (CLOSE Remix)** as deep house with a clubby house spine and an electronic, dubby sheen. It is hypnotic and groovy, but the longing in MNEK's voice keeps an emotional thread running through the whole journey.

## Song Summary

The original is a soulful, upbeat pop-house single about being ready to commit, sung by MNEK with soulful warmth. Will Saul, remixing as CLOSE, slows the energy down and stretches the song out to more than six and a half minutes, turning it into a late-night, dub-flavoured deep-house excursion. The vocal is used sparingly, appearing in fragments and echoes over a rolling bassline, warm pads and crisp, understated percussion. The result feels like hearing the hit from another room at four in the morning, familiar but transformed.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix is a slow build, rewarding patience with a deep, steady groove.

### Intro

A soft kick, ticking hats and a warm pad open the track, giving DJs a long, smooth runway to mix in.

### Build-up

The bassline slides in, deep and rounded, and the percussion gradually fills out the space.

### Main Section

MNEK's voice emerges in echoing fragments, the hook floating over the groove rather than dominating it.

### Bridge

The beat drops away for a short, spacious break, letting the pads and vocal snippets drift in the dark.

### Climax

The groove returns at full depth, bass and vocal locking together in the remix's most immersive stretch.

### Outro

The layers peel away one by one, ending on drums and atmosphere.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City, the London duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott, released "Ready for Your Love" with MNEK in January 2014 as the second single from their debut album Sirens; it entered the UK Singles Chart at number four. The single's remix package included this CLOSE remix by the British producer Will Saul, alongside a separate CLOSE dub, versions later gathered on Sirens (Remixes).

## Impact and Reception

While the original was a radio hit, the CLOSE remix found its home in clubs and on long late-night sets, a favourite among DJs looking for a deeper, more patient take on a familiar song. It shows how a good remix can reveal a different side of a hit without losing its heart. It is here for its hypnotic patience and warmth. Put it on late and let the groove carry you.
