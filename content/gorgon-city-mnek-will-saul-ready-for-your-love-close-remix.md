## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City**, **MNEK** and **Will Saul** reshape **Ready For Your Love (CLOSE Remix)** as deep house with a clubby house spine and an electronic, dubby sheen. It is hypnotic and groovy, but the longing in MNEK's voice keeps an emotional thread running through the whole track.

## Song Summary

The original is an upbeat pop-house single about being ready to commit, sung by MNEK with soulful warmth; Digital Spy praised how it "strips house back to basics" and how his rich vocal lifts it. Will Saul, remixing as CLOSE, slows the energy down and stretches the song to six minutes and thirty-six seconds, turning it into a late-night, dub-flavoured deep-house excursion. The vocal is used sparingly, appearing in fragments and echoes over a rolling bassline, warm pads and understated percussion. The result feels like hearing the hit from another room at four in the morning, familiar but transformed.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix is a slow build, rewarding patience with a deep, steady groove.

### Intro

A soft kick, ticking hats and a warm pad open the track, giving DJs a long, smooth runway to mix in.

### Build-up

The bassline slides in, deep and rounded, and the percussion gradually fills out the space.

### Main Section

MNEK's voice emerges in echoing fragments, the hook floating over the groove rather than dominating it.

### Bridge

The beat drops away for a short, spacious break, letting the pads and vocal snippets drift in the dark.

### Climax

The groove returns at full depth, bass and vocal locking together in the remix's most immersive stretch.

### Outro

The layers peel away one by one, ending on drums and atmosphere.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} in the classic club sense: the same vocal, a new purpose. The official remix package of January 2014 shows the range of choices available, with an extended mix, a dub mix, an MNEK refix and remixes by Etherwood and CLOSE all on one release. The original is built for radio; CLOSE's version is built for the long middle of a DJ set.

It borrows from {{ <concept_link name="dub" /> }}, the Jamaican art of stripping a record back and letting echo and space do the work. CLOSE went further with a separate version called "Ready For Your Dub", seven minutes long, but even this remix treats the vocal as something to break into pieces and let drift rather than as a song to be sung through.

The tool that makes the drift possible is {{ <concept_link name="reverb" /> }}. When FACT revealed CLOSE's identity in 2012, it described his edits as full of "blushing synth tones and reverb galore". Listen to how MNEK's voice seems to sit far behind the beat, in a large, dark room, rather than right at the front as on the single.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City, the London duo of Kye Gibbon and Matt Robson-Scott, released "Ready for Your Love" with MNEK on 26 January 2014 as the second single from their debut album Sirens; it entered the UK Singles Chart at number four. CLOSE was the anonymous alias of the British producer Will Saul, who runs Simple Records and co-founded Aus Music with Fink; before he was unmasked in 2012, some assumed CLOSE was a rotating group. His remix appeared on the Ready For Your Love (Remixes) release on Virgin EMI and later on Sirens (Remixes).

## Impact and Reception

While the original was a radio hit, the CLOSE remix lives on longer, darker sets, a deeper and more patient take on a familiar song. It shows how a good remix can reveal a different side of a hit without losing its heart. Put it on late and let the groove carry you.
