## Genre and Mood

**Klangkuenstler** and **Alice Phoebe Lou** wrote the song, but **Man On the Moon (Miguel Campbell Remix)** belongs to the remixer's warm, rolling take. It sits between deep house, indie dance and classic house, and its mood is dreamy, groovy and quietly hypnotic: a track that glides rather than pounds, built for sunset sets and long drives home.

## Song Summary

At its core is Alice Phoebe Lou's voice, soft and slightly wistful, singing of longing for someone as distant as the man on the moon. Miguel Campbell keeps that intimacy and wraps it in a disco-tinged house groove, with a supple bassline, shuffling percussion and bright chords. The result feels both cosy and propulsive, the vocal floating above a groove that keeps nudging you forward. It is a remix that respects the song rather than overpowering it.

## Musical Breakdown

Across nearly six minutes, the remix unfolds patiently, letting the groove establish itself before the vocal takes the spotlight.

### Intro

Crisp hi-hats and a steady kick open the track, soon joined by a round, funky bassline that sets the unhurried, swinging tempo.

### Build-up

Chords and percussion layers arrive one at a time, filtering in and out so the groove gains warmth without ever getting crowded.

### Main Section

Alice Phoebe Lou's vocal drifts in, airy and close, and the track settles into its best stretch: voice, bass and chords locked in a mellow, sunlit loop.

### Bridge

A short breakdown pulls the drums back, leaving the voice and soft chords suspended for a moment before the beat returns.

### Outro

The groove rolls on with the vocal fragments fading, the bassline carrying the track out the way it came in.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} from a single that was built to be remixed. The first digital release in July 2014 already carried a radio edit, an extended version and remixes by Umami and Sokool; Miguel Campbell's version appeared that autumn on KlangKuenstler's debut album That's Me on Sunday Best, beside the 5:41 album version. Put {{ <track_link source="klangkuenstler-alice-phoebe-lou-man-on-the-moon.md" /> }} and this remix side by side: the vocal and the chords are the same song, while the rhythm and the space around them belong to the remixer.

What Campbell changes most is the {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }}. His version leans on a round, bouncing bass that moves under the vocal and gives the track its gentle push. Listen to how the voice barely changes from section to section while the bass keeps the remix moving; in this kind of house, the bass is the engine and the voice is the passenger.

## Artistic Context

KlangKuenstler is the German producer Michael Korb, who founded the label Zuckerton Records in 2011 and wrote, produced, arranged and mixed That's Me himself, co-writing Man on the Moon with Alice Phoebe Lou. Lou is a South African singer who became known busking on the streets of Berlin before building her own career as a folk and indie artist. Miguel Campbell is a British DJ and producer and the founder of Outcross Records.

## Impact and Reception

The original single caught on online and on compilations from German radio and Ibiza clubs alike, and Campbell's version gave it a second life in DJ sets. It is here for its gentle, glowing groove and for the way it turns a melancholy lyric into something you can sway to. Let it play as the light fades.
