## Genre and Mood

**Lucky Luke**'s **F.E.E.L.** is an electronic dance single, and that is about as far as the documented record goes: Shazam files it under electronic, and no published review describes how it sounds. Its two feelings here, groovy and mysterious, rest on that classification and on the subject of its lyric, the sense of being watched, rather than on a critic's account.

## Song Summary

F.E.E.L. came out on 18 September 2017 on Lithuania HQ as a stand-alone single. Lukas Kemėšis, the producer behind the Lucky Luke name, is credited as producer and composer, and next to him stands Kennedy "Rockwell" Gordy, who wrote "Somebody's Watching Me", the 1983 Motown hit with Michael Jackson's uncredited voice in its background. The lyric, as Shazam lists it, keeps coming back to that feeling of being watched. No source I found explains what the title's letters stand for.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or timed analysis describes how F.E.E.L. is built, and this review does not invent a map. Two facts can guide a first listen. It runs 3:24, the length of a single rather than an extended club mix. And its credits point back to a song whose chorus is one of the best-known hooks of the 1980s. Listen for where that older song surfaces, and for how much of it the new track keeps.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part of a song people carry away, and Rockwell's chorus is a textbook case. Wikipedia notes how prominent the backing vocals are on the original record, and when Beatfreakz made a house version in 2006 they sampled the chorus and left out the verses, and reached number three in the UK. A credit like Rockwell's on F.E.E.L. is how a release acknowledges that material from an older song is in it. It does not say whether that is a sample of the 1983 recording or a newly sung interpolation: a question for your ears.

The credits also show the role of the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} in this kind of music: Kemėšis is named both as the producer and as a composer, one person shaping the song and the sound of the finished track.

## Artistic Context

Lucky Luke is the Lithuanian producer and DJ Lukas Kemėšis, whose work began in his childhood in Panevėžys, according to the Lithuanian public broadcaster LRT. Lithuania HQ, which released F.E.E.L., also runs the YouTube channel that published it. Two years later Kemėšis broke through with {{ <track_link source="lucky-luke-cooler-than-me.md" /> }}, a house reworking of Mike Posner's hit released by Columbia and B1 Recordings, which LRT says passed 300 million Spotify streams and went diamond in France.

## Impact and Reception

LRT counts F.E.E.L. among the Lucky Luke tracks that found success around the world, and puts the streams of his catalogue past one billion; in 2026 it reported him as only the third Lithuanian artist ever booked for Tomorrowland. No chart run or review of F.E.E.L. itself is documented. Start here, then play Somebody's Watching Me, and hear what travelled across thirty-four years.
