## Genre and Mood

**DRAMAS**' "Bloodbath" is art pop made with electronic means, a dark song with a defiant ending: its subject is the evil in the world, and its last word is that we can still act.

## Song Summary

The Viennese duo released "Bloodbath" as a single on Fabrique Records on 9 October 2020, and it opened the way to their self-titled second album in April 2021. The band explained where it began: "We wanted to make a song consisting of real life sounds like footsteps, table tennis, crushed ice, a car crash or party people. When we finished it, we deleted almost everything." What remains, they say, tells the story of offender and victim, of observation and ignorance, of dependence and autonomy, and ends on a refusal: "We have the capability to choose and act, now!"

## Musical Breakdown

No published source walks through the song section by section, so this stays with what the band and its label say about how it is made.

### The Palette

The band describes its sound as unconventional chord progressions and structures, spare synthesis of organic sounds, soft but powerful synths, crisp percussion and harmonised vocals, with cinematic synthesizer, drum and piano sounds merging with Winter's voice.

### The Production

Mario Wienerroither wrote and performed the song with Viktoria Winter and produced it; Arnold Kasar mastered it at Calyx in Berlin. Wienerroither also works in sound design for film, TV and advertising, which helps explain a song that began as a collection of recorded noises.

## Concepts in Focus

Building a track from recorded everyday sounds is {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} at its most literal: not a borrowed record but a ping-pong ball, ice, footsteps, captured and turned into rhythm and texture. The surprising part is the band's admission that they deleted almost everything at the end. A sample can be scaffolding: it decides the shape of a song, then steps aside. Listen for whether any rhythm still sounds physical rather than programmed.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed and edited by Rupert Höller, puts a masked red character, played by Rita Schöffl, into the story. For another Viennese act that builds dark electronics around a voice, compare {{ <track_link source="sofa-surfers-mani-obeya-broken-together.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

DRAMAS formed in 2016; Winter and Wienerroither met by chance at Ars Electronica, and say of their ironic name: "We can't do without irony." Their debut album, Nothing Is Permanent, came out in 2018 with the single "Mess", and they toured with the British composer Cosmo Sheldrake. Their third album, Jewel Drums, followed later.

## Impact and Reception

Austrian Music Export calls "Bloodbath" an anthemic single, and Fabrique counts it among the songs that took DRAMAS far beyond Austria. It is here for its method as much as its mood: a song that hid its own raw material. Listen for whatever ghosts of the ice and the ping-pong remain.
