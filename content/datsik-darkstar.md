## Genre and Mood

**Datsik** is a dubstep producer, but **Darkstar** starts somewhere else. He described it as primarily a hip-hop record at 100 bpm that turns to dubstep only at the end. Travis Barker plays drums on it and Liinks sings. His label called the result hauntingly beautiful yet aggressively energetic, and those are the feelings here: haunting, energetic, intense.

## Song Summary

Darkstar is the title track of a six-track EP that Datsik's own label, Firepower Records, released on 25 March 2016. Datsik told Whopper Jaw he had started it a year before, wanting to write something influenced by hip-hop. He met Barker through Famous Stars and Straps, which sponsored him through Barker; Barker said he was into Datsik's music, and when Datsik showed him Darkstar, Barker loved it and drummed over the whole thing. The announcement named it, with the closing Feel Good, one of the EP's two most melodic tracks.

## Musical Breakdown

No source times the track, so this breakdown follows the stages Datsik and his label describe.

### Main Section

The track moves at a hip-hop pace, 100 bpm by Datsik's count, with Liinks's voice on top and what Datsik called tons of swag.

### The Drums

Barker's live drums run through the track. The label promised a drum track and a drum solo from him; Datsik explained that he used the acoustic drums for grit and layered them with clean processed samples to make them punchy.

### Outro

At the end, in Datsik's words, it goes from 100 bpm to dubstep.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited on someone else's track. Here the two guests do different jobs: Liinks sings and Barker, famous as the drummer of the punk band Blink-182, gives it a human pulse inside an electronic production.

A {{ <concept_link name="tempo change" /> }} shifts the speed or the felt pulse of a track partway through. Datsik's account of Darkstar is exactly that: a 100 bpm hip-hop record that becomes dubstep in its last part.

{{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} is where the two worlds are reconciled. Datsik said that because the music is primarily electronic, live drums need balance to sound as punchy as everything else, so he layered them with processed samples to give them depth. Compare {{ <track_link source="bring-me-the-horizon-can-you-feel-my-heart.md" /> }}, where a rock band met electronics from the other side.

## Artistic Context

Datsik is Troy Beetles, born in 1988 in Kelowna, British Columbia. He first released music in 2009, described his sound as dark and robotic, and founded Firepower Records in Los Angeles in 2012. The Darkstar EP also featured Trinidad James, Armanni Reign, Soltan and Zack the Lad, and its release coincided with his seventeen-date Spring Loaded Tour.

## Impact and Reception

In March 2018 Datsik was accused of sexual assault spanning several years, stepped down from Firepower and saw his appearances cancelled; he returned with new music in 2020. Darkstar remains the record of his meeting with Barker. Listen for the moment at the end when the beat changes its mind.
