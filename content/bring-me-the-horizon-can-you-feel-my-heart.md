## Genre and Mood

**Bring Me The Horizon** open their fourth album with **Can You Feel My Heart**, the song Oli Sykes called the band's turning point towards electronics. Wikipedia lists it as hard rock, emo and electronica; Music Feeds heard electronic music meeting hard rock, and Alternative Press counted it among the emo songs of the decade. It is intense and dark, and its lyric makes it cathartic.

## Song Summary

Oli Sykes wrote the words and has said the song is all about admittance: admitting you have a problem, admitting something is wrong, which he called the first step of the whole album. The music was written by Sykes, the guitarist Lee Malia and Jordan Fish, then a session keyboardist who joined the band officially in 2013. Bassist Matt Kean recalled that this was the song where the band first realised Fish was having a big impact on the writing, and Sykes said you can hear it, because the song is very heavy on the electronica. Terry Date produced it in 2012.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

Metal Hammer described cascading walls of electronica at the start, which PopMatters' Dean Brown heard as bright beginnings where Fish's contribution is already audible.

### Verse 1

The electronics then break into a mid-tempo song. No source describes the verses in more detail, so listen for how guitars, bass and drums take over from the opening.

### Chorus

The chorus rests on a looped vocal pattern. Fish made it because Sykes could not think of lyrics that fit there, and the loop stayed, becoming a central part of the song's structure.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is what critics argued about. BBC Music's Mike Diver heard synthetic textures brought to the front of the band's mix; The Guardian's Dom Lawson disliked the dubstep swagger and overwrought synths.

A {{ <concept_link name="loop" /> }} need not be a drum pattern. Here a short vocal phrase repeats, and a placeholder became the song's shape.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} gave the song a second life. The 2013 single carried remixes by Shikari Sound System and Jakwob; in 2021 the TikTok creator Jeris Johnson remixed it, Sykes replied publicly that the band loved it, and the full remix came out on 5 March 2021.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Richard Sidwell and Alistair Legrand in Los Angeles, follows a man hunted by figures in plague-doctor masks.

## Artistic Context

Sempiternal came out on 1 April 2013 through RCA, and through Epitaph in North America, the first album with Fish. It reached number three in the UK and eleven on the Billboard 200, and Metal Hammer later called it the most influential metal album of its generation.

## Impact and Reception

Released as the album's fourth and final single on 8 October 2013, the song reached the top five of the UK Rock & Metal Singles Chart and number 26 on US Mainstream Rock. In early 2021 it spread on TikTok and became the first song released after 2000 to top Billboard's Hard Rock Streaming Songs, as {{ <track_link source="jain-makeba.md" /> }} would later find a new audience the same way. Start with the opening electronics, then wait for the loop in the chorus.
