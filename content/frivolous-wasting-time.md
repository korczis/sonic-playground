## Genre and Mood

**Frivolous** is Daniel Gardner, a producer from Canada's west coast, and **Wasting Time** closes his 2011 album Meteorology. Resident Advisor heard the album as vaguely tropical and twitchy tech house, and Exclaim heard microhouse sampling techniques. The feelings on this page, playful and bittersweet, are our reading of an album that critics heard as cheerful on the surface and troubled underneath.

## Song Summary

Wasting Time is the eleventh and last track of Meteorology, released by Luciano's label Cadenza in February 2011, and it runs a little over five minutes. The album has a backstory that Resident Advisor and Textura both told: Gardner found Berlin unwelcoming, moved to a small cabin on a mountaintop on an island in the Pacific, and his partner ended their relationship just before she was due to join him. Textura found that under the circumstances, calling a track Wasting Time was a risky move.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} is the method behind Meteorology. Exclaim heard microhouse sampling techniques building subtle grooves, somewhere between the dub of early Deadbeat records and the techno funk of the Montreal producer Mossa. In microhouse, small recorded sounds, cut short and repeated, take the place a synthesizer riff would take elsewhere.

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is what Textura praised even while it found the album underwhelming: Gardner's grooves were deliciously tight throughout. Exclaim heard a record that builds nicely at an amiable mid-tempo pace without ever going for the jugular.

## Artistic Context

Gardner left British Columbia for Montreal in the early 2000s, when the city was a techno hotbed, and moved on to Berlin with many of the same Canadian producers. Meteorology was his third album in seven years, after Midnight Black Indulgence on ~scape in 2007, and his first for Cadenza. Compare another Vancouver act abroad, {{ <track_link source="bob-moses-tearing-me-up.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The verdicts were mixed. Resident Advisor found the album fascinating, honest and sometimes too eager to be different; Textura, which liked the grooves, wrote that the closer is not mandatory listening. Decide for yourself whether five minutes of Wasting Time are time wasted.
