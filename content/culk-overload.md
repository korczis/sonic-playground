## Genre and Mood

**Culk** recorded **Overload** for a compilation of 2000s songs reworked by Vienna's musicians, and they made the Sugababes' smooth debut single into post-punk. A German radio review heard it as bass-driven, distorted and tinged with shoegaze; mica - music austria heard a post-punk sound with a sultry edge. Gritty and sultry is the pairing: a pop hook dragged into a darker room.

## Song Summary

The original came out in September 2000, the first single of the London girl group Sugababes, and reached number 6 in the UK. Wikipedia describes it as pop and R&B with soul and hip-hop elements, built from drums, bass, keyboards, electric guitar and horns; its lyric is a teenage girl's crush and how hard it is to handle. Culk released their version on 18 September 2024. The review on Radio free FM says they turned the song completely inside out while keeping its catchiness, and the four members are credited as its producers.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the cover in order, so this breakdown follows the layers the reviews describe.

### The Bass

The bass guitar leads. The radio review heard the cover as bass-driven, a post-punk habit that puts the low end in front.

### The Guitars

Around it the guitars are distorted and hazy, the shoegaze tinge the review names.

### The Voice

Sophie Löw sings over the top, the voice the band is built around and the source of the sultry edge mica heard.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} can keep a song's words and melody and change everything else. Overload is a good test: the hook survives, as the review notes, while the genre around it is replaced.

{{ <concept_link name="post-punk" /> }} grew out of punk at the end of the 1970s with bass-led songs and a colder mood; critics hear The Cure, Bauhaus and Joy Division in Culk. Hear the same instinct in {{ <track_link source="belako-sinnerman.md" /> }}, another band rebuilding someone else's song around bass and drums.

{{ <concept_link name="distortion" /> }} is the sound of a signal pushed past what it can carry cleanly. On the guitars here it turns a sleek pop production into texture and noise.

## Artistic Context

Culk, active in Vienna since 2017, are a quartet around Sophie Löw (vocals, guitar, synthesizer), with Johannes Blindhofer on guitar, Jakob Herber on bass and Christoph Kuhn on drums. Their albums on Siluh Records include Zerstreuen über euch (2020), which Der Spiegel made its album of the week, and Generation Maximum (2023). Death To The 00's, released by Ink Music on 11 November 2024, revives the label's cover series after Death To The 80s and Death To The 90s, with eleven reworkings by artists such as RAHEL, Sophie Lindinger and Lylit.

## Impact and Reception

Radio free FM made the compilation its record of the week in December 2024. The original was nominated for Best British Single at the 2001 BRIT Awards; Culk's version asks what the same crush sounds like twenty-four years later. Play the two back to back.
