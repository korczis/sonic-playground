## Genre and Mood

**Parra for Cuva** and **Anna Naklab** made **Fading Nights** as deep house that leans toward pop. Its label, Délicieuse, built its description around a hypnotizing beat and an emotional voice, and those are the feelings here: hypnotic and emotional, a long track for the end of a night, as the title suggests.

## Song Summary

Fading Nights opens the three-track EP that Délicieuse Records of Bordeaux released on 8 May 2013, its sixth release and the start of its work with Parra for Cuva; Naklab sings on all three tracks. The label's notes describe the build: the track opens on a full beat, then the producer's synthesizers and Naklab's voice come together in a mix of pop and deep sounds that the label found surprising but effective. It runs seven minutes and five seconds.

### Intro

A full beat starts the track, the label wrote, before the synthesizers and the voice.

### Main Section

Synthesizers and voice meet over the hypnotizing beat. Délicieuse's phrase for the result is du lyrisme enjoué, a cheerful lyricism.

### The Vocal

The label heard emotion in Naklab's voice, which still stays light. Listen for how a pop singer's phrasing sits over seven minutes of deep house.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is an electronic instrument that builds sound from oscillators and filters, and in deep house it supplies much of the harmony and colour. Délicieuse credits the synths here to Nicolas, the producer, and sets them against the voice: that division, machine and singer, is the track's whole design.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit can hide a long partnership. Naklab is credited as the guest, but she and Demuth grew up in the same town and had made music together before as Nianband; the label called her his faithful sidekick.

## Artistic Context

Parra for Cuva is Nicolas Demuth, born in Göttingen in 1991 and now based in Berlin; in 2013 Délicieuse introduced him as a producer from Cologne who had studied sound design at the Hochschule der populären Künste in Berlin. Anna Naklab, born Annika Klose in Göttingen in 1993, grew up in nearby Northeim. In 2015 she sang Supergirl with Alle Farben, whose {{ <track_link source="alle-farben-graham-candy-sometimes.md" /> }} shares the warmth of this record.

## Impact and Reception

Fading Nights was the beginning. Later in 2013 the pair released Wicked Games, a remake of Chris Isaak's Wicked Game on Spinnin' Records, which charted in Britain, the Netherlands and Australia and went platinum in two of them. Délicieuse followed up that summer with Fading Nights (The Remixes), including Artenvielfalt's version of this track. Start here, before the hit, and give it the full seven minutes.
