## Genre and Mood

**Fischerspooner**'s **Get Confused** is electro-pop from the moment the duo stopped sounding like electroclash. It sits between synth-pop and electronic music, and its mood is haunting and sultry rather than brash: a record that starts on the crackle of vinyl and keeps something hushed about it all the way through.

## Song Summary

Get Confused is the seventh track of Odyssey, Fischerspooner's second album, released by Capitol in April 2005; it runs 4:11. It was a late arrival. The Fader announced it a month before the release as a last-minute addition, with old lyrics by David Byrne sung by Mira Billotte of the band White Magic. Gay City News told it slightly differently: Byrne penned the words especially for Fischerspooner, and the additional lyric stylings came from Billotte. The credits name Warren Fischer, Casey Spooner, Kyle "Slick" Johnson and Byrne as writers.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The track begins with the scratchy sample of a record player needle finding its groove.

### Main Section

Chimes and electronica come in over that start, and the voice carries Byrne's lyric.

### The Breaks

Gay City News heard sultry, haunting breaks and found the overall effect very Duran Duran.

## Concepts in Focus

The opening is a small, clear case of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}: not a borrowed melody but a borrowed sound, the noise of a needle meeting a record. Placed at the start of an electronic track, it frames everything after it as something played back, a record within a record.

Get Confused also shows a way of working with a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} without putting a name in the title. Byrne appears as a writer, Billotte as the voice; the song still goes out under Fischerspooner's name alone.

Both matter because of where the duo came from. Their debut, {{ <track_link source="fischerspooner-emerge.md" /> }}, was one of the defining records of {{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }}; on Odyssey, Wikipedia notes, they moved away from it towards a more synth-pop sound. Gay City News reported that they wanted a richer sound inspired by the music of the 1970s, with as many analog sounds and live instruments as possible.

## Artistic Context

Odyssey gathered an unusual cast: Linda Perry, Mirwais, Susan Sontag and Eric Erlandson of Hole alongside Byrne. PopMatters heard the album leaving the cold, digital electroclash of the debut for the pop tradition of Erasure and the Pet Shop Boys; Treble heard echoes of mid-period Depeche Mode.

## Impact and Reception

Get Confused was never a single, and few reviews named it. Odyssey scored 70 on Metacritic and reached number two on Billboard's electronic albums chart. Not everyone was won over: Rich Juzwiak of the Miami New Times wrote that Byrne's lyrics barely stand out from Spooner's college poetry. Listen for the needle, then decide.
