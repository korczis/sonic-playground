## Genre and Mood

**Prada Meinhoff** wrote **Express** as what laut.de calls electro-punk; **Pilocka Krach**, a Berlin techno performer, remade it as a six-and-a-half-minute remix that Apple Music files under electronic rather than alternative. No review describes the remix itself. Intense and energetic come from what critics heard in the original, urgent and desperate sounds and driving melodies, and from the remix's own promise of a ride.

## Song Summary

The original Express came out as a single on 27 October 2017, the second taken from the duo's self-titled debut album, according to Terrorverlag, after Maske. Christin Nichols and René Riewer wrote words and music. Pilocka Krach's Night Train Express Remix followed on 20 December 2017 on Freudenhaus Recordings, the band's label, nearly four months before the album itself, which came out on 6 April 2018. Her announcement wished listeners an enjoyable ride.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts, and length is one of its tools: this one runs 6:25 against the original's 3:33. A band single becomes something a DJ can mix in and out of.

Pop-Kultur described the duo as influenced by {{ <concept_link name="electroclash" /> }}, the scene that joined 1980s electro and new wave to punk attitude and deadpan vocals, and said they breathe new glitter into it. Compare {{ <track_link source="miss-kittin-frank-sinatra.md" /> }}, from the scene's first wave.

The band's sound rests on Riewer's bass, which Terrorverlag and Minutenmusik both single out. A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} can carry a dance track's hook as much as a voice can, so it is the part to follow from the original into the remix.

## Artistic Context

Prada Meinhoff are Christin Nichols and René Riewer, a Berlin duo working, as Pop-Kultur put it, with bass, drum machine and clever lyrics; the festival repeated the story that their name came from a Wu-Tang name generator. Pilocka Krach was born in Berlin-Köpenick, runs her own label, Greatest Hits International, and in 2016 went to Detroit on a Berlin Musicboard grant, where she connected with the Underground Resistance collective. Prada Meinhoff's channel also credits her with a teaser for Maske. Hear her own work on {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-my-name-is-fame.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Terrorverlag's critic wrote that the album made her electro-punk-rock heart beat faster, and Minutenmusik expected the duo's sound to bring them success quickly; none of them reviewed the remix. Pop-Kultur booked the duo in 2019. Board the night train and judge the ride yourself.
