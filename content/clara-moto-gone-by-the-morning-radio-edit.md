## Genre and Mood

**Clara Moto**'s "**Gone by the Morning**" in its radio edit is electronic pop with a house producer's sense of sound, dreamy on the surface and bittersweet underneath, a song about a lovers' quarrel set to a bassline that refuses to calm down.

## Song Summary

The song is the title track of the Gone by the Morning EP, which InFiné released on 15 February 2017; the radio edit followed as a single on 22 March. The label presented the EP as four tracks balanced between immersive home listening and dancefloor delights, the first pop electronic sunshine rays of the year, and described the title track this way: Mimu's voice sings about sentimental disputes while shining on an equally tumultuous and passionate bassline. The credits name Clara Prettenhofer as composer and Mimu Merz as lyricist, with additional production by Johannes Stampfer.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes either version section by section, so the breakdown names only what the label and the release say.

### The Voice

Mimu sings the lyric she wrote. Clara Moto's listeners know her voice from Silently, the 2008 single that FM4 says shows the producer's pop side, and from Deer & Fox.

### The Bassline

Under the voice runs the bassline the label calls tumultuous and passionate: the quarrel of the lyric played out in the low end.

### The Edit

The EP version runs about six minutes; this edit runs about three and a quarter. What was shortened, no source says.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is a shortened version of a track for radio and streaming. Cutting Gone by the Morning roughly in half turns a track that unfolds at club length into a pop single. Listen to the EP version on InFiné's channel next to this one and hear where it gets to the point sooner.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit, feat. Mimu, marks a partnership rather than a hired voice: the label calls the two the most ecstatic duo on InFiné. Compare another guest singer on a French production duo's record, {{ <track_link source="slove-sarah-rebecca-flash-radio-edit.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} carries the emotion. In the label's description it is not the beat but the bass that is tumultuous and passionate, which tells you where to listen.

## Artistic Context

**Clara Moto** is a DJ and producer from Graz. FM4 tells how a chance meeting with Agoria at Geneva Airport brought her to InFiné, which released her single Glove Affair in 2007, her debut album Polyamour in 2010 and Blue Distance in 2013, an album Resident Advisor described as ambient techno with dance-floor moments. She told Inverted Audio that she works mostly alone at her computer, adding recorded instruments, field recordings and her own voice.

## Impact and Reception

The radio edit has outlived its year: InFiné included it on its InFiné Pop compilation in 2025. It is here for the quarrel in the bass. Play it, then the six-minute version, and decide which tells the story better.
