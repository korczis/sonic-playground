## Genre and Mood

**Imagine Dragons** made **Believer** for the biggest rooms there are. Wikipedia files it as arena rock and pop rock, with what one critic called big thundering pop production and a catchy sing-along chorus; the mood is intense and defiant, and by the end empowering, because the song is about turning pain into strength.

## Song Summary

Dan Reynolds told People in March 2017 that the song reflects the painful things in his life: anxiety around crowds, the pressure of the band's success, depression and ankylosing spondylitis, the disease he had developed. The song, he said, is about how pain made him a believer. It was the band's first work with Justin Tranter and the producers Mattman & Robin, and Reynolds said it came quickly in a room with Tranter, one of those songs waiting to be written.

### Beat and Harmony

The Musicnotes sheet music, as Wikipedia reports it, puts Believer in 12/8 at 125 beats per minute, in B♭ minor, over three chords: B♭m, G♭ and F.

### Verses

The verses are confessions: Reynolds saying the words inside his head, choking in the crowd, the anxiety he described to People.

### Chorus

Reynolds belts a single word, pain, before the title line. Glenn Gamboa of Newsday heard in the song how flexible Reynolds' voice can be; Rob Harvilla of The Ringer heard a blaring, all-caps chorus.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="time signature" /> }} tells you how to count. In 12/8 each bar has four main beats, and each beat divides into three rather than two, which gives a stomping, rolling pulse.

The {{ <concept_link name="minor key" /> }} of B♭ minor sets the dark colour, and the {{ <concept_link name="chord progression" /> }} i–♭VI–V keeps pulling back to it: the F major chord, the dominant, leans hard towards home, so every cycle feels like a struggle that resolves and starts again.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, released on 7 March 2017 and directed by Matt Eastin, stages that struggle as a boxing match between Reynolds and Dolph Lundgren; Reynolds took boxing lessons for a month to prepare.

## Artistic Context

Believer was released on 1 February 2017 by Kidinakorner and Interscope as the lead single of Evolve, the band's third album, which followed on 23 June 2017. Its writers are the four band members with Tranter, Alex da Kid and Mattman & Robin, who also wrote {{ <track_link source="dnce-cake-by-the-ocean.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Critics were divided at first: ABC News found it predictable but with dark edges, the Standard called it shouty. It still reached number four on the Billboard Hot 100, topped the Hot Rock & Alternative Songs chart for 29 weeks and was the fifth best-selling song of 2017 in the United States. It was used in a Nintendo Switch advert during Super Bowl LI, and in 2018 Billboard ranked it the band's fifth-best song. Listen for the pain in the chorus, and count the threes underneath it.
