## Genre and Mood

**Kutiman** made Inner Galactic Lovers from other people's parts, and **Spada** reworked it: **Inner Galactic Lovers (Spada Remix)** appears here in its three-minute radio edit. The original was electronic music built from funk and jazz playing, and Laughing Squid called it ethereal; the cosmic feeling is in its title. No review describes what Spada did with it, so this page names the remix's facts and leaves its sound to your ears.

## Song Summary

Inner Galactic Lovers began in 2015 as the Fiverr-Kutiman Project. The label's own description, under its upload of the Spada remix, puts the method plainly: 24 musicians from a dozen countries, hired through the freelance platform Fiverr, sent in parts, and Kutiman cut and shaped 23 elements into one piece, a forty-six-handed composition. Spada's remix of that collage was released by Ego, with Ophir Kutiel credited as composer and Spada as remixer. The full remix runs about six and a half minutes; this radio edit runs three minutes and thirteen seconds.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version of a recording built from its parts by another producer. Here the idea doubles back on itself: Kutiman's original was already a kind of remix, a song edited together from contributions by strangers, and Spada's version reworks that edit again. Compare it with the original, {{ <track_link source="kutiman-inner-galactic-lovers.md" /> }}, where Kutiman told American Songwriter that he asked one drummer for a couple of beats and received about ten different loops.

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shortens a track for broadcast and playlists. Club remixes are usually long, leaving DJs room to mix in and out; at three minutes and thirteen seconds, this edit is about half the length of the six-minute-thirty-four remix that opens Ego Music's release The Remixes, dated 28 December 2015. MusicBrainz lists the edit on a 2016 promotional compilation of the song's remixes, among radio edits of the versions by Truss Rod and by Tommy Vee, Mauro Ferrucci and Keller.

## Artistic Context

Kutiman is the Israeli musician and producer Ophir Kutiel, known since 2009 for ThruYOU, songs assembled from amateur YouTube clips. Spada, Ermanno Spadati, is an Italian producer and DJ based in Prague whose style, in the words of his Gray Area profile, sits between progressive house and melodic techno. EARMILK wrote in 2015 that his single Red Velvet Dress had put him on its radar the year before, and named him an official remixer for ZHU and Above & Beyond.
