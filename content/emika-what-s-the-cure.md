## Genre and Mood

**Emika**'s **What's the Cure** is electronic music bending towards R&B and electro-pop. Ibiza Spotlight heard the second half of DREI nudge towards seductive R&B pop with this track, Without Expression and Serious Trouble, and Spectrum Culture heard a blissful decay in it. Those two phrases are where the feelings here come from: alluring, and bittersweet.

## Song Summary

The song is the fifth of eleven tracks on DREI and runs four minutes and fifteen seconds. Its title is a question, and the album around it answers to Emika's own statement that DREI is about freedom: coping with it, limitations against it, the perils of a limitless creative mind. She wrote, produced and mixed the record herself and released it on her own label.

## Concepts in Focus

What's the Cure is a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}, because its remixes go so far from it. On the 12-inch that took this track and Battles further, Cyclic Defrost heard CNCPT turn it into a spacious, dubby techno wander, where a pumping four-on-the-floor kick locks in halfway against hypnotic looped samples, like a ghostly echo of a warehouse rave. CNCPT removed the vocal entirely, and Emika told Ibiza Spotlight she found the track totally transformed into his production, and wicked for it. Eomac kept only smeared remnants of the voice under spectral pads and strings. Emika also remixed it herself with Borai, an old friend from Bristol, by jamming in her studio.

That last version is closer to an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the artist reworking her own song, as she did again with the instrumental released in 2017. And because Emika did the {{ <concept_link name="mixing" /> }} of DREI herself, the balance of voice and electronics in the original is hers too.

## Artistic Context

Emika is Ema Jolly, born in Milton Keynes to a Czech mother and based in Berlin. After two albums on Ninja Tune she founded Emika Records, which released DREI on 4 May 2015. The album opens with {{ <track_link source="emika-battles.md" /> }}, and the next track is {{ <track_link source="emika-without-expression.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Spectrum Culture placed the song among Emika's emotive electronica contemporaries and named {{ <track_link source="the-xx-crystalised.md" /> }} as its kin. Play the original first, then a remix, and ask what is left of the cure.
