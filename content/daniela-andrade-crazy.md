## Genre and Mood

**Daniela Andrade** takes **Crazy**, Gnarls Barkley's 2006 hit, and makes it a cover in the singer-songwriter tradition: acoustic, minimal, built around one voice. The mood is intimate and sultry. Stereofox called it devastatingly intimate and said it turned the original on its head; Hypebeast heard a smooth, mellow track with a light, sultry vocal.

## Song Summary

Crazy was written by Danger Mouse and CeeLo Green with the Italian composers Gian Franco and Gian Piero Reverberi, because it is built on a sample of their Last Men Standing, from the 1968 Western Django, Prepare a Coffin. The original drew on Spaghetti Western scores, Ennio Morricone's above all. Andrade's version, released as a single in January 2014 and posted on her YouTube channel, reworks the instrumentals for an acoustic feel and lets the voice carry the song.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song by someone other than its original performer. Andrade keeps Crazy's melody and lyric and changes nearly everything else: the instrumentation, the production, the sense of scale.

That matters because the original is a record of {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Danger Mouse built Gnarls Barkley's track on the Reverberi brothers' Western music, borrowing its melody and chords. A cover cannot sample its way into that drama; it has to rebuild the song from its melody and words alone.

Her recording found its widest audience as {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }}. It was used in Suits, and in 2020 it played in the premiere of The Umbrella Academy's second season; that weekend it climbed to number 31 on the US iTunes sales chart, six years after release. Compare {{ <track_link source="ane-brun-big-in-japan.md" /> }}, another quiet cover that television carried.

## Artistic Context

Andrade, a Honduran-Canadian singer born in Montreal in 1992 and raised in Edmonton, began posting covers on YouTube in 2008. She became known for stripped-down acoustic covers, of Beyoncé, Nirvana and Edith Piaf among others. The Crazy single came out on Crooked Lid Records.

## Impact and Reception

The original was the first UK number one on download sales alone and the best-selling UK single of 2006. Andrade's version went viral in 2014. Listen to both back to back.
