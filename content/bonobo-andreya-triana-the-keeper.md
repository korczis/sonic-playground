## Genre and Mood

**Bonobo** and **Andreya Triana** made **The Keeper** a slow burn. It is downtempo with the patience of trip-hop and the instrumental colour of nu-jazz, but its centre is a voice: soulful and sultry, smoky rather than polished. Ninja Tune, who released it, called it the pair's most intense, emotional soul jam to date, and the intensity comes from restraint, not volume.

## Song Summary

The Keeper was the first single from Black Sands, Bonobo's fourth album, issued in autumn 2009 ahead of the album in March 2010, where it is the ninth of twelve tracks. Simon Green builds it on a xylophone-like lick and a driving beat, and Triana sings over it in what SoulBounce called knockout smoky vocals. No source we found explains the lyric, so its meaning is left to your own listening. Reviewers agreed on the effect: a seductive richness, amplified by production that only sounds simple.

### Intro

The lick and the beat come first. Listen for the mallet-struck tone of the melody and the steady push beneath it.

### Verse

The Line of Best Fit heard a sleuth-like downtempo number that takes full advantage of Triana's sultry, husky voice.

### Refrain

SoulBounce called the refrain lovely, if seemingly dissonant. Listen for where her line seems to pull against the harmony under it: the slight friction is part of why it sticks.

## Concepts in Focus

The Keeper shows what a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} can change. Black Sands is mostly instrumental, with Green playing most of the instruments himself; on the three tracks Triana sings, the voice becomes the centre, and reviewers singled out those tracks.

The backbone is a {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} in the broad sense: a short melodic figure, here the xylophone-like lick, repeated so that everything else can move around it. In downtempo, where the beat stays steady, such a figure gives the track its identity.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} takes the same patient approach. SoulBounce described it as a slowly paced, panning single-shot video: one continuous take, with no cuts to set a rhythm, so the music sets the pace. Compare the earlier Ninja Tune collaboration {{ <track_link source="bonobo-bajka-days-to-come.md" /> }}, where another guest voice turns Green's jazz palette into a song.

## Artistic Context

Bonobo is Simon Green, born in 1976 in Brighton, who debuted on Tru Thoughts and signed to Ninja Tune in 2001. Andreya Triana, a London singer and Red Bull Music Academy alumna, toured North America with his live band in 2009 and sings on three Black Sands tracks: Eyesdown, The Keeper and Stay the Same. Green then produced her debut album, Lost Where I Belong, released by Ninja Tune in 2010.

## Impact and Reception

Black Sands scored 73 on Metacritic, and its reviews kept returning to Triana: Scratched Vinyl called her silky, soulful vocals the icing on the cake. The album's tour was the first on which Bonobo played with a full band. Put the track on late and let the lick pull you in.
