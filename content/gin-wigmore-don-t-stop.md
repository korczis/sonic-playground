## Genre and Mood

**Gin Wigmore**'s **Don't Stop** is pop with a soul singer's grit: playful, upbeat and romantic, a song about being giddy in love sung by a voice that sounds as if it has already seen how these things end. A Sputnikmusic reviewer filed it among the album's toe-tapping numbers and called it twirling, which is exactly how it moves.

## Song Summary

Wigmore has explained the song from the stage. Introducing it at a London show in 2015, she described the honeymoon period of a relationship, when your partner can do no wrong, said the song was about that, and told the crowd not to worry, it will end. The music takes the first half of that joke at face value. The New Zealand Herald heard hints of her theatrical side in it, from the opening "twinkle toes" line to the childlike, music-box-style piano, but judged the result more playful and naughty pop than a chorus line from High School Musical.

## Musical Breakdown

### Opening

The song begins in miniature: a music-box-style piano, the Herald's detail, and a first line that sounds like a nursery rhyme.

### Main Section

From there the song becomes a twirl, light on its feet and built for tapping along. Sputnikmusic credited the band on Holy Smoke, the Cardinals, with lively, well-structured backdrops for Wigmore's voice, and with doing their best work on the upbeat songs. No review we found describes the song's later sections in detail, so listen for how the rasp sits against that sweet opening colour.

## Concepts in Focus

The song is a small lesson in {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }}. Its character comes less from the melody than from the instrument it starts with. A music-box piano says childhood, innocence and wind-up delight; put a raspy, worldly voice over it and the song sounds knowing rather than naive.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} here is also a co-writer. Mike Elizondo, a bassist and producer whose credits run from Dr. Dre and Eminem to Fiona Apple and Regina Spektor, wrote Don't Stop with Wigmore and produced the album in Los Angeles. A producer who writes the song shapes it twice: once in its melody and once in the sound it is dressed in. For the same album in a louder, chanted mood, hear {{ <track_link source="gin-wigmore-hey-ho.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Gin Wigmore, born in Auckland in 1986, made Holy Smoke as her first full-length album after the Extended Play EP of 2008. Don't Stop is track four; Holy Smoke came out in Australia on 25 September 2009, in New Zealand in October and in the United States in March 2010. The Herald and Sputnikmusic both noted the band behind her, the Cardinals, formerly Ryan Adams's backing group.

## Impact and Reception

Holy Smoke debuted at number one in New Zealand and was eventually certified four times platinum there. Don't Stop found a second life in September 2011, when the American retailer Lowe's began a brand campaign featuring the song, and it was listed as a single in 2012. Press play and enjoy the honeymoon while it lasts.
