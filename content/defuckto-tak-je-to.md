## Genre and Mood

**DeFuckTo** made **Tak je to** ("that's how it is") the track that took their hip-hop and rap beyond the scene that bred it. Discogs files its 2001 vinyl under conscious hip-hop, which is why this page calls the mood reflective as well as iconic. No review or interview describes how the track sounds, so this page says what the credits and the releases show.

## Song Summary

Headdy wrote the lyric and, on the album, the music. The track first appeared in 2001 as a demo version on Czechoslovak Sound System 02, a compilation given away with the magazine Tripmag, and it drew listeners from all over the country. Later that year it became a 12-inch single with Promarněná šance, the first release of the new label P.A.trick Records and one of the first Czech hip-hop vinyl records. In 2002 it returned on Pár vět, the band's first official album, as track eight of eleven.

## Concepts in Focus

Tak je to is {{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} in Czech, and one of the tracks that made Czech rap familiar to people who did not follow the genre. Listen to how the verses are phrased against the beat: in rap the rhythm of the words is as much the music as the beat beneath them.

It reached them through a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}. Tripmag's Czechoslovak Sound System 02 put the demo next to Floex, Ohm Square and Indy & Wich, on a disc that Discogs files under everything from breakbeat and house to drum and bass and hip-hop: a magazine supplement that carried a Zlín crew to the magazine's readers.

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is part of the track's credits. The vinyl lists scratches by DJ Wich, and it came with an instrumental and an a cappella, tools a DJ uses to cut a track live. Gang Starr's {{ <track_link source="gang-starr-full-clip.md" /> }} shows the same craft.

## Artistic Context

DeFuckTo grew from X-Kult, which Headdy and Late founded in Zlín in 1996, and from Pro-Past, the group of Jazzy, DJ Fatte and Zatlec. The five-member crew took its name on 19 March 1999. After Pár vět and Sólo pro dva it split in 2006, returned with Deja Vu in 2010 and played its last concert on 1 July 2016.

## Impact and Reception

Tak je to made DeFuckTo known far beyond the hip-hop audience, and a decade later the band's own biography still counted it among the biggest Czech hip-hop hits. Put on the album version, then DJ Wich's remix two tracks later, and hear what a new beat does to the same verses.
