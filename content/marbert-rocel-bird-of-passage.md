## Genre and Mood

**Marbert Rocel**'s **Bird of Passage** is electro-pop on a small scale, close to the indie pop the band tag their own music with. The feeling the sources point to is dreamy and intimate: an album whose songs, in the band's words, welcome you like good friends.

## Song Summary

Bird of Passage is the ninth of twelve tracks on In the Beginning, the Leipzig trio's fourth studio album, released by Compost Records on 9 October 2015. It runs four minutes and five seconds and sits between Dance Slow and Hold On. The band's own album text calls the record a living utopia and a tart kiss for every purist genre lover: nothing hewn raw, everything sculpted delicately. A bird of passage is a migrating bird, and by extension a person who never stays long in one place; no source we found says what the title means to the band.

## Artistic Context

Marbert Rocel are Marcel Aue (Malik), Robert Krause (Panthera Krause) and the singer Antje Seifarth (Spunk); the band was founded in Erfurt in 2004, is based in Leipzig, and takes its name from the first names of the two men. They design their own artwork, website and videos. Their albums on Compost are Speed Emotions (2007), Catch A Bird (2009), Small Hours (2012) and In the Beginning (2015), and with Mathias Kaden and Michael Nagler they also recorded as Karocel.
