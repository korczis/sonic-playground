## Genre and Mood

**Jean du Voyage** and the singer Djéla made **The Closest Ghost** for his first record, and it sits where electronic music meets hip-hop production: Discogs files the EP under trip hop and abstract hip-hop. The feelings are introspective and haunting, set by a lyric that begins in the dark and stays with doubt.

## Song Summary

The Closest Ghost is the second track of The Closest EP, released on 13 January 2014 on JFX Lab, an imprint of the French label Jarring Effects, with a limited 12-inch following for Disquaire Day, France's record store day. Bandcamp credits the song to Djéla and Jean du Voyage, and Jean du Voyage alone with recording, mixing and producing it; Sven Friederichs mastered the EP. The lyric, sung in English and printed with the official uploads, describes feeling surrounded by darkness and treats doubt as a power of its own, before the title phrase arrives inside a question asked of oneself.

### Main Section

What can be said comes from the credits. Djéla carries the song as its voice and co-writer. The beat is Jean du Voyage's; the label describes him as drawn to ethnic sounds and syncopated rhythms, and his live sets as compositions built on turntables and machines. Catalogue copies of the track run 3:35 to 3:41, the longer on the vinyl and the artist's Bandcamp, the shorter on Slab Note's digital edition.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="turntablism" /> }} is where Jean du Voyage began. The label's biography says he bought his first turntable in 1998, started producing in 2000 and competed in DJ championships, TKO in France, DMC in London and IDA in Kraków, before founding the bass-music duo Broken Drop with Kraps. The Closest EP is the personal project the label says he wanted to build after those years.

As a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}, Djéla is more than a guest: she is credited as co-writer, the official video names the lyricist as Angéline Savelli and the composer as Jean Onillon, and the label's biography lists Djéla among the artists Jean du Voyage works with, alongside the multi-instrumentalist Pierre Harmegnies.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, posted by Jarring Effects in May 2014, was directed by Jean-Charles Couty with the actors Gabriel Perez and Charlie De Lavau; no source describes its story.

## Artistic Context

The label traces Jean du Voyage's taste from soul, funk, reggae, hip-hop, blues and jazz, through maloya, gnawa, raga and flamenco, to the heavy bass and breaks of dubstep, grime and breakbeat.
