## Genre and Mood

**AFAR** wrote **low high**, and **Roe Deers** remade it as **low high - Roe Deers Remix**, as the streaming services title it. Discogs files the remix as electronic music, house and neofolk; Bandcamp tags the EP it belongs to as krautrock, psychedelic and experimental. The feelings we attach to it, hypnotic and dark, come from how the sources describe the two acts: AFAR's machine-driven pulse and Roe Deers' dark, devilish streak.

## Song Summary

low high is the third song on tell me more, a four-track EP that AFAR released on 9 December 2022 on Laut & Luise. The label's notes describe the EP as heavy distortions and gentle sweeps, the second chapter of the duo's work there, and say it was followed by six remixes, each artist unique in approach and style; four of the six rework low high. Roe Deers' version runs 5:18 against the original's 4:33 and came out as a digital single on 21 December 2022. We do not quote the lyric, and no source we found describes what the remix does with the song.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording's parts into a new version, often by another producer. Laut & Luise turned that into a format: the EP's six remixes sit on the same Bandcamp release as its four songs, and the notes present the whole as a tale repeatedly told and adjusted by its narrator. Benedikt Frey, Jonathan Kaspar and Johanna Knutsson also remixed low high, so four producers handled the same song at once.

{{ <concept_link name="distortion" /> }} is the deliberate overdriving of a signal until it clips and gains grit. It is the first word the label uses for the EP, and AFAR describe their own sound as analog synth warmth with raw guitar bursts. Whether Roe Deers keep that grit or smooth it away is the question to listen with.

## Artistic Context

AFAR are a Berlin duo; their site calls the project a collaboration between two musicians that seeks to bridge the natural and the industrial, and their Bandcamp calls the sound psychedelic krautwave. They followed tell me more with the albums The Refuge (2023) and Changing Rules (2025). Roe Deers is an audio-visual project from Vilnius, Lithuania, started by Liudas Lazauskas and Oskaras Gudas and now led by Lazauskas alone, a regular of the city's Opium club who took the name in 2014. For another Lithuanian take on slow house, hear {{ <track_link source="mario-basanov-we-are-child-of-love.md" /> }}.
