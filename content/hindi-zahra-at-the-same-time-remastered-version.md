## Genre and Mood

**Hindi Zahra**'s **At the Same Time (Remastered Version)** is a short love song from an album that critics heard as folk-pop with deep roots: Songlines found Berber styles, desert blues, jazz, folk and reggae on it, and a blogger heard jazz, gypsy music and French chanson. The feelings here are romantic and bittersweet, because the song is devoted and wounded at once.

## Song Summary

At the Same Time is the sixth of the eleven songs on Handmade, credited to Zahra and Thomas Naïm as writers and to Zahra as producer. One reviewer heard it as an answer to the song before it, Kiss & Thrills: where that song asks who could love a man as much as the singer does, this one gives the reason, in the words of a hopeless romantic who finds love both beautiful and cruel. The remastered version, issued in February 2011, runs a few seconds longer than the 3:23 listed for the original album.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes this song part by part, so the breakdown follows what writers said about the album and its singer.

### Main Section

The weight is on Zahra's voice and the words she gives it. Critics compared her with Billie Holiday; Songlines added that a shallow listen hears only torch songs, while the surface hides real depth. Around her, one writer heard the whole album as mainly acoustic, with Berber blues, trance-like songs and echoes of the jazz of the 1930s and 1940s.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="remastering" /> }} explains the title. A remaster prepares a finished recording again at the last stage before release, adjusting loudness, tone and dynamics, while leaving the performance and the balance between instruments as they were. This is the 2010 song with a new final polish, released on Oursoul Records, a label that shares its name with the album's second track.

The {{ <concept_link name="arrangement" /> }} is the second idea: who plays what, and when. Interview Magazine reported that Zahra arranged the whole album herself, and she told the Khaleej Times that she dreams her arrangements and vocals, working by instinct without writing anything down. She also said she wanted to bring piano and electric guitar into the music of her homeland.

## Artistic Context

Zahra was born in Khouribga, Morocco, in 1979 and moved to Paris at fifteen. She is a self-taught multi-instrumentalist who sings mostly in English, a language she said gives her distance from what she says, and sometimes in Shilha. Handmade came out on Blue Note in 2010 and opens with {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-beautiful-tango-remastered-version.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

At the Same Time was not a single, but the album around it won the Prix Constantin in 2010 and a Victoires de la Musique award for world music album in 2011, and went gold in France. Songlines gave it four stars and called it a persuasive opening gambit. Play it straight after Kiss & Thrills, as the album does, and hear how it turns longing into an explanation; then let {{ <track_link source="hindi-zahra-old-friends-remastered-version.md" /> }} close the record.
