## Genre and Mood

**Angus & Julia Stone** made **Big Jet Plane** sound like it was recorded on a lazy afternoon with the windows open. It is indie folk at its gentlest, acoustic and unhurried, with a mood that is dreamy, romantic and softly nostalgic: a love song about leaving everything behind, sung by someone in no hurry to go.

## Song Summary

The lyric is a fantasy of escape, of taking someone away on a big jet plane to somewhere far from here. Angus sings lead in his drowsy, laconic voice, Julia's higher harmony curls around it, and the arrangement grows from fingerpicked guitar into a quiet swell of drums and a fuller band. The memorable detail is the sibling blend, two voices that sound like they share one breath.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

A soft, repeating guitar figure opens the song, warm and slightly hazy, as if heard through a summer screen door.

### Verse 1

Angus enters almost mumbling, his phrasing loose and sleepy. The words paint a simple picture, and the guitar keeps rocking gently underneath.

### Chorus

The title line arrives with Julia's harmony, and the song lifts just enough to feel like take-off, lighter and brighter without losing its calm.

### Build-up

Drums and fuller instrumentation gradually join, the arrangement swelling in slow waves rather than one big rise.

### Climax

The last choruses carry the most weight, voices and band together, the daydream at its most vivid and its most tender.

### Outro

The band pulls back and the guitar is left alone again, the plane disappearing into the clouds. It ends the way a good daydream does, gently and a little too soon.

## Artistic Context

Angus and Julia Stone are a brother-and-sister duo from Australia. Angus first recorded "Big Jet Plane" for his solo project Lady of the Sunshine before the siblings made it the centrepiece of *Down the Way*, their second album, released in 2010. The record took their quiet, intimate folk to a much larger audience at home and abroad, and this song became the one that strangers recognised from the first chord.

## Impact and Reception

The song won Single of the Year at the 2010 ARIA Music Awards and topped triple j's Hottest 100 for 2010, the Australian listener poll, and it has since become one of the best-loved Australian songs of its era. It is on this playground as the perfect antidote to a loud day. Put on headphones, close your eyes and let it carry you somewhere far away.
