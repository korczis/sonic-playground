## Genre and Mood

**FM Belfast** made **Underwear** the kind of electro-pop that sounds like a party someone threw on purpose to beat the boredom. It sits between indie pop and dance music, and its mood is carefree, playful and, under the joke of its title, liberating.

## Song Summary

Underwear is the second track of How to Make Friends, the Reykjavík band's 2008 debut, and runs 3:09. It was written and produced by Árni Rúnar Hlöðversson, Árni Vilhjálmsson and Lóa Hlín Hjálmtýsdóttir. When Morr Music reissued it as a single in 2011, the label called it the biggest hit of that album and an anthem dedicated to escaping the indifference and boredom of everyday life. Drowned in Sound read the lyric the same way: disaffection with the place the band comes from, and a call to make your own fun.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes Underwear part by part or gives times, so this section follows what reviewers heard on the album and leaves the details to you.

### The Sound

Drowned in Sound's James Lawrenson heard How to Make Friends as music for the club and the city, heavy on keyboards, computers and synthesizers, with a trace of The Knife's Nordic electronics.

### The Hooks

Music Ravings praised the album's singable, hip-swaying hooks. Listen for whether this chorus is one of them, and for how a silly image becomes something a crowd can sing.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} can change how a song feels. The video the band posted on their own channel in 2008 was, its description says, made with PhotoBooth and iMovie. In 2010 Stereogum premiered a second, directed by Daniels, Daniel Scheinert and Dan Kwan: people dancing alone in their underwear, slowed, sped up and frozen, which Stereogum found intimate and lonely as well as fun.

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} shows what a song is made of. Morr's 2011 single set the original beside an acoustic version and a live one recorded in the band's backyard, the same song stripped and then let loose.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} is the band's basic instrument. Compare {{ <track_link source="fm-belfast-i-can-feel-love.md" /> }}, the next track on the album, where one reviewer singled out a cowbell above them.

## Artistic Context

FM Belfast began in Reykjavík in late 2005 as a duo of Árni Rúnar Hlöðversson and Lóa Hlín Hjálmtýsdóttir, and grew into a live band at Iceland Airwaves in 2006, often with several percussionists on stage. They recorded, mixed and mastered How to Make Friends themselves, in New York and Iceland, and made its artwork too. It came out on World Champion Records in Iceland in 2008 and on Kimi Records in 2010; in 2011 Morr Music reissued Underwear as a single.

## Impact and Reception

Not every critic was won over: Incendiary counted the song's subject among the album's daft throwaway material, while Boom Boom Chik simply listed a song about underwear among the record's topics, beside being president and VHS tapes. The label's verdict was that it captured the band's carefree, enthusiastic attitude. Put it on when a week has gone on too long.
