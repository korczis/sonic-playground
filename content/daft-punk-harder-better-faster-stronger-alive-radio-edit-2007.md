## Genre and Mood

**Daft Punk**'s **Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger - Alive Radio Edit 2007** is French house in its live, stadium form: electronic dance music cut down for the radio from a concert recording. The feelings are euphoric and energetic, the sound of a set that The Skinny's critic, writing about the whole album, called fast, furious and exhilarating.

## Song Summary

This is the radio edit of the live Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger that Daft Punk recorded at the Palais Omnisports de Paris-Bercy on 14 June 2007, during the Alive tour. On the album Alive 2007 the passage runs as Around the World / Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger, close to six minutes; the CD single offered this radio edit at 3:34, an extended version of 4:43 and a video edit of 3:25. The live track also carries part of the show's Television Rules the Nation / Crescendolls segment, with elements of Around the World, Steam Machine and Television Rules the Nation.

### Main Section

The 2001 hit from Discovery arrives surrounded by pieces of other Daft Punk tracks, part of a set built to flow from one song into the next.

### Bridge

Slant's Eric Henderson singled out how the duo turn the simple walking bassline of Around the World into an anchoring counterpoint to the bridge of Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger, in a different key, even. Listen for two songs locking together.

### Outro

How the radio edit ends is not described in any source; compare it with the longer album version, which runs on into the rest of the set.

## Concepts in Focus

This is a {{ <concept_link name="live recording" /> }} whose sound comes from one night in Paris, released first as a download single on 15 October 2007, more than a month before the album.

It is also a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }}: the same performance trimmed to about three and a half minutes, against nearly six on the album.

Laying Around the World under {{ <track_link source="daft-punk-harder-better-faster-stronger.md" /> }} on stage makes a live {{ <concept_link name="mashup" /> }}: two of the band's own records played over each other in front of a crowd.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Olivier Gondry, uses footage shot by the audience on 250 cameras at the Brooklyn show in KeySpan Park, Coney Island, inspired by the Beastie Boys film Awesome; I Fuckin' Shot That!.

## Artistic Context

Alive 2007 documents a set in which Daft Punk remixed many of their best-known tracks live, combining them with synthesisers, mixers and live effects; The Skinny counted 27 songs from the decade since their debut, spliced and mashed up. Virgin released the album in November 2007, and Pitchfork called it "the Ultimate Daft Punk Mixtape".

## Impact and Reception

The live single reached number 19 in France and 16 in Flanders, and it won the Grammy for Best Dance Recording at the 51st Grammy Awards, at the same ceremony at which Alive 2007 won Best Electronic/Dance Album. Play it after the studio version and listen for what the stage added.
