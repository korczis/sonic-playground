## Genre and Mood

**Gak**'s **Coo Coo** is hip-hop by every classification we found: MusicBrainz tags its album, Pow Europe, as hip hop, and franceinfo introduces Gak as a rapper from Nice. No published review describes the track, so the feelings on this page are not a listening note. Bold is the feeling this playground pairs most often with rap; adventurous is borrowed from the way Gak tells the making of the album, recorded partly in France and partly in the United States, "une expérience de folie" in his words.

## Song Summary

What is documented is the credit and the place. Coo Coo is the seventeenth of thirty tracks on Pow Europe and runs 4:14. MusicBrainz credits it to Gak featuring Babs, Mysonne, Lord Tariq and Cuban Link; the yearbook this page comes from spells the last name Cuban Linx. That is a French rapper and four guests, three of whom MusicBrainz lists as American, on one track, which is the point of the album as franceinfo describes it: "très international", with a "très nette tendance américaine". ## Concepts in Focus

The credit line is the one thing about Coo Coo we can teach from. A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest named beside the main artist, and the "feat." credit tells you whose record it is: Gak's, on an album credited to him and DJ Whoo Kid, with four guests around him. Here the guests carry their own histories. Lord Tariq is one half of the Bronx duo Lord Tariq and Peter Gunz, whose 1997 single Deja Vu (Uptown Baby) reached the top ten of the Billboard Hot 100. Cuban Link came up in the Bronx underground and is a member of Fat Joe's Terror Squad. MusicBrainz credits Mysonne as "from Black Wall Street". For a French rapper in 2010, names like these on a track were a claim to a place in American rap.

## Artistic Context

Gak was born in Nice in 1977, of Beninese origin, and grew up in the Moulins district; he began rapping in 1991 and put out a first single and a street album in 2007. In 2008 he became, in franceinfo's words, the first French rapper signed by DJ Whoo Kid, the Haitian-American tour DJ of 50 Cent and G-Unit, to Shadyville Europe. Pow Europe came out on 27 September 2010, a double CD on Zintertainment with Shadyville Europe, the result of two years' work. Gak told Abcdr du Son in 2025 that some tracks were made in France before the team flew to the United States to record others, meeting Lord Tariq and Nile Rodgers there, and he remembers a night session in the Bronx with Babs Bunny and other women who came to record a track; which track, he does not say.

## Impact and Reception

We found no review of Coo Coo and no chart entry. Gak looks back on the Whoo Kid connection as a source of some press and visibility, not a shortcut: Pow Europe 1.5 followed in 2011, then fifteen years of silence before his return with Touche Noire in 2025.
