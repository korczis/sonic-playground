## Genre and Mood

**Big Data** and **Joywave** meet on **Dangerous**, a track that lives where electronic production, alternative rock attitude and glossy synth-pop overlap. It is edgy and energetic, a little provocative, and it never stops moving: the kind of song that sounds like a warning and a party invitation at the same time.

## Song Summary

"Dangerous" is built on a simple, irresistible contrast. The verses are tight and wiry, with Armbruster's cool, half-detached vocal sitting on top of a stomping beat, while the chorus opens up into a falsetto-laced hook that turns the word "dangerous" into a chant. The production is clean and punchy, every handclap and guitar stab placed with almost surgical precision, which only makes the song's slightly menacing swagger feel more deliberate. It is pop architecture with a rock pulse, the kind of track that sounds great on a festival stage and even better on a long night drive.

## Musical Breakdown

The track wastes no time: it is a lean, hook-driven pop song dressed in electro-rock clothes, and each section adds weight rather than changing direction.

### Intro

A bare, thumping kick and claps lay down the stomp, joined by a scratchy funk guitar figure that sets the strutting tempo before a single word is sung.

### Verse 1

Armbruster enters close to the microphone, cool and slightly sardonic, while a rubbery bassline rolls underneath. The arrangement stays sparse, leaving space for the groove to swagger.

### Chorus

The hook arrives with stacked vocals and a lift into falsetto, the title repeated until it feels less like a description and more like a boast. It is the moment the whole song is built to deliver.

### Bridge

The bridge strips things back to beat and voice, a short breath of tension that makes the return of the full band feel like a door being kicked open.

### Outro

The final choruses pile up claps, guitar and vocal layers, then the track snaps shut rather than fading, leaving the beat ringing in your ears.

## Artistic Context

Big Data is the project of producer Alan Wilkis, built around a fascination with technology and the anxieties of a connected world. "Dangerous" first appeared in 2013 and later anchored the debut album *2.0*. Joywave, the Rochester band fronted by Daniel Armbruster, brought the vocal and much of the song's wry, knowing tone.

## Impact and Reception

"Dangerous" became Big Data's breakthrough, climbing to number one on Billboard's Alternative Songs chart and introducing both acts to a much wider audience. It earned its place here for that rare combination of a dance-floor beat and a rock band's sneer. Turn it up loud and let the claps do the rest.
