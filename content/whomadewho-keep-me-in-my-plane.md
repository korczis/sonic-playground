## Genre and Mood

**WhoMadeWho** play **Keep Me In My Plane** as a dance song by a band: indie dance with a nu-disco shine, electronic in its pulse but made by a singer-guitarist, a bassist and a drummer. The feelings are groovy and carefree, the mood of a song that would rather stay up in the air.

## Song Summary

The fifth track of The Plot is, by one critic's reading, about the pleasure of flying itself: Ground Control heard the feeling of someone completely comfortable sitting in a chair high above the earth, and thought anyone who finds flying comforting would recognise it. DIY's reviewer called it a slinkier effort with metronome-sharp drum stabs. Gomma's own text promised a song that would make you think of Hot Chip going cosmic disco. The writing is credited to the three band members, Tomas Barfod, Tomas Høffding and Jeppe Kjellberg, with the lyric credited to Uzi Frank. No source quotes the lyric, so this page does not paraphrase it further.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song in order, so the breakdown names only what the reviews heard.

### Main Section

The drums lead: sharp, regular stabs that set a slinky pace for the voices. Around them, listen for the cosmic-disco colours that Gomma and The Line of Best Fit's comparison with Röyksopp suggest. The album version runs 3:48.

## Concepts in Focus

Keep Me In My Plane lived a second life as a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}. In June 2009 a single carried remixes by DJ Koze and Kasper Bjørke, and in July a second part followed with versions by Modernaire, Reverso 68 and The KDMS and DJ Koze's Hudson River Dub, which ran past eight minutes and turned up on DJ mixes such as DJ T.'s Fabric 51. A remix like that takes a band song into the club at twice its length.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} for the album's third single was shot in Copenhagen in the summer of 2009, directed by Goodboy! Creative and released that September. According to the band, it was nominated at the UK Music Video Awards for best indie video and for William Stahl's art direction.

## Artistic Context

WhoMadeWho formed in Copenhagen in 2003: Jeppe Kjellberg sings and plays guitar, Tomas Høffding sings and plays bass, Tomas Barfod drums and produces. Their early twelve-inches came out on the German label Gomma, which released The Plot, their second album, on 23 March 2009. The Line of Best Fit noted they had toured with Hot Chip and Daft Punk; compare Hot Chip's own club side in {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-hot-chip-kids-with-guns-hot-chip-remix.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The album's reviews were mixed: The Line of Best Fit gave it 60 percent and found it alright but short of hooks, while DIY's reviewer thought it inconsistent. This track drew some of their warmer lines, and DJ Koze's remixes went on to appear on DJ mixes and compilations for years. Put it on for a long flight, or for the moment before landing.
