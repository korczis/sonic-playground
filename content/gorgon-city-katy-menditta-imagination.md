## Genre and Mood

**Gorgon City** and **Katy Menditta** meet on **Imagination**, a house track that lives in the bright, vocal end of deep house. It is euphoric without shouting and uplifting without a single cheap riser, and its dreamy edge comes from the way Menditta's voice seems to hover a few centimetres above the beat.

## Song Summary

The song is about the pull of someone who lives mostly in your head, and the production treats that idea literally: a tight, rubbery bassline and crisp, garage-flavoured drums keep the body moving while the vocal drifts. The detail that sticks is the hook itself, a soft, slightly breathy line that keeps returning like a thought you cannot shake.

## Musical Breakdown

The arrangement is classic Gorgon City: lean, club-ready and built around the singer rather than around drops.

### Intro

Clipped drums and a muted chord stab set a cool, after-dark tone, with just enough space left for the bass to announce itself.

### Verse 1

Menditta enters low and close, her phrasing relaxed and conversational while the bassline bounces underneath with that unmistakable London swing.

### Build-up

Percussion thickens, the chords open up and a filtered sweep hints at the lift to come without giving it away too early.

### Chorus

The hook lands with a warm rush of harmony, the moment the track turns from cool to glowing and the room tends to sing along.

### Bridge

A stripped-back section pulls the drums away and lets the vocal echo on its own, a short breath before the groove returns.

### Climax

Everything comes back at once, the bass a little fatter and the vocal layered, riding the final chorus with real lift.

### Outro

The elements peel away one by one until only drums and a fading vocal tail remain, perfect for the next record in a mix.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} sits at 122 BPM: a steady four-to-the-floor kick, crisp, garage-tinged hats and a bass that bounces between the beats. It is the classic tempo of vocal house, fast enough to dance to and slow enough for a singer to phrase naturally over it. Listen to how the drums barely change from verse to chorus; the lift comes from the chords and the voice, not from the beat.

The track is built around the {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }}, not around a drop. Gorgon City wrote it with Menditta, Emeli Sandé and the production duo Mojam, Mustafa Omer and James Murray, and it behaves like a song: the soft, slightly breathy chorus line returns like a thought you cannot shake, and the arrangement makes room for it each time.

The single was released as a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} package rather than as the album version alone: an EP with a remix and a dub by Weiss and a remix by Mak & Pasteman, while a Skrillex remix was commissioned and played in his sets but never released. Compare the album version with {{ <track_link source="gorgon-city-katy-menditta-weiss-imagination-weiss-remix.md" /> }}, which keeps the vocal and stretches the track to over six minutes for the club.

## Artistic Context

Gorgon City is the North London production duo of Kye "Foamo" Gibbon and Matt "RackNRuin" Robson-Scott, part of the wave that brought UK garage and deep house back into the charts in the mid-2010s; "Ready for Your Love" and "Here for You" both reached the UK top ten in 2014. Imagination comes from their debut album Sirens, released by Virgin EMI and Black Butter on 6 October 2014, which reached number 10 in the UK. It was released as the album's sixth single in March 2015, first as a Beatport-exclusive remix package, with the South London vocalist Katy Menditta fronting the track.

## Impact and Reception

Imagination became one of the more enduring cuts from Sirens; critics compared it to the earlier singles "Ready for Your Love", "Here for You" and "Unmissable", and it showed again that the duo could write songs as well as club tools. It belongs here as a reminder of how much feeling a disciplined house record can carry. Turn it up, let the bassline do the work and drift with it.
