## Genre and Mood

**Little Boots** wrote Stuck on Repeat as electropop and synth-pop; **Fake Blood** turned it into **Stuck On Repeat (Fake Blood Mix)**, a six-and-a-half-minute electro-house remix for the clubs. The feelings here come from the song: hypnotic and energetic, about a love that keeps playing like a record, and bittersweet because the singer half enjoys being trapped.

## Song Summary

Victoria Hesketh wrote the song in 2007 with Greg Kurstin and Joe Goddard of Hot Chip, who produced it in his London bedroom, largely over email. Wikipedia describes a disco-inspired song that uses music and rhythm as a metaphor for romantic obsession, composed with Kylie Minogue in mind and built mainly on Hesketh's Tenori-on, with synthesizers, keyboards and chimes. The original ran close to seven minutes before it was cut to three for the Arecibo EP. Theo Keating's Fake Blood remix was released officially alongside Arecibo in 2008.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version made by reworking a recording's parts, usually by another producer. Here that producer is Fake Blood, the house and electro alias of Theo Keating, and his version travelled with the original from the start: Wikipedia lists it on a UK promotional CD of 2008 next to a Rory Phillips re-edit.

It returned in 2019 as a {{ <concept_link name="bonus track" /> }}. The tenth-anniversary edition of Hands added eleven tracks, among them demos, the original mix of Stuck on Repeat and this remix; streaming services carry them as Hands: Bonus Disc.

The remix also belongs to {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Writing in 2013, THUMP called Fake Blood's massive remix one of the most enduring tunes of the 2009 blog-house moment. Compare another remix from the same British electropop wave, {{ <track_link source="la-roux-in-for-the-kill-lifelike-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Little Boots is Victoria Hesketh's stage name, and Stuck on Repeat came out under it as a promotional single in February 2008, then as a track on Arecibo in November 2008 and as the encore of her debut album Hands in June 2009. Theo Keating is one half of the big-beat group the Wiseguys and, later, of the Black Ghosts with Simon William Lord; as Fake Blood he also remixed Hot Chip's Touch Too Much and Calvin Harris's Ready for the Weekend.

## Impact and Reception

The song was praised widely: NME called it staggeringly brilliant and the best track on Hands, Pitchfork put it among the best tracks of 2008, and it reached number 7 on Billboard's Dance Singles Sales chart in 2009.
