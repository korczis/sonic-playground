## Genre and Mood

**ESSEL**'s **Lennon** is tech house made for the club, and her label introduced it as a step away from her usual sound: darker, deeper, cut from the underground. That is where the dark and energetic tags come from. Beyond Toolroom's notes no review describes the track, so this page says what is documented and leaves the rest to your ears.

## Song Summary

Lennon was released by Toolroom on 6 January 2023, the record with which the label kicked off its new year. Sophia Essel is credited as its composer, lyricist and producer. Toolroom wrote that it was focused purely on the dance floor, pushed a deeper sound that showed her talent as a producer, and tipped over into clubland. This page covers the 3:25 version; a 5:17 Extended Mix came out on the same day.

## Concepts in Focus

An {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} is the long version of a club track, with more room at the start and the end for a DJ to blend it with the next record. Toolroom put out Lennon in both lengths, and the upload on the label's own YouTube channel runs 5:17, the length of the Extended Mix.

{{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} shaped both the producer and the record. ESSEL has said that growing up in Liverpool she went to clubs in Baytown that played minimal house, and Toolroom found her through its #WeAreListening platform. She has played the label's events at Studio 338 in London and at ADE. Lennon was also pressed on Toolroom Sampler Vol. 5, a 12-inch vinyl sampler. Compare another Liverpool act's club record, {{ <track_link source="camelphat-elderbrook-cola-club-mix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

ESSEL is a Liverpool DJ and producer. Before Lennon she had Toolroom hits with Try and Love Vibration, her label debut and biggest seller, and releases on Solotoko, Love & Other and Good Company. In 2024 she said Lennon and Sweat share the same vibe as The Franchise, one of her early releases.

## Impact and Reception

Toolroom reported that Lennon reached number one on Beatport, the download store DJs use, and that Sweat followed it there, two consecutive number ones. The label later called it her superb club cut. Play it on a good system and judge how dark it really is.
