## Genre and Mood

**Mansfield.TYA** recorded **Tempête** for the album critics heard as their most electronic: new wave and synth-pop codes taken up by a duo that Politis placed somewhere between new wave, folk-rock and French chanson. The feelings, bittersweet and melancholic, come from the band's own description of the album, a record of happy melancholy, twelve songs that make you dance even while you cry.

## Song Summary

Tempête, French for storm, is the fourth of twelve tracks on Monument ordinaire and lasts three minutes. The Bandcamp credits give words and music to M.TYA, Rebeka Warrior and Carla Pallone; the album was mixed by David Chalmin at Studio LFO and mastered by Globe Audio Mastering. No review or interview found describes Tempête on its own, its lyric or its arrangement, so this page does not paraphrase the words and hears the song through what critics wrote about the album.

### Main Section

Indiepoprock's critic heard the album built on a more synthetic base, with a deliberate turn to the codes of new wave: cold synthetic sounds set against the organic beauty of the violin lines, and a base that is at times almost danceable, at odds with the themes the words address. Listen for which of those colours Tempête uses, and whether its pulse pulls against its mood.

## Artistic Context

Mansfield.TYA was formed in Nantes in 2002 by Julia Lanoë, who also sang in Sexy Sushi and records as Rebeka Warrior, and the violinist Carla Pallone; the name comes from June Mansfield, Henry Miller's wife. Monument ordinaire, their fifth album, came out on 19 February 2021 on Warriorecords, with guests Fanxoa of Bérurier Noir and the band Odezenne. The duo's text describes 45 minutes of life and death, poetry imagined like the words of the Zen master Dōgen, set to what it calls simple, catchy Jacno melodies. Hear another duo's French-language song in electronic surroundings in {{ <track_link source="pupkulies-rebecca-fou-de-toi.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Monument ordinaire received the Coup de cœur chanson francophone 2021 of the Académie Charles Cros, presented on 23 April 2021, and Indiepoprock gave it nine out of ten. It was the duo's last album of new songs: after twenty years and the album's tour they ended the band in 2022, with a farewell concert at the Olympia on 1 November.
