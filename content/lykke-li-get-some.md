## Genre and Mood

**Lykke Li**'s **Get Some** is indie pop with its teeth showing: provocative, defiant and sultry. Entertainment Weekly called her debut quirky and coy and Wounded Rhymes hungry, dark and dirty; this single was the first proof.

## Song Summary

"Get Some" came out on 22 October 2010 as the lead single of Wounded Rhymes, Li's second album. She wrote it with its producer, Björn Yttling of Peter Bjorn and John. Li told The Guardian in December 2010 that many people think it is about sex, but it is about power: she had been reading Haruki Murakami's The Wind-Up Bird Chronicle, in which a woman enters a man's mind by making him fantasise about her, and called the song a kind of power play.

## Musical Breakdown

No source maps the song section by section, so this breakdown follows what the reviews describe. The Line of Best Fit heard the whole as a homage to Prince's sexually charged records of the early 1980s, as sensual as "Darling Nikki".

### The Drums

The Village Voice heard dense jungle drums, Spin wrote that Yttling imports heaving tribal drums, and Stereogum called the song pounding. MusicBrainz credits two drummers, John Eriksson and Lars Skoglund, with percussion by Eriksson, Micke Svensson and Yttling himself.

### The Guitars

For The Line of Best Fit, the song is blown apart by frenzied, reverb-soaked guitars of a border-town saloon. Anders Stenberg plays guitar; Yttling adds piano and bass.

### The Voice

The Village Voice described Li's bizarrely engrossing yelps, and Spin heard her writhing all over the track. Mariam Wallentin and Zhala sing background vocals.

## Concepts in Focus

The song shows how much a {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} can shape an artist's sound. Yttling had produced Li's debut, Youth Novels, and returned for Wounded Rhymes; on Get Some he is co-writer, producer and mixer with Lasse Mårtén, and plays piano, bass and percussion. Compare the handclaps of {{ <track_link source="lykke-li-breaking-it-up.md" /> }} with the pounding here.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Johan Söderberg and posted in November 2010, was called swaggering by Stereogum; the Village Voice wrote that it was themed on Barbarella.

Get Some is also a case study in {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. Within months it was heard in 90210 and Grey's Anatomy; later in The Vampire Diaries, Teen Wolf and Sense8, in the 2012 film The Vow, in the games Need for Speed: The Run and The Crew, and in a Victoria's Secret commercial.

## Artistic Context

Li spent six months writing Wounded Rhymes in the Echo Park neighbourhood of Los Angeles, and recorded it in Stockholm. Asked by Pitchfork whether Get Some stood for the album, she called it just a snapshot and said the record was darker and moodier, with heavier lyrics, less atmosphere and more directness. The album was released on 25 February 2011 by LL Recordings and Atlantic.

## Impact and Reception

Li performed it on Late Night with Jimmy Fallon and Conan in March 2011. Wounded Rhymes earned a Metacritic score of 83, and its next single, "I Follow Rivers", went to number one in Belgium, Germany and Italy. Play it loud and start with the drums.
