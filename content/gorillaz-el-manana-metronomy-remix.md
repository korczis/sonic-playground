## Genre and Mood

**Gorillaz**' **El Mañana** here comes in the hands of Metronomy, Joseph Mount's project: a remix of the fourth and last single from Demon Days, filed with the band's alternative catalogue. The original is listed as trip hop and dub, and Audio Snobbery hears it as a conversation between a hopeless man and his god, with a siren in the background that keeps a feeling of fear and danger alive. The two feelings on this page, melancholic and dark, belong to that song; no published review describes the mood of the remix itself.

## Song Summary

El Mañana, Spanish for "the tomorrow", was written by Damon Albarn and released in the UK on 10 April 2006 as a double A-side with Kids with Guns. Its animated video shows Noodle's floating island from Feel Good Inc. shot down by helicopters. Metronomy's remix runs five minutes and forty-four seconds against the original's three minutes fifty. MusicBrainz dates its first appearance to a promotional single in March 2006 and a digital set of Kids with Guns and El Mañana remixes on 11 April 2006; it was collected on D-Sides in November 2007.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} is a new version of a recording made by another producer, and the Demon Days singles collected plenty: the remix disc of D-Sides gathers versions by DFA, Soulwax, Hot Chip, Jamie T, Junior Sanchez, Quiet Village, Schtung and Stanton Warriors alongside this one. Compare {{ <track_link source="gorillaz-19-2000-soulchild-remix.md" /> }}, a remix that became the better-known version of its song.

Behind the name Metronomy stood one {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. Joseph Mount started the project as a teenager recording in his bedroom in Devon, and kept writing and recording the studio material alone even after it became a live band. Under the Metronomy name he has remixed Gorillaz, Franz Ferdinand, Klaxons and Goldfrapp among others, and between 2005 and 2008 he made so many remixes that contract problems delayed his own releases.

## Artistic Context

Metronomy formed in Totnes in 1999, and their debut album, Pip Paine (Pay the £5000 You Owe), came out in February 2005. D-Sides, released by Parlophone and Virgin on 19 November 2007, collected the B-sides and remixes of the Demon Days era. For where Mount's own sound went next, hear {{ <track_link source="metronomy-the-bay.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No source records a chart placing for the remix itself. D-Sides reached number 63 in the UK and 71 in the US, PopMatters gave it eight out of ten, and a vinyl edition carrying this remix came out for Record Store Day 2020. Start with the original El Mañana, then put this on.
