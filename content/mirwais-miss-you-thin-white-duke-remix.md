## Genre and Mood

**Mirwais**'s **Miss You (Thin White Duke Remix)** is a house remix of a cover: electronic dance music made from a rock song that was already halfway to the disco floor. The Rolling Stones' original was, in Keith Richards' words, a damn good disco record, calculated to be one. Mirwais recast it in his own electro, and Stuart Price, remixing as Thin White Duke, took that version back to the club, groove and sultry swagger included.

## Song Summary

Miss You was written by Mick Jagger and Keith Richards, recorded in Paris and released in May 1978 as the lead single of Some Girls; it became the Stones' last US number one. Mirwais's cover is sung by Craig Wedren, formerly of Shudder to Think, with a vocal Stereogum heard as aiming for Jagger. The same review heard the demi-acid oscillations and squishy basslines of Mirwais's Disco Science, with abrupt, clipped guitar chords sparking through the cracks. The cover came out as a single in 2002; this remix runs just over four minutes on its official upload, which credits Thin White Duke as remixer.

## Musical Breakdown

The sources describe the cover and Price's habits as a remixer, not this mix bar by bar; the notes below are questions to bring to it.

### The Vocal

Wedren's lead is the thread. Wikipedia notes that Price favours keeping a song's original vocal arrangement; listen for whether the voice stays whole here.

### The Beat and the Synths

Price's remixes, by the same account, are built on club-led beats with arpeggios and riffs. Listen for those against the acid-tinged synths and guitar chords of the cover.

### The Ending

The same source mentions vocal lines faded and filtered for a climactic effect; listen for whether this remix ends that way.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of someone else's song, and a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks an existing recording. This track is both, a remix of a cover, and its title, lyric and melody survive every change of hands.

Thin White Duke is a {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }} borrowed twice. David Bowie created it; Stuart Price, an English producer born in Paris in 1977, took it as one of his remix names, beside Jacques Lu Cont and Les Rythmes Digitales. Two of his Grammy awards for remixed recordings, for Talk Talk's It's My Life and Coldplay's Talk, went to Thin White Duke mixes. Compare his Jacques Lu Cont work in {{ <track_link source="juliet-avalon-jacques-lu-cont-versus-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Mirwais Ahmadzaï released Production on Naïve in 2000, the album of {{ <track_link source="mirwais-naiive-song.md" /> }}, and in the same year co-produced Madonna's Music. Price first remixed tracks from Music through Mirwais and became the musical director of Madonna's 2001 Drowned World Tour. MusicBrainz lists this remix on a Miss You remix release of April 2003 and on the Remixed disc of the two-disc Production that Naïve issued in 2005.

## Impact and Reception

The Miss You single reached number 85 in France and 92 in Australia; no review of this remix was found. Start with the Stones, then the cover, then this, and listen for what each one keeps.
