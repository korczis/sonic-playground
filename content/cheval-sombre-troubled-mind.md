## Genre and Mood

**Cheval Sombre**'s **Troubled Mind** sits where psychedelia, folk and dream pop blur together. 17 Seconds heard the album it comes from as the place where drone, psychedelia and American folk come together; Chronogram compared it to My Bloody Valentine, Slowdive, Low and Mazzy Star. The mood is dreamy and melancholic, and the slowness makes it hypnotic. Chronogram could not decide whether the record was melancholy or euphoric, or both.

## Song Summary

Troubled Mind is a traditional song, credited on the album as arranged by Cheval Sombre. That makes it a study in arrangement: the words and tune are inherited, and what is new is the sound. No review describes this track on its own, so what follows comes from how critics heard the album and from its credits. Chronogram described a record that floats on a placid sea of guitar, organ, bass, keys and effects, with vocals as spacey and droning as the music around them.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="drone" /> }} is a sound held for a long time under the music. In Indian music the tanpura plays this role, and its digital stand-in appears in this album's credits. A drone removes the feeling of harmony moving forward; the song seems suspended rather than going somewhere, which is why critics reached for words like floating and placid.

The {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} here is Sonic Boom, Pete Kember of Spacemen 3, who produced and mixed most of the album with Nick Kramer. He brings a sound world of drones and effects to Porpora's arrangement. Compare another old American song MGMT chose for the same compilation, {{ <track_link source="charlie-feathers-mound-of-clay.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Cheval Sombre is Chris Porpora, from Cornwall-on-Hudson, New York. His self-titled debut came out on 28 April 2009 on Double Feature Records, the label of Dean Wareham and Britta Phillips of Galaxie 500 and Luna, who also play on it. Troubled Mind is one of two traditional songs on it, alongside I've Been All Around This World, beside a cover of the Doors' Hyacinth House. He went on to work with Sonic Boom again and to make an album with Wareham in 2018.

## Impact and Reception

17 Seconds called the album simply a blissful record. In 2011 MGMT put Troubled Mind on their Late Night Tales compilation, giving it a second life. Play it late, and let it run.
