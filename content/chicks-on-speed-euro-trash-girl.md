## About the Band

**Chicks On Speed** is a multi-disciplinary art collective known for their fearless fusion of music, fashion, and visual art. Formed in Munich in the late 1990s, the group—composed of Melissa Logan, Alex Murray-Leslie, and later Kiki Moorse—blurs the lines between performance and provocation. Their work spans electroclash anthems and cutting-edge gallery installations, always underpinned by themes of feminism, consumerism, and identity.

With their DIY ethos and art-school sensibilities, Chicks On Speed challenge conventions, creating art that resonates as both wildly fun and deeply thought-provoking.
