## Genre and Mood

**Say Yes Dog**'s **My Soul** belongs to the synth-pop and electro-pop of Voyage, an album Discogs files as indie pop. The feelings on this page are borrowed from two sources: the lyric, a love song, makes it romantic, and Soundmag heard the whole record radiate warmth as well as cool.

## Song Summary

My Soul is the second of Voyage's ten tracks, released on 10 May 2019; it runs 3:40. Shazam's credits name Aaron Ahrends on lead vocals, Pascal Karier on drum programming and Paul Rundel on bass guitar; all three wrote it and Ahrends produced it. In the lyric published on Musixmatch, someone is pulled forward by a person who touches the singer's soul, with lines about driving on, a last time and being free to go. The refrain is a single line, sung again and again.

## Musical Breakdown

No published review describes My Soul on its own. What follows rests on the published lyric and on what the reviews say about the album.

### The Verses

Short verses, each a few lines long, set the scene: speed, distance, moving on.

### The Refrain

The title line, repeated six times in each refrain of the transcription, the last time cut off before its final word.

### The Sound

Here only the album's reviews can speak: nostalgic synths, falsetto hooks, funk basses and clapping drums for MusikBlog; clear synth-pop with funky bass and minimal drums for Bedroomdisco. How much of that this song carries, they do not say.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part of a song built to stick. My Soul makes its hook the simplest way there is: one phrase, repeated until the repetition is the refrain. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-into-you.md" /> }}, later on the same album, whose refrain also circles one line.

Aaron Ahrends is the band's singer and also its {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}: the album's credits give him the production, and the three members share the writing. A producer from inside the band shapes the record from the same chair that writes and sings it, which may be one reason Soundmag heard Voyage as a self-contained world of sound.

## Artistic Context

**Say Yes Dog** is a German-Luxembourgish electro-pop trio: Ahrends and Rundel grew up in Berlin, Karier comes from Luxembourg, and all three studied sound engineering and jazz at the conservatory in The Hague. Voyage was their second album, after Plastic Love (2015). For Bedroomdisco it gives itself over to synths and space-pop, sinking into the electro-pop of the 1980s.

## Impact and Reception

The album's critics were warm and reserved at once. Soundmag praised clever electro-pop it was happy to float through; MusikBlog found it smooth and relaxed but at times monotonous; Bedroomdisco heard more and more monotony set in as the record went on. None of them named My Soul. Hear it straight after {{ <track_link source="say-yes-dog-deep-space.md" /> }} and judge it in its place.
