## Genre and Mood

**Chase & Status** put **Tough Talk** ninth on their 2023 mixtape 2 RUFF, Vol. 1, and it is one of its hardest edges: drum and bass carrying a rap vocal rather than a sung hook. The voice belongs to Kwengface, a drill rapper from Peckham, and Notion called it gruff and gritty. The mood is intense and energetic, with no softness anywhere.

## Song Summary

Tough Talk was not made in a room with its guest. Will Kennard told Apple Music that the duo never did a session with Kwengface for the record; their manager simply sent them a folder of his vocals, which Kennard called pretty sweet from a producer's standpoint. They found a section they liked, chopped it up and made it sound a bit more electronic. Notion's feature on Kwengface describes the result as his a cappella threaded through a delirious jump-up framework, a mutant, breakbeat-heavy track with the immediacy of 1990s rave.

## Musical Breakdown

No source maps Tough Talk section by section, so this breakdown follows what the sources do describe: the chopped vocal and the beat around it.

No source describes the opening; listen for when Kwengface's voice first appears.

### Main Section

The body of the track is the meeting Notion describes: a gritty vocal cut into pieces and set against busy, rave-minded breakbeats. Kennard's own word for the treatment is electronic, and the vocal works as much as a rhythmic part as a verse.

### Outro

At about 3:20 the track is short by club standards, like the whole ten-track, 34-minute mixtape.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} usually means a collaboration in the studio. Here it means something closer to sampling a living artist with his blessing: the vocal existed first, and the track was built around it. Kennard said a real session would probably have produced a more vocal-led song.

The {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} is the engine. Drum and bass grew out of sped-up, chopped breaks, and jump-up is the scene's rowdiest branch; Notion's reference to 1990s rave points to that lineage.

{{ <concept_link name="rapping" /> }} over drum and bass is nothing new, but drill is not its usual voice. Compare the MCs of {{ <track_link source="bru-c-mr-traumatik-tsuki-wavey-one.md" /> }}, who performed for their beat.

## Artistic Context

2 RUFF, Vol. 1 came out on EMI on 10 November 2023, ten tracks that Saul Milton told Official Charts the duo had played in full during their Ibiza season, the setting he said the record was built for, at a time when drum and bass was having a moment. It opens with {{ <track_link source="chase-status-selecta.md" /> }}. Kwengface, born Ninian Fosu, started out in the Peckham collective Zone 2 and had UK chart singles of his own, among them Petrol Station.

## Impact and Reception

Tough Talk was not one of the mixtape's singles. Reviewing the record for the Evening Standard, David Smyth heard breakbeats that never rest and electronics that sound immense, music for dark, crowded rooms. Notion's cover story on Kwengface lists it among the collaborations he released in that period. Play it loud and follow the vocal fragments around the breaks.
