## Genre and Mood

**Laid Back** wrote **Gethigh**, and **Kasper Bjørke** remixed it: **Gethigh (Kasper Bjørke Remix)** runs six minutes and twenty seconds. Sound Shelter files the 12-inch under house; electronic is the plainest word for both acts, and remix names what this version is. No review describes how the remix sounds, so its feelings, euphoric and groovy, are this page's reading of a title about getting high and of a band that has made club grooves since the early 1980s.

## Song Summary

Laid Back introduced the song in September 2011 with a note from Copenhagen. The lyric may first suggest drugs, they wrote, but it is meant more widely: everyone wants to get high, in sport, politics, show business, school and love. Musically, they said, the groove belongs to "the family of the horse", their old hit White Horse, with an updated twist. Their note calls Bjørke's version "a really cool remix" and says no more about it.

## Concepts in Focus

Gethigh is a neat lesson in the difference between a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} and an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}. The digital release of 10 October 2011 holds five tracks. Four are Laid Back's own: the original, a version close to the rock-guitar song Gethigh was born as, an "ultra minimalistic" iPad mix, and a ten-minute Space Mix that the band said they made mainly for their own pleasure. The fifth is Bjørke's, the one version made by somebody outside the band. The 12-inch on Brother Music kept three: the original, this remix and the Space Mix.

## Artistic Context

John Guldberg and Tim Stahl formed Laid Back in Copenhagen in 1979. White Horse, first the B-side of Sunshine Reggae, spent three weeks at number one on Billboard's National Disco Action chart and reached number 26 on the Hot 100 in early 1984. In 2010 they started their own label, Brother Music, with Cocaine Cool. Bjørke, a Copenhagen DJ, producer and remixer, had Laid Back as guests on his 2012 album Fool; his label hfn music describes a memorable session in their studio for Bohemian Soul. Compare {{ <track_link source="laidback-beautiful-day.md" /> }}, the band in another producer's hands.
