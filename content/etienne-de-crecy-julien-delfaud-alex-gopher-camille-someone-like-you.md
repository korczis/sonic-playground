## Genre and Mood

**Etienne de Crécy**, **Julien Delfaud**, **Alex Gopher** and **Camille** made **Someone Like You** out of a French house track that was never meant to be a single. It sits between French house and electro-house, a club record with a singer in it. The feelings named here, energetic and groovy, follow from what it was built for, the dance floor; no review I found describes its mood in detail.

## Song Summary

The song is the vocal version of Fast Track, a seven-and-a-half-minute track on Super Discount 2, which de Crécy released on 27 September 2004. In the note he wrote for this upload, he says house had become uncool by 2004, which opened a door for him: music without computers, samples or radio singles; since he did not have to make singles, he asked Camille to sing on Fast Track, and that turned it into one. All four are credited as writers. The CD single followed on 11 April 2005 with a radio edit and remixes by Benny Benassi and Munk.

## Musical Breakdown

### The Machines

No source walks through the arrangement bar by bar. What the sources establish is the method: drum machines and synthesizers of the kind that started techno, and for the whole album, old analog instruments only.

### The Voice

Camille's vocal is the new element. Her discography lists the song in 2005, the year of her album Le Fil, which explored what a voice can do with almost nothing around it.

### The Long Version

The version here runs just over six minutes, the form of a club track rather than a pop song; the official video, directed by Marie de Crécy, uses a three-minute edit.

## Concepts in Focus

Camille is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} in the dance-music sense: a singer brought into a producer's track. Her presence changed the track's fate more than any production choice.

The sound rests on the {{ <concept_link name="analog synthesizer" /> }}. Super Discount 2 was made with old analog instruments such as the Roland TB-303, an answer to a moment when, by de Crécy's account, the French Touch had gone stale.

The single also shows what a {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is for: the same song cut to about three minutes for radio, beside the full version for DJs.

## Artistic Context

De Crécy went to school in Versailles with Alex Gopher and the members of Air, and later founded the Solid label with them. Every track on Super Discount 2 is named after a file-sharing network; Fast Track credits Gopher and Delfaud. Camille had sung on {{ <track_link source="nouvelle-vague-too-drunk-to-fuck.md" /> }} the year before.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 8 on the Flemish Ultratip chart and 140 in the UK. Play the album's Fast Track, then this: the same machines, and a voice that made them a song.
