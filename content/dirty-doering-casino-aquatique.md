## Genre and Mood

**Dirty Doering** released **Casino Aquatique** as a single in November 2011, and Discogs files it under tech house, the hybrid of house's swing and techno's drive. No review describes this track, so the feelings on this page, hypnotic and groovy, are a reading of the genre and of the label's own words, which Resident Advisor quotes as ranging from the narcotic allure of melodic house to the pounding heartbeats of techno.

## Song Summary

Casino Aquatique is a single track of 6:43, released digitally on 24 November 2011 under the catalogue number KATER001: the first number of Katermukke, the label Dirty Doering had just founded in Berlin. The distributor credits two composers, Velten Doering, the producer's real name, and Sascha Winkler. Its length is the most telling detail the sources give: this is a record made to be mixed, not a song cut for the radio.

## Concepts in Focus

The track belongs to Berlin {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. Dirty Doering moved to the city in 1999, hosted after-hours parties in his own flat, and was a resident DJ at Bar 25 and later at Kater Holzig, its successor; Discogs links Katermukke to Kater Blau, the club where he still plays. A label grown from a club's residents carries that club's nights into records.

The {{ <concept_link name="dj mix" /> }} is where Casino Aquatique lived on. MusicBrainz lists it in two Katermukke mixes from 2019, one by Dirty Doering himself and one in the label's series with Animal Trainer. Compare it with {{ <track_link source="mat-joe-david-keno-shamoozey-early-morning.md" /> }}, the label's hundredth release four years later.

## Artistic Context

Velten Doering was born in Leipzig in 1978, left the GDR with his mother in the 1980s and grew up in Baden-Baden, where at about fifteen he began buying records and mixing them at home. He has lived from DJing since 2004, and German Wikipedia describes his style as ranging from tech house and deep house to minimal techno. He co-founded All You Can Beat in 2006 and Katermukke in 2011, with Sascha Cawa according to Discogs and the label itself.

## Impact and Reception

No chart run or review is recorded for Casino Aquatique; its weight lies in what followed. Katermukke now describes itself as a label of melodic house and techno, deep house and indie dance, and Kleine Zeitung, quoted by Wikipedia, counts it among the most important electronic labels.
