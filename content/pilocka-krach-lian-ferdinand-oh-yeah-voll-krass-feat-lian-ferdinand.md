## Genre and Mood

**Pilocka Krach** made **Oh Yeah Voll Krass** for the club, but not for its straightest corner: it is techno and electro with a grin, and the title says so. Voll krass is German slang for something like totally wild. Playful and irreverent are how her critics hear her, and DMC World's Ben Hogwood found that sense of humour right here, in the track's electronic squiggles and breaks.

## Song Summary

Discogs credits Pilocka Krach as the track's writer and producer, and the streaming release names her as lyricist and composer, with Lian Ferdinand as featured artist. No source sets out its lyric or says what Ferdinand contributes, so this page does not guess at either. What her label Monika Enterprise describes is the shape: more a mid-tempo number of rock stage proportions than a club tool, built on a massive bass line, a wave that goes pop the moment it starts to rave.

### Main Section

The bass line is the engine, massive in the label's word, and the tempo stays in the middle rather than pushing for the peak. Around it run the squiggles and breaks Hogwood heard as jokes. Listen for where the guest is credited, and decide for yourself what the feature adds.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest named on someone else's track. Here the credit tells you almost nothing: Discogs lists Lian Ferdinand on no other release, and no source explains the role. A credit is a fact; its sound you have to hear.

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} can do the work a melody does elsewhere. The label's description puts it first: at mid-tempo a big bass part sets the swing of the whole track.

The track is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }}. In May 2012 it appeared on Bar 25: Tage Ausserhalb Der Zeit, a compilation from Bar25, the Berlin club her label counts among her first stations. In 2015 it opened her own Best Of, which gathered this and two other tracks first issued as limited 7-inches on her label.

## Artistic Context

Discogs dates the first release to 2011: a 7-inch limited to 100 copies, catalogue number 01 of her own label Greatest Hits International, with Discolights on the B-side. The digital single Oh Yeah Voll Krass / Discolight followed in April 2012. Monika Enterprise describes Pilocka Krach as a Berlin native who emerged from Vienna's noise avant-garde scene, releasing tracks since 2007, and names Bar25, the Fusion Festival and the Nation of Gondwana as her first stations. Hear the other side of the 7-inch remixed in {{ <track_link source="hrdvision-nsd-discolight.md" /> }} and another limited single collected on Best Of in {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-my-name-is-fame.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Its reception is the reception of Best Of. Hogwood gave the album four out of five and heard its humour immediately in this track; Groove's Christin Bolte called the album a collection of live hits and said it was fun to have them on one record. For a single that began as 100 copies, opening her first album is a verdict too. Put it on loud and follow the bass.
