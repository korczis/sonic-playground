## Genre and Mood

**John Newman** brought the soul and **Kove** brought the speed to **Love Me Again (Kove Remix)**. The track turns a northern-soul pop hit into drum and bass, mixing electronic energy with soul power, and its mood is energetic, euphoric and full of longing. It is a song that sounds desperate and joyful at the same time.

## Song Summary

The original is a desperate plea for a second chance, Newman's raw, gravelly voice begging a lover to love him again. Kove keeps that voice at the centre and drives it into rolling, liquid drum and bass, with fast breakbeats, a deep bassline and bright, uplifting chords. The contrast between the emotional vocal and the high-speed rhythm gives the remix its rush. Liquid drum and bass has always loved a soulful vocal, and Newman's voice, with its cracks and roughness, is a perfect fit: it sounds even more urgent at this tempo.

## Musical Breakdown

The remix follows the classic drum and bass structure, building tension before letting the beat explode.

### Intro

Bright chords and fragments of Newman's voice open the track, hinting at the hook before the drums arrive.

### Build-up

The tension rises with filtered rhythms and a growing vocal presence, pushing toward the drop.

### Main Section

The breakbeats and deep bass kick in at full speed, the vocal hook riding above the rolling groove.

### Bridge

A breakdown strips the drums away, leaving Newman's voice and the chords to carry the emotion.

### Climax

The beat returns with full force, the chorus and the rhythm combining into the track's most euphoric moment.

### Outro

The drums fade out, leaving the chords and a last echo of the voice.

## Artistic Context

John Newman is a singer-songwriter from Settle in North Yorkshire, whose voice and retro-soul style first reached a wide audience on Rudimental's "Feel the Love". "Love Me Again" was his debut solo single in 2013 and a centrepiece of his album *Tribute*. Kove is a British drum and bass producer, and his remix was among the versions released alongside the single. Remixes like this were a common way to carry a pop hit into club and festival culture, and this one stands on its own as a strong piece of drum and bass.

## Impact and Reception

"Love Me Again" went to number one in the UK, and the Kove remix found its own audience in drum and bass circles, spreading through channels such as UKF. It is here for its double energy: soul power and breakbeat rush. Turn it up and let it run.
