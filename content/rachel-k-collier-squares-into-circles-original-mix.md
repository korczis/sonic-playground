## Genre and Mood

**Rachel K Collier**'s **Squares Into Circles** is the original mix of a song from her first EP, an electronic track that she sings and wrote the words for. It is filed under electronica by Apple Music, and Mixmag's review heard it as one of the EP's two songs for a house party playlist. That is where its energetic, upbeat feeling comes from on this page: it is the EP's turn towards the dance floor.

## Song Summary

Words You Never Heard came out on Love & Other on 23 October 2015, four tracks long. Mixmag's Chantelle Fiddy gave it 8 out of 10, singling out the laid-back Nothing Is Forever, co-produced by DJ S.K.T, and the slowed-down title track, which she said showed great power in Collier's voice; Squares Into Circles and Moth, she wrote, would sit nicely on a house party playlist. Collier wrote the words. Apple Music credits the song and its production to her and Benny Benski, while YouTube's metadata lists an arranger, Erik Machado; the services do not agree in detail.

## Musical Breakdown

### Main Section

No review we found describes the track itself beyond Mixmag's one line, so this page describes less than usual and does not guess at its parts. What can be said is its frame: a sung track of four and a half minutes, which Mixmag set apart from the EP's laid-back and slowed-down songs. Listen for the voice first and the groove second.

## Concepts in Focus

The credits make this a lesson about the {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }}. Ableton's profile says Collier first gained recognition as a featured artist and topline writer, the person who brings the melody and words to someone else's beat, before becoming known for her own productions and live performance with Live. On this debut EP she is credited as a producer of her own song, the step from being the voice on a track to building it.

Two weeks later, on 6 November 2015, the label released Words You Never Heard (Remixes), with versions by Røse and Rhythm District. Each {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks the same song for another context; the Rhythm District remix also went onto a compilation, Poolside Ibiza 2016. Røse later worked with Collier again, on Slo Mover, released on Love & Other in 2018.

## Artistic Context

Collier is an electronic producer and performer from Swansea in Wales. Mixmag noted that before this EP she had developed her talents working with Wookie, Ray Foxx, and Mat Zo and Fineart; Ableton names a UK top 20 single and a Grammy nomination from her work with Mat Zo. She later released her debut album RKC and built a YouTube audience for her production and performance tutorials. Hear her on the same label as a featured singer on {{ <track_link source="ryan-blyth_once-twice-feat-rachel-k-collier.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Mixmag's review is the only press notice we found, and it gave the EP a warm 8 out of 10. Squares Into Circles is the sound of a singer stepping into the producer's chair. Play it, then the slowed title track, and hear the range of one debut.
