## Genre and Mood

**Røse** and **Rachel K Collier** made **Craving** as house for the dance floor: Apple Music files it under house, and Discobelle, which premiered it, placed Røse among the breakthrough stars of the future house scene. The blog's own words give the mood, a huge, stomping dancefloor moment that it called hypnotizing. Energetic and hypnotic are those two halves, the stomp and the trance it puts you in.

## Song Summary

Craving came out on 26 August 2016 as a single of its own, released by Røse Music under exclusive licence to the label Too Pop, and runs three minutes and twenty-five seconds. Discobelle premiered it two days earlier and wrote that Røse was teaming up once again with Collier, so this was not the pair's first record together. No source describes the lyric or the arrangement beyond the blog's few words, and none credits a writer, so this page keeps to what is on record.

## Concepts in Focus

The credits are a small lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }}. Too Pop's upload and Røse's own SoundCloud title the track Craving ft. Rachel K Collier, the usual form for a singer on a producer's record. Spotify, Apple Music and Deezer instead list her as a main artist next to Røse. The music is the same; only the billing changes.

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} followed in April 2017, when Too Pop released Craving (Embody Remix) as a single, a few seconds shorter than the original. A remix by another producer hands DJs a second version of the same song, and gives it a second release.

## Artistic Context

Discobelle described Røse as a house producer based in Lyon with three number ones on the blog aggregator Hype Machine and remixes for Years & Years, AlunaGeorge and Just Kiddin; his SoundCloud calls him the rose that makes you move. Collier is a producer and performer from Swansea who studied creative music technology at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama and sang on Ray Foxx's UK top 20 hit Boom Boom (Heartbeat) in 2013. Ableton says she first made her name as a featured artist and topline writer; hear that role on {{ <track_link source="ryan-blyth_once-twice-feat-rachel-k-collier.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

No review beyond the premiere covered Craving, and no source reports a chart place or stream count. Its spread was the blog circuit of its time: a premiere, a Hype Machine link and a label upload.
