## Genre and Mood

**Reptile Youth**'s **Speeddance** is dance-punk with a post-punk spine and an electro pulse, energetic, rebellious and deliberately provocative. Its label's announcement called it furious, sexy and provocative, three minutes of high-octane pop punk; Reflections of Darkness placed the band in the post-punk and synth-pop world.

## Song Summary

Speeddance was the Danish duo's debut single, released on 19 March 2012 as a 7-inch and a download. Singer Mads Damsgaard explained it in the release notes as a reaction to a world of conformity, an encouragement for his generation to riot against it: sometimes you need to lose yourself to find your way, and Speeddance is that way of losing control. It lasts 2:57, on the single as on the album.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song section by section or gives times; this guide keeps to what the label's text and the reviewers describe.

### Intro: The Bass

The song starts, in Best New Bands' description, with a funky bass line and romping percussion. The label credits Esben Valløe's elastic bass, which fires the track out of the traps with kinetic urgency.

### The Hook

The bass gives way to what the label called Mads' anthemic, semi-deranged vocal hook.

### The Pulse

Underneath runs an electro pulse that the label heard as a pure rock and roll statement; Reflections of Darkness wrote that the track ups the ante with absurd kinetics.

### The Length

It ends quickly. Best New Bands noted it as one of the shortest tracks on the album: no filler, just raw, reckless energy.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} usually supports a rock song; here it drives it. The bass, not a guitar, is what you follow first and what keeps the pace.

{{ <concept_link name="post-punk" /> }} took punk's urgency and opened it to synthesizers, dance rhythms and stranger voices. The label's text names Reptile Youth the bastard sons of Iggy Pop, Suicide and the Jesus Lizard; the same idea of punk made for the dance floor runs through {{ <track_link source="lcd-soundsystem-dance-yrself-clean.md" /> }}.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Rasmus Weng Karlsen, was shot at Vega in Copenhagen, at Roskilde Festival and at SPOT Festival. It shows the band on stage, the one whose gigs, by the label's account, bordered on barely contained chaos.

## Artistic Context

Reptile Youth formed in Denmark in 2009 around vocalist Mads Damsgaard Kristiansen and bassist Esben Valløe, previously known as Reptile and Retard; Wikipedia places them in Aarhus. The Danish producer Kasper Bjørke worked with them as A&R, executive producer and co-manager. Their self-titled debut album, on hfn music in September 2012, was produced in London by Dave M. Allen (The Cure, Sisters of Mercy) and Mark Ralph (Hot Chip), with Speeddance as its fourth track. The single's B-side was a remake by the Norwegian band Deathcrush.

## Impact and Reception

Best New Bands, unconvinced by much of the album, called Speeddance its highlight, the song that won the band so many fans and the track that should have shaped the record. Good Because Danish wrote that it all started from this single. Play it loud, and let the bass set the pace.
