## Genre and Mood

**Bobby Nourmand**'s **Smokin' Joe** sits where deep house meets disco and blues, a laid-back groove with a smoky, after-hours feel. The mood is groovy, chill and hypnotic: dance music that smoulders rather than explodes.

## Song Summary

Smokin' Joe is a study in cool restraint. A sweet, rolling disco bassline carries the track, a bluesy vocal drifts over it and subtle melodic touches fill in the corners without ever crowding the groove. The result is moody but warm, the kind of track that works as well on a terrace at sunset as it does in a dark club at the start of the night. Its title suits it perfectly: relaxed, a little mysterious, and impossibly cool.

## Musical Breakdown

The edit is short and tight, getting to its groove quickly and keeping it there with only small shifts along the way.

### Intro

A crisp kick and light percussion establish an easy, mid-tempo pulse, with a hint of warmth in the chords.

### Build-up

The disco bassline slides in, funky and supple, and the groove settles into a laid-back strut.

### Main Section

The bluesy vocal arrives, soulful and slightly weary, floating over the bass while subtle melodic layers shimmer in the background.

### Bridge

The beat steps back for a moment and the vocal and chords hang in the air, a brief pause that heightens the return of the bass.

### Climax

The full groove comes back together, bass, beat and vocal locked in, the track at its most hypnotic and danceable.

### Outro

The layers fall away gradually, leaving the bassline to roll out on its own.

## Artistic Context

Bobby Nourmand released Smokin' Joe in early 2016 on Potion, the label run by the Belgian DJ and producer The Magician, whose taste for melodic, soulful house it matches. The track was distributed through Spinnin' Records' channels and released in an edit as well as an extended mix for DJs. Nourmand followed it the same year with Deep, which was later reworked as Deep in NY with New York rappers.

## Impact and Reception

Smokin' Joe was one of the standout releases of early 2016 for many deep house tastemakers, praised for its cool vibe and its blend of disco bass and bluesy vocals. It belongs on this playground as a perfect example of how much atmosphere a simple groove can create. Play it as the evening begins and let it set the tone.
