## Genre and Mood

**Antiloop** made **In My Mind** for the dance floors of 1997, and Discogs files its first release under electronic music with the styles trance, techno, Euro house and acid. It is the duo's most successful single: hypnotic in the way it repeats three words and little else, energetic as a club single of that year had to be. No published review describes its beat in detail, so this page keeps to what the sources say.

## Song Summary

In My Mind was released in Sweden by Stockholm Records and Fluid Records, and in the rest of Europe by Polydor, as the lead single from Antiloop's debut album LP. Its ambient opening is taken from Hold That Sucker Down (Builds Like a Skyscraper Mix), released in 1994 by The O.T. Quartet, the project of Rollo Armstrong, later a founder of Faithless. Its only lyric, the title, comes from Warehouse (Days of Glory), a 1989 record by New Deep Societys. Both sources are credited among the writers on Wikipedia's listing, beside Antiloop's David Westerlund and Robin Söderman.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

The track begins with the sampled ambient passage from The O.T. Quartet, a calm start before the dance track proper.

### Main Section

The words in my mind return as the track's one vocal idea. How the beat and the synthesizer parts are programmed around it is not described in any source I found, so listen for yourself to what changes under the repeating phrase.

### Versions

The 3:28 radio edit opens the CD single; the extended version runs 6:42. The 12-inch added a Chamber Of Sound Dub and remixes by Poor Impulse Control and Redtop for DJs.

## Concepts in Focus

In My Mind is a compact lesson in {{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }}. Two fragments of older records, an ambient opening and a three-word vocal, carry most of its identity, and the producers' work lies in what they build around them. That is why the sampled artists are listed among the writers.

The {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} shows how one dance track lives in two worlds. The 3:28 edit suits radio and television; the versions over six minutes give a DJ long stretches to mix in and out of.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} is black and white except for details in blue. A girl walks through Stockholm in sunglasses and headphones, and the duo appear only as reflections, one in each lens.

## Artistic Context

Robin Söderman and David Westerlund grew up in Lidingö near Stockholm and played as nightclub DJs before they formed Antiloop in 1994; the name joins the Swedish word for antelope with loop. LP, released in June 1997, reached number eight in Sweden, went gold there and won the Grammis for modern dance of the year. The duo split in 2002.

## Impact and Reception

In My Mind reached number six in Sweden, four in Norway, nine in Finland and thirteen in France, where it sold over 200,000 copies, and it won Best Dance Single at the Swedish Dance Music Awards in February 1998. It later turned up in the Swedish film Sherdil and the video game FIFA Football 2003. Start with the radio edit, then try the extended version and hear what the extra three minutes are for.
