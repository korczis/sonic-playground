## Genre and Mood

**Gin Wigmore**'s **Dirty Love** is rock with the blues running through it, part of an album Wikipedia files under alternative rock, dark cabaret and folk rock. Femme Metal Webzine called it another rock-blues song with a sexy vibe, and that sets the feelings: sultry, and confident enough to say so in under three minutes.

## Song Summary

"Dirty Love" is the seventh of eleven tracks on Gravel & Wine, released in New Zealand on 7 November 2011, and at 2:52 one of its shortest. Wigmore wrote it with the American producer and songwriter Mike Viola. The title states its subject, and it fits the album as AllMusic's Steve Leggett described it: songs about men who don't measure up, jealous girls and the trashy, dangerous side of love. The detail one reviewer took from it was cinematic. Writing for Music Collision when the album reached the United States in 2013, the blogger said it would not be out of place in a James Bond film.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the song section by section, so this breakdown names what the reviews heard and leaves the map of its parts to your ears.

### The Groove

Rock-blues, in Femme Metal Webzine's words, with a sexy vibe. Listen for where the blues sits: in the riff, the rhythm or the voice.

### The Voice

Wigmore is known for a high-pitched, raspy voice, and the song is short enough that the voice carries nearly all of it. Hear how the rasp changes the word "dirty".

### The Band

Butch Walker produced the album with his backing band, The Black Widows, and AllMusic heard big, stomping drums throughout.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="blues" /> }} is the oldest layer here. Before recording, Wigmore spent two months in Mississippi and Alabama looking for Southern inspiration, and Femme Metal Webzine called the result a very good record for anyone who likes Southern sound and style. Dirty Love is where that trip meets a rock band.

A {{ <concept_link name="record producer" /> }} shapes an album's sound as a whole. Butch Walker produced all of Gravel & Wine in Santa Monica in 2011 with his own band, and co-wrote several songs, among them {{ <track_link source="gin-wigmore-man-like-that.md" /> }}. Dirty Love comes from a different partnership: Mike Viola sang the title song of the film That Thing You Do! and co-produced it with Adam Schlesinger, and wrote songs for the comedy Walk Hard.

## Artistic Context

Gin Wigmore, born in 1986, is a New Zealand singer-songwriter. Gravel & Wine was her second album, after Holy Smoke in 2009; it debuted at number one in New Zealand and went double platinum there. Its US release on 2 April 2013 reached number 87 on the Billboard 200, and she toured that summer's Vans Warped Tour.

## Impact and Reception

Dirty Love was not one of the album's singles, but reviewers singled it out: rock-blues with a sexy vibe for Femme Metal Webzine, Bond material for Music Collision. Turn it up and decide which.
