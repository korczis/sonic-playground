## Song Summary

Modus came out on 1 October 2019 as a one-track single, 3:32 long, under the label line De La Suda Records, a name that matches the artist's own. The streaming credits, supplied to YouTube by the distributor recordJet, name Constantin Stefanou as composer and lyricist and Robin Simion on violin. That last credit is the one detail that sets the record apart on paper: house producers have mostly worked with drum machines and samples rather than hired players, and here a violinist is named.

### The Beat

House rests on a kick drum on every beat of a 4/4 bar, snare or claps on two and four and hi-hats on the off-beats, usually somewhere between 115 and 130 beats per minute.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" /> }} kick is the pulse of house: one steady thump on every beat, so the body always knows where the next one lands and everything else can move around it.

The {{ <concept_link name="house groove" /> }} is what grows on top of that pulse: off-beat hi-hats, claps on two and four, a bassline and whatever melody the producer adds. In a genre that grew up on cheap electronic equipment and samples, a bowed instrument changes the texture, and {{ <track_link source="phil-kieran-blinded-by-the-sun-original-mix.md" /> }} shows one way a violin can sit inside a house record. Listen to Modus for how its violin and its groove meet.

## Artistic Context

House began in Chicago in the early 1980s, when DJs and producers of the city's Black and queer underground clubs reworked disco into a more mechanical beat; cheap gear and independent labels carried it from there. De la Suda's catalogue sits in that independent tradition. Modus is the earliest release on the artist's Apple Music page, followed by a run of singles and an EP, from 0711 Funk and Fantasie in 2020 to Flux, Bleu and Steps, filed as house or electronic music, most of them under the same De La Suda Records line.
