## Genre and Mood

**Brodinski** made **Let the Beat Control Your Body** for the dancefloor, and gave its voice to **Louisahhh**. Resident Advisor placed him among the Paris electro producers, with a taste for house and UK bass; the genres named here, techno and electro, follow that. The feelings, energetic and hypnotic, are this page's reading of a title that is an order to the dancer; no review describes how the track sounds.

## Song Summary

The track is the second half of Bromance #1, catalogue number BRO-001, released on 21 November 2011: Gesaffelstein's Control Movement on one side, Brodinski's Let the Beat Control Your Body featuring Louisahhh on the other. It was the first release of Bromance, the label Brodinski launched after the death of DJ Mehdi in September 2011, with Manu Barron and Guillaume Berg.

## Concepts in Focus

Louisahhh is the {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} here, the guest credited on another artist's track. Bromance signed her from late 2011, and Fact described Nobody Rules The Streets, on Bromance #3 in June 2012, as a reunion of the pair after this one.

The track's career shows {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }} at work. In May 2012 Magnetic Magazine's Yosh admitted he had been "caught sleeping" on it "as it grew from a WMC banger to one the biggest songs of Coachella": spread by DJs from the Winter Music Conference in Miami to a California festival, without a chart in sight.

Later Louisahhh made her own {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} of it with Maelstrom, the first track of their Translations EP, presented by Zone and Bromance: the featured singer turned remixer.

## Artistic Context

Brodinski, born Louis Rogé in Reims, later turned towards Atlanta rap on his 2015 album Brava, and eventually closed Bromance. Louisahhh went on to her own techno, heard on {{ <track_link source="louisahhh-like-a-vice-x.md" /> }} and in Vitalic's hands on {{ <track_link source="lousianahh-love-is-a-punk-vitalic-remix.md" /> }}.
