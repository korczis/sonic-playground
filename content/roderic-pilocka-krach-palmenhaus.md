## Genre and Mood

**Roderic** and **Pilocka Krach** made **Palmenhaus** for a club that could not open. Its label tags the track melodic house and techno, downbeat and electronica, which is why this page files it under melodic house, downtempo and electronic music. No review describes how it sounds. The feelings here, longing and uplifting, are a reading of the album's own words: artists who miss the stage, and music meant to lift the spirits of people stuck at home.

## Song Summary

Palmenhaus runs 5:48 and appeared in spring 2020 on Give 'em a future, a 29-track tribute album for the Berlin club Kater Blau, presented by its Blau Konsortium on the club's in-house label, Kiosk ID. The composer credits name Pilocka Krach and Rodrigo Emiliano Ortiz Otero, Roderic's full name. Its title is German for a palm house, the glasshouse where palms grow. ## Concepts in Focus

Give 'em a future is a {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} of a particular kind: not old singles gathered later, but new tracks by artists from one club's orbit, from Oliver Koletzki and Marco Resmann to Dirty Doering and Wareika, curated by Peter Schumann and mastered by Sascha Cawa (heard with Dirty Doering in {{ <track_link source="dirty-doering-sascha-cawa-ding-dong.md" /> }}).

It is also a {{ <concept_link name="charity record" /> }}. The label wrote that every featured artist gifted their music in support of a club that would stay closed until further notice, and that every track sold went a long way in helping it.

Behind both stands {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. The album text calls music, eyes closed and dancing, a collective therapy in times of quarantine, and asks listeners to party at home until the doors reopen. Kater Blau's world reaches back through Katermukke to {{ <track_link source="dirty-doering-casino-aquatique.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

Pilocka Krach, born in Berlin-Köpenick, runs her own label, Greatest Hits International; her publisher calls her the electric rock star from Berlin. Roderic is the Mexican producer Rodrigo Ortiz, whose albums came out on Katermukke. On It All Depends Remixes, released there in December 2019, Pilocka Krach remixed his Choses qui se passent. Palmenhaus followed months later, and the pair met again on her twelve-inch Joy in {{ <track_link source="pilocka-krach-roderic-rain-in-the-desert.md" /> }}.
