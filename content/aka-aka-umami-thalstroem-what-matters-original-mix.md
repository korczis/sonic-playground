## Genre and Mood

**AKA AKA**, **Umami** and **Thalstroem** made **What Matters** for the long night: a house track of more than seven minutes from a duo known for minimal house, with a singing duo and a wind player on top. The mood the label describes for their album is floating and summery, and this page files it as dreamy and carefree, though no reviewer we found wrote about this track itself.

## Song Summary

What Matters is the fourth track on Varieté, the debut album AKA AKA made with Thalstroem, released on their own label Burlesque Musique in 2011, where it is listed as featuring umami. The label called it the second collaboration between AKA AKA and umami. A single followed in August 2011 with this original mix and remixes by Hanne & Lore, Broombeck and Philip Bader. What the lyric says, no source describes, and no review walks through the track, so this page keeps to what was written about the people and the release.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes What Matters part by part. This breakdown follows the three elements the credits and the label name.

### Production

AKA AKA build the track. The duo, Hannes and Holger, met behind the decks in 2008, have worked as AKA AKA since 2009 and are known for minimal house; they produce in Ableton.

### Vocals

umami, Robert and Sam, sing their own songs and play electronic percussion live. They describe their music as techno with an emphasis on songs rather than tracks.

### Trumpet and EWI

Thalstroem plays the trumpet and the EWI, an electronic wind instrument that plays somewhat like a saxophone. The label said he adds a new, floating and harmonic layer over AKA AKA's productions.

## Concepts in Focus

The title's "Original Mix" names the first full version of a club track, and here it works like an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }}: over seven minutes, made for DJs to mix in and out of. A radio edit of under four minutes appeared on a 2013 compilation.

umami are credited as a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} on the album, and on the single's own upload they stand beside AKA AKA and Thalstroem as equals; how the credit is written changes where a track is filed.

The single carried three versions by other producers, each a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }}, and Varieté Remixed in April 2012 added more by Mihalis Safras, Joris Delacroix and Tube & Berger. The duo remix others too: in 2015 they and Thalstroem remixed Solee's Phoenix, heard here in another remix, {{ <track_link source="solee-unique-repeat-phoenix-unique-repeat-remix.md" /> }}.

## Artistic Context

AKA AKA and Thalstroem first played together at Watergate in March 2010 and then toured Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Besides club tracks, Varieté has more melodic pieces, among them a cover of Portishead's The Rip with the Austrian singer Betty Lenard, and guests including the drummer Lizzy and the percussionist Manu Manou.

## Impact and Reception

We found no chart entry or review for What Matters. Its afterlife is in its versions: a single of remixes, a remix album the duo said gathered 31 remixers, and a radio edit years later. Give it all seven minutes and listen for the wind player.
