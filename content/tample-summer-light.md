## Genre and Mood

**Tample**'s **Summer Light** is indie pop with one foot in rock and the other in synth-pop. The album's release notes describe the Bordeaux quartet's sound as both rock and synthetic, rhythmic and romantic, and the song wears all four words: guitars and synths over a bass made for moving, in a mood that is upbeat, carefree and quietly uplifting.

## Song Summary

The lyric, published with the official video, is a summer road song with a question at its centre. The singer is on the road, excited, walking alone in summertime; a light seems to stop him whenever he hears a certain sound. Then the chorus asks whether anybody knows what we are looking for, and in its second half what we are living for. Nothing is answered, and that is the charm: a holiday feeling turned into a small shrug you can dance to.

## Musical Breakdown

No source describes the recording passage by passage, so this walk-through follows the published lyric and leaves the detail to your ears.

### Verse 1

The first verse sets the scene in a few plain images: the road, the excitement, the walk alone, the light.

### Verse 2

The second verse turns inward. The singer cannot sleep because a picture keeps coming back, and the light is now a person, the one by which he is flying.

### Chorus

The chorus is the hook: a question, not a slogan. Its one-word change, from looking for to living for, is the song's whole argument, and it returns after the first verse comes back.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} here is a question. KEXP, which filmed Tample live at the Trans Musicales festival in Rennes in December 2017, singled out the band's catchy vocal hooks alongside its percussion, danceable basslines, synths and guitar, and in Summer Light the hook is the chorus's question, repeated until it sticks.

Summer Light is also a case of {{ <concept_link name="sync licensing" /> }}. Citroën's press release for its new Berlingo Van campaign, directed by Justin Reardon and broadcast in France from 17 November 2018, credits the music to Tample's Summer Light. A song from an independent label was suddenly heard on French television, the route that took {{ <track_link source="lenka-everything-at-once.md" /> }} to a far larger audience.

The official {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, directed by Mathieu Echeverri of Genepifilm, appeared on the band's channel the same month. It is an edit of under three minutes, against almost five for the album track.

## Artistic Context

Tample come from Bordeaux. Summer Light is the title track of their first album, released on 26 January 2018 by Yotanka Records, recorded in July 2017 by Sébastien Lohro and mixed by John Ducasse. The line-up on the album is Samuel Rosas on vocals, guitar and synth, Gérald Daragnes on drums, Frédéric Laclau on bass and guitar and Nicolas Amen on synth and percussion. The song had already appeared on their 2015 EP My River. The release notes compare them to Hot Chip and recommend them to fans of {{ <track_link source="kid-francescoli-come-online.md" /> }} and {{ <track_link source="camp-claude-old-downtown.md" /> }}.
