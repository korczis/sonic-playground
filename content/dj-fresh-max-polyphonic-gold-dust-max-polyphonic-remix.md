## Genre and Mood

**DJ Fresh** made **Gold Dust** as drum and bass, and **Max Polyphonic** reworked it as **Gold Dust (Max Polyphonic Remix)**, the last of six versions on the 2012 single. This page files it under remix and electronic music rather than a narrower style, because no review or interview describes how this remix sounds. The feelings, energetic and carefree, are the song's own: a dance record whose video is all skipping ropes, skateboards and free running.

## Song Summary

Gold Dust has lived many lives. It began on 16 August 2008 as a 7:51 instrumental 12-inch on Breakbeat Kaos. On 1 August 2010 it came back with a vocal by Ce'cile. In 2012 a version with Ms. Dynamite went onto DJ Fresh's album Nextlevelism, and on 2 December Ministry of Sound released Shy FX's re-edit as a single, with remixes by TC, WestFunk & Steve Smart, Kutz, Riddim Commission and Max Polyphonic, whose version closes the download.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} hands a finished song to another producer. On this single six producers took Gold Dust in six directions; Dancing Astronaut heard Shy FX's lead version as drum and bass, jungle and even reggae. Set this remix beside {{ <track_link source="dj-fresh-kutz-gold-dust-kutz-full-vocal-mix.md" /> }}, released the same day, and hear how far two producers can travel from the same parts.

Gold Dust is also a lesson in the {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: an instrumental, a Ce'cile vocal, a Ms. Dynamite vocal, a re-edit. The remixes sit at the edge of that family, versions by other hands rather than by DJ Fresh himself.

## Artistic Context

DJ Fresh is Dan Stein, credited as the song's sole writer and producer. Max Polyphonic is credited only as the remixer; none of the sources consulted here says more about him, so this page does not either.

## Impact and Reception

The Shy FX re-edit reached number 22 in the UK and 39 in Ireland, and the BPI has certified the song triple platinum across all its versions. For its tenth anniversary, in 2022, a new set of remixes followed, among them one by Bou & Used ({{ <track_link source="bou-all-u-gotta-do.md" /> }}). This remix left no trace in the press; its life is on the single and on DJ Fresh's channel. Press play and decide where it belongs in the family.
