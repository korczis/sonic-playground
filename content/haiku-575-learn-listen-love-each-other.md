## Genre and Mood

**Haiku 575**'s **Learn Listen Love Each Other** is deep house that leans towards minimal: electronic, hypnotic and relaxed, eight minutes long and in no hurry. Decoded Magazine described the compilation it came out on as organic, deep, hooky, laid back, dub-techno and minimal house, and the duo's own profile promises a sound on the minimal side with a deep, melodic groove.

## Song Summary

The track is credited to Ciro Napolitano and Massimiliano Abbatangelo, the two producers behind Haiku 575, and was released on 27 October 2017 as the second of ten tracks on a digital compilation from the German label Dantze. Its title reads like a line of a poem, which fits the act's method: their profile describes three tracks per project whose three titles together compose a poem.

### The Setting

The track sits between Aaron Ahrends' My Click and a Niconé remix of Philip Bader's Bonita, on a compilation that Discogs files under deep house, progressive house and techno.

### The Length

At 8:04 it is the longest of the ten tracks in Discogs' timings, a length that leaves room for slow development.

### The Palette

In an interview a week before the release, the duo described their tools: guitar pedals, mono synths, a real bass guitar run through pedals, Mexican shakers and an Elektron Analog 4. They did not say which of these this track uses.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="compilation album" /> }} lets a label show its sound through many artists at once. This one also shows how such records get muddled: Dantze's own SoundCloud, Discogs and Decoded Magazine call it Dantze, Baby III, with the catalogue number DTZ086, while streaming services and MusicBrainz list the same ten tracks as Dantze, Baby II. Its opener is reviewed here as {{ <track_link source="aaron-ahrends-my-click.md" /> }}.

A {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} is what a long minimal track lives on. With few events over eight minutes, the way the rhythm parts lock together has to carry the listener, and that deep, melodic groove is exactly what Haiku 575 name as their aim.

## Artistic Context

Haiku 575 is based in Barcelona. M_Abbatangelo, Massi in interviews, manages and co-owns the label This And That Lab with Davide Squillace; Cipy is a veteran producer long associated with Get Physical and Kindisch. Their profile explains the name: a haiku has three lines of five, seven and five syllables, and for them five are the lines of a staff, seven the notes, five the senses. Dantze is run by the producers Philip Bader and Niconé.
