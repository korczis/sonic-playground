## Genre and Mood

**Chase & Status** open their 2023 mixtape with **Selecta**, a high-energy drum and bass track, in Songfacts' words, with a dancehall-inspired vocal from the British rapper Stefflon Don. Qobuz heard the guest turn as brassy and ragga-tinged. The mood is energetic, intense and confident from the first bar: a track built to start a night, and the one the duo use to start their shows.

## Song Summary

Two things carry Selecta. The first is a sample: the distorted guitar riff of Ashanti's 2004 hit Only U, which Saul Milton told Apple Music the duo had wanted to use for a long time, because it has a sense of grandeur and recalls an era when people fell in love with music of every genre. The second is Stefflon Don, whose lyric speaks Jamaican dancehall slang. A selector is the person who controls the music at a dancehall, and a dub plate is a one-off pressing a DJ plays as an exclusive; her verses follow a partygoer asking the selector for one so he can show off on the floor.

## Musical Breakdown

Beyond the riff, the voice and the drum and bass pulse, the sources do not map the track's sections, so this breakdown follows those three elements.

### Intro

The guitar riff comes first. Milton singled out the opening riff as part of what makes the track special to the band.

### Verses

Stefflon Don takes the front, calling for the selector over the beat; Chase & Status had written a track for her own record, and with her permission reworked its lyrics into Selecta.

### Main Section

The drum and bass engine runs at full energy beneath the riff and the voice, the tone Qobuz said the opener sets for the whole mixtape.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="sampling" /> }} explains the long list of writers on the release: beside Will Kennard, Saul Milton and Stefflon Don, the credits add six more names, among them Ashanti Douglas, whose hit the riff comes from. A sample brings its authors with it.

A {{ <concept_link name="riff" /> }} is a short figure that anchors a song. Here it is a rock-sounding guitar figure lifted from an R&B record and set over drum and bass, as {{ <track_link source="basement-jaxx-good-luck.md" /> }} set a hard guitar riff under a guest singer.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is the other half. The duo told BBC Radio 1 they like working with artists they have not worked with before, and that getting Stefflon on a drum and bass song was a coup.

## Artistic Context

2 RUFF, Vol. 1 came out on EMI on 10 November 2023, a day after Selecta appeared as a single. Qobuz describes a short mixtape rather than an album, meant to feel like a batch of raw dub plates, with guests on almost every track and less drill than the duo's previous record. Mixtape Madness framed it as part of their effort to merge UK rap with drum and bass.

## Impact and Reception

Qobuz counted 2 RUFF, Vol. 1 as Chase & Status's fifth UK top-ten album, and Selecta became their live opener. Start it loud and let the riff set the pace.
