## Genre and Mood

**Kollektiv Turmstrasse** made Grillen im Garten for the club, and **Grillen im Garten - Gerberei Version** is one of several recordings of the track. Discogs files the compilation that carries it under tech house and minimal, and the 2007 12-inch Grillen Im Park under techno, minimal and tech house; listeners on Last.fm tag the compilation deep and minimal techno. Hypnotic and groovy is what those styles aim for, and that is the basis of the two feelings here. No published review describes this version, so the review rests on the release facts.

## Song Summary

The Gerberei Version runs seven minutes and two seconds and is the seventh of eleven tracks on Musik Gewinnt Freunde Collection, a digital compilation released on 22 July 2013 by the label Musik Gewinnt Freunde under the catalogue number MGF_023. Deezer credits the track to Nico Plagemann and Christian Hilscher as composers. The compilation brings together tracks from different years, among them Elemental Pleasure, which Discogs lists on the label's first 12-inch of 2006, a record that names neither its artist nor its tracks.

## Concepts in Focus

The Gerberei Version is one {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} among several. Discogs and MusicBrainz list a Grillen Im Park on a 12-inch that Ostwind Records released in June 2007, credited to the duo as writers and producers and running about nine minutes. A Grillen Im Garten of six minutes and fifty-six seconds opens Living For The Night, a 2009 vinyl compilation on a label called Gerberei, whose name the Gerberei Version carries; MusicBrainz lists it as a separate recording, and no source says how the two differ. A Grillen Im Garten (Original Mix), also seven minutes and two seconds long, is on a 2011 compilation by Wormland Music; again, no source says whether it is this recording, which is why this page embeds no video.

Other producers have reworked it, each {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} under the Park title: Dreher & Smart on the B-side of the 2007 record, and Bart Skils and Kabale und Liebe on Grillen Im Park (Remixes) in 2010. Deezer files the Bart Skils remix on a release titled Grillen im Garten, which suggests that both titles name one track. Compare {{ <track_link source="kollektiv-turmstrasse_on-the-streets-let-freedom.md" /> }}, the duo remixing someone else in the same years.

## Artistic Context

According to Discogs, Nico Plagemann and Christian Hilscher founded Kollektiv Turmstrasse in Wismar, naming it after the street where both lived, and later moved to Hamburg; Hilscher has since left, and the project is now Plagemann's alone. Wikipedia lists the albums Verrückte Welt (2006) and Rebellion der Träumer (2010).

## Impact and Reception

Grillen im Garten is not a chart record. Its reception shows in how many labels pressed it, compiled it or had it remixed between 2007 and 2013. Give the Gerberei Version its full seven minutes.
