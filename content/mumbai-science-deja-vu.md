## Genre and Mood

**Mumbai Science** made their name with fast club hits of electro and techno, and **Déjà Vu**, the single that gave its name to their first album, slows that sound down to make a joke with an edge. Their press materials called it rough and dirty but playful and quirky; the lyric makes it satirical. The mood is cool, deadpan, more raised eyebrow than raised hands.

## Song Summary

The label described the song as a trip to a grim future where partying has been reduced to handing out business cards and personal trainers are the new superstars. The guest vocalist April Rose plays the part of a fictitious can't-be-bothered woman roaming the world of the economic elite, in what the label called a casual, slightly blasé recording. The themes are heavy, moral decline and corporate totalitarianism, but Maarten Elen and Jonas Kiesekoms deliver them, in their label's words, with their usual quirky panache.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

No source describes how the track opens, so listen for how long the duo holds back before the voice arrives.

### Main Section

The Dutch site Festivalinfo heard a laid-back atmosphere, slower than the high gear the duo usually works in, with April Rose's lines delivered without much uplift. That flatness is the character the label described, a woman too bored to care.

## Concepts in Focus

April Rose is a {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} in everything but the credit: the single is released under Mumbai Science's name alone, yet the whole song depends on her voice and the character she plays.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, premiered by THUMP in April 2014, was directed by Ehsan Bhatti and produced by Wanda London, giving the satire a visual world of its own.

The single also came with two remixes that pull it apart in opposite directions. In the description of the duo's publicists, the {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} by Loops of Fury is a high-voltage festival take, while BS1 turns it into a basement techno track.

## Artistic Context

Mumbai Science are the Belgian duo Maarten Elen and Jonas Kiesekoms. They sent their early demos to Dr Lektroluv, who released their singles on his label; Brodinski, heard here in {{ <track_link source="brodinski-louisahhh-let-the-beat-control-your-body.md" /> }}, picked out their first one. Déjà Vu came out on 5 May 2014, and the album followed on 16 June with guests including Peaches, Gustaph of Hercules and Love Affair, DJ Spank-Spank of Phuture and Mixhell.

## Impact and Reception

Mixmag made the album its electro album of the month. Festivalinfo's Jasper Klomp was harsher: for him the title track was where the record first went wrong, and he heard the duo lean too often on the sleaze of Modeselektor's Evil Twin without the Germans' finesse. Listen and decide whether the boredom is a flaw or the joke.
