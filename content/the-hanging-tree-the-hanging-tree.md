## Genre and Mood

**The Hanging Tree** by **The Hanging Tree** is a sound-alike recording of the song from The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1, released under the artist name "The Hanging Tree" by Blue Charts Records. It is not the James Newton Howard and Jennifer Lawrence recording. No store or database classifies it, so its genres follow from the credits alone: a film song performed by someone else. Its feelings, haunting and dark, belong to the song, a murder ballad, and not to anything a source has said about this version.

## Song Summary

The single appeared on 12 December 2014 with one track of 3:38, ℗ and © Blue Charts Records, according to Spotify; YouTube's art track gives the same date and label under the name Hanging Tree. It credits the song's real authors: Suzanne Collins for the words, Jeremiah Fraites and Wesley Schultz for the music. It names no singer and no producer. The official recording came out as a single from the soundtrack three days earlier, on 9 December, also listed at 3:38, on Republic Records.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} is a new recording of a song by someone other than its original artist. A {{ <concept_link name="sound-alike recording" text="sound-alike" /> }} is a particular kind of cover, made to resemble a hit so closely that a listener might take one for the other, and it often appears under a name close to the original's. Here the artist name is the song's own title, the single arrived the same week as the official one and at the same length, and Discogs lists the label but no releases on it. Hear the original in {{ <track_link source="james-newton-howard-jennifer-lawrence-the-hanging-tree.md" /> }}.

The song itself is {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} music: in the story it is a song Katniss learned from her father, and the film turns it into a battle cry. That placement made it a hit, and the hit is what a sound-alike follows.
