## Genre and Mood

**Feathered Sun**'s **Saubohnen** is house music made by a band rather than by one producer at a laptop. Its mood, as its label tells it, is relaxed, carefree and playful: the soundtrack to a perfect idle summer's day spent amongst friends, with just enough bite to keep the vibe lively.

## Song Summary

Saubohnen, German for broad beans, is the title track and the A-side of a three-track EP that Circus Company, the Paris label, released in July 2015; Hmm Hm Hmm and Help fill the B-side. At just over eight minutes it has a whole side of the twelve-inch to itself. The label describes the EP as a whole: playful vocal intonations meeting live instrumentation over laid-back grooves, made without a need for hype or drama.

## Musical Breakdown

No review describes Saubohnen on its own; the label's text covers all three tracks. So this breakdown names only what that text suggests to listen for.

### Main Section

Listen for many hands at work. Circus Company writes of an organic band dynamic that can only be wrought by many hands working in unison, and of a sound born from friends jamming wherever the mood is right rather than in rigid studio sessions. Over eight minutes, notice which parts sound played and which sound looped.

## Concepts in Focus

Everything here rests on the {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }}, the felt way the rhythm parts lock together. The label calls it laid back yet with bite: slow enough to lounge to, steady enough to tap your toes. A jammed groove can breathe and shift in a way a quantised loop rarely does.

The EP's story is also about {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}. The label opens with the season when the electronic music community leaves its darkened rooms and heads back to nature, and ends by setting the collective's down-to-earth sound against the mass identity of the dancefloor. Outdoor daytime parties are part of that culture as much as clubs at night.

## Artistic Context

Circus Company names the six members of the Berlin-based collective as NU, jO.K.e, Raz Ohara, Chris Schwarzwaelder, Iannis Ritter and Acid Pauli, friends who each release music elsewhere. Acid Pauli is the alias of Martin Gretschmann, of Console and formerly The Notwist; Nu and Schwarzwälder made {{ <track_link source="nu-dance-in-my-pants.md" /> }} together. Saubohnen was their second release, after a first one on Platon Records.
