## Genre and Mood

**Mansfield.TYA**'s **Cavaliers** is two and a half minutes of night music. It is chanson in the sense of a French lyric telling a small, strange story, set over what The Quietus heard as skeletal electroclash beats, on the album Vice called the duo's far more experimental one. The feelings are dark and melancholic, and the words earn both.

## Song Summary

The album's credits give the lyric of Cavaliers, like that of Logic Coco, to the duo's friend Marion Jdanoff; the music is by Mansfield.TYA, Julia Lanoë and Carla Pallone. The lyric, printed with the official audio, fills a nearby park with horseless knights crawling on all fours in their armour, dying of love a hundred times; then it turns to a speaker who would rather die asleep than face tomorrow. At 2:33 it is one of the shortest songs on NYX, the twelfth of fifteen tracks.

## Musical Breakdown

No source walks through the track, so the breakdown follows the two stanzas of the printed lyric.

### First Stanza: The Knights

The story opens in the park, the horseless knights dragging themselves through the woods: a fable in the first person, told by the one who put them there and wishes they would never arrive. Listen for how bare the beat stays under it.

### Second Stanza: The Fear of Sleep

The fable falls away: afraid of sleeping, afraid of waking, cowardly about tomorrow, not loving the night but fearing the morning. On an album named after Nyx, the Greek goddess of night, these lines say what the night is for.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="electroclash" text="Electroclash" /> }} is the scene of around 2000 that mixed electro and new wave with punk attitude. David McKenna of The Quietus heard Cavaliers built on skeletal electroclash beats, emaciated cousins of the tunes of Sexy Sushi, the electro duo in which Julia Lanoë performs as Rebeka Warrior. Compare {{ <track_link source="miss-kittin-frank-sinatra.md" /> }}.

NYX is also a {{ <concept_link name="concept album" /> }}. Short markers divide its track list into an overture, two parts and a finale; Carla Pallone told Vice the duo treated it a bit like a film. Cavaliers sits in the second part, late in the night.

A {{ <concept_link name="cover version" /> }} shows what others hear in a song: on ReNYX, the 2012 album that revisited NYX, Cavaliers returns as covers by Françoiz Breut and by Sieur et Dame, as a cover-mix by La Chatte and as a remix by Mensch.

## Artistic Context

Mansfield.TYA began in Nantes in 2002: the singer Julia Lanoë, also of Sexy Sushi and Kompromat, and the baroque violinist Carla Pallone. NYX, their third album, recorded by the duo in Saint-Brévin-les-Pins in the winter of 2010-11, came out on 17 October 2011 on Vicious Circle. McKenna found it their album with the least English, widened by keyboards, electronic textures and danceable rhythms. Corpo Inferno (2015) and Monument ordinaire (2021) followed before the duo split in 2022.

## Impact and Reception

Opinion split: the album's Bandcamp page quotes Magic calling it magnificent, powerful and dark, while the Dutch site festivalinfo heard only thin lines of violin, guitar and synthesizer and advised against buying it. Cavaliers held its place: in 2022 it was included on Twenty Years After, the duo's compilation of favourites from their first four albums. Play it twice: once for the knights, once for the morning.
