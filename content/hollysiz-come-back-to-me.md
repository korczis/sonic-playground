## Genre and Mood

**HollySiz** introduced herself with **Come Back to Me**, a pop song that critics placed between 1980s new wave and dance. 24matins called it rock, danceable, groovy and irresistible; Essentially Pop heard a disco-infused dance track. Under the groove sits a bittersweet subject, which is what keeps the catchiness from feeling light.

## Song Summary

HollySiz is the stage name of the French actress Cécile Cassel. She wrote and composed the song alone, and told Pure Charts that it mattered to her that her first single be a track on which she had done everything. It was her lucky song and the first she recorded; with it she had won the Fair young-talent contest three years earlier. Asked by Metro what it means, she said, as Songfacts reports, that it is about grief, but in a good way: taking the good energy from a bad thing, and wishing someone were still there to dance with. The album version runs 2:46.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="stage persona" /> }} can be a costume or a shield. Cassel told Pure Charts the name was not about distance: Siz is a nickname, and Holly, the plant, is red and prickly, with a nod to a character in Terrence Malick's Badlands. She used it so that people would hear the music before the famous surname, and planned her shows around a red-and-white colour code.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} on her channel was directed by Hugo Gélin with HollySiz herself; she said she already had the idea for it when the song was chosen as the single. It runs a little longer than the album recording, and no source describes what it shows.

## Artistic Context

Cassel, born in 1982 and the half-sister of Vincent Cassel, began writing songs in English while living in England. Meeting Yodelice was decisive: he was one of the first to hear her songs and pushed her to make more. My Name Is followed on 30 September 2013. Asked about influences, she recognised in her songs artists she loves, such as The Kills, Gossip, LCD Soundsystem and Portishead.

## Impact and Reception

The single reached number 22 in France, and BMW chose it in 2013 for a campaign for its electric i3. Essentially Pop reported that My Name Is was nominated for best debut album at the Victoires de la Musique. Press play and listen for how a song about loss still asks you to dance.
