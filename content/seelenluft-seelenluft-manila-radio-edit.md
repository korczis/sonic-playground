## Genre and Mood

**Seelenluft**'s **Manila (Radio Edit)** is the short version of an electronic club hit with a pop song's shape. The Swiss producer Beat Solèr's album of 2002 leaned toward electro-pop, and Manila became its dance record: playful and catchy, built around a voice nobody had heard before.

## Song Summary

The voice belongs to Michael Smith, a twelve-year-old from Compton, California, whom Solèr met in the street while making the album Out of the Woods in Los Angeles. He was not a trained singer; Freaky Trigger called his part a rap. Manila was the album's first single and, as German Wikipedia puts it, an overnight hit. This edit, three minutes six long, comes from Klein Records' release Manila (Mixes), dated June 2003; the owner's Spotify yearbook lists it as Seelenluft - Manila (Radio Edit), the project's name repeated in the title.

## Musical Breakdown

No source we found times or describes this edit section by section, so this guide stays with what is documented.

### The Voice

Smith's voice is the track's identity. Seelenluft's own channel, reposting the original video in 2020, still introduced it as the version featuring the twelve-year-old Michael Smith.

### The Edit

At 3:06 this is a radio length. The same release carried longer mixes by Ewan Pearson and Manitoba, and a separate radio edit of Ewan Pearson's mix, at 3:40.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="radio edit" /> }} is a shorter version of a track made for radio play. Club records are often built long, for DJs to mix; a radio edit gets to the voice and the hook faster. Manila shows the whole system at work: one song released as an album track, an edit and a run of remixes, each for a different room.

The {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} credit on the British single reads Seelenluft featuring Michael Smith. Seelenluft is Solèr's project; Smith is the guest, and on this record the guest is the hook.

Manila is a product of {{ <concept_link name="club culture" /> }}: before it reached the pop charts it topped club charts in France, Germany and Austria, the charts DJs report.

## Artistic Context

Beat Solèr, born in 1971 in Wil, Switzerland, played guitar in new wave and indie bands before turning to electronic music in Zurich; his official debut Bellatrax came out in 1996. Out of the Woods was the first Seelenluft album with song structures. Six years later he took the same approach of producer and guest voice to {{ <track_link source="seelenluft-horse-with-no-name.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

In the UK, released by Back Yard, Manila reached number 70 on the Official Singles Chart in October 2003 and number 8 on the dance singles chart; in Australia it peaked at 45 in March 2004. PopMatters remembered it as a club hit, and it has appeared on more than sixty compilations. Not everyone was charmed: Freaky Trigger called it a vacuous waste of space and objected to the use of a child's voice. Listen and decide for yourself.
