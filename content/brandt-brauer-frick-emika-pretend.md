## Genre and Mood

**Brandt Brauer Frick** play techno with an acoustic ensemble, and on **Pretend** they hand the microphone to **Emika**. It is electronic, minimal and techno in form, made with acoustic instruments rather than only electronic tools. The feelings named here, hypnotic and intense, follow the critics: the Quietus heard something strangely fluid, the Line of Best Fit something increasingly frantic.

## Song Summary

Pretend is one of the new songs on Mr. Machine, released by !K7 on 24 October 2011, an album on which the trio also re-recorded tracks from their 2010 debut You Make Me Real for their ensemble. The song is Emika's: she is credited as its composer and lyricist, and her vocal appears in modified form on her own debut album. The Line of Best Fit called the result a remix-cum-cover of her original.

## Musical Breakdown

The sources describe the track's shape rather than a timeline, so the breakdown follows that shape.

### Intro

The Quietus heard the ensemble build "a dense mesh of percussion and struck melodies". Listen for where the first figure comes from: wood, skin or string.

### Build-up

For the Line of Best Fit, the track is a seven-minute series of crescendo and diminuendo: figures introduced, repeated, warped and returned to the mix. Emika's detached vocal sits over "taut aquatic percussion", in PopMatters' words.

### Climax

The build ends in "a swell of dramatic strings", and the Line of Best Fit heard violins scratched at with something approaching full-blown violence.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="four-on-the-floor" /> }} beat puts the kick on every beat of the bar. In Pretend it is played by people, and the Quietus found the whole "strangely fluid despite the rigid 4/4 drum beat": the pulse is strict, the hands around it are not.

The track is a long {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }}, or rather several: tension that rises, falls back and rises again, until the strings take over.

It also shows how flexible a {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} can be. Brandt Brauer Frick remixed Emika's Pretend, released by Ninja Tune as a single in September 2011, and their version is close to a cover, a new arrangement around her vocal. Soul Clap then remixed theirs.

## Artistic Context

Daniel Brandt, Jan Brauer and Paul Frick formed the group in Wiesbaden in 2008, combining the formulas of dance music with new classical music; after moving to !K7 they played live as the Brandt Brauer Frick Ensemble. Emika, born Ema Jolly in Milton Keynes in 1986 and based in Berlin since 2006, has a mother from Příbram.

## Impact and Reception

The Line of Best Fit called Pretend "one of the very best tracks" it had posted as a Song of the Day; PopMatters gave Mr. Machine seven out of ten and named Pretend one of its most compulsively danceable moments. Play it loud enough to hear the hands behind the beat.
