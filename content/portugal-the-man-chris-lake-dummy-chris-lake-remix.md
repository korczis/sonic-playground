## Genre and Mood

**Portugal. The Man** released Dummy in 2023 as a rock-pop single with a bright surface and a dark lyric; in **Dummy - Chris Lake Remix**, **Chris Lake** moves it onto a tech house floor. Gray Area called it a stomper that turbo-charged the original's laid-back mix of guitar and keys. Energetic is the remix's whole purpose; groovy comes from a bassline the review heard bouncing from side to side.

## Song Summary

John Gourley introduced the song as an ode to The Cure for people worried about nuclear war: its chorus counts dancers onto the floor, its last verse is about a light going out. Lake keeps that tension and, in Gray Area's description, builds on very little: the vocal and the bass from the first beat, percussion it called infectious and unrelenting, and some original elements dropped because less is more. Atlantic released this 2:53 version on 31 March 2023, with an extended mix twice as long.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} rebuilds a recording from its parts for a new setting, here a club: Lake played it in his DJ sets at Miami Music Week before release; what it removes matters as much as what it adds.

The {{ <concept_link name="bassline" /> }} carries the track. In tech house, bass and percussion do the work a guitar riff does in rock, and the vocal hook rides on top; the bass is the part the review described most precisely, buoyant and exact.

Atlantic also released an {{ <concept_link name="extended mix" /> }} of 5:49. The long one gives a DJ room to mix in and out. Compare {{ <track_link source="portugal-the-man-coldabank-feel-it-still-coldabank-remix.md" /> }}, a club remix that made a band single longer instead.

## Artistic Context

Portugal. The Man come from Wasilla, Alaska, are based in Portland, Oregon, and have been active since 2004; {{ <track_link source="portugal-the-man-feel-it-still.md" /> }} won them a Grammy. Dummy was the first single from their ninth album, Chris Black Changed My Life (Atlantic, 23 June 2023), dedicated to a friend of the band who died in 2019. Chris Lake is an English DJ and producer, born in 1982, working in house and tech house since 2002; he launched Black Book Records in 2017, has two Grammy nominations for Best Remixed Recording and also remixed {{ <track_link source="indiana-solo-dancing.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

The original was in the top 15 at US alternative radio within a month and soundtracked a Taco Bell campaign; Far Out called it another inescapable earworm, not the most original number but executed well. Play the band's version, then this one, and listen for what is no longer there.
