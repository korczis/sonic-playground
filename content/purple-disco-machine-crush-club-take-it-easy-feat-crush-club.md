## Genre and Mood

**Purple Disco Machine** and **Crush Club** meet on **Take It Easy**, a track from an album Discogs files under house and nu-disco. John Goodridge of The AU Review heard it as the moment Soulmatic turns back to the smooth soul classics, and that is where this page places it: groovy, soulful, unhurried in its title and rising in its effect.

## Song Summary

Take It Easy is the seventh of thirteen tracks on Soulmatic, the debut album of Tino Piontek, the Dresden producer and DJ who records as Purple Disco Machine. Sweat It Out! released the album on 20 October 2017, and the track runs 4:46, with Crush Club credited as featured artist. Goodridge called it an instrumental burner that builds intensity, one that transported him to a world of disco lights and glitter balls. No lyrics page, interview or credit list says what Crush Club perform on it.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="build-up" /> }} is the stretch of a dance track where tension rises, by adding layers, filtering, or repeating a figure with growing force, until it peaks or releases. Here, by the review's account, the whole track is the build: listen for how it gets more intense; whether it ever releases into a drop, no source says.

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest named in a recording's credit. On Soulmatic most guests are vocalists, from Karen Harding on {{ <track_link source="purple-disco-machine-boris-dlugosch-karen-harding-love-for-days-feat-karen-harding.md" /> }} to Cee-Lo and Kool Keith. ## Artistic Context

Piontek, born in Dresden in 1980, released his first productions in 2012; his father, a record collector, had introduced him to the funk of the 1970s and 1980s. Mixmag announced the album in May 2017 for Sweat It Out!, calling him a deep funk purveyor. Soulmatic gathers that taste into thirteen tracks with guests across the album; compare the sample-built {{ <track_link source="purple-disco-machine-body-funk.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

Soulmatic drew warm reviews: The AU Review found it as full of funk and soul as can be, and DMC World gave it five out of five for its ridiculously good vibes. No single release of Take It Easy is documented, but his channel posted it in January 2018. Turn the lights low and let it build.
