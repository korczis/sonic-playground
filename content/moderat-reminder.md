## Genre and Mood

**Moderat** released **Reminder** as the first sign of their third album, and it sits where the trio's electronic music meets song: electro-pop in its shape, techno in its tools. The mood is melancholic and dreamy. Stereogum heard the vocals as delicate and dreamy, carrying dark storytelling, and Resident Advisor heard Sascha Ring's words evoke both despair and hope.

## Song Summary

Reminder is built as two layers that pull apart. Stereogum described a track huge and airy up top, with Apparat's sweeping atmospheric flourishes high in the mix, and intricate down low, with Modeselektor's quick-hit drum pattern as a subdued anchor. Thump heard choppy percussion and unsequenced melodies under a vocal that aches and croons, and thought it would not be a strange bedfellow to Radiohead's later work. One PopMatters critic called it pulsing, minimally robotic techno.

## Musical Breakdown

### Intro

According to Cool Hunting, the song opens with Ring revealing something intimate through his bare voice, with only a light breakbeat percussion pattern beside him.

### Build-up

The percussion gathers. Magdalen Jenne of PopMatters liked the way unmetered percussion slowly coalesces into a beat you could play in a club.

### Main Section

The vocal floats over the two layers Stereogum described, the airy flourishes above and the quick, intricate drums below.

### Breakdown

Ari Rosenschein of PopMatters was caught off guard by a mid-song break into Depeche Mode-style darkwave.

### Outro

No source describes how the track ends; listen for whether the darkwave turn comes back.

## Concepts in Focus

The rhythm is a study in the {{ <concept_link name="breakbeat" /> }} used with restraint. Cool Hunting heard a light breakbeat pattern rather than a pounding one, Resident Advisor a quasi-dancehall rhythm, and Stereogum drums that act as a subdued anchor, kept low so that the voice leads.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }}, written and directed by Mate Steinforth and made by SEHSUCHT Berlin with Moderat's long-time visual partner Pfadfinderei, uses the look and the production tools of computer games: a rebellious teenager harvesting crystals from a vast grey landscape in a dystopian world, and a shrine under attack from an army of drones.

The single also had a {{ <concept_link name="b-side" /> }}. After the digital release on 19 February 2016, Thump reported, a 10-inch followed on 26 February with Fondle on its flip side.

## Artistic Context

Moderat joins the Berlin duo Modeselektor, Gernot Bronsert and Sebastian Szary, with Sascha Ring, who records as Apparat. Their third album, III, came out on 1 April 2016 on Monkeytown with Reminder as its fifth track and lead single; it completed a trilogy begun with {{ <track_link source="moderat-a-new-error.md" /> }} and the first album in 2009, and it was their last studio record before a hiatus that followed their Berlin concerts of September 2017.

## Impact and Reception

PopMatters' singles panel gave Reminder an average of 7.40 out of 10; one critic called it expansive, percussive, melodic, contemporary and timeless. III reached number five in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Listen with headphones: the song lives in the distance between its top and its bottom.
