## Genre and Mood

**Synapson** and **Anna Kova** made **All in You** a hit by the standards of pop radio, with a producers' duo behind it. Wikipedia files Synapson as electro, nu disco and deep house; the feelings chosen here, uplifting and catchy, are this page's reading of a single that stayed on the French chart for more than a year.

## Song Summary

All in You came out in 2015 and went onto Convergence, Synapson's second album, released that October on Parlophone. The credits divide the work clearly: the music is by the duo, Alexandre Chière and Paul Cucuron, who also produced it; the words are by Anna Spivakova, who sings them as Anna Kova. Synapson themselves called the collaboration one of their best musical encounters of 2015.

All in You (Extended), released in 2015 and running just over six minutes, is the long form of the same song.

### The Live Version

In 2026 Synapson released All in You recorded at the Olympia in Paris, for their fifteenth anniversary.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited on another artist's track. Here the credit hides a real partnership: Kova is not only the voice but the lyricist, while Synapson wrote and produced the music. The title the charts printed, Synapson feat. Anna Kova, tells you whose project it is.

An {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }} is a second finished recording by the same artists. The extended version and the Olympia recording are both Synapson's own; compare them with the album track and you can hear how much of a hit's shape is decided after the song is written.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} was noticed too: Wikipedia reports a nomination for Best Song at the 2016 Berlin Music Video Awards.

## Artistic Context

Synapson are two childhood friends, Chière on keyboards and saxophone, Cucuron on drums, scratching and mixing, together as Synapson since 2009. Their breakthrough was Djon maya maï with the singer Victor Démé; Convergence, named for the meeting of their influences, also carried Fireball with Broken Back, heard here in {{ <track_link source="broken-back-happiest-man-on-earth-radio-edit.md" /> }}.

## Impact and Reception

All in You entered the French chart at the end of May 2015, peaked at number 10 and stayed for seventy weeks; in Wallonia it reached number 4. It outdid Convergence itself, which peaked at 26 in France. Play the album version first, then the extended one.
