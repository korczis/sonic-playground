## Genre and Mood

**The Knife** released **Got 2 Let U** in May 2002, and it is synth-pop in the brightest, least guarded sense the Swedish duo ever allowed themselves: playful, quirky, warm. Drowned in Sound heard a poppy warmth and fuzzy synths in it; a reader's review on Debaser heard funny voices bent by technology and a song that seems to say "don't take me seriously". Neither describes the icy minimalism the band would perfect on Silent Shout.

## Song Summary

Got 2 Let U was the first single from Deep Cuts, out on 6 May 2002 as a three-track CD on Rabid Records, catalogue RABID 010, eight months before the album. On Deep Cuts it is track 12; like the whole album it is written and produced by The Knife, Karin and Olof Dreijer, and mixed by Christoffer Berg. What the accounts that exist single out is the contrast between a cheerful surface and voices bent out of shape.

## Musical Breakdown

### Sound Breakdown

No source walks through the song part by part, so this page does not either. The reviews give texture: fuzzy synths and a poppy warmth, in Drowned in Sound's words, and beats that Stereogum describes for the whole album as rudimentary, brash but effective, made with free software.

### The Voices

The Debaser review hears funny, technologically distorted voices, which fits what Stereogum says of the album as a whole: pitch-shifting let Karin Dreijer change her voice and take on different characters.

## Concepts in Focus

{{ <concept_link name="pitch shifting" /> }} raises or lowers a recorded voice. On Deep Cuts it became what Stereogum calls an invaluable tool in the Knife's arsenal: a way for Karin Dreijer to step out of her own voice and into a role.

The {{ <concept_link name="synthesizer" /> }} carries the song's warmth. When the duo reworked it for Shaken-Up Versions, the 2014 album of songs as their final live show played them, Drowned in Sound noted that the fuzzy synths were replaced by sinister pings and the poppy warmth was gone, and The Line of Best Fit heard a 4/4 kick and metallic synth signal its new techno flavour. The mood lived in the synth sounds.

Got 2 Let U is also a {{ <concept_link name="self-release" /> }}. The Dreijers ran their own label, Rabid Records, and released the single and the album there.

## Artistic Context

The Knife were the siblings Karin and Olof Dreijer from Gothenburg, together since 1999. As Stereogum's anniversary piece recalls, their 2001 debut sold around 1,000 copies and was dismissed as cute indie, so for Deep Cuts they set out to make pop that would reach more people; in Karin's words, "That's why we used hip-hop beats and Euro-techno disco stuff." Olof described the aim as packaging socialist and feminist ideas in a pop format. Deep Cuts was recorded in Stockholm in 2002 and released on 17 January 2003.

## Impact and Reception

Deep Cuts reached number 11 in Sweden and went gold, and the duo won a Grammis for Pop Group of the Year, which they boycotted in protest against male dominance in the music industry. Twelve years later Got 2 Let U was still in their live set. Start with the 2002 original and hear how warm this band once let itself be.
