## Genre and Mood

**Lucian** made **Eye of the Storm** with **Makaw** as a short piece of electronic pop; Apple Music files it under electronic, and Marmoset describes Lucian's production as tight future bass. No review of this song has been published, so catchy and energetic are a reading of what Marmoset says about his songs in general, catchy and future leaning, not a description of this one. How it actually feels is for your ears.

## Song Summary

The facts come from the release. Eye of the Storm came out on 27 August 2021 as a single credited to Lucian featuring Makaw, distributed through TuneCore with the phonographic copyright held by Lucian himself, so it is a self-release. It lasts 2:20. Lucian put it on his own YouTube channel the day it came out, and in October 2021 the YouTube channel Valiant uploaded it again with artwork by the illustrator Nixeu.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} is a guest credited next to the main artist. Lucian has built his catalogue that way since 2016: Apple Music lists singles with Noé, Jasmine Sokko, PhiloSofie, Beth Duck and many other singers, one voice per song, while the production stays his. Marmoset puts it plainly: he teams with female vocalists whose voices meld with his production.

A {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is the part a listener remembers, and in a song of two minutes and twenty seconds it carries almost everything. Compare the lush chords of {{ <track_link source="flume-holdin-on.md" /> }}, the kind of sound future bass grew from.

## Artistic Context

Lucian first reached a large audience with remixes: Marmoset credits his versions of Black Coast's TRNDSTTR and Alessia Cara's Here with over 100 million streams across SoundCloud, YouTube and Spotify, and the Trap Nation channel posted his Here remix in 2015, linking the same Instagram and SoundCloud accounts that Valiant later linked for Eye of the Storm. From 2022 he released singles with IAMSAM that Apple Music files under house and dance. The SoundCloud account linked to Makaw gives Sydney as its city; Stereofox introduced a Sydney singer named Makaw with her debut single Tell Me Why in 2016, placing her between Portishead, Feist, Thom Yorke and Lykke Li.
