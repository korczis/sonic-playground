## Genre and Mood

**Gigi D'Agostino & Lento Violento**'s **Bla Bla Bla (Bla Bla Ton Mix)** is not the 1999 hit but a 2020 reworking of it, credited to Gigi Dag, his own short name, and the producer Luca Noise. It belongs to dance and electronic music as a remix of the artist's own song, and the hypnotic, playful repetition of the original is its starting point.

## Song Summary

The mix is track four of Slowerland, an album of twenty-one tracks that D'Agostino released at the end of September 2020 on the label Gigi D'Agostino Planet; the official upload went up on his YouTube channel on 29 September. It lasts 2:14. The original, released in May 1999 as the third single from L'Amour Toujours, runs 4:15 on the album and 3:10 as a radio edit, and its only vocal is one line from Stretch's 1975 single Why Did You Do It, sampled and distorted. The mix's name, Bla Bla Ton, plays on the title; the brackets on the release name who made it.

## Musical Breakdown

No review or timed analysis of the Bla Bla Ton Mix has been published, so this page describes its frame and leaves the listening to you.

### Intro

How the mix opens is not described in any source. Listen for whether it starts from the sampled voice, as the original does, or from the beat.

### Main Section

The original is a single phrase repeated and distorted in different ways from start to finish. With just over half the time, the mix has to choose which of those variations to keep.

### Outro

At 2:14 it is one of the shortest tracks on Slowerland; only Crazy and Osservo in its dance mix are shorter.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="remix" /> }} reworks a recording's parts into a new version, usually by another producer. Here the remixer is partly the original producer himself, with Luca Noise beside him, and the credit line, mix Gigi Dag & Luca Noise, says so.

That makes it close to an {{ <concept_link name="alternate version" /> }}: the same artist returning to his own song with a different sound and length. Hearing it next to {{ <track_link source="gigi-d-agostino-bla-bla-bla.md" /> }} shows how much of a track's identity survives when its maker changes it twenty-one years later.

## Artistic Context

Lento Violento, Italian for slow and violent, names a subgenre that D'Agostino's 2003 track Ripassa helped shape and a 2007 compilation of thirty-five of his songs in that style; MusicBrainz also lists it as an artist name of his own. Slowerland carries several new mixes by Gigi Dag and Luca Noise, from In My Mind to Bla Bla Bla, and a mini-album sequel, Slower Land 2, made with Luca Noise as Astro Musico, followed in March 2021.

## Impact and Reception

No chart run or critical review of the mix has been found. Its place is as one more version of a song D'Agostino has kept returning to: the Italian Wikipedia notes several drafts of it played at live events and later tracks that reuse its vocal hook. Play it straight after the 1999 version.
