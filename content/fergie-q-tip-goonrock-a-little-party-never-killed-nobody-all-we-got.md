## Genre and Mood

**Fergie**, **Q-Tip** and **GoonRock** made **A Little Party Never Killed Nobody (All We Got)** as a 1920s party seen through 2013 club music. It is electro-swing in spirit, electro house in its beat and hip-hop in its guest verse, and its mood is celebratory, playful and full of energy.

## Song Summary

The song was recorded for the soundtrack of Baz Luhrmann's The Great Gatsby, the 2013 adaptation of F. Scott Fitzgerald's novel, and its lyric is about partying. XXL summed it up as pop dance house music meeting jazz. Fergie sings the verses in a jazz-inflected, lounge style over saxophone, GoonRock's synths take over the hook, and Q-Tip raps after a frantic bridge. PopCrush noted that the beat never changes while everything on top of it does.

## Musical Breakdown

### Verse 1

Fergie opens in her jazz voice, lounge-style and inflected with swing, with saxophone around her; SPIN heard a Billie Holiday-style delivery.

### Chorus

The hook switches to GoonRock's world: synths on top of the same steady electro house beat, a sudden jump from the speakeasy to the club.

### Bridge

A frantic bridge raises the temperature before the guest verse.

### Verse 2

Q-Tip, the smooth voice of A Tribe Called Quest, plays the party rapper and hype man here, his voice sped up by GoonRock, before Fergie returns to close the song.

## Concepts in Focus

A {{ <concept_link name="soundtrack" /> }} placement shaped the whole song. The film's soundtrack set its 1920s story beside contemporary artists such as Beyoncé with André 3000, Lana Del Rey, Florence + the Machine and Jack White, and this track does the same in miniature: what SPIN heard as a flapper party, built from 2013 club sounds. Compare {{ <track_link source="caravan-palace-lone-digger.md" /> }}, which mixes the same ingredients for the dancefloor alone.

The {{ <concept_link name="swing" /> }} lives in Fergie's jazz-inflected phrasing and the saxophone, while, as PopCrush notes, the beat stays the same from verse to hook. That contrast between a jazz singer and a club beat is the song's main trick.

Each {{ <concept_link name="featured artist" /> }} has a clear job: GoonRock produces and frames the hook, Q-Tip brings a rapper's voice and a hip-hop legend's name.

The {{ <concept_link name="music video" /> }} arrived on 6 August 2013, three months after the film. It is shorter than the four-minute soundtrack version, so the page embeds the official lyric video, which carries the full recording.

## Artistic Context

Fergie sang with the Black Eyed Peas, and PopCrush heard in the song everything people love or loathe about the Peas, plus a bit of jazz. Q-Tip came from A Tribe Called Quest; GoonRock is the producer David Listenbee. Interscope released the song on 17 May 2013 after its premiere on Rolling Stone's website in April.

## Impact and Reception

SPIN called it a spectacle and a hot mess it loved anyway, and XXL thought Fergie handled the blend of house and jazz well. On the charts the song reached number three in New Zealand and number ten in Germany, peaked at 77 on the Billboard Hot 100 and went platinum in the United States, Germany and Sweden. Play it loud, preferably in good shoes.
