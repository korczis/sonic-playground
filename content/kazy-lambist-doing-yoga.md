> **Kazy Lambist**'s "Doing Yoga" is warm and sun-drenched: easy beats and a playful melody that make relaxation sound like a small celebration.

"Doing Yoga" was the song that introduced its maker. Light and unhurried, it suits slow afternoons as well as the summer radio that picked it up.

## Genre and Mood

Between **electronic**, **pop** and **chillwave**, "Doing Yoga" is **relaxed** and **uplifting**, with a **dreamy** quality. Its rhythm keeps a steady pulse without ever pushing.

## About the Artist

**Kazy Lambist** is the project of Arthur Dubreucq, a producer and songwriter who grew up in Montpellier in the south of France. "Doing Yoga" was the title track of his debut EP in 2015; his first full-length album, *33 000 FT*, followed in 2018, and the second, *Moda*, in 2025. The song is credited to Dubreucq as lyricist and composer, and was released through Cinq 7 and Wagram Music.

## Song Summary

Opening with a gentle beat, "Doing Yoga" draws you into a light, airy sound and builds subtly to its groove. In the summer of 2015 it appeared on *FG. Dancefloor Summer Hits 2015*, a compilation from the French dance radio station FG, which shows how a soft song found its way onto dance radio.

## Sound Breakdown

### Intro: Breezy Beats

The track begins with soft, breezy beats that set a calm tone.

### First Verse: Playful Melody

A playful melody enters, weaving into the beat rather than sitting on top of it.

### Chorus: Sunlit Flow

The chorus combines the beat with the song's catchiest melodic idea, the part that stays with you.

### Bridge: Reflective Pause

A brief, softer passage gives the track a moment of rest.

### Final Chorus: Blissful Resolution

The final chorus brings the layers back together and closes the song in the same easy mood.

## Concepts in Focus

The {{ <concept_link name="groove" /> }} of "Doing Yoga" is a lesson in restraint. The beat is steady enough for a dance compilation, but soft in attack and spacious, so the song relaxes rather than drives. The same balance between pulse and calm runs through {{ <track_link source="fkj-lying-together.md" /> }}.

Its {{ <concept_link name="hook" /> }} is melodic rather than vocal-heavy: a short, playful phrase that returns until it becomes the song's signature. In electronic pop, where verses are often thin, that returning phrase does the work a big chorus does in a rock song.

## Overall Vibe

"Doing Yoga" is **relaxed**, **uplifting** and **dreamy**, a track for unwinding that still has a pulse.

## Conclusion

With "Doing Yoga", Kazy Lambist made a calling card: a short, sunny piece of electronic pop that led to two albums and international tours. Play it on a warm afternoon, and listen for how little it needs to keep you moving.
